Blogger may have revealed how many Z-10ME helicopters Pakistan bought from China
Previous media reports suggested that Islamabad had sought to acquire as many as 40 Z-10-series helicopters as early as 2022
In the post, the blogger known as Sashoujian said that on July 11 China National Aero-Technology Import and Export Corporation (CATIC) signed an export contract with Pakistan’s Ministry of Defence Production for 30 Z-10ME attack helicopters, along with associated products and services.
