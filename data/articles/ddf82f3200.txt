Kobe – Police have arrested a man in Hyogo Prefecture on suspicion of fatally assaulting his second daughter, who was 3, in January.
The Hyogo Prefectural Police department arrested Shinya Arimoto, a 29-year-old self-employed man in the city of Itami in the prefecture, on Wednesday for suspected assault resulting in death.
The girl, Ranon, had been taken into protective custody twice because she appeared to have been abused since soon after birth. Her death came about a month after she had been returned to her home.
