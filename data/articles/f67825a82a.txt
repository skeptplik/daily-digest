Threat actors started exploiting a high-severity vulnerability in the open source Internet Relay Chat (IRC) server UnrealIRCd one day after public disclosure.
Tracked as CVE-2026-114713 (CVSS score: 8.6), the flaw is described as an improper neutralization of delimiters and allows attackers to execute commands with server/IRCOp privileges.
The issue exists because the labeled-response module “does not escape the IRCv3 ‘label’ message tag when echoing it into replies, which allows any user on a network with two or more linked servers to inject server-to-server commands that are executed with server authority,” according to a NIST advisory.
Patches for CVE-2026-114713 and eight other bugs were released on November 7 in UnrealIRCd version 6.2.7.1. A hot patch was also rolled out.
According to UnrealIRCd project leader, developer, and maintainer Bram Matthys, this is the most severe security defect discovered in UnrealIRCd.
“Any ordinary user can KILL, GLINE, and do many more bad things. This is the most critical issue found in UnrealIRCd in the past 15 years,” Matthys said.
Matthys has provided instructions on how to update to UnrealIRCd 6.2.7.1 or apply the hot patch and warned that UnrealIRCd 5 and previous releases have reached End of Life (EoL) and won’t receive a fix.
“It is likely that several bugs that are fixed in 6.2.7.1 go all the way back to 3.2.x,” Matthys said.
On November 8, he warned that hackers had already started probing vulnerable UnrealIRCd instances, urging immediate patching.
“We are seeing bad guys exploiting this issue on the internet right now. Hopefully everyone has hot-patched or updated their server, or at the very least has the spamfilter workaround deployed. If not, do so ASAP,” Matthys said.
On November 13, the US cybersecurity agency CISA added CVE-2026-114713 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) list, giving federal agencies only three days to address it, in line with BOD 26-04 requirements.
CISA also added CVE-2026-123039 to KEV. A high-severity flaw affecting multiple Check Point products, it has been exploited in destructive attacks against Israeli organizations, Check Point warned last week.
