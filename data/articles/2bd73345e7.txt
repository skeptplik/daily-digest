Abu Dhabi – Washington called for a “new” security framework on the first day of U.S.-led talks between Ukraine and Russia in Abu Dhabi, as Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said he was “happy” with the changes in approach by U.S. mediators.
U.S. leader Donald Trump’s mediators — his son-in-law Jared Kushner and Special Envoy to the Middle East Steve Witkoff — brought together the warring sides for several hours of negotiations that are set to continue Wednesday.
Kushner told delegates the United States had “narrowed the gaps” between the sides — without providing details — and hoped to move toward a “solution” to Europe’s worst conflict since World War II.
