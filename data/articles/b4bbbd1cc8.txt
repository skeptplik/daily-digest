There are several images that are burned into my brain from the mid-2010s, and Taylor Swift and Karlie Kloss sitting courtside at a New York Knicks game is certainly one of them. The best friends, who went everywhere together during that time—from concerts to Big Sur to even the Victoria’s Secret Fashion Show—cheered on their adopted hometown team side by side, sipping beers and high-fiving one another. It was Swift’s 1989 era, and the 25-year-old artist had recently cut her hair short and made the move from Nashville to New York City, embracing her official foray into the world of pop music surrounded by friends.
Over a decade later, Swift’s hair is back to a bob and, if you ask any Swiftie detective out there, she has certainly been wearing a lot of 1989 blue. But perhaps the most unexpected return to her 2014 roots came tonight, when she attended husband Travis Kelce’s Kansas City Chiefs game with her old game-day buddy.
Kloss, who joined the showgirl at Arrowhead Stadium, grew up in St. Louis, Missouri, about a four-hour drive from Kansas City. The two were photographed in a suite alongside Swift’s parents, Scott and Andrea, as well as her mother-in-law, Donna Kelce.
For the occasion, the “Patient Zero” singer turned to Tommy Hilfiger for a striped slim-fit cable-knit polo in Chiefs red. The piece, still available in navy, was designed with an open placket that framed her custom Stephanie Gottlieb “87” pendant.
Kelce, a Hilfiger ambassador, was also seen wearing the brand on his way to the stadium.
