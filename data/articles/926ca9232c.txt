IO Interactive's 007 First Light received Game of the Year at the 2026 TIGA Awards, while Dovetail Games earned four awards, including Best Publisher and Best Educational, Serious or Simulation Game for Train Sim World 6.
The Chatham-based studio also won Commitment to ESG and Commitment to Workplace Wellbeing.
Playground Games won Best Large Studio and Best In-House Recruitment and Talent Development, while Wales Interactive received Best Small Studio and Best VR/AR/XR/MR Game for Maid of Sker VR.
London-based indie studio SFB Games' The Mermaid Mask was recognised for Creativity in Games and Best Narrative/Storytelling.
Elsewhere, Crimson Desert won Best Action and Adventure Game, and Ghost of Yōtei received Best Visual Design.
Side was recognised as Best Services Provider and QA/Localisation Provider, while Room 8 Group won Best Co-Development Partner, and Code Wizards Group received Best Technical Innovation.
The Outstanding Leadership award was presented to Steel City Interactive CEO Ash Habib, and the Outstanding Individual of the Year award was given to Associate Professor Mark Wickham, Director of Computer Arts and Technology at Norwich University of the Arts and TIGA Education Advisor.
Wickham was recognised for "his contribution to games education, his support for TIGA's work to strengthen links between education and industry and his contribution to TIGA's accreditation system."
"These awards recognise the talent and dedication involved in making outstanding games, building successful businesses and delivering excellence in games education," said TIGA CEO Richard Wilson.
"Our winners demonstrate the breadth of excellence across the sector, from independent studios to major developers, specialist service providers and education institutions."
