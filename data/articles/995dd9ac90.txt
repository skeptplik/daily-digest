Grand Theft Auto 6 has a giant crab haunting the swamps and, more importantly, the Crabshack VF5 SMG, a crab-themed gun, to help your future career prospects. It's all part of an optional event called Pelican Valley Outpost that sees you pick up the pieces of some questionable experiments carried out on behalf of a store chain. It's worth the trouble, even aside from the standout weapon. There's some cash to help reach your next family fund milestone and a free jet ski, too. Best of all, this little excursion is completely safe, assuming you don't run afoul of the local gator population.
Below, we show you where to find the giant crab in GTA 6 so you can clear the Pelican Valley Outpost and get the Crabshack VF5 SMG.
Where to find the giant crab safe code in GTA 6
Pelican Valley Outpost, where the giant crab research facility is, has several codes you need to find on your way to getting the standout weapon. You'll find it on a medium-sized island southwest of the "Pelican Valley" name label on the map. Once you remove the fog from the region map, you can see it's one of the only islands in the area with buildings on it.
Take Jason's boat from his house, and set out. Definitely don't swim here, even if you're already nearby. There are too many gators for this to be a safe option. You can dock your boat at the boathouse and follow the jetty to the research facility proper. The boathouse is locked, but you'll get inside before leaving.
You'll see several buildings in the compound, but if you venture inside the first shack, you'll notice the power is off. You can still explore it, though you can't access the researcher's computer, which is essential for clearing the outpost. Head outside and around the back of the building near the blue crab cages. Interact with the breaker box to turn the power on, and head back inside. Ignore the computer for now, and take a right. There's a bag of chips in the kitchen if you want Jason to pack on some calories, and the login password for the computer is in the small bedroom opposite the kitchen. Investigate the small piece of wood on the floor to see all the choices, with "CLEAVES" being the most recent.
Interact with the computer, and press left on the direction pad to enter CLEAVES. You can skim through a few tabs of information on-screen, but the important one is the note in the green cell that says "Safe code = Double Success." Leave when you're finished.
Karkinos, the giant crab from the report, will jump you in a short cutscene, but you don't have to fight her. Press the on-screen button to push her off, and she'll vanish into the swamp.
If you want to get the giant crab safe code yourself, head to the research lab behind the shack next. There's a table with a trauma kit and some Zombix inside, which is handy, and in the specimen room you'll find Karkinos' former home and a list of experiments. Only one was a success, number 36. The code, "double success," refers to doubling the number of the successful experiment, making the code 0072.
Go back into the shack, and enter 0072 on the safe. Inside, you'll find a stack of money ($1,107 for us) and another note that reads "Emergency go protocol: 0212."
How to get the Crabshack VF5 SMG in GTA 6
The emergency protocol is the boathouse code. Leave the research compound, return to the boathouse, and input 0212 on the keypad. The door will open. Karkinos' caretaker wasn't as lucky as Jason, but their loss is your gain. Pick up the Crabshack VF5 SMG from their corpse, and you can grab a bag of marked bills to drop off at Brian's lockup later, too.
You can leave the compound the way you came, or you can hop on the jet ski and make your way home that way.
