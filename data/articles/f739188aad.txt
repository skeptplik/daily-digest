This Saturday night, Teyana Taylor showed up at the Los Vegas Grand Prix clad in a glam biker look complete with an eye-catching pair of Balenciaga’s Black Runway Over the Knee Motocross Heel Boots. When combined with a custom leather jacket and some Millennial-inspired patterns, the “All’s Fair” star fit right in among the professional racing crowd.
Taylor managed to snag a handcrafted Alpinestars racing jacket, one of a limited series of eight, with black and cobalt stripe detailing. She wore the jacket over a simple black shirt with an orange flame motif highly reminiscent of the popular fire-patterned clothing and accessories wildly popular around the early 2000s Y2K era. Pulling the look together was Taylor’s footwear, the star wearing Balenciaga’s Black Runway Over the Knee Motocross Heel Boots.
Standing at 34 inches tall and crafted from both perforated and quilted leather, these massive boots nearly double as pants. They feature a sharply pointed toe, slim stiletto heel, and strategically placed rubber detailing, including massive kneepads, that enhance their sporty aesthetic. All in all, the boots were the ideal footwear choice to complete Taylor’s athletic-inspired race day look.

Taylor is no stranger to unconventionally bold fashion choices, which she always manages to nearly effortlessly pull off. Back in October, the actress wore quite the head-turning look at the Academy Museum gala. Taylor showed up in a strapless robin’s egg blue breastplate perfectly sculpted her hips and chest that gave way to a semi-sheer skirt with floral appliqué detailing.
The Las Vegas Grand Prix is a motor racing event that takes place each year in a temporarily constructed racing circuit that includes portions of the famous Las Vegas Strip. The event tends to attract high-profile attendees, with the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix attended by the likes of Beyoncé, Jay-Z, Keanu Reeves, and Chris Pratt. This year, Dutch racer Max Verstappen came in first place, followed by Lando Norris in second and Oscar Piastri in third.
