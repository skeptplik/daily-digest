Like a nasty rash, AI seems to spread everywhere. Google crams AI Overviews down your throat even though it costs the company billions of dollars in lost ad revenue. Leave your apartment, and you might pass two people heaving an AI-powered humanoid robot up the stairs to clean someone’s rooms. Go to the supermarket, and perhaps you’ll slip on a pool of melted ice cream congealing on the floor thanks to an AI-assisted cyberattack.
You go to the movies and see Sam Altman being played with nauseating intensity by Andrew Garfield, and go back home to find that Elon Musk has released a Grok-fied version of The Odyssey. You try to watch it, but it feels like a long, unskippable mobile ad. In a way, the whole world kind of feels that way now: Like a modern Odysseus, you are cursed to roam AI-infested waters, dueling with machine gods and monsters, trying to find your way back to somewhere that feels like home.
