Want to fly to the moon? Nasa is looking for new astronauts for the Artemis programme
The US space agency’s last such call was two years ago, with 8,000 people applying and only 10 making the final cut
Want to fly to the moon and help build a permanent lunar base? Nasa is accepting applications for a new astronaut class that will help grow the Artemis programme.
Officials announced the news on Saturday during a Kennedy Space Centre air show promoting lunar exploration as well as advances in aviation and technology.
Nasa’s last call for new astronauts was two years ago. More than 8,000 applied, with 10 making the final cut in September 2025.
