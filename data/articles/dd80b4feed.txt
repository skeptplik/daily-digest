Mount Kalaga
As Leonida goes, Mount Kalaga is the top of the world. There are bike trails and hiking trails all the way up the mountain, as well as plenty of hunting opportunities if that’s your thing. Shooting deer makes me sad, so instead I went birdwatching and spotted an eagle.
Wildland Zoo
Yes, Vice City has a zoo. Situated on Waxman Island, it is home to tigers, monkeys, cockatoos and more – a very cute place to wander around on a date. It also has its own lively private beach with a bar.
Salton Memorial Island
This little island is home to a memorial sculpture, but Vice City’s party crowd also come here to picnic and take a dip in the ocean. It’s a very short jetski ride from here to Vespucci Island if you want to check out some VERY nice houses (and steal some very nice cars).
St Lucia Lighthouse
Forget Vice Beach, that’s for tourists. The sand dunes around Richard Barebone State Park look more like a Scottish beach than a Floridia one, all driftwood and exposed rock, and a great place to fill out your wildlife logbook with sea birds. On Hardin Island off the coast there is a lighthouse named for Lucia, and from the top you get one of the best views in Leonida.
Flam art museum
You know those gummy bears in the water in downtown Vice City? They’re part of the game’s art museum, which also has several other installations and sculptures to look at.
Dalton Island
You have to get the ferry to get to this oddly perfect little island (or borrow a helicopter) but it is worth the visit – there’s a big, strange art piece at its centre that I think is going to kick off weeks’ worth of sleuthing. Plus, it’s fun to ride around on a golf cart and annoy the residents in their beautiful apartment blocks.
Nine1Nine nightclub
The story takes you to most of the nightclubs in Vice City – except this one. It’s a blend of strip club and bar, and significantly more high-end than Delights Cabaret. Come here for a dance in opulent and sleazy surroundings.
