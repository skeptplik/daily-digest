Chinese ‘black-hole’ purifier catches tumour cells in blood to stop cancer spread
Study tests a proof-of-concept device that could one day lower the risk of metastasis, the leading cause of cancer-related deaths worldwide
When tested in mouse models of breast cancer, the device effectively removed the breakaway cells, delaying tumour relapse and extending survival, according to the paper in the journal Microsystems & Nanoengineering, part of the Nature portfolio.
