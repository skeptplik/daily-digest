Sandpapergate coach Darren Lehmann welcomes Cricket Australia forum
In short:
Darren Lehmann, Australia's coach during the Sandpapergate Test, says he will be happy to be part of Cricket Australia's new forum on the scandal.
Lehmann, who will co-host the ABC's cricket podcast this season, said the announcement of a forum by Cricket Australia let him "see some light at the end of the tunnel".
What's next?
The details of the forum have not been revealed by Cricket Australia yet.
