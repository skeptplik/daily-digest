Niecy Nash-Betts is giving her “All’s Fair” costars a run for their money with her style choices during the show’s Season Two press tour. The Emmy winner, who is working with styling duo Matthew and Reginald Reisman, stepped out in yet another pair of head-turning heels paired with an equally eye-grabbing look.
Nash-Betts stopped by “Good Morning America” on Wednesday to promote the Hulu show. The stylist dressed her in the Coco Set from the brand Gloma, a beige and black two-piece set.

The set features a structured, halter-neck top paired with a high-volume midi skirt, and both feature a beige/off-white base that is completely covered in a bold, oversize black polka-dot pattern. They kept that pattern going down to her feet, keeping the black-and-white theme consistent on her shoes.
The actress opted for an animal-print shoe, which she tends to keep on rotation. She wore a spotted Dalmatian-patterned print pump with a d’Orsay stiletto design. The instep cutaway design left the inside of Nash-Betts’ feet and her arch completely exposed, delivering a flirtier vibe than a traditional pump. The shoes also featured a sharp pointed toe that is covered by a distinct metallic silver cap toe, giving the shoes a bit of a Western edge.
