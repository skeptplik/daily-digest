New York Giants running back Cam Skattebo’s decision to retire his backflip celebration has the backing of head coach John Harbaugh.
“I’m happy for it,” Harbaugh said during his Friday news conference. “I support his decision, and I hope he scores a lot of touchdowns. I hope he gets an opportunity to prove it real soon. That’d be great.”
Skattebo’s call to curb his post-touchdown acrobatics came after Detroit Lions star defensive end Aidan Hutchinson tore his ACL following his sack of New England Patriots quarterback Drake Maye on Sunday in Munich. Hutchinson’s right knee buckled as he landed after jumping in the air as part of his patented sack celebration. It’s the second season-ending injury of his career and leaves a gaping hole in the Lions’ defense.
