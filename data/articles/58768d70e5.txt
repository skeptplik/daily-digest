Beijing – Donald Trump hailed the fanfare of his meeting last week with Xi Jinping as a victory, but the U.S. president has gained little from three such summits this year while the Chinese leader has strengthened his position on the world stage, analysts say.
Xi emerged from a global gathering in China as a statesman who could convene the first China-U.S.-Russia summit as he keeps a fragile trade truce with the U.S. in a holding pattern — where Beijing likes it — even as Washington takes a harder line.
At least in terms of public announcements, Trump has few concrete gains to show.
