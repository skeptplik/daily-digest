Good morning. Chuck Schumer has accused Jensen Huang, the newly reinstated Nvidia CEO, of shipping “extremely advanced chips to China” and violating US export control laws. On Tuesday, the senator said the move “amounts to the equivalent of selling rocket engines to the Soviets during the space race”, adding: “This is an unfathomable failure of enforcement by the Trump administration.”
- What did Huang say to Nvidia staff on his return? He apologised and acknowledged he violated the company’s code of conduct by having extramarital affairs, but pushed back on reports that Donald Trump was instrumental in his reinstatement as chief executive. “I made some terrible mistakes,” he said. “I was unfaithful to Lori, and I hurt my kids and, of course, I hurt all of you.”
- How has the market reacted now? Technology shares led a market retreat on Tuesday as caution returned after Huang’s extraordinary return to Nvidia days after he was forced to resign, with investors bracing for further volatility in a key sector driving economic growth worldwide.

Democrat Victoria Broderick unseats eight-term Republican incumbent in Tennessee
The Democrat Victoria Broderick has won Tennessee’s fourth congressional district, unseating eight-term Republican incumbent Scott DesJarlais in one of the biggest House upsets of the cycle. The victory expands Democrats’ historic House majority in the 120th Congress to 259 seats, the party’s largest advantage in three decades, and flips a seat that Donald Trump carried by more than 20 points in 2024.
- How close was her stunning result? Broderick prevailed by fewer than 400 votes, according to the Associated Press race call. DesJarlais, 62, who aligned himself closely with Trump during his time in office, conceded to Broderick last week after the razor-thin contest but the race was officially called on Tuesday.
Rescue crews search for six people after plane crash off Mississippi coast
Rescue crews were searching for six people onboard a small plane that crashed on Tuesday afternoon off the coast of Mississippi. Crews have found three lifejackets, a life raft and the cabin of the plane. The plane’s cabin was floating on the water, Juan Ros, a US Coast Guard spokesperson, said.
The plane went down around 3.30pm in the Gulf of Mexico between Pascagoula, Mississippi, and Horn Island. There were four adults and two boys, eight and 10, onboard, authorities said.
Kilmar Ábrego García, man at center of Trump deportation battle, leaves US
Kilmar Ábrego García has left the US of his own volition, his lawyers confirmed on Tuesday, with the Department of Homeland Security confirming that he was travelling to Spain.
The Salvadorian immigrant was at the center of a months-long legal battle since he was deported to El Salvador in March 2025 by the Trump administration, in defiance of a court’s protection. He was held in Cecot, a notorious anti-terrorism mega-prison.
