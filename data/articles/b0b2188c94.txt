U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent recently moved out of his home and onto a military base after Iranian threats against his life, the New York Times reported on Tuesday (November 10, 2026), citing sources with knowledge of the matter.
Iran has ramped up efforts to target U.S. personnel, and lethal plots by Tehran against American citizens have reached “advanced stages”, a senior intelligence official in the Trump administration warned in a memo seen by Reuters late last month.
The newspaper reported that the severity of the threats to Mr. Bessent was not immediately clear.
Iran has previously denied plotting to attack officials and people on U.S. soil.
The newspaper reported that U.S. officials have noted that Iran’s ability to carry out large-scale attacks in the US and other territories overseas is limited.
The U.S. Treasury Department did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
Mr. Bessent has been the face of Washington’s economic pressure campaign against Tehran during the course of the U.S.-Israeli war on Iran that began on February 28.
U.S.-Israeli strikes on Iran and Israeli attacks on Lebanon during the war have killed thousands and displaced millions. American and Israeli attacks have also killed top Iranian officials, including the late Supreme Leader Ayatollah Ali Khamenei.
