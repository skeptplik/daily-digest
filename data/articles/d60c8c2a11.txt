The holiday season is at our doorstep, but Gwyneth Paltrow is well-prepared on the fashion front.
This morning, Paltrow stopped by The Today Show to talk up her new wellness podcast and share more about her forthcoming role in A24’s The Apology. The actor arrived bright and early in a holiday-party-ready look: a Michael Kors Collection tee festooned with black ostrich feathers and sparkles.
Paltrow let her festive top take center stage, but that doesn’t mean the rest of her outfit was understated. She paired it with a below-the-knee skirt with an asymmetrical slit, and added a pair of pointy-toe black pumps and a well-loved black Hermès Birkin.
Paltrow later changed out of her feathered top and skirt into something a bit more businesslike: a pair of slate-gray trousers and a matching turtleneck, styled with burgundy pumps, Wayfarer sunglasses, and a black double-breasted leather jacket (also Michael Kors). While it could also work for an office holiday party, it would also work just as well at Thanksgiving.
Whether it’s adding a little sparkle or feathers to your New Year’s Eve outfit, or dressing up a comfy Thanksgiving ’fit with a statement leather jacket, Gwyneth Paltrow has all the answers to our holiday dressing woes.
