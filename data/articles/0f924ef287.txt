Quinta Brunson subtly brought extra shine to a minimalist look at the 2026 LACMA Art+Film Gala in Los Angeles on Saturday with a pair of Giuseppe Zanotti heels.
The “Abbott Elementary” creator showcased Giuseppe Zanotti’s Intriigo Mule Jewelry 105 style. The open-toe mule design is finished in black patent leather, with the silhouette featuring a towering 105 mm stiletto heel, two thin straps reaching across the foot for a barely-there look, and a pointed toe in front.
The pointed sole features a subtly glamorous cap-toe moment; made from golden metal, the understated jewelry detail is set with crystal-colored baguette and carré rhinestones. Part of Giuseppe Zanotti’s Intriigo line featuring other pointed-toe silhouettes, the Intriigo Jewelry Mule 105 is marked at a price of $1,095.

Brunson paired her Giuseppe Zanotti heels with a black halter neck dress featuring a low, draped neckline and a fitted silhouette. She also modeled a layered silver necklace look and wavy copper hair.
The Los Angeles County Museum of Art’s 15th annual Art+Film Gala brought Hollywood and the art world together on Saturday, honoring artist Vija Celmins and “Dune” director Denis Villeneuve. Leonardo DiCaprio and LACMA trustee Eva Chow returned as co-chairs, while Gucci continued its presenting sponsorship, a role it has held since the gala’s 2011 debut. Blackpink’s Rosé was tapped to perform. Proceeds support the museum’s film programming, exhibitions and educational initiatives.
