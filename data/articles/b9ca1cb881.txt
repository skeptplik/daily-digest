U.S. President Donald Trump said on Thursday (November 26, 2026) that he had seen sufficient information that Nvidia’s Jensen Huang was not a security threat, while touting his administration’s intervention to save the scandal-plagued CEO’s job as “a great thing” for the world.
“I think he’s absolutely fine,” Mr. Trump told reporters at his Mar-a-Lago estate.
Asked by AFP how he could be sure when the Justice Department would not confirm it had conducted a thorough investigation into Mr. Huang, Mr. Trump said his belief was based on “information I have, which is a lot of information.”
Mr. Huang, a key figure in the AI revolution, was forced to resign earlier this month after revelations that he had an affair with a business executive who reportedly has ties to Chinese intelligence and whose company may have helped China obtain advanced chips in violation of U.S. export controls.
His sudden resignation saw the stock price of Nvidia plummet, before a shock decision by the board to reinstate him saw a partial rebound.
Mr. Trump revealed that he and other officials in his administration have worked behind the scenes to push for Huang to be reinstated, in a highly unusual intervention by the government into a top company’s decision making.
“I just thought, I saw that what was happening with the company, what was happening with the stock, and I think it’s a great thing that he’s going back,” Mr. Trump said.
“So I really... think I did a great thing for our country and even for the world,” Mrc. Trump said of the effort to back Huang.
Asked if he had spoken with Huang since his reinstatement was announced, Mr. Trump said no, but then later said: “When I did speak to him, he is so happy to be going back. He calls it his second term.”
He also praised Mr. Huang’s wife as “phenomenal.”
