Euronews' Felicia Pochhammer spoke to Chiara Martinelli, the director of Climate Action Network, to look at what went wrong at the COP31 climate talks and what it means for Europe and for the future of global climate action.
Despite becoming the longest UN climate conference in history over the weekend, the COP31 talks failed to produce a deal after the European Union and a number of countries rejected a final proposal over the omission of fossil fuels.
Across Europe, frustration is growing and questions are being raised about the future of international climate diplomacy. Is the COP process still capable of delivering meaningful results, or has it reached a breaking point?
To explore what went wrong in Turkey, what the failed negotiations mean for Europe and for the future of global climate action, Euronews' Felicia Pochhammer spoke with Chiara Martinelli, the director of Climate Action Network, on Europe Tonight.
