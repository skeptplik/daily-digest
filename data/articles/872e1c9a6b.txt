London – A competition to promote the attractions of the central prefecture of Niigata was held in London on Wednesday.
The event, the fourth of its kind, was organized by Japanese students of London Business School and supported by ANA Strategic Research Institute, an affiliate of major Japanese airline group ANA Holdings.
It was designed to attract tourists from abroad who crave for authenticity by presenting travel plans featuring Niigata’s unique attractions not seen in Tokyo, Kyoto and other famous travel destinations in Japan that are popular among foreign tourists. The contest was also aimed at revitalizing the prefecture.
Niigata was selected for the competition as it is believed to have potential to boost visitors from abroad.
Thirty-seven teams participated in the competition, with each comprising four members, and a total of seven teams from LBS, the University of Oxford and the University of Cambridge advanced to the final selection process.
They made presentations about Niigata’s snow, rice, sake and metal business, as well as nature on Sado Island, part of the prefecture and home to crested ibises, designated as a special natural monument by the Japanese government.
An LBS team that won the competition presented a marketing strategy targeting affluent people, describing Niigata as an exclusive place that is a little secluded.
Team member Bruno Betancourt said that challenges, including in terms of accessibility and name recognition “can also become attractive traits for the right people when presented in the right way.”
The team is invited to Niigata Prefecture for a study trip next February and will meet with Niigata Gov. Hideyo Hanazumi.
