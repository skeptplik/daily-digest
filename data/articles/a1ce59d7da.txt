Two months after her marriage to Cameron Fuller, Phoebe Dynevor’s wedding ring has finally been seen out on the town. The actor gave us our first glimpse of the stack on her ring finger while stepping out for coffee in London’s Notting Hill neighborhood—and it proved to be a true classic.
Since her wedding in France this August, Dynevor has worn a simple gold band paired below her gold solitaire oval engagement ring, which features a slender bezel setting. Rather than choosing a style embellished with micro-pavé gemstones or a large eternity ring wrapped with diamonds, the minimal band proves you can let the center stone of your engagement ring do the talking.
Just because the wedding band is simple doesn’t mean Dynevor’s stack is boring, though. Her oval-cut diamond likely sits around three carats, taking up some major real estate on the Bridgerton star’s slender finger. And while the gold band isn’t flashy, the way it pairs with her ring makes it feel modern. Since the diamond on her engagement ring has a low-profile setting, it makes it difficult for another band to sit flush beneath the stone. So rather than tucking underneath the diamond, the band rests at the edge of the stone and leaves some negative space free on Dynevor’s finger. Paired with the engagement ring’s bezel setting—which also offers protection to the diamond—the two rings together lend the stone a floating effect.
Dynevor isn’t the only famous bride who has opted for a simpler gold wedding band as of late. Zendaya and her new husband Tom Holland have each stepped out wearing the minimal ring style since their nuptials, and Anne Hathaway also wears one underneath her large emerald-cut diamond engagement ring. As thicker bands and unique settings have become trendier for engagement rings this past year, we’ve also seen many couples choosing to stick with gold bands to balance out these styles.
The style is a classic for a reason. First, the timeless band remains true to the original symbolism of a wedding ring, whose circular shape represents eternity, unbroken commitment, and mutual fidelity. It’s also an excellent choice for those who lean towards practicality. A simple gold band requires very little maintenance, doesn’t risk losing any stones from improper care, and is easy to wear at all times without needing to remove it. Many people also choose to have gold bands as a spacer ring, which can be worn alongside a more elaborate eternity band given for an anniversary gift or push present down the line.
Engagement and wedding ring trends have changed dramatically in recent years. Lab-grown diamonds are now so popular that many to-be-weds have been looking towards bold styles to stand out, from modern, off-center settings to colorful gemstones and antique jewelry pieces. Phoebe Dynevor’s wedding ring might be a sign that amid the search for originality, you shouldn’t ignore the classics.
