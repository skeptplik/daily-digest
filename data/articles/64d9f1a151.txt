The ABC has projected the Coalition will win the Victorian election, ending Labor’s 12 year run of government in the state.
The national broadcaster’s election and data analyst, Casey Briggs, called the election for the Liberal and National partners at 7.40pm on Saturday evening. .
While it is unclear whether the party will govern in minority or majority, Briggs said there was no pathway for Labor to win a fourth term.
It means the Liberal leader, Jess Wilson, will become premier. At 36, she will be Victoria’s youngest leader since federation and the first woman to be popularly elected as the state’s premier – a feat neither Jacinta Allan nor Joan Kirner achieved.
The deputy Labor leader, Gabrielle Williams, said she was “deeply saddened” by the result but there was a responsibility for the party to learn from it.
“Clearly we have to be deeply humbled by the results and take the lessons from them,” Williams said.
“We need clearly to take the lessons from the result tonight. But I would go further and say we actually need to go back and have a look at the lessons from 2022 and where those warnings really began to emerge.”
The Labor leader, Ben Carroll, is also facing a swing away from him in seat of Niddrie, in Melbourne’s northwest, which the party has held for its entire 50-year history.
