Despite its somewhat muted critical reception, Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 has predictably topped the US video game charts in October. The Infinity Ward-developed shooter was October's best-selling game in the US across digital and retail, beating the month's other big-hitters, including Gears of War: E-Day and Star Wars: Galactic Racer.
That's according to market analysis firm Circana's latest US market report, which revealed Modern Warfare 4 was October's leader in dollar sales. This marks the 19th-year running that a Call of Duty game topped the charts in its release month, a streak that began with November 2008's COD: World at War.
However, despite a successful October, Modern Warfare 4 hasn't managed to land in Circana's top ten list of year-to-date biggest sellers. In fact, the only COD title on the list is the Black Ops 2 port released this summer. NBA 2K27 continues to lead Circana's ranking, having overtaken Resident Evil: Requiem last month to secure the top spot. It's a reverse of Circana's September report, when NBA 2K27 failed to lead its own debut month but claimed the top spot in the year-to-date list. Here's the list of the top 10 year-to-date:
- NBA 2K27
- Resident Evil: Requiem
- 007 First Light
- MLB: The Show 26
- Crimson Desert
- Call of Duty: Black Ops 2
- Forza Horizon 6
- Tomodachi Life: Living the Dream
- Madden NFL 27
- LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight
Away from Modern Warfare 4, October saw the release of several other well-performing and critically acclaimed games, and six of the top ten were new releases. That hasn't stopped Circana from posting 14 percent lower spending on games compared to October last year. Analyst Mat Piscatella noted much of that dip can be attributed to a big slump in mobile spending, and also because October 2025 was particularly strong, with both Battlefield 6 and Pokémon Legends: Z-A releasing. Here's October's top 10:
- Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4
- Gears of War: E-Day
- Star Wars: Galactic Racer
- EA Sports FC 27
- NBA 2K27
- Final Fantasy Resonance
- Madden NFL 27
- Control: Resonant
- Kingdom Hearts Collection 1 - 3
- Nintendo Switch Sports Resort
In Eurogamer's Modern Warfare 4 review, Dom Peppiatt awarded it four stars, writing: "Modern Warfare 4 represents the highest tide for Call of Duty in seven years. Infinity Ward needed to pull off a balancing act I don't envy; injecting just enough nostalgia into a series propped up with so many conventions and expectations there's barely room for anything new, alongside enough innovation and intrigue to keep people interested."
