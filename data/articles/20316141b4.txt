In Europe, five cities made the top 10 for expats – including three in Spain.
Earlier this year, Panama was named by InterNations as the best country for expats in 2026.
Now, the global expat community platform has named its top cities for those moving abroad – and Ras Al Khaimah in the UAE has come out on top.
The ranking is based on a survey of 7,786 expats living around the globe who were quizzed on things like the friendliness of the local population and availability of online admin services.
Asked to rank their satisfaction in each category out of seven, these were then used to judge cities against indices including personal finance, ease of settling in, quality of life, expat essentials and working abroad.
Ras Al Khaimah came out on top for ease of settling in, working abroad, and expat essentials, and was second for personal finance. Of those polled, 84% of expats said they are happy with life in RAK, versus 70% globally. Nearly a quarter (24%) said they were very happy.
The city, which is around an hour’s drive north of Dubai, is growing in popularity thanks to its accessibility to nature, with an abundance of hiking trails as well as beaches nearby.
The emirate is also undergoing a transformation with an influx of luxury developments, with the UAE’s first casino resort set to open here in 2027.
The best European cities for expats
As for Europe, five cities made it into the top 10 – three of which are in Spain.
Málaga placed third, coming out number one for happiness, with 86% saying they are happy with their life, 42% of which are completely happy.
It’s no wonder then that 51% of those polled planned to stay forever, compared with just 27% globally.
The Andalusian city came out third for personal finance, with 73% of people rating the cost of living positively, while it also came fifth for quality of life.
Next up is Lisbon, which is regularly ranked among the top places to live abroad, placing sixth overall.
The Portuguese capital came out fifth on both the personal finance and ease of settling in indices, as well as 10th for quality of life.
Next up is Madrid, which was eighth, Valencia in ninth, and The Hague in the Netherlands rounding out the top 10.
Valencia was ranked first for quality of life, the fourth year in a row.
The best cities for expats in 2026
- 1. Ras Al Khaimah, UAE
- 2. Mexico City, Mexico
- 3. Málaga, Spain
- 4. Dubai, UAE
- 5. Abu Dhabi, UAE
- 6. Lisbon, Portugal
- 7. Singapore
- 8. Madrid, Spain
- 9. Valencia, Spain
- 10. The Hague, Netherlands
The worst European cities for expats in 2026
It’s not good news for those considering Germany as their next move, as all four cities included in the ranking placed in the bottom 10, with Berlin at the very bottom.
It should be noted that to be included in the study, at least 50 people had to answer the survey – with 33 cities making the cut this year.
The German cities ranked poorly for housing, language, and digital life.
Istanbul, which came 30th, did poorly because of its salaries and job security, as well as the working culture.
Zürich offers a good quality of life, placing 16th, but came last in the settling in index. London came 31st overall for both quality of life and personal finance.
