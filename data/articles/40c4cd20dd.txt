Rockstar just issued a new patch for GTA 6 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S, targeting a few issues players have been reporting since the game launched last Thursday (19th November).
The 23rd November patch for the game deploys seven major fixes for the game, including a pretty significant bug that prevented phone calls not triggering at certain points, resulting in a scenario where you could be locked out of progressing the game. The official update is titled 'version 01.002.002', and its weighs in at different sizes on different consoles: it's 603.8 MB on PS5, and 1.6 GB on Xbox Series X/S.
Thanks to an update on the Rockstar website, you can read all of the updates that arrive with the latest pathc below.
- Fixed several issues that caused the game to crash
- Fixed an issue that resulted in the weather not changing for extended periods
- Fixed an issue that resulted in the game not autosaving correctly
- Fixed several issues that resulted phone calls not triggering correctly including those that progressed the story or unlocked mission content
- Fixed an issue that resulted in the players being unable to lose their Wanted Level
- Fixed an issue that resulted in the player being unable to use the deadlift bar in the Gym
- Fixed that resulted in partners not handing over bags so that players could sell them to fences
There have been a few fairly high-profile cases of the autosave not working as the developer intended, leading to a few cases of streamers losing plenty of real-world time thanks to unpredictable auto-save behaviour. JasonTheWeen lost 12 hours of progress on the PS5, for example.
But there's one issue here the developer hasn't addressed: vignetting. Plenty of players have taken to online communities to complain about the vignette effect that pops into the game - theoretically to help with motion smoothing and various grapihcal elements as the game runs in 30FPS. THe effect is worsened when you're fatigued, hungry, or have some other status effect, but it is present quite a lot of the time, too. Most of the responses to a tweet about the patch posted by the Rockstar Support account reference the vignetting in GTA 6, and the developer has issued no response at the time of writing.
