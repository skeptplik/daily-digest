Fall is finally here! If you’re wondering what to wear to embrace the drop in temperatures, we’re here to simplify matters with outfit ideas, courtesy of our favorite celebrities spotted on the streets. Consider this week’s edit your guide to everything from transitional fall looks to clever layering techniques, all infused with plenty of off-duty flair.
Vogue’s Favorite Celeb-Inspired Finds of the Week:
This week, celebrities embraced the seasonal chill, stepping out in their fall outerwear best. Jennifer Lawrence wore Auralee’s navy barn jacket for a walk in New York, styled with a graphic T-shirt, red pants, and a plaid button-down tied around her waist. Naomi Watts chose a double-breasted pinstripe blazer over a crisp white T-shirt and relaxed denim, finishing the look with playful accessories, including a floral brooch, an oversized-buckle belt, and a printed silk scarf. Both looks reinforced how a classic topper is truly the easiest way to pull together every fall outfit.
Meanwhile, guests at the CFDA Awards in New York offered a glimpse of what’s to come for occasion dressing as the holiday season approaches. Alexa Chung arrived in Tory Burch’s velvet robe dress and leather pumps in mustard tones, accessorizing with Elsa Peretti for Tiffany & Co. jewelry for a decadent finish. Hailey Bieber, too, embraced a gilded approach, leaning into the rich brocades and sumptuous embellishments seen on the spring 2027 runway. Her Saint Laurent skirt set, trimmed with faux-fur cuffs, was accented simply with strappy metallic heels for a touch of extra shine. Gold is having a moment, and there's no better time to embrace a little evening opulence for upcoming weddings, cocktail events, or festive celebrations.
