Trump’s first move after midterms? Ensuring future presidents can’t change his Oval Office
It was the third day this week that the US president, a real estate mogul, delivered remarks fixated on his pet projects in Washington
The proclamation was Trump’s first official action after his party was swept aside in midterm elections on Tuesday.
Sitting on the Resolute Desk as he signed the document was a red baseball cap with “VETO” on the front, a not-so-subtle nod to the shifting of powers in Washington. But when he gathered reporters, it was to talk about his effort to keep the Oval Office as it is.
