US midterms: Democrat Colom defeats Hyde-Smith in Mississippi to win historic Senate seat
The stunning election of the state’s first black senator since 1874 gives the Democrats a 55-44 advantage in the chamber
Democratic challenger Scott Colom defeated incumbent Republican Cindy Hyde-Smith in the Mississippi election for the US Senate, media projected on Wednesday, making him the Southern state’s first black senator since the Reconstruction era that followed the US Civil War.
The result was a stunning upset in the heavily Republican state and expanded on the national drubbing the Democrats delivered to President Donald Trump’s Republicans in the November 3 midterm elections.
The Mississippi victory gives the Democrats a 55-44 advantage in the 100-member Senate, with the winner in one Senate race from last week’s election – in Alaska – yet to be determined.
