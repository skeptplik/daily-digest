Au revoir, Emily! Darren Star’s frothy Netflix romp will be ending with its sixth season. Here’s everything we know so far.
Is there a trailer for Emily in Paris Season 6?
Yes—watch the first official teaser here.
So, what will the plot of Emily in Paris Season 6 be?
With Emily now back in Paris, everything’s to play for. Will she answer Gabriel’s (Lucas Bravo) postcard and head to Greece? (It’s looking like it, yes!) Now that she’s broken up with Marcello (Eugenio Franceschini) for good, is that prior relationship now the endgame? Will Mindy (Ashley Park) actually marry Nico (Paul Forman)? If not, will she at least have a Big Fat Greek bachelorette in Mykonos? (The answer to this also seems to be yes, per the first stills and teaser.) And after that, will she just end up getting back together with Alfie (Lucien Laviscount)? And on the work front, what will become of Sylvie’s (Philippine Leroy-Beaulieu) beloved Agence Grateau now that Princess Jane (Minnie Driver) has moved in?
The official log line for the next season reads: “Stepping into a new era of ambition and romance, Emily faces defining choices that test her ultimate loyalties. But as unexpected career conflicts and shifting relationships force her to reevaluate where her true future lies, she must make one final choice between her career, her heart, and the life she’s built in Paris.”
“I wanted the final season to feel like the biggest Emily in Paris experience yet,” Star added in a statement. “We went to multiple locations this season, and Greece was somewhere we really wanted to showcase in a spectacular way. That’s part of the fantasy of Europe: you’re always within reach of another extraordinary place, another culture, another adventure. I wanted the audience to have that vicarious experience of traveling right along with Emily.” We can hardly wait.
Who will be in the cast of Emily in Paris Season 6?
After the fifth season finale, we know that the aforementioned Driver is sticking around, as well as the rest of the ensemble: Samuel Arnold (Julien), Bruno Gouery (Luc), and William Abadie (Antoine Lambert).
When is the release date for Emily in Paris Season 6?
Netflix has now revealed that the final season will be arriving sooner than we might have expected: on December 24, to be precise. Mark your calendars now.
