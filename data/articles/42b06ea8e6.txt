Malaysia’s halal chicken scandal exposes hygiene breaches at filthy plant
A raid at the Puchong facility that operated without certification revealed slaughtering breaches, sparking calls for stricter oversight
The hygiene failures at the Puchong facility came to light after authorities seized more than 6.5 tonnes of chicken during a raid last week.
The factory, which processes more than 40,000 chickens daily and supplies businesses across the Klang Valley in Kuala Lumpur, is accused of killing the birds by electric shocks before slaughter, breaching Islamic requirements.
