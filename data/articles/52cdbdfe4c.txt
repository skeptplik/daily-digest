After 13 years and an estimated $1 billion or more in production costs, “Grand Theft Auto VI” began rolling out across the globe on Thursday, gaining rapturous early reviews for the most anticipated video game release in history.
The game, which could bring in billions of dollars for its developer, Take-Two Interactive-owned studio Rockstar Games, raced to the top of Metacritic’s all-time gaming leaderboard with a score of 99, a mark matched by only one other title, Nintendo’s 1998 classic “The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time“.
“GTA VI is popping off. It is the best game that’s ever been made and we’re only 20 minutes deep,” said 33-year-old Sydney gamer Asif Mir as he started to play and livestream in his gaming room just after midnight. “I cannot wait to go 12 hours, 10 hours until tomorrow morning. I ain’t getting any sleep. Let’s go, man!”
