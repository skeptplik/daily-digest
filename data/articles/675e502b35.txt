Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 led US sales in October according to Circana, marking the 19th consecutive year a Call of Duty title has been the best-selling game during its launch month.
The game, which was generally well received by critics, beat two other new releases in October's Top 5 – Gears of War: E-Day in second and Star Wars: Galactic Racer in third.
There were six new releases overall, including Final Fantasy Resonance (No.6), Kingdom Hearts Collection 2 – 3 (No.9) and Nintendo Switch Sports Resort (No.10).
Control Resonant moved into the top 10, from No.11 to No.8, following its physical release on October 15. The game launched digitally on September 24, and has become Remedy's fastest-selling title to date.
Total US video game spending declined 12% in October to $4.2 billion. Circana attributed this to a 14% drop in content sales to $3.7 billion, primarily due to a 16% decrease in mobile.
Console content spending fell 18% year-on-year, reflecting the impact of major releases Battlefield 6 and Pokémon Legends: Z-A in the previous period.
Hardware spending increased 2% year-on-year to $357 million, with 42% growth in Xbox Series sales and 20% growth in PlayStation 5. These gains offset an 11% drop in Nintendo Switch 2 sales.
Circana attributed PS5 and Xbox Series growth to higher prices. PS5 unit sales increased 5% with a 13% rise in average selling price. Xbox Series unit sales grew 10%, and its average selling price rose 29%.
Switch 2 led hardware in both unit and dollar sales for October and year-to-date 2026, with PS5 ranking second.
