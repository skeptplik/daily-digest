Hundreds of thousands of Ukrainians were left without electricity on Sunday following Russian airstrikes targeting the country's energy infrastructure. Moscow has intensified its attacks as winter approaches, leaving residents in Kyiv and other parts of the country struggling with power cuts as temperatures continue to drop. Clemence Waller reports.
