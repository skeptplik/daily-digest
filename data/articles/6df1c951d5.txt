GTA 6 preorders are from archetypal, high-spending console gamers, overwhelmingly male, and most have a history of spending on the franchise, according to data from Newzoo shared exclusively with GamesIndustry.biz ahead of the game's final release on Thursday.
While the majority were regular game buyers, 16% of them hadn't purchased a full-price game in the last 12 months - suggesting the title is bringing back lapsed players - and the firm projected that while the launch would temporarily draw playtime and spending away from other titles, it would ultimately drive additional demand. .
The news follows the firm's previous forecast that the title would be the single largest contributor to a 17.5% increase in console full-game spending in 2026, and without it the console market would be expected to decline year-over-year.
Addressing the widely-held concerns that the game will damage other releases – which it said had lead to only two AAA releases between October 8 and November 18, compared to ten in 2025 and eight in 2024 – Director of Market Intelligence Emmanuel Rosier anticipated a short-term impact that would be followed by additional demand. "Some players are likely to stretch their gaming budgets and buy it on top of what they’d normally spend in a year," he said.
52% of people who pre-ordered GTA 6 have spent money on GTA 5 or GTA Online since 2018, the firm said. Across all people who pre-ordered, at least 37% have spent on GTA Online and 31% on the GTA V full game, with around one in six having done both - Rosier classified those figures as a minimum, as the firm does not have data going back as far as the GTA 5 launch in 2013.
The buyer profile closely matches conventional buyers of premium games, the firm said. 56% of those pre-ordering are under 35, compared with 55% of all premium game buyers, and 22% are 45 or older, compared with 23%. There was a sharp difference in gender, though: while women made up 27% of all premium game buyers during the pre-order period, under 15% of the people pre-ordering GTA 6 were women.
This exceeds even the franchise's previous history of a male-dominated audience; Newzoo data shows women accounting for 22% of GTA 5 game buyers since 2018. The difference was most pronounced among younger demographics, while women made up 21% of pre-orders in the 45-54 demographic. Rosier said that the firm would "expect launch buyers to be a broader group," given that "players who pre-order tend to be the most committed fans."
Newzoo found that most GTA 6 pre-order customers regularly buy premium games, with 84% purchasing at least one other premium title in the 12 months previously. The typical person pre-ordering the game spent $129 across three other premium games, and a quarter spent more than $290, not including any in-game spending.
The remaining 16% of those pre-ordering the game had not purchased a full game in the preceding 12 months, and for 3% of them the firm had no evidence of a premium purchase at all, indicating no games had been purchased since at least 2018. "For those players, GTA 6 looks like genuinely new spending," said Rosier, "because it’s bringing lapsed and occasional buyers back."
The firm expected that the game's launch will "encourage some players to upgrade to the current console generation" – while noting that 85% of current GTA 5 players have already done so – and said this would expand the audience for other titles, as its data indicated that current-generation console players typically play a much wider range of games than those using previous hardware.
"We expect that effect to build gradually," said Rosier. "GTA 6 launches late in the year, so much of the benefit is likely to come in 2027 rather than 2026. Even after accounting for the pressure on competing titles, our view is that the net effect will be positive. GTA 6 will grow the market rather than simply redistribute it."
Console launch limits global reach
The firm said that GTA 6's console-only launch would limit its reach, pointing to its previous data suggesting that console would only represent 22% of global games revenue in 2026.
"While GTA 6 is undoubtedly one of the most important releases of the decade, its initial impact will be concentrated in Western console," said Rosier. "That’s still a big market, which we forecast will be worth $36.3 billion in 2026, but it’s only part of the industry. The longer-term global story will depend on what happens next, particularly on PC and online."
"The real test of GTA VI’s global reach may come with the unconfirmed PC launch. PC is far bigger than console in several major markets, including China, and a PC release could expose the game to a much broader international audience."
