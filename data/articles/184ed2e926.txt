Seven people have been injured - four of them critically - after an explosion at an oil refinery in Bangkok, local government officials have said.
A fire broke out after the blast at the Phra Khanong facility run by energy giant Bangchak Corporation in the east of Thailand's capital at around 14:45 (07:45 GMT).
The company said the fire was brought under control and put out within 30 minutes, adding that the cause of the incident was being investigated.
Black smoke could be seen across Bangkok's skyline after the blast, while officials told residents to leave the area.
A alert sent to mobile phones by Bangkok authorities confirmed the fire was under control, but urged people to avoid the location.
Eyewitnesses shared video clips on social media of flames and smoke rising above the refinery.
The city-run Erawan Emergency Medical Centre said it was treating four "red" cases and three "yellow" - less severe - cases.
Bangchak Corporation said it had shut down production at the site, which can process up to 120,000 barrels of oil a day. The company has a second refinery in Chonburi province, about 50 miles (80km) from Bangkok, handling a capacity of about 180,000 barrels daily.
An explosion occurred at the Bangkok refinery in July 2012, sparking a fire but no reported injuries.
