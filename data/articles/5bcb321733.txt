Working alongside people who shop for a living has its advantages, not least of which is having an exceptionally stylish sounding board for my own prospective purchases. Collectively, we’ve dedicated hundreds upon hundreds of hours to navigating the ever-changing shopping landscape (some of us have been doing this professionally for over a decade), attended countless fashion shows, and taken notes from the streets of London, New York, and Paris.
And yet, despite our collective fashion expertise, our Black Friday shopping lists are refreshingly different.
Tim Chan, our VP of Commerce, is making a case for the life-changing comforts of Uggs. I’m revisiting an old flame in the form of COS, the Scandinavian-born fashion label that stole my heart during my years studying fashion in London, long before shopping became my profession. Meanwhile, our Managing Editor of Commerce, Oscar Hartzog, whose personal style runs the gamut from laid-back California boy to impeccably tailored city dweller, knows the value of a perfectly fitting pair of Levi’s.
What makes this particular edit interesting is that our selections aren’t solely informed by what’s trending or which retailer is offering the most generous discount. They’re informed by years of getting dressed, developing our own sartorial identities, and professionally scrutinizing the very things we spend our money on.
So, in the spirit of sharing good taste (and even better deals), I’ve compiled the Black Friday fashion finds my colleagues and I have deemed worthy of our own shopping carts.
Levi’s Men’s 501 Original Shrink-to-Fit Non-Stretch Jeans
I’ve been on the hunt for good, affordable non-stretch jeans. These Shrink-to-Fit 501s are the most affordable option I’ve seen with a sale price of $42 at Macy’s. They feature a rigid, classic feel and come in some very cool colors, so I might end up buying a couple pairs. – Oscar Hartzog
Vans Classic Slip-On Shoe
It’s probably nostalgia for my childhood surfing and skating in California, but Vans Classic Slip-Ons have been on my mind. For Black Friday, Vans is offering 30% off the iconic skate shoe (plus a bunch of other sneakers). – O.H.
