It’s that time of the year again. Heading into the holiday season, Gigi Hadid broke out her cozy winter layers—bundling up in browns for a late-November walk in New York City.
For the outing, the model played with contrasts, pairing a structured topcoat with slouchy boots. The outerwear, courtesy of Helsa, was crafted from deep-chocolate wool with sharp, sculpted shoulder pads and wide peak lapels that added a distinctly formal feel to the piece.
The width of the top half was only exaggerated further by a tapered waist, which was tailored with darting for a snug fit. At the hips, two flap pockets were stitched into the fabric.
Underneath, Hadid wore a baby-blue crewneck knit with rolled edges, and a pair of black sweatpants that were mostly hidden by her coat. Her shoes, the aforementioned slouchy pair, were Miu Miu’s take on workwear boots, with worn-in leather and a relaxed (though sturdy) construction.
For an extra bit of warmth, she added a latte-brown brushed cashmere beanie from her Guest in Residence label. The piece was reversible, and at one point during the day, she turned it inside out to reveal a striped pattern in shades of cream, light blue, and brown.
On her ring finger, Hadid wore her diamond-dotted Boucheron wedding band. The model was first seen wearing the ring in early August, during a trip to Paris with partner Bradley Cooper. He wore a similar band from the same jeweler, and both were reportedly purchased as promise rings, representing the couple’s commitment to one another.
