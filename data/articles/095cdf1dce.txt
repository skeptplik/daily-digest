Prime Minister Narendra Modi took part in three back-to-back multilateral meetings on the side-lines of the annual Association of South East Asian Nations (ASEAN) summits in Manila this week. The summits, including the ASEAN-India Summit, East Asia Summit and then the Quad Summit along with U.S. President Donald Trump, Japanese PM Sanae Takaichi and Australian PM Anthony Alabanese ended with several concrete announcements. They also sent deeper underlying message on India’s broad-basing its ‘Act East’ and Indo-Pacific policies, even as the world turned its attention to the Trump-Xi-Putin trilateral later in the week.
In 2025, PM Modi had pulled out of attending the ASEAN Summits in Kuala Lumpur due to what was seen as a combination of domestic commitments around Diwali and trade friction with U.S. President Donald Trump. Ties with the Philippines have improved dramatically since President Bongbong Marcos’ visit to Delhi in August 2025, and hence it was doubly important for PM Modi to attend the two conferences hosted by him- the ASEAN- India summit, which includes Brunei, Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Timor-Leste, and Vietnam, as well as the East Asia Summit, which brings in U.S., Russia, China, Japan and South Korea. In addition, the invitation to the Quad Summit hosted by the U.S. and being held for the first time in Mr. Trump’s turbulent presidency was important to fit in, given concerns over his commitment to the Indo-Pacific grouping, and PM Modi ended his day with that.
