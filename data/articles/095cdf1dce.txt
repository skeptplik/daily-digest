Prime Minister Narendra Modi took part in three back-to-back multilateral meetings on the sidelines of the annual Association of South East Asian Nations (ASEAN) summits in Manila this week. The ASEAN-India Summit, East Asia Summit, and Quad Summit, which was attended by U.S. President Donald Trump, Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi, and Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese apart from Mr. Modi, ended with several concrete announcements. They sent a deeper underlying message on India’s broad-basing of its “Act East” and Indo-Pacific policies, even as the world turned its attention to the Trump-Xi-Putin trilateral later in the week.
In 2025, Mr. Modi skipped the ASEAN summits in Kuala Lumpur over what was seen as a combination of domestic commitments around Deepavali and trade friction with U.S. President Donald Trump.
