By now, it’s well-documented that Emma Corrin is a true red carpet star. The actor and their right-hand stylist Harry Lambert are a true match made in heaven. Together, they always find looks that are forward-thinking, intriguing, and slightly unconventional. (Remember Corrin’s pantless Miu Miu look at the Venice Film Festival, for one?)
This month, Corrin is continuing that energy throughout the Pride and Prejudice press tour. The Netflix miniseries reboot sees Corrin portray Elizabeth Bennet, premiering December 3. For their latest fashion serve-upon-serve, Corrin has been focusing in on the classic suit, finding fresh ways to twist and subvert it. Over the course of the past month, the star has stepped out in a few different takes on the tailored two-piece, each time finding a new detail or beat.
Things kicked off at a Pride and Prejudice fan Q&A event at the British Library earlier this month. Corrin emerged wearing a Chanel suit from Matthieu Blazy’s spring 2027 collection. The tailored checkered jacket and trouser felt fresh, thanks to the whimsical details like the playing card-themed buttons including squared heart, spade, clover, and diamond decals.
For an appearance at the Heart Breakfast Radio Studios yesterday, Corrin followed up the kitschy suit with another win—this time, by British designer Talia Byre, from her spring 2027 collection. The slightly shrunken, high-buttoned blazer felt very reminiscent of the ’90s, when the cuts were slim and the minimalist black suit reigned supreme. Three decades later, the look still feels modern.
And just today, Corrin broke away from the timeless suit but kept things streamlined in a long, sharp-shouldered black overcoat and white flared jeans. The peek of a studded belt? The hint of a fierce pointed pump? Chef’s kiss. Corrin’s approach to the dressy and professional is proof that you can suit up without looking like a total corporate drone. If Emma Corrin worked in a fluorescent-lit cubicle, they’d definitely be the coolest person in the office.
