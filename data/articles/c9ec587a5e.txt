Euronews’ Evening Show Europe Tonight was joined by María Fernanda Espinosa, UN Secretary-General candidate and former UN General Assembly President, to debrief COP31 talks.
As the COP31 climate conference enters its final stretch, negotiators from more than 200 countries are racing to reach a contentious deal ahead of Friday's deadline. Tensions have emerged over climate financing and the phase out of fossil fuels.
To debrief, Euronews' evening show Europe Tonight was joined by María Fernanda Espinosa, UN Secretary-General candidate and former UN General Assembly President.
