Along with cooler temperatures and shorter days, colorful cues signal the holiday season drawing close: silver bells, white ribbons, and evergreen trees are obvious signs of the most festive time of the year. But two more hues make the moment more merry and bright: gold jewelry in Tiffany blue gift boxes. Both have been hallmarks of the heritage brand since its earliest days. The same brilliant robin’s egg blue tone adorned the cover of the first Tiffany Blue Book, a catalog of the quintessential American jeweler’s most exceptional items, beginning in 1845. From historic French crown jewels to modern cuffs by famed designers, gold jewelry has been indispensable to the Tiffany identity then and now.
