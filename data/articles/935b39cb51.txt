Customers reported difficulty accessing the online accounts at some of the U.S.’ largest banks on Monday (November 16, 2026).
Bank of America, Chase, J.P. Morgan, U.S. Bank, Wells Fargo and Zelle were listed by users as down or experiencing issues, according to the website downdetector.com.
Chase Bank’s services are experiencing a technical bug issue, not a cyber issue, a spokesperson said Monday (November 16, 2026).
“Due to a technical issue, some transactions are taking longer than usual to complete but customers still have access to their accounts and funds,” a Chase spokesperson said in a statement. “We are working to resolve this as quickly as possible.”
The reports follow a major outage by Microsoft that began on Friday (November 13, 2026) and lasted for hours, which impacted services including Outlook, Teams and the AI assistant Copilot.
Some Chase customers received a message that said, “Some services aren’t available. We’re working on them. Please try back later.”
