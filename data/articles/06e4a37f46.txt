Stealing the Pegassi Infernus in GTA 6 can be quite tricky, considering it's being held in the police department lockup, the building to the west of the police station in southwest Crosstown.
You can access the building via a yellow window cleaning elevator on the southern side of the building. But this won't appear until Randy texts and gives you the Infernus's location. If you go here before, your options for entering are far more limited and risky, so it's worth waiting. To get to the car:
- Use the elevator to get inside.
- Turn right, then left, and make your way up to the seventh level of the multi-story car park—the purple Pegassi Infernus is easy to recognise since it's the only sports car in there.
- Select the key cloning option to get inside.
Now you just need to drive down a few levels to exit via the police station parking lot—which I'd recommend—or keep going all the way down to exit at the bottom of the car park. Remember, you need to drive fast to stop the tracker going off, and if you hear it beeping, you should speed up to prevent it pinging the cops.
If you do bash around the car a bit, you can get it repaired at a gas station on the way. Bring it back in pristine condition and you'll net 15k for the trouble.
