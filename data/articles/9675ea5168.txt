Another modder has used AI to make a multiplayer mode for The Last of Us Part 2, insisting there isn't much Sony can do about it "even if they do shut me down."
The Last of Us had a brilliant multiplayer mode, with tense gunfights powered by weapon sway, smart cover, and nail-biting in-round crafting that forced you to dig around in your backpack as bullets whizzed over your bonce.
That’s why so many people were excited when Naughty Dog announced a Factions multiplayer game in the works based on The Last of Us Part 2. Naughty Dog, however, eventually realized that making a live-service game would change the studio forever, and cancelled the project to focus instead on its upcoming sci-fi game, Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet.
The Last of Us Part 2 made some brilliant tweaks to the combat, allowing for better mobility and deeper stealth. The feel of the weapons, the crunch of the impacts, the audiovisual spectacle of it all was just something else, but we can only experience it in single-player, officially.
It’s no wonder people want to make Factions happen, even without Sony’s approval. And in September, a promising mod made some big strides towards getting multiplayer working in The Last of Us Part 2. As soon as it started to get traction, however, the mod was sent a cease and desist letter from Sony. “Hey everyone, as you all know, the TLOU 2 Multiplayer mod that has been in development since January was supposed to [be released] this month,” wrote creator 'Speclizer' at the time.
“Unfortunately I recently received a letter on behalf of Sony Interactive Entertainment requesting for the mod not to be released. Extremely sad that this never got to see the light of day but I hope you’ll continue to follow me onto the next project, this time something of my own.”
Now there’s a new mod hoping to avoid the eye of Sauron, and it’s already out. The mod is called Twelve Weeks and was built specifically for The Last of Us Part 2 Remastered on PC, using, its creator has admitted, generative AI. Modder 'Kellie' admitted to using Claude code for most of the game's development, and AI was even used to write the lore dumps you can find in the game. The art was, however, made using Blender.
The Last of Us returns with a new online multiplayer mode called TWELVE WEEKS created for The Last of Us Part 2 Remastered on PC. This free mod is a work in progress allowing players to experience a new version of the beloved Factions multiplayer with tactical survival gameplay https://t.co/2P8Wv4abQb

It features reworked levels — Supply Raid and Interrogation for now — with team-based 4v4 combat, new weapons, and character customization. Players can use gadgets like molotovs and smoke bombs, while fighting against other players as the WLF and Seraphites. The mod even adds brutal execution moves. You can grab Twelve Weeks on Nexus Mods.
However, the mod probably won't last long. The modders have attempted to cover their backs with a repository disclaimer that said: "Twelve Weeks is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Sony Interactive Entertainment or Naughty Dog. The Last of Us is a trademark of its owners. You need your own legitimately purchased copy of the game (Steam). This repository contains no game files and no assets taken from the game."
Will Sony take action? In a reply to someone on YouTube, Kellie said the previous Factions mod got hit by Sony because it was paywalled. "Mine is released already for free and in open source," they said. "Since people have the source code to the full mod already, there ain't much they can do even if they do shut me down."
While The Last of Us Online was canceled, there are hints that Naughty Dog may be ready to return to the series for The Last of Us 3. And then there is HBO's The Last of Us TV series, which is currently shooting its third (and likely last) season.
