This month, PC Gamer delivers world-exclusive access to Metro 2039, the hyper-dark new FPS from 4A Games. And we're pleased to say that PC gamers who have been itching for the next great single-player FPS campaign are not going to be disappointed. This is shaping up to be a graphical powerhouse shooter that combines the best parts of each previous Metro game into a gripping, atmospheric FPS that pulls no punches in terms of action and narrative.
There's another awesome feature in this issue of PC Gamer, too. On the long-running World War II RTS series' 20th anniversary, we speak directly to the developers who are currently looking after it and planning for its future, Relic Entertainment. And, with plenty of insider stories from the series past and present, this is a must-read for fans.
This issue is also loaded with great previews. First up we have our lead BlizzCon 2026 show report, which covers both Diablo 5 as well as the exciting new StarCraft FPS. This is followed by hands-on previews of Masters of Albion, Gundam Rogue Orbit, Guns of Eschaton, Warhammer Blood Bowl, Muramasa: Revenant Blades, and Crown of Hispania, among others.
Then, in terms of reviews, we pass our official judgement on the adrenaline-inducing Star Wars Galactic Racer, as well as Control Resonant, Endless Legend 2, Nighthawks, Valor Mortis, and Silent Hill: Townfall, among others.
Cover feature: Metro 2039

Secondary feature
Company of Heroes
Written by: Leana Hafer
What's in this feature: From its first foray back in 2006, Company of Heroes has developed into one of the genre's most beloved series, with a trilogy of games and various expansions letting gamers live out some explosive, tactically satisfying World War II campaigns. So, on the series landmark 20th anniversary, we speak to Relic Entertainment about Company of Heroes' past, present, and future.
