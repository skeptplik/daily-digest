GTA 6 fans are a bit surprised that the actor playing Jason wasn't nominated at The Game Awards.
Earlier today, The Game Awards gave us our first indicator of how GTA 6 will be received. It's up for eight awards, including Game of the Year. It was a bit odd, as there are no reviews for the game at the moment, but the jury clearly deemed it worthwhile. The other notable thing to come out of the Game Awards nominations was that it confirmed the actress for Lucia: Manni L. Perez. Fans have speculated she was Lucia for years now, but now it's confirmed, as she was nominated for Best Performance.
However, the actor who plays the game's other protagonist was not nominated. Fans were taken aback, since the two are clearly a package deal in the game, and one has to imagine they elevate each other. However, the jury must not have felt strongly enough about Jason to give his actor the nomination. There's no rule against nominating multiple actors for a single project, either. Last year, three performers from Expedition 33 were all nominated in the same category, after all.
Jason has been seen as a much more subtle and reserved character compared to Lucia. He's a bit quieter, isn't too flashy with his actions, and maybe lacks the fire in his character compared to someone like Lucia. One fan on Reddit noted that Jason didn't make a strong impression on them in any of the trailers: "Honestly, I'm not a fan of his VO," they said. "Jason doesn't sound good, at least in the footage we saw; he lacks diction. Most of the time, I can't understand what he's saying without subs."
However, another fan pushed back, noting that other great Rockstar actors have been snubbed in the past: "Benjamin Byron Davis wasn't nominated for his Dutch performance [in Red Dead Redemption 2]," they said. "Didn't take away one bit and [it's] arguably the best performance in the game."
It's certainly a valid point, especially since Rob Wiethoff also wasn't nominated for his role as John Marston in Red Dead Redemption 2, despite being a secondary protagonist. As of right now, we're not sure who is playing Jason, but it won't be long before we find out. GTA 6 will officially go live tomorrow morning in select regions, as the game becomes available in New Zealand. It'll be interesting to see which of the two protagonists fans end up gravitating toward more in the end, but it seems like Lucia may be a favorite based on her nomination.
