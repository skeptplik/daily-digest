For our Weekly Drop Watch column, Fashionista scours the market to curate the most noteworthy releases from our favorite fashion and beauty brands. Keep scrolling for this week’s highlights.
Skiwear brand Halfdays partnered with Gigi Hadid’s knitwear label Guest in Residence on an eight-piece winter capsule collection that includes a ski suit, bib, ski jacket, turtleneck sweater, base layers and accessories. The lineup aims to “[bring] together the warmth and softness of luxury knitwear with the functionality of technical skiwear,” melding each brand’s signatures for a cozy, cold-weather-ready wardrobe. Shop the collab, which is priced from $100 to $795, at guestinresidence.com and halfdays.com.
Etsy tapped whimsical fashion brand Lisa Says Gah to partner with a selection of its award-winning sellers to create a playful home collection based on LSG’s newest drop. The collaboration, dubbed “Etsy Says Gah!,” marks the fashion brand’s first-ever home collection. The pieces span handmade ceramics, crochet pieces and frames featuring Lisa Says Gah’s playful details like swans and horses. Shop them all at etsy.com.
Just in time for Thanksgiving, Gap is reissuing The Happy Stripe, its iconic 100% cotton knit from the 2000 holiday archive, for a limited time. Shop it for $198 starting at noon EST on gap.com.
Alo dropped the latest addition to its handbag line this week, introducing the Mini Serenity bag ($1,050). A compact cylindrical shoulder bag made in pebbled leather, the Mini Serenity was designed in Los Angeles and handcrafted in Italy. It comes in two colorways: Black and Rich Bordeaux. Shop the bag at aloyoga.com.
Fashion label Campbell & Kramer, which is known for its elevated basics made from deadstock fabric, tapped Los Angeles-based celebrity stylist Rachel Bode as guest creative director for a five-piece capsule collection. Designed with the aim of “bringing a fresh perspective to holiday dressing,” the collection incorporates masculine tailoring, feminine silhouettes, cutouts, sculptural details and a “touch of boudoir-inspired dressing.” Shop all the pieces at campbellandkramer.com.
Indie fashion label Jandéqene partnered with fashion content creator Aly Kima for its first-ever collaboration. The resulting design is a two-piece silk set inspired by “Audrey Hepburn, Parisian elegance and timeless femininity,” according to the brand. Available in Ivory (shown above) and Mocha, the collection features the Aly Top ($365), crafted from 100% silk charmeuse with adjustable back ties, and the Chérie Skirt ($525), featuring silk chiffon lining and a flowing asymmetrical hemline. Shop the collab at jandeqene.com.
Madewell partnered with The Six Bells for a holiday collection spanning apparel, accessories, home decor and other gifts. The collection came about because Madewell Creative Director Steven Cateron was drawn to the “distinctive character” of The Six Bells, a country inn inspired by an imagined English village called Barrow’s Green. Together, he and Six Bells Founder Audrey Gelman created a nostalgia-fueled collection that includes heirloom-inspired knits, Edwardian-influenced loungewear and “crafty decorative touches” such as hand-painted candles and needlepoint ornaments. Shop the collab at madewell.com.
Frame unveiled its Winter 2026 collection, inspired by “the quieter moments of the season and the understated ease of winter dressing,” this week. The range of statement outerwear, knits and layering pieces incorporates fair isle, cashmere, heritage plaids, soft wool, distressed leather and relaxed denim. See and shop them all at frame.com.
For the second time, French footwear brand Carel Paris teamed up with Spanish designer David Méndez Alonso’s colorful Outsiders Division label on three versions of Daisy K Mary Jane pumps ($790 each). Each is made in Italy and features Alonso’s playful pop aesthetic with daisies and colorful leather details. Shop the collection at carel-paris.com.
New York-based fashion brand Guizio entered beauty with the launch of Heaven Knows, its debut fragrance. Described as a “warm floral gourmand with a soft, addictive sweetness that is deeply feminine, intimate and magnetic,” the scent features notes of pear, black currant, jasmine, orange blossom, tonka bean, praline and patchouli.
“Fragrance has been a dream of mine for a long time because I’ve always thought about Guizio as more than clothing. To me, it’s a feeling and an entire world you can step into,” said Danielle Guizio, the brand’s founder and creative director, via a press release. A 50mL bottle retails for $104 at guizio.com.
