Everyone has their favorite pair (or pairs!) of jeans, but sometimes coming up with new ways to wear them can stump the best of us. Who better to help with some fresh denim outfit ideas than a few of our favorite street-savvy celebs? From beloved straight-leg jeans to trend-forward indigo washes and relaxed baggy cuts, these celeb-inspired denim outfits are the perfect way to rethink the perennial closet classic.
Vogue’s Must-Have Denim:
Fall dressing is in full effect, so think of these combinations as sartorial cures for the season ahead. Many fashion insiders know that a fail-proof formula starts with blue jeans, a white T-shirt, and standout outerwear. From Zendaya’s cuffed denim and long leather coat to Alexa Chung’s vintage-inspired straight-leg jeans and ladylike wrap coat, and Monica Barbaro’s flared wide-legs and menswear-inspired Pub jacket, these outfit formulas offer plenty of inspiration for reworking the tried-and-true pairing.
Still, we can’t help but look beyond denim’s daytime appeal. Dakota Johnson made a case for clean cropped jeans after dark, wearing hers with a shearling-trimmed jacket, silky champagne-colored camisole, and black ballet flats for an evening out in New York. Meanwhile, Rihanna was spotted in Paris sporting her favorite baggy jeans with a satin evening jacket, hobo bag, and pointy pumps for a dinner date. Both looks strike the perfect high-low balance, proving that denim can feel just as polished after hours as it does by day.
Whether you’re keeping things casual with wardrobe staples or dressing denim up with elevated statement pieces, the possibilities are endless. The trick is all in the styling—finding the right finishing touches to make each silhouette feel like your own.
Below, find ten celeb-inspired denim outfit ideas to give your fall wardrobe a stylish refresh.
Cuffing Season
Zendaya made a case for cuffed denim, wearing a relaxed pair for a day of shopping in Paris. The actress styled the jeans with a simple white baby tee and black leather coat (hers was vintage Prada), keeping the look pared-back and effortless. Maison Margiela Tabi wedges and tiny sunglasses provided a dose of downtown ’90s cool with a distinctly modern edge.
Rinse and Repeat
Katie Holmes recently tapped into one of fall’s biggest denim trends: indigo wash jeans. The actress opted for a barrel-leg pair in a deep rinse wash, balancing the voluminous silhouette with a burgundy leather bomber jacket and striped sweater from Banana Republic’s recent collaboration with Zankov. Soft leather loafers, Toteme’s T-Lock bag, and cat-eye sunglasses completed the city-ready look.
Straight to the Point
Alexa Chung turned to a familiar favorite for a chilly London outing: vintage-inspired straight-leg jeans. Styling the iconic silhouette with Aligne’s gray faux-fur-trimmed wrap coat and a white T-shirt, she reinforced the ladylike feel with black ballet heels and a Louis Vuitton shoulder bag. Oversized wraparound sunnies brought a dose of East London attitude to the look.
Mom On Duty
Jennifer Lawrence loves Mom jeans, often gravitating towards the relaxed silhouette in her everyday life. While out in New York, the actress chose to wear hers with elevated basics, including an oversized navy crewneck sweater, roomy black T-shirt, and brown suede mules. A bold lime-green handbag added a vibrant unexpected pop to the otherwise understated off-duty outfit.
Middle Ground
Sarah Pidgeon kept things classic in Rùadh’s Agnes mid-rise jeans, styling them with a white T-shirt and Aflalo’s black leather coat—repeat favorites within her wardrobe. The actress leaned into a relaxed, understated feel using other reliable go-tos, including black leather loafers and Balenciaga’s Rodeo bag. This one’s worth bookmarking to wear all season long.
Plenty of Flare
Monica Barbaro embraced a classic fall formula, choosing a pair of slightly flared, wide-leg jeans as the base. The actress paired them with Me+Em’s houndstooth Pub jacket as a statement layer, along with a white crewneck T-shirt and pointy black boots. Equally suited to the office or weekend plans, grab a suede tote and minimal jewelry to round out the effortless everyday uniform.
Sky High
Daisy Edgar-Jones gave subtly distressed, high-waisted blue jeans a romantic spin, pairing them with Mango’s white brocade jacket and a simple white tee while on the press tour for Sense & Sensibility. White satin pumps and a black mini shoulder bag brought the look back to the present, balancing the feminine, Regency-coded details with a more contemporary feel.
Crop to It
Dakota Johnson is making a case for cropped denim, stepping out multiple times in recent weeks in clean, ankle-grazing styles. The secret to giving this casual silhouette a dressier feel? A fanciful topper. Take a cue from the actress, who layered a shearling-trimmed Valentino jacket over a satin camisole, finishing with black high-vamp flats and stacks of jewelry for even more luxe detailing.
Room to Move
Baggy jeans are a signature in Rihanna’s wardrobe. For a dinner date in Paris, she dressed up Citizens of Humanity’s wide-leg silhouette with a black satin jacket for a chic evening look. Simply accessorize with Dior’s quilted Toujours bag, pointed black pumps, and a choker necklace for a desk-to-dinner pairing that works no matter where the day takes you.
White Out
Sienna Miller is keeping white jeans in rotation, even as the temperatures turn. The War actress was seen styling a frayed pair with an oversized navy sweater, red bomber jacket, and her favorite high-vamp black flats—an easy way to keep white denim going well into the colder months. Gucci’s Ophidia tote bag and a printed scarf make this feel especially on point for transitional dressing or as a polished travel outfit.
