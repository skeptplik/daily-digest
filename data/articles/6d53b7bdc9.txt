Streaming prices have been going up for years now, with no sign they'll ever come back down. To make matters worse, streaming deals have become increasingly rare, and there's really only one time of year when services offer a discount.
That time has now arrived. Pretty much every service (other than good ol' Netflix) has announced a Black Friday deal for 2026, offering a rare respite from the onslaught of price hikes.
These deals are by far the best way to try out a new service or lock down your favorites for their cheapest price of the year, but some services are being more generous than others. A few are even offering 12-month deals that cover you until the next round of Black Friday discounts.
Black Friday Streaming Deals for 2026
Here’s a breakdown of all the biggest streaming deals live right now for a quick comparison. All deals last until midnight on November 30 (Cyber Monday), and ad-free deals are marked with an asterisk:
Scroll on for a deeper breakdown of our top picks, but the TL;DR is that Disney+ and Hulu are offering the only bundle deal of the year, while Apple TV, Paramount+, and Crunchyroll are splurging with discounts on ad-free plans. Peacock decided to bring back its Black Friday deal after skipping the festivities in 2025, but Netflix is still completely MIA and likely always will be.
Disney+ and Hulu
Disney+ and Hulu have brought back the exact same Black Friday deal they offered in 2025. The Basic bundle with ads is down to $4.99/month for a 12-month period. However, after another round of Disney+ price hikes, the deal technically offers better savings than last year.
Apple TV
Apple TV only offers one standard plan, making it one of the simplest deals. The $14.99/month ad-free Apple TV subscription is now $6.99/month for your first six months, available to both new and qualified returning subscribers.
Paramount+
Paramount+ is offering the same Black Friday deal on both of its plans, dropping the monthly price to $2.99/mo for your first two months. That’s a whopping 67% discount on the ad-supported Essential plan and an even more ludicrous 79% discount on the Premium plan, which removes ads and gets you the Showtime library.
With Black Friday deals dropping both plans to the same price, you might as well splurge to go ad-free and get in on the Yellowjackets action.
Crunchyroll
Crunchyroll has discounted all three of its plans at different rates. These plans have changed a lot in the last year, but the short story is they all get you access to the same ad-free streaming library.
The differences come down to access to the platform’s other features like access to Crunchyroll Manga, the Crunchyroll Game Vault, and the now restricted Crunchyroll Store. If you’re just looking for cheap and ad-free anime, you’d be all set with the $3.99/month Fan plan.
HBO Max
HBO Max has only discounted its Basic with Ads plan for Black Friday 2026. Both new and eligible returning subscribers can sign up for the ad-supported plan at the discounted rate of $5.49/month, down 50% from the standard cost of $10.99. On the plus side, the discount covers a full 12 months.
Peacock
Peacock has some weird plans, to be honest, but it’s discounted both its limited Select and slightly more robust Premium options. Both plans have ads, but Premium opens up access to the entire Peacock library.
With Peacock's Black Friday deals, you can choose to get three months of either plan at a discounted monthly rate, but the best savings are on the upfront annual plans that cover a full year.
