At this point, upgrading from the Switch is totally worth it
The Nintendo Switch 2 console first released back in June of 2025 at a starting MSRP of $450. After the recent Nintendo price hike, the price went up to $500. At this point, upgrading from the Switch to the Switch 2 is a no-brainer. Several Switch 2 exclusives have been released including Mario Kart World, Donkey Kong Bananza, Pokemon Pokopia, Kirby Air Riders, Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave, and more. Zelda: Ocarina of Time scored a 9/10 from IGN's Logan Plant.
Triple-A ports have also made their way to the Switch 2 console, including Cyberpunk 2077 and Final Fantasy VII Remake, making the Switch 2 a veritable competitor to pricier handheld gaming PCs. The Switch 2 hardware is powerful enough to run these games at a very playable clip, a feat that's impossible on the original Switch's hardware.
