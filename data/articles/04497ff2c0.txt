Odessa A’zion brought sartorial drama to the 2026 LACMA Art+Film Gala in Los Angeles on Saturday with a high-shine red dress contrasted by brown suede lace-up boots.
The “I Love L.A.” star, who is known to style herself for red carpets, opted for a pair of boots with Victorian design cues. In a soft chocolate suede color, the boots featured tonal laces zig-zagging through gold grommets and then silver hooks up the shaft. The silhouette featured a fitted knee-high shaft and a gently squared toe.
A’zion’s brown suede boots served as a neutral earth-toned complement to her striking ruby red dress. The dramatic, lustrous garment featured an off-the-shoulder neckline and capacious long sleeves which added significant volume to the look. The skirt featured a slit in front which allowed A’zion’s footwear to shine. She accessorized with layered necklaces and modeled brown curls in an updo.

The Los Angeles County Museum of Art’s 15th annual Art+Film Gala brought Hollywood and the art world together on Saturday, honoring artist Vija Celmins and “Dune” director Denis Villeneuve. Leonardo DiCaprio and LACMA trustee Eva Chow returned as co-chairs, while Gucci continued its presenting sponsorship, a role it has held since the gala’s 2011 debut. Blackpink’s Rosé was tapped to perform. Proceeds support the museum’s film programming, exhibitions and educational initiatives.
