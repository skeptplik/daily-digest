This week the US lost its measles elimination status, an official recognition that the infectious disease is endemic in the country after being eliminated for 26 years. To find out what this means in practical terms for citizens, Ian Sample hears from the Guardian US health reporter Jessica Glenza. She explains why it comes at an already difficult time for the health secretary, Robert F Kennedy Jr, and his Make America Healthy Again movement, and what newly elected Democrats will be focusing on when it comes to health
