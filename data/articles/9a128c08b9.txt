'Novaya Gazeta', an independent Russian newspaper critical of Putin, closes
Issued on:
Share
Russian investigative newspaper Novaya Gazeta was winding up its operations on Monday, November 9, and preparing to quit its central Moscow office after holding its final editorial meeting, bowing to what it said was a campaign of state pressure that had made it impossible to carry on reporting.
