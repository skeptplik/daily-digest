Los Angeles – Anita DeFrantz, an Olympic rowing bronze medallist who became a pioneering member of the International Olympic Committee, has died at the age of 74.
The IOC said in a statement on Thursday that DeFrantz, the first Black American and first American woman to hold a seat on the committee, had died. A cause of death was not disclosed.
“I feel an immense sense of loss at Anita’s passing,” IOC president Kirsty Coventry said.
