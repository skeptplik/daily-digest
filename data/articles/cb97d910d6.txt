This Saturday night, Kylie Jenner stepped onto the red carpet for the Los Angeles County Museum of Art’s 15th annual Art+Film Gala. For the night, Jenner was styled by Ashi Studio, sporting a floor-length fitted gown with a uniquely subtle deer print pattern.
The Kardashian-Jenner family is certainly no stranger to animal print, with Kris Jenner being a well-known fan of all things leopard. However, deer print patterns tend to be a rarer sighting in the world of exotic prints in general. This makes Jenner opting for Bambi-core for her LACMA look an elegantly distinctive choice.
Her dress featured a backless scoop and halter top with a pooling, floor length skirt and a small tassel embellishment hanging off the back. Delicately rendered light white fawn prints speckled the dress’s pale burgundy backdrop. Jenner also opted for a unique texture for her look, the gown having a latex-like appearance that rendered her semi-luminous under Chris Burden’s “Urban Lights” sculpture.
This is far from the first time that Jenner has turned to Ashi Studio for a red carpet look, the makeup mogul a well-documented fan of the brand. Jenner wore an Ashi corset strapless dress with floral detailing at the 2025 Vanity Fair Oscar party, which she attended along with boyfriend Timothée Chalamet.
The skin-colored gown made headlines for its bold, lingerie-inspired appearance. For this year’s Golden Globes ceremony, Jenner again opted for Ashi, showing up in form-fitted metallic silver gown with multiple thin, beaded straps circling her shoulders.
The Los Angeles County Museum of Art’s 15th annual Art+Film Gala brought Hollywood and the art world together on Saturday, honoring artist Vija Celmins and “Dune” director Denis Villeneuve. Leonardo DiCaprio and LACMA trustee Eva Chow returned as co-chairs, while Gucci continued its presenting sponsorship, a role it has held since the gala’s 2011 debut. Blackpink’s Rosé was tapped to perform. Proceeds support the museum’s film programming, exhibitions and educational initiatives.
