China’s fifth plenum: cadre promotion rules overhauled, focusing on problem-solving
Drafted by high-profile team led by President Xi Jinping, resolution lays out new criteria ahead of 21st party congress
Analysts said that as provincial and ministerial reshuffles begin ahead of the 21st party congress, the fifth plenum sent a clear message: Beijing wants proven crisis managers and not credential-chasing bureaucrats steering the world’s second-largest economy through headwinds.
