Cardi B turned heads at the Baby2Baby Gala in Los Angeles on Saturday night thanks to a Tamara Ralph Spring 2026 Couture look.
The 34-year-old rapper wore the look head-to-toe as it appeared on the runway, including its large, feathered statement headpiece. Hat-like in nature, the wide headpiece created quite a radius around Cardi B, with voluminous plumes of white feathers seemingly obstructing the singer’s viewpoint.
Traveling south, Cardi B — who was tapped to perform at the West Hollywood fundraiser — wore a pair of dangling earrings, the only detail of her ensemble that didn’t mirror the runway look. Cardi B also added elbow-length sheer white gloves and kept the cream gown flowing till it reached the floor.
Up top, the gown’s halter neck straps created a deep V and wrapped around the back of the performer’s neck. An intricate corset of gold and reflective stone made up the midsection, cinching in Cardi B’s waist and creating the impression of a mosaic. The rest of the fabric fell in soft waves, billowing out from an invisible focus point in the middle of the dress, located just below the corset detail.
With most of her face obscured, it’s hard to determine the glam Cardi B went with, but the beauty that was visible was kept bronzed and glowing, with a fun Y2K style on the lips. The “Bodak Yellow” singer sported warm blush and bronzer on her cheeks and golden highlight across the bridge of her nose. Brown liner rimmed her lips while a pale bubblegum lipstick and gloss completed the look.
Held at the Pacific Design Center and presented by Paul Mitchell, the Baby2Baby Gala aims to aid the charity’s mission to provide basic necessities to children living in poverty, including diapers, clothing and baby formula.
