The world’s leading architects and designers from over 40 countries joined to celebrate Dezeen Awards 2026, in partnership with Trimble, announcing this year’s winners at a party in London.
More than 550 guests from countries including Angola, Canada and India, as well as Japan, South Africa and Uruguay, joined Dezeen for the event at the historic Royal Opera House in central London.
Guests were welcomed with a champagne reception provided by exclusive drinks partner Veuve Clicquot as they entered the Grade-II listed Paul Hamlyn Hall at the opera house.
The hall’s glazed interior was illuminated and washed in vivid colours drawn from this year’s awards graphic identity in a show design led by Hingston Studio.
Partygoers enjoyed their drinks set against a textile softwall bar backdrop designed by Stephanie Forsythe and Todd MacAllen for Canadian studio Molo, known for its sculptural space partitions, furniture and lighting made from paper and textile.
Dezeen’s co-CEOs Benedict Hobson and Wai Shin Li opened the evening’s ceremony. They welcomed Dezeen’s founding director and board member Rupinder Bhogal to the stage to lead a tribute to the magazine’s founder, the late Marcus Fairs, in honour of its 20th year.
After guests joined in a toast, Hobson and Li then introduced the evening’s keynote speaker, Norman Foster.
The British architect delivered a speech that explored creative and artificial intelligence within the evolving landscape of global architecture. Drawing on his prolific career, the nonagenarian advocated that meaningful innovation requires challenging the status quo.
Dezeen’s editorial director of special projects Max Fraser and awards and events director Claire Barrett then led the evening’s winner announcements.
First to be announced were the design categories, which saw design project of the year awarded to the V&A East’s wayfinding scheme.
Stockholm-based Simon Skinner and British-Nigerian designer Yinka Ilori were named as this year’s emerging designer of the year and designer of the year, respectively.
Skinner and Ilori won in this year’s Designers of the Year initiative, which recognises the best emerging and established talent whose innovative work has made a notable impact on the industry across architecture, interiors and design.
Interiors project of the year, sponsored by Gaggenau, went to Barcelona-based Raul Sanchez’s renovation of an old sailor’s home. Australian designer Yasmine Ghoniem was awarded emerging interior designer of the year, with Tokyo-based Keiji Ashizawa taking the award as this year’s established designer. The interiors project categories were also sponsored by Programa and Framery.
Architecture project of the year was awarded to Daichi’s treetop sauna. The architecture project categories were also sponsored by Michelmersh and Sanderson.
The evening’s presentations culminated with Japanese architect Shohei Kuma and French‑Spanish architect Elsa Escobedo jointly winning emerging architect of the year, while world-renowned practitioner Lina Ghotmeh was named architect of the year.
All the winners received a bespoke trophy made from sustainably harvested cork produced by Portuguese manufacturer Corticeira Amorim.
Dezeen Awards winners, VIPs and invited guests continued celebrating into the early hours at an exclusive afterparty sponsored by creative technology studio Made by On at the House of Louie‘s sequestered cocktail hideaway, the Alligator Bar.
All Dezeen Awards 2026 winners were published on Dezeen last Monday evening. Each winner was also featured on a dedicated page on the awards website.

Dezeen Awards 2026 in partnership with Trimble
Dezeen Awards is the ultimate accolade for architects and designers worldwide. The ninth edition of the annual awards is in partnership with global technology company Trimble. Through connected hardware and software solutions, Trimble helps designers to transform the way they shape spaces and buildings as well as drive productivity in the process. Trimble’s collaboration with Dezeen Awards demonstrates its ongoing commitment to inspiring and supporting creative excellence across the globe.
