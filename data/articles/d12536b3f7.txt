It’s not even been a week since the Delta Reign hit the Pokémon TCG, but it already seems we’ve got a price war between retailers on our hands, meaning you can now get the nine-pack Elite Trainer Box almost at market price.
Since the Mega Evolution set’s latest expansion, we’ve surprisingly seen a sharp drop in estimated market values for particular products from the collection, despite there being plenty of expensive chase cards you can pull in Delta Reign. Walmart slipped under the $100 price for the ETB first, Amazon sellers caught up, and now the former has undercut competitors once again to be the cheapest of all.
Specifically, the Pokémon TCG: Delta Reign Elite Trainer Box can be found as little as $89.95 at Walmart, with free delivery currently set for November 12 (at the time of writing). To be clear, this lowest-price deal — with $50.04 taken off the current $139.99 list price — comes courtesy of third-party seller "Realgooddeal", but is still fulfilled by Walmart itself.
For comparison, Walmart’s new best price is $1.13 above its estimated $88.12 market price by dedicated trading card marketplace TCGplayer, with current legit listings going for a slightly higher $89.99.
Keep in mind, however, that market price is an entirely separate metric compared to MSRP — but we have a dedicated guide explaining the difference if you’re unaware of the current stock issues in the Pokémon Trading Card Game’s market.
On the other hand, if you’re the type of Pokémon collector who only prefers to buy through Amazon, the Delta Reign ETB can be bought for as little as $98.49 at Amazon US through third-party seller “venado” — which is a new all-time low for the retailer, confirmed with price tracker camelcamelcamel.
Of course, no matter where you buy your new and sealed Delta Reign Elite Trainer Box from, the contents will be all the same: nine titular Pokémon TCG booster packs from the expansion, a full-art Kyogre promo card, 65 card sleeves, 40 Pokémon TCG Energy cards, a player’s expansion guide, six damage-counter dice, a competition-legal coin-flip, a plastic coin, and a code card for the Pokémon Trading Card Game Live app.
All of course come in a collector’s box, which works as its own storage, since six dividers to keep everything organised are also included.
