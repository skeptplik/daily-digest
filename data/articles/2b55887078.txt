In the version of the article initially published, there was a copy–paste error in the Extended Data Fig. 4h source data where the ACTB zipcode-2, shSETD2#2 values were duplicates of the EEF1A, shSETD2#2 values. The error was confined to the source data entries and the plotted individual data points for the affected group. The mean values, error bars, and P values in the original Extended Data Fig. 4h remain unchanged. The corrected Extended Data Fig. 4h is shown as Fig. 1 below), and the revised source data file is available in the Supplementary Information accompanying this amendment. This notice serves to correct the error.
