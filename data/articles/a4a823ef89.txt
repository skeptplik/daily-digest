Glenn Maxwell retires from international cricket, will continue playing franchise T20
In short:
Glenn Maxwell has announced his retirement from international cricket.
Maxwell had previously retired from one-day cricket last year and will now no longer represent Australia in T20 internationals.
What's next?
The 38-year-old will continue playing in franchise T20 leagues, including for the Melbourne Stars in the upcoming BBL season.
