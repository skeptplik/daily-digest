Sanae Takaichi slammed online for praising calligraphy without mentioning China
Prime minister welcomes Unesco bid for Japan’s ‘shodo’ tradition, but is criticised for not mentioning the artform’s roots
On Thursday, Unesco released this year’s nominations for inclusion on its Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity.
The 59 nominations submitted for consideration for the UN cultural body’s list include Chinese cuisine, Spain’s traditional jota music and dance genre and Japan’s shodo calligraphy.
