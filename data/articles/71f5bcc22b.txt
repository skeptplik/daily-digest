Moesha Johnson underlines 2026 dominance with double World Cup open water gold
In short:
Moesha Johnson has claimed gold in the 10km and 3km elimination race at the World Cup opener in Cancún.
Johnson won every single race in last year's World Cup, as well as the Pan Pacs in California.
What's next?
The next leg of the Open Water World Cup will take place in Somabay, Egypt on March 19 and 20, 2027.
