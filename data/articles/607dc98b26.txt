FIFA: Infantino, Tashima and Diallo enter presidential race
Issued on:
Three candidates have officially submitted their bids for the FIFA presidency. Gianni Infantino, who has been in office for 11 years, is seeking a fourth consecutive term. Backed by UEFA, French Football Federation president Philippe Diallo wants to offer an alternative to Infantino’s leadership. He will face Japan’s Kohzo Tashima, honorary president of the Japan Football Association. The election will take place on March 18, 2027 in Rabat, Morocco.
In the Women’s Champions League, OL Lyonnes beat Arsenal 2-0 in London. Still unbeaten and yet to concede a goal, the French side have secured direct qualification for the quarter-finals for a record 18th time.
Paris FC claimed their first win in the competition, beating HB Koge 2-1. The Parisian side will play for a place in the play-offs against Servette in Geneva on 16 December.
In Argentina, Lionel Messi is reportedly considering legal action against the football federation over the commercial use of his image.
In tennis, Arthur Fils was knocked out of the ATP Finals by Alexander Zverev (6-4, 7-6). Ben Shelton qualified for the semi-finals, while Carlos Alcaraz has been cleared to continue in the tournament following a reassuring MRI scan.
Finally, Paris Basketball beat Hapoel Tel Aviv 97-96, with Nadir Hifi scoring 18 points and hitting his third consecutive game-winner in the competition. The Parisian club has now won six Euroleague games in a row and moved back into the top 10, which secures a place in the play-offs.
