Sensor Tower estimates Grand Theft Auto 6 has generated over $1.4 billion in pre-order revenue, with 87% of orders for the Ultimate Edition.
As detailed in a new report on the game's impact on consumer engagement, pre-orders rose sixfold when Netflix released its Extended Look at the game on August 27.
According to Sensor Tower, daily GTA 6 pre-order revenue across PlayStation and Xbox ranged from $2.4 million to $3.8 million through "most of" August.
Revenue reached $16.3 million the day after the Netflix special and has continued to rise ahead of launch, making GTA 6 the most pre-ordered game on PlayStation and Xbox storefronts.
The market intelligence firm also analysed which games are most likely to lose players to GTA 6. Fortnite, GTA Online, and other AAA single-player titles are expected to be most affected.
Among PlayStation users, 42% with GTA 5 also own Fortnite, compared to 22% of all players. Sensor Tower noted that "Fortnite and Roblox seem [the] most exposed from an audience overlap perspective."
Sensor Tower expects Roblox to retain its player base during GTA 6's launch. It noted Roblox "beat the catalogue in player count change in the month following five recent releases," including Monster Hunter Wilds, Oblivion Remastered, Resident Evil Requiem, Crimson Desert, and First Light.
"Roblox's audience looks less likely to be lured away by acclaimed single-player launches, and is likely to weather GTA 6 better than its peers," the firm suggested.
Last month, gaming consultancy Open World assessed which games might lose players to GTA 6. Fortnite, Call of Duty, Battlefield, Apex Legends, EA Sports FC, and NBA 2K were identified as most at risk during GTA 6's launch.
