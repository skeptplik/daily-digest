Anthropic’s much-hyped arrival on Wall Street fizzled out on Friday as a dramatic unraveling of the AI trade showed that even a blockbuster listing could not shake the gloom hanging over the sector.
As the first large machine-learning model maker to go public, Anthropic is widely seen as a proxy for the AI trade, and its listing had been viewed as a potential boost for a sector struggling to regain its footing.
But the stock closed at $88.91, 8.5% below Thursday’s close and not far from its $83 IPO price, while declines in other technology heavyweights suggested the listing had done little to stem the broader selloff.
