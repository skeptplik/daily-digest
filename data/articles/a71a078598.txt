If you’re still deciding what makeup look to wear this Thanksgiving and for the holidays ahead, let Ariana Grande offer a little beauty inspiration.
The Focker-in-Law star was spotted yesterday on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon wearing her take on fall’s purple eyeshadow trend, “Sugar Plum” eyeshadow, a muted, shimmery lilac look that feels extra festive. Grande debuted the seasonal look in an Instagram post for her brand, r.e.m. beauty, where she captured a few quick touch-ups on her way to the New York City soundstage.
The singer recently revealed that the limited-edition palette she used to create her “Sugar Plum” eyes is the same one she wore onstage during her Eternal Sunshine concert tour: r.e.m. beauty’s new The Prima Palette. “For those of you who have been asking for the last six months, this palette right here is what I’ve been using on my eyes on tour every single night,” Grande explained in a separate post early last month. For a monochromatic finish, the beauty founder paired her lavender lids with her brand’s Glossy Barre Mini Glossy Balm in the shade Sugar Plum, a mauve-toned gloss that also launched just in time for the holidays.
Grande isn’t the only star trying the purple eyeshadow trend. Along with supermodel Bella Hadid, who wore a deeper shade on Saint Laurent’s Fall/Winter 2026 runway, Zendaya, Zara Larsson, Cardi B, and more have tested the makeup look over the past few months, suggesting the buzz won’t slow anytime soon.
