Bangkok – The top U.N. court’s ruling on Thursday that Myanmar committed genocide repeatedly name-checked one man — Min Aung Hlaing, the country’s president who, as a general, led the military during the campaign against the Rohingya.
The International Court of Justice’s findings concerned state culpability rather than individual guilt over the Myanmar military’s campaign of rape and killings against the mostly Muslim Rohingya minority in 2017.
But Min Aung Hlaing was called out for his speeches against the Rohingya while armed forces chief during the so-called “clearance operations” in western Rakhine state. “The court considers that the Senior General’s messages, as reported, were capable of fueling hatred against the Rohingya,” the ruling said.
