The Game Awards has announced its nominations slate for 2026, revealing that Grand Theft Auto 6 is this year's most-nominated game. Rockstar's hotly anticipated crime epic has secured eight nominations including Game of the Year, for which it'll be competing with games including Resident Evil Requiem, Control Resonant, and The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.
Now you—and the countless other people preparing to argue about this online—may have noticed that GTA 6 has been nominated for a number of awards despite the fact that it hasn't been released yet. In a press release, The Game Awards notes that jurors were in some cases provided with early access to enable awards consideration.
"The nominees for The Game Awards are selected by a global jury of over 160 media publications and creator outlets around the world, who may be provided with advance or pre-release access to games for critical evaluation," The Game Awards said.
In a Variety interview last week, Game Awards host and co-creator Geoff Keighley refuted speculation that TGA 2026 eligibility deadlines had been sculpted to allow GTA 6 to have a shot for this year's nominations.
"There was a thing a few weeks ago where everyone thought that we had changed our dates to accommodate GTA, and it's like, no, actually our dates have been very set every year. It's the same calendar that we have," Keighley said. "I don't know this, but I'm sure some of these companies consider that when they're looking at their release calendars."
You can view and vote on the Game Awards nominees at the TGA website—but it's been crashing consistently since the nominees were announced, so we've collected all the nominees below. The Game Awards airs at 7:30 pm ET / 4:30 pm PT on Thursday, December 12 on Twitch and YouTube.
