A recently patched Drupal core vulnerability is being exploited in the wild, according to the cybersecurity agency CISA and Drupal developers.
The vulnerability, CVE-2026-132165, can be exploited for remote code execution. It was patched on November 18, and Drupal’s advisory says in-the-wild exploitation began shortly after.
“The Security Team has received a report this was exploited on live sites as early as 2026-11-19 19:00 UTC,” Drupal noted.
The discovery of the flaw is credited to Anthropic’s OSS Scanner, a free opt-in service that regularly scans enrolled open source projects for vulnerabilities.
Anthropic has not said how many vulnerabilities OSS Scanner has found since its October launch, but as of early November, it reported disclosing more than 5,000 vulnerabilities overall, across 680 open source projects.
CISA added CVE-2026-132165 to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (KEV) catalog on November 24, instructing federal agencies to address it by November 27.
No public information appears to be available on attacks exploiting CVE-2026-132165. It’s unclear how many websites are affected.
The security hole affects the installer that ships with Drupal core, which enables users to set up a new site. An unauthenticated attacker — under certain conditions — can abuse the installer to reinstall Drupal, which can allow them to hijack the website or execute arbitrary code.
“Some (but not all) exploit paths are mitigated if the web server cannot write to or change permissions on settings.php. In addition to upgrading, the Drupal Security Team recommends securing the Drupal installer after installation,” Drupal said.
Patches are included in Drupal 11.4.13, 11.3.22 and 10.6.22; end-of-life branches will not get a fix.
It’s worth noting that Drupal raised the vulnerability’s rating to ‘highly critical’ (CMSS score of 20/25) after it learned of the attacks.
This is the third Drupal vulnerability exploited in the wild this year. Drupal warned in late October that CVE-2026-118539, a highly critical access bypass flaw in the Commerce Core module, had been exploited against online stores.
In May, security firms reported seeing attacks exploiting a vulnerability tracked as CVE-2026-9082 against thousands of websites.
