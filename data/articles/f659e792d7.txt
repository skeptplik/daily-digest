Poland begins 2 days of national mourning after gunman kills 6

Poland has begun two days of national mourning after a gunman opened fire in Krakow's main square Saturday afternoon, November 7. Six people were killed and at least 15 were injured. The gunman was shot and detained by police. He then died later in hospital.
