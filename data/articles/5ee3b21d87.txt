Remember in the closing days of 2023, when Rockstar dropped the first trailer for Grand Theft Auto 6? Amidst the everything it had going on was a brief shot of a man with extensive facial tattoos, who was quickly called out by people hip to such things as a knockoff of Florida Joker, aka Lawrence Sullivan, a Florida man who went viral for his 2017 mug shot.
Sullivan himself called it out in a since-deleted TikTok video, first telling Rockstar "we gotta talk" and then, with no response forthcoming, threatening legal action. Not long after that, though, he put forth a new offer: "Let me voice the character, give me more storyline in the game… Let's make history happen, man."
It seems that Sullivan got what he wanted: In a video posted to Instagram, Sullivan said he was given a role in GTA 6.
"I'm an official voice actor on GTA 6, the game." Sullivan says. "For the last three years a lot of people were saying I was not going to be in the game, I was not going to be nothing, they were destroying my mental health. They were saying a lot of fucked shit. But now it's time for me to shit on them and show them what the fuck Florida Joker is about!"
"Y'all gonna see. Special voice actor for GTA 6. This ain't no gimmick, this ain't no lie!"
I had doubts, but this apparently really ain't no gimmick or lie. Rockstar has posted the full GTA 6 credits and sure enough, Lawrence Sullivan is in there. Now, it could be a different Lawrence Sullivan, sure, but under the circumstances I don't think that's very likely.
Sullivan's role doesn't appear to be a particularly prominent one: He's credited, along with a great many other people, as one of "The Local Population." But at the end of the day, he made it: Florida Joker really is in GTA 6, and he seems pretty chuffed about it. I'd call that a happy ending.
