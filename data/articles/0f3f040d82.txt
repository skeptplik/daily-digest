Countries from North America to Europe and East Asia know that trying to “de-couple” from this powerful but predatory nation would be ruinously expensive, if not futile; but also, for the sake of their security and sovereignty, that they at least need to “de-risk.” But is this even possible?
For years, that predatory nation was understood to be authoritarian China. These days, though, the superpower that America’s allies want to de-risk from is America itself.
Their concern has become no less urgent after the U.S. midterm elections. The lame-duck American president, having been repudiated and chastened, will now be livid and resentful, and foreign policy is the domain in which a Democratic Congress has the least power to restrain him. Besides, any effort by America’s allies to de-risk will outlast the era of Donald Trump by decades, because that’s how long de-risking would take.
