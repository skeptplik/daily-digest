A flash of red is a holiday party classic, and Katie Holmes is making a persuasive case for doubling down on the festive color. The actor stepped out in New York for a holiday celebration hosted by Meiomi Pinot Noir, with her boyfriend Jason Bard Yarmosky by her side, serving up a masterclass in laidback party dressing.
While fellow guests embraced the season’s faux fur, feathers, and sequins, Holmes stuck to her trusty winter uniform: a sweeping black coat from Lafayette 148 layered over a simple white top and dark Banana Republic corduroys, with black shoes by The Row. The pièce de résistance was a cherry-red Gucci shoulder bag, perfectly coordinated with her punchy red lipstick.
It’s an easy styling trick, but an effective one. The two flashes of crimson brought a festive sensibility to an otherwise pared-back ensemble, while the lipstick also felt particularly appropriate for an evening celebrating Pinot Noir. Holmes looked very much in the holiday spirit, without a sequin in sight.
Of course, Katie Holmes is something of an It-bag enthusiast: a classic quilted Chanel 19 Flap Bag, the sleek Private Eye bag by Métier, the trapezoid Akris Ai shoulder bag, an iconic Celine Triomphe, a suite of Ferragamo. Her extensive rotation runs from roomy everyday totes to polished, structured shoulder bags, often serving as the focal point of her otherwise uncomplicated outfits. She has a knack for making even the most coveted accessories look effortless, pairing them with straight-leg denim jeans and louche tailoring.
And then there’s her enduring affection for a red lip. Holmes has long understood its transformative powers, particularly when paired with her favored minimal makeup and swept-back hair. Here, with her dark hair neatly pulled back, the bold lipstick was allowed to do the talking.
Matchy-matchy glam has been all over the celebrity circuit: everyone from Taylor Swift to Kylie Jenner is choosing coordinating mani-pedis in deep shades of seasonal onyx and Bordeaux, while Jennifer Lopez embraced all-over burgundy.
Forget matching your bag to your shoes. This holiday season, Katie Holmes proves matching it to your makeup can be a true festive delight. And, as demonstrated here, you don’t have to sacrifice comfort—or your favorite corduroys—to look party-ready.
