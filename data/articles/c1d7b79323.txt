Bahrain accused Iran of striking a civilian industrial facility on Saturday (November 21, 2026), causing a fire and damage but no casualties, while two tankers were hit in the Strait of Hormuz.
"As a result of the heinous Iranian aggression, a civilian industrial facility was targeted, causing a fire at the site. Civil Defence is working to bring the fire under control," the Interior Ministry said on X, without specifying the location.
Residents told AFP they saw smoke rising over Sitra Island, on Bahrain's east coast, home to several industrial sites including a refinery run by state oil company Bapco.
Iran's Fars news agency reported the Revolutionary Guards had "attacked two U.S. bases, Sheikh Isa and Juffair, on the island of Bahrain in response to the illegal actions of the United States".
The attacks came as two tankers were struck in the Strait of Hormuz, which has become the focus of the stalemated conflict, with Iran continuing in its efforts to blockade the strategic shipping line.
The U.S. military, meanwhile, has been escorting ships through the strategic strait, which carried one-fifth of the world's oil before the war, and maintains its own counterblockade of Iranian ports.
Iranian deputy Foreign Minister Kazem Gharibabadi said on Saturday (November 21, 2026) that a Qatari delegation visited Tehran the previous evening and "held two hours of talks", without providing details.
Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said: "The ongoing talks in the region must be given a chance, and the blockade must be lifted rationally."
"Our position is clear: as long as Iran's demands are not met, the discussion on the Strait of Hormuz and its opening is pointless," he said, quoted by state TV.
Iran's demands include ending the war on all fronts, unfreezing Iranian assets, lifting oil sanctions and ending the U.S. counter-blockade of Iranian ports.
British maritime agency UKMTO said on Saturday (November 21, 2026) one tanker was struck by an "unknown projectile" while transiting Hormuz.
"There are no reported casualties at this time," it said, adding another tanker was hit in its engine room.
Iran's army later reported a ship was on fire after entering a "restricted zone" and hitting a mine in the strait.
The tanker had switched off its identification and navigation systems "in defiance of issued warnings and maritime transit regulations", it said in a statement carried by state TV.
Tehran has repeatedly reported ships catching fire in the strait in recent weeks, often claiming they struck mines.
But the U.S. military recently said it had cleared all mines from the main routes through the waterway.
U.S. Central Command, which oversees U.S. military operations in the region, said last month that it had helped Gulf countries export 1.5 billion barrels of oil via Hormuz since the summer.
Iran said on Saturday (November 21, 2026) it shot down a U.S. drone over Qeshm Island in the strait, according to state TV.
American fighter jets struck an Iranian missile site on Qeshm last Tuesday, according to a U.S. military official.
