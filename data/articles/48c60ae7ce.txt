South Korea investigates 580 finance firms over data leaks, issues warning
The Financial Services Commission warns banks against ignoring known security risks and unsafe practices
The Financial Services Commission (FSC) revealed the scale of the breaches on Thursday as authorities vowed to strengthen cybersecurity measures following a string of similar cases affecting the financial sector and government agencies.
The FSC detailed the incidents at a meeting with officials from financial trade associations and government agencies to review data protection systems and the capabilities of artificial intelligence in the financial sector, Yonhap News reported.
