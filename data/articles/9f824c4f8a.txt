Sydney Sixers beat Melbourne Renegades in WBBL despite Ellyse Perry run-out
In short:
The Sydney Sixers have beaten the Melbourne Renegades by 11 runs.
Ellyse Perry was initially given not out, but a replay on the big screen prompted the umpires to send it to the third umpire.
Replays showed her bat bouncing over the line as she dived to make her ground.
