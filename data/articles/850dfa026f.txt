After Marc Jacobs changed hands earlier this year from LVMH Moët Hennessy Louis Vuitton to WHP Global and G-III Apparel Group, its new owners have inked a long-term beauty license extension with Coty Inc.
The extension, for which terms were not disclosed, runs 40 years.
Markus Strobel, executive chairman and interim chief executive officer of Coty Inc., said: “Marc Jacobs is one of the great creative names in fashion, and we are proud of what we have built together over more than two decades. This extension is a powerful testament to Coty’s position as a partner of choice for the world’s leading luxury fashion and lifestyle brands. We look forward to building on this successful partnership and shaping the next chapter of Marc Jacobs Beauty.”
Since partnering with Marc Jacobs in 2003, Coty has launched a succession of fragrances, including Daisy Marc Jacobs and Daisy Wild, as well as Perfect Marc Jacobs.
It announced that it would be launching makeup with Marc Jacobs in 2023, the second iteration of Jacobs’ makeup brand, which originally debuted with Kendo Brands in 2013, before being shuttered in 2021.
The Marc Jacobs Beauty makeup range officially launched in May, with Coty describing its start as “outstanding.” It is now available in the U.S., the U.K., Australia and Canada via Sephora, as well as online. According to Coty, Marc Jacobs Beauty retail sales are already exceeding targets and ranking amongst the top 20 makeup brands at Sephora U.K. and ANZ, or Australia and New Zealand, with both the eyeliner and eye shadow ranking within the Top 5 in Sephora U.S.
On the licensing deal, Yehuda Shmidman, founder, chairman and CEO of WHP Global, said: “Marc Jacobs is a powerful global brand with tremendous momentum and opportunity ahead. Beauty is an important part of the brand’s connection with consumers, and Coty has been instrumental in building Marc Jacobs’ fragrance business and relaunching Marc Jacobs Beauty. We look forward to continuing this partnership and unlocking new opportunities for Marc Jacobs.”
Morris Goldfarb, chairman and CEO of G-III Apparel Group, added: “Coty has been an exceptional partner for Marc Jacobs, with the expertise and global scale to build on the brand’s momentum in beauty. The renewed agreement reflects our shared confidence in Marc Jacobs’ long-term potential and our ambition to grow the brand around the world.”
On Sept. 1, LVMH Moët Hennessy Louis Vuitton completed its roughly $925 million sale of Marc Jacobs to WHP Global and G-III Apparel Group. WHP and G-III each own 50 percent of the brand, while G-III also bought the company’s operating business.
