Indonesia mulls palace bug sweeps after presidential wiretaps
Government acts to stop agencies misusing surveillance equipment after investigators pinpoint location of listening devices
The government had said that the wiretapping scandal pointed to treason. An investigation is underway and no formal charges have yet been announced.
Asked if authorities would sweep the presidential palace for covert electronic devices, senior minister for legal affairs Yusril Ihza Mahendra said “it is something we will consider as an anticipation measure”.
