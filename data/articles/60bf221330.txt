Flipping your car in Grand Theft Auto 6 works a bit differently than it did in previous games. In Rockstar's bid for realism, the team made it so you can't flip your car while you're still in it. Instead, whichever character you're controlling will have to get out of the car and turn it over. Note that flipping only works on certain vehicles, though. You can flip cars, boats, even larger construction vehicles, but not planes.
Below, we explain how to flip your car in GTA 6.
How to flip your car in GTA 6
If you manage to turn over your sedan, ATV, boat, or cement truck, and you do it without getting wasted (GTA 6's term for "destroying it completely and dying"), you might think you'll have to get Scooter Bros to deliver the vehicle for you. Well, you don't. Keep that hard-stolen cash in your BuckMe account and fix it yourself.
GTA 6 never teaches you how to flip your car back over, though, and it's not mentioned in any of the control layout options. Here's what you have to do:
- Walk against the side of your car until the "shove" action prompt appears in the bottom right corner of the screen.
- Hold the action button (Square on PlayStation 5 and X on Xbox) until the vehicle is right-side-up.
And you're good to go on your merry way. That said, there are a few caveats to bear in mind if you're trying to flip your car.
Try to push the car from the middle. If you're at either end of it, we noticed the prompt tends not to appear. Also, if there's an obstacle on the other side of the car, like a thick hedge or a wall, you won't be able to flip it. The same is true for when your car is just stuck on something, like a guardrail. The game won't let you flip it off the offending object, as it has to be completely upended for the "shove" prompt to appear.
