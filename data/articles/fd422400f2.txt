As the holidays inch closer, temperatures are gradually dropping — and that means it’s time to reassess your shoe closet. In the casual footwear realm, set aside your flip-flops and slides in lieu of something a little warmer. Now is the moment for slippers, boots, knit socks and the like to shine. Below, FN rounds up cozy winter shoe styling inspiration from celebrities including Jennifer Lawrence, Timothée Chalamet and more names.
Gigi Hadid’s cashmere brand Guest in Residence teamed up with Moon Boot for a second collection which recently dropped. Featuring knit textures and joyful hues, the cozy collection features four special-edition boot styles which “fuse alpine nostalgia with space-age design.” In campaign imagery, Hadid herself models the shoes with warm layered looks including cable-knit sets and cashmere sweaters.

No stranger to quiet luxury, Jennifer Lawrence stepped out in red velvet slippers from The Row in New York City earlier this month. She showcased the brand’s Friulane style, which blends a slipper and a loafer together into a slim style with a d’Orsay outline making it all the more distinct. She paired the leisurely shoes with a dark ensemble of straight-leg trousers and a single-breasted coat.
“Ginny and Georgia” star Brianne Howey put a cozy spin on formal footwear for the 2026 Elle Women in Hollywood celebration last week. She wore a Peserico holiday look grounded by cream suede pumps and ribbed knit socks — adding a warm layer to the classic footwear silhouette.
Timothée Chalamet is a major supporter of Ugg boots, whether he’s sitting courtside watching the Knicks or pulling double duty on “Saturday Night Live”; he gravitates toward the label’s classic sheepskin-lined boots in shorter heights and versatile chestnut or black colors. He’ll style the shoes with laid-back ensembles including pieces from his beloved Chrome Hearts.
His penchant for cozy boots extends beyond the Southern California-based brand, as he recently wore Merrell Winter Pull-On boots at a Knicks game this month; the style features a water-resistant suede leather upper and faux fur lining.
Argentine rapper and singer Milo J brought a pair of embellished black slip-on shoes to “The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon” this month. Similar to the braided collar of an Ugg Tasman style, the shoes featured a zigzag trim. Milo J paired the shoes with long green shorts and a brown and green zip-up jacket.
