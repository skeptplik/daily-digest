When “Paprika” debuted in theaters in November 2006, viewers got to experience two of Japan’s most distinctive auteurs, animator Satoshi Kon and musician Susumu Hirasawa, at the zenith of their creative synergy.
Twenty years later, the cult anime continues to thrill younger audiences with not only Kon’s mind-bending story but also Hirasawa’s score, with innovative tracks that later became social media hits.
The duo had collaborated in the 2002 film “Millenium Actress” and 2004 television series “Paranoia Agent,” but “Paprika” was their last joint effort to reach audiences before Kon’s untimely death in 2010.
