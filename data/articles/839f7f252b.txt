The Adidas Samba has enjoyed quite the tenure in fashion’s collective consciousness. But for those whose sneaker collections already house a pair (or three), might we suggest exploring the rest of the German sportswear giant’s repertoire? Its Black Friday sale offers ample incentive to do precisely that, with select styles marked down by as much as 60 percent.
Adidas’ ability to reinterpret its archives has given us some of the more interesting footwear propositions in recent years. The brand’s assortment now extends from streamlined racing-inspired sneakers to decidedly feminine silhouettes that blur the lines between ballet flats and athletic shoes.
Adidas’ Black Friday Sale at a Glance
- Savings: Up to 60 percent off select styles.
- Prices: Sneakers starting at $34 in the sale assortment.
- Standout styles: Samba, Gazelle, Japan, Tokyo, and Handball Spezial.
- What to prioritize: Retro silhouettes, colorful suede sneakers, and less ubiquitous archival designs.
- Where to shop: Adidas.com
Among the silhouettes worth familiarizing yourself with are the Japan, the Grand Court, and the Gazelle Lo Pro. Each offers a slightly different interpretation of the slim sneaker aesthetic, making them excellent alternatives for those suffering from a little Samba fatigue.
There’s also plenty to please shoppers with a penchant for statement footwear. Leopard-print finishes, punchy primary colors, and richly textured suede can introduce an entirely new dimension to the wardrobe staples you already own.
Of course, we’re not advocating for the retirement of your beloved Sambas. But with some of Adidas’ more interesting designs available for a fraction of their original prices, there’s little reason not to broaden your sartorial horizons.
