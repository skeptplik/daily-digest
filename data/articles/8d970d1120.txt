England sets Australia target of 329 in final ODI after century to Ben Duckett
In short:
A Ben Duckett century has sent England past 300 once again, but late Australian wickets have limited the damage in the final ODI in Hobart.
Duckett made 133 off 115 balls in a total of 328, with Matt Renshaw taking 3-51 and Jack Edwards 4-31.
The winner of the ODI will take the series 2-1.
