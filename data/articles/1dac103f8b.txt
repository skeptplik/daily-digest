There’s something to be said about having expensive taste and the shopping savvy to accommodate it. And if your idea of a little retail therapy involves a beautifully cut cashmere sweater, a supple leather handbag, and perhaps a tin of caviar for good measure, Quince’s Black Friday sale deserves a spot on your shopping itinerary.
The retailer has built its reputation on making traditionally expensive materials more accessible, from Mongolian cashmere to Italian leather. Its Black Friday offerings make the prospect of upgrading your wardrobe (and your dinner party repertoire) all the more enticing, with many of our favorite finds ringing in under $100.
This year, Quince’s Black Friday deals vary by day, with deeper discounts on select products throughout the sale. Among the noteworthy savings are approximately 15 to 20 percent off select handbags, around 20 percent off bedding sets, and 25 percent off home fragrances.
Quince Black Friday Deals 2026 at a Glance
- Handbags: 15 to 20 percent off select styles
- Bedding sets: Around 20 percent off
- Home fragrances: 25 percent off
- Luxe finds: Cashmere, leather, and caviar
- Price range: Many picks under $200
- Sale details: Discounts vary by day
As for what we’re adding to our carts? Cashmere knits that lend themselves to winter layering, leather jackets with the potential to become permanent fixtures in our wardrobes, and gourmet caviar that feels entirely appropriate for the holiday hosting season.
Ahead, shop our favorite Quince Black Friday finds that cater to our expensive taste without exhausting our holiday shopping budgets. After all, good taste isn’t necessarily dictated by a price tag.
Quince Mongolian Cashmere Fisherman Turtleneck Sweater
A substantial cashmere knit is arguably one of the wisest investments you can make for your winter wardrobe, and this Quince number is no exception. Its Grade-A Mongolian cashmere composition and relaxed silhouette lend themselves beautifully to tailored trousers, dark-wash denim, and silk midi skirts.
Quince Mongolian Cashmere Oversized Crewneck Sweater
The proportions of this cashmere crewneck are what make it so chic, from its generously cut silhouette to its pronounced ribbed neckline. Available in sizes 1X through 3X, it’s the sort of knit we’d style with a satin maxi skirt and sculptural silver jewelry.
Quince 100% Suede Harrington Jacket
There’s a certain Steve McQueen-esque sophistication to the Harrington jacket, especially when rendered in supple suede. Quince’s take on the classic comes fully lined, with understated hardware that lets the luxurious material do the talking.
Quince Mongolian Cashmere Structured V-Neck Cardigan
Somewhere between a tailored jacket and a cozy knit lies this impossibly chic cardigan from Quince. Its dense Milano stitching lends the pure Mongolian cashmere a sculptural quality rarely associated with conventional knitwear.
