- Group sales at Hugo Boss dropped 5% year-on-year on a constant currency basis in the third quarter of 2026, attributed to an ongoing strategic turnaround and macroeconomic factors.
- Boss and Hugo were down 5% and 18%, respectively.
- EBIT fell 15% to €81 million, down from €95 million, while gross margin improved by 220 basis points to 63.3%.
- Across territories, sales were down single digits.
- The company updated its outlook for the full year, down to the high-single-digit range, just as Frasers Group assumes a 50.08% position; Hugo Boss is due to become a subsidiary of the British retail group.
