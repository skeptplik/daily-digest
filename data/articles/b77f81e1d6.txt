Down from its $219.99 list price, you can currently grab the 36-pack Delta Reign Booster Display Box for just $199 at Amazon. With that $20.99 chopped off (so 10% off), also being Amazon’s lowest ever price, you’re now only paying $5.53 per booster pack.

For comparison, amongst our Black Friday round-up of Pokemon TCG deals at Amazon, we spotted the six-pack Delta Reign Booster Bundle available for $47.88 — working out to $7.98 per pack.

Alternatively, TCGplayer itself also has listings going as low as $199.90, which is handy if you want to take advantage of its Cyber Weekend promotion offering 10% back in store credit.
