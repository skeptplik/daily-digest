Operator should be marked down for tenders after mass parking meter glitch: experts
Hong Kong authorities should also waive relevant fines after outage that disabled half of city’s parking meters, industry expert and lawmaker say
Hong Kong authorities should waive relevant fines and introduce a penalty mechanism for the parking meter operator after a four-hour outage disabled about half of the city’s meters, an industry expert and a lawmaker have suggested.
They also called for clearer refund terms for Octopus card users on Wednesday, a day after the government promised to return fees to affected drivers.
