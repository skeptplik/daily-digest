Drone attack sets Russian cargo ship ablaze in Gulf of Finland
Issued on: Modified:
Share
A Russian cargo ship caught fire after being hit by drones in the Baltic Sea on Saturday and drifted into Finnish waters. Reporting from Kyiv, FRANCE 24's Gulliver Cragg notes that the ship is affiliated with a major Russian military logistics company. While Ukrainian authorities typically neither confirm nor deny responsibility, analysts cite a clear motive for the targeting, Cragg explains.
