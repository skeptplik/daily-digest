Songs allow you to change the time of day, call your horse, literally teleport, and much more in The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake. They're essential to your progress, and they're just great melodies to listen to. However, all the songs are not available to you at the start of the game. Instead, you'll need to progress in the story, and scour all of Hyrule to unlock them all.
Here's all the songs in The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake.
