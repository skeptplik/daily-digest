Anna Sawai is the latest in a long line of celebrities to co-sign a minimal glam movement.
The “Shogun” star let her natural beauty shine at the New York City premiere of her upcoming movie, “How to Rob a Bank.” Joined by costars Pete Davidson, Zoë Kravitz, Nicholas Hoult and more, Sawai turned heads in her simple look on Tuesday night.
The 34-year-old actress was styled by Karla Welch and donned a black ruched dress from Jean Paul Gaultier’s resort 2027 collection, which featured thin straps, a plunging neckline and a full, floor-length skirt.
In contrast to her dark flowing gown, Sawai kept her beauty minimal and fresh, sporting a glowy base that paired well with the light pink blush applied to her cheekbones and a matching soft, natural lip.
Sawai’s natural arched brows framed her slight eyeliner wing and long, flirty lashes, completing the glam designed by makeup artist Yukari Obayashi Bush, a frequent collaborator of Sawai’s whose extensive clientele includes Lily-Rose Depp, Sadie Sink and Kaia Gerber. WWD has reached out to Bush for comment.
The “Beatles” actress tapped celebrity hairstylist Anh Co Tran to help achieve her elegant and elevated updo. Still maintaining a casual edge, Sawai’s long brown waves were gathered at the back of her head, with ends drifting out in a deliberately messy look. Meanwhile, at the front, Sawai left out a few face-framing tendrils to bracket her face and tie the look together. WWD has reached out to Tran for comment.
Elsewhere on the carpet, her costar Kravitz also opted for an updo, piling her brown tresses into a messy topknot with thin strands designed to float across her forehead.
