The Forerunner 265S may not be the newest or flashiest Garmin, but it’s one that I’ve always loved most in testing. For runners with smaller wrists, it fits nicely. The battery life is pretty good thanks in part to its OLED touchscreen. When I reviewed it a few years ago, I called it the Goldilocks of fitness watches at the time, and the only thing that bugged me was its price. But now that it’s down to $299.99 (a $150 discount off its MSRP) at Amazon and Best Buy for Black Friday, I can easily recommend it.
The Garmin Forerunner 265S is down to $300, its best price of the year
Older Garmins still make for a great buy if you’re hunting for bargains during Black Friday.
Garmin’s Forerunner 265S is identical to the standard 265, only it features a slightly better battery life and a smaller 42mm build for those with more petite wrists.
The neat thing about Garmins in general is that you really don’t need the latest models to get the most out of the company’s platform. Its watches tend to get firmware updates for several years after launch, which is how long the average Garmin user tends to hold on to theirs. This particular watch is best suited for folks who want something a bit slimmer and smaller, enjoy a pop of color, and want a little extra fitness tracking prowess — but don’t need the whole kit and caboodle that sends the price up by hundreds.
There are a few other Garmin models I like that are also on sale during Black Friday. I wish that I had 10 arms to fully vet every single last one, but alas. That said, here are some others that I have tested and recommend:
- The Garmin Fenix 8 is $749 at Amazon, Best Buy, and REI, which is a 25 percent discount off its original price. Its higher price comes down to having more features than the Forerunner 265S, so it’s really best for someone who does multiple outdoor sports and could make use of its handy LED flashlight.
- Garmin’s Venu 4 is $449 at Amazon, Best Buy, and REI, an 18 percent discount. This is a great option if you’re looking for more of a smartwatch that does fitness than a fitness-y watch that looks like a smartwatch. It’s a fine line, but if you know, you know.
- Finally, Garmin’s Forerunner 970 is $100 off, costing $649 at Amazon, Best Buy, and REI. This is what I’d recommend to marathoners who need something a bit more robust but not as heavy-duty as the flagship Fenix line. I’ve used it to train for a few races and it’s a solid upper-midrange (for Garmin!) option.
