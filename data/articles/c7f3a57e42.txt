Comcast has said GTA 6's launch triggered the busiest traffic day the company has seen since it was founded in 1963.
The latest record broken by Rockstar Games' latest actually comes from one of the biggest media and technology companies in the United States. After previously reporting that Grand Theft Auto had led to a 4x increase in traffic on Xbox consoles November 18, Comcast has now confirmed that pre-loads for the day resulted in the busiest day it has ever seen – and it wasn't close.
Comcast recorded peak traffic 31% higher than the average day and 9.6% higher than the previous record holder. Moreover, GTA 6 has resulted in five out of 10 of the busiest days the network has ever seen. Even more remarkable is the news that, as reported by Comcast, 24% of all of its internet traffic during peak times was from users downloading and playing the game.
If Rockstar fans weren't playing GTA 6, they were watching it. Twitch traffic increased by almost 50% on November 18 and is continuing to rise in the days following that launch. GTA 6 launched for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X/S November 19, but thanks to the New Zealand trick, a massive chunk of players actually took their trip to Leonida hours early. It certainly helped boost traffic across the board, and in Comcast's case, it resulted in its busiest day ever.
To put some of these numbers into perspective, the company shared a very relevant measurement. November 18 traffic generated on PlayStation and Xbox alone delivered enough data for one million homes to stream The Odyssey in 4K… for two days straight.
It's shocking to see how GTA 6 affected Comcast, but then again, we've known Rockstar's new game would be a juggernaut when it eventually launched. The developer's parent company, Take-Two Interactive, reported that it had earned an unbelievable $3.79 billion in the first day after its release. That makes it not just the biggest video game launch of all time, but the biggest entertainment launch across any medium.
Critics everywhere helped GTA 6 earn the title of highest-rated game of all time. IGN was included in that list after delivering a 10/10 review. We said, "Exquisitely detailed, endlessly ripe for experimentation, and uncompromisingly engineered, Grand Theft Auto 6 is the single most impressive video game I have ever played."
