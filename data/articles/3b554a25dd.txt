On Friday night in Los Angeles, actress and comedian Quinta Brunson attended the 100th episode celebration for “Abbott Elementary” in Los Angeles. Brunson wore a visually arresting gown for the evening, walking the red carpet in a modified version of the Magda Butrym x H&M rose draped corset midi-dress.
Brunson’s dress was immediately noticeable thanks to the color alone, the gown an eye-catching shade of crimson. However, the cut and details also made the dress quite the remarkable fashion feat. The bodycon midi-dress had statement-making sculptural bustier cups embellished with tightly-wound 3D rose appliqué. It featured a plunging u-shaped diamond cutout neckline, thin spaghetti straps, and a slim-fit, contoured well to Brunson’s figure.
Brunson paired the look with a pair of open-toed pumps in a matching red shade and wore her hair in a chin-length bob curled at the ends. Multiple rings adorned both hands and she wore a pair of thick silver hoop earrings.
Brunson is no stranger to bold colors and patterns and has been seen sporting some similarly standout outfits while out and about promoting “Abbott Elementary” this fall. Back in a press tour in October, the actress wore multiple notable outfits in a single day, all of which were marked by vibrant colors and striking patterns. Brunson wore one Vivienne Westwood Sunday minidress with a tartan print in various shades of blue and brown. That same day, she was seen in a sleeveless patchwork mini-dress by Dries Van Noten consisting of a quilt-like amalgamation of different patterns in bold colors like bright blue and reddish orange.
Created by Brunson, “Abbott Elementary” is a mockumentary-style sitcom that has been going strong since its 2021 premiere. The show now spans six seasons and Brunson plays the kindhearted and idealistic second grade teacher Janine Teagues. “Abbott Elementary” has received critical acclaim throughout its run, receiving multiple awards including four Primetime Emmys. The show’s 100th episode is set to premiere on December 6, 2026.
