Last year the Middle East and Africa region grew at nearly three times the global rate. Saudi Arabia supplies the scale, while the UAE supplies the capital, the talent and the export engine behind a new generation of Arab fragrance houses now topping sales charts in the U.S. Premium beauty is outpacing mass in every GCC market, and categories such as skin care and hair care remain underpenetrated. Here, a quantitative look at the market.
$20.8 billion
The GCC beauty and personal care market’s projected value by 2030, up from $14.3 billion in 2025.
	16%
How much the Middle East and Africa beauty market grew in 2025, against roughly 6% globally. 
	$8.1 billion
Saudi Arabia’s 2025 beauty market, more than double the UAE’s $3.4 billion. 
	$164.90
Per-capita beauty spending in Saudi Arabia, ahead of most Western European markets. 
	12-14
The number of fragrances in the average Saudi home, versus two or three in Europe or North America, according to Givaudan. 
	35 million
The number of people in Saudi Arabia who use “four times more fragrance than anyone in Europe or the U.S.,” meaning the country’s consumption is equivalent to that of a country of 140 million people, according to Givaudan.
	$2.9 billion
Saudi Arabia’s fragrance market in 2025, heading for $5.5 billion by 2030. Its 13.8% annual growth rate is the fastest of any category in any GCC market. 
	7X
How much UAE fragrance exports grew over the past decade. The UAE is now one of the world’s top 10 perfume exporters by value.
	$54.8 million
Sales of Lattafa, the family-owned Emirati perfume house that has become the top-selling fragrance brand on TikTok Shop in the U.S., in 2025.
	Under 15%
Skin care’s share of the Middle East beauty market, versus 30% in the U.S. and over 50% in Asia.
	75%
The projected growth of Saudi premium beauty from 2025 to 2030, versus 32% for mass beauty.
	$317.8 million
Online beauty sales at the Saudi pharmacy chain Nahdi.
