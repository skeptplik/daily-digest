An evening celebrating the world’s best architecture and design
Taking place at the Royal Opera House in London on 16 November, the party will bring together shortlisted studios, judges and the international architecture and design community to celebrate this year’s projects and reveal the 2026 winners.
Guests will begin the evening with a champagne reception provided by this year’s exclusive drinks partner, Veuve Clicquot.
The champagne house will serve its signature cuvée, recognisable by its distinctive yellow labels, which have become synonymous with the brand since 1877.
Earlier this year, the brand partnered with British-Nigerian designer Yinka Ilori to create a colourful installation at Milan design week to to mark the launch of his Chasing the Sun gift collection.
Later in the evening, British architect and 2026 judge Norman Foster will deliver a keynote speech that will focus on the future of architecture, drawing on his position as one of the most influential architects of his generation.
Guests will then watch the highly anticipated announcement of this year’s Dezeen Awards winners, with 392 shortlisted projects competing in 49 categories across architecture, interiors and design.
The winners have been selected by this year’s panel of industry-leading judges, following last week’s announcement of the separate public vote, in which readers selected their favourite projects to win.
Category winners announced on the evening will then go on to compete for the prestigious overall project of the year titles in architecture, interiors and design.

Dezeen Awards 2026 in partnership with Trimble
Dezeen Awards is the ultimate accolade for architects and designers worldwide. The ninth edition of the annual awards is in partnership with global technology company Trimble. Through connected hardware and software solutions, Trimble helps designers to transform the way they shape spaces and buildings as well as drive productivity in the process. Trimble’s collaboration with Dezeen Awards demonstrates its ongoing commitment to inspiring and supporting creative excellence across the globe.
