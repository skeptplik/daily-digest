Former Australian captain Greg Chappell tells feuding cricketers to move on over Sandpapergate
In short:
Greg Chappell says Australian cricket has to "move on" from the controversy of Cape Town.
The former Australian skipper said Sandpapergate still had a way to go before it trumped the underarm controversy.
Chappell said that he hoped Pat Cummins' press conference had "put a bit of a line under it".
