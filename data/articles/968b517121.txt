Live: Deadly Russian attacks damage two bridges in Kyiv, mayor says
Russian attacks on Kyiv killed three people and damaged two bridges in the Ukrainian capital, city authorities said on Friday. The fresh wave of missile and drone attacks came after Russian President Vladimir Putin vowed a "painful" retaliation after Ukraine this week damaged the Crimea bridge.
