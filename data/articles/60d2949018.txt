French publisher and distributor Plug In Digital has acquired Paris-based indie publisher Twin Sails Interactive - formerly the digital division of boardgame publisher Asmodee, and publisher of News Tower - and announced the two will combine to form an indie-focused distribution and publishing group that combines "Plug In Digital’s global scale with Twin Sails Interactive’s hands-on creative collaboration."
Twin Sails CEO Nicolas Godement has stepped up to Group CEO of Plug In Digital Group. Francis Ingrand, founder and former CEO of Plug In Digital, will become Group Chairman, saying the firm had had to "navigate a challenging period.” The organisation unites Twin Sails with Plug In Digital's own label Dear Villagers, which published the acclaimed Caravan SandWitch and Fabledom and will continue to operate within the combined group, along with porting studio Pixel Ratio and dev studio Celsius Online.
"The rationale for this combination is strategic," Godement tells GamesIndustry.biz. "Both companies are financially sound. We’re joining forces because our strengths complement each other, putting us in a stronger position to compete in a highly competitive market." He cites Plug In Digital's range of additional support for games, including in-house porting expertise, a presence in China, and access to "hundreds of PC and mobile storefronts, subscription services and commercial deals."
The firm is funding new games with up €500,000 in development budget, along with guaranteed support marketing spend, and is aiming to publish "six to eight new titles per year," Godement says. "We focus on a select few games so our producers, game designers and marketers can work closely with each studio, helping shape great games and build lasting success."
Plug In Digital's own label Dear Villagers, which published the acclaimed Caravan SandWitch and Fabledom, will continue to operate within the combined group.
In a press release Godement said that "the economics of publishing indie games have changed dramatically."
"While finding the right market fit and delivering impactful marketing remain absolutely crucial, independent developers increasingly need extensive sales and distribution infrastructure to make their voices heard," he said. "We are building this group with long-term ambitions. By bringing Twin Sails Interactive's publishing track record together with Plug In Digital’s commercial scale, we are creating a company that can support developers sustainably, helping their games stand out and building long-term value around their IP."
Twin Sails separated from the Embracer-owned Asmodee Group in a management buyout in April 2025, having rebranded from Asmodee Digital in 2022. It originally specialized in digital versions of the board games like Gloomhaven, Carcasonne and Settlers of Catan before publishing a broader range of indie titles, and had a hit with News Tower, which exited Early Access last year and has sold 248,112 copies according to GameDiscoverCo data.
Twin Sails acquired its back catalogue from Asmodee as part of its departure, and Godement says it still represents "a significant share" of the firm's revenue.
At the time of the management buyout Godement told GamesIndustry.biz that the firm was seeking to "nurture and grow a line-up of beloved brands" that would enable it to build a steady business. He says that's still the goal. "Our announced games Ascenders: Beyond the Peak, Wind Runners and Sky Dust all have a strong identity and long-term potential – as do the multiple unannounced titles we currently have in development."
