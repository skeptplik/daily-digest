Everyone has that traveler in their life. That person who feels more at home on a 14-hour train ride than a quiet Sunday on the couch. They speak multiple languages, have a gut like a goat, and make friends wherever they go. Whether they’re your kindred spirit or the person you live vicariously through, one thing never changes: They always need new gear.
The Verge’s 2026 holiday gift guide for travelers
Globe-trotting can be a real slog, but with the right tools, hitting the open road (or air) doesn’t have to be a struggle.
That’s what makes travelers so fun to buy for. But you don’t want to get them just anything. The key is to find that clever gift even they didn’t know they needed, and that’s where we come in. From streaming gadgets for the hotel to portable espresso makers and high-capacity chargers, these are our favorite gifts to help travelers take their next adventure to the next level.

Every traveler needs a go-to backpack, and the Uniqlo Utility stands out for all the little things. Its minimalist, water-resistant design hides plenty of pockets on the front and sides, plus a roomy laptop sleeve and extras, including a handy key holder in the front pocket.
Few things are more frustrating on a long flight than trying to keep your phone stable between your seasoned pretzels and fellow passengers. Thankfully, MiiKare’s adjustable, inexpensive clamp can make things less stressful for your favorite frequent flyer, so they can finally watch Olympus Has Fallen in peace.
We all tend to skimp on simple items for ourselves, which is why this fancy-pants sleep mask makes such a thoughtful present. Its 3D contoured fit is designed to block all light while providing plush memory foam comfort so your loved one can get the rest they need between adventures.
For frequent flyers, a plain suitcase sticker may not be enough. In the age of QR codes, Pebblebee’s Link Bag Tags make a lot more sense. They’re battery-free, scannable by anyone with a phone, and offer built-in privacy with the ability to share or deny personal information.
They say once you go bidet, you never go back. That’s certainly harder to achieve when traveling outside Japan, though Tushy offers an effective solution with its handy, collapsible bidet. The manual contraption isn’t heated, but one portable bathroom luxury at a time.
Mechanism’s clever, durable creations are a favorite here at The Verge. The company’s foldable tray table mount is particularly brilliant, offering travelers an ergonomic way to mount devices — including larger gaming handhelds like the Steam Deck — for easy access on long flights. Just don’t forget to pick up the proper grip.
Built for jet setters, Anker’s 100W travel adapter features a durable design, a handy LCD display to track charge percentage, and compatibility in over 200 countries. It doesn’t support AC plugs, but it does have four USB inputs for all your portables, including a trio of USB-C ports.
What if you could give the gift of stress-free Wi-Fi on the go? That’s the promise of TP-Link’s dual-band Roam 7, a portable Wi-Fi 7 router that simplifies connecting to public portals so travelers can connect everything to a single device and be done with it.
For the fastidious person on your list who can’t stop polishing their camera, tablet, or other delicate piece of equipment, there’s the lightweight Magic Wrap. The foldable, plush fabric sticks to itself, with no zippers, edges, or even magnets to scuff or scratch your most precious gear.
You can’t project anything anywhere from your phone or watch (yet), but the Aurzen Zip makes that moment feel closer. Bright and durable, the foldable projector can mirror content from your phone or tablet on most flat surfaces, but fair warning: It doesn’t work with streaming services like Netflix without a $129.99 CastPlay HDMI dongle.
Whether your favorite traveler is a packing nerd or a slob who’s always losing everything (we’re not here to judge), everyone can use a little extra organization. Cocoon’s Grid-It accessories store it all, from chargers and gadgets to makeup and passports, with flexible straps to lock it all in.
Losing important documents on a trip can be disastrous. Bagsmart’s Blast Travel Pouch is a great way to keep them all safe, with multiple pockets, an easily adjustable strap, and even RFID blocking to deflect electronic thieves.
It’s time for a smarter robovac. Thankfully, there’s the Matic, which looks less like the rambling discs that pervade the space and more like an actual robot that, well, vacuums. It doesn’t get under beds, but it also doesn’t get stuck under them, and it does a great job providing truly no-fuss cleaning thanks to its superb mapping.
One charging port, two phones. That’s not a problem with Anker’s dual-headed USB-C cable, which offers either 4 or 6 feet of length to provide game-changing versatility when you need it most.
Who says you can’t travel with a big screen? The Xreal One Pro feature dual micro-OLED displays to project a virtual 171-inch screen from your device of choice. A 57-degree field of view, max 120Hz refresh rate, and built-in speakers round things out, delivering portable cinematic entertainment for one.
Apple’s AirPods Pro are the epitome of wireless earbud refinement, with fantastic noise-canceling and transparency modes, a light and airy fit, and clear sound thanks to ear tips that seal your ears without plugging them up. The latest pair continues the quest for perfection with faster processing, more features, and better ergonomics than ever.
Oura didn’t reinvent the wheel with the Ring 5, but it did reshape it into a slimmer, thinner, more wearable design that could make all the difference. Inside are the same extensive health and fitness-tracking features for charting everything from sleep to heart health, backed by long battery life.
Whether you’re white-water rafting or trekking through a damp jungle, keeping your gear dry is essential. The Sea to Summit dry bag is a slick, affordable solution with clever features, including a reflective interior and a D-ring buckle to secure it to boats and bikes. The solid warranty is just a plus.
SwitchBot’s powerful, battery-powered fan is a big upgrade over your average plug-in. As a 3D circulator, it can tilt to push air in all directions. You can also control it via an app, and it even detaches from its stand to sit on a desk or tabletop. Quiet and versatile, it’s equipped for nearly any adventure on or off the grid.
Espresso in the woods? Yes, please. The GR2’s magical mechanics make it possible, letting you turn 12 ounces of grounds into espresso without power. Its carrying case houses all the accessories needed to get that sweet shot of bean, making real coffee available virtually anywhere.
Theragun’s latest therapeutic cure for sore muscles is as packable as it is luxurious. The third-gen Mini includes clever features like a travel lock to keep it off during transport, guided routines, and USB-C charging. There’s even a Y2K edition for the ’90s kid in all of us.
For travelers hitting the road when the thermometer is rising, cooling off is essential. Nitecore’s long-lasting izzCool 10 Pro offers heat relief with a powerful fan and fillable reservoir that mists you for a refreshing respite from the sun. It even works with larger containers to double as a nighttime humidifier.
Every traveler needs quality noise-canceling for long flights, but flagship headphones are becoming increasingly spendy. Sony’s former flagship WH-1000XM4 were so good, they’ve been bestsellers through two new generations. Now the company has revamped them in the XM4C, adding minor improvements to a stone-cold classic at a nice price.
Packing light is essential for all travelers, but it’s particularly vital for those who trek long distances. This high-capacity Nitecore charger is a killer addition to any pack, with water resistance, fast charging, and a slim, lightweight design that can power your devices without weighing them down.
The Switch 2 leverages the same ideas as its predecessor, only with a larger, 7.9-inch display, a faster chip, and a bevy of new social features. However, its most important trait is that it’s the newest Nintendo console, which means access to a host of weird, beautiful, family-friendly games that only Nintendo could dream up, now on a better screen.
Every traveler’s been there. You get to the hotel or rental place, go to plug in your gear, and there’s only one outlet available. Fortunately, Tessan’s pack-friendly power strip offers all the ports, with multiple USB-C, USB-A, and AC inputs to charge everything in one place.
If you have an avid road tripper on your list, Ugreen’s four-port car charger blends slick design with serious utility. More importantly, it has a built-in retractable USB-C cable that delivers up to 60W of power and keeps things tidy.
For on-the-go creators, it’s all about capturing the moment in the highest video quality possible. Insta360’s Luna Pro steadycam offers plenty of features to get the job done right, including 4K vertical video, swift file transfers, four hours of battery life, and a detachable touchscreen that doubles as a remote.
Buying your loved one a pair of shoes is tougher than you might think, but a gift card to The Walking Company is a step in the right direction. The store offers a wide variety of ultra-comfortable kicks, boots, sandals, and even orthotics, along with a shoe finder to help them find the right fit.
Retroid has had its stumbles, but the Pocket 5 is a hit, making it a great gift for the globe-trotting gamer. With a 5.5-inch OLED screen, a light yet durable design, and tons of titles available from the Google Play Store, the portable deck provides plenty of reasons to keep playing through the runway taxi.
If you have a friend who constantly complains about their partner’s snoring habits, Soundcore’s Sleep A30 could be a gift they’ll never forget. With a comfy design, even for side sleepers, and advanced noise-canceling tech that actively adjusts to mask unwanted sounds, they’re a recipe for sweet, silent relief.
New parents need everything, all at once, and traveling always raises the stakes. The Hatch Go is the sound machine they’ll be thanking you for well into toddlerhood. Offering 10 soothing sounds from a crazy-compact frame with up to 15 hours of juice, it’s simple, effective, and essential.
How is your giftee supposed to bring you back something special when they’re already overpacked? The Matador Packable Duffel, of course. The durable, 30-liter carrier breaks down to micro size, then easily expands to hold loads of overseas paraphernalia. It’s a win-win!
