The winter season is just around the corner, which means the time to stock up on warm outerwear options is now. The best shearling jackets and coats are the wardrobe solution to get through the snow storms, polar vortexes, and mind-numbingly cold temperatures we will certainly face. A puffer coat might seem like the most sensible choice in most of these scenarios, but shearling coats and jackets provide a little more polish to every look, while still offering the warmth necessary to tide us over until spring.
Vogue’s Favorite Shearling Jackets and Coats:
Shearling outerwear has long been a wardrobe essential. It’s sturdy, durable, and made to last forever in your coat closet. Their timeless reputation makes them the perfect investment piece, an important fact to consider when shopping for items that you can love and own for years, and perhaps pass down one day. You can find faux options, otherwise billed as fleece or teddy, that look and feel just as good as the real thing.
Think of these shearling pieces as the easy outfit elevators in your late-fall and winter wardrobe, with plenty of styling possibilities. You need not go further than the FW26 runways for a jolt of inspiration. In Paris, Alaïa teamed impeccably tailored leather options, outfitted with sweeping asymmetric hemlines and monochromatic-toned shearling at the collar, with sporty joggers and semi-sheer stilettos. In New York, Altuzarra went the downtown-cool route with its offerings, pairing oversized shearling-adorned aviator jackets with Fair Isle knits and an array of leather and suede trousers in neutral olive and cream tones. This, in all honesty, is how I’ve been styling my favorite shearling jackets and coats for years: Over equally sumputous textures that lean into their inherent cozy-cool design ethos.
