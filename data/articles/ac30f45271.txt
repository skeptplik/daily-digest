Multiple myeloma patients have been able to utilize a surge of powerful new immunotherapies in recent years, including CAR-T therapy and bispecific antibodies like Carvykti and Tecvayli from Johnson & Johnson. But myeloma, a blood cancer, can often develop resistance against these treatments, leading to relapse.
A new biotech called Caronilex is developing a next generation CAR-T therapy that may help circumvent that resistance, and the therapy’s promising safety profile could make it a potential candidate for use against autoimmune diseases as well.
Researchers showed the therapy, called TriPRIL, was able to reduce or eliminate signs of cancer in 12 of 15 patients with relapsed or refractory multiple myeloma, many of whom had prior CAR-T therapy, in a small, early phase trial published last week in The Lancet.
