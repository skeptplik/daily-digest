The Declasse Vamos is a car that you can steal for Moses in Grand Theft Auto 6. However, when you find the Declasse Vamos, you realize that it's locked away inside a garage, and you need to find the door code to get to it.
Luckily, the code can be found nearby, but it's not as clear as some of the other safe codes in GTA 6. You won't find any notes that explicitly tell you what the code is, but if you pay close attention to your surroundings, you'll find a common number that just so happens to be the code.
Here's what the Declasse Vamos code is in GTA 6, and how to find it.
Declasse Vamos code location in GTA 6
Before you can steal the Declasse Vamos for Moses, you need to first steal the initial three cars that he asks you to look out for. Then, Moses will eventually hit you up with more vehicles to steal, which will include the souped-up Declasse Vamos. The Declasse Vamos is the car that's located at the eastern edge of Grayson, in Lummox County.
The Declasse Vamos you need to steal for Moses is locked in the standalone garage beside the house. On the west side of the garage, you'll find a door and a keypad that you need a four-digit code for.
To find the code, you need to go inside the house to the west of the garage, but it's important to note that there are a few people inside. In our experience, there was a person sleeping in the living room and a few people talking on the back porch. You can either go guns a-blazing or sneak in.
There isn't a note that will explicitly tell you "this is the code for the garage." Instead, you need to look for a four-digit number that could be the code. Luckily, there's one plastered across the walls of the house: 2727.
What is the Declasse Vamos code in GTA 6?
The keypad code for the garage is 2-7-2-7. Input the code and the door to the garage will unlock. Head inside, and make sure to open the garage door by pressing the button to its right.
From here, you're free to get into the car and drive off. The people inside the house will notice that you're stealing their car and try to shoot you, but if you drive off fast enough, they won't be following you for long. Take the car back to Moses, and he'll pay you $4,135 dollars.
