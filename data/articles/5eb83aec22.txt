Japanese soccer official Kozo Tashima said on Friday that the handling of Folarin Balogun’s red card at the World Cup was what prompted him to run for the role of FIFA president against Gianni Infantino.
FIFA suspended forward Balogun’s automatic one-match ban for a red card in July, allowing the American striker to play against Belgium after U.S. President Donald Trump said he had personally asked Infantino to review the sanction.
The decision triggered a swift and fiery backlash and, along with an aborted attempt to sell a stake in FIFA tournaments, fueled opposition to Infantino’s reelection as president.
