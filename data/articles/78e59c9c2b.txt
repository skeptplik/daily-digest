Ukraine struck a key bridge between mainland Russia and occupied Crimea days before Russian President Vladimir Putin is due to meet U.S. President Donald Trump and Chinese President Xi Jinping.
The attack is a show of Kyiv’s military strength right as concerns are growing that the gathering of the three leaders in Shenzhen could add pressure on the war-ravaged country in peace talks with Russia. It’s also likely to delay a resumption of grain shipping in the area.
One of the arches and a span of the bridge’s road section collapsed as a result of the attack, the second in as many days, Ukraine’s General Staff said on Tuesday. The structure stretches 19 kilometers across the Kerch Strait and was a signature project for Putin after he annexed Crimea in 2014.
