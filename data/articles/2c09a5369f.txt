Ownership of Château Miraval, a prestigious estate in the Var, France, was one of the main points of contention in Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie’s divorce.
Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie have resolved a long-running legal battle, a decade after the A-list celebrity couple split.
The actors have ended a dispute over the ownership of a French winery, saying in a joint statement: “We have settled out of court in the best interests of our family and will not be commenting further.”
Pitt, 62, and Jolie, 51, had bought a controlling interest in Château Miraval in 2008 and wed there six years later.
In the lawsuit, Pitt argued that Jolie acted without his permission in selling her share in the winery to Russian oligarch Yuri Sheflzer, stating that he and Jolie had agreed not to sell their stakes without the permission of the other.
Jolie counter-sued, accusing Pitt of drunkenly attacking her and their children on a flight, prompting her to file for divorce in 2016.
Pitt was not charged following an FBI and child services investigation into the incident and he denied the allegations.
They reached a divorce settlement in 2024 after an eight-year legal battle.
All six of the Pitt-Jolie children are now adults, with many of them having dropped their father's surname.
