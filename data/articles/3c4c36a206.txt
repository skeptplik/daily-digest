Australian racehorse So You Are has died from a "severe bout of colic", just 13 days after finishing eighth in the Melbourne Cup.
Trainer Bjorn Baker announced today that the five-year-old had been rushed to hospital with colic, which refers to abdominal pain in horses.
"We are devastated to share the news that So You Are has died following a severe bout of colic," the statement said.
"He had been enjoying a well-earned spell when he was found showing signs of colic this morning. He was rushed to a specialist equine hospital for emergency veterinary treatment, but sadly could not be saved.
"Beyond his achievements on the track, So You Are was a much-loved horse, and his loss will be deeply felt by everyone who knew and cared for him."
So You Are was trained by Baker at Warwick Farm in Sydney, and had a terrific spring where he won four times, including a trio of Group 3 races at Rosehill, Randwick and Bendigo.
He was owned by Stuart Ramsey, a long-time breeder and racehorse owner, and his wife Trish.
So You Are ran in the Melbourne Cup 13 days ago, finishing eighth in a race won by Point of Law.
So You Are was running second in the home straight with about 600 metres left in the race, leading the chase for long-time leader Royal Supremacy.
