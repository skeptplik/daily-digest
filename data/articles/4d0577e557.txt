Usman Khawaja and David Warner sidelined from commentary duties as Cricket Australia backs Pat Cummins' captaincy
In short:
Fox Cricket has rested Usman Khawaja and David Warner from commentary duties during Australia's T20 series against England.
The decision comes as Cricket Australia's board unanimously backed Pat Cummins as captain of Australia's men's Test and one-day teams.
Cummins met with CA chair David Boon and CEO Todd Greenberg on Friday and says he will partake in the governing body's upcoming truth-telling "forum" regarding the 2018 tour of South Africa.
