China’s top general targets PLA arms in call for joint military-civilian corruption watch
Zhang Shengmin urges better ‘information-sharing, coordinated action and joint sanctions’ mechanisms in article on fifth plenum outcomes
General Zhang Shengmin, vice-chairman of the Central Military Commission (CMC) and a former PLA anti-corruption chief, made the remarks in a supplementary reader for the resolution adopted at the Communist Party’s latest plenum.
Published earlier this month, the collection features contributions from dozens of senior officials across various policy areas, offering explanations of the resolution to “deepen party self-governance” adopted at the Central Committee’s fifth plenum held last month.
