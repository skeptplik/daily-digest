Amanda Seyfried made a case for satin pumps at the “Steps” movie premiere at The Plaza hotel in New York City on Sunday with a style from Stuart Weitzman on foot.
The Stuart Power 100, made by Stuart Weitzman artisans in Spain, is an updated version of an icon silhouette. It reinterprets the brand’s signature pointed-toe Stuart pump with extra style and comfort. The construction was reworked to offer improved support, and a new foam insole provides additional flexibility and comfort — all packaged together in a modern and sleek silhouette. The style is completed with a 100mm stiletto heel, which is carefully placed at a slight angle for added stability.
Seyfried wore the Stuart Power 100 in a cream satin iteration, which conferred a soft and delicate aura to the silhouette. Satin heels have been embraced by a range of brands and celebrities lately, from Dua Lipa in black Schiaparelli slingbacks to Ziwe in unique thong heels from Courrèges.
