Live: Zelensky and Kushner confirm that Europe will join next round of Russia-Ukraine talks
US President Donald Trump's envoy and son-in-law Jared Kushner said on Thursday Russia and Ukraine agreed to expand the next round of talks to include European nations and that Washington was working toward what Kushner called the war's "largest prisoner exchange." Follow our liveblog for the latest updates.
• US special envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner met with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky in Dublin following their talks with his Russian counterpart Vladimir Putin in Moscow.
• Zelensky said that Ukrainian military units had recorded new and significant disruptions to the Starshield satellite communications service in recent days, affecting the entire front line and Russian-occupied parts of Ukraine.
• Putin indicated that the Kremlin would not compromise on its hardline demands to end the war in Ukraine, but offered a short-term ceasefire in a key frontline city during a meeting with US envoys.
(FRANCE 24 with Reuters, AP and AFP)
