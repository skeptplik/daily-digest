Marvel Studios has confirmed Kate Bishop actress Hailee Steinfeld as a last-minute addition to the cast of Avengers: Doomsday.
Marvel Studios announced the surprise return of Hawkeye's Disney+ partner in a nonchalant Instagram reel tonight. Serving as promotion for Steinfeld's other new Disney movie, Hexed, the video sees her jokingly say someone delivered a chair "from a different movie," but the post's caption makes things pretty clear.
"Catch Hailee Steinfeld in Disney's Hexed before she returns in Avengers: Doomsday," the post said.
You'd be forgiven for thinking Steinfeld was already included in Doomsday's already enormous lineup of superheroes and A-listers. Everyone from Robert Downey Jr. and Pedro Pascal to Tom Hiddleston has previously been confirmed to make an appearance of some kind, but Steinfeld was one of a few names to have been curiously benched up until this point.
She first appeared as the bow-wielding Kate Bishop in the Jeremy Renner-led 2021 show, Hawkeye. Aside from a The Marvels cameo and some animated voice work, Steinfeld hasn't spent too much time in the Marvel Cinematic Universe as the multiverse saga has rolled on. That won't stop her from helping out in the first chapter of its finale, though.
With the cast already stacked, it's hard to know just how prominently her character will be featured amongst the chaos. Between the Avengers, the New Avengers, Fantastic Four, and X-Men, there will likely be little room to explore how she gets along with the rest of the crew. For many, just the fact that she'll help in the fight against Doctor Doom is probably enough.
Avengers: Doomsday premieres December 18, 2026. That's a little less than one month away, but Marvel's made it clear that it's saved plenty of surprises.
Another major MCU name was teased for the movie when VisionQuest wrapped up earlier this month. Fans are expecting at least one more trailer to drop between now and the film's premiere, and if Captain Carter actress Hayley Atwell is to be believed, we won't have to wait much longer to see it.
