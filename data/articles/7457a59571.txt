Sensor Tower estimates Grand Theft Auto 6 sold over 24.5 million copies through pre-orders and on launch day, generating approximately $2.36 billion in revenue.
"Grand Theft Auto 6 has captivated consumers, becoming 2026's best-selling game based on pre-orders alone," Karl Kontus, Sensor Tower's SVP of Video Game Insights told Deadline.
He added that "total pre-order revenue exceeded $2 billion through launch day."
According to the market intelligence firm, 75% of sales were on PlayStation, with approximately 18.4 million copies sold on PS5 and 6.1 million on Xbox Series X|S.
Niko Partners estimates GTA 6 will generate over $3 billion in global sales by the end of the year.
In addition to record revenue, GTA 6 set new benchmarks for critical reception and streaming audiences.
The game reached 6.38 million concurrent streaming viewers within 90 minutes of launch and is currently ranked as Metacritic's top game of all time, with a score of 99 based on 151 reviews.
Following the game's record-breaking launch, Rockstar co-founder Sam Houser thanked players for their support.
"Whether you can trace your journey to this game through the entire series or are experiencing Grand Theft Auto for the first time, this is a narrative set in a world full of possibilities – and it is up to you as the player to shape how it ultimately feels and concludes."
"We hope it felt both familiar and entirely new, and above all, we hope that you had fun. We are incredibly grateful to you and everything that you do to celebrate games. We look forward to many more years of sharing these experiences with you."
