As we all know, off-duty models are the most elite source of personal style inspo. After years in the modeling industry — working in Paris, New York and Miami for houses like Dior, Chanel and Saint Laurent — Magdalena understands better than most what makes for an effortlessly memorable look, from the interplay between clothing and accessories, to the innate confidence needed to pull it all off. Now based in Palm Beach, Florida, she’s brought her expertise to a second career: designer and founder of NAYLA Jewelry.
NAYLA offers the jewelry we’re always looking for: It’s sophisticated but not stuffy, romantic but still grounded, and stands out without overpowering your look. Inspired by the relaxed energy of Palm Beach and its ocean backdrop, every piece has a certain indefinable pull.
“The ocean has always fascinated me,” Magdalena tells Fashionista. “Its colors, its movement and the mystery beneath the surface are an endless source of inspiration.” Another fascination of hers: natural gemstones. “Each one has its own light, its own character and a beauty that could never be exactly reproduced,” she shares.
It all ties back to Magdalena’s central thesis: that meaningful jewelry shouldn’t be reserved for special occasions, but worn every day. NAYLA’s website is filled to the brim with versatile pieces that truly let the wearer shine — not that they don’t offer a good amount of sparkle on their own. An onyx pendant might dial up an already-moody look or add edge to a jeans-and-tee moment. A turquoise bracelet can top off an eclectic Western-inspired outfit or bring a more formal, serious ensemble down to earth. A moonstone ring, with it’s muted colors and shine, pairs easily with virtually anything. Every NAYLA piece inevitably looks and feels like something you’ve had forever.
