Bilkis Bano case: Supreme Court grants bail to two convicts
The Supreme Court of India suspended the life sentences and granted bail to two of the 11 convicts in the a 2002 gang rape and murder case on Monday.
The top court noted that the two convicts have already spent more than 17 years in jail and one of them is severely handicapped, Indian legal news outlet Live Law reported.
The two-judge bench passed the order against the Bombay High Court's May order, upholding their conviction and sentence.
Bipin Chandra Joshi and Pradeep Mordhiya are among the 11 convicts whose remission the Supreme Court quashed in 2024.
The Bilkis Bano case dates back to 2002, when the state of Gujarat was hit by communal riots during which some 2,000 people were killed — of whom most were Muslims, according to Human Rights Watch.
Bano was five months pregnant when she was gang-raped during the riots while her three-year-old daughter and six other family members were killed.

Sabarimala temple in Kerala opens for annual pilgrimage
The Sabarimala temple in the southern state of Kerala will open at 5 p.m. local time on Monday ahead of the annual Mandala pilgrimage season, which begins on November 17.
The temple attracts millions of devotees during the two-month-long pilgrimage, which starts in November.
While the pilgrimage season, which marks the first day of the Malayalam calendar, begins on Tuesday, the temple doors will open to pilgrims on Monday evening.
Reportedly, cameras equipped with AI features have been installed as part of security and to improve crowd management.
In 2011, Sabarimala saw a stampede which killed more than 100 people. More than 30 million pilgrims had visited the temple during that year's pilgrimage season.
Delhi building collapse leaves eight injured
At least eight people were injured after part of an under-construction hotel collapsed in Old Delhi’s Paharganj area on Sunday, police said.
This is the second such incident in less than 10 days. Earlier this month, a building collapse in Old Delhi's Chawri Bazar claimed seven lives.
According to authorities, a call was received around 4:40 pm on Sunday, claiming that a house had collapsed in Paharganj.
When a team of firemen reached the spot, they found a two-storey structure collapsed in a narrow lane, Indian newspaper The Indian Express reported.
"A total of eight people sustained injuries and were shifted to Lady Hardinge Medical College and Hospital for treatment," Indian news channel NDTV reported, citing a senior police official.
Indian PM Modi to attend ASEAN summit in Manila today
Prime Minister Narendra Modi is expected to leave for Manila, in the Philippines, to attend the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN)-India summit and the 21st East Asia Summit.
The ASEAN meeting will focus on three main issues: maritime security, economic cooperation and development partnership.
According to the Ministry of External Affairs (MEA), the Prime Minister will attend a dinner hosted by Philippine President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. on November 16 before participating in the India-ASEAN Summit and the East Asia Summit process on November 17.
During his two-day visit to Manila, Modi is also likely to meet US President Donald Trump, Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi and Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese as the leaders of the Quad group meet on the sidelines of the ASEAN summit.
US Secretary of State Marco Rubio had indicated last month that the Quad meeting was planned alongside the wider meetings in the Philippines.
Announcing his visit to Manila, PM Modi said in a post on X, "Will be in Manila, Philippines later today and tomorrow to attend the following programmes: 23rd ASEAN-India Summit. 21st East Asia Summit."
