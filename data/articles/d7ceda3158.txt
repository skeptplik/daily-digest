Lord of the Rings: Fate of the Fellowship
This is one of the best Lord of the Rings board games on the market right now. We gave it an 8 out of 10 score in our review:
"Lord of the Rings: Fate of the Fellowship expands on the 2008's Pandemic game system and manages to create one of the best Lord of the Ring tabletop experiences out there, but its increased reliance on random card draws and dice rolls can lead to some turns feeling wasted, or that you are biding your time for what you need, detracting from the game's excitement." - Scott White
Heat: Pedal to the Metal
If you're looking for a racing board game, look no further than this one right here. We gave this game a 9 out of 10 in our review:
"Heat: Pedal to the Metal is a vivid and delightful racing board game that surpasses the accomplishments of all that came before it. It’s an inspired title in both product and design, offering a range of options while presenting a dynamic racing system that is extremely impressive." - Charlie Theel
Harmonies
For a fun and unique family board game, Harmonies is an excellent choice. We gave it an 8 out of 10 in our review:
"Harmonies masterfully blends its drafting system and token-laying mechanics to create a satisfying gameplay loop with just the right amount of tension in its decision-making. At its core, it's an elegant optimization puzzle that offers a fresh challenge every time you play. The added verticality introduces another layer of strategy as you work to build the perfect mix of habitats. Its playful, inviting design features vibrant colors and beautifully illustrated animal cards that are a joy to engage with throughout the game. It's quick to set up and easy to teach, making it a great fit for families and couples, while still offering enough crunchy decisions to keep seasoned players satisfied." - Matt Adler
7 Wonders
If you're looking for a fun strategy game with civilization elements, 7 Wonders will be right up your alley. It's not for everyone, but it's still worth picking up at this price. We gave it a 7 out of 10 in our review:
"You can’t argue with the sheer number of copies that 7 Wonders has sold, nor the plethora of awards that it has accrued. This is a fun game, a good way to pass a half hour, with a great balance of luck and strategy. But it got that way by spreading itself quite thin, making a lot of gamers fairly happy rather than bringing ecstasy to smaller fanbases. There are times when the lack of theme and long-term strategy makes it feel more like an exercise than a game and it’s a tad too involved for casual family play. But whatever kind of games you like, if you fancy taking the plunge, you’re almost guaranteed to find plenty of enjoyment." - Matt Thrower
