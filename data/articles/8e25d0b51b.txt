Many of us have found ourselves in that pinch where we’re trying to put together a last-minute Halloween costume. And it’s an experience that even Dua Lipa shares, as evidenced by her latest Instagram post.
The English pop singer documented her and husband Callum Turner’s costumes in a new carousel yesterday afternoon, which she captioned: “When the halloween costume comes together 5 mins before leaving the house.”
When you’re one of the biggest pop stars in the world though, you don’t turn to Dollar Tree to make the outfit come together. Instead, you turn to some of the latest runway collections.
For the holiday, Lipa ended up dressing like a gothic witch, thanks to some help from a little black maxi dress pulled from the Lou De Bètoly Fall/Winter 2026 ready-to-wear collection.
The see-through design featured a bustier-style bodice pinned with a tiny bow in the center, while the body of the dress was composed of a sheer fabric embroidered with beaded floral lace.
Lipa layered the dress under a fluffy fur coat in matching black, while adding a statement, multi-strand rosary necklace from the Gucci Fall/Winter 2002 collection by Tom Ford, sourced by Era London.
To tie everything together, Lipa accentuated the look with smudgy black eye makeup and thick mascara, while rocking a pair of pointy black nails as well.
Turner, meanwhile, wore a clown mask worn by the character Chains in the video game Payday 2.
Leave it to Lipa to transform her Halloween costume into a high-fashion moment.
