There are two types of people come winter: those who have already planned their cold-weather wardrobes down to the last accessory, and those who would happily spend the next three months wrapped in a blanket. Nike’s latest winter essentials offer a stylish compromise (thankfully).
The brand’s approach to cold-weather dressing is rooted in the sort of practicality that fashion has become increasingly comfortable embracing. Cozy fleece, sporty silhouettes, and substantial outerwear are no longer confined to early-morning workouts or ski weekends. They’ve become perfectly respectable components of a well-dressed wardrobe, particularly when styled with a little imagination.
A fleece jacket with tailored trousers? Absolutely. A puffer thrown over an otherwise carefully assembled outfit? Arguably the most sensible decision you’ll make all day. The beauty of Nike’s winter lineup is that it doesn’t ask you to choose between dressing for the weather and dressing for yourself.
And then there’s the holiday shopping situation. While finding the perfect gift for someone with very specific tastes can feel like an Olympic sport, good winter essentials tend to be a much safer bet. They’re useful, easy to incorporate into an existing wardrobe, and unlikely to inspire the dreaded question of where to store them.
Of course, the real challenge may be resisting the temptation to shop for yourself. But given the number of cold mornings still ahead, a little wardrobe self-interest seems perfectly reasonable.
Nike Studio Fleece Medium Weight
Sometimes the most convincing wardrobe additions are the least complicated. Nike’s oversized crew gets the basics right with a smooth exterior, softly brushed fleece, and just enough structure to avoid looking shapeless. But don’t mistake simplicity for boring. With its chocolate-brown cow print and bright red Swoosh, this sweatshirt has absolutely no intention of blending into the herd.
Nike Tech Boreas
Named after the Greek god of winter, these pants have rather ambitious expectations to live up to. Thankfully, their plush fleece lining, water-repellent finish, and heat-retaining Therma-FIT technology make a convincing case.
