Veja is teaming up with Marimekko for a limited-edition sneaker collaboration.
This new tie-up sees the Finnish lifestyle design house bring its bold prints to the French footwear brand’s adult Salar and kids’ Small Canary sneaker silhouettes.
For this collaboration, Veja and Marimekko chose the Popkorni print, a floral motif from 2024 designed by Finnish artist Antti Kekki. The print, which means “popcorn” in Finnish, was painted by Kekki through paper-cutting and resembles little fluttering flowers.
Looking closer, the Salar features an alveomesh and suede upper with suede and organically traced leather panels. The Small Canary’s upper is made of the same alveomesh and suede panels. Both shoes are completed with insole consisting of a bio-based EVA made from sugar cane. Other materials used in the production of this sneaker include Amazonian rubber, mineral silica, recycled polyester and organic cotton.

“We wanted a design rooted in Marimekko’s archives and recognizable at first sight, offering a fresh take on its iconic floral patterns,” Caroline Bulliot, art director at Veja, told FN. “Antti Kekki’s Popkorni felt like a natural choice: we can feel the joy, the movement, an electric and artistic edge. Marimekko has a unique ability to give artists room to express themselves freely. We share a deep appreciation for the people behind each product, refined materials, and craftsmanship.”
This release follows two other shoe collaborations released this year by Veja. In September, the footwear brand teamed up with fellow French brand Arpenteur to release its first-ever sneaker without its iconic “V” logo on the side panel.
In February, Veja released an updated take on its Panenka sneaker with Magliano, the Italian fashion brand founded by Luca Magliano. Inspired by vintage football boots, the unisex shoe featured a folded tongue over the upper, reminiscent of classic cleat styles.
The new Veja x Marimekko shoe collection, which retails between $125 and $235, is now available.
