We’re in the thick of Black Friday season, a great time to get ahead on gifts for the holidays. I happen to have a recommendation for the Pokémon fan in your life, and I won't judge if that's yourself.
I got the new Pokémon Character Guide Book a couple of months ago, and it’s now a prized possession on my coffee table. The recent release from Pikachu Press is basically your own hardcover version of a Pokédex, collecting illustrations and information about every single Pokémon from across the video games, TV shows, and trading card scene.
Amazon has discounted both the standard and box set editions of the book to their lowest ever prices, but if you're looking for max savings (or gift-giving), the standard book is also included in a particularly electric Black Friday BOGO sale.
New Pokémon Deluxe Character Guide Book Is the Perfect Gift
Pokémon Deluxe Character Guide (Pokemon Pikachu Press)
This hardcover character book was released back in August as part of Pokémon’s 30th anniversary celebrations. The book itself features all 1,025 existing Pokémon (not including the newly revealed Gen 10 starters) in chapters dedicated to each region.
Aside from Amazon’s current discount, which drops the book's price to an all-time low, you can also save on the Pokémon guide in a '3 for 2' sale. Add 3 eligible items to your cart, and Amazon automatically discounts the cheapest item to $0.
The limited box set edition also has a 16% discount, but unfortunately isn’t included in the larger sale. This more expensive version comes in a big collector’s box that, on top of the book itself, comes packed with an exclusive TCG playmat, collectible prints, and a Certificate of Authenticity.
Getting the book actually inspired me to start (another) Fire Red run now that I can play on my Switch 2, and I've been enjoying flipping to each Pokémon’s entry as they appear in-game. Because the book highlights little details from every iteration of the franchise, there’s always something new for even dedicated veterans to learn.
The gold-lined hardcover book also has a pretty luxurious feel that allows it to blend in with an otherwise 'traditional' stack of coffee books on my living room table. I’m consistently entertained by guests who are either very familiar with or deeply confused by Pokémon flipping through its contents, and forcing them to pick a favorite.
