- In Q2, Burberry marked five consecutive quarters of growth, up 4% in Q2 and H1.
- The Americas led growth (11%), driven by local consumer engagement and strong in-store conversion. Greater China followed (5%), carried by local spending, while the Asia-Pacific region also grew (3%).
- Europe, Middle East, India, and Africa (EMEIA) dragged on group revenues, falling 2% due to declining tourism.
- In the first half of the fiscal year, wholesale climbed 17%, retail increased 4%, and licensing declined 5%.
- Gross profit margin increased in H1 to 68.3%, up from 67.9%.
- In H1, womenswear, menswear, childrenswear, and accessories all grew.
