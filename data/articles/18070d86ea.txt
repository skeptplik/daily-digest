Surely a camera puts the lie to the idea that the world exists only when we are present? A picture taken from a remote camera, without any humans present, shows us the same world as our eyes do. The geological record shows that the world existed before us and that the natural forces we experience today have been present for billions of years. Our senses tell us that rock is solid; the geological record tells us that rocks have always been solid.

The geological record can be used to infer the past landscapes and processes before humans evolved, and we infer this without using our senses – only our intellect. The structure of animals and plants that were present before humans tells us that they reacted to the same forces (such as gravity and light) as we do today. This implies that there is an objective reality out there – whether observed or not – and that our senses, and those of other animals, are quite good at describing it.
