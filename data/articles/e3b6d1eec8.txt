Music retailer Tower Records Japan said customer information was leaked following unauthorized access, making it the nation’s latest retailer to disclose a data breach.
The intrusion exposed login information for about 10.2 million accounts, including email addresses and user IDs, the company said in a statement updated Sunday. The unauthorized access was detected on Thursday, it said.
Tower Records’ disclosure follows a data leak involving online apparel retailer Zozo, which said on Friday that information belonging to about 15.2 million customers had been compromised. Zozo said it was affected by the breach of a Plaid Inc. marketing platform that the retailer uses to manage customer information; Marui Group, which uses the same platform, has also reported a similar incident.
Japanese companies’ longstanding reliance on traditional IT systems has frequently been cited as a vulnerability in the face of increasingly sophisticated cyberattacks. The impact of recent weaknesses found in PHP, a widely used programming language, has been particularly evident in Japan.
