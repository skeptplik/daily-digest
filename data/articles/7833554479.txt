No shade to those who love their subscription services, but viewing physical movies is a time-honored tradition that shouldn’t go away. Watching them doesn’t feel all that different, but deciding which movie to watch feels less annoying; sifting through a movie collection on your shelf or in a storage binder is so much more fun (and less overwhelming) than scrolling endlessly in apps.
Many of our favorite Blu-ray movies are over 50 percent off for Black Friday
Deck the shelves with discounted films, box sets, animated series, and more at some of the lowest prices we’ve seen.
While standard Blu-ray players that display movies at up to 1080p are fairly inexpensive, 4K Blu-ray counterparts that can show movies in all their 4K HDR glory are significantly pricier (but totally worth it if you’re a movie buff). No matter the player you own, there are always Blu-ray deals, and the discounts are especially good during Black Friday.
