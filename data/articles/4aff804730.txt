The Crimean Bridge was attacked for a third time this week, a Russian official said on Thursday.
Traffic had been temporarily suspended, according to Crimea's Russian-installed governor.
"The damage is currently being assessed," Sergei Aksyonov, the governor, said on Telegram, adding that emergency services were at the scene.
The bridge, which supports a four-lane roadway and a railroad, is the only bridge linking mainland Russia with occupied Crimea.
Crimean Bridge attacked for a third time this week
This is the third Ukrainian attack this week targeting the strategic bridge that is a key symbol of Russia's annexation of Crimea in 2014.
The bridge has played a key role in supplying Russian troops in occupied parts of southern Ukraine since the 2022 Russian invasion, making it a key target for Kyiv.
Steel arch of bridge collapses after Ukrainian drone strikes earlier this week
On Tuesday, Ukraine carried out a major attack on the bridge using missiles and drones, destroying the steel arch of the 19-kilometer-long road section of the crossing.
Ukraine's SBU security service had said that the "road span beneath it (the arch) has been completely destroyed."
The latest attack appears to have impacted rail traffic on the bridge, according to German press agency DPA.
The Ukrainian attack came just hours before an unprecedented meeting scheduled between Putin and his US and Chinese counterparts on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit.
