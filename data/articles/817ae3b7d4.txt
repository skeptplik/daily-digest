The worst COP ever? Climate talks collapse in chaos
Issued on:
Share
The COP31 climate talks ended in failure Sunday as a contentious deal collapsed over fossil fuels and the summit in Turkey stretched into record overtime. The final agreement was rejected in a chaotic finale to a UN climate summit that ran two days over schedule, underscoring the strains on diplomacy in an increasingly fractured world. France 24’s Douglas Herbert takes a look at whether the failure to reach an agreement makes COP31 the most disappointing climate summit to date.
