Kim Kardashian has been holding nothing back on the All’s Fair season two press tour. With the help of her stylist, Law Roach, the Skims founder has stepped out in everything from a daring cut-out Galia Lahav gown to a vintage beaded Todd Oldham design. At one point, she even had the opportunity to do a photoshoot in Elizabeth Taylor’s emerald-and-diamond Bulgari necklace, which was gifted to the Old Hollywood star on her 30th birthday by Richard Burton. So, it makes sense that for the NYC premiere of her Hulu legal drama, Kardashian brought out yet another showstopping ensemble.
On Monday night, the reality star and business mogul arrived to the red carpet in a silvery, impossibly-cinched gown, custom-made by Ashi Studio.
According to the brand’s official Instagram, the design took over 500 hours to create, and it was constructed through meticulous craftsmanship from artisans at the Ashi Studio ateliers in Paris.
The sculptural design was composed of alternating panels of gray Chantilly lace, suede, and crocodile leather. Kardashian’s waist was cinched with a corset bodice, while the sides of the dress were trimmed with long suede cords that hung down like fringe and also formed the off-the-shoulder sleeves. Meanwhile, folds of fabric hung from the skirt to create a slight train, and the look was completed with a matching thick leather choker up top.
On-screen, the characters in All’s Fair wear powerful, dramatic, and elegant ensembles that suit their formidable nature. Clearly, Kardashian wanted to bring that same energy to the carpet, too.
