Christmas did not come early for Sony, but it came close: the firm has declared that GTA 6 was the largest game launch in the platform's history, driving a record number of concurrent players on its November 19th launch, although the number of new consoles playing the game fell short of year-end highs.
A post on the PlayStation Blog said the game has attracted more than 39 million unique players since launch, who have cumulatively logged 683 million hours of gameplay. The average total playtime was 17.4 hours, the firm said.
The GTA 6 launch day saw a strong but not unprecedented spike in new console usage, which Sony described as the largest number of connected consoles in a single day aside from the console launch and end-of-year holidays. GTA 6 was the first game played on 70% of new PlayStation consoles.
Xbox has made no public statements as to the game's success on its platform. Data from Ampere Analysis suggested strong initial performance due to the relative ease with which users could set their location to New Zealand and start playing early, with over two million players getting early access on the platform. Subsequent data from Circana indicated that over 56% of global Xbox players had engaged with the game on launch day.
Rockstar previously announced that pre-orders for the game totalled 32.5 million units, achieving $3.79 billion in retail sales revenue on its first day, a new record for an entertainment release.
