After expanding its Charmera collection with a Millennium Edition featuring Y2K-inspired designs earlier this year, Reto’s follow-up to its tiny Kodak camera is an equally small digital photo frame you can use to display your shots. But while the Charmera’s usefulness stopped at snapping grainy photos and terrible videos, the Kodak SlideTag goes one step further. It also functions as a Bluetooth tracker that’s compatible with either Apple’s Find My or Google’s Find Hub tracking networks.
Kodak’s next collectible is a tiny digital photo frame that doubles as a tracker
You can personalize the SlideTag with a photo slideshow, message, or QR code transferred from your phone over Bluetooth.
The Kodak SlideTag will be available for $34.99 each and, like the Charmera, will feature a series of retro designs distributed in mystery boxes so you don’t know which one you’re going to get. Kodak also sells a complete set of all eight vintage designs for $279.92 if you don’t like mysteries; however, there’s a ninth “secret edition” only available in the individual boxes with a sleek metallic finish.
For those who grew up in an era where projectors are small enough to integrate into phones, the SlideTag is designed to look like a modernized version of the film slides that were used to project photos decades ago. Instead of film, the SlideTag features a 1.54-inch color E Ink screen that mixes six colored inks to display photos at a resolution of 240 x 240.
The color reproduction won’t be as accurate as an LCD or OLED screen, but the SlideTag can hold and cycle up to 12 photos transferred to the device over Bluetooth from Kodak’s Memory Link app running on a mobile device. You can also use it to display a short to-do list, contact details, a countdown timer, or a QR code linking to wherever you want.
As a tracker it’s not quite as functional as an Apple AirTag or Google’s Pixel Tag, which use features like ultra wideband (UWB) and Channel Sounding to make them easier to find. The SlideTag sticks to basic Bluetooth with a range of up to 30 meters out in the open and a 75-decibel buzzer to help locate it when you’re close. Battery life will vary depending on how frequently you use tracking features or refresh the displayed image, but the SlideTag uses a common CR2032 coin cell battery that’s easy to replace.
