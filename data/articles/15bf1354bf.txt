When will we see Robert Pattinson as Bond in cinemas?
Robert Pattinson has been announced as the next James Bond, becoming the seventh actor and first in more than 20 years to take up the mantle of Britain's famous spy.
The new Bond film is expected to go into production in 2027, before its reported release in 2028. No exact dates have been announced.
It will be directed by Denis Villeneuve, known for Prisoners, Arrival and the upcoming Dune 3, which also stars Pattinson.
The BBC's culture correspondent Noor Nanji has this report.
