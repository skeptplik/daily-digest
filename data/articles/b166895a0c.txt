SINGAPORE – Singapore-based tech group Megaspeed dealt in billions of dollars’ worth of the world’s most advanced artificial intelligence chips, but its office was an empty shell during a visit, its staff seemingly vanished long before claims of espionage landed at their door.
Megaspeed’s former director, Alice Huang, was this month outed as an alleged Chinese spy, accused of having an affair with Nvidia co-founder Jensen Huang while digging up company secrets for Beijing.
Details about Alice Huang’s life are vague, making it almost impossible to pin down where she lives, where she works, or when she was born.
