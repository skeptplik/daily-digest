Trump unveils new tariffs on 14 trade partners over 'unfair' industrial capacity
Issued on:
Share
US President Donald Trump fired a new tariff salvo on Wednesday on 14 major trading partners including China, Japan and the European Union, undeterred in his protectionist push by the losses his Republican Party suffered in US midterm elections. His administration proposed new maximum potential rates of 20% to 40% to counter excess industrial capacity that it says these countries are promoting.
