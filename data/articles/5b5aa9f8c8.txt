Man sues former AFL players over alleged Mornington Peninsula brawl
In short:
Former AFL players Dion Prestia and Steven May are being sued in the Victorian Supreme Court over an alleged brawl on the Mornington Peninsula in 2024.
The plaintiff is making a personal injury claim, but the exact details of the claim are yet to be released.
Mr Prestia played for Richmond and Gold Coast and Mr May played for Melbourne and Gold Coast.
