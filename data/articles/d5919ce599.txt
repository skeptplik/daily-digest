Publisher confirms 'adjustments' to Usman Khawaja's book tour after fiery TV interview
In short:
Usman Khawaja's publisher Penguin has confirmed that "adjustments" have been made to his book tour.
Khawaja will continue to do interviews regarding his new book, Sabr, despite reports suggesting he had cancelled his book tour altogether.
Cricket Australia is refusing to reopen its investigation into the 2018 ball-tampering incident despite the claims made in Khawaja's book.
