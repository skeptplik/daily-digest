Cameron Bancroft to let bat do the talking after Usman Khawaja's sandpaper revelations
In short:
Western Australia coach Beau Casson has hailed Cameron Bancroft's professionalism after the 2018 sandpaper incident was revisited on Saturday.
Usman Khawaja claimed the entire squad knew about the ball-tampering plot, for which Bancroft was caught and banned for nine months.
What's next?
Bancroft will play for Western Australia at the MCG in the Sheffield Shield this week.
