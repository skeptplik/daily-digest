Anthropic and its seven co-founders have raised $107.3 billion in an upsized initial public offering and private placement, becoming the first frontier artificial intelligence lab tapping the public markets to fund its development of thinking machines.
The AI firm sold 956.4 million shares and the co-founders sold 23.6 million shares for $83 each, according to a statement Wednesday confirming an earlier report, above the marketed range. The company had marketed 801.4 million shares, while the co-founders offered 23.6 million shares, in a range of $70 to $78 each, according to its filings.
The offering also includes a $26 billion private placement listed in Anthropic’s filings, with firms including Alphabet’s Google, Advanced Micro Devices and Microsoft participating. Anthropic’s IPO is the biggest of all time even without the placement, with the $81.3 billion base deal topping SpaceX’s $75 billion.
