LeBron James became the oldest player in NBA history to produce a triple-double, leading Philadelphia to a 114-104 victory over the Miami Heat in the NBA Cup on Wednesday.
Four-time NBA Most Valuable Player James, who turns 42 on December 30, scored a season-high 25 points, passed off a season-high 12 assists and pulled down 10 rebounds to break his own triple-double age mark.
“Father Time always wins, but right now he’s giving Father Time hell,” Heat coach Erik Spoelstra said.
Miami grabbed a 32-29 lead after the first quarter, but James scored 14 consecutive Sixers points in a second-quarter run for a 62-53 half-time lead, and the hosts never lost the edge in the second half.
“We wanted to get back to our defensive identity,” James said. “We didn’t do that in the first quarter, but for the next 36 minutes I thought we got back to that.”
The 76ers improved the best record in the Eastern Conference to 15-4 while snapping a seven-game win streak for Miami (10-7).
Both clubs are 2-1 atop Cup East Group B, but with a win, Miami would have clinched the group and advanced to the knockout stage of the in-season tournament.
With Joel Embiid out due to illness and Jaylen Brown out after early left hamstring tightness, it fell to James to score and set up his teammates.
“That’s my favourite aspect of my game besides my IQ,” James said.
“To be able to go out there and just play like a quarterback role and watch these guys run the right routes, and it’s my job to put it on time and on target, it was fun.”
Tyrese Maxey led the 76ers with 30 points while VJ Edgecombe added 18.
“These are my young bulls right here,” James said. “They keep me going. They keep me energised.”
Giannis Antetokounmpo led Miami with 32 points and 14 rebounds.
James left the Los Angeles Lakers in the off-season and selected the 76ers from several clubs in hopes of capturing a fifth NBA crown.
Mavericks win group
The idle Dallas Mavericks became the first team to reach the Cup knockout round, clinching the West Group A title when the Phoenix Suns lost 116-104 at Houston.
Alperen Sengun had a triple-double with 23 points, 11 rebounds and 11 assists for Houston, while Kevin Durant added 20 points and Devin Booker led Phoenix with 25.
At Portland, Deni Avdija scored 33 points, and Damian Lillard added 29 to lead the host Trail Blazers over San Antonio 116-111, leaving West Group C deadlocked with the Spurs, Lakers, Portland and Golden State atop it at 2-1.
“It showed our maturity,” Lillard said. “We won a tough one.”
French star Victor Wembanyama scored 44 points and grabbed 16 rebounds to lead the Spurs, who fell to 15-4 after their first road loss of the season.
Jalen Brunson and Mikal Bridges each scored 19 points as the defending NBA and NBA Cup champion New York Knicks beat visiting Cleveland 121-102 for their sixth consecutive victory.
Karl-Anthony Towns had 12 points and 10 rebounds, and Jose Alvarado scored 21 off the bench for New York. Donovan Mitchell scored 30 points, and James Harden added 28 for the Cavaliers.
Minnesota’s Jaden McDaniels scored 32 points and grabbed 10 rebounds to lead the Timberwolves in a 113-106 victory at Oklahoma City.
Minnesota’s LaMelo Ball had 19 points, eight assists and seven rebounds but left the game late with a left ankle injury.
Two-time NBA MVP Shai Gilgeous-Alexander led Oklahoma City with 32 points.
Julius Randle scored 28 points to lead Brooklyn’s 104-97 victory at Milwaukee, the Nets snapping a five-game losing skid.
Jaylen Wells scored 19 points to lead Memphis’ 115-97 home victory over the Los Angeles Clippers.
Golden State’s De’Anthony Melton scored 24 points to lead the Warriors over visiting Sacramento 129-110.
Matas Buzelis scored 24 points to lead Chicago over host Charlotte 107-105 as the Hornets’ losing skid reached six games.
