Minibus driver arrested in Hong Kong for alleged drug driving while carrying passengers
Hong Kong police intercept vehicle and arrest 44-year-old after he tests positive for methamphetamine
A minibus driver has been arrested in Hong Kong on suspicion of driving under the influence of drugs while carrying passengers, after he tested positive for methamphetamine during a police interception.
Acting on intelligence, officers intercepted the minibus without incident on Tuesday and removed the seven passengers on board to safety, according to Senior inspector Pun Chun-ho of the Kowloon East task force’s enforcement and control division.
“Drug driving isn’t simply a rule violation, but a serious threat to the safety of passengers,” Pun warned.
