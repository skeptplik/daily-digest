Keeper error and rare cricket rule gives Melbourne Renegades first win of WBBL season
In short:
The Hobart Hurricanes and Melbourne Renegades thought they were going to a super over in their WBBL clash.
Replays showed the Hurricanes wicketkeeper Lizelle Lee taking the final delivery of the innings in front of the stumps, giving the Renegades the match-winning run.
The Hurricanes remain winless at the bottom of the ladder after three games.
