Miley Cyrus is married. The Disney star turned pop sensation announced her nuptials on Instagram yesterday, tying the knot with Los Angeles-based drummer Maxx Morando at a small poolside ceremony in Palm Springs officiated by Wayne Coyne of the Flaming Lips. Cyrus’s look was one of understated elegance, the singer opting for a Saint Laurent gown designed by Anthony Vaccarello.
Cyrus’s white silk wedding dress was somewhat modest in appearance with a high-cut front with long, relaxed, sleeves somewhat loose near the shoulders and a plunging v-neck cut in the back. The classic column silhouette gave way to a slight train that pooled at her feet and she accessorized with 18k white gold set earrings and, of course, her custom cushion-cut diamond engagement ring by Jacquie Aiche.
For the festivities, Cyrus had mostly neutral makeup save a darker shade of eyeshadow and wore her hair in an updo with a slightly windswept effect. She paired her wedding dress with a matching silk set of white slingback pumps for a well-coordinated, somewhat minimalist outfit imbued with a touch of Old Hollywood glamour.
Morando also kept things somewhat simple, wearing a single-breasted tuxedo jacket and tuxedo pants in grain de poudre with a cotton poplin shirt. He paired this with a silk satin tie and simple pair of Lewis sneakers in grained leather. The musician wore his long hair in a played-down, slightly messy style and accessorized with a pair of SL 970 sunglasses for a chic semi-rocker look that added a little extra flair to the otherwise traditional wedding day attire.
Cyrus and Morando have been together for five years after having been set up on a blind date in 2021. Later that year, they were seen holding hands at a Gucci event. In the ensuing years, the pair became more public about their relationship, often spotted by paparazzi behaving affectionately in public and attending events together. The couple even began to collaborate artistically, with Maxx working on Cyrus’s album “Endless Summer Vacation” in 2023.
Cyrus and Morando confirmed their engagement in December of 2025 after Cyrus was spotted wearing a ring at the “Avatar: Fire and Ash” premiere in Los Angeles.
