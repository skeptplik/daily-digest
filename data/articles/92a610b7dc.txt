The Best Advent Calendars of 2026
What began as a religious countdown to Christmas in the 19th century has since become a holiday shopping staple. Advent calendars evolved from illustrated designs for children to chocolate-filled treats and, today, curated collections of beauty, fashion, and lifestyle products. For retailers, they’re a chance to package new exclusives and longtime customer favorites into 12-, 24-, or 25-day countdowns, with the promise of surprises worth more than the price of the calendar. It’s a win-win, at least when brands get the formula right.
Skims learned that lesson the hard way when its $148 12-Day Accessories Advent Calendar sparked backlash on TikTok this week. Instead of luxurious surprises, customers unboxed an assortment of branded hair ties, bobby pins, keychains, and other underwhelming accessories that have been compared to “junk” by netizens.
Although the calendar has sold out since its release in October, Skims has offered refunds following the online uproar over the last few days. To help you avoid a similar holiday letdown, we’ve rounded up the best Advent calendars on the market, featuring carefully curated products and impressive total retail values that make the splurge worthwhile.
Macy’s 25 Days of Beauty Advent Calendar Set
For under $70, this Macy’s Advent calendar includes 25 days of beauty surprises and delivers an impressive lineup of skin care, makeup, haircare, and fragrance finds from recognizable brands. With names like Clinique, Bobbi Brown, Lancôme, Shiseido, and Carolina Herrera, it’s a particularly good deal for beauty enthusiasts who enjoy discovering new products without committing to full-size purchases.
Nespresso Vertuo Advent Calendar Coffee Pack With Flask
Coffee lovers can count down to Christmas one brew at a time with Nespresso’s Vertuo Advent Calendar. Priced at $55, it includes 24 days of surprises, from classic coffee capsules to flavored and decaffeinated varieties, plus two biscuits and a flask. It’s a practical pick for anyone who considers coffee a daily essential.
Lovehoney 24-Day Enjoy Advent Calendar
With a retail value exceeding $300, this 24-day calendar brings a sexy twist to the holiday countdown. Curated by Lily Allen, the $129 collection features intimate toys, accessories, games, and self-care essentials. It’s a generous gift for couples or solo explorers.
The Twelve Loves of Christmas: An Advent Romance Collection
For those who prefer romantic plot twists to beauty samples, this Advent calendar offers 12 sealed romance stories by 12 authors to unwrap throughout the holiday season. Created by bestselling sapphic romance novelist Alexandria Bellefleur, known for “Written in the Stars” and “The Fiancée Farce,” it’s a charming literary countdown for bibliophile giftees. It’s also gaining plenty of attention, ranking as Amazon’s No. 1 new product and selling more than 5,000 units at Target in the past month.
Revolve 12 Days of Beauty Advent Calendar
Beauty enthusiasts get 12 reasons to look forward to the holidays with Revolve’s $89 Advent calendar, which boasts more than $409 in retail value. The assortment spans skin care, makeup, fragrance, body care, and wellness, with six full-size products from brands like Kosas, Ilia, and Sweed. It’s a particularly strong deal for several top brands.
Williams Sonoma Oliviers & Co. Advent Calendar
Give a kitchen a gourmet countdown with this 24-day calendar from Oliviers & Co. Designed like a miniature pantry, it hides a collection of culinary treats behind its numbered doors, including olive oils, vinegars, tapenades, truffles, salts, and sweet spreads.
Victoria’s Secret 12 Days of Thong Panties Advent Calendar
Victoria’s Secret’s 12-Day Thong Advent Calendar includes 12 pairs of underwear in a mix of signature lace styles, cotton lace-trim designs, and mesh V-strings. With exclusive festive patterns, embroidered details, and six available sizes, it’s a fun and practical alternative to traditional holiday gifts.
Lush 25-Day Advent Calendar
Lush turns holiday self-care into a 25-day affair with this reusable hamper packed with bath, body, and shower favorites. Alongside seasonal staples, it includes 10 exclusive products, from bath bombs and bubble bars to body scrubs, perfume, and a scented candle.
Voluspa 12 Days Alpine Advent Calendar
Set the mood for the holidays with Voluspa’s 12-Day Candle Advent Calendar, featuring petite pedestal candles from its popular Japonica and Holiday collections. Free of sulfates, phthalates, and parabens, each of the 12 votives offers a different fragrance, including Mediterranean Lemon and Gingerbread Chalet, with approximately 25 hours of burn time apiece.
Mac Cosmetics 24 Days of M·A·C Icons Advent Calendar
Makeup lovers get a little of everything with M·A·C’s 24-Day Icons Advent Calendar, featuring an impressive $565 retail value. The collection combines full-size favorites and mini surprises across lipsticks, glosses, liners, mascaras, eyeshadows, and skin care. With iconic shades like Ruby Woo and Candy Yum Yum, it’s a standout holiday splurge for devoted M·A·C wearers.
How We Chose the Best Advent Calendars for Your Buck
We scoured social media for some of the most popular Advent calendars of the season, prioritizing reviews from creators who peeked behind every door and gave viewers a look at the products inside. We also considered audience engagement, including likes and comments, to gauge how shoppers reacted to each calendar’s surprises. From there, we compared product selections and total retail values against their price tags to identify calendars that offer a solid return on your spend. We also made room for more budget-friendly options that don’t skimp on the contents, including The Twelve Loves of Christmas Advent Calendar at $17.98 and the Nespresso Vertuo Advent Calendar at $55.
