Japanese teenager Ami Nakai won the Skate America title on Sunday, delivering a personal best performance to clinch her second Grand Prix title of the season.
Olympic bronze medalist Nakai, 18, who had scored 82.20 points in Saturday’s short program, produced a flawless display in the free skate to score 154.61, giving her a combined personal best of 236.81.
That was enough to give Nakai a commanding victory over Russia’s Alexandra Ignatova, the 2022 Olympic silver medalist who finished 22.91 points behind in second place.
Rino Matsuike took bronze with 211.63 points at the event in Everett, Washington.
With the win, Nakai qualified for December’s Grand Prix final, the biggest event left on the calendar that features the world’s top six in each discipline.
Ilia Malinin won the men’s title for the fourth time, topping the podium in his first competition since claiming his third world championship in March.
The American, whose quadruple jumps have redefined the sport, wasn’t perfect, struggling to hang onto the landing of his opening quadruple flip.
He finished second in the free skate with 187.57 points, but after leading the short program his total of 284.31 gave him a comfortable victory over Italy’s Daniel Grassl.
The Italian topped the free skate with 188.25 points for a total of 274.73.
Israel’s Tamir Kuperman, seventh after the short program, was third with 248.55.
Malinin, who finished eighth after a disastrous free skate at last year’s Olympics, says he wants to challenge himself technically and artistically with his new programs.
“It definitely does feel different,” he told NBC. “I feel a lot more powerful skating this program, and I feel a lot more connected to the audience while I’m in the program.
“Before I always felt like I was always in this weird, awkward zone of trying to be perfect. Now I really just was able to skate out there just for myself.”
