The holiday season is upon us, and with it comes the annual gifting dilemma. Shopping lists grow every year as new little members join the family. Babies may be small, but their wish lists are anything but simple. They speed through milestones overnight and grow by the minute, making baby gifting particularly challenging.
When my daughter was born, we were showered with gifts—from onesies to chic outfits, rainbow colored toys, and an endless amount of books. She outgrew most of the clothes before they could make their debut. (My advice? Always size up if you’re giving clothes, that way they can eventually grow into them.) I realized early on that the best gifts were the most useful ones. They weren’t always the textbook cutest, but they were everyday essentials I relied on at home and on-the-go: swaddles, gentle bath cleansers, tiny personalized towels, diaper bags, and portable bottle warmers.
Vogue’s Guide to the Best Baby Gifts:
Of course, there’s always room for a little whimsy. Toys and reading materials, especially personalized ones, grow with a child and may even become keepsakes long after the baby years. And you can never have enough books, especially the black and white board books for those precious first months.
Whether it’s your first time gifting a new parent or you’re a seasoned pro, consider this your cheat sheet to gifting for the little ones. From newborn necessities to milestone-worthy finds, we’ve broken down the best gifts for each age and stage.
One Month: Home from Hospital
The first month at home with your baby is usually spent in a delirious cycle of feeding, sleeping, and staring at this beautiful creature you created. The gifts at this time are so adorable that they’ll distract you from the lack of sleep. A newborn outfit to get baby home from the hospital is a thoughtful gift; other failsafe options include a set of muslin swaddles and a snuggly infant lounger to keep that bundle safe and sound!
Two Months: Bed Bath & Beyond
Two months is about the time babies are slowly getting into a nice evening routine: bath, bottle and (hopefully!) bed. Make sure they’re all ready for bathtime with the gentle cleanser by Santa Maria Novella and cozy towels from Weezie. After bath it’s off to bed and arguably the best gift to give new parents is a few hours of unbroken sleep: enter the Snoo, an electronic crib that gently rocks baby to sleep so mom and dad don’t have to—priceless!
Three Months: Out & About
The first few outings with a new baby can be nerve wracking, wondering if you have all the right equipment, wondering if they’ll poop mid car journey, and wondering if you’ll wake the sleeping babe by lifting her from the car. A great option is Artipoppe’s beautiful baby carrier that is both comfortable and chic. Any new mom would be thrilled with a portable bottle warmer—no more racing home to get a screaming baby some warm milk! As a plus (and a gift most new parents don’t know they need), a UV sterilizer for all those dropped pacifiers.
Six Months: Mini Gourmand
Six months is a huge milestone in baby land: many babies will be sitting up, hopefully sleeping through the night and are ready for their first bit of “real” (pureed) food. The Beaba Baby Cook makes pureeing baby concoctions a breeze and can turn any parent into a gourmet baby chef! These burp cloths come in so many great colors and is a mainstay in every stylish kitchen and don’t forget silicone bibs in complementary color schemes!
Nine Months: Play & Explore
The fun really kicks off once baby starts crawling. A play mat like this one from House of Noa is great for tummy time. Otherwise, gift a colorful blanket from Oso and Me for in and outdoor use; Lovevery’s toy kits are designed for each stage of development to keep them busy.
Twelve Months: The First Birthday!
Hooray, your baby is one! Go all out with tons of balloons and a memorable party outfit like this one from La Coqueta. A keepsake box is the perfect gift for nostalgic parents to save all those mementos from baby’s first year. I also love to give a pair of sneakers; if they haven’t taken those precious first steps yet, they will soon.
Eighteen Months: On The Go
Walking, talking, climbing—the eighteen month mark is when babies are activated! Give the gift of entertainment with an alphabet puzzle, a set of rainbow stepping stones, or, a tent and easel for endless hours of play and paint.
