An Israeli air strike has killed the head of Hamas's internal security service in Gaza.
Gaza's Hamas-run interior ministry said Naeem Abu Nahel was targeted by an aircraft in western Gaza City. The 50-year-old had only been in the position since September, when his predecessor was captured in a raid by Israeli special forces.
Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu and Defence Minister Israel Katz announced that Abu Nahel had been "eliminated" by the Israeli military.
Earlier, Palestinian first responders and health officials reported that six other people had been killed in two Israeli air strikes elsewhere in Gaza on Tuesday.
Three people, including a teenage boy, were killed in the Wadi Gaza area, south of Gaza City, they said. The other three, including two police officers, were killed in a separate strike on a police station in the al-Mawasi area, west of Khan Younis, they added.
Gaza's police force, which operates under the interior ministry, condemned the second attack, saying it was "part of an ongoing campaign targeting the police apparatus aimed at sowing chaos" in the territory.
The Israeli military said it had "struck terrorists in the area".
Israeli strikes on Gaza take place almost daily despite a US-brokered ceasefire agreed by Israel and Hamas in October last year.
At least 1,679 people have been killed in Gaza since then, according to the territory's Hamas-run health ministry, whose figures are considered reliable by the UN.
Israel says six of its soldiers have been killed in Gaza over the same period.
The Gaza war was triggered by the Hamas-led attack on southern Israel on 7 October 2023, when about 1,200 people were killed and 251 others were taken hostage.
Since then, more than 74,740 people have been killed in Israeli attacks in Gaza, the health ministry says.
