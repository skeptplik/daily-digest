Why has China sent the first Pakistani astronaut to space?
China has sent a Pakistani astronaut along with two Chinese astronauts onto its Shenzhou-24 mission, marking the first time a foreign national has set foot aboard the Tiangong space station.
The landmark moment for 40-year-old air force pilot Muhammad Zeeshan Ali highlights China's desire for its space station to become an international research facility.
The mission is expected to last for several days before returning to Earth.
