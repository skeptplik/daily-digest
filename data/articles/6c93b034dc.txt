Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi got a cold reception in her first outing to China, but Beijing’s “blatant” snub — including a visibly scaled-down welcome — could galvanize her conservative base, experts said on Friday.
Her trip to Shenzhen for the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit marked the first time she met Chinese President Xi Jinping face-to-face since her comments about Taiwan last year plunged relations between the two major Asian powers to historic lows.
Takaichi suggested that Tokyo could intervene militarily in the event that China attacked Taiwan, and a furious China, which views the democratic island as part of its territory, retaliated with trade restrictions and warned its citizens not to travel to Japan.
