Welcome to “Hair Me Out,” where we pull up a chair with the industry’s top hairstylists, scalp experts and hair-care authorities to talk shop, myths and everything in between — including their hottest takes, most controversial opinions and the hard truths the industry doesn’t say out loud enough.
Although hair extensions are practically ubiquitous among the women we see on TV and in movies, they have a somewhat notorious reputation for damaging natural hair. I’ve always been afraid of them for that very reason. But not all extensions are created equal, and not all hairstylists are specialists. And as I’ve recently learned, the installation and removal process are often what’s most important to keeping the integrity of your hair.
Celebrity hairstylist Laura Rugetti has spent the past 14 years building a name for herself in Hollywood, working with a roster of A-list clients that includes Noah Cyrus, Emily Alyn Lind, the Hamlin sisters and several “Real Housewives” stars. As the founder of Los Angeles-based salon The Beauty Can, she has become known for her color transformations and expertise in hair extensions — ones that look so real, no one would guess they aren’t.
Rugetti promises hair extensions have come a long way, and it’s all about the lightweight, custom-color blend now. “Back in the day, the color selection for extensions was so solid, like a solid blonde, solid brown,” she says. “And nowadays people are asking for more of a rooted, more blended blonde, something that will match their hair color.”
She specializes in all types, but she’s made me a believer in both clip-ins and tape-ins for less maintenance and easy removal. As a brand ambassador for Luxy Hair, Rugetti swears by the Seamless Clip-In Collection for a big event, and the Effortless Tape-In Collection when you want something that’ll last a bit longer (up to six weeks) without weighing down even fine hair.
And when it comes to Clip-In Ponytails, bigger isn’t always better. “Let’s go for more of a natural pony, honey, not one that looks like a horse’s tail,” she says.
