On Thursday night, Jackie Tohn attended T-Mobile’s TMIX house event in Los Angeles where she sported a cozy fall ensemble made up of all things plaid. The “Nobody Wants This” star was photographed in the Netflix section of the experiential marketing event in a casual chic outfit. Her look consisted of a simple black top and plaid skirt combined with an adorable pair of plaid Larroude’s Cindy Hi Boot in printed felt.
Tohn’s attire was a great look for late autumn, combining warmer, fall-inspired colors with a semi-casual look to create a laidback but nevertheless fashionable style. Tohn wore a simple long-sleeved black top with asymmetrical snap buttons and a red plaid midi-skirt.
Her skirt was the exact same pattern as her Larroude boots, which were the true star out of the outfit here given their unique pattern. Tohn wore knee-high boots with a block heel and fuzzy texture that granted the footwear a warm, relaxed feel, perfect as we enter the holiday months.

Since the release of the third season of “Nobody Wants This” on Netflix in October, Tohn’s been out and about promoting the show while sporting stylish outfits and eye-catching shoes. On the subject of matching styles, Tohn twinned with co-star Kristen Bell during a recent appearance on “The Jennifer Hudson Show.” Both stars wore icy blue tops with black skirts and black heels. Tohn stated on Instagram the matching outfits were a complete coincidence. Her shoes of choice for the talkshow were the Jimmy Choo Isa 80 slingback pumps.
On Thursday, Tohn was attending a T-Mobile marketing event to promote the brand’s new T-Mix plan, which allows customers to choose certain weekly perks like food delivery and access to streaming services. For the event, called T-Mix House, the company rented out an actual house in Los Angeles. Each room showcased different brands currently partnering with T-mobile. Tohn was of course in the Netflix house, spotted alongside other stars such as Bryan Cranston.
