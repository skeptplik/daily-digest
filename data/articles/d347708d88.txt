AFCON: Ten months on, court to rule on 2025 winner
Issued on: Modified:
Share
Ten months after the Africa Cup of Nations final was chaotically played on the field between Morocco and Senegal, the title will be awarded Tuesday by sports’ highest court. The Court of Arbitration for Sport set the target Monday to publish the verdict of its panel of three judges on Senegal’s appeal to regain the title the team won on the field in Morocco in January and then lost in a legal case in March.
