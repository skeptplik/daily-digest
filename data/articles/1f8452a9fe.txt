Not content with plaguing your phone's lock screen, Duo the Duolingo owl is next set to appear in Pokémon Go.
A crossover between the two hugely-popular smartphone apps has been announced, unlocking bonuses for players in both experiences. (Sadly, no, you won't be able to trap Duo in a Pokéball forever.)
The fun begins on November 17, when a time-limited event begins in Duolingo that requires you to complete at least one language lesson on seven separate days. This will then unlock a series of Pokémon-themed outfits for your Duolingo profile.
Completing all of that will also grant a promo code for Pokémon Go, unlocking timed research that awards Pokémon such as Pikachu and regional exclusive Chatot with a unique Duolingo background. It's rather cute — even if it does feature more of Duo the owl, too.
Why is all of this happening? While Pokémon Go has featured sponsored cosmetic items in the past, the game rarely offers this level of collaboration, and particularly not with another smartphone app. Still, earlier this year, the game also partnered with LEGO to offer similar bonus gameplay, centered around LEGO stores.
More prescient, perhaps, is the rollout of a new feature within Pokémon Go that will allow players to view the name of their creatures in multiple languages for the first time. While Pikachu is known as Pikachu the world over, other species have some very different names on foreign shores. (Snorlax is "Relaxo" in German, for example.)
And finally, in other Pokémon Go collaboration news, the creature catching app will soon feature a crossover with Crocs, the shoe brand that already offers a range of Pokémon clogs. All players can now unlock a virtual pair of Pikachu shoes by entering the promo code "POKEMONGOxCROCSxELECTRIFYING". Owners of the real-life Gengar and Jigglypuff shoes, meanwhile, will get codes for similar virtual versions of their own footwear.
