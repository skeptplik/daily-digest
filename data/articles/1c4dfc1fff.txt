Skier Lindsey Vonn made a red carpet appearance this Saturday night while attending Los Angeles County Museum of Art’s 15th annual Art+Film Gala. The athlete was dressed head-to-toe in Gucci for the event, wearing a purple, single-sleeve sequined gown coupled with a classic pair of pointy-toed horsebit pumps.
Vonn’s dress was certainly noticeable, embellished with large, round sequins that glittered dramatically under the LACMA lights. The dress was a deep shade of royal purple and featured a single long sleeve on one side and an asymmetrical off-the-shoulder cut on the other with a dramatic thigh-high slit running up Vonn’s right leg. Vonn’s gown was an excellent match for her equally eye-catching heels.
A classic for fans of the Gucci brand, the horsebit heels are known for their sharply pointed toe, stiletto heel, and eponymous golden hardware running across the vamp. Vonn opted for a black pair of the shoes, which went well with the dress’s violet shade.

Like many stars in attendance that night, Vonn has been a longtime attendee of LACMA’s Art+Film Gala. The Olympic skier is also a well-known fan of the Gucci brand, often sporting their attire while out and about. Earlier this year, she wore another one-sleeved sequined Gucci number while presenting at the 2026 ESPY awards. She opted for a floor-length dress with a high neckline and fabric so thoroughly covered in silver sequins it created a nearly metallic effect. The dress was cinched at the waist with a slight fold to create some texture in an otherwise uniform look.
The Los Angeles County Museum of Art’s 15th annual Art+Film Gala brought Hollywood and the art world together on Saturday, honoring artist Vija Celmins and “Dune” director Denis Villeneuve. Leonardo DiCaprio and LACMA trustee Eva Chow returned as co-chairs, while Gucci continued its presenting sponsorship, a role it has held since the gala’s 2011 debut. Blackpink’s Rosé was tapped to perform. Proceeds support the museum’s film programming, exhibitions and educational initiatives.
