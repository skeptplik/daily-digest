Washington – The U.S. Agriculture Department and FBI have been targeted in apparent cyberattacks, according to U.S. media reports, adding to mounting concerns over the security of American computer systems following a wave of major technical outages.
The incidents were first reported on Friday by NBC News and CNN, respectively, and it remained unclear whether they were related and who was responsible.
Asked about the reported FBI breach on Saturday, President Donald Trump said he had only recently learned of it.
