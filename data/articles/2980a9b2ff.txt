'All eyes on talks with Putin, Xi' as Trump arrives at APEC summit
Issued on:
Share
US President Donald Trump arrived in China on Wednesday where the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit is taking place. However, all eyes will be on a historic trilateral meeting between Trump Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russia's Vladimir Putin on Thursday. Trump has said that the talks would cover the wars in Iran and Ukraine as well as nuclear disarmament.
