It’s finally here. GTA 6 is now available to play for both physical and virtual residents of New Zealand, and we’ve got a brand new slice of gameplay for you to check out. While the video below may not be the very first minutes of Rockstar’s latest Grand Theft Auto, it is taken from the first proper mission of the game, which takes place after a short prologue. The reason for this? We think it would be nice for you to experience the start of the story for yourself, and it means we can avoid any potentially cool reveals or spoilers in that story-heavy prologue and focus on the gameplay itself.
In the roughly 15-minute section, you’ll see Jason, alongside his friends Cal and Donnie, complete a little drug-running operation near Key Lento — part of GTA 6’s fictional Florida Keys archipelago. You’ll get to check out just how stupidly gorgeous that water looks, a blend of both stealth takedowns and loud gunplay action, as well as some of that trademark Rockstar humour. Enjoy!
