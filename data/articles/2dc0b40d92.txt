Thousands flock to South Korean border park for rare view of North Korean weapon
The influx has sparked frustration among residents, who say visitors are treating a source of daily security anxiety as a tourist attraction
Despite the heightened tensions between the two Koreas, a total of 13,243 people visited the park from October 26 to Wednesday, up 7.5 per cent from 12,321 during the same period last year, according to Gimpo city on Sunday.
