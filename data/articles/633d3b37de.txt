Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelensky has said Vladimir Putin is a "killer" in response to Donald Trump referring to the Russian leader as his "friend" in China this week.
Speaking to CNN, Zelensky said it was up to the US president "how to call Putin" but "for us, he is an enemy... for us, he's a killer".
Before Thursday's historic trilateral meeting with Chinese leader Xi Jinping and Putin, Trump called Putin his "friend" and said the two leaders have had a "great relationship for many years".
Russian attacks continued overnight, with two people killed, including a boy, and 52 injured in missile and drone strikes on Zelensky's hometown of Kryvyi Rih in the central Dnipropetrovsk region, officials said.
Ukraine's state emergency service DSNS said 11 people were also injured in the north-eastern Kharkiv region, and one person in Rivne, in the north-west.
On Friday, at least nine people were killed in Russian strikes targeting bridges in the capital Kyiv and railway stations in western Ukraine - with Zelensky calling the attack the "biggest this year" after an onslaught of 110 missiles, including 27 ballistic, and more than 400 drones.
The strikes came days after Ukraine had badly damaged the Crimean Bridge.
Hours after Friday's strikes on Ukraine, Trump told reporters at the White House that Putin would "love to end the war".
"They [Zelensky and Putin] both want to end it and it's a very good thing I have a relationship," he said on Friday.
Trump also doubled down on his description of the Russian leader as a friend but said he "can't speak" to whether Putin is a friend to the US.
However Zelensky said despite Trump's stance, he saw Putin as "a killer".
"I think mostly for the people in the world, he is a killer because he's killing - by the way- he's killing each day and night," he told CNN.
Zelensky also said speaking about Putin like he was a "friend" meant "he doesn't feel enough pressure".
