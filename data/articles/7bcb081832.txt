EU and Western Balkan leaders are meeting in Brussels today to revive stalled efforts to bring the region into the bloc. After years of waiting, frustration is growing. Democratic backsliding and tensions between Serbia and Kosovo are blocking progress, and the EU is facing questions about its own readiness to expand.
Austrian Social Democrat MEP Andreas Schieder spoke to Europe Today about why he sees Montenegro and Albania as the frontrunners for membership. He discussed how faster progress on enlargement could help counter Russian influence in the region.
