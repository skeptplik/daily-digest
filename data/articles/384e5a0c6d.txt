The Hunger Games franchise has enjoyed prolonged success thanks to Suzanne Collins' wonderful prequel novels. With Sunrise on the Reaping arriving in theaters next week, there's still time to read or listen to the story before the movie adaptation ruins it for you forever. And Amazon is currently running multiple Black Friday deals that can help you get the book for pretty cheap.
If you prefer to listen to books, Amazon is currently running a great Black Friday Audible deal that can get new and returning subscribers Sunrise on the Reaping for $1. Or if you'd prefer to read the physical version, there's also a '3 for 2' book sale going on that includes both the paperback and deluxe hardcover edition.
Get the Audible Black Friday Deal
New and previous subscribers
Amazon's sale isn't a discount on Sunrise on the Reaping exactly, it's a discount on Audible as a service. For a limited time new and returning subscribers can get three months of the Audible Standard plan for $0.99 per month as well as $20 bonus credit. Each month of the Standard Plan will let you pick one audiobook to listen to, so in this case you could just choose Sunrise on the Reaping as your first book. Then after that there are plenty of other really good audiobooks to choose from for your other months.
Alternatively, if you don't want to pay anything at all, there are also Audible free trials available for new subscribers that will let you listen to your first book for free.
Buy 2, Get 1 Free Physical Copies
For those that prefer to read actual pages, Amazon is also running a buy 2, get 1 free sale on a ton of popular books. The standard Sunrise on the Reaping hardcover isn't included here, but you can get the paperback, collector's edition, or the 5-book paperback box set as part of the sale. You'll need to buy three books or box sets to take full advantage of the promotion, but there are plenty of other great options to choose from
