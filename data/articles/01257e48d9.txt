They say that nature is cyclical, and it’s true that there comes a time every year when the celebrities flock to Aspen, Colorado, to enjoy the colder seasons on the slopes and in chalets, all while showing off their luxurious winter style. And it appears that Bella Hadid is enjoying this holiday season with a new friend.
Earlier this week, the supermodel and Orebella founder was spotted out and about with Nathan Lacy, a fifth-generation cowboy and the project manager at a construction company in Crested Butte, Colorado, who has been seen hanging out with Hadid on a number of occasions.
On Wednesday, the two went thrift shopping arm-in-arm throughout the snowy vacation town, where each showed off their cozy mountain style.
Take a look back at her cowgirl getups or her Cannes festival gowns, and you’ll see that Hadid can switch between aesthetics with ease, and here, the chameleonic model evinced the après-ski life with a chic double-denim fit.
Up top, she wore a jean barn jacket featuring a contrasting brown corduroy collar, which she layered over a white knit, V-neck sweater, and a chocolate tee, with a gold medallion necklace stacked on top.
To complement these pops of brown, Hadid also carried a maple leather tote bag from Coach and popped on a pair of hickory-hued sunglasses.
Then, for a full Britney Spears double-denim moment, the Beauty star added a pair of straight-leg blue jeans, which rested over a set of black Doc Martens loafers. Finally, she finished off the fit with a cream beanie and gold hoop earrings.
Lacy, meanwhile, wore a khaki hoodie over a plaid henley and black jeans, which he accessorized with Converse and a Coors baseball cap. In his hand, he carried the leash of his Golden Retriever, who joined them for the shopping excursion.
Hadid and Lacy were last seen together on November 7, when they attended a Colorado Avalanches hockey game at Denver’s Ball Arena, where they sat courtside and were featured on the NHL’s official Instagram account. Days later, Hadid documented the moment on her own account as well.
