GTA 6's fast-food hamburger chain, Burger Shot, is expanding with new locations across Florida starting this weekend thanks to a collaboration with Skinny Louie.
Last month, Burger Shot became available at the Kaseya Center, allowing fans to try the real version of the in-game restaurant during Miami Heat games, courtesy of a partnership with a fast-food joint known as Skinny Louie. The chain is now expanding this activation through its standalone stores across Florida. From November 13 to December 31, stores will feature Burger Shot signage, uniforms, and, of course, a menu themed after items in the game.

All of this just goes to show how big GTA 6 has gotten. There aren't many video game franchises that could completely command and rebrand a chain of restaurants, but GTA 6 certainly has that power. The game is already the most pre-ordered title in the history of Xbox and PlayStation's digital stores, and analysts estimate that the game has generated $1 billion in revenue ahead of launch.
GTA 6 will officially release next week on November 19 and players can start pre-loading right now. Rockstar has also been trickling out small clips of in-game footage, giving players a taste of what's to come when they load in to Vice City next week.
