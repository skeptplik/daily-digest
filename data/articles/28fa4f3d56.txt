With his second consecutive — and seventh overall — top-division title, yokozuna Onosato this month further solidified his position atop Japan’s national sport.
The 26-year-old also moved a step closer to overtaking Terunofuji on the leaderboard of Reiwa-era championships.
All seven of Onosato’s Emperor’s Cup wins have come in the current imperial era, while nine of Terunofuji’s 10 championships also were won after its May 2019 start.
