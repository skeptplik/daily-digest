Pat Cummins says Usman Khawaja has been 'casual' with people's lives in intense press conference
In short:
Pat Cummins said Usman Khawaja's claims that everyone knew about ball tampering in 2018 were "complete and utter nonsense".
In a press conference that lasted more than an hour, the Test skipper said Khawaja had been "casual" with people's lives and reputations.
Cummins also said he had seen ball tampering in Sheffield Shield, but would not elaborate on who or which team.
