Grand Theft Auto VI nominated for game of the year, even though it isn't out yet
The yet-to-be-released game racked up 8 nominations, the most for any single game this year
Grand Theft Auto VI has yet to be released to the general public, but already it's being touted as the potential game of the year.
The Game Awards released its nomination list for 2026 on Tuesday, with Grand Theft Auto VI receiving eight — the most nominations for a single game with eight — including Game of the Year.
The game, which releases Nov. 19, slipped under the wire, as games needed to be available by Nov. 20 to be considered.
Nintendo received the most nominations for a single publisher, with 12, for games including The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, Nintendo Switch Sports Resort and Pokémon Pokopia.
The Game Awards are voted on by over 100 media outlets, including CBC, and influencers. The winners of each award will be announced on Dec. 10.
