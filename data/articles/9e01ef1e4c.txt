Ebola is adding to the threat of violence and abuse facing women and girls in the Democratic Republic of Congo, compounding the human toll of an epidemic that’s killed more than 5,800 people since May.
Almost 6 in 10 women and adolescent girls surveyed in Ituri, the eastern province most severely affected by the epidemic, said violence or abuse had begun or worsened in their lives since the outbreak started, according to preliminary data released by the United Nations Children’s Fund.
“Women and girls are facing a growing threat of violence and abuse on top of a deadly disease outbreak and continued conflict,” Unicef Executive Director Catherine Russell said in a statement. “Too often, in the most impacted areas, there are no protective spaces outside the home that they can safely access.”
