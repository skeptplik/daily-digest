In all her time as a Hollywood A-lister, Jennifer Lopez has never lost her zest for the extravagant. She carries Birkins to the gym and travels with J.Lo-branded Gucci luggage. She prefers her coats to have dramatic fur collars and for her shoes to have chunky platform bases. With her, no animal print or striking pattern is too much, no silhouette too showy.
So, as she celebrated the premiere of her Paramount+ concert film, The JLo Show: Live in Las Vegas, on Thursday evening, she brought a bit of that same showgirl energy to the red carpet.
For the premiere, Lopez wore a head-to-toe look from one of her go-to brands: Dolce & Gabbana. Her longtime stylists, Rob Zangardi and Mariel Haenn, began with a boho maxi dress, which was constructed from a vanilla floral lace. The spaghetti-strap design featured a plunging neckline and drop waist, with sheer fabric that cascaded into a ruffle skirt.
Over top, Lopez wore a dramatic embroidered coat from the D&G Alta Moda Taormina 2026 haute couture collection. The matching off-white outerwear was stitched with eye-popping silk florals and sequins in a rainbow of colors, which covered the back and sleeves. Meanwhile, white shearling fur outlined the collar and cuffs, while the bottom of the coat was trimmed with swinging fringe. Inside, a hot-pink silk lining added another bright pop of color.
To complete her look, J.Lo accessorized with a few dramatic jewelry pieces fit for red-carpet royalty, including a large pendant necklace and drop earrings, both of which appeared to be set with yellow topaz diamonds. Finally, her hair was pulled back into a knotty bun and pinned with a floral hair clip, and she carried a bedazzled clutch in her arms.
