THE HAGUE – The International Court of Justice’s landmark ruling that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya will strengthen South Africa’s genocide case against Israel over the war in Gaza, legal experts said, establishing a precedent that genocidal intent can coexist with a military objective.
While the conflicts in Gaza and Myanmar are legally distinct, both cases before the World Court are based on allegations that states breached their obligations under the 1948 Genocide Convention.
On Thursday, the court found that Myanmar had violated the convention on multiple grounds stemming from mistreatment of the Muslim Rohingya minority by the armed forces.
