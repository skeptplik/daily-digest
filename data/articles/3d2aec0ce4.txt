For our Weekly Drop Watch column, Fashionista scours the market to curate the most noteworthy releases from our favorite fashion and beauty brands. Keep scrolling for this week’s highlights.
Gap Studio unveiled its 12-piece Winter 2026 capsule, designed by Gap Inc. Executive Vice President and Creative Director Zac Posen. “For Winter 2026, we wanted to move beyond traditional occasion wear with versatile pieces designed to be worn from day to night,” Posen said in a press release. “There’s still that level of drama with thoughtful tailoring and exaggerated lines, but it’s balanced by a focused palette that feels elegant and wearable.” Highlights include the Brannan Bear Sherpa Jacket ($268, shown above), Rib Maxi Dress ($268), Contoured Rib Maxi Dress ($268) and Denim Tuxedo Pants ($128). See and shop the collection at gap.com.
MM6 Maison Margiela and Salomon introduced Drop 2 of their collab, releasing the MM6 XT-Boot, an “evolution of an earlier form,” designed to be “easy to wear, comfortable and intuitive,” per a press release. Featuring a soft inner shoe that naturally wraps the foot and an upper that’s “like the hoodie of a puffer jacket,” the style comes in three colorways. Meanwhile, the ACS Pro 3 (shown above) now comes in two new colorways. Shop the drop at salomon.com.
Dairy Boy and Hanes partnered on a limited-edition intimates and sleepwear collection that launched this week exclusively at Target. The collection combines Dairy Boy’s “playful, distinctly Americana world” with some of Hanes’ classic underwear, bras, tanks, sleepwear and accessories. The drop also introduces Hanes’ beloved men’s boxer and white tank silhouette and fabric cut for women, reimagined in Dairy Boy’s signature prints and colorways. Shop all the styles — most priced at less than $25 — at target.com and in select Target stores.
Handbag brand Polène launched a new silhouette this week: Meet the Lopi, a flap bag featuring a double strap and designed to be worn crossbody or on the shoulder. The double strap extends to the bottom and sides of the bag, “creating the illusion of an endless loop, which inspired its name,” according to press materials. The Lopi is crafted from a new-to-the-brand drummed leather, characterized by a natural grain and selected for its rich, soft texture, according to Polène. It’s available in seven colorways and retails for $540. Shop it at eng.polene-paris.com.
For its latest partnership, Sézane tapped artist, stylist, photographer and collector Lucia Zolea to design a range of ready-to-wear, footwear, jewelry and accessories in collaboration with Sézane Founder Morgane Sézalory. See and shop the collection at sezane.com.
Shopbop’s in-house label Le Bop tapped content creator and entrepreneur Julia Berolzheimer for a limited-edition collab inspired by Berolzheimer’s “polished maximalism aesthetic.” The holiday-ready lineup features plenty of fringe, adorned trims, moiré textures, jacquards in a colorful palette of chartreuse, dusty rose, icy blues and chocolate browns. The 33-piece collection is priced from $98-$448 and available at shopbop.com.
Los Angeles-based lab-grown diamond brand Pascal introduced its Color Classic collection, a range of watches and jewelry that “reimagines traditional classics through bold color, unexpected materials and contemporary design,” according to a press release. Featuring 10 pieces that incorporate the brand’s signature green lab-grown diamonds and black titanium, the lineup ranges from $325 to $2,950 and is shoppable at pascal.com.
L’Agence introduced a sleepwear capsule this week, marking the brand’s first in-house line of silk pajamas. The collection includes two styles, each crafted from 100% silk charmeuse and available in three colorways: The Rosey short-sleeve pajama set ($295, shown above in Soft Pink Butterfly Jungle) and The Mimi long-sleeve pajama set ($325). Shop the collection at lagence.com.
Uniqlo and Cecilie Bahnsen launched their 2026 Fall/Winter Collection this week. Inspired by the concept of “Wandering Together,” the collection centers on soft fleece and cold weather accessories. “I’m very excited that for this second collaboration with Uniqlo we have been able to create a collection themed around fleece, which is attracting renewed attention. Fluffy, warm fleece has a certain nostalgic charm, and for me, it’s a staple item essential for the season,” Bahnsen said in a press statement. “This collection expresses femininity that women and girls of all ages can enjoy. I hope everyone will delight in the charms of the collection through layering and free styling.” Shop the collection – priced from $8 for socks to $50 for a fleece jacket — at uniqlo.com.
In honor of Founder Hailey Bieber’s 30th birthday, Rhode introduced a limited-edition navy capsule collection. The lineup includes: Pocket Plush ($16), a new matte blush in a limited edition mini navy stick and two shades, Baldwin (Berry Rose) and Tucson (Rich Burgundy); Peptide Lip Boost ($23), a limited-edition version of the brand’s plumping lip mask in a shimmery sheer pink with a cherry vanilla cola scent; Peptide Eye Prep ($25), Rhode’s depuffing eye patches in two birthday exclusive navy styles; a Reversible Windbreaker ($148, for pre-order only); the Carry Case ($38); and a Snap-On Lip Case in navy ($46). Rhode also introduced The Birthday Set ($55), which includes the limited-edition cheek staples from Hailey’s 30th birthday capsule, including both shades of Pocket Plush and Peptide Lip Boost in Cherry Vanilla Cola. Shop the drop at rhodeskin.com.
Indie fragrance brand Nette entered a new category this week with the launch of Orange Peel Room Spray ($85), created in collaboration with Room, a company focused on changing the future of “the way we work” by perfumers Pascal Gaurin and Patty Hidalgo. Featuring notes of tangerine heart, osmanthus absolute and saffiano leather, it’s described as a “juicy scent that feels bright, zesty, sophisticated and unexpectedly addictive” by the brand. Shop it at nettenyc.com.
New York City dermatologist Dr. Amy Wechsler launched the debut product under her eponymous skin-care brand, The Spot Gel ($34), an acne treatment gel designed to reduce the appearance of pimples and redness while hydrating the surrounding skin. The formula features encapsulated 2% salicylic acid, designed to “release slowly over time,” as well as green tea extract and niacinamide to soothe skin. Shop the product at dramyskin.com, as well as in Spotless Clinics and in Dr. Wechsler’s private practice.
Fragrance house Gamine Parfums introduced a scent with the perfect name for 2026: Doomed, a “boozy, spicy, woody” gourmand inspired by “the intoxicating pull of temptation.” The scent features notes of dark rum, incense, pink pepper, cardamom, olbanum, cedarwood, balsam and vanilla. It’s available in two formats — a 100mL Eau de Parfum ($290) and 2.8g solid fragrance composite ($75). Shop them both at gamineparfums.com.
