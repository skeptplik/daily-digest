T-Mobile's Previous Experience Plans Have Just Been Remixed
Launched in late October, T-Mobile's new T-Mix plan replaces the Experience plans from earlier this year. It's a similar plan that gives you unlimited talk, text, and 5G premium data, but this time instead of pre-assigned perks, you're free to choose your own perks. Interested in a particular tier but it offers a free Hulu subscription that you'll never use ? Well, now instead of being stuck with it you can, for example, pick another streaming service which you might watch on a more regular basis.
Each T-Mix tier gives you a certain amount of "picks" every month that you can use to select your different perks. The T-Mix Loaded tier, which you'll need to sign up for to get the iPhone discount, costs $95 per month with AutoPay and gives you three picks. There are several picks to choose from, including streaming subscriptions Netflix, Apple TV, Hulu, and Paramount Plus, music subscriptions like Pandora or SiriusXM, and features like T-Satellite.
The T-Mix Loaded plan also gives you 250GB of high-speed mobile hotspot, unlimited text and 30GB of high-speed data in Canada and Mexico and unlimited text and 15GB of high-speed data in over 215 other countries.
