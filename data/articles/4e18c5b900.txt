Nvidia is facing intense pressure as it embarks on a search for a new chief executive after ousting Jensen Huang, with U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent saying the chipmaker needs a “dramatic shakeup” in leadership.
The company will be on the spot when it reports earnings on Tuesday, with Bessent demanding answers about Nvidia’s export controls and some Huang backers campaigning for his return.
Speaking on Fox News’ Sunday Morning Futures, Bessent suggested Nvidia should go outside the company to find new leadership after a series of accusations that it facilitated sales of its advanced artificial intelligence chips to Chinese entities.
