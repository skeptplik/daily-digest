Eight games studios will share over £45 million in a government funding package with other creative sectors as part of a wider £500 million investment by UK Research and Innovation (UKRI) in the Creative Industries Sector Plan.
Huey Games and Ten24 Media are among eight studios chosen for the Next Wave Pilot, an Innovate UK initiative supporting 40 small and medium-sized createch businesses with £10 million.
Other recipients are AUXA, Digital Tactics, Giggle Byte Studios, Locat Technology, Lurtis, and Parable Studios. Each will receive grants for projects costing £110,000 to £500,000, with businesses contributing at least 30% to match funding.
"AI is about to reshape how games get made, and Britain has a real chance to lead rather than follow," said Huey Games CEO and creative director Rob Hewson.
"The backing from Innovate UK's Breakthrough Wave gives Huey Games the runway to build AI-assisted tools for studios worldwide — and to do that engineering work from Manchester, with a full UK team."
The funding package also allocates £34 million for a second wave of five creative industry clusters, £2.2 million for the Great North Creative Corridor, and £5 million to extend the Creative Industries Policy and Evidence Centre.
"The investments announced today in our world-leading creative industries are ambitious and wide-ranging, taking in five new Creative Industries Clusters that reach right across the UK and 40 businesses working at the current edge of createch," said UKRI Creative Industries Champion Professor Christopher Smith.
"Together, these investments demonstrate the extraordinary vitality, diversity and ingenuity of our world-class creative industries, and the central role they play in shaping the UK's future economy through innovation, collaboration and creativity."
Earlier this year, the UK Games Fund announced its largest group of funded companies since its 2015 launch. 29 indie studios across the UK received grants of up to £100,000 through the expanded Prototype Fund.
