Lauren Sánchez Bezos is all in on the holiday spirit—but she’s doing Christmastime dressing her own way. Last night, the former journalist got sexy and cozy in a fuzzy black minidress from the Balenciaga Winter 2025 collection to go out with friends in New York City.
The piece was knitted from high-pile yarn, which gave it its fuzzy texture. Its body-hugging silhouette, meanwhile, included a high cowl neckline and a chunky leather belt that cinched it at the waist, accentuating its wearer’s hourglass figure.
Sánchez Bezos followed a tried-and-true outfit solution for the dress, styling it with sheer black tights and tall leather square-toe boots, plus inky cat-eye shades.
Then, things got really festive. To accessorize, Mrs. Bezos popped on little earrings in the shape of bows, and carried the Louis Vuitton LV lodge bag—a novelty chalet-shaped piece from the brand’s LV Ski collection. The little leather house, with LV monogram accents on the roof, door, and window panels, retails for a frosty $5,950. (Who needs a gingerbread house for the holidays when you’ve got this fun gem?)
Sánchez Bezos has been on a festive style streak while promoting her Christmas-themed children’s book, The Fly Who Flew to the North Pole.
Just yesterday, she stepped out in a separate fuzzy piece: a black miniskirt cinched with a bright red tweed band that looked a lot like a cummerbund. She paired the bottoms—pulled from the Chanel Fall/Winter 1994 ready-to-wear collection by Karl Lagerfeld—with a turtleneck bodysuit from Wolford and a pair of black Christian Louboutin heels with their red bottoms.
Don’t threaten Sánchez Bezos with a jolly good time!
