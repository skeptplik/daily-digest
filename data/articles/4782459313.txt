Those hoping to save some cash on a Nintendo Switch 2 can do so starting today, November 22nd, through Saturday, December 5th, giving you plenty of time to think about it after Cyber Monday has passed. For a limited time, Nintendo has knocked $50 off one of its two current console bundles, temporarily rolling back the $50 price increase that went into effect in early September.
Nintendo’s Switch 2 console is $50 off for Black Friday
The hybrid handheld / home console was already one of the best values in gaming, and now it’s an even better deal.
It’s tough to predict which bundle will be most popular for your household. The Mario Kart World bundle runs $499.99 (previously $549.99) and includes the console, a digital copy of the aforementioned game, and three months of Nintendo Switch Online. Then there’s the Nintendo Switch Sports Resort bundle for $529.99 (price unchanged for Black Friday), which includes the more recent minigame-filled title instead. Both are currently available from Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart, and Nintendo.
Nintendo has delivered hit after hit since the console launched in mid-2025. There are now plenty of excellent games to justify snagging a Switch 2, including, most recently, the stellar remake of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. Plus, the newer console can play nearly all of the original Switch games, and it’s wirelessly compatible with the older system’s Joy-Con and Switch Pro controllers.
