Inflation in Tokyo has accelerated to its fastest pace this year, backing the case for a rate increase by the Bank of Japan next month as authorities focus on keeping price growth around their 2% target.
The consumer price index (CPI) excluding fresh food rose 2.9% in November from a year earlier in Tokyo, the highest level since July 2025, the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications reported Friday. The result matched the consensus forecast of economists.
Tokyo inflation is a leading indicator of nationwide price trends. The index that strips out both fresh food and energy, a gauge closely monitored by the BOJ as a measure of underlying inflation, climbed 2.5%, while overall CPI rose 2.8%.
