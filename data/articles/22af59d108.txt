Second Indian dies of injuries from Riyadh airport attack, embassy says
A second Indian national critically injured in the October 10 attack on Riyadh's King Khalid International Airport has died, the Indian Embassy in Saudi Arabia said.
The man died in intensive care, increasing the death toll from the attack to 14, including two Indians.
"We have been by his family's side throughout, and we remain with them now," the embassy said in a post on X. It said it would help bring his remains to India or arrange a funeral in Riyadh, depending on the family's wishes.
The earlier Indian victim was 46-year-old Mohammed Yusuf from Hyderabad, local media reported. A Kerala national injured in the same attack remains in intensive care, Indian Ambassador to Saudi Arabia Vipul told Arab News, and another injured Indian is being treated in a general ward.
US sanctions Chandigarh couple over fake ship flags for Iran’s oil fleet
The United States Treasury has sanctioned a Chandigarh-based couple, Suniel Kumar Sharma and Jaspreet Preeti Shahid, accusing them of running a network that helped Iranian oil tankers dodge international rules, Reuters news agency reported.
Two other Indian nationals, Ashwani Kumar Dhiman and Ishwinder Singh, listed as directors of the couple’s firms, were also named, local media reported. Nine entities linked to Sharma were sanctioned too.
The US Treasury alleges the couple falsely claimed to speak for countries including Guyana, Eswatini and Laos, issuing flags and maritime certificates to sanctioned vessels, among them ships serving Iran's military and its proxies.
They reportedly set up websites posing as official registries and training bodies. Treasury called it "the vast criminal network."
The measures fall under Executive Order 13902 and are part of Washington's "Operation Economic Outcast" pressure campaign on Iran.
Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said, "We know where you are, and we will sanction those who continue supporting the Iranian regime, whether by land, sea, or air."
Foreign firms and banks that help the designated parties can also face penalties.
Two people killed as building under construction collapses in Old Delhi
Two people were killed when a building under construction collapsed in Old Delhi's Chawri Bazaar on Thursday morning, local media reported. A third person was pulled out injured.
The collapse happened in Gali Sitara, near Chawri Bazaar Metro station. The first alerts came around 10 a.m. local time. Six fire units were sent to the site, and it was initially unclear how many people had been inside.
The accident hit a crowded wholesale market amid the festive shopping rush. It comes two months after a building collapsed in the crowded Satya Niketan area frequented by college students, killing seven people.
Delhi Chief Minister Rekha Gupta visited the site to oversee ongoing rescue and relief efforts.
Opposition legislator Saurabh Bharadwaj slammed the BJP-ruled state government, questioning the number of building collapses in recent months.
"Rekha Gupta's government and MCD conducted multiple safety audits of all buildings in Delhi, yet buildings keep collapsing. Why?" he wrote on X.
"Safety audits have been ordered repeatedly, but instead of real action, multiple departments collected bribes many times, which is why buildings collapse every month."
All 11 missing people found safe after boats capsized in flooded Telangana lake
All 11 people reported missing after two rescue boats capsized in a flooded lake in Telangana's Khammam district have been found safe, officials said Thursday, according to local media.
"One person was rescued at around 2.15 am (local time), followed by four others at approximately 3 am. We subsequently located three more personnel, and they were brought to shore. The operation was suspended temporarily and was resumed at 5 am. In the next couple of hours, all the remaining stranded persons were rescued," Khammam district fire officer Ajay Kumar told the Hindustan Times.
Those rescued include State Disaster Response Force (SDRF) personnel, fire services department members and a local resident.
The boats overturned Wednesday evening in the swollen Bethupalli lake near Sathupalli. The crews were reportedly trying to reach fishermen stranded by rising water after heavy rain. Strong currents flipped both SDRF boats.
Early accounts put the missing at 10, but later reports raised the figure to 11. Chief Minister A Revanth Reddy asked officials to step up efforts to bring the rescuers back safely.
