Jennifer Lawrence is heading back to District 12. After confirming she will be reprising her role of Katniss Everdeen for the forthcoming Hunger Games prequel, Sunrise on the Reaping, the Oscar-winning actor stepped out in New York City in a look riddled with references to her alter-ego.
On November 3, Lawrence leaned into primary colors with a cheerful dose of blues and reds. Photographed on a walk, she was seen wearing an oversize blue barn jacket with a chocolate-brown corduroy collar over a white graphic crewneck tee with a bright drawing of Sesame Street’s Ernie. She contrasted the sky hues with a pair of baggy red trousers from Free City, and brought both the shades together with a plaid flannel tied around her waist. She slipped into some black Vans and headed out, flashing the Hunger Games three-finger salute to paparazzi for an IYKYK nod.
She also revived her character’s signature braid—a style she previously told Harper’s Bazaar she would never revisit: “I can’t put my hair in a braid without being like, ‘Oh, you want somebody to bring up the movie?’” (The long singular plait, braided to one side, was no doubt accomplished with a little help from her new haircare brand, Mes.)
Out on November 20, The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is set 24 years before Katniss volunteered as tribute for her sister. It follows a young Haymitch Abernathy (Joseph Zada) during the 50th Hunger Games, with Mckenna Grace as District 12 tribute Maysilee Donner, Elle Fanning as a young Effie Trinket, and Whitney Peak as Lenore Dove Baird. Lawrence, along with Josh Hutcherson (as Peeta Mellark) and Woody Harrelson (as an older Haymitch), have been confirmed to make appearances, though we do not yet know in what capacity.
