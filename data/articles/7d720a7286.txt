Philippine envoy says US immigration detention rules may change after midterms
Jose Manuel Romualdez says the potential rule change would ‘definitely’ ease the concerns of Filipinos with questionable visa status
Trump’s Republican Party was routed in Tuesday’s polls, with the Democrats taking control of both houses of the US Congress and gaining legislative power to check his policies.
The drubbing has given hope to Jose Manuel Romualdez, Manila’s envoy to Washington, who said potential changes to the US immigration rules would “definitely” ease the concerns of Filipinos with questionable visa status.
