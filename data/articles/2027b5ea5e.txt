John Bolton, Donald Trump’s former national security adviser, has dismissed the idea that Thursday’s trilateral meeting in China between US President Donald Trump, Russian President Vladimir Putin and Chinese President Xi Jinping amounted to a “Yalta 2.0”. Instead, he argues that Moscow and Beijing emerged as the winners of the encounter.
Bolton points to Trump’s fascination with Putin, arguing that the US president “envies the authoritarian figures” because they can do “what they want” — a level of freedom he himself does not enjoy. In Bolton’s view, the meeting was little more than a “photo-op”, giving Trump an opportunity to signal that “he is one of the big guys too”.
The former Trump adviser also believes it is entirely possible that the US and Russian leaders discussed Ukraine at some point during their encounter.
