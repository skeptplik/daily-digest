Every year, the Festival of Remembrance welcomes people from all over the country to Royal Albert Hall for an evening of celebration, honoring active members of the military, veterans, and all the families who have lost loved ones in the armed forces. With musical performances and personal testimonies, the Saturday evening event kicks off Remembrance Sunday, the official day of remembrance in the United Kingdom.
As always, members of the royal family made an appearance at the annual festival. But this year saw a particularly strong turnout, with King Charles III and Queen Camilla in attendance, as well as Prince William and Princess Kate.
For the occasion, Kate, always intentional with her style choices, put together a look full of meaningful pieces fitting for such a meaningful event. Her black-and-white polka-dot dress, for starters, was the same piece worn to Prince Philip’s memorial service back in 2022. Courtesy of Alessandra Rich, it featured a high mock neckline and long sleeves, with a drop-waist pleated midi skirt and a black belt accent. The princess updated the piece by adding a red poppy—the symbol of remembrance—and her silver Irish Guards regimental brooch, which represented her role as Colonel of the Irish Guards.
For jewelry, the future queen brought a piece of her late grandmother-in-law with her, wearing a sparkling pair of diamond earrings that once belonged to Queen Elizabeth II. The statement jewels featured a cluster of diamonds that created a floral-shaped stud, from which an additional strand of diamonds hung, framed by an outer oval frame of... yes, more diamonds.
