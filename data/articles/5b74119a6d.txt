My hair has never been bleached, so for a long time I didn’t think of it really could be damaged. Then I started noticing tiny broken pieces in my brush, ends that felt rough no matter what I put on them, and tangles every time I washed. Between regular blowouts, hot tools, and the occasional color appointment, the damage had built up slowly, and my conditioner at the time wasn’t doing much to help.
Truthfully, I had been underestimating what this rarely-skipped step can do in our routine. Damaged hair tangles easily and snaps when it’s wet, and the right formula helps with both. “It’s a great choice when damaged hair needs more softness and flexibility,” says celebrity hairstylist Paul Perez. Softness is only part of it—a great conditioner for damaged hair also helps strengthen the strand and smooth the cuticle, so your hair is easier to detangle and less likely to break.

The humble conditioner has also gotten a lot smarter. The bond builders and peptides that used to be reserved for salon treatments now show up in everyday formulas, along with proteins designed to mimic your hair’s own keratin. And, better yet, some of the best ones cost less than a blowout. Ahead, a handful of conditioners that myself, hairstylists, and trichologists recommend for damaged hair.
Best Overall: Olaplex No. 5 Bond Maintenance Conditioner
- Why We Love It: There’s a reason this bottle shows up at so many salon shampoo bowls. Trichologist Jay Small has looked at it under magnification, and he’s convinced. “Olaplex No. 5 stands out because it delivers true structural repair rather than simply coating damaged strands in heavy silicones,” he says. In other words, it fixes the broken bonds that bleach, heat, and tight styles leave behind instead of just hiding them, and it does it without the stiff feel some protein treatments leave. “This bond-multiplying molecule rebuilds tensile strength without creating the brittle protein buildup that causes fragile fibers to snap,” he explains. Celebrity hairstylist Olivia Lusala keeps it in her own shower and calls it “a great everyday conditioner, especially if your hair is color-treated, dry, or prone to breakage.” Use a generous amount, give it three minutes, and rinse.
- Editor’s Experience: I’ve used plenty of conditioners that make my hair feel nice for an afternoon. This one actually changed how my ends hold up. I heat-style more than I should, and after a few weeks of using it every wash, there’s a lot less hair left behind in my brush. It’s also light enough that my fine hair doesn’t fall flat by lunch. —Valeriya Chupinina, contributor
- Key Ingredients: Bis-aminopropyl diglycol dimaleate
- Size: 8.5 ounces
Best for Severe Damage: Davines NOUNOU Conditioner
- Why We Love It: The Davines NOUNOU Conditioner is the one for hair that crunches. Perez reaches for it when hair “feels dry, brittle, or overprocessed from coloring and bleaching.” Small calls it the most nourishing of his picks, made “to rescue severely depleted, porous hair.” The secret is actually tomatoes: Davines uses a local variety grown in Torre Guaceto in southern Italy that’s packed with antioxidants to help protect the hair’s keratin, then pairs it with fatty acids and sweet almond butter to put back what bleach strips out. “The result is a restoration of elasticity, body, and silky softness for strands that previously felt brittle, straw-like, and over-processed,” Small says. Because it’s so rich, he recommends it most for thick or coarse hair.
- Editor’s Experience: My fine hair can’t handle this every wash, so it’s my emergency bottle. After a week of back-to-back blowouts, I work it into the bottom few inches only, and my ends go from rough to soft in one shower. Detangling takes seconds instead of minutes like it usually does. —V.C.
- Key Ingredients: Tomato extract, sweet almond butter, fatty acids
- Size: 8.45 oz.
Best for Everyday Maintenance: K18 Damage Shield Protective Conditioner
- Why We Love It: Think of this as damage control for hair that’s already been through enough. Perez likes it as “a good maintenance option between repair treatments,” especially for clients “who want to keep damaged hair soft and manageable without weighing it down.” It also guards against the everyday wear people forget about, like brushing and time spent outside. Don’t be thrown by the texture. This clear, lightweight gel is very concentrated, so use a little less than you would of a regular conditioner and build from there. We recommend smoothing it through mid-lengths to ends, giving it a minute or two, and rinsing thoroughly to get all of the product out.
- Editor’s Experience: The gel texture of this conditioner sort of confused me the first time, and then I noticed my hair didn’t feel coated and heavy after rinsing like with other conditioners. It’s what I use on the days between masks. My ends stay smoother, and brushing out my curls doesn’t feel like a daily fight. —V.C.
- Key Ingredients: K18’s patented peptide, Biotech-Produced Polysaccharide, ceramide blend
- Size: 8.5 oz.
Best for Color-Treated Hair: Redken Acidic Bonding Concentrate Conditioner
- Why We Love It: Ask a professional hairstylist what’s on their salon shelf, and there’s a good chance it’s this. Lusala uses it every day, especially after color. “I love how it helps strengthen the feel of the hair, improve softness, and bring dry, damaged-looking strands back to life,” she says. The formula is acidic and built around citric acid, which helps reinforce the bonds color services weaken, and leaves hair smoother and easier to manage. Then there’s the scent, which gets a reaction from her clients every time. “I constantly have clients commenting on how good their hair smells after using it,” she says. Apply it to wet hair after shampooing, leave it on for a minute or two, and rinse.
- Editor’s Experience: Every time my colorist uses this on me after a gloss appointment, I get compliments on how shiny my hair looks and how good I smell. My hair felt noticeably stronger and silkier after use and I can confirm it lives up to the hype. —V.C.
- Key Ingredients: Citric acid
- Size: 10.1 oz.
Best for Fine Hair: Sisley Paris Restructuring Conditioner
- Why We Love It: Yes, we know it’s $100 but stylists and trichologists agree, it earns that price. “As a stylist I recommend this for mildly-damaged hair or for those with fine-to-medium hair,” Small says. Here, cotton proteins rebuild strength from the inside-out, and make hair less porous, while hazelnut oil and shea butter add back the lipids damage strips away, all without the heaviness that flattens finer strands. Provitamin B5 helps hair stretch instead of snapping when you detangle it wet, “which is often when the most breakage occurs,” Small explains. What you’re left with, he says, is “a sleek, highly reflective cuticle and reinforced cortex.” Apply it to mid-lengths and ends, leave it on for two to three minutes, detangle, and rinse.
- Editor’s Experience: Most conditioners that promise repair leave my fine hair a little on the limp side. This is one of the only ones that doesn’t. It detangles instantly, making my hair look shinier while still maintaining movement throughout the day. —V.C.
- Key Ingredients: Cotton proteins, provitamin B5, hazelnut oil, shea butter
- Size: 6.7 oz.
Best Bond Builder: Bumble and bumble Bond-Building Repair Conditioner
- Why We Love It: Bumble and bumble designed this for the kind of people who go blonde in summer and back to brunette by October. That kind of back-and-forth is rough on hair, and the brand’s Honey Bond-Building Complex is meant to help it bounce back by building new bonds inside the strand. “If your hair is feeling weak or prone to breakage, this is definitely one worth trying, " recommends celebrity hairstylist Taylor Murphy. “It’s designed to help strengthen compromised hair while smoothing and detangling, which is huge because a lot of breakage actually happens during brushing.” For her, it creates a smooth slide, allowing hair to dry softer and less frizzy.
- Editor’s Experience: “If I’m way past due for a haircut, I start to incorporate this conditioner from Bumble and bumble in to help strengthen my split ends and prevent further damage. My hair is super long and gets tangled easily, so bond-building plus repair is an easy solution for when my next appointment is farther away than I’d like.”
- Key Ingredients: Honey Bond-Building Complex, shea butter, coconut oil
- Size: 6.7 oz.
Best Drugstore: Pantene Miracle Rescue Deep Repair Conditioner
- Why We Love It: Don’t let the drugstore price fool you, this is one of the best deals on our list. Pantene’s formula is packed with tiny Pro-V pearls that melt as you work them into wet hair, releasing panthenol and other conditioning ingredients to soften and repair. Visibly repairing six months of damage in one use, it’s safe for color-treated hair and consistent use. “This is a great drugstore option if your hair is feeling dry, damaged, or just needs a little extra love. It helps soften and smooth the hair without having to spend a ton of money,” says Murphy. “Good hair care doesn’t have to come with a luxury price tag and this is a perfect example of a conditioner that packs a salon quality punch for a wallet friendly price.” As for how to apply it, rub it between your palms first, massage it into mid-lengths and ends, wait one to three minutes, and rinse. At around $13, it’s also the easiest bottle here to replace when you run out.
- Editor’s Experience: This conditioner is easily my most purchased vacation item of all time. Any time I go somewhere longer than a few days and can’t take a travel-size set with me, I pop into the drugstore for this tried-and-true conditioner. It works wonders in any situation, but especially warm weather trips with long beach days. —V.C.
- Key Ingredients: Pro-V pearls, panthenol
- Size: 8 oz.
Best for Dry Hair: Moroccanoil Moisture Repair Conditioner
- Why We Love It: Not all damage looks like breakage—sometimes hair just looks dull and feels thirsty, and this is the go-to conditioner for that specific problem. Moroccanoil’s famous argan oil gets a boost from keratin here, so it hydrates and strengthens hair that’s been colored, chemically treated, or heat-styled one too many times. “I love this one for hair that’s been through a lot, especially if you’re someone who colors or heat styles regularly. It’s great for bringing some softness and manageability back to hair that’s feeling dry or brittle,” says Murphy. The stylist loves the sulfate-free formula for detangling easily, and the way hair always feels soft afterward, not coated.
- Editor’s Experience: This is my pick for the weeks my hair feels dry and dull rather than truly damaged. My ends feel soft again after one wash, and the scent is the same one that made me love Moroccanoil in the first place. —V.C.
- Key Ingredients: Argan oil, keratin
- Size: 8.5 oz.
Best Keratin Conditioner: Virtue Recovery Conditioner
- Why We Love It: If your ends look fried no matter how much you condition, this is worth a try. Virtue’s whole line runs on one ingredient, Alpha Keratin 60ku, a protein the brand says is identical to the keratin your hair is made of. The idea is pretty simple—damaged hair has lost keratin, so this puts some back, filling in the weak spots along the strand. “This is a great option for anyone dealing with damage from coloring, bleaching, or excessive heat styling. I love that it’s focused on repairing the hair while still leaving it feeling soft and manageable,” says Murphy. Then there’s baobab oil and grapefruit extract, which add moisture and antioxidants. Safe for color- and chemically-treated hair, a quarter-size amount is generally enough.. Work it between your palms, smooth it from mid-shaft to ends, wait one to three minutes, and rinse. It’s one of the pricier bottles here, but since you only need a little, it lasts longer than you’d think.
- Editor’s Experience: My ends are always the first to go when I’m heat-styling a lot, and this is what I use to bring them back. My fine hair only needs a small amount, and after a few washes, my ends actually look like I just got a trim in the salon when I get out of the shower. —V.C.
- Key Ingredients: Alpha Keratin 60ku, baobab oil, grapefruit extract
- Size: 6.7 oz.
Best for Curls: SheaMoisture Jamaican Black Castor Oil Strengthen & Restore Conditioner
- Why We Love It: For our curly girls, this $15 staple comes highly recommended. “My curls need that extra moisture and nourishment to stay soft, defined, and manageable,” she says. It softens dry, brittle hair and gives enough slip to make detangling a lot less of a battle, which means less breakage along the way. The Jamaican black castor oil, she says, “makes it a great option for hair that needs extra care, especially if you’re dealing with dryness or damage from styling and color.” Apply it generously, comb it through so every curl gets some, and leave it on for three minutes before rinsing.
- Editor’s Experience: SheaMoisture’s Jamaican Black Castor Oil is a classic in my household (and friend group!). A stape in the beauty cabinets of many curly girls I know, this is a reliable option to restore hair that needs urgent TLC. I like to follow with a really nourishing leave-in or deep conditioner for repair and hydration in one wash day. —Kiana Murden, beauty shopping editor
