MILAN – The Ermenegildo Zegna Group has for years invested in its own vertically integrated supply chain spanning raw material sourcing, textile milling, leather and accessory production, and direct-to-consumer retail. The latest step sees the opening its new luxury footwear and leather goods production facility in Sala Baganza near Parma, Italy.
The plant will manufacture products for all of the brands under the group’s umbrella – Zegna, Thom Browne and Tom Ford Fashion – and progressively scale up operations over the coming months, ultimately employing more than 300 people.
The facility, first announced in February 2024, is beginning operations on schedule, but its official inauguration is scheduled for the first half of 2027.
“For more than a century, we have believed that creating something designed to endure begins with people: with their hands, their knowledge and their passion, and that this must be achieved in harmony with communities and nature. The art of making lies at the very heart of our group,” said Gildo Zegna, executive chairman of the group.
“The new Parma center of excellence for footwear and leather goods marks another step forward on this journey: an investment in our unique filiera [supply chain], in Italy and its extraordinary manufacturing culture, and in the next generations, who will preserve these skills while continuing to evolve them. Safeguarding and passing on this heritage of knowledge, the living soul of Made in Italy, is a responsibility we have towards our people and our country,” he added.
Inspired by the vision of the namesake founder, who created the Oasi Zegna surrounding the Trivero headquarters, the facility, designed by ACPV Architects Antonio Citterio Patricia Viel, is surrounded by 400 trees and includes a 1.3 megawatts-peak photovoltaic system to integrate renewable energy generation into the site’s operations.
Large openings overlooking the park and expansive roof sheds allow natural light to flow throug the spaces, creating continuity between the different environments and stages of production. Accordingly, the facility has been dubbed “La Fabbrica della Luce”, or “The Plant of Light” in English.
The group, under Gildo Zegna’s lead, has strengthened its manufacturing platform for years, committed to investing in Italy to preserve and enhance the country’s rich heritage of manufacturing skills and traditions.
In addition to its own Lanificio Zegna, the group’s textile supply chain includes Tessitura di Novara, Bonotto and Dondi, all acquired over the years, helping to raise its position in the country in terms of variety and size.
In 2018, the family-owned group also finalized the acquisition of a controlling stake in Pelle Tessuta, which specializes in the weaving of leather, and bought a majority stake in Cappellificio Cervo, a historic men’s hat brand based in Biella.
In June 2021, in a sign of an increased effort to protect Italy’s unique supply chain, Zegna and Prada joined forces to acquire a majority stake in Filati Biagioli Modesto SpA, each with a 40 percent stake in the cashmere firm.
