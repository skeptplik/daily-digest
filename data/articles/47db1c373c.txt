On Black Friday 2026: Best Deals on Sneakers
There’s something to be said about a sneaker that can hold its own alongside a designer handbag and still serve you well during a morning run. Swiss footwear brand On has managed to strike that balance, earning a place in the wardrobes of marathon runners, fashion insiders, and those simply looking for a comfortable shoe to spend the day in.
The only caveat? A pair of On sneakers can easily set you back upwards of $150. Fortunately, a little digging into the brand’s Black Friday offerings reveals some rather enticing markdowns, including sneakers for under $100.
On’s Black Friday Sale at a Glance
- What’s on sale: Select running, walking, and performance sneakers.
- How much you can save: Up to 50 percent off select styles, with finds under $100.
- What to shop: Previous-season silhouettes and colorways with On’s signature CloudTec cushioning technology.
- Where to shop: On’s official website, under its Last Season section.
The secret to shopping On’s Black Friday sale lies in its Last Season collection, where select silhouettes and colorways are available at discounted prices. And if you’re less concerned with owning the latest iteration of a sneaker, there’s plenty of incentive to shop previous-season styles.
Much of On’s popularity can be attributed to its proprietary CloudTec cushioning technology, recognizable by the hollow pods that give its soles their distinctive appearance. It’s a design element that’s become as much a visual signature as a performance innovation.
Our advice? Consider what you’re primarily purchasing your sneakers for before adding to cart. A cushioned running shoe serves a different purpose from a lightweight walking sneaker, even if both work beautifully with your favorite pair of jeans.
On Cloudzone Moon
With Zendaya and her longtime stylist Law Roach behind its design, the Cloudzone Moon comes with some serious fashion credentials. Its layered mesh construction and sculptural sole make it one of the more sartorially interesting finds in On’s Black Friday sale.
