Though Polo by Ralph Lauren may conjure up images of days spent in the countryside, the brand celebrated the launch of its new Blaze bag in quintessential California fashion on Wednesday afternoon, with a poolside lunch at Sunset Tower Hotel.
On a refreshingly cool day, the likes of Chloe Bennet, Charlotte Lawrence, Laura Harrier, Diana Silvers, and Justine Lupe descended upon the storied hotel with the new purses slung over their shoulders.
Named after the white marking on a horse’s face, the Blaze, which launched as part of Fall 2026, has already become a staple in Lawrence’s rotation. “It’s been my number-one bag. I’ve been using it for three months now,” the “Sleep Talking” singer told Vogue. “It’s so cute, and it’s also way bigger than it looks. It’s even been my carry-on when I’m traveling.”
Though most attendees arrived with the aforementioned bag in tow, each put their own spin on Ralph Lauren’s signature Western-inspired preppy aesthetic. Fringe was a recurring theme, with both Bennet and singer Scout Willis in leather and suede jackets. Harrier, meanwhile, leaned into her crisp style in a white button-up and straight-leg jeans, while podcaster Liz Perez opted for the same pared-back formula.
Lupe, best known lately for her role in Nobody Wants This, eschewed outerwear in favor of a long-sleeve navy-and-white striped polo top and fiery red sweater draped over her shoulders. “I love something that feels boyish and androgynous, but also feminine at the same time,” she explained. “And I think a polo does a really great job of marrying those two qualities.”
For Lupe, who lives across town in Eagle Rock, any visit to the Sunset Tower Hotel feels like a special occasion. “I feel like I'm doing something fancy, and it’s never a casual thing. It’s like my equivalent of when you’re a kid and you’re dressing up to go out with your grandparents,” the actress shared.
For the first hour, the group socialized inside as servers strolled around with mini lobster rolls, watermelon and feta bites, and Champagne. When it came time for lunch, attendees were greeted by a long table adorned with burgundy floral arrangements. Before taking their seats, some stopped to snap photos of the picturesque white-and-maroon umbrellas overlooking the pool. A chopped salad with tomato, cucumber, fennel, chickpeas, haricots verts, and mozzarella kicked off lunch. For the main course, guests chose between chicken paillard and grilled salmon, both served atop a bed of arugula.
Willis and Lawrence settled in next to each other, facing Harrier and chef Eleonore Toulin. Farther down the table, writers Laurel Pantin, Amanda Alagem, and Alessandra Codinha caught up and swapped shopping recommendations. Sorbet with fresh berries gave guests an excuse to linger a little longer, soaking up the scenic views and mild afternoon air before heading back to their day.
