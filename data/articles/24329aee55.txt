Legal body’s new Hong Kong office targets developing Southeast Asian economies
Unidroit is looking for sponsors for its first office outside Rome, with government support and hopes to stay beyond an initial six-year deal
An international legal body’s new regional office in Hong Kong aims to help developing Southeast Asian economies and promote the wider use of uniform commercial law across the Asia-Pacific, its secretary general has said.
Ignacio Tirado, secretary general of the International Institute for the Unification of Private Law (Unidroit), said its new Asia-Pacific Liaison Office was receiving financial support from the Hong Kong government and seeking additional sponsors.
He was speaking after the opening ceremony for the office, the body’s first outside its Rome headquarters.
