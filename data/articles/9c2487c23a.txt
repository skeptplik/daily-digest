The footage, captured by the European Space Agency's Hera spacecraft, shows Dimorphos circling its larger companion Didymos, with a vast crater clearly visible on its surface.
The side struck by NASA's DART probe in September 2022 has been flattened into a crater the size of a sports stadium.
The images also show the small asteroid tumbling as it orbits and briefly passing into Didymos's shadow. The 2022 impact, humanity's first planetary defence test, shortened Dimorphos's orbit by about 33 minutes.
