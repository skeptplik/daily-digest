A teachers union in the city of Fukuoka has obtained time sheets showing that almost every public junior high school teacher there had taken “zero minutes of break time” despite Japan’s labor law requiring employers to provide breaks.
The records, obtained from the municipal board of education through a freedom-of-information request, covered time sheets of about 4,000 teachers and staff members at all 72 municipal junior high schools from April through June. The more than 48,000 pages of document blacked out teachers’ names but listed their schools, start and end times, and break periods.
After examining the records, the union found that only eight entries showed anything other than zero in the column labeled “total break time.”
