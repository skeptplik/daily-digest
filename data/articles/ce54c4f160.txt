Toby Greene accused by ex-teammate of abandoning GWS Giants to join Geelong
In short:
GWS defender Joe Fonti says former captain Toby Greene abandoned them by joining Geelong as a free agent.
Greene is one of a number of veteran players to have departed the Giants this summer, with Lachie Whitfield, Jesse Hogan, Jake Stringer and Kieren Briggs also gone.
What's next?
The Giants are yet to nominate their next club captain after Greene's departure, with Tom Green and Connor Idun considered contenders for the role.
