Russia and Ukraine exchanged the bodies of nearly 300 soldiers killed fighting in the war, officials in Moscow said Thursday.
Russia returned 250 bodies to Ukraine and received 22 fallen Russian soldiers in exchange, said Shamsail Saraliyev, a State Duma lawmaker from the ruling United Russia party and member of a parliamentary group dedicated to the conflict.
Ukrainian officials did not immediately comment on the repatriation.
The two countries last exchanged the bodies of war dead in late October.
Thursday’s repatriation effort follows talks between Russian and Ukrainian delegates in Abu Dhabi earlier this month aimed at reviving U.S.-mediated peace negotiations, after which both sides announced plans for a major prisoner swap.
Speaking to reporters on Thursday, Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said Russian and Ukrainian security services were working through intermediaries to finalize lists for upcoming prisoner exchanges.
Peskov said Moscow hoped preparations for a large-scale swap would conclude soon, though he declined to provide further details.
