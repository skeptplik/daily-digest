China warns against fake spy claims after jealous public servant frames rival
Top intelligence agency said that even though the smear was motivated by personal grievance, it still hurt national security
A Chinese state employee who falsely accused a colleague of spying for Beijing after losing out on a coveted overseas posting has been penalised by the Ministry of State Security (MSS).
The perpetrator, identified only by his surname, Liu, earned a master’s degree from a prestigious university in 2018 and was regarded as a rising star at the public institution where he worked, the post said.
