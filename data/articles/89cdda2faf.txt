Black Friday is officially here—and whether or not you made it through the roster of early sales that broke last week, it’s time to reap the benefits of waiting for the main event. With fresh discounts, deeper markdowns, and plenty of coveted finds now up for grabs, there’s no shortage of pieces worth adding to your cart today. With so much to sift through, I always like to start with recommendations from my fellow Vogue editors for inspiration.
Vogue Editors’ Must-Shop Black Friday Deals:
This year, the deeper discounts are calling—and Vogue shopping editors are finally pulling the trigger on the pieces they’ve been eyeing all season. From luxe outerwear and cozy cold-weather accessories to covetable footwear (think elevated mules, sleek leather boots, and everyday loafers), plus loads of chic home and beauty finds. These wish lists are filled with all manner of luxuries, both big and small, at the best prices of the season.
I myself am grabbing a few fall wardrobe heroes—from Arma’s sumptuous suede blazer to Khaite’s deep indigo denim and A.Emery’s little black sock boots that go with just about everything. Why wait when some of the best pieces are finally within reach?
Consider this your inside look at what’s actually worth adding to your cart this Black Friday, as our team continues to scout the standout deals and wish-list-worthy pieces worth shopping through the Cyber sale weekend.
