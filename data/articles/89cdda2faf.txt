Black Friday is officially here—and whether or not you made it through the roster of early sales that broke last week, it’s time to reap the benefits of waiting for the main event. With fresh discounts, deeper markdowns, and plenty of coveted finds now up for grabs on the big day, there’s no shortage of pieces worth adding to your cart (especially as sales continue through Cyber Monday). With so much to sift through, I always like to start with recommendations from my fellow Vogue editors for inspiration.
Vogue Editors’ Must-Shop Black Friday Deals:
This year, the discounts are even better than we could have imagined, and Vogue shopping editors are finally pulling the trigger on the pieces they’ve been eyeing all season. From luxe outerwear and cozy cold-weather accessories to covetable footwear (think elevated mules, sleek leather boots, and everyday loafers), plus plenty of chic home and beauty finds, these wish lists are filled with all manner of luxuries, both big and small, at some of the best prices of the season.
I myself am grabbing a few fall wardrobe heroes with a twist. From Nour Hammour’s sumptuous suede barn jacket in ruby red—it’ll look so good with my usual black sweater-and-jeans uniform—to Emme Parsons’s leopard-print mules (after writing about the return of the slip-on shoes, I had to have a pair!), these are the kinds of pieces that will earn their keep in my wardrobe. And a little cashmere alert: Banana Republic has some seriously good basics for under $100. Why wait when some of the season’s best finds are finally within reach?
Consider this your inside look at what’s actually worth adding to your cart this Black Friday, as our team continues to scout the standout deals and wish-list-worthy pieces worth shopping through the Cyber Sale weekend.
