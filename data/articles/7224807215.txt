Cowboy boots are a year-round style staple, and celebrities have certainly been embracing that notion lately. And not just classic Western styles, but custom designs and glamorous luxury silhouettes, too. From Joseph Zada at a premiere for the upcoming “Hunger Games” film to Taylor Swift at the CMAs, FN rounds up a few of the celebrity cowboy boot moments you might’ve missed in the last week.
“The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping” star Joseph Zada was outfitted in custom Louis Vuitton for the film’s New York premiere on Monday. His double-breasted burgundy suit jacket and pants were accented by black leather touches including a tie, gloves and his eye-catching cowboy boots. From Pharrell Williams’ first Louis Vuitton collection, the striking shoe style features a gold toe and swirling stitching.
Taylor Swift tapped into her country music roots both literally and sartorially at the CMA Awards on Wednesday, where she performed a mashup of “I Knew It, I Knew You” and “Our Song.” On foot, she wore a custom pair of cowboy boots made by the Italian label Sonora. It was a multicolored design with dreamy shades like pale blue and an arrangement of draped crystals across the front of the shaft and more crystals on the toe. The shoes matched an Erdem dress.

Glenn Close, who also stars in the upcoming “Hunger Games” film, took a softer approach to Western footwear on Wednesday. Embodying the minimalist quiet Western trend, she wore a brown suede boot featuring a Western silhouette and minimalist stitching. The subdued mocha color blended in with her argyle sweater dress featuring shades of navy, green and red.
Diplo brought an edgy take on cowboy boots to the GQ Men of the Year Party on Nov. 12. His style played with harder edges and metallic touches, including a silver cap toe and chains draped around the back. The shoes weren’t the only Western accent in his outfit, as he also wore a bolo tie with his deep aubergine-colored suit.
