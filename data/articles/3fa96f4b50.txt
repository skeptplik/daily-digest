Cameron Bancroft makes first statement since sandpaper article, Pat Cummins has captaincy questioned
In short:
Cameron Bancroft says he feels "really freed" after his published statement alleging that every member of the Australian team knew about the ball-tampering plot.
Pat Cummins's Test captaincy has been called into question by former New Zealand player Simon Doull ahead of the four-match series between the two nations this summer.
David Warner, who has been vocal about Cricket Australia's handling of the 2018 investigation, said he was happy Bancroft was able to "get some stuff off his chest".
