As large demonstrations in the Czech Republic challenge Prime Minister Andrej Babiš's plans to change the funding model of public broadcasters, Mikuláš Minář, Chair of the Million Moments for Democracy movement, joins François Picard from Prague for Spotlight, warning that his country risks following Hungary and Slovakia in democratic backsliding. "We are really afraid that the Czech Republic is following the Orbánian Hungarian way, (Fico's) Slovakian way," he says.
As the Czech people mark the anniversary of the 1989 Velvet Revolution with commemorations, Minář recalls the importance of the freedoms gained after the fall of communism. "And that's really precious." He calls for stronger opposition parties and continued public mobilisation, insisting: "We don't aspire to run in elections as a political party. We are a civil movement."
