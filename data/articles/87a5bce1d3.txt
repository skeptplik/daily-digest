Talks between China, the US and Russia could yield a commitment to control AI in nuclear arsenals as part of a broader agenda on global governance, sources told the South China Morning Post.
The three nations have engaged in weeks of back-and-forth discussion to nail down the agenda and protocol for talks between the countries’ leaders on the sidelines of the Apec summit in Shenzhen next week.
The sources said that the war in Ukraine and denuclearisation of the Korean peninsula had largely fallen off the table as possible topics, with the focus shifting to areas of potential consensus, particularly the role of artificial intelligence in nuclear command and control.
