Amazon is planning to sell cars in the UK from early next year, in a move that threatens to disrupt the vehicle retail market.
The Amazon Autos service will be launched in “early 2027”, starting with cars from the Korean manufacturer Hyundai. It will be the first time the US tech company has sold cars outside its home country.
Amazon, which is the world’s biggest online retailer with a market value of $2.7tn (£2.1tn), started selling cars at the end of 2024 in an effort to expand into one of the few remaining major consumer markets it had not already targeted.
Its latest move will be watched closely by car retailers that share a UK market worth more than £200bn a year.
Amazon Autos operates in 150 US cities and counts thousands of dealers in its networks, offering vehicles from 35 brands.
UK buyers will be able to buy new electric hybrid and petrol Hyundai models, as well as used cars from Vertu Motors, a large British dealership. Amazon said customers would be able to explore financing options before reserving a car and completing the purchase in physical dealerships. More brands and dealers will be announced before the launch of the UK service.
Amazon will go up against online new and used dealers, as well as traditional physical dealerships. Car companies also sell directly to consumers through their websites.
The share price of Autotrader, the UK’s largest car sales website, fell 3% on Wednesday after news of Amazon’s move.
An Autotrader spokesperson said: “We’ve always welcomed innovation in automotive, but our focus remains on delivering the best possible experience for UK car buyers.”
The FTSE 100 company also said it had a “unique depth of automotive data with AI models developed specifically for the UK market” that had “taken decades to build”.
Vertu’s share price rose 4% although it also announced acquisitions of dealerships. The company, listed on London’s Alternative Investment Market, was valued at £269m before the announcement.
Matt Nuffort, the director of Amazon Autos International, said: “Buying a car is one of the biggest purchases people make, and we think it should feel as simple and transparent as shopping for anything on Amazon.
“In the UK, that means making it easy for customers to browse available cars, see upfront pricing, reserve the car and connect with the dealer to finalise the purchase.”
Hyundai was also the first brand to work with Amazon in the US. José Muñoz, its chief executive, said: “Our partnership with Amazon Autos has changed the way many customers discover Hyundai in the US, and 78% of the Amazon-influenced sales at our dealerships have come from customers who are new to our brand.”
Robert Forrester, Vertu’s chief executive, said his company was “committed to meeting customers wherever they choose to begin their buying journey”.
