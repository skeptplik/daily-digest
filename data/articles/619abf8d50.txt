If you want a taste of what's coming in director Denis Villeneuve's third and final Dune film, you need to read Frank Herbert's 1969 novel, Dune Messiah. Luckily, there's a new option for interested Dune fans, as Abrams ComicArts is continuing its line of Dune graphic novels with the first of two Dune Messiah adaptations.
Ahead of its November 3 release, IGN can exclusively reveal a new preview of Dune Messiah: Book 1. Check it out in the slideshow gallery below:
Dune Messiah: Book 1 is adapted by Kevin J. Anderson and Brian Herbert, with art by Michael Shelfer and a cover by Bill Sienkiewicz. Here's the official summary of the book:
In Dune Messiah: Book 1, Paul Atreides is now Emperor of the known universe, but absolute power has made him both a ruler and a target. Revered as a messianic figure by the Fremen and feared by rival houses, Paul must confront a growing conspiracy that threatens to unravel his reign. As political enemies close in and his prophetic visions begin to shift, the future of his empire—and the people he loves—hangs in the balance. Perfect for fans of the Dune films, longtime readers of the novels, and newcomers alike, this adaptation offers a unique view of the saga's darker second act.
A visually stunning graphic novel adaptation of Frank Herbert's Dune Messiah, this volume brings one of science fiction's most pivotal stories to life in a bold, immersive new format. Rich, detailed artwork captures the scale of Arrakis and the complexity of its characters, while the visual format sharpens the story's core tensions—power, religion, and destiny—with new immediacy.
“Dune Messiah, Frank Herbert’s sequel to Dune, was a surprise to readers in its tone and message and how the great hero Paul Atreides falls to corruption and treachery,” said Anderson in a statement. “In interpreting this story in a faithful, scene-by-scene graphic novel, we focused on the scope of the vast landscapes, the color of the culture, and the characters struggling with forces beyond their control. Michael Shelfer’s art makes the story expansive and a delight for the reader’s eye."
Dune Messiah: Book 1 will be released on November 3, with Book 2 to follow in 2027. You can preorder a copy on Amazon.
Dune: Part Three hits theaters on December 18, 2026, a day movie lovers have crowned "Dunesday." But if the latest trailer is any indication, Part Three may diverge from the plot of Dune Messiah more than fans are expecting.
