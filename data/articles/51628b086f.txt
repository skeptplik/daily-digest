HANOI/TAIPEI – In staging its most significant deployment of military aircraft across East Asia in possibly more than a decade this week, Russia is displaying its enduring reach and likely sending a potent message ahead of this week’s U.S.-China-Russia meeting.
Two Tu-95 strategic bombers, known to NATO as “Bears,“ flanked by two long-range tankers, remained on the tarmac of Da Nang Airport in Vietnam on Wednesday, after Russian military aircraft alarmed the air forces of both Taiwan and Japan on Tuesday as they flew south from Russia’s Far East.
The flights took place just ahead of Russian President Vladimir Putin’s arrival in the southern Chinese city of Shenzhen for this week’s APEC Summit and just as leaders, including U.S. President Donald Trump, gathered in Manila for an ASEAN meeting.
