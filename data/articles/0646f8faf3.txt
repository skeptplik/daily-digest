New anti-corruption law arms Beijing with expansive global enforcement powers
Authorities can now probe Chinese firms’ misconduct in any jurisdiction, while blocking unauthorised foreign investigations
The Anti-Cross-Border Corruption Law was approved at the close of the 25th session of the 14th National People’s Congress Standing Committee (NPCSC) and took effect immediately, according to the state news agency Xinhua.
The statute establishes formal mechanisms for handling cross-border cases, international cooperation and asset recovery.
