A fire broke out at the Slavyansk ECO oil refinery in the southern Krasnodar region after a Ukrainian drone attack inflicted serious damage at the facility on Thursday.
In a statement, the company operating the refinery said the attack took place during a maintenance stoppage that has been in place since a previous drone strike in September.
It added that an atmospheric crude oil distillation unit at the site sustained significant damage.
The company also said that storage facilities for heavy petroleum products were destroyed and that pipelines carrying heavy petroleum products and gasoline fractions were damaged.
Ukraine has intensified its attacks on Russian oil refineries since the end of October, when an attempted energy truce announced unilaterally by U.S. President Donald Trump earlier in the month failed to materialize.
According to estimates from two industry sources, Ukrainian drone attacks have cut the combined crude processing volume of Russian oil refineries by 45% since late October.
