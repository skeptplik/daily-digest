Australia cricket captain Pat Cummins to speak about Usman Khawaja's Sandpapergate revelations
In short:
Pat Cummins says he will speak to reporters about revelations in Usman Khawaja's book about the 2018 ball-tampering incident.
In 2018, Cummins had just returned to the Test team after missing years with injuries.
What's next?
Cummins is set to speak to media at NSW Cricket's headquarters at 9:30am AEDT tomorrow.
