Action game maestro Hideki Kamiya really wants to make a new Castlevania game with publisher Konami.
The long-time developer best known for his work in the director's chair on a host of beloved games - Bayonetta, Resident Evil 2, Okami, Devil May Cry and The Wonderful 101 included - recently published a new video to his personal YouTube account. While the video was first recorded last year, it does give us some hints on what Kamiya is interested in making after his in-development Okami sequel wraps up.
Perhaps the most noteworthy tidbit came from Kamiya advocating to make a new game in Konami's vampiric action-adventure series. "We'll definitely make Castlevania. If you let us work on it, we can make something truly insane, so just contact us and we'll be at your service," Kamiya said. (Thanks, Automaton!)
Kamiya presumably has his hands full with Capcom's Okami 2, which is currently in production at the director's newfound independent studio Clovers. Needless to say, any potential Kamiya-led Castlevania game will likely need to wait in a coffin for a while more.
In the meantime, it's not like Castlevania has remained dormant and defanged. Earlier this year, Konami promised that "numerous new products around Castlevania" are in the pipeline. While new games weren't explicitly confirmed, it's safe to assume Konami would be interested in hearing pitches from outside devs. Kamiya can stay hopeful knowing that Evil Empire just dropped another gorgeous, aggressive action-platformer in the series.
"Like Rose herself, the game is earnest, intelligent, and capable of brilliance. But, mimicking its protagonist, it is also awkward, and unable to fully come to terms with its own unique identity in the shadow of its over-bearing legacy," Dom wrote in Eurogamer's Castlevania: Belmont's Curse review. "By no means is Belmont's Curse a bad game, I just think Evil Empire sunk its teeth a bit too deep into something it wasn't quite ready to consume."
This isn't the first time Kamiya has publicly expressed interest in reviving older games, either. He recently said he's "100% serious" about bringing back Scalebound, his cancelled Xbox One exclusive.
