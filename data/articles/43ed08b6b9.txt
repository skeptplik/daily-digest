Pakistan’s former Prime Minister Imran Khan was taken to hospital in Islamabad late on Tuesday (November 10, 2026) for medical treatment and returned to prison, sources said.
Khan, who is imprisoned in Adiala Jail in Rawalpindi, had an eye check-up at the Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences (PIMS), one of the officials said on Wednesday (November 11).
“He had some blood pressure issues ... and was given required treatment last night. He has been shifted back to his prison cell,” the person said.
The cricketer-turned-politician has faced multiple corruption charges since he lost power in a no-confidence vote in 2022, and has been in prison for more than three years. All of Khan’s convictions bar one have been suspended or overturned, with appeals pending. He denies wrongdoing and says the charges are politically motivated, a claim the government denies.
Khan’s lawyers say the 74-year-old’s health has deteriorated in prison and that he has lost significant vision in his right eye.
It is the first known instance of Khan being taken to hospital for treatment and returned to jail since August, when his hospital visit was cut short.
A Pakistani court has also ordered jail authorities to allow Khan’s legal team to see him and his wife, his party said.
His party’s deputy chairman, Shah Mehmood Qureshi, was also taken to hospital in the eastern city of Lahore from a jail in the same city on Tuesday (November 10). He remains in hospital.
