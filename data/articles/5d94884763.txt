Starting now, the season of never-ending holiday parties begins—and if one is in need of dressing inspiration, some recent red carpet moments had plenty to offer. The best dressed stars of the week were those who leaned into glamorous fun and really went for it. Formal looks were accentuated with bright colors and saucy little ties, which made everything feel vibrant and not-so-stuffy. It’s a perfect mood to embrace for the festive holiday schedule ahead.
Take Ayo Edebiri at the GQ Men of the Year Awards in L.A. this weekend. The actor chose a pinstriped Connor Ives suit, but amped it up with a minty shirt and elongated red tie. Imagine wearing that to your office party: you’d be the talk of the water cooler for weeks! Another sleek two-piece moment came courtesy of Zoe Kravitz, at the premiere of How to Rob a Bank. The star chose a golden Saint Laurent crop top and trouser set, putting a sexier new twist on holiday metallics.
The week also had some zingy dress moments, of course. Hunter Schafer wore the label of the moment—Lii—at a Blade Runner Q&A, her mini dress color-blocking a bright red against a minty green. At the Museum Of Modern Art's 2026 Annual Film Benefit, supermodel Christy Turlington stood out in a black and red Chanel gown made of a graphic knit crochet. I mean, when you’re an iconic ‘90s super, you can make just about anything look good.
