The 3rd annual DevGAMM Awards took place today, November 18, in Lisbon, Portugal, and celebrated 70 nominated games from 25 countries across 17 award categories. The show streamed on IGN and can check out all of the winners below, including Phonopolis, the game that led the way by securing both the Grand Prize and Excellence in Visual Art award.
The winners were decided by an international jury of 191 industry professionals from 40 countries. Beyond simply voting, however, the judges' "expertise and thoughtful feedback offered participating teams something that lasts considerably longer than an evening of applause."
Without further ado, here are the winners of The DevGAMM Awards 2026;
