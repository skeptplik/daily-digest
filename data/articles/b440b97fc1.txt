Around 5.3 million people live in New Zealand, but for a few hours on the evening of Nov. 18, the remote island nation saw its virtual population suddenly soar by millions more.
This was the hack that gamers around the world used to play “Grand Theft Auto VI” a few hours ahead of its global launch, thanks to a rolling, region-specific release schedule from developer Rockstar Games that gave New Zealand the world’s earliest access. Thirteen years in the making and one of the most anticipated pieces of media of all time, “GTA VI” is now playable worldwide.
For a title of this magnitude, the stories surrounding its release have been almost as fascinating as the game itself.
