Capcom has announced it will end service for its online dinosaur shooter Exoprimal on September 29, 2027.
In a notice on the game's official site, Capcom confirmed that the game will no longer be playable once service ends at 8pm PDT that day.
Capcom did not give a reason for the decision, but thanked players for their support and apologized for the "gravity of this announcement."
Premium tiers for the game's Survival Pass, covering seasons one through four, were removed from stores yesterday (November 24). The game itself, its DLC, and all other Exoprimal products will be delisted on March 30, 2027.
Players who already own the game will still be able to redownload it after it's delisted, but only up until service ends. Ater September 29, Exoprimal "will no longer be playable."
The developer/publisher recently published its financial results for the six months ended September 30, 2026, and reported increases across the board, including a 67.5% rise in ordinary profit to ¥61.2 billion ($387.2 million).
These results were primarily driven by the Digital Contents business, which encompasses games, generating net sales of ¥85.1 billion ($538.4 million), up 70.8% year on year. Operating profit rose 66.7% to ¥52.3 billion ($330.9 million).
