Being recruited to a World of Warcraft party as a warlock, only to be swiftly kicked again after using your powers to magically summon the party members together in the same place, may soon become a thing of the past. All thanks to a mod made by one especially peeved warlock.
The practice of exploiting warlocks in this way is called summon-kicking, and aside from the in-game reporting feature, there is little recourse to admonish such behavior.
Enter Summoners Union, the add-on mod, which grants the ability to flag and track players who summon-kick thereby building a collective case against them.
The mod was showcased on the World of Warcraft: Forever Reddit by user 274Moons, highlighting its ability to flag players after they boot you from a party, but also that it warns you if anyone in the party has a record of summon-kicking. It can also be combined with an in-game chat channel where warlock comrades can pull together and discuss and ultimately maybe fend off this nasty behavior.
"I play a lock and got tired of the usual 'hey can you summon?', ten minutes at the stone, last person comes through, *You have been removed from the group.*," 274Moons wrote. "So I made an addon about it. It's called The Summoners Union."
According to the add-on's creator, over 500 Warlocks have already downloaded Summon Union. It's a relatively small number in comparison to the wider World of Warcraft: Forever population, but it's a notable start.
"Thank you for this, I hate being just a taxi," wrote user Gregore997. Others also were quick to thank 274Moons for their service to all the Warlocks out there, such as Nangz who wrote: "Please keep the community sharing!".
World of Warcraft: Forever has been out for a little while now and it's been received rather well, breaking records for the series in terms of active players. The playerbase is currently awaiting the release of the game's first raids next month.
