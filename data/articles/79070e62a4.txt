Earlier on Tuesday, TikTok experienced some issues, with videos, search results, favorites, and other features not loading as they usually do, following a slew of other outages over the past couple of weeks. Videos sometimes wouldn’t load in the For You feed on my iPhone, and search results occasionally didn’t show up as expected in the app on iOS or Android, or on the web.
TikTok was down
TikTok says a ‘brief technical issue’ impacted access to the platform.
Even trying to pull up a profile would show a blank page, while links to videos are dead in the water too. However, as of 3:45PM ET or so, things appear to be working normally again. Downdetector showed around 25,000 reports of issues at around 2:56PM ET, and many people on social media were saying that TikTok was down for them.
“We experienced a brief technical issue that impacted access to TikTok,” TikTok’s Jessica Casano-Antonellis tells The Verge. “The issue has been resolved. We apologize for the inconvenience and thank you for your patience.”
