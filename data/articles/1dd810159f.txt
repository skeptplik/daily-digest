Angela Diffley is pleased to welcome Bernard Benhamou, Secretary General of the Institute of Digital Sovereignty and Senior Lecturer in Internet Governance at Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne University. What does Jensen Huang's return to Nvidia mean for Trump, Lutnick, the US, the AI industry and China? Describing Huang as “the global banker of the AI industry”, Benhamou explains: “He is the visionary, he is the banker. He's not only the chip maker.” He also warns of the repercussions of Donald Trump's decision to reinstate the Nvidia CEO, with congressional and judicial scrutiny looming. “It's far from over. It's just the beginning.”
