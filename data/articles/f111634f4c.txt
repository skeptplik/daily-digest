Sydney Swan Tom Papley banned for three matches for conduct unbecoming after roadside drug test
In short:
Sydney's Tom Papley has apologised to his family, the Swans and the wider football community after he was banned for a positive roadside drug test.
The AFL found him guilty of conduct unbecoming after a secondary sample from his November 2 test proved the presence of cocaine and MDMA.
He will miss Sydney's preseason match and the first three rounds of the 2027 season.
