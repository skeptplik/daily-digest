MindsEye developer Build a Rocket Boy owes £61.7 million in debt, which it plans to recoup by pursuing litigation against alleged "saboteurs".
According to an administrator's proposal filed by Opus Advisory Group with Companies House, part of the £61.7 million is owed to NetEase (£36.4 million) and the Diameter Dislocation Master Fund (£4.2 million).
To address these debts, BARB plans to pursue litigation against what the company's co-CEO Mark Gerhard described as "saboteurs," including a "very big American company" that allegedly spent over €1 million in 2025 "committing criminal activity against BARB."
Gerhard claimed that this company as the UK influencer agency Ritual Network, along with three unnamed journalists and certain BARB employees.
In the filing, BARB's administrators claimed that BARB had been "investigating the circumstances surrounding the commercial performance of MindsEye, which was released in 2025 following significant development investment. The directors formed the view that the game's commercial prospects may have been adversely affected by a coordinated campaign of negative online reviews and commentary."
"The directors subsequently received information which they considered suggested the involvement of a number of third parties in organising and funding such activity and commenced litigation against some of those parties."
It continued: "The directors have indicated that any such claims may be substantial in value, although the extent of any recovery remains uncertain and will be dependent upon the outcome of ongoing investigations, legal proceedings, and asset recovery actions."
Administrators were appointed to BARB in September, indicating the company was insolvent and required protection from creditors' legal actions.
After MindsEye's release in 2025, staff expressed concerns to GamesIndustry.biz about the company's future. "I believe the end is coming for BARB, and it's not heralded by saboteurs, but by Mark and Leslie [Benzies] themselves."
