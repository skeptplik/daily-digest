David Warner backs Usman Khawaja's ball-tampering claims, slams 'botched' Cricket Australia investigation
In short:
David Warner has emphatically backed Usman Khawaja's revelations on the 2018 ball tampering scandal, saying claims detailed in his new book are "100 per cent accurate".
Warner slammed Cricket Australia's 2018 investigation into the incident, saying the interview process was "botched".
What's next?
The other players banned in 2018, Steve Smith and Cameron Bancroft, are yet to speak on Khawaja's revelations. It remains to be seen whether Cricket Australia takes any official action.
