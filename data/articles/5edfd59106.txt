A doctor was murdered in South Korea. 8 years on, ‘nothing has changed’
With assaults hitting 1,200 a year, doctors want metal detectors and an end to rules requiring victims to press charges
The attack at Severance Hospital in Seoul last Wednesday left the victim, a psychiatry professor surnamed Kang, with a severe hand injury that required surgery and cuts to his face and head.
South Korea’s leading medical bodies have called for urgent new security measures inside hospitals in the wake of the incident, arguing that the current legal regime has failed to prevent violence against staff.
