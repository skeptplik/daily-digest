Unity has launched the Unity Fellowship program, offering indie developers planning to launch a game within the next 12 months a $50,000 grant with no equity or repayment.
Ten studios will be selected to receive the grant, with recipients announced at GDC in March 2027, along with Unity support including a dedicated point of contact.
Teams will also receive expert guidance, industry connections, pitch preparation, introductions to publishers, access to Unity events, and PR and marketing support.
"Democratising game creation has been Unity's mission from day one, and the Unity Fellowship takes it beyond tools," said Unity SVP of product Adam Smith.
"We're putting our resources, our time, and our people behind teams with original ideas, in the hope that those ideas become successful, memorable games that reach and resonate with players around the world."
Unity's primary grant program is its annual Unity for Humanity grant, which provides funding and support to developers using Unity to create projects that address global challenges.
Ten developers received funding from Unity's $600,000 Charitable Fund this year, including Six Wing Studios, Khalayan Arts, and Charles Games.
Other platform-specific grants include Epic Games' MegaGrants program, which supports developers using Unreal Engine.
The program launched in 2019 with a $100 million fund, and surpassed $60 million in financial support for developers, media projects, and other Unreal creators by 2020.
Epic MegaGrants has supported several independent game projects, including Stairway Games' Coral Reef, Chris Perry's Little Bird, and 9heads Game Studios' No Captain Allowed.
In 2022, Frogwares used MegaGrant funding to relocate and support its Ukrainian employees.
