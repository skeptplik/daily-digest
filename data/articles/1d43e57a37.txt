Australian Cricketers' Association says treatment of players involved in Sandpapergate 'disgraceful'
In short:
The Australian Cricketers' Association says players have been "hounded" by the media as the Sandpapergate story exploded in the past week.
Paul Marsh, the CEO of the ACA, said he is "really concerned" about the players' wellbeing.
Marsh said he was still waiting on the details of Cricket Australia's proposed forum.
