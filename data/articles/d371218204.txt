PLA cybersecurity scientist Guo Shize removed from Chinese Academy of Sciences website
Guo and his two roommates at Harbin Institute of Technology all rose to become prominent academicians at top Chinese scientific institutions
A leading cybersecurity scientist in the Chinese military, Guo Shize, has been removed from the website of the country’s top national research institute.
It is not known when his page was removed from the academy’s website, but Chinese media reports noted the change on Monday. Neither the academy nor Beijing’s defence ministry has explained the removal.
