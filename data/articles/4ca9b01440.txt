Live: Two Libyan soldiers killed in Ukrainian attack on Russian vessels in Benghazi
Two Libyan soldiers have died from injuries sustained in an attack, claimed by Ukraine, on Russian ships near Benghazi, a senior figure in the administration in eastern Libya said late on Tuesday. Members of the European Parliament will convene in Strasbourg on Wednesday to discuss 'Europe’s role and capacity to protect the international order and secure a just and lasting peace for Ukraine.' Follow our liveblog for all the latest updates.
- A Russian attack killed two people and wounded 22 more, including two children, in Ukraine's Zaporizhzhia overnight, Ivan ‌Fedorov, co-head of the Zaporizhzhia Regional Defense Council, said on Telegram.
- Ukraine's Foreign Minister Andrii Sybiha said on its drone attacks against a Russian military vessel and cargo ship in Libyan waters at the weekend was a legitimate act of self-defence, following criticism from the government in Tripoli.
- More than 500,000 Russian soldiers have been killed in the war with Ukraine, according to an estimate by a number of media organisations. The figures are based on a huge dataset that was hacked from Russia’s state statistics agency Rosstat by Belarusian activist group Cyber Partisans and Ukrainian hacker group Silent Crow.
