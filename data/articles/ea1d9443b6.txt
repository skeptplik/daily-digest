Director of U.S. National Intelligence Jay Clayton said that Nvidia is a perpetual target for people trying to illegally obtain its high-end chips and the U.S. is investigating an alleged Chinese spy who had an affair with the company’s former chief executive.
“Nvidia chips, they are the jewel. They are the absolute top that everybody wants,” Clayton said Sunday on ABC’s “This Week.” “That’s why we’re focusing on this. People will do a lot, they will break the law. There are indictments already involving other companies where people have broken the law to get access to Nvidia chips.”
He said in a separate interview on CNN’s State of the Union that Attorney General Todd Blanche has made it clear that the U.S. is looking into Alice Huang, a former executive for Singapore-based Megaspeed International, and her company but declined to comment further.
