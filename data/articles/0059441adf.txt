More than 80 percent of U.S. shoppers are willing to buy from an online store they’ve never heard of during Black Friday, which makes the shopping rush that weekend a key time for retailers to grow their customer base.
The problem, of course, is that in a world of infinite deals and promotions it’s extremely difficult to grab people’s attention and then convert them into repeat customers. Ahead of the peak shopping weekend, Sourcing Journal spoke to Sherry Smith, president of retail media at Criteo, the ad tech company, about how brands can use commerce intelligence, adapt to the new generative AI search behavior and turn those Black Friday deal-seekers into customers for the new year.
Sourcing Journal: The Black Friday/holiday shopping season is great chance to win new customers. But what are the challenges for retailers and brands in trying to do so?
Sherry Smith: The holiday shopping season creates a significant opportunity for retailers to reach new customers, but standing out in an increasingly crowded market can be challenging. Consumers are exposed to countless promotions, products and recommendations, making it harder for brands to capture their attention and turn interest into a purchase.
At the same time, shoppers are more willing to explore unfamiliar retailers than they might be throughout the rest of the year. Criteo’s research found that 82 percent of U.S. shoppers purchase from online stores they don’t typically use at least sometimes during Black Friday, with 19 percent doing so most of the time or always and another 63 percent doing so sometimes. Among those open to trying unfamiliar retailers, 49 percent say deals convince them to take the leap.
The challenge for retailers and brands is reaching these shoppers with relevant offers and experiences at the right moment, then turning that initial interest into a relationship that extends beyond the holiday season.
SJ: Once engaged, how do you convert them to repeat customers?
S.S.: Turning a first-time shopper into a repeat customer starts with delivering on the experience that initially attracted them. While a compelling offer or promotion may encourage someone to try a new retailer, factors like product quality, convenience, relevant recommendations and consistent service are what build long-term loyalty.
Retailers can leverage commerce insights and signals from shopper interactions to better understand consumer preferences and deliver more relevant experiences over time. Relevant product recommendations, thoughtful follow-up offers and loyalty programs can help keep customers engaged without overwhelming them with promotions.
Ultimately, the goal is to make shoppers feel understood and give them a reason to choose that retailer again, beyond just the next discount.
SJ: What other opportunities does the holiday shopping season present for retailers and brands?
S.S.: Beyond driving immediate sales, the holiday shopping season gives retailers an opportunity to introduce their brands to new audiences, learn more about consumer preferences and build relationships that extend beyond the season itself.
It’s also an important time to understand how shopping behaviors are evolving. For example, one in five (20 percent) U.S. consumers now turn to generative AI tools to kick off their holiday gift searches. This creates new opportunities for brands to be discovered across a wider range of touchpoints, whether consumers are browsing social media, using search engines, asking AI tools for recommendations or visiting physical stores.
As discovery expands across AI assistants, social media, search and retailers’ own digital and physical channels, the opportunity is to create more connected experiences that help brands reach shoppers wherever their journey begins, while keeping retailers at the center of the shopping experience.
SJ: How can Criteo help?
S.S.: As a global commerce intelligence platform, Criteo helps retailers, brands and agencies connect with consumers throughout the shopping journey through AI-powered technology, deep commerce insights and access to a broad network of commerce partners.
By connecting advertiser demand with retailer media opportunities across channels, we help businesses reach relevant audiences, drive measurable outcomes and create more valuable shopping experiences.
During the holiday season, this means helping retailers connect with consumers who may be actively exploring unfamiliar brands, while also re-engaging existing customers with relevant offers and recommendations. By leveraging commerce data and AI to better understand shopping behaviors and intent, Criteo helps retailers turn seasonal interest into meaningful customer relationships and long-term growth.
SJ: How does your platform differ from others in the market? And how would you describe the overall value proposition?
S.S.: Criteo’s differentiation starts with our deep expertise in commerce and our ability to connect the broader commerce ecosystem. As a global commerce intelligence platform, we bring together AI-powered technology, extensive commerce insights and relationships across retailers, brands and agencies to help businesses connect with consumers at meaningful moments throughout their shopping journey.
What makes our approach unique is our ability to connect the broader commerce ecosystem, helping retailers unlock the value of their media assets while enabling brands and agencies to reach high-intent shoppers across channels. Ultimately, we’re focused on turning commerce intelligence into measurable business outcomes, whether that’s acquiring new customers, driving conversions or building longer-term relationships.
