Is Russia’s seizure of Japan Tobacco assets the last straw for Tokyo amid frosty ties?
Analysts say Takaichi will need to mount a ‘more assertive’ response after Moscow seized Japan Tobacco assets
The Japanese government on Friday lodged a formal complaint with Russia and demanded that Moscow immediately reverse a decree announced the previous day that transferred the assets of Japan Tobacco International (JTI) to Russian control.
Analysts say the decree, signed by Russian President Vladimir Putin, is likely a calculated decision by Moscow, given that the Japanese government holds more than one third of the stocks in the company.
