Japan has a knack for producing pop songs that seem to come out of nowhere and then become impossible to escape. This year’s earworm is “Honmayade Nandeyanen Shirankedo,” a goofy bit of Eurobeat-inflected pop by the four-member group Monaki.
With its Kansai-dialect chorus, sense of humor and infectious hook, the track has found an audience well beyond the typical J-pop fan. You might not know Monaki, but there’s a good chance you’ve heard their hit song.
