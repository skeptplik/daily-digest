Gaza: Israel launched new offensive on Nuseirat
Issued on: Modified:
Share
Israeli tanks have moved east of the Nuseirat refugee camp in central Gaza, with witnesses reporting heavy shelling, gunfire and airstrikes as the Israeli military says it has launched an operation in a Hamas-controlled area. Strikes killed at least three people on Wednesday, and displaced families are fleeing again, saying there is no real ceasefire. Israel and Hamas accuse each other of breaking the truce in force since October 2025, while Gadi Eisenkot tries to form a government after inconclusive elections.
