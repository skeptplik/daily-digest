It was her second marriage. She could not afford to fail again.
Mei Nanaumi, a woman in her 30s from Iwaki, Fukushima Prefecture, believed she had finally found a partner to spend the rest of her life with. Divorced and raising a child on her own, she had been welcomed warmly by her new husband’s family.
“They accepted me and my child with open arms,” Nanaumi recalled. “I thought I could get along with them well.” What she didn’t know about before remarrying, however, was her soon-to-be husband’s addiction to gambling.
