What's a better way to start getting ready for the festive season than watching Home Alone and Home Alone 2? It's a classic, and many of us have grown up watching this heartwarming holiday comedy. It's been 35 years since the first Home Alone premiered, and since then, Macaulay Culkin has played Kevin McCallister several times, mostly for publicity campaigns or skits, like the Google Assistant ad and the short where he plays a tormented Uber driver. It's always great to see him in that role again. This holiday season, Macaulay Culkin returns as Home Alone's Kevin McCallister in McDonald's latest ad campaign.
Instead of being home alone, he's alone at a McDonald's restaurant. There, he's free to live out his wildest dreams by pretending to be both an employee and a customer, making a McFlurry with all available toppings, dubbing the Big Mac "the Big McCallister," and making his own McShaker Fries. The ad promotes the Home Alone Meal, which will be available in every state across the US starting Dec. 1, 2026.
Home Alone-themed food comes to McDonald's this holiday
The meal includes one of four collectible, limited-edition socks inspired by Kevin's iconic screaming face after he puts on aftershave. While the campaign is active, there are also special items available on the menu, such as Little Nero's Pizza McShaker Fries and the Lovely Cheese Pizza Dipping Sauce, in honor of the McCallisters' favorite pizza place and Kevin's go-to pizza. There's also the Everything Sundae McFlurry, which features toppings such as hot fudge, Oreo cookies, and M&M's. This McFlurry is based on Kevin's ice cream concoctions when he found himself alone at home in Home Alone and at the Plaza Hotel in Home Alone 2.
