AI disruption, geopolitics and partnerships in focus at Hong Kong business summit
About 100 business owners, executives and academics attend fourth edition of the Family Business Summit, co-hosted by the South China Morning Post

Technology-driven disruptions, superpower rivalry and global partnerships were among the topics that dominated a closed-door summit in Hong Kong, as countries and tech giants seek to boost growth and lead the race in artificial intelligence.
About 100 business owners, executives and academics from the United States, mainland China, Hong Kong, Southeast Asia and the Middle East attended the fourth edition of the Family Business Summit, co-hosted by the South China Morning Post and Blue Pool Capital, with UBS as the presenting partner.
At a welcome dinner on Wednesday, Singapore’s Minister for Digital Development and Information Josephine Teo discussed the city state’s approach to capitalising on AI while ensuring no one was left behind.
