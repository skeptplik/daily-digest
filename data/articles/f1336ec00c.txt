Nintendo has been having some of the best Black Friday video game deals this year. Walmart was even dropping $30 first-party Switch 2 game deals at one point, and there's plenty of other discounts worth checking out if you haven't already. But if you already have a Switch 2 and plenty of games, what good are these discounts to you? If you've had your console since day one, you may be looking to add some extra accessories to your collection instead.
I'm not talking about necessary accessories like controllers and gaming headsets, though. I'm talking about the stuff that you don't actually need to enjoy your gaming experience. I'm here to shine a light on the accessories that are truly extra in all the best ways.
Retro Switch Game Case
Let's start with one of my favorites, this Game Boy-inspired Switch 2 game case. This bad boy can hold up to three cartridges and three microSD Express cards at any given time. So you could likely hold most of your game library in this thing if so inclined. It is definitely less safe to put all of your games on a keychain, rather than an actual protective case, but who cares about safety when you've got that retro gaming style working its magic.
Master Sword Game Card Holder/Sword Grip
Of all of the Switch accessories I've seen in my life, this one never fails to make me laugh. It's trying to solve multiple problems that don't actually exist while also attempting to appeal to fans of The Legend of Zelda. The Master Sword design is compatible with the Joy-Con 2 controller and is meant to be used with Skyward Sword HD, Tears of the Kingdom, and Switch Sports Chambara. On top of that, you're able to store 12 games in this thing for no reason at all.
I still think this is a very silly thing, but I almost buy it every time it's on sale. I don't even know where I'd put this thing.
Basecase for Switch 2 Gaming Pillow
Now that we've gotten the silly card holders out of the way, let's get to the things that solve those first-world problems. If you've spent a good bit of time playing your Switch 2 in handheld mode, then you likely know about "the tingles". This is that funny feeling at your elbows telling you that your arms are not properly being utilized for their biological purpose. If you want those weird feelings (and existential thoughts) to be banished forever, this Switch 2 gaming pillow is great for that.
My coworker actually tested out a pillow just like this one and said it was surprisingly awesome.
Spigen JoyCon Grips
Have you ever had to use a Joy-Con as an actual controller? If you're used to using a Switch Pro controller, it's just going to feel wrong. My nephew always wants to play Super Smash Bros. whenever the family gets together, but he only has one good controller and the rest of us are left with what is essentially half of a remote to play with. I still usually win, but it's getting more difficult every time. If I had one of these grips, using the Joy-Con in this manner would definitely feel less awkward.
DIY GameCube Shell and Buttons for Switch 2 Pro Controller
Last but not least, arguably the least necessary accessory there is — a purely stylistic change. After a full year of the Nintendo Switch 2 being on the market, there's a surprising lack of special edition Switch 2 controllers available to buy. So if you want something that better fits your style, I propose taking the DIY route. This GameCube-inspired shell and buttons will transform your controller into an early 2000s fever dream. The only downside is that you'll need to be able to take apart (and reassemble) your Switch 2 Pro Controller to get the look. Still cheaper than buying a new controller.
