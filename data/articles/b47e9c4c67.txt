A little over a week ago, Jay-Z held the final shows of his Jay-Z 30 Tour at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California, where he had the support of his wife, Beyoncé, at both back-to-back concerts.
But Beyoncé wasn’t the only loved one in the cheering section. As seen in a series of photos shared by the “Texas Hold ’Em” singer’s stylist, Ty Hunter, other starry attendees included former Destiny’s Child members Kelly Rowland and Michelle Williams, as well as Beyoncé and Jay’s eldest daughter, Blue Ivy. Even Queen Bey’s mom, Tina Knowles, came out to watch.
On Jay-Z’s second night at SoFi, Beyoncé posed for a rare three-generation photo with her mom and eldest child, where each woman showed off her individual style (as seen in slide four above).
For her part, the pop superstar wore a bone-colored set from the LaPointe Spring/Summer 2027 ready-to-wear collection, which consisted of a sheer wrap top layered underneath a cropped jacket and paired with matching boyshorts, both of which were decorated with silver gemstones.
As for Blue Ivy, she wore a striped baseball top tucked into cutoff jean shorts, and she accessorized with Timberland boots, frameless sunglasses, and a black bandana nestled into her pocket.
Meanwhile, mama Knowles chose a tartan off-the-shoulder blouse with black leather pants and inky pointed heels. Talk about three generations of stylish women.
The last time we saw this trio together was back in October, when Beyoncé celebrated the opening of the new Knowles-Rowland House in Houston, Texas. At the unveiling, the Grammy winner was joined onstage by her mom, and they eventually called up Blue Ivy and Beyoncé’s other daughter, Rumi, who both helped to cut the ceremonial ribbon.
