UK games retail revenue increased 99% week-on-week to £142 million during Grand Theft Auto 6's three-day launch period.
NielsenIQ estimates that from November 19 to 21, GTA 6 generated £32 million in physical sales, accounting for 74% of the UK's total physical software sales. Of these sales, 88% occurred on launch day.
Boxed software revenue reached £43 million, a 409% increase from the previous week. NIQ noted that GTA 6's launch week marked the highest physical game sales since Christmas 2018, despite being sold with a download code rather than a physical disc.
GTA 6's launch ranked No. 20 among all UK games retail days since NIQ tracking began in 2000. For first-week boxed revenue, it ranked No. 17. GTA 5 holds the top spot, while GTA 4 is at No. 11.
Console hardware sales rose 38% to their highest level since late 2025, while accessories increased 123% from the previous week, generating £21 million. PlayStation 5 sales grew 46% week-on-week, whereas Xbox Series X|S sales increased 75%.
External console SSD sales rose 30%, marking their strongest week in 2026. Joypad volumes increased 194%, with PS5 up 355%.
Gaming headset sales grew 94%, while battery packs and docking stations rose 69%. Gaming chair sales increased 32%, which NIQ noted was "broadly in line" with the 40% increase during GTA 5's launch week in 2013.
"While the physical games market looks very different today compared with previous blockbuster launches, GTA VI has still made a huge impact," said NIQ Senior Client Insight Director Dorian Bloch.
"Back in 2008, boxed software accounted for half of all games retail revenue for the combined sectors of Software, Hardware, and Gaming Accessories, compared with 15% so far in 2026. This is a reflection of the market increasingly shifting towards digital downloads."
Bloch continued: "Against this backdrop, GTA VI’s physical launch performance is particularly impressive. There have only been 16 games released since 1995 that generated higher boxed software revenue in their opening week. This demonstrates the enduring appeal and retail power of one of gaming's biggest franchises."
A GTA 6 preview on Netflix earlier this year led to a jump in UK PlayStation and Xbox hardware sales, with PS5 sales rising 33% and Xbox Series X|S up 34%.
GTA 6 achieved the largest launch in entertainment history, generating $3.79 billion in first-day revenue and pre-orders reaching 32.5 million units.
