Live: 600 Ukrainian drones target Moscow, Crimea bridge strike halts traffic
Russian air defences intercepted 600 Ukrainian drones flying towards the Moscow region overnight and into Monday morning, Moscow Mayor Sergey Sobyanin said. Ukraine also struck the bridge linking Russia and Crimea across the Kerch straight, the peninsula's Russia-backed governor said. Follow our liveblog for all the latest developments.
- Russian officials confirmed Sunday that the central industrial city of Nizhnekamsk had endured "prolonged" Ukrainian airstrikes overnight, damaging a residential building and sparking a fire at a major petrochemical complex, injuring three people.
- Separate overnight strikes on Taganrog city killed an electricity substation engineer, according to the regional governor.
