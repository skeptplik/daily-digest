When it comes to selecting jewelry, Princess Kate is always intentional. But when it’s Remembrance Sunday, her choices are particularly deliberate.
Taking her place at the Cenotaph alongside Queen Camilla for the United Kingdom’s National Service of Remembrance, the princess was photographed wearing a new bespoke black coat by Catherine Walker and a touching pair of drop pearl earrings.
Originally presented to Princess Diana as a 1981 wedding gift from jeweler Collingwood, the earrings featured a large pear-shaped pearl, capped in diamonds and suspended from an additional stud. The people’s princess wore the set frequently, and after her death in 1997, it was passed onto her eldest son, Prince William. He later gifted them to Kate.
Further accessorizing, the princess paired her new coat—with its structured shoulders and velvet collar—with a wide-brimmed Jane Taylor London hat. She pinned three red poppies to her chest, along with her silver Irish Guards regimental brooch, representing her role as Colonel of the Irish Guards.
Today’s service brought together members of the royal family, politicians, veterans, and military families to honor those who lost their lives in combat. The commemorations officially kicked off last night, with the annual Festival of Remembrance—for which Kate’s look was just as intentional.
Arriving at the Royal Albert Hall on Saturday evening, Kate wore a familiar polka-dot dress last worn at a memorial service for Prince Philip in 2022. She paired the black-and-white midi with diamond earrings that once belonged to her grandmother-in-law, Queen Elizabeth II. The sparkling, statement jewelry featured a floral-shaped cluster of diamonds, from which an additional strand of diamonds, as well as an outer frame of (more) diamonds, hung.
