Toby Fox has given eager fans an update on the development of Deltarune Chapter 6 and Chapter 7 - the Undertale follow-up's final two episodes.
"Chapte 6 is progressing well," he wrote in a new blog post. "The game is now a structurally complete draft that can be played from the beginning to the end. All the maps, events, and battles exist. (Although there's still one optional event which is dragging out a bit…)"
While the bones are supposedly all there, Fox explained that he and his team still need to knock out localisation, testing and polishing. "Much of the game looks complete… but, some important scenes and details are still undercooked in their presentation, so it's going to take a bit longer before we can really move to full-on testing."
Deltarune Chapter 6 was previously described as a slightly shorter episode that should hopefully set up the game's finale quite nicely. Despite being on the smaller side, Fox said he understandably doesn't want to rush development and "would like it to be as good as possible."
And since Deltarune's next chapter is almost past the finish line - at least, in some respect - that means certain members of the team have already moved over to Chapter 7.
"Well, we've only just started," Fox continued. "I read out the events and dialogue of Chapter 7 to the team. However, just when the story reached a critical moment, I stopped reading… I told my team they need to complete more of the game before I tell them what happens. Everyone agrees it's more fun this way"
"Unlike every other Chapter, I actually feel like I know what I'm doing this time, so I have a lot of confidence."
Deltarune's been on a long journey to get here, but, thankfully, the end is finally in sight. Deltarune debuted as a free download all the way back in 2018, a second chapter then dropped in 2021, the next two launched together four years later, before the arrival of the most recent Chapter 5 this year.
We don't know exactly how long Deltarune's last two chapters might take, but the good news is that it's already been a pretty fantastic game for years. Here's hoping those loose threads don't dangle for much longer.
