Just like in real life, there are some people you really shouldn’t mess with in GTA 6. It’s not always easy to tell at a glance either. Books, covers, and all that.
GTA 6 has a wild range of NPCs with unpredictable and unique behavior — you never know what you’re gonna get. Players have been documenting their adventures across Leonida and their interactions with the locals, and have come across the weird, the funny, and the downright scary.
Not only do some NPCs fight back, but they fight hard and can take more punches to the head than Rocky. Some of them even bring little friends along to the throwdown.
The viral clip below, from @WIZZYGM, shows Lucia attempting to take someone’s car. Instead of allowing themselves to be dragged out, however, they got psyched up about having a tussle. They were into it.
Not only did they take a ridiculous number of punches directly to the nose, but at one point they even gave Lucia some free shots. Then they banged some powder up their snout and Hulked out. He even took a gunshot straight to the head!

One clip going around is downright terrifying. The player, having found a sniper rifle at the top of a tower, wanted to test their aim. They looked through the scope at an NPC, took aim, and fired a shot directly at their chest.
Instead of falling over, the NPC started walking towards the tower. Blood pooled on their clothing where the bullet landed, more shots rang out and landed right on target, and they kept coming, tucking their drinks under the arm and breaking into a full sprint.
On the way, they were hit by a car and shot multiple times. There was a trail of blood marking out their route, but they just kept coming.
