Hong Kong police arrest man after hit-and-run at checkpoint sparks 10-hour hunt
Man in his thirties held after police chase through Tuen Mun, hours after a car hit a barrier at the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macau Bridge checkpoint
Hong Kong police arrested a man on suspicion of furious driving after a 10-hour manhunt sparked by a hit-and-run crash at a cross-boundary checkpoint in the early hours of Monday.
Police said a car hit a barrier gate at the Hong Kong-Zhuhai-Macau Bridge checkpoint at 1.17am. The incident was classified as a damage-only traffic accident and a hit-and-run.
Officers located the car near Hoi Wing Road in Castle Peak at about 10.30am and gave chase, intercepting it on Hoi Wong Road at 11.08am.
