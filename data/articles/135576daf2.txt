Odyssey cruise ship’s voyage ends in Singapore arrest after owner goes bust
About 200 passengers disembarked in Cambodia after soaring fuel costs caused the operator’s financial collapse

A cruise ship left stranded in Southeast Asia after its US-based operator ran out of money has been arrested in Singapore, the Maritime and Port Authority (MPA) said on Monday.
The Odyssey, meant to take passengers on a continuous voyage around the world, was arrested after arriving on Sunday from Cambodia, where around 200 guests were told to disembark.
“The vessel has been placed under arrest pursuant to a court order,” MPA said in a statement, without giving further details.
