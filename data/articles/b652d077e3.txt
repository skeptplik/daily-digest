China hits the brakes on controversial trials
China’s fast, cheap route to human testing is getting considerably less speedy. New government restrictions and news of recent patient deaths have slowed investigator-initiated trials, or IITs, STAT’s Jason Mast reports. This is perhaps to the chagrin of U.S. biotechs eager to bypass lengthy development timelines.
