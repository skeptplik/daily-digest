Oura, the smart ring maker that just over a month ago delayed plans to go public over market uncertainty, announced on Tuesday that it will spend $625 million to acquire Counsel Health, a clinical artificial intelligence startup.
Counsel Health is the maker of a chatbot that users can talk with about health concerns before being transferred to a clinician for treatment and diagnosis over telehealth. The company had previously disclosed $36 million in funding.
The large transaction reflects a growing desire to connect consumer wearable devices to clinical care as well as the conviction that AI chatbots will be an important part of health care.
