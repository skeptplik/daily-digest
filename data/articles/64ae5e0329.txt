Monster Hunter Outlanders has earned more than $10.2 million in in-app purchase revenue in just over two weeks.
The open-world RPG based on Capcom's series launched on October 28 via two publishers: Tencent's Level Infinite and Garena. According to AppMagic, the RPG has since racked up 5.1 million downloads across both publishers.
Tencent's version accounts for most of the revenue ($7.3 million from 2.2 million downloads), with Japan making up 79% of that.
That means the Japanese market alone accounts for more than half of the game's total revenue. Tencent's second-biggest market is the US, which generated just 13% of its revenue.
Interestingly, while Garena's version has been downloaded more (2.9 million times), it has generated less revenue, accounting for $2.9 million. The highest share came via Hong Kong (35%), followed by Taiwan and South Korea with 20% and 14%, respectively.
Tencent's version, on the other hand, made $3.2 million in its first five days on sale, placing it among the highest-earning games by IAP for the week of October 26 to November 1.
Between them, the two versions make Outlanders the second-highest-grossing Monster Hunter mobile game still available on app stores, though there's still a way for Outlanders to match Scopely's Monster Hunter Now, which is thought to have generated $193.6 million since 2023.
Capcom recently published its financial results for the six months ended September 30, 2026, reporting increases across the board, including a 67.5% rise in ordinary profit to ¥61.2 billion ($387.2 million).
