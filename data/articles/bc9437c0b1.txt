China’s Communist Party plenum targets passive ‘nice’ guys in anti-corruption push
‘Lao hao ren’, conflict-averse officials who turn a blind eye to wrongdoing, face crackdown in escalation of party clean-up drive
China’s ruling Communist Party has pledged to target “old nice persons” within its ranks as it steps up its anti-corruption drive ahead of a major leadership reshuffle next year.
Rendered in Chinese as lao hao ren, the term refers to unprincipled, conflict-averse officials who turn a blind eye to misconduct to protect their own careers and social standing.
