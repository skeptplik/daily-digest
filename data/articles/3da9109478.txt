With Black Friday around the corner, some deals have gone live ahead of the biggest shopping day in the US. Amazon is getting in on the fun early, offering a wide variety of sales and deals on all kinds of categories. If you're a big reader or Tolkien fan in general, one of the best book deals right now is the Hobbit and Lord of the Rings illustrated edition collections, which are down to $61.83 from its $150 sticker price. This is currently its lowest price ever, and a great deal if you're on the hunt for a good Lord of the Rings gift for that Middle-Earth fan in your life this year.
Each of the four hardcover volumes is illustrated by longtime Tolkien artist Alan Lee, who also provided the concept art for Peter Jackson's The Lord of the Rings film trilogy. The books' covers and spines are all painterly depictions of events from the stories, and look great displayed on a bookshelf, held together nicely by the included slipcase. The stories themselves are unchanged from Tolkien's original novels, but you can now better immerse yourself in his rich world thanks to Lee's illustrations as a visual aid.
If you're concerned about space, the box set together measures in at 10.6in x 6.7 x 6.6, so it's larger than your average book. But it's still manageable to throw on your bookshelf if you have the space, or each book separately work well as a smaller coffee table piece. At this price, it's a good opportunity to finally jump into reading the books if you've been curious for awhile, while having something really nice to look at when you're done.
Is this a good deal?
While it's true that Amazon could drop a better discount on Cyber Monday, it seems unlikely. So considering this box set is currently at its lowest price ever, there's really no reason to wait for a better deal.
