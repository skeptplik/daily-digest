NEW TALENT: Gianmaria Pizzoccheri has joined Fendi as worldwide private client relations director, effective immediately, WWD has learned. This is part of Fendi chairman and chief executive officer Ramon Ros’ review of the house’s organization.
Pizzoccheri began his career at Harrods and brings to Fendi extensive experience in luxury client relations, having held key positions at Dior, Valentino and Louis Vuitton, and an established international network of top-tier clients.
Pizzoccheri succeeds Giulia Girardi.
Sources say the appointment is part of a broader strategy at Fendi to elevate expertise.
Ros joined Fendi in July 2025 from Louis Vuitton in mainland China, where he held the role of president and CEO, naming Maria Grazia Chiuri the Rome-based brand’s chief creative officer in October 2025. Sources say he has been aiming to build a more agile and sophisticated organization with new talent, in order to meet the expectations of the global luxury market and deliver an increasingly personalized and superior client experience and service with a focus on craftsmanship.
In September, Ros told WWD that through Chiuri’s products, Fendi is “restoring gradually the client excitement and the momentum at all levels and globally, in ready-to-wear, shoes and bags. For the first time we are recapturing all sleeping clients that we had or clients from other high-end brands.” He also underscored the importance of craft at Fendi, “always one of the main engines, a really detailed product, for life, and that’s something that we are making even bolder and stronger.”
Clienteling in retail with a view to building long-term, high-value relationships between brands, sales associates and shoppers is increasingly key in this uncertain and volatile market, and luxury houses continue to heavily invest in dedicated high-end clienteling projects and private client activations.
