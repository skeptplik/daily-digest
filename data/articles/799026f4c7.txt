How surf lifesaving is influencing these Gold Coast AFLW players' performance
In short:
Nine Gold Coast Suns players have surf lifesaving backgrounds.
Players say their experience helps them prepare for the unknown.
The Suns are drafting a high percentage of local talent, and have gone from wooden spooners to fifth-placed finishers in a year.
