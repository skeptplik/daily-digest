The trade-secrets fight between OpenAI and Apple is widening, with the artificial intelligence company accusing the iPhone maker of undermining its supplier relationships and using unfair employment agreements to prevent talent from jumping ship.
OpenAI made the allegations in counterclaims against Apple on Friday in federal court in San Jose, California. The filing also accuses Apple of stealing OpenAI’s trade secrets, transforming the lawsuit Apple brought earlier against OpenAI into a two-way battle over the companies’ intellectual property.
The case has overshadowed OpenAI’s bid to sell its own devices, a move that would put it in competition with the iPhone maker. Former Apple design guru Jony Ive is helping the AI company develop a family of products, starting with what’s expected to be a doughnut-shaped smart speaker.
