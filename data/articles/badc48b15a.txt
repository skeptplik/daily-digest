Black Friday is entering the evening, which means one of the best times of the year to save some cash on a new pair of AirPods or the latest Apple Watch is coming to a close. After all, the holiday savings can help offset the unfortunate price hikes from earlier this year, when Apple raised the cost of some of its most popular gear. Some discounts still apply to recent releases like the Apple Watch Series 12 and touchscreen MacBook Pro, too, which are available at their lowest prices to date.
The best Black Friday deals you can still get on AirPods, Macs, and other Apple gear
We dug up the biggest price drops on Apple hardware and accessories before Black Friday officially ends.
In addition to a variety of discounts across retailers, Apple is offering up to $300 in gift cards with select purchases between November 27th and November 30th. How much you get back is determined by how much you spend, with up to $100 available for some iPhone and Apple Watch purchases, and up to $300 for the 16-inch MacBook Pro. That said, in some cases you may be able to save more by buying from other retailers.

They also deliver relatively bass-rich sound for their design, along with solid noise cancellation, wireless charging, and AirPods features like adaptive audio thanks to Apple’s H2 chip. The latest Beats Studio Pro feature improved noise cancellation, sound quality, and comfort. They also support lossless audio over USB-C and offer native support for a range of iOS and Android features like other Beats headphones. Read our review.

MacBook deals
Apple’s most affordable laptop uses the same A18 Pro chip found in the iPhone 16 Pro. It features a vibrant 13-inch display, surprisingly good speakers, and 8GB of RAM, making it a solid option for students and anyone who needs a laptop for everyday tasks. Read our review.
Apple’s latest MacBook Air features an M5 chip and starts with 512GB of storage, with significantly faster performance than prior generations. It’s still an excellent machine despite the higher base price, especially given the battery life and speakers. Read our review.
- The recently released 16.3-inch MacBook Pro with a PROLED XDR display, 24GB of RAM, and 1TB of storage is on sale starting at $3,099 ($300 off) — a new low price — at Amazon. The redesigned laptop is Apple’s first with an OLED touchscreen, which allows for rich colors and deep blacks. It’s also noticeably thinner and lighter than the non-touchscreen 16-inch MacBook Pro, and features a new Dynamic Island that replaces the notch and provides quick access to controls and notifications. Read our review.
- The 14-inch M6-powered MacBook Pro is on sale starting at $1,749 ($250 off) at Amazon and Best Buy, which is a new low price. The Pro builds on its predecessor with a faster chip and support for Wi-Fi 7. In our testing, it handled multitasking, photo editing, and occasional video editing with ease while still delivering exceptional battery life. It lacks the newer MacBook Pro’s gorgeous OLED display, but its Mini LED screen still looks great, especially given its smooth 120Hz refresh rate. It also offers excellent speakers and a good selection of ports, including three Thunderbolt 4 ports, HDMI, and an SD card slot. Read our review.
