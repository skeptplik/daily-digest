Warning: This article features light spoilers for the early hours of GTA 6.
GTA 6's first chapter has been somewhat divisive due to its slower pace, but it's also a part of the game that some fans have spent dozens of hours playing due to it highly immersive slice-of-life elements.
Instead of throwing you headfirst into crazy heists or explosive set pieces, the first chapter of GTA 6 is largely spent bumbling around Rockstar's version of the Florida Keys. You can go fishing, scuba diving, and even fix up your house before Lucia's release from prison. It's an exceptionally low-key and chill opening to a game filled with all kinds of crazy action, which is why it's also rubbed some people the wrong way. They don't want to do chores or kick it on the beach; they want to cause some mayhem.
However, for others, this has become an unexpected hook for GTA 6 that actually delayed their interest in getting to the meat of Rockstar's open-world epic. Various fans of the game have said they spent anywhere from 10 to as many as 40+ hours in just Chapter 1 alone. Keep in mind, this is a sliver of the overall experience, and much of the game's most interesting activities don't get added until subsequent chapters. However, some players just enjoyed living inside of this part of the game before things go horribly wrong and they're forced to start ramping up the violence and crime.
"Best thing about that early chapter is just how low stakes and casual it feels," said X user @CS11__ after spending 41 hours in Chapter 1 alone. "I forced myself not to go farther than Hamlet, so I really only ever experienced these two small areas in 40 hours. [...] The game hardly opened up, but it still felt unbelievably rich and fun."
But... what is it these people are actually doing, if not blowing stuff up and creating tons of chaos? Some fans were just going through the motions, giving themselves daily routines that involved working out, taking on relaxing hobbies, and exploring.
"[A] lot of roleplaying, day-to-day living type stuff," said Aaron Espionza on X, who estimated that he spent around 15–18 hours before picking up Lucia from prison. "I had a routine even when I would wake up, drive to the juice bar down the street, get my orange juice, and then say hello to the locals. If anyone said anything mean, I would get violent and throw empty juice cups at them. [I] did the workout routines. Basketball was a favorite of mine. Side stuff with Cal fishing, Donnie pick-ups, and scuba. But so much of it was testing how much detail Rockstar put into it. 'If I do this, will they do that?' type of systems exploration. Always came away surprised."
X user @scaasm noted that the lower stakes nature of Chapter 1 even prompted him to follow traffic laws and use manual turn signals in GTA 6: "I follow traffic laws unless I'm doing something criminal, and generally just taking it very slow. [I'm] loving every moment."
The location itself was also a big driving point for a lot of fans, some of whom have personal connections or apprecation for its real life counterpart: "As someone from Florida, I think the Keys is one of those locations that they absolutely nailed (waning sands outlets too; they look exactly like the Orlando ones)," said @piterparker69 on X. "It felt kinda uncanny driving around (especially the 7 Mile Bridge) because of how good it replicates real life."
"I have been obsessed with the Florida Keys for the last 5 years, and finding out you spend the start of the game there, catching fish, fixing my house, working out, and just vibing gave me little motivation to move on," said @AnchormanV2 on X after spending roughly a dozen hours in Chapter 1.
"I took my sweet time in Key Lento, not necessarily to avoid progressing the story, but more so [bcause] I was just having so much fun exploring the keys and just taking it all in," said X user @HorrorUntitled. "There’s just so much to see to see and happening. It’s very overwhelming."
Of course, some people did get into a bit of open-world crime as well, or explore a bit beyond the Keys, but a lot of it was still a result of the story feeling low-stakes and not having much urgency in its first chaper. X user Ryan Ranc even noted that he would go around Key Lento acting as a vigilante: "I’m playing as Batman and roaming at night on my motorbike stopping crime."
Chapter 1 in GTA 6 is a very interesting piece of the game, and some even noted that they longed for that tranquility after moving on in the story. It's an intentional slow burn, as things get turned up to an 11 at the end of the chapter and make you come to appreciate what you had. It's the calm before the storm in many ways, and some people are really basking in that.
