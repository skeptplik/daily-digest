Gravity Well has entered hibernation after failing to secure funding.
Founded by veterans of Respawn and Infinity Ward, the studio lost funding earlier this year, resulting in layoffs last month.
At the time, the developer stated its "future was uncertain" and that a smaller team would continue to "fight for survival." Gravity Well was developing an unannounced game with original IP using Unreal Engine 5.
"We don't exactly know what is next for us or Gravity Well, but we do know we still believe in the IP and game we were building," said CEO Drew McCoy, CTO Jon Shiring, and COO Mon Saetern in a joint statement.
"Even more so, the team we managed to cultivate was truly world-class, and we're going to miss getting to work with them every day. If you're a hiring manager or recruiter and a Gravity Well resume hits your desk, they come with the highest recommendation."
Gravity Well was founded in 2020 by McCoy and Shiring as a "remote-native" developer, having launched during the pandemic.
Speaking to GamesIndustry.biz at the time, McCoy said he wanted to keep the team small as an AAA developer so employees could take ownership.
"We want it to feel like there is a sense of ownership. Not just in terms of what they are building on a day-to-day basis, but on the studio itself – how we operate, how we make decisions, and our processes."
McCoy also addressed the studio's funding approach. "For us, the important part of the funding is retaining control, both of the studio and the games we end up making. The early days of Respawn were amazing in terms of the control we had."
