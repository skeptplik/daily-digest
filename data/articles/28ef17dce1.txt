Esthetician Mimi Luzon has been working on famous faces (such as Naomi Campbell, the Hadid sisters, Sabrina Carpenter and more) for more than 30 years. Her commitment to skin health and product innovation keeps clients coming back — whether to her hands or her own line of skin-care products. If you’ve ever seen a celebrity post what looks like a gold leaf facial, that’s probably from Luzon’s famous 24K Pure Gold Mask Treatment. She even performed the facial on many of the Victoria’s Secret models before last month’s big show.
We caught up with Luzon to try the famous facial and learn all about her love of precious metals (especially copper, silver and gold, of course) for their anti-inflammatory and skin-firming benefits, as well as her favorite in-office treatment that will snatch your face without any downtime.
