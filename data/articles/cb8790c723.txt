IGN Entertainment, the parent company of GamesIndustry.biz, has released the Official Online Guide for GTA 6, created through direct collaboration with Rockstar Games. The free guide, which was published on IGN.com, includes an extensive interactive map built using the firm's MapGenie platform, along with checklist trackers for completed tasks and detailed guides on missions, mechanics, and the game's vast open world.
In a press release, the firm said that the guide was "inspired by classic print strategy guides", and "will be continuously updated post launch."
The partnership has the potential to drive significant long-term traffic for the site. Game guides have served as the primary traffic source for consumer gaming media for many years, and GTA 6 has been hotly anticipated as a top-selling title with a wide range of systems and challenges that players will seek guidance on. While many sites have seen recent traffic drops attributed to Google's rollout of AI summaries in search results, which reduced the number of users sent to the sites from which the information is drawn, tools like MapGenie are expected to prove more enduring as they can't be directly reproduced.
“IGN is celebrating its 30th anniversary this year – our guides and game help offerings have evolved alongside our website and the internet," said Peer Schneider, Chief Development Officer and co-founder of IGN Entertainment. "We are excited to add official guides to our product offering and couldn’t be more proud to start off with the most-anticipated game on the planet, GTA 6.”
“In addition to lending gamers a helping hand when they most need it, one of our primary goals is to aid discovery and player agency by keeping the experience spoiler-safe.”
