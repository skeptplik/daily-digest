What do Millennials want for the holidays? A housing market that doesn’t make us want to scream and cry? A time machine to go back to 2016, when Instagram was just filtered pictures, TikTok didn’t exist, AI was science fiction and our biggest problem was living under the tyranny of the skinny jean trend?
OK, maybe we should be a little more optimistic. After all, #MillennialHopeCore is on the rise once again; our unofficial evidence includes recent election results (!!), upcoming reboots of “Glee,” “Girls” and “Spring Awakening;” and the resurgence of 2010s fashion. But just because we’re feeling the vibe shift doesn’t mean we want disco pants, crusty matte lipstick and jeggings for the holidays.
To help you navigate the increasingly specific tastes of the Millennial in your life, three Fashionista editors from the generation in question have assembled an extensive holiday gift guide. Consider it a non-exhaustive snapshot of what we’re into for 2027: wardrobe upgrades that make us feel chic and put-together, beauty tech that promises to turn back the proverbial clock and products that makes a good hair day feel achievable quickly.
We’re not saying we’re high-maintenance. We’re just saying our standards have evolved considerably since the days of $5 cocktails.
