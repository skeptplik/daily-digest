End of the line: the last Star Ferry slips its moorings at Central Pier
by Norma Connolly and Winnie Yeung
The lights of the Star Ferry clock tower and pier in Central went out at midnight last night (November 11, 2006) as the iconic green ferries left their Central berth for the last time – sparking protests for some and nostalgia for others.
