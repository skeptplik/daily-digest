Apparently, no one told Marvel that GTA VI is out today, because they've been busy showing off new glimpses of Avengers: Doomsday. In addition to a behind-the-scenes video featuring Robert Downey, Jr., Marvel has also revealed a massive lineup of posters showcasing the film's ensemble cast.
These 11 posters form one large montage showing the combined forces of the X-Men, Fantastic Four, and Avengers in battle against Downey's Doctor Doom and his army of Sentinels and Doombots. Get a closer look at all these connecting posters in the slideshow gallery below:
These images tease some interesting new details for the film, like giving us a better look at Alan Cumming's Nightcrawler in costume and showcasing the Doombots (Doctor Doom's android servants/bodyguards) for the first time.
Perhaps most interesting is the poster featuring Chris Hemsworth's Thor and Chris Evans' Steve Rogers. Steve is wielding Mjolnir and channeling the weapon's signature blue lightning, but Thor's Stormbreaker is instead summoning yellow lightning. This has already led fans to speculate that Thor is now empowered by the Odinforce, signalling a major power upgrade for this already mighty Avenger. Though if the Doomsday trailers are any indication, that's not going to be enough to stop Doom.
We also have to wonder if the character pairings in these posters are significant and meant to tease various team-ups in the film. Certainly, Steve and Thor have a long history together. And we doubt it was merely coincidence that Marvel chose to pair Vanessa Kirby's Invisible Woman and Tenoch Huerta's Namor, given the (mostly one-sided) romantic attachment they share in the comics.
But what about the rest of the posters? Will Channing Tatum's Gambit partner up with Florence Pugh's Yelena Belova? What about Tom Hiddleston's Loki and Rebecca Romijn's Mystique? We'll have to wait until Doomsday hits theaters on December 18 to find out.
In the meantime, Marvel has already begun teasing Doomsday's sequel, 2027's Avengers: Secret Wars, revealing that the film will be very different in terms of tone and genre. We've also learned that Ryan Reynolds and Hugh Jackman have been given writing credits on Doomsday, suggesting that Deadpool and Wolverine will appear in the film.
