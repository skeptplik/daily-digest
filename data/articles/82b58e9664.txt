Watch: Moment troops raid home and capture Mexican drug lord
Newly-released bodycam footage shows the moment troops entered a house and detained a Mexican drug lord who had managed to evade capture for years.
The video shows armed personnel entering via a garage and finding Fausto Isidro Meza Flores, also known as 'El Chapo Isidro', who was on the FBI's most wanted list.
Seven suspects were killed in last Friday's operation, as were four members of the Mexican army who were travelling to reinforce security in Guasave, Mexico's defence secretariat said.
The arrest of Meza Flores in the northern Sinaloa state sparked a wave of unrest, which led to eight alleged drug cartel members being detained for their involvement in the retaliatory violence.
