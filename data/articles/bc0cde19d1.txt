A purple rabbit-like creature was selected as the new mascot for East Japan Railway’s (JR East) Suica brand, winning more than half of the 1.6 million votes cast from Japan and abroad.
The yet-to-be-named critter — considered by many to be the oddest of the options, which also included a squirrel and a mole-like character carrying a smaller creature in a crossbody bag — garnered 860,152 votes from the public.
“In this character, I incorporated the possibilities of Suica’s evolution together with JR East, as well as the ideas and feelings behind that evolution, into every aspect of its colors, shape and gestures,“ said Hiroaki Seto, the character’s designer, at an unveiling ceremony Wednesday.
