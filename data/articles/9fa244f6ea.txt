One of my favorite Black Friday deals, the big TCGplayer Cyber Weekend deal, is back for another year, and it couldn’t be easier to buy the cards you want and be rewarded for it.
Whether you’re looking for singles, packs, or even entire booster boxes across just about any trading card game, it’s a great time to invest in your collection.
Here’s how it works!
How TCGplayer’s Black Friday Deal Works
If you’re new here, it’s pretty simple to take advantage of the TCGplayer Black Friday deal. Rather than discounting cards or products, the retailer awards buyers 10% of their purchase price back as store credit.
This applies from 9 AM New York time on November 27 to 11 PM New York Time on November 30.
So, buying a $30 card nets you $3, and so on and so forth. Because you can fill your cart with TCGs from all over the world, however, it’s easy to rack up plenty of credit.
For example, if you buy a Play Booster Box of Magic: The Gathering and some preconstructed Riftbound decks, you’ll end up with plenty to spend on, say, some Lorcana cards.
It’s a smart system that keeps you coming back to TCGplayer’s list of reputable sellers, helping them keep selling.
It’s also a great excuse to pick up a bunch of cards you’ve been waiting for. If I wasn’t in the UK, I’d be dipping into my card fund to nab myself Yawgmoth, Thran Physician, and Vorinclex, Monstrous Raider for my Magic collection, knowing the store credit would help me secure some bonus cards, too. Buy a bunch of bulk, or go for that splashy purchase - you’re getting 10% back regardless.
Why TCGplayer?
We recommend TCGplayer for a few reasons. For one, it’s backed by eBay, so there’s an inherent trustworthiness you might not find elsewhere.
For another, its sellers are reactive to the market. All too often, you’ll see a card or product crater in value, but the sellers don’t adjust. Here, you know you’re getting a good deal.
Finally, TCGplayer just has a ridiculous number of card games covered. Whether it’s Magic: The Gathering, the Pokémon TCG, Disney Lorcana, One Piece, or just about anything else, you’ll find a vast library of singles and sealed product available.
