Is artificial intelligence putting entry-level jobs in science at risk? As the technology increasingly takes on coding and basic data analysis, alongside other purely cognitive tasks, what will happen to graduate student and postdoctoral roles? And what does the future hold for programmers and technicians? These roles have conventionally been stepping stones to other scientific positions.
A 60-minute webinar organized by Nature’s Careers team will investigate how AI is transforming careers in science, as well as the technology’s short- and long-term impact on early-career researcher roles.
The live event takes place on 2 December at 15:00 GMT.
