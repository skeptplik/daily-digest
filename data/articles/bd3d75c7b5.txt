39" Alienware AW3926QW 5K RGB Tandem Stripe OLED Monitor
Earlier this year, Dell announced the "world's first 39" 5K monitor with RGB stripe tandem OLED technology."It launched with a retail price of $1,099.99 but for Black Friday you can get it for $100 less. The Alienware AW3926QW has no lack of features that make it stand out from the crowd, but perhaps its most impressive inclusion is the LG RGB stripe tandem OLED panel. Tandem OLED stacks multiple OLED layers on top of each other instead of a single layer. Tandem OLED is better than other OLED panels because: 1) it's much brighter (the AW3926QW in particular has a very impressive 1,300 nits of peak brightness) and 2) it's less prone to burn-in.
The AW3926QW doubles down on this with new RGB stripe technology. Whereas traditional tandem OLEDs use white pixels, RGB stripe tandem OLED breaks the color down further into vertically aligned red, green, and blue pixels. This reduces color fringing and in turn significantly improves text clarity. It goes without saying that there are obvious benefits here for both gaming and productivity purposes alike.

Alienware's most popular 4K OLED gaming monitor is also down to its lowest price ever. The AW3225QF is a rare gaming monitor that combines a native 4K resolution, 240Hz refresh rate, and a gorgeous Samsung-sourced quantum dot OLED panel. If you're looking for the best 4K gaming monitor, this should definitely be on your short list. The Alienware AW3225QF is HDR True Black 400 certified with up to 1,000 nits of peak brightness. It also boasts a factory calibrated 99.3% DCI-P3 color range, 1700R curve, and G-Sync certification.
32" Alienware AW3226Q 4K RGB Tandem Stripe OLED Monitor
The 32" Alienware AW3226Q gaming monitor is another recent release that's aimed toward the more budget-conscious crowd. The monitor launched about a month ago at a very reasonable $699.99 price tag, but it's already $50 off ahead of Black Friday. Unlike the slighty more expensive AW3225QF which uses a curved 240Hz QD-OLED panel from Samsung, the Alienware AW3226Q is equipped with a flat panel 165Hz RGB stripe tandem OLED panel from LG. In exchange for a lower refresh rate and no official G-Sync certification (it's still G-Sync compatible), you get better text clarity and arguably a better monitor for non-gaming use.
