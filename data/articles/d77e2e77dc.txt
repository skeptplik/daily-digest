Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 still receives significant support two years after launch. We’re now up to patch 16.0, which makes significant balance changes to the game.
In terms of new features, we’ve got a major expansion of the Vanguard class’ combat abilities (the Grapnel Launcher can now be used at close range!), the new Revenge status effect (hitting enemies afflicted with Revenge also deals damage to nearby enemies), and Wargear 2.3 (Bulwark can now equip and customize Cloak!).
As for cosmetics, the hotly anticipated Deathwatch Champion Pack is out now as part of the Season Pass. There’s also a Mortifactors Cosmetic Pack. I’ve put the balance changes as part of the patch notes below, if you want to skip to them to see what’s happened to your class.
Also interesting is the note about tech improvements with patch 16. Publisher Focus Entertainment says loading times have improved, which players will welcome. There are also memory optimizations, “minor” performance improvements, and “general” connectivity improvements. Anyone who’s played or is still playing Space Marine 2 will hope these improvements are meaningful.
Focus has said that next year, Space Marine 2 will receive three more updates until its third anniversary. Patch 17, 18 and 19 will offer free content as well as Season Pass 3 content with the cosmetic DLCs. There’s a lot of excitement around Patch 17, which adds a brand-new class, the Apothecary, complete with its signature Absolvor Bolt Pistol. Patch 17 also adds a new Operation and fresh Heroic weapons, and a rework of the Heavy class. A new Imperial Fist Champion Pack and a Cosmetic Pack for the Minotaurs chapter are also on the way.
The Switch 2 version of Space Marine 2, however, is delayed out of 2026 into 2027. “Considering its current polishing needs, we are constrained to pushing the release to 2027,” Focus said.
Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 update 16 patch notes:
NEW FEATURES
New Feature: Vanguard Class Expansion
The Vanguard class receives a major expansion to its combat capabilities. The Grapnel Launcher can now be used at close range, making the ability more versatile and responsive in the heat of battle. The class perk tree has also undergone significant changes, with numerous perks reworked and repositioned, alongside new key perks designed to improve crowd control and increase effectiveness against groups of enemies. These include a new perk that allows the Grapnel Launcher to apply Stun and Revenge status effects.
New Feature: Revenge Status Effect
Hitting enemies afflicted with Revenge status effect also deals damage to nearby enemies.
🟡Revenge base damage increased by 50%.
🟡Revenge damage radius increased from 10 to 12 meters.
🟡Revenge maximum number of targets increased from 4 to 5 enemies.
🟡Revenge effect duration increased from 8 to 10 seconds.
New Feature: Wargear 2.3
The Bulwark class receives expanded customization options. Robes and tabards can now be customized through a dedicated Wargear slot instead of the Chest slot, offering more freedom to personalize its appearance. Additionally, Bulwark can now equip and customize Cloak.
Chaos Siege Mode Map Expansion: Sector #3
The final part of the "Hive" map. Deep within the hive city of Avarax, the forces of Chaos are preparing a Daemon Host ritual. Loyalists are attempting to put an end to this heresy.
