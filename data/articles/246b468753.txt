The Verbatim Vi7000 is Playstation 5 compatible. That means it fulfills all of Sony's requirements. It boasts sequential speeds of 7000MB/s read and 6500MB/s write, which easily surpasses the minimum recommended speed of 5,500MB/s. The SSD uses the PCI-Express Gen4x4 interface which slots neatly into the PS5's drive bay. It also has a preinstalled heatsink that will do a fine job of keeping your SSD cool.
The Vi7000 is also a great option as a boot drive for your gaming PC. Surprisingly, this RAM boasts actual DRAM cache. Pretty much every other "budget" SSD at this price point is DRAM-less and relies on HMB, or system memory, as a less expensive alternative. The advantage of having a DRAM cache is faster random read/write performance and faster sustained transfer speeds. It doesn't make much of a difference in a PS5 console but PC users might notice it.
