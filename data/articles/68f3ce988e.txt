Over the last eight months, STAT has investigated the ways MRT is marketed as a fertility treatment for older women, despite scant evidence it works.
The procedure, popularly called mitochondrial replacement techniques or “three-parent IVF,” is illegal in the United States, but it is accessible via a sprawling for-profit tourism industry, connecting women in the U.S. and Europe to clinics abroad.
