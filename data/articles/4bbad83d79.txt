Blow for Netanyahu as Israeli election body rejects claims of Arab party fraud
The prime minister has alleged fraud by Arab-majority parties, pointing to the arrests of people with envelopes of cash on polling day
President Isaac Herzog will at noon officially receive the results of the October 27 election as he decides whether to give Netanyahu or his rival, Gadi Eisenkot, the first chance to form a government, with neither commanding enough votes alone.
The Central Elections Committee said it had conducted an audit of 9,069 polling stations following complaints and was confident that the announced results “accurately reflect the will of the voters”.
