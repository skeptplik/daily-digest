Cuban singer-songwriter Camila Cabello presented at the 27th Annual Latin GRAMMY Awards held at MGM Grand Garden Arena last Thursday night. For the evening, Cabello showed up in a distinctive ensemble. The singer wore a two-piece, lingerie-inspired look from the Simone Rocha Spring 2027 collection and paired her outfit with a simple-but-fashionable pair of Paris Texas’ Lidia mules.
On top, Cabello’s wore a pale pink negligee bralette with slinky, off-the-shoulder straps and ivory lace trim hanging down from the underbust. Cabello combined this unmissable top with a midi-skirt in a matching shade of pink featuring embroidered tulle lining the hemline. She paired the look with classic Lidia pumps from Paris Texas.
These versatile shoes feature a translucent, nearly invisible PVC strap at the vamp, an almond-shaped open toe, and sharp stiletto heel. Cabello’s pumps were a metallic silver shade. She embellished her choice of footwear slightly, wearing a silver-tone rosary anklet on her right foot. Stemming for the oval medallion resting on Cabello’s upper ankle was a beaded cross medallion extending down to her toes.

This past year, Cabello’s been seen in a number of bold outfits that included some inspired footwear. At Chanel’s fall Ready to Wear fashion week, Cabello stepped out in a well-coordinated plaid tweed ensemble complete with an eye-catching pair of black platform heels. While attending the Three Kings Parade in Miami last January, Cabello was all in black down to her calf-high pointed-toe boots adorned with understated stitching detail and silver straps and buckles.
On Thursday night, Cabello attended the Latin GRAMMY awards to present the Record of the Year Award. Cabello has previously won Latin Grammys herself in 2017, 2019, and 2023. In 2019, she received the award for Record of the Year for Mi Persona Favorita, an album written in collaboration with fellow singer Alejandro Sanz. This year, Cabello ended up handing over the award to Argentine rapper Milo J for his song “Niño.”
