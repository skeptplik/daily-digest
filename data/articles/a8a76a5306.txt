Malaysia Hindu rites row: live stream of Muslim youth’s repentance fuels fury
The public spectacle highlights the tightening boundaries of religious tolerance and state control over personal worship in multiracial Malaysia
The spectacle has raised questions about the enforcement of Islamic orthodoxy in Muslim-majority Malaysia, where state religious departments wield significant influence to police the personal conduct and religious beliefs of Muslims.
Wearing emerald-green traditional Malay attire, 21-year-old Aliff Daniel Adli read his declaration before senior Islamic officials at the Sultan Salahuddin Abdul Aziz Shah Mosque in Selangor, the state surrounding Kuala Lumpur. The proceedings were broadcast on the official Facebook page of the Selangor Islamic Religious Department.
