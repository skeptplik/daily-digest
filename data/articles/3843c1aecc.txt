US loses measles elimination status, major setback for US public health – video
The US lost its measles elimination status, global health authorities have announced on Tuesday, an official recognition that the infectious disease is endemic in the US after being declared eliminated for 26 years. The announcement comes as the US endures the worst measles outbreak since 1991 under Donald Trump and his health secretary, Robert F Kennedy Jr, with more than 5,400 cases and eight deaths reported in the US in 2026
