CISA warns of exploited N-able bug as company restores email platform
The federal cybersecurity agency is giving U.S. government departments until Thanksgiving to patch a vulnerability impacting a critical email security platform that blocks spam, malware and other threats.
All federal agencies were urged to patch CVE-2026-134624 – a bug affecting N-able’s Mail Assure platform. The vulnerability carries a 9.3 severity score and was added to the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency’s (CISA) exploited vulnerabilities platform on Monday evening.
N-able has dealt with a week of outages tied to the vulnerability after hackers breached one of the company’s servers through the bug, causing damage to Mail Assure and SpamExperts services.
In a statement to Recorded Future News, N-able said it remediated the bug over the weekend and restored access to internal dashboards for customers.
Inbound and outbound email flow and spam filtering continued to operate throughout the incident, they added.
“Since completing these remediation actions, we have continued scanning and monitoring the Mail Assure environments and have found no evidence of exploitation beyond the single N-able-hosted server identified in our initial investigation,” a spokesperson said, adding that they hired incident responders to validate their findings.
The company pledged to publish a root cause analysis within the next two weeks.
N-able said the breach and service issues are not connected to more than 30 vulnerabilities impacting its N-central platform that were publicicized two weeks ago by a security researcher.
N-central is a popular remote management tool that thousands of IT teams use to monitor and run networks remotely. N-able took all hosted N-central environments offline and ordered customers to turn off their servers.
A bug in the company’s N-central platform was involved in a supply-chain attack by ransomware actors in August.
N-able was previously a business unit within software company SolarWinds before it was spun off as a separate cloud-based software solutions company offering tools to managed service providers.
