Vienna – Members of the OPEC+ alliance meet Sunday to hammer out production quotas for next year despite little visibility on the U.S. war against Iran, which has roiled oil markets worldwide.
Ministers are also to set maximum capacity levels for each country, which aim to increase transparency of their industries and will form the basis for output quotas moving forward.
But some of the alliance’s 21 members are already contesting the new levels, reached after two years of analysis by an outside consulting firm, U.S.-based DeGolyer and MacNaughton.
