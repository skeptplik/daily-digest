First Northern Metropolis Marathon trips up as runners hit out at poor arrangements
Experts urge rethink after runners slam hot conditions, uneven terrain, lack of water stations, traffic stops and long trek to retrieve belongings
Organised by the New Territories’ Heung Yee Kuk to mark the rural body’s centenary, the inaugural event drew about 15,000 registered runners across four race categories. The organisers had pitched the marathon as a showcase for the region’s development.
