This year's Black Friday deals have dozens of neat video game-themed accessories and collectibles on sale. At Amazon, you can pick up an officially licensed mini Yoshi Egg light. It's marked down to $19.94, only about $4 off the sticker price, but it makes a great gift for any gamer or to spruce up your game room. It's sold by a third party but shipped by Amazon and is a Paladone product, who are known for their nice quality third-party items.
This miniature egg measures in at just 5in. tall and roughly 4in. wide, so it won't be too hard to display on a game shelf or as part of a larger Super Mario collection. It's USB-powered and the cord is included, but I think I'd have preferred it to be battery powered instead to move it around more easily. The light itself is LED, so it'll illuminate your room nicely, and the green spots from the shell help add a little more personality to it when lit.
It doesn't make any fun Yoshi noises like some other licensed products, but it's still a nice collector's item that serves a practical purpose.
