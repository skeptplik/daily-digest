Liverpool, England – The scale of Mauricio Pochettino’s rebuilding job was laid bare on Sunday as Manchester United crashed to a 4-0 Premier League defeat against Liverpool, with its first match under the Argentine unravelling within minutes.
Reduced to 10 men after eight minutes, United fell two goals behind within 13 minutes and never recovered as Liverpool cruised to victory through goals from Dominik Szoboszlai, Florian Wirtz, teenager Rio Ngumoha and Victor Munoz.
United’s nightmare start to the Pochettino era could hardly have been worse as Leny Yoro was sent off for bringing down Alexander Isak, and the visiting club paid the price moments later when Szoboszlai curled home the resulting free kick.
