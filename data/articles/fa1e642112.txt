What went wrong for the Cybertruck? What do we know about the fire that ravaged an award-tipped new building? And why have wired headphones made a comeback? Listen to the latest episode of Dezeen Weekly now.
In this episode, Nat Barker, Jennifer Hahn and Tom Ravenscroft reflect on the news that Tesla is discontinuing its controversial Cybertruck.
After that, they discuss the developing situation regarding a Dezeen Award-shortlisted architecture project designed by Jean Nouvel that has been badly damaged in a fire.
Finally, they consider Sony’s reissue of its classic Walkman-era headphones and the wider resurgence of wired headphones.
