Elon Musk said Ukrainian president Volodymyr Zelenskyy is lying about disruptions to Starshield in Ukraine and that he’s trying to misuse SpaceX’s consumer service, Starlink, for military purposes. Musk made the comments at The New York Times’ DealBook Summit on Wednesday as he tried to explain the conflict following weeks of back and forth between Zelenskyy, Musk, and the US government.
Elon Musk tries to explain what’s going on with Ukraine and Starlink
The SpaceX CEO claims that Ukraine president Volodymyr Zelenskyy is ‘lying’ about using Starshield.
“From the beginning, I’ve said that Starlink is a civilian system,” Musk said. “It is not meant to be used for offensive drone strikes that kill people. If you want to do that, you need to do Starshield, which is a separate system that is controlled by the US government. But do not ask me as a private individual to make kill decisions.”
Musk also said that “if they want to do something that’s an offensive military action, it needs to be the Starshield system where the kill decision is that of the War Department, perhaps ultimately the president, and not mine.”
The interviewer, Andrew Ross Sorkin, said that President Zelenskyy refers to the system as Starshield and is claiming that is the system that was slowed down. “That’s false,” Musk said, claiming that Zelenskyy is “not always truthful.”
Sorkin asked, “So when President Zelenskyy talks about using Starshield, he is not using Starshield, or he’s lying?”
“He is lying,” Musk said. “What he is actually saying is he wants to use Starlink for offensive military operations.”
