An on-duty police officer has been shot and a man found dead nearby as police investigate two firearm incidents in a tiny Tasmanian town.
The officer was working at Whitemark police station on Flinders Island, north-east of the island of Tasmania, when he was shot at about 9.45pm on Saturday night.
Police sent to investigate the shooting of the officer discovered the body of a man, believed to be in his mid-20s, with a gunshot wound on Walker Street, near the police station.
“At this time, police believe these two incidents are linked and that the situation has been safely contained,” Tasmanian police said in a statement on Sunday.
“Our thoughts are with the families and loved ones of those involved, and with the wider community of Flinders Island.”
The policeman is receiving treatment at the Royal Hobart hospital and remains in a serious but stable condition.
Whitemark has a population of about 300 according to the 2021 census, and the entire population of Flinders Island is fewer than 1,000 people.
Extra police resources, including forensic and uniformed officers, have been deployed to the island. Those with information about these incidents, including CCTV or dashcam footage of Walker Street, have been urged to contact police.
It is the second shooting of an on-duty police officer in Tasmania in 18 months. A Tasmanian officer was killed in the state’s north-west in June 2025 while executing a court-issued warrant to repossess a residence. The occupant of the home pleaded guilty to murder.
