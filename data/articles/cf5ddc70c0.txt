In 2018, Burger King posted a Chocolate Whopper as an April Fool’s Joke. Nearly a decade later, the decadent burger is actually coming to life.
The Chocolate Whopper is coming to Burger King Korea’s new flagship restaurant, the Flameground, which recently launched its first location in Seoul. Flameground somehow turns Burger King food into a luxurious dining experience, showcasing the fast-food chain’s most experimental creations.
Burger King Korea accounts have shared AI-generated renderings of the Chocolate Whopper’s concept: layers of sauces and brownie patties encased in a chocolate fudge bun. We'll have to wait until the burger launches on November 19 to see what it actually looks and tastes like.
Unfortunately, the Chocolate Whopper is getting an extremely limited rollout. The burger isn’t even coming to general Burger King Korea locations, and is instead entirely restricted to the single Flameground restaurant, which requires a reservation.
The generated rendering is jam-packed, to say the least. Each burger is handmade, and orders are restricted to one per person with daily quantity limits. The Chocolate Whopper will also only be available as a dine-in experience, no take-out allowed, so Burger King is really trying to stop you from sharing.
Ahead of the launch, Burger King also reshared the original April Fool's prank video. The original prank featured some candied tomatoes the real version understandably skips out on.
Burger King Korea launched its first Flameground restaurant this past September. The Seongsu location currently offers three signature burgers: the K-Cube Galbi, the Philly Cheese Steak, and the Memphis BBQ. These are priced between ₩12,900 and ₩13,900, equivalent to around $10 USD. The restaurant has yet to announce pricing for the Chocolate Whopper.
The entire Burger King franchise has taken to wacky marketing, though most of the chain’s big promotions stick to licensed collaborations. For example, Burger King Italy just launched a Magic: The Gathering meal with an exclusive deck box everyone has been trying to get their hands on. Surprisingly, the fast-food chain has yet to announce any new food items inspired by GTA 6, which features the parody restaurant Burger Shot.
