Watch: New York teen reunites with cat missing after building collapse
A teenage girl and her cat have been reunited four days after a building in the New York borough of Brooklyn collapsed.
Zariah Coleman, 13, made an emotional plea when her pet went missing after she and her family were forced to relocate when the side of their building was stripped off in the unexpected collapse.
City officials later found her cat, Isaac, in the debris of the their home and shared the reunion on Sunday afternoon.
Eight people died and 18 others were injured when the three-storey building collapsed in the Flatbush neighbourhood last Wednesday.
