The East of Eden method dressing isn’t over yet. Last night, the cast of Netflix’s limited drama series returned to the red carpet for a FYC (“For Your Consideration”) event in Los Angeles, California. And for the occasion, Florence Pugh embraced the story’s time period with a sculptural, Victorian look with over-the-top power shoulders.
Designed by Harris Reed, the ensemble echoed classic Victorian mourning dressing, a custom popularized by Queen Victoria after the 1861 death of her husband, Prince Albert. (Just one year before the first chapter of East of Eden takes place.) The modest style required all-black fabrics, high necklines, and long sleeves—all of which can be seen in Pugh’s tailored peplum jacket, with its V-notch stand collar and fabric-covered buttons. Of course, the actor’s version modernized the concept, with exaggerated puff sleeves that demanded attention.
Stylist Rebecca Corbin-Murray paired the look with platform heels and Bulgari jewels, once again breaking the traditional dress code, and hair stylist Adir Abergel gave Pugh’s blonde locks a messy updo. Makeup artist Alex Babsky finished things off with a moody deep-red lipstick, a custom shade he mixed together himself.
Pugh was joined by co-star Christopher Abbott and series creator Zoe Kazan, who adapted John Steinbeck’s 1952 novel for the screen. The Little Women actor not only starred in the 2026 interpretation, but also served as executive producer. She brought a nuanced performance to the role of Cathy, who has traditionally been seen as a soulless antagonist, but here was given a more complex, compelling backstory that humanized her.
