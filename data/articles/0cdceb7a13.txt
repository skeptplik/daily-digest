Reconstruction begins at Limoges City Hall after devastating fire
Issued on: Modified:
Share
It's been a month since the nationwide protests that degenerated in violence across France. The dramatic pictures of the historic City Hall in the French city of Limoges set on fire made headlines worldwide. To the relief of many, the reconstruction work on the building has now begun. Since the fire, donations have poured in and already about 80 percent of the necessary funding has been secured. FRANCE 24's Caitlin Brown reports.
