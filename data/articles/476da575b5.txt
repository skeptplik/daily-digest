On Saturday night, Kim Kardashian stepped out on the red carpet for the Los Angeles County Museum of Art’s 15th annual Art+Film Gala. The reality star opted for a darkly alluring look, dressed in a custom floor-length burgundy gown by Gucci.
Kardashian sparkled under Chris Burden’s famous “Urban Lights” sculpture on Saturday thanks to the head-to-toe sequins sewn into her dress. It was a glamorous look for Kardashian, whose gown beautifully combined modest and sensuous.
The turtleneck bodice gave way to two sharply-angled side cutouts and a form-fitted silhouette. Kardashian’s makeup combined a bold smokey eye with subtler, nude shades in her lipstick and blush. A small detail that added a touch of an understated elegance to the look was Kardashian’s nail polish, her manicure an exact match for the dress’s burgundy shade.
Kardashian is a long-time supporter of LACMA and has attended several LACMA galas in the past. In 2024, she sported a particularly notable accessory that nodded to art history. Kardashian wore the Attallah Cross, a pendant dating back to the dawn of the 20th century. The elaborate piece, made from white and gold silver with set amethysts and diamonds, was worn by the likes of Princess Diana at many charity auctions back in the 80s.
Kardashian paired this noticeable accessory with an equally commanding outfit, wearing a bright white Gucci dress with a plunging neckline. Other Kardashian LACMA Art + Film looks include a hot pink Balenciaga gown in 2023 and a black sculpted Balenciaga gown in 2022.
The Los Angeles County Museum of Art’s 15th annual Art+Film Gala brought Hollywood and the art world together on Saturday, honoring artist Vija Celmins and “Dune” director Denis Villeneuve. Leonardo DiCaprio and LACMA trustee Eva Chow returned as co-chairs, while Gucci continued its presenting sponsorship, a role it has held since the gala’s 2011 debut. Blackpink’s Rosé was tapped to perform. Proceeds support the museum’s film programming, exhibitions and educational initiatives.
