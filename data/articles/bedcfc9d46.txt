A nuclear war in Space? NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte has promised that the alliance will defend against any threats in space after reports that Russia is preparing to launch a nuclear-armed satellite.
Intelligence reports about a possible launch in the next 60 days had been shared among NATO allies.
Jamie Shea, former NATO spokesperson joined Euronews' new evening show Europe Tonight to discuss how Satellite systems could be affected and what it would mean for the word, as well as what we have already learnt from past nuclear detonations in space.
