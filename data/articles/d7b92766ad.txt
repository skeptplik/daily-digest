Haley Kalil attended Nickelodeon’s 2026 Kids’ Choice Awards this past Saturday night in Los Angeles. The social media personality pulled out all the stops when it came to referencing hallmarks of the classic kids’ television network. She appeared in a slime-inspired lime green bubble dress complete with slime green heels.
Kalil certainly sported one of the more eye-catching looks of the evening, truly going all in on the fun, childlike ambiance of the affair. She wore a ballgown-style dress made entirely of lime green bubbles. The look included a bubble handbag, a fitted bubble corset, a bubble hoop skirt embellished with tendrils of tiny bubbles, and an amorphous bubble cluster on the right shoulder reminiscent of a splash of Nickelodeon slime. Kalil took the slime references all the way to her footwear. To finish off the look, she wore slime green pointed toe heels embellished with petite, bead-like sequins. Even her toenails matched the theme, painted in that quintessential Nickelodeon orange.
Slime is of course a defining feature of the Nickelodeon brand, a network tradition that actually dates all the way back to 1979 when the gooey green liquid first debuted on the sketch show “You Can’t Do That On Television.” Since then, “getting slimed” has been a much-beloved Nickelodeon tradition. It is not uncommon for celebrities attending the Kids’ Choice Awards to incorporate major aspects of Nickelodeon media into their outfits. For example, in 2025, Ice Spice showed up wearing bright yellow platform heels covered in pink, purple, and turquoise flowers, a nod to Patrick Star’s swim trunks on the long-running show “Spongebob Squarepants.”

The Kids’ Choice Awards is a well-established Nickelodeon tradition where the network’s viewers vote for nominees in categories spanning movies, television, and pop culture in general. Recipients receive orange blimp statues emblazoned with the “Nickelodeon” lettering. This year, Kalil was nominated for Best Female Creator, but lost the award to teenage social media personality Salish Matter.
