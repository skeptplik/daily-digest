European leaders gather in Dublin under the shadow of hybrid threats
Issued on: Modified:
Share
Ireland is mounting the largest security operation in its history as around 50 European leaders gather in Dublin this week amid growing concerns over hybrid attacks. The operation follows a wave of hoax bomb threats targeting airlines and media organisations across Europe, with Irish police reporting new incidents on Monday and Tuesday. FRANCE 24's Olivia Bizot reports.
