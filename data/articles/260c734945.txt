Blizzard has officially unveiled Diablo 4's next live-service season: Season of Tyrael's Gambit. This is the ARPG's 16th season, and it follows on from the popular and well-received Season of Hell's Legacy.
In an unexpected move, Blizzard announced it's keeping some of the nostalgia-laiden content from the previous season, Season of Hell's Legacy, as a permanent addition. This is unusual, as the ephemeral nature of live seasons means new content goes away at the end of each season.
Season of Hell's Legacy paid tribute to Diablo's legacy and its 30th anniversary by introducing a lot of content inspired by previous Diablo games, chiefly Diablo 2. The list includes the return of iconic character Deckard Cain, a recreation of Old Tristram, and a big fight against the three Prime Evils - Diablo, Baal, and Mephisto - in their classic forms.
"We heard overwhelming requests for features from Season of Hell's Legacy to carry forward as permanent game features," Blizzard wrote in Season 16's announcement post. "While the entire season couldn't come along for the ride, we are implementing the following features with the launch of Season of Tyrael's Gambit that will carry forward in both Eternal and Seasonal realms."
So here's what's sticking around:
- The biggest is undoubtedly the recreation of Old Tristram and the reincarnation of Deckard Cain - both of which are staying. This includes Cain's books - which can be accessed from a book pedestal in town - as well as World Boss Diablo.
- The Prime Evil Council boss fight is now available in Uber Tristram as a normal Lair Boss. You can summon it, beat it and claim rewards to your heart's content. While you don't need materials to summon the boss, each player in the party needs Wirt's Lucky Coin to open the Council's Hoard chest, which spawns upon defeating it.
- Season 15's Capstone Dungeons moved to Torment 10+, with monsters within having Torment 13 difficulty. This is your best source for earning Prime Evil Splinters, required to earn Prime Evil powers.
- On the topic of dungeons, Waking Nightmares - inspired by encounters from past games - are staying, but can only be accessed using Chronicle of Nightmares Dungeon Keys. In other words, they won't appear in the overworld like normal dungeons. If you like to run The Pit, you'll be happy to learn it now has a chance to spawn monster families from previous Diablo games.
- In terms of items, Legacy Uniques - based on items from classic titles - are staying, as are the Annihilus Seal and Hellfire Torch Charm. Other fun Season 15 additions that won't be removed include Cow Island and the Moophisto boss summon.
Season of Tyrael's Gambit kicks off 1st December, so you still have time to access all of that in Diablo 4 until Season 15 ends. Certain things, such as craftable items, won't return, so you may as well try them while you can. That said, Blizzard is aware of the love some of those items have, and the developer said it may bring them back in the future.
