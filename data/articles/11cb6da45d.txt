And the Fashion Week circuit keeps rolling along … This week we’re headed to Lagos, Nigeria, where African designers are presenting their spring 2027 collections. One of the continent’s biggest labels, Orange Culture, is back on the runway after a quick detour in Berlin, while longtime local stalwarts Kilentar, Emmy Kasbit, and Lisa Folawiyo will also be presenting their latest creations. In Lagos, we’ve come to expect no shortage of vibrant color, riotous prints, and spirited accessories and already, this season is proving to be no different both on the catwalk and outside of the shows. Follow along as our intrepid photographer Stephen Tayo snaps the best-dressed guests at the spring 2027 shows.
