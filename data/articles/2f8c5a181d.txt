Weddings during the holiday season come with a built-in sense of joy. The venue is likely already decked out in garlands and candlelight, everyone is in a celebratory mood, and if the date falls on New Year’s Eve, there’s a midnight countdown baked right into the reception. Your outfit should match that energy. While standard winter guest attire often leans moody, a December through February fête (don’t forget about Valentine’s Day nuptials!) leaves plenty of room to play—think rich textures, high-shine finishes, and statement silhouettes.
Vogue’s Guide to Holiday Wedding Guest Dresses:
As a bridal stylist, I always recommend letting the calendar dictate your palette. A Christmastime wedding begs for jewel tones and seasonal classics like cranberry red, deep emerald, and antique gold—all of which you’ll find at labels like Christopher John Rogers, Posse, and Simkhai. A New Year’s Eve ceremony, on the other hand, calls for anything that catches the light and has movement for the dance floor, from Altuzarra’s gold gown done up in cascading paillettes to Kallmeyer’s swingy fringe. Even a strict black-tie dress code can get a festive upgrade: Trade a standard black gown for a golden lamé maxi courtesy of Ulla Johnson. (Just be sure to check the couple’s wedding website for any specific color requests.)
Of course, a few standard wedding-guest rules still hold true. Steer clear of anything that reads as bridal—that includes winter white, no matter how tempting a cream silk slip might be. Skip the costume-like details, too: A ruby-red J.Crew mini feels spirited, while a tinsel-trimmed ensemble in the same shade can quickly veer kitschy. Beyond that, pay attention to the dress code, as that will dictate your hemline; minis and midis are fair game for less formal invitations, especially when paired with tights and a great heel. And don’t forget about a chic evening coat that’ll tie your outfit together.
