There were mixed fortunes for the French clubs in Thursday's Europa League action, as Marseille saw off Levski Sofia (1-0) in the only win of the night. Lyon were held at home by Lillestrom of Norway, while Rennes fell to Dutch side Nijmegen.
In tennis, Jannik Sinner's withdrawal from the Australian Open in January was confirmed on Thursday, with the Italian set for a further period on the sidelines after undergoing an operation on a persistent knee injury.
The new biathlon season kicked off in Kontiolahti, Finland, where Campbell Wright became the first American to win a World Cup event on the curtain-raising 20km race.
