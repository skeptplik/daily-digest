Tokyo police have arrested four Vietnamese men for allegedly taking part in special fraud schemes believed to be part of the activities of a fraud ring that may have caused about ¥1 billion ($6.3 million) in damages.
The Metropolitan Police Department has not disclosed whether the suspects — including Tran Van Truong, a 28-year-old company executive — admitted to the allegations.
The four individuals, members of a group headed by a Vietnam-based ringleader, allegedly withdrew or laundered money obtained through special fraud schemes, according to the police.
The MPD has obtained arrest warrants for the ringleader and another person as part of the investigation into the group.
Among the four individuals arrested, Truong and one other allegedly impersonated the head of a Tokyo company and sent a fake message instructing an employee to wire money into a designated bank account, swindling ¥10 million.
The other two suspects allegedly posed as police officers and others in phone calls and withdrew some of the funds transferred under the pretext of investments.
