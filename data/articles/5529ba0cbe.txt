Footage appearing to show a large feline in woodland on the outskirts of Istanbul prompted a security operation, with teams searching from the air and on the ground.
Gendarmerie officers, the disaster management agency AFAD and veterinarians took part, using thermal-camera drones, bait and camera traps.
The grainy images raised concerns and Belgrad Forest was closed during the search. After reviewing new footage from different angles, the Istanbul governor's office said the animal was neither a lion nor any other big cat.
Authorities have not said what it actually was.
