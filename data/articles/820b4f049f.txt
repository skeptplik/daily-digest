The Corsair Xeneon Edge is a 14.5-inch display designed to serve as an extension for your desktop and give you a small screen to host your most important apps. Its versatility means you can mount it practically anywhere — horizontally under your main monitor, vertically beside your primary display, or as a digital status panel inside your PC case.
The Xeneon Edge sports a small 14.5-inch screen with a 2560 x 730 resolution and a 60 Hz refresh rate. It features a five-point touch screen and can be set up via Corsair's iCue software to do just about anything you want: monitoring your system, adding Stream Deck-style functionality via buttons and sliders, displaying a slideshow, tracking your social media, etc. The screen connects via USB-C (DisplayPort Alt Mode) or HDMI 2.0 + USB-C for power if you don't have a USB-C video out port.
You can set up the Xeneon Edge to do whatever you like, but if you need some inspiration, you can also grab some pre-made widgets from the iCUE section of the Elgato Marketplace. Some of these widgets are paid, but there are plenty of free options for various types of monitoring, tracking, and aesthetics.
