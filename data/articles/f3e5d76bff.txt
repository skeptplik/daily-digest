Mitch Marsh refuses to answer key Sandpapergate questions in testy press conference
In short:
Mitch Marsh has refused to answer key questions about the 2018 Sandpapergate scandal in a fiery press conference exchange with journalists.
Marsh said: "I respect that certain people have told their stories, but I'm not here to share mine."
What's next?
Australia's Test captain, Pat Cummins, is set to front a press conference in Sydney on Friday morning and has promised to answer "any and all media".
