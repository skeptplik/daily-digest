Firaxis Games, the studio behind the Civilization and XCOM franchises of computer strategy games, last week laid off about a third of its staff, totaling around 60 people. That’s according to a recent report in Bloomberg.
The report cites people “familiar with the cuts,” saying that the flagship game the studio is currently working on, a new entry in the XCOM series, “has faced development struggles and missed a significant milestone.” The departures also include the studio head, Heather Hazen.
A Firaxis spokesperson released the following statement:
Firaxis is restructuring and recentering around the studio’s core strength—designing world class strategy games that have set the industry standard for the genre for the last 30 years. As part of these changes, the size of the studio is being reduced to focus on design, technology and the player experience, which means saying goodbye to talented colleagues who helped build Firaxis. These decisions are never easy, but they’re essential to build the next chapter of Firaxis.
The storied studio has had a rough run of things of late. Its most recent game, Civilization VII, launched to solid enough sales but divided players in terms of reception. Players have long criticized new Civilization titles for being barebones compared to immediate predecessors that have seen multiple post-launch updates and expansions, but the response was sharper this time.
