For the first time in its history, the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade did not take place on actual Thanksgiving.
Macy’s postponed Thursday’s Thanksgiving Day Parade due to weather, with the retailer announcing that the event will instead be held this weekend, on Saturday, Nov. 28.
Macy’s is celebrating the 100th anniversary of its Thanksgiving Day Parade this year, and the retailer had said that it was “going all out” for the star-studded holiday spectacle, though Mother Nature apparently had other plans. A chance of rain and high winds ended up derailing the original date for the annual parade, as the large inflatable balloons are grounded when wind gusts reach 35 mph or higher.
This year’s Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade is now scheduled to take place live on Saturday, Nov. 28 at 8:30 a.m. ET, with the balloons and floats marching down a 2.5-mile route through Manhattan beginning at West 77th Street and Central Park West and ending at Macy’s Herald Square on West 34th Street.
