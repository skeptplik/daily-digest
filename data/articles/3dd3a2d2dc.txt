Macy’s is celebrating the 100th anniversary of its Thanksgiving Day Parade this year, and the retailer says it’s “going all out” for the star-studded holiday spectacle — that is, if the weather holds up.
This year’s Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade is scheduled to take place live on Thursday, Nov. 26 at 8:30 a.m. ET, with the balloons and floats marching down a 2.5-mile route through Manhattan beginning at West 77th Street and Central Park West and ending at Macy’s Herald Square on West 34th Street.
However, a chance of rain and high winds could threaten the annual parade, as the large inflatable balloons are grounded when wind gusts reach 35 mph or higher.
How to Watch the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade 2026 on TV
For now, the Thanksgiving Day Parade is still a go (as of this writing), and if you aren’t among the thousands of spectators expected along the (rainy) route, you can watch the festivities on TV through NBC or Telemundo at 8:30 a.m. Thursday.
The night before, NBC will broadcast a new special titled Countdown to Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade, airing Wednesday, Nov. 25, at 8:00 p.m. ET.
