Nexperia’s China unit claims supply chain ‘localisation’ as rift with Dutch parent deepens
The shift has slashed delivery times for some components to four to six weeks, down from 12 to 16 weeks previously, CEO John Chang says
Speaking at the 23rd China International Semiconductor Expo in Beijing on Thursday, Nexperia China CEO John Chang said the local division had partnered with domestic foundries while relying on its own facilities and local contractors to handle chip packaging and testing.
“We have completed the localisation of the entire chain, from product definition and development to manufacturing,” Chang said.
