Happy weekend! Several movies are hitting theaters this week, from the action-comedy How to Rob a Bank, starring Nicholas Hoult, Zoë Kravitz, and Christian Slater, to Ti West’s take on A Christmas Carol, titled Ebenezer and starring Johnny Depp.
If that’s not enough to get you into theaters, we understand. Thankfully, Polygon has a full slate of new movie recommendations, available on streaming and VOD. The End of Oak Street is available on HBO Max, taking the crown for the best dinosaur movie since Steven Spielberg's Jurassic Park. It also has one of the scariest T-Rexes ever captured on film. Heart of the Beast is now available on VOD, following Brad Pitt and an adorable German Shepherd as they try their best to survive the harsh Alaskan wilderness.
If you’re looking for Netflix dramas, there’s Heartland, a musical-crime-western mashup starring Jessica Chastain and Garrett Hedlund. There’s also Chris Rock’s latest feature, Misty Green, following an out-of-work actress in Los Angeles desperate to make a career comeback. If you're into documentaries, there’s Avedon, exploring the life of career of the life of the beloved photographer whose work left a big imprint on fashion photography.
Lastly, Being Heumann, based on the autobiography of Judy Heumann’s life as an activist, is now streaming on Apple TV. There’s also a new 4K reissue of one of Guillermo del Toro’s most beloved movies, Pan’s Labyrinth.
1 The End of Oak Street
- Available on HBO Max
- Genre: Sci-fi
- Run time: 1h 39m
- Director: David Robert Mitchell
- Cast: Anne Hathaway, Ewan McGregor, Maisy Stella
David Robert Mitchell’s The End of Oak Street is an incredible riff on Spielberg’s Jurassic Park, following a family in the '80s going through some personal challenges. As the parents contemplate a divorce, their neighborhood gets hurled back in time to the dinosaur era, with the outside of their home crawling with all sorts of terrifying pre-historic reptiles.
Per our Polygon review:
                        The dinosaurs themselves are magnificent, rendered with the feathers and fuzzy hair that we now know dinosaurs sported without losing the fearsome nature imbued by Spielberg 30 years ago. This is a movie where dinosaurs repeatedly slaughter and eat random humans, and each kill got an applause from my theater's rowdy audience every time. Mitchell and his team also take some creative swings with the creature designs that largely pay off, including a family of large rocky turtles and a terrifying silent serpent as long as a train with rows of impossibly sharp teeth.
2 Heartland
- Available on Netflix
- Genre: Crime
- Run time: 1h 52m
- Director: Adam Carter Rehmeir
- Cast: Jessica Chastain, Garrett Hedlund, John Hawkes
Heartland is one of the strangest movies in Jessica Chastain’s career. The Oscar-winner plays Misty Jones, a former country megastar who returns home to Nashville to investigate the sudden disappearance of her niece, Charlie Hart, who’s following in her footsteps as a country musician.
3 Being Heumann
- Available on Apple TV
- Genre: Drama
- Run time: 1h 47m
- Director: Siân Heder
- Cast: Ruth Madeley, Mark Ruffalo, Dylan O'Brien
Being Heumann is based on Judy Heumann's memoir, "Being Heumann: An Unrepentant Memoir of a Disability Rights Activist," dramatizing the 28-day sit-in protest at the San Francisco Federal Building by disability activists. The demonstration led to the first U.S. federal civil rights protections for people with disabilities.
4 Avedon
- Available on Netflix
- Genre: Action
- Run time: 1h 47m
- Director: Ron Howard
- Cast: N/A
Ron Howard’s most recent film is a documentary centered on Richard Avedon, one of the most important photographers in fashion history. The film has great access to Avedon’s personal files, showing never-before-seen images and interviews with family members and figures like Twiggy, Isabella Rossellini, and more.
5 Misty Green
- Available on Netflix
- Genre: Drama
- Run time: 1h 43m
- Director: Chris Rock
- Cast: Rosalind Eleazar, Adam Driver, Daniel Kaluuya
Misty Green follows a once-successful actress and performer whose career has been derailed due to addiction, self-sabotage, and an evolving, challenging industry. After facing challenges from all sides, Misty goes on a desperate job hunt, pursuing a starring role in a remake of the musical Dreamgirls.
6 Heart of the Beast
- Available on VOD
- Genre: Survival action
- Run time: 1h 41m
- Director: David Ayer
- Cast: Brad Pitt, JK Simmons, Anna Lambe
Heart of the Beast follows Brad Pitt as a traumatized soldier who gets stranded in the Alaskan wilderness alongside his combat-trained German Shepard. The two band together to survive and return to civilization.
7 The Incomer
- Available on Netflix
- Genre: Dark comedy
- Run time: 1h 42m
- Director: Louis Paxton
- Cast: Domhnall Gleeson, Gayle Rankin, Grant O'Rourke
Two isolated middle-aged siblings' lives (Gayle Rankin and Grant O’Rourke) are disrupted when they receive an eviction notice from the government. Raised to view all outsiders as threats, the siblings are shocked by the arrival of a mild-mannered land recovery coordinator (Domhnall Gleeson), who doesn’t know how to communicate with them.
8 Pan’s Labyrinth 20th anniversary
- Available on VOD
- Genre: Fantasy
- Run time: 1h 58m
- Director: Guillermo del Toro
- Cast: Ivana Baquero, Maribel Verdú, Doug Jones
Pan’s Labyrinth is a special film in Guillermo del Toro’s filmography, and his most direct exploration of fairy tales. The series is set in the aftermath of the Spanish Civil War and follows Ofelia (Ivana Baquero), a 12-year-old girl who’s just moved to a military outpost managed by her terrifying stepfather (Sergi Lopez). She uncovers a rich fantasy world after stumbling upon a labyrinth and a faun, who informs her that she’s the reincarnation of a Princess and must complete three tasks to return to her kingdom.
9 Other Mommy
- Available on VOD
- Genre: Horror
- Run time: 1h 47m
- Director: Rob Savage
- Cast: Jessica Chastain, Jay Duplass, Arabella Olivia Clark
After terrifying everyone with its amazing trailer, Other Mommy is finally available on streaming. The film follows Bela (Arabella Olivia Clark), the daughter of parents who are going through a marital crisis. She starts to form a relationship with a malignant shapeshifting entity that calls itself Other Mommy.
10 V/H/S Mixtape
- Available on Shudder and VOD
- Genre: Horror
- Run time: 1h 38m
- Director: Ernest Dickerson, David Moreau, RZA
- Cast: Neela Jolene, Barbara Crampton, Mike Ferguson
V/H/S Mixtape is the latest entry in the VHS franchise, a found-footage horror anthology that has featured directors like Ti West, Adam Wingard, Scott Derrickson, and more. This year’s version is centered on the music industry and framed in the style of an MTV news show.
