Tivoli—the wondrous amusement park in the heart of Copenhagen that inspired a Hans Christian Andersen fairytale (The Nightingale), and allegedly so charmed Michael Jackson that he wanted to buy the place—was the genesis for Rikke Baumgarten and Helle Hestehave’s pre-fall collection. As such, one would expect clothes infused with a sense of joy and whimsy rather than ones that were mostly plain and practical. The waist-emphasis applied to jackets and coats was on the upcurve, but floral prints intended to conjure Tivoli’s beautiful gardens felt insipid. In contrast, a jacquard skirt suit with dots meant to reference the “magical lights that you see after dark” at the theme park, as Hestehave put it, had some star potential.
