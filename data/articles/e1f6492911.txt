When the fragrance world picks a theme, it goes in hard. In the space of one month, I had close to a dozen new “lactonic” (that is, milky or creamy themed) perfumes to try. Instinctively, I wasn’t keen – I haven’t touched a glass of milk since nursery school – and so I eased myself in with DedCool’s Mocha Magic (£24 for 15ml), which adds a shot of coffee to the brand’s Xtra Milk fragrance that I love. This is immediately creamy, rich and bittersweet, like tiramisu, right down to that same dry hit of cocoa powder at the back of the throat. As in life, the coffee wears off, but the vanilla, spice and woody notes hang around for an impressively long spell. As with almost everything right now, it’s sweet, but not overbearingly so.
Phlur, so beloved by gen Z, has leapt onboard the trend with Heavy Cream (from £26), available in fragrance and scented body spray. This is creamy, obviously, but also very sweet and immediately brings to mind Swizzels Drumstick lollies and the retro fizzy drink American cream soda. If this sounds like a kids’ party from hell, blame me, because it’s actually perfectly pleasant, got me heaps of compliments and got better and mellower as the hours passed. If you’re a milk with three sugars sort of a person, I think you may love it.
The Nue Co’s lactonic scent is called First Milk (£38 for 10ml), and is decidedly non-dairy in scent. This is almondine, a little peppery and unlike the others, has a translucence and a delicateness to the vanilla. It’s intimate, wholesome and comforting – a bit fluffy towel around a clean baby – but a little synthetic smelling. I get a nostalgic whiff of Silly Putty, and I say that with love.
I didn’t get off to a great start with supermodel Bella Hadid’s new Orebella Cashmere Milk ($35 for 10ml) because the bottle lid is so fiddly as to be prohibitive to use. So I was stunned to find myself liking the juice. This is gentle, clean, musky, a little nutty, like cold skimmed milk on gourmet granola. And unusually, it’s alcohol-free for sensitive skins.
Jodhpur 6am (£56 for 10ml) comes from L’Entropiste, a luxury Parisian perfumery doing interesting things. The milk here is spiced with cardamom, ginger and black tea, to give a creamy, sophisticated chai scent at first, before developing into something much fruitier. The longevity is excellent – expect to identify juicy peaches and exotic white flowers for around eight hours after first spritz.
