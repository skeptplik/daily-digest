Grand Theft Auto 6 has only been out for a matter of hours, and players have already tracked down a UFO in the woods and a Bigfoot in the swamps.
The UFO encounter is a random night-time event in Kelly County, on a dirt road near the Starlet Motel safehouse east of Port Gellhorn. You'll need to have finished the game's first chapter, and it's most reliable after 2am in-game.
As word spread late yesterday (18th November), it seems the event will kick off if you find a car at the side of the road with its headlights flickering, its trunk open, and clothes strewn around it. Follow the trail into the trees towards a blue glow, and you'll catch a UFO beaming down on a woman before it drops her and flies off. Head back to her car, as she requests, and the UFO returns for you. You apparently black out and come round three days later, and a bronze trophy called The Truth Is Out There should unlock.
You can seemingly trigger the event as either Jason or Lucia, and it isn't needed for 100 percent completion.
And that's not the only close encounter you can expect, either. YouTuber Lazarbeam bumped into Bigfoot on the afternoon of 18th November, hours before the game launched in the UK, while one Reddit user reported that they weren't quite sure what they'd stumbled on, titling their post "I think I found Bigfoot".
Leonida's version of Bigfoot is actually the Skunk Ape, Florida's own swamp cryptid, and it can be found in the swamplands near Cottonmouth Lake in Mariana County as soon as free roam opens up. Listen out for a loud roar, then use Jason's tracking ability to follow it through the swamp. You may find it feeding on a deer carcass, while a second sighting has been reported south of Watson Bay.
Either way, the trail seemingly ends at a strange, human-like tree. Investigate it, and Jason blacks out, triggering a short cutscene in which the creature creeps up on him, as shown in this video. He later wakes near his vehicle, drenched in sweat. Save your ammo, though, as shooting at it won't do you any good.
Cryptids and aliens are, of course, something of a Rockstar tradition by this point. GTA 5's Mount Chiliad mystery kept players hunting for years, and Output Lag has already spotted two NPCs in a GTA 6 bar talking about goings-on there. Nobody has cracked a full Chiliad-style puzzle in the new game yet, but I'm sure that's just a matter of when, not if.
GTA 6 launched today on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S, although some have been getting in on the action earlier as a whopping 2m Xbox players in the UK and US took advantage of the trick of changing their console's country. Even New Zealand's tourist board tipped a wink to the sudden influx of virtual 'visitors', telling them "We'd love to have you". It also cheekily offered a video of cheerful tourists admiring the country's resplendent natural beauty. "Life," read the accompanying caption, "because we're getting GTA 6 first."
