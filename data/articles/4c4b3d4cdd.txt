Rockstar has revealed a new batch of GTA 6 screenshots – and even a Weather Channel forecast – showcasing Leonida's dynamic weather.
The 13-year wait for a proper return to Vice City is almost over, but with just days to go until launch, no one had any idea what to expect from the week. Would more details leak? Would Rockstar reveal more gameplay details? Would we get more gameplay footage? Either way, it's probably safe to say no one was expecting to check the forecast to find out the latest on GTA 6.
The Weather Channel struck when it published a series of screenshots showing Vice City covered in everything from torrential downpours to ocean fog. We even get a true-to-life breakdown of what we're seeing here, giving would-be Leonida citizens a chance to prep before the storm hits.

"Grand Theft Auto VI takes place in Leonida – a fictional world inspired by modern-day Florida, a landscape with one of the most unpredictable weather systems in the US," Rockstar VP of art: light and rendering Owen Shepherd said. "To match the fidelity of all aspects of what is the deepest, most immersive take on the Grand Theft Auto series yet, we had to completely reimagine our in-game weather systems to feel more localized, persistent, and above all dynamic. Weather can range with everything from clear blue skies to towering vertiginous cumulonimbus clouds, grounding players in a world through weather we hope Florida locals – and new visitors to Leonida – can appreciate."
Rockstar confirmed it had developed an "advanced" weather system when speaking to Game Informer in September. Another update that arrived shortly afterward revealed that Vice City could, in fact, be hit by simulated tropical storms and hurricanes. At the time, these were said to bring "heavy clouds, strong winds, dense atmospherics, and a lot of rain." That means more than simple visual changes as Leonida is overrun by Mother Nature.
These early details promised something a little more intense than the occasional shower, and now, we have our first look at what that might actually look like. While there's a lot of variety here, there's no reason to believe these six screenshots represent everything the skies of Leonida have to offer.
