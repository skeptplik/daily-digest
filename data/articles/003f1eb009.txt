Diablo 4 Season 16: Season of Tyrael's Gambit officially kicks off on December 1. We finally know a bit more about what the new season holds in store, and it's going to make one of the biggest activities much less of a pain.
Season 15, Hell's Legacy, is a tough act to follow. It brought in a ton of nostalgic content like the Prime Evils, runewords, and even Whimsyshire. It does seem like a lot of that anniversary content is going to be retired (I'll miss runewords), but at least it seems like Season of Tyrael's Gambit is going to make farming Helltides a bit less annoying.
In the patch notes for the new season, Blizzard says one Helltide will always be up in the vanilla zones, with another active in either Nahantu or Skovos, offset by 30 minutes. In practice, that means there will always be an active Helltide. That would be good enough on its own, but Blizzard is also changing the way Abberant Cinders work. Instead of expiring at the end of each Helltide, Blizzard is introducing a cap, and will let you carry cinders between Helltides.
For the uninitiated, that might not seem like a big deal, but it's a massive quality-of-life change. If you're not aware, Helltide is essentially an in-game invasion event, where demons overrun an area in the open world. You go in and kill demons for 55 minutes to get Abberant Cinders, which you can then use to unlock chests scattered throughout the zone. Helltides have always been one of the best ways to farm XP, and there have been several seasons that revolve entirely around them.
Now that War Plans, which were introduced with Lord of Hatred, are such a central part of how Diablo 4's gameplay loop works, there's always a chance that you're going to need to do bit of Helltide farming to progress. The problem was that Helltides spawned on a timer, and there was always a 5-minute gap between them. That doesn't sound too bad, but sometimes a Helltide would pop up in your to-do list and you'd either zone in with a mere 10 minutes to gather a ton of Cinders, or there simply wouldn't be a Helltide active. This new change solves both of those problems.
Now, even if you go into a Helltide with just a few minutes remaining, you can still gather Cinders without having to worry about opening 250 Cinders worth of chests before the timer expires. You can simply farm until the Helltide moves and then teleport to the next one. Easy. It's such a seemingly small change, but it's made me more excited for the 16th season of this ARPG than anything I've heard about it so far.
Beyond that, the Season of Tyrael's Gambit also brings changes to War Plans, where you'll be able to customize them to favor the activities you actually like doing. So, if you hate Helltide, even with the new changes, it sounds like you'll be able to make them appear a little less often in your War Plans.
The new season will also have a new item type called Horadric Items, which will synergize with set talismans. In the example Blizzard shared in its post, this could be as simple as adding +1 set count to your talismans to an ability that is only activated if you have a certain set of talismans equipped.
Seasons like this are a prime example of the way Blizzard will support Diablo 4 going forward, now that Diablo 5 is in the works. Diablo 4's director has come out and reassured fans that the game will continue to get new seasons as long as people are playing, and with how different Diablo 5 sounds so far, it's possible that Diablo 4 will have a long life ahead of it.
