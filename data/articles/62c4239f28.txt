Earlier this month, Anna Wintour and Daniel Lee, the creative director of Burberry, gathered at the Vogue Business Summit to discuss this incredible year in fashion. After an introduction from Nicole Phelps, the two began a conversation about the industry at large—including the endless reshuffling of creative directors.
“It takes time to understand and have a dialogue with the company that you work for…to understand the archive, the culture, the people. It also takes time to build a team, and I think people need stability,” Lee said.
