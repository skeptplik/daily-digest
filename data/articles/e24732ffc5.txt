The story so far: After 26 years of holding on to its measles elimination status, the United States lost this distinction. On November 17, 2026, the Pan American Health Organisation (PAHO) revoked the United States’ measles elimination status. This was after over 5400 cases of measles were reported in the U.S. in 2026.
This is the highest annual total since 1989-91, according to the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention(CDC). As per data put out by the Unicef, 96% of cases are in unvaccinated people or those with unknown vaccination status. Children under five account for 18% of cases and those aged 5-19 for 40%.
