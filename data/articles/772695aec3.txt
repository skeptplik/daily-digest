Why Democratic Congress may enhance US-Philippines alliance, pivot to Asia
An end to the Iran war could see a greater shift of US military assets to the Indo-Pacific region, including the Philippines, analysts say
Analysts expect bipartisan support for Manila to endure, with Democratic oversight set to rein in Trump’s unpredictability. But they warn that Washington could still turn inward to deal with domestic challenges, diverting attention from the Indo-Pacific.
The rebuke of Trump at the midterms follows mounting anxiety in Asia over the US’ continuous focus on the Middle East conflict.
