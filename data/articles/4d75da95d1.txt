While Kith has always catered to its women customers, following its collaboration with Blackpink’s Lisa the brand is now evolving to present distinct men’s and women’s collections. But though the two are now divided into separate categories, there’s plenty of dialogue between them. One of the strongest throughlines was the use of faux fur, which trimmed knits, bomber jackets, and vests.
These silhouettes set the tone for the comfy chic vibe of the season. Many of the tonal pieces were multifunctional and layerable, including a hoodie featuring detachable scarves and a brown knit top with a built-in tied wrap around the waist. The more casual second skin hoodie and matching palazzo-like pants also looked cozy.
A pumpkin orange mock-necked zip-up lined with fleece would bring vibrancy to a holiday outing. For a dash of edge, there was a black ponyhair biker jacket. School-girl pleated skirts and matching oversized knits gave a touch of vampy academia to the proceedings. These combos balanced t “micro” tailoring in the form of short shorts and an ultra-mini suit skirt. The office siren lives on, though there was also a more sober gray suited set, again paired with its own matching scarf.
