Malaysia’s debut Hyrox event in jeopardy over beer sponsorship
Some Muslim entrants said they would forfeit entry fees, as officials demanded Hyrox ditch its partnership with Carlsberg
The tie-up angered some participants who said it conflicted with both their faith and the event’s promotion of fitness, highlighting tensions over alcohol marketing in Muslim-majority Malaysia.
On Wednesday, Youth and Sports Minister Mohammed Taufiq Johari confirmed that the four-day event, scheduled for December 10 to 13 at the Malaysia International Trade and Exhibition Centre in Kuala Lumpur, had yet to be approved after a promotional announcement naming Carlsberg as the event’s “official beer partner” circulated online.
