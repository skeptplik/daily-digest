Fingerprint trains specialized models to detect distinct fraud types
Fingerprint has launched Fraud Score, a predictive fraud signal trained directly on customers’ own fraud outcomes and powered by Fingerprint’s device intelligence and machine learning models. Fraud Score learns the fraud types at each organization to help them identify fraud more accurately without having to build and maintain their own models.
Basic risk scoring can’t match the many types of fraud
“Businesses today face a wider range of fraud threats, amplified by AI-powered techniques,” said Suzanne Sando, Lead Analyst at Javelin Strategy & Research. “Those threats vary by organization and attack type, but one thing is clear: static, one-size-fits-all risk scoring is no longer enough.”
As online fraud becomes more sophisticated, security and risk teams struggle with translating more signals than ever before into accurate, actionable decisions. Despite these complexities, most tools treat fraud as a monolithic problem. However, account takeover (ATO), payment fraud, promotion abuse and fake account creation each exhibit distinct behavior, requiring distinct detection strategies. Generic risk scores fail to capture these nuances, forcing engineering and fraud teams to spend countless hours on manual analysis, complex rule-building and ongoing model maintenance.
“Fraud teams have a signal-to-decision problem,” said Catherine Woneis, VP of Product at Fingerprint. “We built Fraud Score to give every organization the power of custom machine learning without the complexity of building and maintaining custom models. Fraud Score trains specialized models on each customer’s actual fraud outcomes and our device intelligence, enabling teams to stop specific threats with far greater precision.”
Transforming raw device signals into informed business decisions
Fraud Score eliminates complexity and uncertainty for risk teams by delivering turnkey, customer-tailored modeling. In a head-to-head evaluation, Fraud Score caught nearly 50% more fraud than one customer’s existing in-house model, with no increase in good users blocked.
Fraud Score pairs Fingerprint’s broad set of device intelligence signals with fraud outcomes provided by each organization to build and maintain specialized models tailored to each organization’s unique traffic. Fraud teams can act on Fraud Score’s specialized predictions directly or integrate them into their existing decisioning systems via Fingerprint’s API responses. As customers provide new outcomes, Fingerprint continuously retrains the Fraud Score models.
Unlike alternatives that rely on generalized network rules or require internal data science teams to maintain their own prediction layers, Fraud Score is:
- Specialized by fraud type: Optimizes separate models and predictions for distinct threat vectors, including payment fraud, ATO, multi-accounting, fake account creation, promotion abuse, platform abuse, content abuse and more
- Trained on customer data: Learns directly from an organization’s fraud outcomes, ensuring predictions reflect their specific users and traffic patterns
- Powered by device intelligence: Ingests Fingerprint’s device intelligence signals, uncovering correlations and anomalies that manual rules miss
“Simply blocking the visitor ID wasn’t enough to stop our savvier fraudsters,” said David Fennessey, Principal Software Engineer at Flink. “With Fraud Score, we can partner with Fingerprint to collaborate on a model based on our own internal fraud analysis and stop future abuse before the order is placed.”
