Rockstar Games president and co-founder Sam Houser has issued a rare statement as GTA 6 launches, celebrating the game's debut and its glowing critical reception.
"Rockstar Games was founded on the belief that games could achieve the same distinction as all other forms of storytelling, become essential to modern culture, and to be thought of as stories and worlds that live on with all of us," said Houser in a statement shared with IGN.
"This week, Grand Theft Auto returns to Leonida and modern-day Vice City. While things have changed and time has passed, one aspect holds true: We have tried to create an experience that will exceed your expectations while showing what this medium is capable of, and why we believe games are so special.
"Whether you can trace your journey to this game through the entire series or are experiencing Grand Theft Auto for the first time, this is a narrative set in a world full of possibilities – and it is up to you as the player to shape how it ultimately feels, and concludes.
"We hope it felt both familiar and entirely new, and above all, we hope that you had fun. We are incredibly grateful to you and everything that you do to celebrate games. We look forward to many more years of sharing these experiences with you.
"Thank you, Sam Houser."
The game is currently available to players in New Zealand and Australia, and will unlock at midnight on the 19th in other territories. The review embargo lifted this morning, with the game receiving near-universal critical acclaim and setting record viewership figures on YouTube and Twitch.
