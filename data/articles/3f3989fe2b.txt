Winter is coming, and brands like Paris Texas, Alo and Margaux aren’t letting the cold weather tamp down their style. They’re simply reinventing their fall and winter footwear lineups with the most winterized footwear accent one can think of: furry detailing. From wool-blend to shearling, warmth is top of mind.
FN rounds up some of these brands’ furry footwear options below, which include glamorous boots and heels, easy-on sneaker mules, cozy loafers and more silhouettes.
Paris Texas is welcoming the winter 2026 season with its furry collection featuring an array of reinvented iconic silhouettes. The luxury brand, which opened its first stand-alone store this year, blends texture, volume and femininity in the lineup. There are knee-high boots, ankle boots, mules and heels decked out with plush detailing, fur trims and juxtaposed materials. The styles are elevated by Paris Texas’ recognizable signature stiletto heel shape.
Alo expanded its footwear lineup this year with the launch of its first boot in September, the Malibu Boot, a wool-blend-lined silhouette which launched alongside a shorter version and a slip-on loafer in the same materials. Two months later, Alo launched more boots including the Malibu Tall Boot and Aspen Lace-Up Boot, which came out alongside the Sherpa Sunset Sneaker Mule.

“We’ve always designed beyond the studio — studio-to-street is really the cornerstone of our design philosophy, and footwear is an important part of completing that wardrobe,” Abby Gordon, chief merchandising and design officer at Alo, told FN, around the launch of the brand’s first boot. “The Malibu Collection brings the comfort and functionality people expect from Alo into a completely new footwear expression, but it’s also incredibly easy to style.”
Meanwhile, Margaux is prioritizing texture this fall season. The brand aimed to strike a balance between cozy and polished with shearling silhouettes spanning flats, suede loafers, mules and winter boots. This includes the James Loafer, designed with a slipper-like fit, and the Wells Shearling Cuff Boot, made with flexible leather that allows the wearer to style the shaft up or cuffed at any height.
