Police uncovered hundreds of intimate images of Joanne Young after investigating her husband in 2024 for uploading images depicting bestiality, exposing a wider pattern of sexual abuse.
Four British men were found guilty on Thursday of multiple sexual offences, including rape, against a woman whose then-husband drugged her and advertised her for sex online.
Her then-husband, Philip Young, also filmed her without her knowledge at their home in Swindon, in a case bearing striking similarities to that of Gisèle Pelicot in France.
Joanne Young, who waived her right to anonymity, expressed her "relief" at the verdict and urged other women who have experienced similar abuse to come forward, saying they "can be believed and understood."
“Knowing what has happened to me without my knowledge or consent has been incredibly difficult to process, and this abuse has cost me my life as I knew it,” she said outside Winchester Crown Court in southern England.
“The person I trusted most in the world betrayed me in the most horrific way."
The 49-year-old mother of three spoke after a jury found the four men, aged between 32 and 62, guilty of sexually abusing her at her home in Swindon after being recruited online.
The verdicts and guilty pleas
Philip Young, had previously pleaded guilty to dozens of offences, including 11 counts of rape and 11 counts of administering a substance with intent.
Norman Macksoni, 48, was convicted of rape, while Richard Wilkins, 62, was found guilty of rape and assault by penetration.
Connor Sanderson-Doyle, 32, was convicted of assault by penetration and sexual touching, while Mohammed Hassan was found guilty of sexual touching.
A sixth defendant, Dean Hamilton, 47, admitted one count of assault by penetration, two counts of sexual assault and one count of rape.
Justice Pushpinder Saini said the four men faced “significant” prison sentences and praised Joanne Young for her "amazing bravery throughout these proceedings.”
