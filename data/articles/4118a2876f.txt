A hawkish member of the Bank of Japan’s policy board called for early action to keep raising the BOJ’s benchmark rate, supporting market expectations for a rate hike next month.
“I believe that the bank needs to bring the policy rate to the neutral level soon,” Naoki Tamura said Thursday in a speech to local business leaders in Yamanashi, west of Tokyo. A neutral level refers to a level that’s neither restrictive nor stimulative for the economy.
“Given that underlying consumer price inflation in Japan isn’t firmly anchored at our 2% target, I believe the bank is required to respond swiftly to signs of an upward deviation in prices, including through accelerated policy interest rate hikes,” Tamura said.
