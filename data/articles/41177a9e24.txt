After kicking off their Champions League title defence with four consecutive wins, Luis Enrique's men were held to a draw at home by the Italian side Roma. Real Madrid are now two points clear at the top of the standings.
Elsewhere in Wednesday's Champions League action, Lille clinched a memorable victory against a depleted Bayern Munich side, while Mauricio Pochettino was denied his first win as Manchester United manager in Lisbon.
In rugby, the captains of all twelve Nations Championship teams were at Twickenham on Wednesday for the build-up to the inaugural edition's six-game finals weekend. The showpiece fixture will see France and South Africa face off for the title on Sunday evening in London.
Finally, in tennis, Italy survived an unlikely comeback from South Korea in their Davis Cup quarter-final, with the tie going to a decisive doubles match before the reigning champions sealed their spot in the last four.
