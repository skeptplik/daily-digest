Live: Ukraine, Russia and US to resume peace talks in Abu Dhabi
Negotiators from Ukraine, Russia and the United States are meeting in Abu Dhabi for their first trilateral talks in more than nine months, as Washington pushes for an end to the war and Kyiv faces pressure to make territorial concessions. European representatives will also attend, amid fears that US President Donald Trump could press Ukraine to accept a deal on Moscow's terms. Follow our live blog for the latest events.
