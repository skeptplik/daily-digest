Gloria Steinem’s life, work, and legacy were celebrated this week, as many gathered at a memorial service for the feminist activist in New York City.
The celebration of life took place at the Park Avenue Armory and came two months after Steinem’s passing at age 92.
Among those who came together to pay their respects were former United States first ladies Michelle Obama, Hillary Clinton, and Jill Biden—as well as Chelsea Clinton, Diane von Furstenberg, Meg Ryan, Lucy Liu, Emma Watson, Marisa Tomei, and Mariska Hargitay. In total, there were about 1,000 invited guests, all who were encouraged to donate to Gloria’s Foundation, which aims to preserve Steinem’s Upper East Side brownstone on East 73rd Street as a permanent archive and center for feminist organizing.
Rather than a somber service, the memorial was a joyful, music-filled celebration titled “It’s Steinemite!” Cynthia Erivo sang John Lennon’s “Imagine,” Cyndi Lauper performed “Girls Just Want to Have Fun,” and Kathy Sledge of Sister Sledge closed the event with “We Are Family,” joined by Lauper and Gloria’s Choir.
For the evening, attendees coordinated in all-black ensembles—but it certainly wasn’t your average funeral attire. Clinton wore an elegant pantsuit—one of her favored looks. Obama was so chic and cool in a cropped blazer and a pleated skirt with a silky sash and golden accents. And Biden went for a fun, high-fashion ensemble that included Schiaparelli’s black Corsini suit jacket with asymmetric gold buttons.
If she could’ve looked into that crowd of incredible women, Steinem would’ve been endlessly proud.
