Gains by Japan’s consumer prices inched up to the fastest pace this year, firming up the case for the Bank of Japan to raise its benchmark interest rate next month.
The consumer price index excluding fresh food picked up for a second month to 2.2% in October compared with a year earlier, the internal affairs ministry reported Friday. While it was the biggest advance since December, the result was a tad weaker than the median economist forecast of 2.3%.
The index that strips out both fresh food and energy, a measure closely monitored by the BOJ as a measure of underlying inflation, also climbed 2.2%, and overall CPI rose 2.3%.
