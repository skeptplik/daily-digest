Best for Cats and Small Dogs
The Mini GPS and AI-powered tracker is the lightest and smallest I’ve tested, measuring 1.5 x 1.0 x 0.5 inches. It comes with a strap loop and two soft cases for attachment. I loved that it has a super-long battery life compared to other models, lasting up to nearly two weeks, and that it uses the same USB-C charger as my iPhone, which made it extra convenient. The Fi Mini tracks daily and historical location data so you can monitor movement patterns over time, and it alerts you when your pet leaves the designated safe area. Through the app, you can also measure health metrics like sleep and activity and compare historical data to your pet’s norms. You can even upload vet records, receipts, and insurance information, and add your vet to the app to share pet-related documents and get appointment reminders.
Best for Dogs
I’ve started testing this on my sister’s dog, so my review is forthcoming. In the meantime, WIRED Commerce Director Cooper Fleishman uses the Fi Series 3 GPS dog collar on his mini goldendoodle, Howie, when he leaves them with a caretaker. Cooper likes being able to track Howie’s steps and see their neighborhood walks from afar. Knowing he can quickly find his young, adventurous dog if Howie slips their leash and bolts after a squirrel gives Cooper extra peace of mind. This is Fi’s longest-lasting GPS, with up to three months of battery life and an LED night light, plus escape alerts and a virtual geofence you can set up. It also offers tons of tracking metrics, including activity, sleep, scratching, licking, eating, drinking, and even barking detection.
Best for Outdoor, Adventure, and Working Dogs
This is the GPS dog collar you need if your dog spends most of its time outdoors, such as on multiple acres or a farm. The Fi Ultra promises an off-leash GPS tracking solution using T-Mobile’s T-Satellite with Starlink, enabling it to work beyond the limits of traditional cellular coverage and through thick vegetation. The tracker attaches to your dog’s existing collar, but its high-powered tracking system requires charging every one to two days. The integrated clip works with harnesses, working dog vests, and thick leather collars. It also charges via a standard USB-C cable, is IP68-rated waterproof, and features Fi’s “CallBack Technology,” which uses sound and vibration signals to help you find your dog.
