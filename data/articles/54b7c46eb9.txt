TAIPEI – The timing of Washington’s $14 billion arms sales package to Taiwan is “very much up to” U.S. President Donald Trump and will not be affected by the U.S. midterm results, according to a Taiwanese government official.
Taiwan has been waiting months for Trump to approve the record sale of weapons and equipment that the island says it needs to strengthen its defense capabilities to deter a potential Chinese attack.
Beijing claims the island democracy is part of its territory and has threatened to use force to annex it.
