LONDON — Last month, the biotech Genmab went on a run that would make any of its rivals envious. Three of its drugs succeeded in pivotal trials in three different forms of cancer, leading more than one analyst to observe it had scored a hat trick.
Now, the Danish company needs to deliver on getting the medicines approved and launched and, in the case of two of the products, to demonstrate it can do so without partnering with a larger pharma company.
“It’s all coming down to execution,” Genmab CEO Jan van de Winkel said at the Jefferies Global Healthcare Conference here last week.
