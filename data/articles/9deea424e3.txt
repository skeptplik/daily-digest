Changes in cities’ ranks in the natural sciences are shown for 2016 to 2025, as measured by Share in the Nature Index. Beijing remained in first place, while five more Chinese cities entered the top ten during the period. Two US cities that were once in the top 10 have since fallen out of the top 20.
Lifeline
The leading cities for biological sciences in 2025 are shown alongside their 2016 and 2025 adjusted Share in the subject. This is the most geographically diverse of the natural-sciences subjects, with four US, four Chinese and two European cities in the top 10.
Strong chemistry
Chinese cities take all top 10 places in the chemistry rankings for 2025, shown here with their 2016 and 2025 adjusted Share in the subject. Beijing and Shanghai had the highest growth over the period among the top 50 cities in the subject, each increasing their chemistry adjusted Share by almost 900.
Down to Earth
Beijing contributed 25% of China’s Earth and environmental sciences Share in 2025 — the highest percentage among the top-five cities in the natural-sciences and health-sciences subject areas. Among the top 10 cities in Earth and environmental sciences for 2025, Qingdao had the biggest percentage change in adjusted Share between 2016 and 2025, at 545.5%, and Beijing gained the most adjusted Share points, at 703.
