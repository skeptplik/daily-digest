Usman Khawaja defends timing of sandpaper revelations, says Steve Smith 'wanted to take the blame'
In short:
Usman Khawaja has defended the timing of his bombshell revelations about how many people knew about the plot to rough up the ball with sandpaper during the Cape Town Test in 2018.
In a lengthy Instagram post, Khawaja said Steve Smith convinced him not to come forward with the revelations earlier.
Khawaja said the fact he had held back the book until after Australia's Test return to South Africa last month was proof this was not a money-making venture.
