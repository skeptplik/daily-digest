Abby Choi’s ex-brother-in-law admits to erasing evidence by wiping down car
Anthony Kwong tells court he felt ‘safer’ after the cleaning, even though no blood resulted from his brother strangling his ex-wife in the back seat
The former brother-in-law of slain Hong Kong socialite Abby Choi Tin-fung has admitted to erasing evidence of her killing by cleaning the car used to transport her body to a village house where she was dismembered.
Anthony Kwong Kong-kit told the High Court on Thursday that he felt “safer” after wiping down the Toyota Alphard seven-seater as he had “complicated” feelings after his brother strangled Choi during a violent quarrel. He claimed to have cleaned the vehicle even though there was no blood in it.
Giving evidence for a fourth day in the murder trial, the defendant also highlighted what he retrospectively thought was his cowardly and irrational behaviour, saying he should have called police instead of disposing of his former sister-in-law’s corpse in secret.
