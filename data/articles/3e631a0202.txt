Nasa’s Voyager 1 just reached a massive milestone in space - video
Nasa sent the Voyager 1 and 2 probes into space almost 50 years ago to explore the outer reaches of our solar system. Now Voyager 1, the farthest human-made object from Earth, is about to reach a distance of one light-day away from our planet. To find out more about its astonishing journey, Madeleine Finlay hears from Jim Bell, a planetary scientist and professor at Arizona State University’s school of Earth and space exploration. He explains the seismic impact the mission has had on our understanding of the solar system, and where Voyager 1 could be heading now it has left the heliosphere
