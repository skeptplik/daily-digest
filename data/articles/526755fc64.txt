The biggest video game release of all time is nearly upon us. GTA 6 is dropping on November 19, and the anticipation for the game has been absolutely bonkers. Tons of people are already planning to take time off of work to play, and some schools are even offering gift cards to kids to avoid mass absences. It's going to be a massive launch, to say the least.
But there's also another event kicking off on that same day — Black Friday sales. Both Amazon and Best Buy have revealed that their official sales will be launching on November 19, well ahead of Thanksgiving.
The GTA 6 release date is obviously a very big deal in the world of gaming. Other than Nintendo's Ocarina of Time Remake and that one Barbie game, most of the upcoming video game releases have been moved to avoid clashing with Rockstar. So it's interesting to see major retailers actually launching their Black Friday sales at the exact same time.
Amazon and Best Buy have been launching their Black Friday sales the week before Thanksgiving for several years now. It's not a deliberate choice, just what the plan was going to be regardless of GTA 6. That being said, it will be interesting to see how much the clashing dates impact the first weekend of Black Friday sales this year.
On one hand, with so many people essentially treating the GTA 6 launch like a major holiday, gamers will potentially have more free time to browse deals throughout the day. On the other hand, the game could be so engrossing that folks won't have any interest in taking a break to scroll through their phones.
Upcoming Black Friday Sale Dates
For those not using the New Zealand trick to play early, GTA 6 will officially be playable starting at midnight on November 19 for each time zone. Amazon and Best Buy's sales, however, will kick off at midnight PST for everyone. Here's a quick rundown of the upcoming Black Friday sale dates we know about:
- Amazon Black Friday Week - November 19
- Best Buy Black Friday - November 19
- Xbox Black Friday - November 19
- Target Black Friday - November 22
- Nintendo Black Friday - November 22
- Walmart Black Friday - November 23
