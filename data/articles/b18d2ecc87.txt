Russian projectiles hit Poland as NATO tensions rise
Issued on:
Share
Three Russian projectiles came down in Poland on Friday, prompting NATO to condemn what it called a grave violation of Polish airspace. A suspected jet-powered drone and a missile struck separate locations near the Ukrainian border, while another missile crashed just 35 kilometres from an area where European troops are taking part in military exercises. Meanwhile, troops from eight NATO countries are conducting drills in neighbouring Lithuania. With US-led peace talks yet to make a breakthrough, tensions between Europe and Russia remain high. Peter O’Brien reports.
