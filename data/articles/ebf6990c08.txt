Amazon's Black Friday sale has officially started, which means good LEGO deals are finally starting to arrive. With many sets officially retired, Amazon is dropping prices on some of the coolest builds that are about to disappear forever. One such set is the 3-in-1 T. rex, which has dropped down to to the lowest price I've ever seen, now $41.99 (30% off).
The 3-in-1 T. rex set has been officially retired by LEGO, which means you can't buy it directly from the LEGO website anymore. Many major retailers still have the set in stock, but discounts on retired sets like this one tend to sell out rather quickly.
LEGO T. rex Deal at Amazon Today
There are a lot of really cool LEGO dinosaur sets out there, but most of the modern builds are part of the Jurassic World theme. This 3-in-1 T. rex was one of the last remaining sets without an outside IP influence, but it was officially retired a the end of last year.
Like any 3-in-1 set, the coolest thing about it is that you can build mutliple things. The T. rex is the biggest and most impressive use of the bricks included, but you can also build a triceratops or a pterodactyl. Each different version of the set includes movable parts that let you pose your dinosaur in a variety of positions. With an age rating of 9+, it's a fun build for both kids and adults who have the patience for 626 pieces.
As someone who has built a few 3-in-1 sets myself, I'd definitely suggest having an idea of which dinosaur you want to build before diving into it. The default set will be the T. rex as it used all of the pieces, but once you build that it gets harder to change it to one of the other options. I had the chance to build the big 3-in-1 Horse Knight Castle last year and just didn't have the heart to tear it down to try the other two builds.
