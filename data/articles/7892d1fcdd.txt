Taikoo Li Qiantan, Swire Properties‘ flagship project in Shanghai‘s Pudong District, has forged an ambitious partnership with Thailand‘s luxury mall operator Siam Piwat Group, joining its Global Privilege Partnership network.
The collaboration links Taikoo Li Qiantan’s customer loyalty and lifestyle ecosystem with that of membership programs at Siam Paragon, Siam Center, Siam Discovery, IconSiam and Siam Premium Outlets Bangkok.
Taikoo Li Qiantan said the partnership will help the luxury shopping mall better service “high-net-worth and upper-middle-market consumers.” A wide range of privileges will include “exclusive VIP services, brand benefits, concierge services and cross-border shopping perks,” Qiantan Taikoo Li said in a statement.
“Tax refunds and payment conveniences will be integrated into the entire Shanghai-Bangkok journey, delivering a smoother and more seamless shopping experience for consumers,” the statement noted. “The initiative aims to turn cross-border shopping from a one-off visit into an ongoing, mutually connected experience.”
Mayuree Chaipromprasith, president, corporate affairs and communications at Siam Piwat, added that the partnership will expand beyond customer privileges to include marketing collaboration, knowledge exchange and retail development.
“We aim to create shared value for customers, partners and related businesses, while supporting Thailand’s position as a leading destination in the region,” said Chaipromprasith.
Driving Bangkok‘s luxury evolution, Siam Piwat holds around 70 percent of Thailand’s luxury retail market share and has secured leasing commitments from over 51 global and local brands, including Moncler, Brunello Cucinelli, Zimmermann, and more. This April, Thailand’s first Hermès Duplex opened at Siam Paragon, while Prada unveiled its first duplex store at IconSiam.
Earlier this year, Siam Piwat signed strategic partnerships with the British luxury travel company Belmond, the French department store Galeries Lafayette, the Dubai-based luxury lifestyle management service provider Insignia, and the Thai private aviation provider MJets. The partnerships fall under a unified network dubbed Onesiam.
Swire Properties’ second luxury shopping mall project in Shanghai, Qiantan Taikoo Li was opened in 2020. Located within the Pudong New Area and overlooking the Huangpu River, the Qiantan area is envisioned as a livable future anchored by an expansive riverside park, a school cluster that includes NYU Shanghai, and the Taikoo Li destination mall, which spans 259,000 square meters and over 600 brands.
