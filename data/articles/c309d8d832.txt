Kangaroos survive scare from Swans to book trip to fourth straight AFLW grand final
In short:
North Melbourne has advanced to its fourth straight AFLW grand final after beating Sydney in its preliminary final at Princes Park.
The Swans drew to within two goals in the fourth quarter before the Kangaroos ran out 32-point winners.
What's next?
North Melbourne will face either Melbourne or Carlton in the AFLW grand final, with the pair facing off in the second preliminary final on Saturday afternoon.
