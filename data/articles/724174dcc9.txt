Among all the big Black Friday deals this year, one of the most popular promotions has been at Macy’s, with the retailer offering diamond bracelets, rings, necklaces and earrings for up to 80 percent off.
The Black Friday sale highlights Macy’s customer-favorite line of lab-grown diamonds, which promise the same quality and clarity as natural diamonds, at a much more attractive price point.
As its name suggests, lab-grown diamonds are created using specialized equipment in a lab, but are designed to be optically, physically and chemically the same as natural diamonds. Also worth noting: lab-grown diamonds are conflict-free, and do not take up precious natural resources like diamonds that are mined from the earth.
Of course, the biggest selling point for lab-grown diamonds is the value, with the ability to score biggest rocks at a fraction of the price. As Marty Hurwitz, founding member of the Global Diamond Trade Organization, told WWD back in 2021, “Consumers are most drawn to the size-to-value equation. Shopping within a budget, as most consumers do, when shown both mined and lab-grown diamonds, the bigger stone of the same quality in lab-grown diamond appears to be the winning proposition for most.”
Shoppers and social media users are taking notice, with more than three million videos about “Macy’s diamonds” on TikTok alone. And shopping editors from other publications tell WWD that the Macy’s diamond bracelets have been a huge hit this week, as one of their most popular items sold during Black Friday.
Just note: diamonds are forever, but these Black Friday deals won’t be here for long so you’ll want to act fast to score one of these coveted pieces for yourself.
