Catley's 150th cap
Matildas vice-captain Steph Catley will play her 150th game for the national team tonight. Where has all the time gone?
I remember when she was a spring chicken running around in the A-Leagues!
Given we are the same age, it's a stark reminder of my own clock. Although her knees seem to be holding up a lot better than mine.
But back to Catley... She's just the seventh player to reach the milestone. A huge effort from the stalwart defender.
Matildas coach Joe Montemurro described this week as one of the world's best footballers. Something I won't be quick to argue against.
Catley has been at the heart of this generation that has driven women's football in Australia at full throttle.
"This team feels like my family. We've grown up together, and I'm honoured every single time I touch the pitch with these girls, and I wear that jersey," Catley said.
"So 150 times is massive, and I'm very, very proud of that."
