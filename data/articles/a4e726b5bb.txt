Matildas salute in Catley's 150th in classy outing against Colombia
Steph Catley celebrated her 150th appearance for Australia in style, playing a key role in the Matildas' go-ahead goal as they kicked away in the second half to secure a 3-1 win over Colombia.
After Alanna Kennedy and Linda Caicedo netted inside the opening 15 minutes, Catley’s 59th-minute corner ricocheted off Daniela Arias's back to put Australia into the ascendancy, before Caitlin Foord struck three minutes later to secure a match-winning cushion.
The defender had donned the armband for her milestone appearance at a wet GIO Stadium on Saturday night, with Joe Montemurro also fielding the returning Mary Fowler and Kyra Cooney-Cross in his XI — the latter making her first Matildas appearance in 252 days.
Chasing continuity and insight ahead of the Women’s World Cup next June, the coach will get another chance to look at his side — and chase a final win in 2026 — next Wednesday, when the two sides meet in Geelong.
Every time 21-year-old Caicedo got near the ball, alarm bells rang for the Matildas, with her combination of pace, close control and positional nous leaving defenders scrambling.
But Montemurro’s adjustments at half-time, which saw Kerr withdrawn for Hayley Raso, put the clamps on the South Americans, who didn’t produce another shot until Manuela Vanegas’s 94th-minute header.
Catley’s set piece and Foord’s strike — the latter the result of class build-up from Fowler and Cooney-Cross — then served to secure an assured result.
