Your phone is probably the one accessory you carry more than your favorite bag. So why are we still treating its case as an afterthought?
ESR, a leading consumer tech accessories brand specializing in MagSafe accessories, is teaming up with creative duo Craig & Karl for Pocket Full of Joy, a new collection that brings the artists’ colorful, graphic world to everyday tech essentials. Think bold typography, playful illustrations and a little pop-art sensibility, all designed to make the things we reach for most feel distinctly personal.
The collection features three graphic directions: a sticker-inspired collage filled with colorful symbols, an optimistic “OK” design and a cheerful “HI” magnetic wallet. Each translates Craig & Karl’s recognizable aesthetic into something wearable-adjacent, turning a practical accessory into another opportunity for self-expression.
But the collaboration isn’t sacrificing function for fashion. ESR’s Classic Hybrid Magnetic Case features MagSafe compatibility, protective construction and a built-in Flex Stand for hands-free viewing. Coordinating magnetic wallets keep cards and cash close, with select styles offering adjustable stands, RFID protection and integrated grips.
It’s an approach that feels particularly relevant as personal technology becomes increasingly intertwined with how we dress. Much like a statement bag or a piece of jewelry, a phone case can bring personality to an otherwise simple look. And with Craig & Karl’s unmistakable graphics, these accessories are anything but anonymous.
The ESR x Craig & Karl Pocket Full of Joy collection launches November 14, 2026, on ESR’s official website and Amazon. Phone cases start at $49.99, while magnetic wallets start at $25.99. Customers purchasing a matching case and wallet through ESR’s website will receive a complimentary luggage tag.
