Wolfbox Cordless Jump Starter and 24,000mAh Power Bank
The Wolfbox is a 2-in-1 device. First is a 12V cordless car jump starter that can supply a whopping 4,000A of peak power. It's powerful enough to jump start a 10L gas or diesel engine, and the 88.5Wh lithium battery can do it up to 40 times before needing to recharge. Cordless jump starters essentially make traditional jumper cables obsolete for most people, since you no longer need the assistance of someone else's car (or a tow truck) to get you back on the road.
The Wolfbox also doubles as a massive 24,000mAh power bank that can charge your iPhone 17 or smartphone several times over in an emergency. There are two outputs: one 65W USB Type-C port and one 18W USB Type-A port. The 65W output, which is rarely found in a jump starter, is powerful enough to fast charge even the most power hungry handheld gaming PCs like the Asus ROG Xbox Ally X or Legion Go 2. Maybe you might question why such a high-output port is even necessary in a jump starter. If you are on a long car ride and your gaming device runs out of juice well before you reach your destination, well then I'd certainly consider that an emergency.
