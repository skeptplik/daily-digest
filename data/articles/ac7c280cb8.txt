If you couldn't guess by its 210 GB file size, Grand Theft Auto 6 is gigantic. Rockstar's latest open-world game is stuffed to the brim with things to do and steal. It also sports the beefiest story of any GTA game to date. Jason and Lucia star in a sprawling crime epic that takes the couple all around the state of Leonida. While it's difficult to put a consistent hour count on a true 100% playthrough of GTA 6, we do know that you won't be able to binge it all in one long weekend.
Here's how long it takes to beat GTA 6's story depending on how much side-content you intend to complete.
How long does it take to beat GTA 6?
Even if we're just talking about how long it will take you to beat the story and reach the end credits, expect to spend a lot of time with GTA 6. In our fastest playthrough, we were able to complete the game in around 53 hours. While that playthrough was focused on clearing the story, it still included a healthy number of side-quests, optional robberies, mini-golf games, and plenty of free-form exploration.
A faster playthrough may be possible, but note that GTA 6 features several points where players will either have to amass a certain amount of money or wait for a call to proceed to the next story mission. Even if you only plan on playing for the story, you'll likely still need to do some side-activities to get through it all with enough money to reach the final missions.
Players who want to take their time can expect an even longer experience. In another playthrough that was more focused on completing lots of optional side-activities, our save file crossed the 100-hour mark. That matches what Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson recently told Polygon about his most recent full-story playthrough before launch, which he said took him just over 100 hours, in a run that he described as "rushed."
Though that’s a wide range of hours, what we’ve seen so far in testing lines up with How Long To Beat's data on Red Dead Redemption 2. User playtime data puts an average main story playthrough at around 52 hours and one with a focus on side-content at 89 hours, which matches with what we found in GTA 6.
All in all, expect to spend 70 to 80 hours clearing the main story unless you plan to mainline it. That makes GTA 6 the series' longest game by a wide margin, so budget your time accordingly.
