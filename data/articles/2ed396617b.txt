Nike has been having a bit of a comeback—“comeback” might be generous, but the sentiment is there. In 2024, the sportswear giant faced restructuring challenges, accusations of sexism, and layoffs that exceeded two percent of its staff. Under Hill’s leadership, Nike is refocusing on its core market and working to repair relationships with retailers, athletes, and creatives.
Despite these shortcomings, Nike remains one of the most popular brands worldwide.
