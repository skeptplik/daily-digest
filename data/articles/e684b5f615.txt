TURIN, Italy – World number one Alexander Zverev cruised past Ben Shelton 6-2 7-6(4) on Tuesday to book a semifinal spot at the ATP Finals with a group match to spare.
The German, who won two Grand Slams and topped the rankings in his most successful year to date, delivered a dominant performance to extend his perfect record against Shelton to 7-0. He next plays France’s Arthur Fils in his final group match.
Shelton, playing in his second straight season finale, is 1-1 in the group and will play Rafael Jodar in his last match.
