Craig Bellamy says Melbourne Storm coaching tenure will conclude at end of 2027 NRL season
In short:
Craig Bellamy will depart from his role as Melbourne Storm coach at the end of the 2027 NRL season.
Bellamy, 67, hopes to remain involved with the club in various non-coaching roles after his exit.
He has previously said that Melbourne's next coach should come from within.
