- Estée Lauder Companies (ELC) reported organic net sales growth of 6% in Q1, generating $3.7 billion.
- The company’s full-year growth guidance lifted to 4-5.5%, from a previous estimate of 3-5%.
- The turnaround plan introduced in February 2025, Beauty Reimagined, is maintaining quarterly growth.
- Fragrance was once again a standout, up 20%, driven by Le Labo, Tom Ford, Jo Malone London, and Kilian Paris.
- The Asia-Pacific region led growth (10%), followed by Mainland China (9%), EUKEM (Europe, the UK, Ireland, and emerging markets) (4%), and the Americas (4%).
