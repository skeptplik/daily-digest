Between Suki Waterhouse’s Loveland Tour and Robert Pattinson’s Primetime release, Hollywood’s favorite fiancés have been booked and busy this fall. Their projects—combined with the responsibilities of raising their two-year-old daughter—have kept them so busy, in fact, that wedding planning has been the last thing on their minds.
On Sunday, The Times shared an interview with Waterhouse, in which the Daisy Jones and the Six star spoke openly about her relationship with Pattinson. The creatives, who got engaged back in 2023 and welcomed their first child the following year, have been together for nearly a decade now—having first started dating in 2018.
For years, rumors that the two already secretly wed have spread, but in her new interview, Waterhouse revealed that they actually have not even started planning the ceremony.
The one thing she knows for sure? “It would either be all or nothing,” she told the outlet, noting that the wedding wouldn’t take place until after the end of the year. “If we were going to go for it, we’d have a massive party, blow it out,” she added.
The next day, during Monday night’s CFDA Awards, Waterhouse reiterated this statement to People. When asked about the forthcoming event, she got slightly flustered, joking that it’s “embarrassing” how few wedding plans she and Pattinson have made. “Oh my God, I don’t even know. I literally said that in that [Sunday Times] interview, I was like ‘It might be small, it might be big.’ But I don’t know,” she said, with her signature chill-girl nonchalance. “I literally don’t have any—it’s like embarrassing, ’cause I don’t have any plans.”
