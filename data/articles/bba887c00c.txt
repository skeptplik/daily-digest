Spencer Leniu handed two-match ban over money gesture to referees in World Cup semifinal
In short:
Samoa's Spencer Leniu has been suspended for two matches after making a money gesture at the referees in his side's loss to Australia in the Rugby League World Cup semifinal.
If Leniu contests the grade three contrary conduct charge and loses his appeal, he will incur an additional one-match suspension.
What's next?
Leniu will miss Parramatta's first two games of the 2027 NRL season, including the team's game in Las Vegas.
