The notion that gray hair lacks versatility couldn't be further from the truth: from “silver framing” to “frosted hair,” innovative color trends keep proving how chic it can be. The latest to join the roster? “Smoky steel.”
With its cool, dark undertones and soft, silvery dimension, this modern take on gray hair makes a compelling case for embracing the shade at every age. Seen to great effect on Andie MacDowell and, more recently, Olivia Colman, smoky steel strikes the perfect balance between sophisticated and modern—whether worn in long cascading curls, cut into a sleek bob, or styled in a polished topknot.
The hue's depth makes it even more appealing. Rather than a classic uniform gray, smoky steel adds density by combining dark, ashy tones with cool silver, creating dimension in the light. “By adding subtle beige, taupe, or mink warmth into gray, the color looks more luminous against the skin as it creates a healthy, diffused glow,” Adam Russell, creative master colorist at Josh Wood, previously told Harper’s Bazaar. “Warm grays also blend beautifully with naturally graying hair, so the growing-out process is far more seamless.”
This is especially true for smoky steel, which sits somewhere between classic silver and ashy brunette. Its softer, multitonal finish blends more easily with natural grays, creating a result that feels both effortless and polished. “These shades balance warm and cool pigments, keeping the color looking chic rather than yellow or brassy,” says Russell.
A skilled colorist can also soften the contrast between sharper roots and lighter lengths, adding shine and dimension with strategic highlights. However, allowing silver strands to emerge without coloring can be just as striking.
Ultimately, smoky steel reminds us that gray hair doesn’t mean compromising on a dynamic, multidimensional look. If you've ever had a reason to reconsider your next hair appointment, this might be it.
