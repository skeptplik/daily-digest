Accountability check: Let’s talk about my “Alnylam is the next Vertex” take from the Aug. 7, 2025, edition of this newsletter.
Alnylam and Vertex are not direct competitors, but I was intrigued by the possibility that Alnylam’s rapidly accelerating growth — fueled by sales of its blockbuster cardiomyopathy treatment Amvuttra — could push its $60 billion market value equal to or higher than Vertex’s $100 billion market cap in the near future.
