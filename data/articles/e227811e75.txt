Ciara’s latest maternity look involved sheer black fabric, plenty of feathers and a rather substantial pearl necklace. The singer arrived at the 2026 Baby2Baby Gala on Saturday night with her growing baby bump on display, framed by feathered detailing around her hips.
The “Goodies” singer, who is expecting her fifth child, attended the annual fundraiser at the Pacific Design Center in West Hollywood in a transparent, long-sleeved ensemble layered over a black bra. More feathers covered the cuffs and continued down the sides, while the sheer material fell to the floor and trailed behind her.
Ciara accessorized with a necklace of oversized white pearls, along with a metallic cuff and rings. Her hair was styled half up with a wide black headband and plenty of retro-inspired volume at the crown, leaving long waves falling over one shoulder.
The appearance comes a little over a month after Ciara made a surprise turn on the runway at Junya Watanabe’s spring 2027 ready-to-wear show during Paris Fashion Week on Oct. 3. She walked three times in variations on a purple-and-pink cropped ensemble with a low-slung belt and red shoes, including looks with a trench coat and a deconstructed leather jacket. Her final appearance included a crystal-covered headpiece that extended into a chandelier-like structure around her body.
For last year’s Baby2Baby Gala, she went in another direction, selecting a vintage black leather dress from Ann Demeulemeester’s spring 1996 collection, complete with leather gloves.
Ciara and husband Russell Wilson announced in September that they are expecting their fourth child together. She also shares son Future Zahir with rapper Future.
The 2026 Baby2Baby Gala returned to the Pacific Design Center in West Hollywood on Saturday, Nov. 14, honoring Demi Moore with the nonprofit’s Giving Tree Award. Presented by Paul Mitchell, the annual fundraiser supports Baby2Baby’s work providing diapers, clothing and other essentials to children living in poverty across the United States. Moore follows 2025 honoree Serena Williams, with past recipients also including Charlize Theron, Kim Kardashian and Salma Hayek Pinault. Last year’s event raised a record $18 million.
