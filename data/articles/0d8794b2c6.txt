Premiership-winning halfback Sam Walker has signed with the Papua New Guinea Chiefs for the 2028 season.
Walker, whose relationship with the Sydney Roosters fell apart after the club's grand final victory, will head to Port Moresby on a two-year deal.
The Queenslander is coming off the best year of his career, making State of Origin and Australian Test debuts.
"Sam is undoubtedly a gifted footballer who has already shown that he has tremendous vision, an outstanding kicking game and an ability to create something out of nothing," PNG Chiefs coach Willie Peters said.
"He is also a very smart footballer who understands how to influence and control a game and has shown that he can perform on the biggest of stages across club, state and now international level.
"We're really excited about what he can bring to the team and his partnership alongside Jarome Luai allows us to have one of the game's most creative and influential halves combinations — something which I'm excited to see develop as we take to the field."
The 24-year old was the 2021 Dally M Rookie of the Year and has been the Tricolours starter ever since.
He also attracted interest from South Sydney and Canterbury.
PNG Chiefs General Manager of Football Michael Chammas said the team believed Walker's best football was still yet to come.
"Sam was someone we identified as a player who we believe could have a transformational impact on our team — both in how we play on the field but also on the way we build our side and the way other players excel around him," Chammas said.
"He is a premiership winner, an Origin star and a World Cup winner and he has shown that he can perform under pressure in the biggest moments of our game.
"He is also still young and we believe that his best years are ahead of him.
"We're thrilled that Sam has committed to what we are building and we look forward to seeing him play a key role in the bright future of the PNG Chiefs."
Walker is the latest star signing for the Chiefs, who have created shockwaves in the player market with their ability to offer tax-free contracts.
In the past week, they have signed Canberra and New South Wales backrower Hudson Young and North Queensland star Jeremiah Nanai.
Luai, Brian To'o, Zac Lomax and Joseph Manu will also line up for the Chiefs in their inaugural season.
Walker is the ninth player they have signed with either Test or Origin experience.
