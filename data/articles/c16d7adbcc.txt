MILAN — Driven by luxury fashion, home and accessories’ growing relationship with top hospitality brands, Raffles Hotels & Resorts is taking the plunge into the e-commerce business.
On Monday, the firm told WWD that it will launch The Little Shop by Raffles, its first global e-commerce platform, featuring a bespoke collection developed in collaboration with stylist Jessica Diehl. The offering embraces the banner’s signature palm print and deep green and spans apparel, resortwear and activewear. The offer also includes exclusive collaborations with heritage brands such as Frette slippers, Christofle coffee cups, Globe-Trotter carry-on cases, Orlebar Brown swim trunks, Vanessa Sposi swimwear, Amambaih bags, Z Lab sleep masks, Smythson leather goods and Ginori 1735 tableware.
“The Little Shop by Raffles is a natural expression of who we have always been as a brand,” said Raffles Resorts & Hotels chief brand officer Claudia Kozma Kaplan, adding that each piece was envisaged to extend the travel experience long after a stay has ended.
The core collection includes separates emblazoned with the banner’s signature phrase “The Butler Did It,” Raffles’ crest-adorned cashmere, palm-print pajamas and hats, as well as a resort edit of caftans in the Raffles print. Designed for life at and beyond the hotels, each piece comes with a personal note from the Raffles Butler.
The name revives the original Little Shop, opened at Raffles Singapore in the 1940s. Select pieces will be available at Raffles London at The OWO, Raffles Boston and Le Royal Monceau — Raffles Paris, with the London hotel also introducing a dedicated boutique.
Founded in Singapore in 1887, Raffles Hotels, Resorts and Residences is a collection of landmark properties known for elegant design, service and cultural experiences. With locations including Singapore, Paris, London, Boston, Istanbul, Dubai, Jaipur, Phnom Penh and Bali, Raffles prides itself on its connection with the arts, local communities and the environment. The brand is part of Accor, which operates more than 5,800 properties in more than 110 countries.
Hospitality and luxury fashion, accessories and home collaborations are on the rise.
In September, U.K. hotel chain The Hoxton unveiled a partnership with Los Angeles-based artist Michael McGregor to create a limited-edition, seven-piece capsule collection.
The Dominick, SoHo, the only hotel in New York with an AAA Five Diamond designation, partnered with Alexis Bittar this year to offer guests custom jewelry boxes delivered to their rooms for an additional fee. Hartmann, America’s oldest premium luggage brand, marked the nation’s semiquincentennial with The Beatrice in Providence. The hotel’s “Rhode to Everywhere” package, offered through the summer, featured women’s bestsellers from Hartmann’s Reserve collection.
W Florence teamed with local designer Domenico Orefice on a custom leather bag for overnight guests to use during their stay and take home. The accessory celebrates Italy’s leatherworking heritage with a tanned-leather finish and woven intrecciato handle.
