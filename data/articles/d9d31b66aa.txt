The NBA's Miami Heat unveiled the special Vice City court they'll be playing on for one night only, tomorrow in a home game at the Kaseya Center against the Milwaukee Bucks, which doubles as the first time that superstar Heat center Giannis Antetokounmpo will play against the team he'd spend his whole career with before being traded to Miami in the offseason.
Posting on the team's X page (see below), the court features the GTA 6 Roman-numeral "VI" logo along with "Vice CIty" at center court and "I love Vice City" along the baselines. All of it is in the now-familiar hot pink and blue color scheme that have been at the forefront of Grand Theft Auto 6's branding.
