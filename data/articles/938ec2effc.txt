Is Spain 'nostalgic' for Franco? Would traditional right PP consider an alliance with far-right Vox?
Issued on: Modified:
Share
After eight years of socialist-led government, Spain is at a crossroads. Can Pedro Sánchez pull off another electoral comeback, or will the conservative Partido Popular make way for the far right into national government for the first time since the end of Franco's 40-year brutal reign? Just back from Madrid, Richard Werly, Europe correspondent for the Swiss daily Blick, joins Angela Diffley to examine the final days of campaigning and the generational divisions shaping Sunday’s snap elections. The youth are divided between the left and the far-right Vox party. Sánchez, nicknamed the “Teflon politician”, has campaigned heavily on housing and social issues. “We may see an outgoing prime minister performing better than expected,” says Werly.
