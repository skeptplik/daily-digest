EA has added a "don't delete" pop-up message to Madden NFL 27, and players can't help but feel the company is begging them not to make room for Grand Theft Auto 6.
Reports of the new in-game notice began cropping up over the weekend, with CharlieIntel relaying their findings online. EA doesn't explicitly mention GTA 6 in the new message, but considering the pop-up features some familiar pink-and-purple colors and sun-kissed palm trees, it's pretty easy to put two and two together.
The message encourages Madden NFL 27 users to refrain from uninstalling the game from their devices, promising that those who stick around can look forward to a new update coming Tuesday, November 17. It's a bit beside the point, but we should also mention that, at least based on early screenshots, EA's message includes a typo, writing "awating signal" instead of "awaiting signal." Newer posts suggest it was fixed pretty quickly. Whoops!

"Don't Delete!" the Madden pop-up says. "[ENCRYPTED SIGNAL] The target is locked. The crew is assembling for a high-stakes run. Await the signal from our insider on 11/17… do not leak this transmission!"
There's just no getting around it; the next week is going to be very, very weird for the gaming industry. GTA 6, a decade-in-the-making video game said to bring the biggest launch of all time, has mostly seen every other game clear the calendar just to make room for it. The atmosphere surrounding Rockstar Games' latest is something to behold, and with millions of players still turning up for live-service titles, one can wonder if any of their developers are worried about GTA 6 taking a chunk of their attention.
That at least seems to be the case for EA and Madden NFL 27. If a Vice City-themed pop-up in this year's annual football game release wasn't enough to convince you, its official X/Twitter account actually recently doubled down. A November 10 post promoting a November 18 update referenced GTA 6's massive file size by saying, "Don't let 210GB get between you and Your League."
It all seems in good fun, but fans say they can't help but feel like EA is doing everything in its power to keep players interested. Some even say it feels like the company is begging them to stay.
When one Reddit user asked what the message was related to, one user replied, "It means that EA is terrified of GTA VI's launch." Another said it felt like the pop-up was the publisher's way of saying, "'PLEASE DON'T DELETE MADDEN TO CLEAR SPACE FOR GTA. PLEASE PLEASE PLEASE!'"
"EA begging us not to delete Madden is so funny," one X user said.
"Respectfully I might never play your game again after GTA comes out," another added.
