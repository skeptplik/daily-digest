Cricket NSW rules out allowing Perth Bears to play NRL trial at North Sydney
In short:
Cricket NSW will not allow NRL side the Perth Bears to play a trial match at North Sydney Oval.
The expansion side was hoping to play a pre-season game at the venue but cricket officials denied the request.
A match would have rendered the ground unusable for cricket for at least five weeks.
