The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping premiere, the Beauty Inc. Awards, a Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon appearance, and more: Jennifer Lawrence has spent much of the past week all glammed up. But on Friday, November 20, during a rare day off, the actor kept things casual—taking a New York City walk in a fall look that reminded us her street style still rivals a red-carpet alternative.
Known for her effortless layering, high-low styling, and hints of leopard prints, Lawrence stepped out in West Village wearing an easy, casual black-and-white ensemble... with a little pop of autumnal red. On top, she wore a plum-purple T-shirt under a porcelain-beige car coat from the Row, crafted from structured cotton and tailored with a long body lined with silk. The outerwear featured a little collar, concealed buttons, and welt pockets—creating a sleek silhouette.
As for trousers, Lawrence opted for casual black track pants with parallel red stripes down each leg. She matched the accent hue to her bold statement slippers: a pair of deep-ruby calf-hair slip-ons perched atop a black platform base. The shoes appeared to be a version of Birkenstock’s classic Boston style, with silver-tone buckle straps across each instep.
For further accessories, the Hunger Games star wore black sunglasses, some Airpods, her go-to gold Sidney Garber necklace, and a small Guidi handbag. The new bag, first spotted on Lawrence’s arm in October, was crafted from black leather with thick rounded straps and shiny silver hardware.
