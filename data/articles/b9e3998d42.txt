Thick black smoke billowed into the sky after a powerful explosion rocked a major oil facility in Bangkok, Thailand, on Thursday, 26 November 2026.
Flames rose above the sprawling industrial site in the east of the capital as a fire engine headed towards the scene.
Authorities advised nearby residents to evacuate, but no casualties were immediately reported.
Bangchak Corporation, which operates the site, said the situation had been brought under control, according to the Bang Na district office.
The cause of the blast was not immediately disclosed. With a daily processing capacity of 120,000 barrels, the refinery is Thailand's fifth-largest and the only one located in an urban area.
