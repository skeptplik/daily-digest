Steven J. Marcus died from complications of Parkinson’s disease on 26 August. He lived with the condition, which affects 8–12 million people worldwide, for about 15 years. In all that time, Steve never totally lost the passionate and incisive intellect that led him to excel as a science and technology magazine editor for publications such as MIT Technology Review and The New York Times.
