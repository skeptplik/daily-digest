Josh Addo-Carr's immediate playing future in Parramatta's hands after B-sample returns negative
In short:
Josh Addo-Carr's future with the Parramatta Eels remains up in the air as he waits for the club to decide.
Addo-Carr's B-sample from a roadside drugs test has come back negative, and he faces no further action from police.
What's next?
Addo-Carr met Eels officials at a board meeting on Monday to respond to a breach notice. His lawyer said he remains "committed to the club".
