Proton Pass is an advanced password manager and auto-fill tool that makes it easy to stay secure online by serving as a digital safe for your personal information. With Proton Pass, you can create strong passwords for every site you visit and more effectively anonymize your accounts with unique hide-my-email aliases — no more giving out your personal email to every website you visit so they can flood your inbox. Proton Pass will remember all the complicated details for you, so logins are still quick and easy, just now they’ll be more secure.
Proton Pass goes beyond just login information, too. It can autofill details like shipping and credit card information when you’re shopping, pull up your passport details when you’re booking a flight, and help keep important details in reach when you’re travelling.
You won’t be limited to just your PC either. Proton Pass works across devices from Windows to Mac and iOS to Android, and it includes support for a range of browsers. Proton Pass encrypts your details, has built-in two-factor authentication, and even monitors the dark web to make sure your details don’t wind up in the wrong hands.
