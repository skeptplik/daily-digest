This is a rig for 1440p gaming. This CyberPowerPC machine has an AMD Ryzen 7 9800X3D, an Nvidia GeForce RX 9070 GPU, 32GB of DDR5 5600 RAM, and a 1TB SSD. It ships with Wi-Fi 6 connectivity.
The Ryzen 7 9800X3D is the headline spec of this build. With its generous 3D cache, it's one of the very best gaming CPUs on the market right now, edged out only by the 9850X3D in terms of gaming performance, as you can see from our bench database below. With 8 cores and 16 threads, this CPU will chew through any gaming title thanks to that 3D cache, which means the chip has to rely much less heavily on RAM, drastically improving performance.
The GPU in this build is the RX 9070 with a generous 16GB of VRAM. Sadly, it isn't the more potent XT version, but this is still a great gaming GPU, averaging nearly 75 FPS in our 19-game rasterization benchmark suite at 1440p. 4K performance was around 41 FPS without frame generation, too, so there is some 4K potential.
The PC also comes with 32GB of DDR5 5600 memory, a slight compromise over 6000 speeds but one you shouldn't notice too much. You also get a 1TB PCIe 4.0 SSD for your operating system and storing games, plenty to get you started.
It's all held together by a B650 motherboard, and ships inside one of CyberPower's more impressive cases, along with fans aplenty. You also get Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth, lots of USB ports, HDMI and DisplayPort connectivity, and a liquid cooler for the CPU, as well as an 850-watt power supply. And, of course, a copy of Windows 11.
With Black Friday still a couple of weeks away, this is one of the best gaming PC deals going right now and one not to be missed.
