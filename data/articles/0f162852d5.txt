Cameron Bruce named new Brisbane Lions senior coach, replacing Chris Fagan
In short:
Cameron Bruce has been appointed Brisbane's new senior coach following the departure of Chris Fagan.
Bruce has served as an assistant under Fagan for the last five seasons after leaving Carlton at the end of 2021.
What's next?
Bruce is expected to be officially unveiled as Brisbane's coach on Tuesday.
