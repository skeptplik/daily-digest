'Europe not going anywhere': G7 summit a 'signal to Russians' that Europe is standing behind Ukraine
Issued on:
Share
As the US Midterms reshape Ukraine diplomacy, Annette Young is pleased to welcome James Townsend, Atlantic Council's Senior Advisor at the Scowcroft Center's for Transatlantic Security Initiative. "Europe is not going anywhere." With this unequivocal assessment, Townsend, taking place alongside trilateral negotiations in Abu Dhabi. While the latter may command international attention, Townsend argues that the Paris meeting carries a message of its own: European support for Ukraine is intended to remain durable, and Moscow should not mistake diplomatic negotiations for an erosion of Western resolve. The G7 meeting therefore emerges as more than a forum for diplomatic declarations. It represents a test of Western cohesion, the credibility of future military and financial assistance, and Washington's willingness to endorse a common position on Ukraine. As Townsend emphasizes, the final communiqué will also be "a test as far as what Marco Rubio is going to agree to put his name to."
