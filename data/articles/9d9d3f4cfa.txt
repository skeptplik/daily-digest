After years of collaborating with other brands on sneakers, JW Anderson is finally launching one of its own.
On Thursday, designer Jonathan Anderson’s namesake label unveiled the Anchor sneaker, a new unisex shoe inspired by the streamlined proportions of vintage running shoes. The design introduces an athletic dimension to the JW Anderson wardrobe, combining ergonomic asymmetry with a slim profile and lightweight construction, the company said.
The signature anchor motif, originating from Anderson’s grandfather’s coat of arms, appears prominently across the upper. A Run Rabbit charm sits on the shoelace, presenting another familiar JW Anderson motif seen across ready-to-wear, objects and the wide world of the house.
Made in Italy from nylon and Italian calf suede, the Anchor sneaker also features a blind-embossed JW Anderson logo, which adds a discreet finishing touch, while cushioned construction and a lightweight EVA rubber sole offer comfort and ease of movement.

“It’s exciting to introduce the first sneaker produced under JW Anderson,” Anderson said in a statement. “I wanted the Anchor sneaker to bring a new dimension to the house, bringing the proportions of vintage running shoes with a focus on comfort, character and detail.”
The launch is captured in a campaign starring actors Conor Sánchez and Dree Hemingway, both friends and collaborators of the house. Photographed by Sam Khoury against softly colored backdrops, the pair bring an offbeat sensuality and a sense of spontaneity to the imagery, the company noted.
Sánchez also appeared in a similar campaign shot by Khoury earlier this year to highlight Anderson’s collaboration with Diadora. In May, Anderson took on the Italian shoe brand’s Equipe trainer, which was originally created by Diadora in 1975 for elite athletes. The collaborative shoe featured a slim silhouette, swallowtail toe and heel-wrapping outsole.
In a separate collaboration, Anderson recently released updated takes on Ugg’s Classic Boot and Tasman slipper last month. Both styles merge the brand mainstays with Anderson’s playful use of the straddle strap detail most commonly used on penny loafers.
Other shoe collaborations for Anderson include a tie-up with Wellipets on a pair of rubber clogs that featured a frog’s face on the upper’s toe. Featured in an array of colors, the style was first seen on the designer’s fall/winter 2023 runway show at Milan Men’s Fashion Week.
Aside from Wellipets, Anderson was in a long-term partnership with Converse that began in 2017. In a series of several drops, the duo created memorable takes on the classic Chuck 70 and the Run Star Hike featuring a chunky platform.
JW Anderson’s new Anchor sneaker, which retails for $595, is now available in four colorways — “Petrol” blue, “Peanut” tan, “Off White” and “Brown” — at JW Anderson stores and Jwanderson.com.
