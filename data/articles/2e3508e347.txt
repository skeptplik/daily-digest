Avengers: Doomsday has a new TV spot, shown in the U.S. overnight, that reveals key new scenes involving Magneto, Doom, Steve Rogers, and Thor.
The highlight of the TV spot, below, is Magneto desperately trying to use his mutant power to rip Doom’s mask off. "He is pain,” Magneto says. “He is extinction.” “I am Doom," Doom adds.
Unfortunately for our heroes, Doom is so powerful that he’s able to force it back on before his — presumably Robert Downey Jr.’s — face is revealed.

But is it actually Robert Downey Jr.’s face underneath the mask? There are all sorts of theories doing the rounds that suggest Marvel may be misdirecting fans about Doom’s true identity in Doomsday. We get a decent look at Doom’s green eyes before he uses his overwhelming power to conceal his face once again. Some are convinced it’s Oppenheimer star Cillian Murphy under the mask. That would be quite the shock given everything we know and have seen about the movie so far.
Earlier this month, Marvel Studios boss Kevin Feige confirmed that Doctor Doom is not a variant of Tony Stark in Doomsday. “Is Steve Rogers a Johnny Storm variant?" asked Feige. "People wondered, ‘Is Doom just a variant of another character? Is this just a shortcut to bringing back another character?’ The answer is no. He’s Victor Von Doom.” Is Feige misdirecting fans here?
Why would Magneto try to rip Doom’s mask off in the first place, rather than, say, immediately crush it? Perhaps the plan here is to remove the mask so that Professor X can get in his head? Either way, it doesn’t work.
We also have a new look at Steve Rogers and Thor calmly walking up to Doom in his lair in Latveria. Are they trying to reason with Doom? Do they mean to confront him about his true motivation after some dramatic discovery? Steve Rogers looks pretty mad in this brief glimpse at the scene. Perhaps he feels betrayed?
And finally, we get the shot of Thor trying to hit Doom with Stormbreaker, only for Doom to block the hit with his finger.
Certainly, the more Marvel shows this movie, the more powerful Doom looks. Thanos was a powerful antagonist for sure, but he wasn’t this powerful. I expect the Avengers, X-Men, and The Fantastic Four are in for a rough ride.
Time for some speculation! Lots of people are suggesting there’s something off about Steve Rogers in what we’ve seen of him so far in this movie, to the point where they’re wondering if it’s actually Loki casting some illusion over everyone. We know Loki goes to see Steve and Peggy in what we now believe is The Fantastic Four’s universe to offer his help. Is this the help he was talking about?
We also see Doom’s three Latverian Witches in the new TV spot. While their identities remain a secret for now, fans believe they could be Sylvie, Clea, and Wanda. Are the masks they wear part of Doom’s magic mind control?
