Jennifer Lawrence’s street style may gravitate toward the minimal, but she’s a strong advocate when it comes to the unexpected pop of color. Sure, she loves her branded baseball caps and her chunky pendants, but her most common trick has become incorporating a vibrant statement piece (or two), whether it’s a pair of baby-pink party pants, a lime-green handbag, or some blood-red mesh flats.
This fall, Lawrence is at again as she eschews the standard autumnal palette for her new favorite color: chartreuse.
Just a few weeks ago, the Oscar winner turned heads when she stepped out in a yellow-green Miu Miu cardigan, and now, it appears she’s doubling down on her chartreuse campaign.
On Wednesday, Lawrence was seen walking around New York City in that same electric cardigan, layered underneath a matching cotton overcoat from rising NYC-based designer and LVMH Prize finalist Colleen Allen. (Upon a closer look, you could see that she even wore a scrunchie in the same color, too.)
If anyone can pull off stacks of chartreuse though, it’s Lawrence, and she balanced the eye-popping pieces with a few grounding base layers, including a white graphic tee and some black slacks. She also added inky calf-hair loafers from the Row, a set of oversize sunglasses from Dita Eyewear, and a handwoven tote from Tory Burch.
These past few years, we’ve seen the domination of everything from butter yellow to “brat” green. Now, it seems Lawrence is making the case for a neon tint somewhere right down the middle.
