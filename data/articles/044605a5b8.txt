Kumamoto – Young local government employees who experienced the devastating 2016 earthquakes in Kumamoto Prefecture are now engaged in relief efforts following a major earthquake that struck the same area in July this year.
Drawing on their own experiences as disaster survivors, they are determined to support people facing hardships similar to those they endured.
Ayu Nakase, 25, was a third-year junior high school student in the town of Mashiki when the town was hit by two earthquakes registering 7 on the Japanese seismic intensity scale, the highest level on the scale, in April 2016. She was impressed by local government employees, who worked tirelessly to assist residents at evacuation shelters.
