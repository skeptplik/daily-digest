Nathan Lyon's Test career in doubt, set to miss Australian squad for New Zealand series
In short:
Australian spinner Nathan Lyon is set to miss out on the squad for the Test series against New Zealand.
Lyon, 39, has taken 577 wickets in 145 Tests for Australia.
What's next?
The four-match series against New Zealand begins with the first Test in Perth on December 9.
