Ted Lieu seeks Congress leadership post, aiming to break another glass ceiling
Currently most high-profile Asian and Chinese-American in Congress, Lieu announced his caucus chair candidacy in letter to fellow Democrats
He announced his caucus chair candidacy in a letter to fellow Democrats on Thursday. He’s currently the most high-profile Asian and Chinese-American in Congress, serving as vice-chairman of the caucus, the fourth-ranking position in the House Democratic leadership hierarchy.
