WASHINGTON – The Trump administration plans to press ahead with efforts to block imports of Chinese data center equipment despite pushback from Amazon, Google, Meta and other U.S. technology companies, people briefed on the matter said.
It was reported in August that the Federal Communications Commission was seeking to ban U.S. imports of new models of Chinese optical transceivers, which allow data to travel over fiber-optic cables at the speed of light within data centers, over national security concerns.
But the measure has faced more opposition than was previously known, with Amazon, Google, Meta, CoreWeave and Cisco joining Microsoft in a meeting with White House officials earlier this year previously reported by Reuters. U.S. firms could need up to a decade to comply with any such mandate, the executives told the officials, according to two people familiar with the matter.
