Some handbag trends come and go, but the bucket bag has proven its staying power. Somewhere between a slouchy hobo and a structured tote, the cylindrical style has long struck a balance between practical and effortlessly cool.

Part of the silhouette’s enduring popularity is its versatility. What began as a utilitarian design (one of the earliest iterations was a Louis Vuitton bag from the 1930s, originally intended to transport Champagne bottles) has become grounds for experimentation, with designers playing with proportions, materials, and distinctive details. This season, in particular, the bucket bag is enjoying renewed attention, with everything from cinched drawstring closures and sculptural top handles to unexpected hardware giving the classic a fresh look.
When shopping for a bucket bag, consider more than just the looks. Think about how and where you’ll carry yours: A spacious style can double as a work tote, while a mini version can add a touch of whimsy to evening looks. Fortunately, there’s no shortage of choices, whether you’re looking to invest in a luxury piece or discover something at a more accessible price point.
While preferences are unique to each shopper, the best options combine quality craftsmanship with functional details, from comfortable straps to secure closures and interiors that accommodate everyday essentials. Ahead, we’ve rounded up bucket bags to shop now, from understated versions to modern interpretations of the timeless silhouette.
Best Suede Bucket Bags
Few materials make a handbag feel as luxurious as suede, particularly in wintertime. In rich shades of chocolate brown, navy, and burgundy, the tactile surface exudes sophistication. Just remember that suede requires care, particularly when water is involved.
Best Oversized Bucket Bags
For anyone who carries their entire life in their handbag, oversized bucket bags offer a stylish alternative to traditional work totes, with enough room for a laptop, water bottle, and whatever else the day demands. Supple leather designs have a relaxed, lived-in appeal, while sturdier versions offer a professionally glossy finish. Look for a wide strap, a secure closure, and an organized interior to make the most of the extra space and keep it gentle on your shoulders during the commute.
Best Drawstring Bucket Bags
There’s something undeniably charming about a drawstring closure, which gives bucket bags their signature gathered appearance (and helps keep belongings secure). The cinched opening creates visually impactful folds that look particularly elegant in buttery leather, soft suede, or evening-ready satin.
Best Structured Bucket Bags
If your wardrobe leans minimalist, a structured bucket bag may be the perfect match. Unlike softer iterations that collapse when set down, these styles maintain their architectural structure thanks to firmer leather and well-constructed bases; longer straps also allow for crossbody wear. Their shape makes them feel more like design objects than everyday accessories, especially when finished with distinctive hardware.
Best Mini Bucket Bags
Good things really do come in small packages, and mini bucket bags are proof. These scaled-down versions trade generous storage space for playful proportions that are ideal for evenings out, weekend plans, or occasions when you only need the essentials. Choose a style with a detachable strap for added versatility, or carry it by hand for a more dressed-up effect.
Best Woven Bucket Bags
Woven bags have moved beyond their beach-vacation beginnings. Intricately interlaced leather lends the bucket silhouette an artisanal quality, while tightly bound raffia in darker shades is appropriate for city dressing. Look for details like leather handles and tonal finishes that give these textured designs a more elevated, winter-ready look.
Best Top-Handle Bucket Bags
For a ladylike approach to the bucket bag, consider a top-handle silhouette. Short, sculptural handles give the otherwise casual shape a more refined appearance. Some feature rigid circular handles, while others have softly curved leather straps that sit comfortably in the crook of the arm.
