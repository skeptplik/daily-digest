Flipper Devices, the company behind the popular Flipper Zero pentesting device, has launched a crowdfunding campaign for Flipper One, a portable Linux computer and networking multi-tool.
Despite global RAM shortages and unpredictable price fluctuations, the company said Flipper One will launch at $399, a price that reportedly covers production costs and leaves no profit margin.
Flipper Devices said the device’s RAM and UFS storage account for half of the total hardware cost, but after years of development, they decided not to hold back now.
“Instead of waiting for perfect market conditions or raising the price, we’re moving forward with the Kickstarter campaign,” reads the press release Flipper Devices shared with BleepingComputer.
“We aim to make Flipper One as accessible as possible without building a profit margin into the hardware's price.”
Flipper One is a portable computer that runs a Debian-based system called Flipper OS, and features built-in Wi-Fi 6E, Gigabit Ethernet, and an eSIM.
It can manage up to five network uplinks, act as a hotspot, run VPNs and firewalls, and connect to an external display over USB-C.
It is a separate product from Flipper Zero, focused on IP networking and on-device computing.
In May, Flipper Devices asked the community to help build it, highlighting the project's complexity and listing several ambitious goals.
Development appears to have progressed at a good pace since then, and the company is now preparing to build the first actual units of the Flipper One.
The company says that Flipper One already runs on the mainline Linux kernel, while USB-C DisplayPort patches are under review for inclusion in the mainline Linux.
Work continues on memory power efficiency and removing the remaining closed-source boot components.
The campaign also offers LoRa (support for MeshCore, Meshtastic, Reticulum, and walkie-talkie mode), a 5G modem, and software-defined radio expansion modules (full specifications to be disclosed).
The Kickstarter campaign for Flipper One will run between November 17 and December 17, with the goal of funding the first production run.
Device shipment for the first buyers is estimated for July 2027, and the package will include the device and a USB-C cable.
Flipper Devices also relied on Kickstarter to launch its iconic Flipper Zero in 2020, since then selling over 1 million devices.
Flipper Zero reached its funding goal within eight minutes and raised $4.8 million from crowdfunding in 30 days.
