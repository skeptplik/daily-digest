Single check-in for those flying through Hong Kong from mainland China under new scheme
Those travelling from Hangzhou or Wuhan can have bags checked through to final destination overseas under new one-stop service launched on Tuesday
Travellers flying from Hangzhou or Wuhan to overseas destinations via Hong Kong on different airlines can have their baggage checked through to the final destination under a newly launched service that aims to step up the connections between mainland Chinese cities and other countries.
Under the new one-stop service that launched on Tuesday, travellers departing from Hangzhou Xiaoshan International Airport or Wuhan Tianhe International Airport will not need to check in their baggage again when transferring through Hong Kong, even if they are flying on a different airline on the next leg of their journey.
“Hong Kong International Airport is launching a game-changing service,” Secretary for Transport and Logistics Mable Chan said.
