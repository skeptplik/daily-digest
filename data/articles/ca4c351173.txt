Jennifer Lopez sure knows how to make an entrance. Last night, the Grammy-nominated singer appeared on the stage of New York City’s Metropolitan Museum of Art atop the shoulders of two dancers, and proceeded to deliver an intimate performance.
Merging classic Old Hollywood glamour with her quintessential pop-star look, the artist paired a slinky metallic dress and fishnet tights with a dramatic black fur stole and a crystal headpiece reminiscent of a 1920s flapper cap. As Lopez moved, the gown was revealed to have a corset bodice and gathered skirt, cinched with a bejeweled brooch.
Later, Lopez stripped down into a shimmering, skintight fishnet catsuit designed with a statement halter neckline and a barely-there nude leotard underneath. The built-in choker necklace was embellished with chunky crystals, which appeared to cascade down the catsuit’s bodice by trimming the hemline. The jewels were the perfect match to the singer’s long, dangly earrings, crafted from strands of crystal fringe that danced as she did. (They were also the perfect match to her bedazzled microphone.)
It was in this second look that the star debuted a new song, “Proof of Love,” which was accompanied by hits such as “On the Floor,” “Waiting for Tonight,” and “Love Don’t Cost a Thing.” Throughout it all, Lopez’s red-bottomed lace-up Christian Louboutin boots grounded her on-stage looks with a bit of edge, while her long wavy locks made for some theatrical hairography.
The performance took place during an event for financial technology company Bilt, which provides members with exclusive rewards and events (like surprise mini concerts).
