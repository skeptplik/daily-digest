Le Pen party leaks: prefect dismissed, Paris deputy prosecutor placed under investigation
Issued on:
Share
Two senior figures in France have been caught up in an investigation into suspected leaks to the far-right party of Marine Le Pen, the National Rally. The prefect of the Loiret, Hugues Moutouh, a former top official at the Interior Ministry, has been removed from his post after being suspected of passing on confidential information about investigations. And a deputy prosecutor in Paris, who was found at the party's headquarters during a police search, is now under administrative inquiry. FRANCE 24's Camille Knight reports.
