How many times a month do you think your average Briton has sex?
Do you think that number has gone up or down since the 1990s?
What about sexual partners … how many do you think British people have these days, over their lifetime?
Helen Pidd speaks with Dr Karen Gurney and Prof Kirstin Mitchell about the National Survey of Sexual Attitudes and Lifestyles, one of the largest and most detailed scientific studies of sexual behaviour in the world.
The results give a fascinating snapshot of what we all get up to in our bedrooms, and the answer is, increasingly … not that much.
