'We are not fighting culture, we fight to end FGM: It's a human rights violation of women and girls'
Issued on: Modified:
Share
“We fight to end FGM because it is a human rights violation of women and girls. We are not fighting culture.” Caroline Lagat, Legal Advisor at Equality Now’s Africa office in Nairobi, discusses the challenges of ending female genital mutilation (FGM) and child marriage. She points to insufficient funding, conflicting marriage laws and growing community resistance to campaigns against FGM. While acknowledging some progress, Lagat calls on the government to be "the face of the campaign to end FGM and child marriage.”
