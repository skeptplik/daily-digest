Player of the match Stacey Waaka speaks after New Zealand's semfinal win
A tremendous outing for Stacey Waaka.
She scored three tries in the semifinal, and now has a chance to add the Rugby League World Cup to her list of accomplishments.
Waaka has already won two Olympic gold medals in rugby 7s and two women's Rugby Union World Cups for New Zealand.
"A really close first half, but the team who was going to win was the one who played right to the end, then I think we done that," Waaka told the 7 Network post-match
"I love playing games with these young guns. It's fun learning off them too, because they've been in the game a little bit longer, but I'm just enjoying the whole experience.
"I can't wait for this next week and bring on whoever is in the final with us."

VALE — Paul Langmack
The rugby league world lost one of it's great people this week.
Paul Langmack, a man who won three premierships with Canterbury in the 1980s, and dedicated so much time in later years to running mental health programs, died this week aged 61.
