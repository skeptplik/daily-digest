Dezeen Events Guide has launched its guide to the 2026 edition of Miami art week, which is sponsored by colour specialist Pantone.
The festival, which runs from 30 November to 6 December 2026, takes place across Miami, in neighbourhoods such as the Miami Design District and Miami Beach.
The event includes fairs, exhibitions, installations, open showrooms, talks, launches, pop-up shops and parties throughout the week.
Highlights include the Design Miami and Art Basel Miami Beach fairs, alongside satellite fairs NADA Miami, Art Miami and Fridge Art Fair.
