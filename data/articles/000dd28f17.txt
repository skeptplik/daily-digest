The GTA 6 airport, Vice City International, has a huge footprint on the map. You really can't miss it once you get into the city and start wandering around. There are two main runways that both have commercial jets parked in them at all times which you can sneak your way onto and fly off with if you throw it in reverse before those tarmac support vehicles get under your wheels.
What's at the airport?
Other than tons of jets, there aren't really a lot of map icons for specific activities at VCIA. It's a big campus, so there's lots of exploring, but not a very structured to-do list. After running off with jets and maintenance vehicles, you can also break into a ton of ATMs at the central terminal part of the airport.
