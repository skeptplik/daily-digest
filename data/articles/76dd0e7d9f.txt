The LEGO Koenigsegg Jesko Absolut Grey Hypercar, like many sets before it, is retiring at the end of the calendar year. Typically, however, LEGO runs out of inventory weeks or even months before the official retirement date. The Koenigsegg Jesko Absolut Grey Hypercar, for example, is sold out on the official LEGO store. But it is available at Walmart, and you can purchase it for $24.99 – a full 50% off the retail price of $49.99.
LEGO Technic Koenigsegg Jesko Absolut Gray Hypercar
It's a LEGO Technic build, which means very few bricks, but lots of rods, pins, and plates, The hypercar comes with several hidden features: a movable V8 engine; a differential; steering via a knob at the top of the model; and doors that open upwards 90 degrees.
This is likely not the only, soon-to-be-retired LEGO set that will experience this sort of third-party markdown over the next month. LEGO pushes a lot of new inventory at the start of a new year, and the retail stores will want to clear their shelf space for new inventory. So if you look on the official LEGO Store and see that something is sold out? Check your local Target, Barnes & Noble, or Walmart instead. You might get lucky.
