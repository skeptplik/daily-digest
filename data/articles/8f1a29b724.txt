Paris/London – A convoy of Russian ships carrying military vehicles bound for Libya has turned around in the Mediterranean after Ukrainian maritime drones sank a Russian naval corvette in Benghazi port on Nov. 22, three people from military and security services said Friday.
The convoy’s diversion, which has not been previously reported, shows how Russia is acting to protect its naval assets following a Ukrainian sea drone attack that sank a Russian warship in Libya some 2,000 kilometers from the battlefield in Ukraine.
The convoy, which was escorted by a Russian navy frigate, Golovko, turned around near the Strait of Sicily on the same day as the attack in Benghazi, a European military official, who had been tracking the convoy, said.
