Film festivals tend to follow a familiar pattern: Audiences watch a screening, applaud and chat among themselves, then move on. The Japan Prize takes a different approach, making discussion an integral part of the experience.
At public broadcaster NHK’s international competition celebrating educational content from around the world, filmmakers, journalists, producers, animators, educators and members of the public watch selected works together. After each screening, they gather around discussion tables to exchange views. Comments and questions are submitted and displayed on stage for the creators to address. This year’s event ran from Nov. 16 to Nov. 19 at With Harajuku Hall in Tokyo’s Shibuya Ward.
The format gives attendees an opportunity to engage directly with creators, who in turn hear how their work resonates with people from different backgrounds and professional fields. That exchange is one of the reasons both audiences and creators keep coming back.
