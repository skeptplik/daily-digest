Hailee Steinfeld knows that when something works, it’s worth trying it again. The “Sinners” star rewore a pair of Fendi shoes she recently wore in Los Angeles when she was spotted outside BBC Radio Studios promoting her new movie “Hexed” in London on Friday.
Steinfeld has taken a liking to the new Fendi Very Colibrì slingback heels. The black patent calf leather style is a high-heeled slingback that boasts a streamlined and feminine design, which includes narrow straps in elastic webbing and leather that wrap around the foot.

They also feature a small, hiking-shoe-inspired padded cushion and a mini sideways tab at the back of the ankle, adding a sporty touch to the otherwise elegant heel. The slightly flared structural heel has a wider base than a traditional stiletto and measures 95 mm, or a modest 3.7 inches. The pumps have a pointed toe, V-shaped vamp and thin elastic and leather straps that wrap around the front of the foot to hold up the slingback strap.
This shoe style was paired with black socks on the runway. Steinfeld followed suit and also wore them with socks. The design retails for around $1,050 through Fendi and Saks Fifth Avenue.
For the appearance, the actress wore the heels with a sheer floral lace skirt, a white mock-neck button-down shirt and a black peacoat. The look was a bit more toned down than the ensemble she originally wore the socks and pumps combo with.
Earlier this month, Steinfeld attended the world premiere of “Hexed” at the El Capitan Theatre on in Los Angeles wearing a black macramé leather dress from the Fendi fall 2026 collection.
The Fendi dress is crafted from black lambskin with matching full-grain leather details. The garment features a fitted waist, a flared skirt, a shirt collar and long sleeves with a zip at the hem, along with a visible front zip fastening to the waist and palladium-finish metalware.
