New York – Major League Baseball team owners will not agree to a new deal with the players union that does not include a salary cap, MLB Commissioner Rob Manfred said on Wednesday.
With the MLB Players Association calling such terms a nonstarter, the stage is set for a lockout that could jeopardize the 2027 season when the current collective bargaining agreement expires Dec. 1.
“I can’t imagine the owners ratifying a deal that does not include a cap and floor,” Manfred said at MLB’s team owners’ meetings.
