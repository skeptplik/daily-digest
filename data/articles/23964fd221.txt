The Nikkei 225 stock index has been trading in or near bear market territory for a week and is not expected to recover its highs until artificial intelligence and data center optimism returns.
It declined 6% on Nov. 6 to close at 57,434.77, down more than 20% from a record 72,366.34 hit on June 25. A 20% fall is considered to be a technical bear market, though analysts and academics warn that a decline alone does not make a true bear market.
A landslide victory in U.S. midterm elections by the Democratic Party earlier this month rattled investors, stoking fears of a slowdown in data center construction given that many candidates pledged stricter regulations for these projects.
