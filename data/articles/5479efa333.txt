Simon Property Group went big for its holiday campaign, which launched on Thursday.
And the nation’s largest owner, operator and developer of shopping and mixed-use centers is sticking to to its theme, integrating the campaign into the “It’s a Simon Thing” brand platform, which launched in September.
“‘It’s a Simon Thing’ can be reused and remixed for the season,” Lee Sterling, chief marketing officer at Simon, told WWD. “So the fall campaign was our launch. It featured a CTV (connected TV) spot and an extensive social campaign. It ended up being the most engaged social campaign we’ve ever had with very strong engagement in terms of comments, likes, shares overall. And what we’ve now launched is the holiday version of it. So there’s a new CTV spot all about holiday moments, traditions, memories that happen in our properties, and a corresponding social campaign.”
The holiday campaign also appears in an out-of-home campaign at all Simon properties, on Simon’s website and on Simon’s owned social channels. The campaign showcases many of the retail brands operating at Simon properties.
Sterling said some of the social series launched in the fall have a holiday iteration. “‘Mic’d Up @TheMall,’ the man-on-the-street interview series, was one of our most popular social segments and has been remixed for the holiday.” The segment, which features people going back over their holiday memories, was filmed at various Simon properties, including Woodbury Common in Central Valley, N.Y., and the Houston Galleria. The holiday campaign is set to the song “Holiday” by Vampire Weekend.
Last fall, when the “It’s a Simon Thing” campaign and tagline were introduced, the Simon name was sometimes omitted and replaced with another word or words, creating different slogans with the same tone. “You’ll remember we used lines like ‘It’s a discovery thing’ or, ‘It’s a together thing.’ So those have been evolved to, for example, ‘It’s a very merry thing’ or ‘It’s a Santa thing’ and it all kind of leads up to ‘It’s a Simon Thing.’
“The holidays are all about being together, and people are craving real connection now more than ever,” said Sterling. “For generations, the mall has been a third place [followed by home and work] where families and friends have shared the season, from a special visit with Santa to that annual trip to find the perfect gifts. This season, and always, we’re celebrating those traditions and the new ones being made every day.”
Also as part of the campaign, Simon’s original content series @The Mall and @TheOutlets on social media return with holiday editions, including documentary-style takes on early bird shoppers and dads waiting by the fountain.
Simon also campaigns with a strong value message.
“It’s always an emphasis this time of year, so this year it’s comparable to where we’ve been in the past,” said Sterling. “We keep enhancing the loyalty program, with new offers and new rewards. Some of the rewards that we introduced this holiday include complimentary valet parking and upfront parking at select properties if you have 500 points.”
Sterling said the platform “will live on beyond into 2027, but the holiday-focused content piece of it will run right through Christmas, and even the week after Christmas, which is important to us as well.”
On Nov. 27, Simon teams up with MrBeast Feastables, giving members of the Simon+ loyalty program limited-edition Feastables Creamy Peanut Butter Snowman Cups at 95 Simon centers. Scanning the QR code on the product connects shoppers to The Feastables $10,000 Dash Contest, where shoppers can submit a plan to spend the $10K in 25 minutes at one of three participating Simon centers. Whoever submits the best plan will get to attempt that shopping spree with travel provided. Feastables is a global snacking company founded by creator Jimmy Donaldson as a force for good. By working with farmers in cocoa-growing communities throughout West Africa, Feastables aims to revolutionize the chocolate snacking industry by helping kids get off working farms in hard labor and into local schools.
