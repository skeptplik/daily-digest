From beachfront resorts to skyscraper hotels overlooking the Arabian Gulf, Qatar is home to a growing portfolio of luxury properties.
When Qatar hosted the FIFA World Cup in 2022 it wasn’t just its sporting infrastructure that got an upgrade – but also its collection of hotels and resorts.
Now, Qatar’s luxury hotel portfolio has grown with five-star properties extending from the centre of the capital city Doha to the country’s islands and coastline.
For travellers seeking a high-end stay, the choice extends beyond location, with properties offering everything from private beaches and spas to fine dining and stunning views of the Arabian Gulf.
Looking for a five-star stay in Qatar? Here are some of the best luxury hotels and resorts to consider in Doha and Lusail.
The Chedi Katara Hotel & Resort
Opened in November 2022 in Katara Cultural Village, The Chedi has 91 rooms, suites, and villas with Mughal- and Ottoman-style interiors and sea views. Each of its 32 villas has a private garden and pool.
It features a private beach with water sports and two pools – a 60-metre-long infinity pool and a 35 metre family pool. Its Eastern-inspired spa includes a couples’ room and separate hammams for men and women.
Dining options include overwater restaurant La Marsa, which is reached by jetty.
Fairmont Doha
Fairmont Doha shares the landmark Katara Towers with its sister property, the Raffles Doha, in Lusail Marina. Both opened in November 2022 for FIFA World Cup guests and VIPs.
The hotel’s 362 rooms and suites take inspiration from sleek megayachts, while a 57-metre “crystal swirl” chandelier runs through the lobby.
For active travellers, there’s Fairmont Fit and a climbing wall. For a more relaxed stay, Fairmont Spa features nine treatment rooms and a traditional hammam. There is also a main outdoor pool, a 25-metre indoor lap pool and a private pool for Fairmont Gold guests.
The hotel offers five dining options, including Provok, a pan-Asian restaurant with sweeping rooftop views.
Four Seasons Hotel Doha
On the Corniche in West Bay, the Four Seasons Hotel Doha originally opened in April 2005. After a complete interior design overhaul by French designer Pierre-Yves Rochon, it reopened in 2020 with renovated guest rooms, suites, floors, lobby and dining outlets.
As well as 237 rooms and suites, the hotel has a private beach and five pools, including grotto pools and pools with slides. Its three-storey spa and wellness centre has 11 treatment rooms and a large hydrotherapy area with an ice room.
Nobu Doha, one of the hotel’s culinary highlights, is located on a private peninsula overlooking the Arabian Gulf and Doha skyline.
Four Seasons Resort & Residences at The Pearl-Qatar
Opened in November 2023 on The Pearl island, the second Four Seasons property in Qatar combines a beachfront resort with private accommodation. It has 161 guest rooms and 84 private residences.
The resort has a private beach and three pools, including a 25-metre indoor pool. Guests can also use the luxury Loulou spa, with aqua-thermal facilities, while younger guests have access to the Kids for All Seasons programme, with activities for children aged 4-12.
Dining options include Chicago Rare, a modern steakhouse with views of the Gulf and live jazz.
Mandarin Oriental, Doha
Mandarin Oriental, Doha opened in March 2019 in the heart of Msheireb Downtown, with the popular Souq Waqif just a short walk away.
It includes 117 rooms, 41 suites and 91 serviced apartments, offering views of Barahat Msheireb town square and the city landscape.
The hotel has two rooftop pools, including a 25-metre lap pool. Its spa offers a signature “Singing Sand Dunes” treatment inspired by the desert dunes in Qatar.
Dining facilities include Mosaic, which has nine open kitchens serving dishes inspired by the Silk Route.
Marsa Malaz Kempinski, The Pearl-Doha
Opened in December 2014 on its own private island at The Pearl, Marsa Malaz Kempinski blends palatial Arabian and European design across its 281 rooms and suites. It is also just a short walk from Qanat Quartier’s colourful buildings and canals.
Guests can take part in water sports from the private beach and jetty, while children have their own pool and the Titan Tikes playroom. The outdoor pool area also has a large whirlpool, while the Marsa Malaz Spa offers a private indoor pool.
Dining options include the Doha outpost of Harry's Dolce Vita, an Italian restaurant that serves classic dishes.
The Ned Doha
Located in the former Ministry of Interior building on Doha’s Corniche, The Ned Doha opened in 2022. Its 90 rooms and suites feature 1970s-inspired interiors, with balconies overlooking the Arabian Gulf or neighbouring Al Bidda Park.
Ned's Club includes a health club with a spa, a traditional Moroccan hammam and a 30 metre heated outdoor pool, while the hotel's central atrium hosts live performances.
Millie’s Lounge, one of several restaurants and bars, has curved booths and views of Doha's skyline, while Electric Diner is styled as a 1950s American diner.
Raffles Doha
Raffles Doha opened in November 2022 in Lusail’s landmark Katara Towers, whose curved design is inspired by Qatar’s crossed swords.
The hotel has 132 suites, each with dedicated butler service. The hotel’s 55-metre outdoor pool has private cabanas, while Raffles Spa has five private spa suites, each with its own hammam, jacuzzi, outdoor pool and treatment space.
Dining includes ALBA, an Italian restaurant that earned one star in the 2026 Michelin Guide Doha.
The Ritz-Carlton, Doha
Set on its own private island between The Pearl and Lusail, The Ritz-Carlton Doha has been a mainstay in the hotel scene since 2001.
It has 374 rooms and suites, with a beach offering watersports and cabanas. The resort also has a 25-metre indoor pool and active guests can use the two indoor tennis courts and a squash court.
Families can explore the Ritz Kids garden, where children can learn about local flora and sustainable gardening.
Dining includes STK Doha, a modern steakhouse with skyline views that also hosts the 3D Le Petit Chef experience.
Rosewood Doha
Officially opened in January 2026, Rosewood Doha is just steps away from Lusail's marina and boardwalk. The hotel offers 155 rooms and suites, 162 apartments and 276 residences, with the Reef Suite offering a butler service.
Asaya, a wellness club open to members, hotel guests and residents, features anti-gravity heated loungers and a padel court, along with a 25-metre lap pool and a family pool with a dedicated children's area.
Koo Madame, inspired by Madame Wellington Koo, a former First Lady of the Republic of China, is one of eight dining destinations at the hotel. There’s also The Butterfly Room, a dedicated tea lounge.
The St. Regis Doha
Personal butler service is part of every stay at The St. Regis Doha, regardless of room category. The West Bay hotel has 335 rooms and suites, with views across the Arabian Gulf or The Pearl.
The hotel has a private beach that offers a range of water sports and an outdoor Olympic-sized pool, with private poolside cabanas that include their own plunge pools, kitchenette, and flat-screen TVs.
Dining includes steakhouse Astor Grill and Sarab Lounge, known for its English afternoon tea with live music.
Waldorf Astoria Doha Lusail
Opened for the 2022 FIFA World Cup, Waldorf Astoria Doha sits on a private beach in Lusail, with 429 rooms, suites and apartments.
There’s a waterpark and Aloha Surf Club, which has a dedicated surf pool. The resort also has three swimming pools, including one for families. Children and teenagers have their own clubs, while the three-storey ESPA Life Spa features a private indoor pool, thermal facilities and a hammam.
The hotel’s F&B options include SUSHISAMBA, which has a beach club with a private pool surrounded by cocoon-style cabanas.
