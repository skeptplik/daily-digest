Chinese twin sisters reunite after 20 years thanks to chance sighting in short video
Proving they are biologically related requires DNA testing with their birth parents, whom they have been unable to find
A chance discovery on a short-video-sharing platform reunited Chinese twin sisters 20 years after they were separated at birth.
Recently, 20-year-old Shenzhen resident Luo posted a video documenting her reunion with an identical twin sister she had never known about.
While scrolling through Douyin, Luo’s cousin came across an account belonging to a woman surnamed Xia whose face bore a striking resemblance to Luo’s.
