Does anyone mind if we turn back the clock to 2012 for a sec?
Last night in New York, much of the original cast of The Hunger Games series reunited at the premiere of the prequel, Sunrise on the Reaping. Jennifer Lawrence and Josh Hutcherson, who will reprise their respective roles of Katniss and Peeta, were joined by their erstwhile castmates—Woody Harrelson, Elizabeth Banks, Amandla Stenberg, and Willow Shields (not to mention those who starred in various sequels and spin-offs).
All this nostalgia has us wondering: what was the 2012 carpet like, again? Well, they’re certainly not the method dressing spectacle they are now. At the Los Angeles premiere, a 21-year-old Lawrence dressed the part of an ingenue in a gold lamé Prabal Gurung mermaid gown with a back cutout, while Hutcherson looked gentlemanly in a three-piece charcoal suit.
As for other members of the cast, Banks channeled Effie’s extravagance in a tangerine Atelier Versace one-shoulder minidress that matched the late, great Donald Sutherland’s sweater (but lest we lose sight of his power-clashing stripes!). Meanwhile, 13-year-old Stenberg looked perfectly age-appropriate in a sweet green ombré sequin dress from Collette Dinnigan.
There were plenty of other familiar faces on this carpet of yore: Miley Cyrus, Kendall and Kylie Jenner, and Maude Apatow among them. Here, revisit the 2012 Los Angeles premiere of The Hunger Games.
