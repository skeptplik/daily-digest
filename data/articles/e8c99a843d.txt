Hong Kong charity helps neurodivergent people build confidence through nutrition, dance
With funding from Operation Santa Claus, Love 21 offers tailored nutrition programmes and stages performances to raise awareness and fight stigma
For 33-year-old Coco Choi Pak-yin and 24-year-old Crystal Lee Wing-wai, few things bring more joy than dancing at the Hong Kong charity Love 21 Foundation.
As neurodivergent individuals, they take part in a variety of tailored nutrition and sports programmes offered by the organisation, including dance classes. Through dance, they have found a space to connect with others and express themselves, and gained the confidence to showcase their talent in public.
“Compared with before, she is much more confident and outgoing now,” Crystal’s father, Lee Tak-wai, said.
