With all due respect to tournament co-leader Wakamotoharu, the story of the Kyushu Basho — after 10 days of action — has been rising star Atamifuji.
The sekiwake is off to his best start in over three years and, with consecutive wins over yokozuna, is in prime position to claim his first Emperor’s Cup, and possibly even earn promotion to ōzeki.
Any doubts that lingered from the September meet, where an 8-2 Atamifuji collapsed down the home stretch, losing his last five bouts, were dispelled by the 24-year-old’s demolition of yokozuna Onosato on Tuesday.
