Washington – Professional wrestling star Jermaine Haley, known to his fans as Bronson Reed, has died aged 38, World Wrestling Entertainment (WWE) said Friday.
His death comes weeks after he competed in a match against Oba Femi at the Crown Jewel event in Saudi Arabia’s capital.
WWE said it was “devastated to announce that Jermaine Haley, also known as Bronson Reed, passed away earlier today at the age of 38 due to medical complications,” in an X post. It did not provide further details.
The company paid tribute to Reed, calling him a “larger-than-life figure in WWE.”
“But more importantly, he was a phenomenal person, a devoted family man, and a beloved colleague,” it added.
Australian-born Reed had been described as a “powerhouse” in the arena and made his WWE debut in 2019, according to the company’s website.
He was said to combine “raw strength with surprising agility to overwhelm his opponents.”
Reed is a former NXT North American Champion and also won the André the Giant Memorial Battle Royal in 2024.
