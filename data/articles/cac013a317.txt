Malin Akerman may stir up romantic trouble as Margo Banks, the notorious ringleader of The Hunting Wives, but off screen, her love life is much less chaotic. The actress has been happily married to fellow actor Jack Donnelly since 2018, and over the years, the two have even found ways to merge their professional and personal lives.
Akerman and Donnelly have collaborated on several projects, including four movies (and an NSFW sex scene, but more on that below). In a 2023 interview with People, the actress opened up about their relationship, crediting her husband with helping her become a better communicator.
“He’s allowed me to be vulnerable and lean into him a bit, and it’s been beautiful to be able to communicate with someone who’s your partner,” she said of their marriage.
Ahead, everything you need to know about Jack Donnelly, his career, and his marriage with Akerman.
He’s a British actor.
Donnelly was born in Bournemouth, England, on October 28, 1985, and later attended the Central School of Speech and Drama in London. He made his acting debut in 2011 on the Nickelodeon series House of Anubis. Two years later, he landed a breakout lead role in the BBC’s series Atlantis, as Jason—which remains one of his best-known roles to date.
He’s also a writer and director.
In addition to acting, Donnelly has branched out behind the camera as a television writer and director. He wrote, directed, and starred in the short comedy series The Players, which debuted in 2023.
He met Malin through her sister.
The couple’s love story began with a family connection. Donnelly first met Akerman through her younger sister, whose boyfriend was friends with him at drama school. During a 2018 appearance on Live With Kelly and Ryan, the actress explained that she had actually known Donnelly for several years before their relationship turned romantic.
“I had known him for the past four years but didn’t see him,” she recalled. “Then all of a sudden he came back from England like a year-and-a-half ago, and I went, ‘Wow, have you always been this cute?’”
The couple got married in Tulum.
Akerman announced the couple’s engagement via Instagram in October 2017. Sharing a selfie with Donnelly and flashing her new engagement ring, she captioned the post, “This sweet and loving man has stepped into our lives and stole both our hearts,” she wrote, referring to her and her son. “I think we’re gonna keep him around for a while,” she continued with a wink.
Akerman and Donnelly tied the knot a little over a year later, on December 1, 2018, in a beach ceremony held in Tulum, Mexico. “It was all about being authentic...not adhering to somebody else’s standard of what a wedding should be,” Akerman told Brides.
“We wanted something beautiful and lush,” she continued, sharing more about their wedding venue. “When we visited Papaya Playa Project [in Tulum], we fell in love with the boho vibes and beach setting.”
In 2023, Akerman shared new photos from the beachside wedding on Instagram to celebrate their five-year anniversary.
He’s a stepfather to Malin’s son.
When discussing her engagement on Live With Kelly and Ryan in 2018, Akerman also shared how excited her son, Sebastian, was about the news. The actress shares Sebastian with her ex-husband, musician Roberto Zincone, and recalled his excited reaction when she told him that she and Donnelly were getting married. “He jumped up on the couch and went, ‘Yay, we’re getting married!’” she said.
In another 2018 interview with Us Weekly, Akerman gushed about the close bond Donnelly had already formed with her then-five-year-old son. “They’re inseparable,” she said. “It makes my heart warm. I’m very glad to be bringing Jack into our lives for good.”
Over the years, the three have also made several red-carpet appearances together, offering a glimpse into their life as a family.
They’ve worked together on several movies.
Akerman and Donnelly have shared the screen in four films so far: Friendsgiving, A Week in Paradise, Slayers, and Us or Them. Their first movie together was the 2020 comedy Friendsgiving, in which they played a couple—and even had to film a steamy sex scene. During a 2020 appearance on The Talk, Akerman opened up about filming the “crazy S&M scene.”
“[It was] just like being in our home,” she joked when asked what it was like filming the sex scene. She added that she was glad to have her husband as her costar. “Any chance I get to have him do a scene like that, who better than to do it with than your husband?” she said. “It’s a really, kind of, intimate, crazy scene, and thank God we know each other well enough instead of just meeting somebody on the first day and [doing] this crazy S&M scene. It was lovely to have him there.”
He’s part of “The Hunting Husbands.”
During a November 2026 appearance on The Drew Barrymore Show, Akerman and her The Hunting Wives costar Brittany Snow discussed Snow’s recent engagement to fiancé Hunter Moreno. When Barrymore asked if their significant others got along too, Akerman gushed, “They’re so close!”
“We call them all the Hunting Husbands,” Akerman added, sharing their nickname for all the boyfriends and husbands of the cast.
