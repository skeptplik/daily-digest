If you're normal, you probably never think about the Steam community market. If you're like me, you only touch it to sell the mountains of trading cards you get from idling. If you're a true lunatic, you're running a roaring trade in Counter-Strike 2 skins and are either unfathomably rich or trapped in a life of penury.
Either way, some news: Valve has been tinkering at the Steam marketplace again, and in an update yesterday announced a rebuilt market home page that's slightly more pleasant to handle and look at, while being more personalised to the games you're actually playing. This is in addition to a set of changes to the market Valve released (in beta) back in May.
The new market home is, says Valve, faster to load up, but it's not just a new gloss of paint. It's swapped out the pre-update Popular Items section—which mostly just showed a list of CS2 crates—for new Trending Items lists that show the popular market items for games you've actually been playing. When I look at it, I see Path of Exile, Warframe, Crisis In The Kremlin, and, uh, Dota 2. I've never touched Dota 2 in my life, so not sure what's happening there, but you get the idea.
You've also now got a watchlist that will let you keep track of various market items across multiple games. You can save specific items, a general item type, or even a particular search. I imagine this will be useful to those of you who play the Steam market like an '80s Wall Street guy shouting into a big phone.
The biggest change for me personally—that's why people read the news, for the biggest changes for me personally—is streamlined selling and relisting. You can now change the price of items you have listed for sale without laboriously taking them down and relisting them. You can just update the price tag and have done with it. Similarly, if you're selling directly from the Steam mobile app, you'll be able to approve those sales via a pop-up rather than navigating to the confirmation page. This is truly huge news for the weirdest guys you know (me).
All that aside, the market home page will now also track your in-progress badges, and search results now have a list view. There are a few more new things, but Valve confined them to an 'and finally' set of bulletpoints, so here they are in their original bulletpoint form.
- My Listings and History are now on a dedicated page, with a summary on the Market Home page
- "Trending", or items that are newly popular, is now a sort option for results and buy/sell listings
- Updated Sell Dialog with the newer graphs, with improved usability on mobile devices
- Counter-Strike 2 containers show and link to their possible contents
- Sort by Popularity and Sort by Price return better results, faster
- All Market pages load faster and are more responsive
