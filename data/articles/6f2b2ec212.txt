Magic: The Gathering has seen crossovers with Marvel Super Heroes, The Hobbit, and Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles this year alone, but there’s more to come with the final set of 2026: Star Trek.
Taking the long-running card game back to space after last year’s Edge of Eternities, we recently spotted that its Play Booster Box had been discounted heavily before it even launched this week. And yet, just days before it beams onto tabletop matches worldwide, it’s just got another price cut.
Now, though, Amazon has chipped another few bucks off the price, bringing it down to $124.95 — a big 40% drop from the MSRP of $209.70 you’d usually need to fork out for with a Universes Beyond set.
Save Even More On Magic’s Newest Set, Star Trek
In terms of pricing per pack, this shakes out at around $4.17 for each Play Booster. That’s a great saving on the MSRP, which would have been $6.99 per pack.
As ever, we need to highlight that you’re much less likely to pack some of the set’s rarest cards in Play Boosters because many of the alternative art treatments are in Collector Boosters.
These are more likely to contain cards worth a lot more, but they retail for around $40 per pack if you can find them, and even then, there’s a good chance they sell for even more than that.
If you’re a Star Trek fan just getting into Magic: The Gathering, this is a great price for a whole host of cards to build a deck with, or to upgrade one of your Commander precons from the set.
