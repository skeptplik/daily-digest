Cate Blanchett lights up Champs-Élysées as Christmas market returns

Australian actress Cate Blanchett joined Paris's mayor for the official switch on of the Christmas lights along the Champs-Élysées on Sunday. This year also sees the return of a Christmas market on the famous Paris avenue for the first time in nearly 10 years.
