North Melbourne claims historic three-peat after downing Melbourne in AFLW grand final
North Melbourne has become the first club to claim three straight AFLW premierships after downing Melbourne in a thrilling grand final.
The Kangaroos were pushed to the limit in wet conditions at Princes Park, but kicked the last three goals of the match inside the final five minutes to run out 6.8 (44) to 2.6 (18) winners.
Kangaroos skipper Jasmine Garner was named Best on Ground after finishing with 26 disposals and five clearances to go along with a goal.
North came out of the gates kicking the first three goals of the game, but were pegged back when the Dees kicked the next two to cut the margin to six points at half-time.
Tayla Harris was inspirational for Melbourne in the second quarter, kicking her team's first goal of the match after taking a towering contested mark inside 50.
With rain increasing after the main break, the second half turned into a dour slugfest as both teams held each other goalless in the third quarter.
The Kangaroos were forced to defend grimly for much of the final quarter as the Demons drew to within one goal with five minutes remaining, but proved to be too strong in the end, with Bella Eddey's final quarter goal proving to be the eventual backbreaker.
North forward Blaithin Bogue was the only multiple goalscorer of the match, kicking both of her goals in the final few minutes to seal the premiership win.
