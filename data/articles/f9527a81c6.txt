Judo star Teddy Riner announces bid for French presidency
French judo star Teddy Riner announced on Monday that he would run in next year's high-stakes presidential election.
France's five-time Olympic judo champion Teddy Riner announced on Monday that he would run in the country's high-stakes presidential election next year.
"My name is Teddy Riner and today, I am telling you face to face: I am a candidate in the 2027 presidential election," the 37-year-old athlete said in the surprise announcement on social media.
