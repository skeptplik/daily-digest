Last night, awards season kicked off with the 2026 Governors Awards, an annual gala hosted by the Academy of Motion Picture Arts and Sciences, which acts as a precursor to the Oscars and includes the presentation of three lifetime achievement awards: the Academy Honorary Award, the Jean Hersholt Humanitarian Award, and the Irving G. Thalberg Memorial Award.
To celebrate these special honors, many of Hollywood’s biggest stars united at the Dolby Theatre in Los Angeles. The guest list included A-listers like Julianne Moore, Charlize Theron, Timothée Chalamet, A$AP Rocky, Penélope Cruz, and Florence Pugh.
And a few of the attendees chose to turn the evening into a date night. While Kylie Jenner joined Timothée Chalmet inside the ceremony rather than walk the carpet with him, The Gilded Age’s Carrie Coon did walk the carpet alongside her husband, actor and playwright Tracy Letts, while Matt Damon brought along his wife, Luciana Barroso. Emma Stone also made a rare public outing with her husband, Dave McCary.
But, perhaps one of the most high-profile couples present on the carpet was Zendaya and Tom Holland, who chose to walk the carpet separately. Throughout the years, these two have made very few public appearances together, usually only tied to their joint work in the Spider-Man franchise. In the past, Holland has even noted that he tends to avoid walking carpets for Zendaya’s movies, because “it’s not my moment, it’s her moment, and if we go together, it’s about us.”
It’s always about them.
