Japan’s longest-serving coach, Hajime Moriyasu, leaves a mixed legacy after the curtain fell on his eight-year reign, with historic wins offset by a lack of trophies.
Moriyasu led Japan to shock wins over Germany and Spain at the 2022 World Cup in Qatar and he also engineered first victories over Brazil and England in friendlies.
But the 58-year-old was unable to deliver Japan’s first World Cup knockout win in his two tournaments in charge, and he did not lift the Asian Cup in two attempts.
