It’s been 13 years in the making, and may be the most highly anticipated video game of all time. Grand Theft Auto VI has finally arrived.
The new instalment of the epic crime saga is said to have cost more than $1bn to make and is widely expected to break sales records: some predictions put total sales at 40m copies in its first three months.
Its predecessor GTA V was the bestselling game of the past decade in the US, and reached US$1bn in sales faster than any entertainment product in history. GTA VI is predicted to surpass that number with ease.
The game will be available to buy on Thursday from midnight local time, with gamers in New Zealand among the first to officially be able to download it.
