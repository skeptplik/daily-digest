Black Friday is arguably the best time of the year to shop for Apple products. While the tech giant doesn’t run sales of its own, other retailers are offering markdowns during Cyber Week. You’ll find AirPods, MacBooks, Apple Watches, and other Apple products on sale right now. Amazon just kicked off its Black Friday sale, so you don’t need to wait any longer to get a head start on your holiday shopping.
Several Apple products are at their lowest price ever, including the Apple Watch Series 12 and SE 3. If you’re traveling for the holidays, take advantage of the retailer’s deals on Apple AirTags. Individual tags are $23, but the discounted four-pack brings the cost down to an all-time low of $20 apiece. Our tech-obsessed editors are also excited to see the new MacBook Neo on sale for the first time.
Record-Low Apple Deals
AirPods Deals
The new AirPods 5 remain at full price for now, but other Apple headphones are on sale. Currently, you can save over 20 percent on the noise-canceling AirPods Pro 3 and over-the-ear AirPods Max 2. These products could easily sell out before the holiday weekend, as we’ve seen happen in years past. At $15, the corded EarPod headphones are a steal and also make an easy stocking stuffer.
MacBook Deals
If you need a new laptop, now’s a good time to buy one. The 2026 MacBook Pro and 2026 MacBook Air are both on sale during Amazon’s Black Friday sale. We don’t expect prices to drop significantly, if at all, by Cyber Monday, so we don’t recommend waiting to shop.
iPad Deals
Three iPad models—the Pro, Air, and classic 11-inch iPad—are also on sale. The Apple A17 Pro model is currently marked down, while the latest iPad mini is still full price, and we expect it to stay that way. Now’s a good time to shop accessories like chargers and keyboards, too
