Warner Bros. announced on Nov. 25, 2026, that production on the next installment in The Lord of the Rings movie franchise has wrapped. The Hunt for Gollum, directed by Andy Serkis, is currently headed for post-production ahead of the movie's Dec. 17, 2027 release. The news follows Serkis' announcement in November that filming had ended.
In the video shared by Warner Bros., Serkis playfully steps back into the pale, clammy skin of Gollum, the character he played in The Lord of the Rings trilogy and The Hobbit: An Unexpected Journey. Gollum has locked himself (themselves? Does Smeagol count?) in Serkis' trailer and is refusing to open the door while the production crew needs to move the trailer away from the studio lot.
The Hunt for Gollum is an "interquel" to Peter Jackson's The Lord of the Rings trilogy. The events of the new movie take place during The Fellowship of the Ring. After Bilbo's 111th birthday, Gandalf, who has become suspicious of the true nature of the magic ring found by his old hobbit friend during their previous adventure, sends Aragorn to track Gollum, the ring’s previous owner. After searching far and wide, Aragorn finally finds his mark, but not before Gollum is captured and interrogated by Sauron, revealing that the One Ring has been found.
The movie stars Jamie Dornan, who will take over the role of Aragorn from Viggo Mortensen, Elijah Wood and Ian McKellen reprising their roles as Frodo Baggins and Gandalf, respectively, Kate Winslet as Marigol, Leo Woodall as Halvard, and Anya Taylor-Joy as Seren. Serkis will direct and also step back into the motion-capture suit that let him bring Gollum to life for the first time twenty-five years ago, as seen in this first teaser filmed on set.
The Hunt for Gollum has a big challenge ahead
A whole year just for post-production seems like a long time, suggesting that the movie will feature plenty of special effects. Serkis also said that he would focus entirely on promotion for the next year, which reveals just how important this movie is for the franchise. The last live-action Lord of the Rings movie to hit theaters was The Hobbit: The Battle of the Five Armies in 2014, which concluded the critically panned Hobbit trilogy. Peter Jackson's second movie trilogy was commercially profitable but its poor reception among critics and Tolkien fans put a blemish on the franchise. The animated movie The War of the Rohirrim, which premiered in 2024, failed to impress both critics and audiences.
The universe created by J.R.R. Tolkien remains popular despite the reception of its adaptations, as proved by the controversial Rings of Power Prime Video show. The Hunt for Gollum faces the challenge of bringing The Lord of the Rings back to the big screen with a movie that can please longtime fans of the franchise and Tolkien purists while also achieving commercial success. It's a tall order, considering the somewhat inconsequential nature of the source material chosen, but if anyone can pull it off, that's Andy Serkis.
