- November 14, 2026 10:16Hours-long halt to U.S. flights leaves travellers frustrated, anxious Tens of thousands of fliers were forced to change their plans when the FAA issued a ground stop that lasted four hours and led to the cancellations of more than 6,600 flights, with 8,600 delayed, according to FlightRadar24. It was just the third nationwide stop issued by the Federal Aviation Administration this century. Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy blamed the problems not on malicious actors but on a failure of aging network technology in the radar and communications system used for air traffic control. At the Ronald Reagan Washington National Airport, where information displays were almost completely filled with flights marked “Delayed” or “Cancelled,” some travellers appeared resigned as they wondered how long the delays would last and how their travel plans would be affected.
