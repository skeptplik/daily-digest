Fortnite's Chapter 7 mini season, "Treasure Hunters," has added a new collectible to find throughout the season: map fragments. These map fragments will be added periodically during the season, and you'll get a special reward for finding them all.
Finding all six map fragments (which will be added throughout the season in updates) will unlock the "X Marks the Zero Point Sliver" loading screen.
Below, we list where to find all of the map fragments available so far in Fortnite Chapter 7's mini season.
Update (Nov. 8): We've updated this guide with the location of the map fragment added on Lil' Uzi Vert's Captain Day.
Map fragment #1 location in Fortnite
The first map fragment, made available on Nov. 1, is at The $.$. Rocky:
As you get closer to the ship, you should see the usual exclamation-point-inside-a-diamond shape on your screen, pointing you where to go. The map itself is just sitting on the floor of the ship, on the bottom-most floor at the tip of the ship. If you're having a hard time finding stairs to this area, you can break the ship walls on the outside to get in.
Map fragment #2 location in Fortnite
The second map fragment, made available on Nov. 8, is at Lil' Uzi's Stronghold (formerly known as Fortune's Fort):
The map itself is inside a small shack in the southeast corner of the POI. The shack is just north of a small tower. Try to land as close as possible, and you'll see the exclamation point diamond shape nearby. Follow it into the shack, and you'll find the map on the floor.
We'll update this guide as more map fragments get added to the game.
