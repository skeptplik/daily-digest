CALL HER AGENT: At just 1 year old, Rihanna’s daughter, Rocki Irish Mayers, has made her campaign debut for Dior.
The toddler poses with her mother, a longtime Dior brand ambassador, in the new advertisements for the Lady Dior handbag, photographed by Sam Rock under the creative direction of Dior designer Jonathan Anderson.
It marks the duo’s second major Dior moment this year, after they appeared on the cover of W magazine in April with Rihanna dressed in Dior haute couture, and Rocki wearing a custom Dior couture diaper and floral headpiece created specifically for her by Anderson.
She appears to be a natural in front of the camera, having also appeared in a recent holiday campaign for her mother’s Savage x Fenty label alongside her brothers RZA and Riot.
Rihanna fronted her first Dior campaign in 2015, and has showcased the house’s ready-to-wear, leather goods and eyewear in dedicated ads over the years, becoming the official face of the J’adore fragrance in 2024. This marks her first Lady Dior campaign.
In one image, she is pictured wearing an oversize black leather jacket over a maroon slipdress, with her daughter wearing a miniature version of the same jacket while seated in her lap. In another, Rihanna plants a kiss on the child’s forehead.
The ads are designed to reflect the many facets of her identity as a singer, songwriter, entrepreneur, actress and philanthropist, in addition to her family life with partner A$AP Rocky and their three children.
“A lady is a prism. In a way that light shines through a prism and becomes multidimensional, and every color performs at a different angle, there’s no one way to truly define us,” Rihanna said in a statement.
The campaign was styled by Benjamin Bruno and Rihanna’s longtime stylist Jahleel Weaver, with Yusef on hair, Hector Espinal doing makeup and Jenny Longworth in charge of manicures. The campaign film was directed by Melina Matsoukas.
Rihanna has shared her appreciation for fashion with her daughter, even dressing her in a miniature version of the memorable yellow Guo Pei gown she wore to the 2015 Met Gala. Rocki also joined her dad on the cover of a recent issue of W China.
