Oh look, how lovely – a party invitation. That’s nice of them, isn’t it? How exciting. Haven’t seen the whatsits since, ooh, last Christmas, I suppose. Mmm, hope they have mince pies. Can’t wait!
Fast forward to the night itself and you have spent 45 minutes trying on tops in front of the bedroom mirror. Too dressy? Not dressy enough? Should I have got my nails done? Will everyone else be in jeans? Are sequins too much? Maybe we should just stay in and watch the last episode of Black Doves?
In other words: no, it’s not just you. Party dressing makes absolutely everyone lose the plot. You need to look fabulous, obviously, but also interesting. And sexy. (Oh, come on, we all do.) You want to show you’re up to date, because it is important to look like someone who goes out all the time, even if your usual Friday night actually involves a takeaway and slippers. But you definitely don’t want to appear as if you’ve tried too hard.
This is quite a lot to ask of a dress, so it is no surprise we get in a pickle. Here are 51 ideas to help. There are lovely things to buy, from proper party dresses to inexpensive jazzy bits that can transform an outfit. And there are also plenty of suggestions that cost absolutely nothing. Clever ways to wear the jewellery you already own, the latest styling tricks, a few bossy reminders about not hobbling your chances of a fun night by wearing the wrong shoes.
A lot of good party dressing comes down to dressing with a bit more nerve. I am a big believer in wearing clothes the “wrong” way. A fitted jacket worn buttoned up with nothing underneath looks fabulous as a sophisticated, daring evening top. Daytime clothes can go nocturnal, with the right accessories: try all the brooches you own, clustered together on one shoulder of a plain dress. A little bit Carrie Bradshaw, a little bit Elsa Schiaparelli, but all you.
Should inspiration not strike, the classics never fail. Sheer black tights have a particular kind of after-dark magic – a little bit French New Wave, a little bit Madonna in Desperately Seeking Susan – and make everything above them look more expensive. And if you simply cannot face getting dressed up, don’t. Well, not entirely. A floor-length sequin dress and heels will look ace worn with your favourite oversized sweater thrown over the top.
Mostly, though, be a little bit shinier, a little bit louder, than you usually are. Wear the slightly-too-much thing. After all, you’re not going to wear it in January, are you? Have a drink, say yes to the second mince pie, stay a bit longer. It’s a party, remember! It’ll be fun. I promise.

Turn a silk scarf into a going-out top
Do you know about this? It is genius. Discovered it in the heatwave and I am reviving it for party season. You need a large silk scarf (90cm square or bigger) and a ring – any ring will do. Fold the scarf in half diagonally so you have a triangle, and pull the point of the triangle – opposite the long edge – through the ring. Tie those two ends behind your neck, with the ring sitting in the middle of your collarbone. Then tie the two corners of the scarf behind your waist. Backless evening glamour for nothing.
