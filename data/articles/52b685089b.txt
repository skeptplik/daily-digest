Luka Doncic has scored 32 points as the in-form Los Angeles Lakers have rolled to a sixth straight victory with a 124-114 defeat of the Atlanta Hawks in the NBA.
Doncic, whose sensational early-season form has propelled the Lakers to the top of the Western Conference, once again led from the front on Monday to secure an impressive win on the road.
The Slovenian star finished with 32 points, 10 assists, eight rebounds, five steals and two blocks in a dominant all-around performance.
Doncic, who is averaging 40 points per game this season, received offensive support from Sandro Mamukelashvili, who delivered an eye-catching 23 points from the bench, including five three-pointers.
Walker Kessler added 21 points. Collin Sexton scored 18 and Ziaire Williams 12.
Lakers coach JJ Redick paid tribute to the contributions of Doncic, Sexton and Mamukelashvili on a night when the team were missing Austin Reaves (illness) and Quentin Grimes (injury).
“I thought Collin did a really nice job of giving us some juice, and Sandro had his best game as a Laker,” Redick said. “That was awesome.”
“Luka, again, just orchestrating. He was out there without a secondary ball handler, so he had to be on the ball a lot, and that was taxing. I thought he handled it well,” Redick added.
The Lakers improved to 10-2 at the top of the Western Conference as Atlanta fell to 7-5.
The San Antonio Spurs, meanwhile, maintained their hot pursuit of the Lakers in the West, extending their winning streak to nine games after blowing out the Phoenix Suns 122-96 on the road to improve to 9-2.
Spurs star Victor Wembanyama – fresh from a career-high 52-point performance last week – was once again in dazzling form, dropping 40 points with 11 rebounds, eight blocks and three assists in a dominant victory.
“There’s a lot of good going on, obviously,” San Antonio coach Mitch Johnson said of Wembanyama’s rich vein of recent form.
“I think he’s allowing the game to unfold naturally, and he’s doing a great job of putting himself in positions to take advantage of the opportunities that are in front of him. And his teammates are doing a good job of utilising him when appropriate.”
The Golden State Warriors kept their own winning streak alive on Monday, thrashing the New Orleans Pelicans 138-87 to record their eighth consecutive victory.
Stephen Curry and Brandin Podziemski scored 25 points apiece while rookie sensation Yaxel Lendeborg added 23 as the Warriors improved to 8-3.
In other games on Monday, the Detroit Pistons improved to 7-3 with a 122-111 defeat of the Washington Wizards after 24 points from John Collins.
The Memphis Grizzlies are also on 7-3 after their 118-102 victory over the Toronto Raptors. The Cleveland Cavaliers, meanwhile, stumbled to a 124-112 loss to the Charlotte Hornets.
In Houston, Jamal Murray scored 38 points for the Denver Nuggets, but it was not enough to stop the Rockets from claiming a 110-100 victory.
Kevin Durant scored 23 points, and Alperen Sengun added 20 as the Rockets halted their two-game losing streak.
