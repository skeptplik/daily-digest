With the holidays approaching, with parties and moments spent with friends and family, now is the perfect time to snag one of the best Lord of the Rings board games out there, The Lord of the Rings: Fate of the Fellowship, which is on sale for $59.99 over on Amazon. And it just so happens that canonical the Fellowship of the Ring set out from Rivendell on their quest to destroy the Ring on December 25th, does make this a Christmas game (yes, I’m serious - check Appendix B at the back of Return of the King).
Fate of the Fellowship is a cooperative adventure where one to five players assume the roles as various heroes from the Fellowship of the Ring and other Free Peoples of Middle-Earth in an attempt to thwart the spread of dark forces of Sauron and Sarumon. And, hopefully, cast the One Ring into the fires of Mt. Doom, and grasp victory.
Designed by Matt Leacock, and based on the Pandemic system (another line of fantastic cooperative board games we recommend) in our review of Fate of the Fellowship, we found that our playthroughs “were full of tense moments, dramatic victories, and risky plays that sometimes paid off in spades.”. With each character featuring unique abilities and each player dividing their actions between two each, results in title that rewards teamwork and strategy, being wrapped up in some beautiful artwork and designs that really helps sell the adventure.
