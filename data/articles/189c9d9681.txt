Apple AirPods Pro 3 for $200 ($49 Off)
The flagship AirPods Pro 3 are Apple’s best AirPods yet. Upgrades include stronger noise-canceling, a new acoustic architecture for deeper bass, and redesigned ear tips for a more secure fit. The ear tips also keep my AirPods from slipping during longer runs and sweatier workouts. New tools include a built-in heart rate sensor for fitness tracking, live translation, and a camera remote to snap photos or videos on your iPhone. These earbuds are also the first AirPods to be IP57 rated against dust, sweat, and rain.
Right now, they are 20 percent off at Amazon, Target, and Walmart. There’s no telling if these discounts will change once Black Friday is here, so I’d grab a pair now if they’re on your wish list.
Apple AirPods Max 2 for $449 ($100 Off)
The AirPods Max 2’s newer H2 chip improves active noise cancellation and transparency mode and enables a suite of intelligent features, including conversation awareness, live translation, and improved Siri interactions. The AirPods Max 2 are designed with a new high-dynamic-range amplifier for deeper bass, more natural vocals, and cleaner highs. The ear cuffs, while plush and comfortable for longer listening sessions, do trap some heat, but I have yet to find a pair of over-ear headphones that don’t.
At $549, the AirPods Max 2 can be a tough sell, especially with so many excellent, more affordable alternatives on the market. But $100 off, they’re a lot easier to justify, especially if you want the seamless integration that comes with Apple’s ecosystem.
