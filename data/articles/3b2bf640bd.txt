A chance for revenge. Beaten by Spain in the World Cup semi-finals last July, France will meet La Roja again in the Nations League quarter-finals next March.
Elsewhere, Friday's Nations League draw in Nyon, Switzerland, also paired England with Norway. Another rematch from the last World Cup, where the Three Lions claimed a 2-1 victory in the quarter-finals.
In Ligue 1, PSG head to Nice on Saturday on the back of eleven consecutive wins, but face a side unbeaten at home this season.
In England, Arsenal will look to strengthen their grip on top spot with a trip to Newcastle, while Manchester City desperately need to bounce back against Fulham after three straight league defeats.
In Germany, a nightmare evening for Bayern Munich, held to a draw by Cologne and dealt major injury blows to Harry Kane and Michael Olise.
In Saudi Arabia, Cristiano Ronaldo scored the 986th goal of his career but couldn't prevent Al-Nassr from losing.
And finally, in tennis, Carlos Alcaraz eased concerns over his fitness with a convincing victory over Flavio Cobolli at the ATP Finals in Turin. The Spaniard will face Ben Shelton in the semi-finals, while Daniil Medvedev takes on world number one Alexander Zverev.
