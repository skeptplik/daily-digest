Woman hurt in flat fire suspected to have been caused by aromatherapy items
Rescuers called to Kingswood Villas in Tin Shui Wai after smoke seen billowing from flat, with 100 residents fleeing
Hong Kong firefighters have rescued a 67-year-old woman from a flat in Tin Shui Wai after a blaze broke out, prompting about 100 people to flee.
A preliminary investigation suggested aromatherapy items ignited objects in the flat.
Police said officers were alerted at 5.39pm on Saturday after smoke was seen billowing from a flat at Locwood Court in the Kingswood Villas private estate.
