Grand Theft Auto 6 has been out for a little while now, and already players are showing off their skills when it comes to the game's many heists. Despite the challenges in getting away clean, some are proving it's possible.
For one, sneaking into a bank while the vault is open and stuffing your bags full of marked bills is a tricky process of gradually making your way through rooms and taking out guards as quietly as possible. While the police in the clip below were called to an eventual breakout of violence, a lengthy getaway allowed for a cheeky payday of almost $100,000 in cash.
One especially impressive element of Grand Theft Auto 6 is how many of its little mechanics interact with each other. As you can see below, if you bring a vehicle with a winch to a cash machine, you can actually pull it off the wall and drag it away from the scene of the crime.

As you progress through Grand Theft Auto 6, the game actually introduces you to both styles of heists. Without spoiling exactly how this happens, you're told how to go about taking on such criminal ventures, and you can continue to do so throughout the game at your pleasure. Still, even after you've been tutorialised, pulling off one of these heists is no joke, and can easily turn chaotic.
Here's a little tip for those of you looking to pull off a heist yourself. You're told that safety deposit boxes can be rifled through for additional loot. However, when you actually make your way into a vault, these will be closed without much sign on which you can access. If you use the focus mode (by pushing in both L3 and R3), it will highlight boxes which you can shoot open. This, of course, may garner unwanted attention, so whether or not you think it's worth doing is part of the risk/reward dilemma you'll have to reckon with. As is the case with so much stuff in GTA 6 including, but not limited to, car-jacking.
