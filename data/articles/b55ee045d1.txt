Demi Moore wore an intricate number with sheer details from Carolina Hererra’s spring 2027 ready-to-wear collection at the 2026 Baby2Baby Gala in Los Angeles on Saturday night.
Moore was honored with Baby2Baby’s Giving Tree Award at the nonprofit’s annual gala in West Hollywood. “The Substance” star joins a long list of A-list recipients, including Serena Williams, Kim Kardashian and Charlize Theron.
Dressed for the occasion, Moore opted for a floor-length black gown with a train, featuring full sleeves and a V-shaped neckline. The most interesting details were the large furls of fabric embellishing her neckline and the cuffs of her sleeves, while a sheer baby-blue sash tied around her middle and flowed downwards, breaking up the black fabric. The look was styled by Brad Goreski, a longtime go-to for Moore.
The 64-year-old actress tied her long dark brown locks into a ponytail, leaving out a few tendrils to add a playful touch and frame her face. For glam, Moore opted for silver eyeshadow, cluster lashes, candy pink blush on the apples of her cheeks and a glossy warm pink lip. Moore also sported a nude square-tipped manicure, large silver rings on both of her hands and square dangling earrings made of diamonds.
The actress also recently wore a custom GapStudio denim gown created by Zac Posen for the CFDA Awards and also wore a Standing Ground Fall 2026 strapless gown for the Academy Museum Gala.
Held at the Pacific Design Center and presented by Paul Mitchell, the fundraiser event aims to aid the charity’s mission to provide basic necessities to children living in poverty, including diapers, clothing and baby formula. The 2025 Gala raised a record $18 million.
