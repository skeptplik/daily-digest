Surprise, surprise - GTA 6 is officially the biggest entertainment launch of all time, surpassing its predecessor's 13-year-long record and the already optimistic analyst estimates.
Rockstar Games today confirmed that its cultural juggernaut sold a staggering 32.5 million pre-release copies across PS5 and Xbox Series X/S before its official worldwide launch on the 19th of November. By the end of its first day on sale, GTA 6 managed to rake in a frankly absurd $3.79bn. Not a bad haul.
As Rockstar was quick to brag, the whopping performance means GTA 6 set "a new record for entertainment across any medium" by "becoming the biggest entertainment launch in history."
For context, GTA 5 made around $800m in its opening 24 hours more than a decade ago. That mammoth debut almost looks quaint next to GTA 6's stunning numbers, although it's notable GTA 5 didn't launch immediately in Japan or Brazil - GTA 6 is available in almost every market with access to PlayStation and Xbox consoles.
"It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI," said Rockstar co-founder and company president, Sam Houser, in a recent press release. "We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn't be more excited to share this moment with you."
Previous estimates guessed that GTA 6 had sold almost 25m copies ($2.5bn in revenue) on day one. It's fair to say the game's staggering performance blew even those sky-high projections out of the water.
Every early indicator pointed to GTA 6's inevitably record-breaking launch. Parent company Take-Two previously said the game was tracking "well above expectations," as the crime fest sold more pre-orders than any other game in PlayStation Store and Xbox Store history.
The only question now is whether GTA 6 can ever outsell GTA 5's mammoth 230m copies, which it reached thanks to a very healthy cadence of GTA Online updates. How high GTA 6 can go will likely depend on its multiplayer offering, though Rockstar hasn't said anything concrete about that just yet.
"A game of opposites and awe, minute detail and impossible scale, Rockstar pushes back the horizon of video games once again with GTA 6. An irreducible mix of silliness, intimacy, grand ideas and rock 'n' roll action - and a load more after that," Chris Tapsell gushed in Eurogamer's GTA 6 review.
