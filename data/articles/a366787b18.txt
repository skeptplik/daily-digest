Spanish police dismantle Madrid housing protest camp ahead of snap election
The camp sprang up in September after Spain’s housing crisis hit the spotlight following the eviction of an 87-year-old from her home
Officers closed off much of the Spanish capital’s emblematic Puerta del Sol square on Tuesday morning as municipal cleaning staff swept away remaining debris, tents and tarpaulin.
The Madrid camp sprang up in September, after Spain’s chronic housing crisis burst into the centre of public debate following the eviction of an 87-year-old pensioner from her lifelong Madrid home.
