Last month the actor Christina Applegate revealed that she had been misdiagnosed with multiple sclerosis. She announced that she had the condition in 2021, and has spoken candidly about her experiences of it, becoming a prominent advocate for people living with MS.
“I’m back at square one ... I don’t know what’s going on with me.” Applegate said on her podcast MeSsy, adding: “There’s a pretty damn high number of misdiagnoses with MS.”
