Below you can follow the latest updates from Russia's war in Ukraine on Friday, 20th November:
Ukraine death toll rises to 8 after Russian bombardment
Ukrainian officials said that the death toll has risen to at least 8 following an overnight Russian aerial bombardment.
Prime Minister Sergii Koretskyi said six of the eight people killed were in Kyiv. Two deaths were also reported at a railway station in the western Ukrainian city of Lviv.
Ukraine's Air Force said Russia had launched 84 cruise missiles in the attack, adding that almost all of them were destroyed.
An unspecified number of ballistic missiles and more than 400 attack drones were also reported to have been destroyed.
Poland's defense chief confirms Russian attack on Ukraine 'impacted' Poland
Poland's Defense Minister Wladyslaw Kosiniak-Kamysz said that several Russian flying objects entered Polish airspace during recent air attacks on Ukraine.
"Russian long-range aviation was operating, launching both Kinzhal and Kh-101 missiles, as well as a large number of jet-powered drones and conventional drones. … This Russian Federation attack on Ukrainian territory, which also had an impact on the Republic of Poland, was one of the largest since the beginning of Russia's full-scale war against Ukraine," the Defense Ministry quoted Kosiniak-Kamysz as saying.
It comes after Polish prosecutors reported earlier that a flying object had crashed in Poland's eastern Lublin region.
Polish officials said it had taken place during heavy Russian airstrikes on neighbouring Ukraine.
In July, a missile entered Polish airspace and crashed in the same region near the village of Tarnawa Kolonia.
According to the Polish government, it was a Russian CH-101 cruise missile.
Poland probing 'violations' of airspace at three locations
Poland's Interior Minister Marcin Kierwinski said that investigations were taking place at three sites after reporting that Polish airspace had been violated.
"In connection with violations of Polish airspace, authorities are conducting operations at three locations: Rudnik nad Sanem, Molodycz and Nowy Lipowiec (Lublin Province)," he said in a post on social media platform X.
Prime Minister Donald Tusk said that he had convened a meeting of senior government and military officials along with the heads of security services in a post on X. He said there was currently no danger from the air.

Deaths and damage reported in Russian missile strikes on Kyiv
Kyiv's Mayor Vitali Klitschko said that a Russian missile bombardment in Kyiv has killed at least three people and injured 13 others.
Klitschko said in a post on Telegram that three bridges had also been damaged following the strikes on Ukraine's capital.
Experts would only be able to give a proper assessment of the damage caused to the structures once the aerial threat had ended, Klitschko added.
"Given the difficult traffic conditions between the left and right banks of the capital, I ask residents not to leave the city by private car unless absolutely necessary," the mayor said.
Klitschko also appealed to employers to allow remote-working arrangements, where possible.
Bridges have been key targets in Russia's ongoing invasion of Ukraine, but Kyiv's forces have managed to hit the Kerch bridge multiple times this week in what is being seen as a major psychological blow to Russian President Vladimir Putin.
He warned of a harsh response after the bridge linking occupied Crimea with mainland Russia was struck three times this week.
One of the archways and a section of roadway was destroyed in the attack.
Putin said he expected Moscow's response to be "far more painful" than the damage Ukraine had caused to the Kerch bridge.
