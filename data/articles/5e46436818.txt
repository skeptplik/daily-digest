We do mean own, too. All too often, we've sat down to rewatch a film to find it's vanished from our streaming service. At best, it's rematerialized on a different streaming service; at worst, it's been exterminated as some media conglomerate's tax write-off. Even 'buying' a movie, you're just getting a license that can be revoked at any time.
The solution? By buying these time travel movies on Blu-ray or 4K UHD Blu-ray, they'll still be sitting on your shelf ready to watch and rewatch for years, even decades, from now.
