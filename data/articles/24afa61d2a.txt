Steve Smith trains in secret at SCG as media locked out of batter's return to Australia
In short:
Steve Smith returned from overseas to New South Wales to train at the SCG today.
Media were locked out of the venue, with Smith entering through a back entrance.
What's next?
Smith is set to play for NSW against Queensland in the Sheffield Shield from Sunday.
