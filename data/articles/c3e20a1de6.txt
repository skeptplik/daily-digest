Aid cuts to Taliban-ruled Afghanistan further curb women's access to healthcare, education and food
Issued on: Modified:
Share
Having lived and worked in Afghanistan for seven years, photojournalist Élise Blanchard offers an intimate account of what daily life is like for Afghan women today. Joining Genie Godula on the International Day for the Elimination of Violence against Women, she explains how restrictions vary across the country, depending on where women live, their families and the local enforcement of Taliban decrees. She stresses the need to maintain access to healthcare, primary education and food, pointing to severe cuts in humanitarian assistance by the US and EU.
