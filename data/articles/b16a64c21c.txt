If you’re a gamer of a certain age, you may have fond memories of playing the original PlayStation or Xbox consoles. Now you can get gorgeously rendered tiny versions of both classic systems in the form of Christmas ornaments from Hallmark’s Keepsake line. They even light up and make authentic sounds when you press their power buttons. Both ornaments are on sale for under $20 each at Amazon for Black Friday.
PlayStation Hallmark Keepsake Ornament
The original PlayStation came out in the US in 1994 and has been getting a decent amount of attention lately. LEGO recently turned it into a near 1:1 scale buildable set that I reviewed. This Hallmark ornament PS1 is even closer to the real thing (though much smaller). It’s highly detailed and just beautifully made. When you press the power button the little green light flashes and it plays the iconic PS1 startup sound. The Amazon listing has a video of it. Check it out.
Xbox Console - Hallmark Keepsake Ornament
The Xbox is just as authentically detailed as the PlayStation. It depicts Microsoft’s black-and-green machine, complete with a Duke controller. Press the power button, and the circle on the front lights up, and it plays the startup sound, followed by the opening music from Halo: Combat Evolved. You can see a video of the process on the Amazon listing.
The PS1 normally sells for $29.99, but it’s on sale for $18.80 for Black Friday. That’s only a few cents higher than the all-time low price, not enough to worry about. The Xbox has an MSRP of $27.99, but it’s on sale for $19.60. That’s a new all-time low price.
Either way you go, you probably won’t regret the purchase, assuming you have fond memories of playing these consoles in their heyday. I have the SNES ornament, and I love to put it on the tree each year (and press the power button to hear the music from Super Mario World). That one’s currently out of print. I expect these to go out of print as well at some point in the coming years. Grab one now while it’s on sale if you want it.
