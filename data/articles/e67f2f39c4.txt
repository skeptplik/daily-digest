Philippines seeks bespoke BrahMos missiles from India for island defence
A deal worth up to US$800 million to double its batteries could help Manila erect a coastal ‘missile wall’, analysts say
Indian sources later told Reuters that India was in “final-stage talks” to supply more BrahMos missiles. The Philippines is expected to buy about five systems, or nearly 60 missiles in total, in a deal reportedly worth US$700 million to US$800 million.
