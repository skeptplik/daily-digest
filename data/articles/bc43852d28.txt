Gryphline's Arknights: Endfield has been named Google Play's best game of 2026, taking the top prize in every market except Japan and Korea.
Google announced the winners on November 17 in a blog post from Sam Bright, VP and general manager of Google Play and its developer ecosystem. Hololive Dreams won best game in Japan, while Nexon's MapleStory: Idle RPG won in Korea.
Bright said this year's picks reflected a shift toward games that span phones, tablets, and PCs, and highlighted Arknights: Endfield's Sidekick integration and its cross-device play through Google Play Games on PC.
XSeed Games' farming sim Moonlight Peaks won best cross-device game in the main list. Perfect World's Neverness to Everness took the same award in many Asian markets.
Digital Extremes' Warframe was named best multiplayer game and Sybo's Subway Surfers City won best pick up and play. Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor, published by Ghost Ship Publishing, won best indie, and Sea of Stars, published by Playdigious on mobile, won best story. Hoyoverse's Zenless Zone Zero won best ongoing game in the Americas and Europe.
Outside games, note-taking app Notability was named best app, and Strava won best cross-device app. Google's Asia Pacific list also included regional awards, with Blast+ Games' Scarfall 2.0 named best made in India and Own Games' Tukang Sate VS Ojol Rush best made in Indonesia.
Arknights: Endfield is also a finalist for iPad Game of the Year in Apple's App Store Awards 2026, revealed earlier today, and is nominated for best mobile game at The Game Awards alongside Monster Hunter Outlanders, Neverness to Everness, Pokémon Champions, and Where Winds Meet. Winners will be revealed on December 10.
Last month, Google Play added a batch of new discovery and social features for games, including a personalized Game Finder, timed competitive Sprints, and clip-sharing via its Sidekick overlay.
