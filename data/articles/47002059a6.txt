Country music and fashion have always had a rather fabulous understanding: More is more, and there’s rarely such a thing as too many rhinestones. From sculptural cowboy hats and embroidered Western suits to gowns that would look right at home on a couture runway, the CMA Awards red carpet has become a showcase for the genre’s increasingly ambitious approach to dressing. And this year, the star power is getting a significant upgrade.
The 60th annual CMA Awards are taking over Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena on Wednesday, Nov. 18, with Lainey Wilson returning to hosting duties for the third consecutive year. The evening’s biggest draw? Taylor Swift, who is making her first CMA Awards performance in 13 years. The singer, whose country roots helped launch one of music’s most formidable careers, is also nominated for Single of the Year for “I Knew It, I Knew You.”
Swift won’t be the only familiar face commanding attention. Kacey Musgraves, Miranda Lambert, Ella Langley, Megan Moroney, Luke Combs, Tim McGraw, and Chris Stapleton are among the performers slated to take the stage. And while their musical contributions are the official reason for gathering, we’ll be keeping a particularly close eye on what everyone wears before the ceremony even begins.
After all, country music’s fashion vocabulary has expanded considerably beyond denim and cowboy boots. These days, the genre’s biggest stars are just as comfortable experimenting with dramatic silhouettes, luxury jewelry, and custom designer creations as they are embracing their Western roots. The result is a red carpet where Nashville tradition and fashion-world extravagance make surprisingly good company.
Luckily, you won’t need a front-row invitation to see it all unfold. Below, find everything you need to know about watching the 2026 CMA Awards red carpet live online, including where to stream the arrivals for free.
How to Watch the 2026 CMA Awards Red Carpet Online for Free
The official CMA Awards red carpet coverage begins Wednesday, Nov. 18, at 6 p.m. ET/5 p.m. CT, two hours before the ceremony.
Viewers can tune in to On The Red Carpet at the CMA Awards, a live pre-show hosted by Joelle Garguilo and Ryan Chiaverini, streaming on Disney+, Hulu, OnTheRedCarpet.com, and YouTube TV. Expect celebrity interviews, behind-the-scenes access, and, naturally, a closer look at the outfits making their Nashville debut.
Disney+
Disney+ is your ticket to the evening’s festivities, starting with the official CMA Awards red carpet pre-show at 6 p.m. ET and continuing with the ceremony at 8 p.m. ET. That means you can admire the night’s most ambitious Western-inspired looks before settling in for Taylor Swift’s first CMA Awards performance in 13 years, all without changing platforms.
Hulu + Live TV
Prefer to keep your live television and streaming subscriptions under one roof? Hulu + Live TV lets subscribers watch the ceremony as it airs on ABC, where available. The red carpet pre-show will also stream on Hulu, while the full awards show arrives on demand Nov. 19, giving you another opportunity to revisit the performances and fashion moments worth a second look.
For those who prefer their fashion commentary delivered straight from the carpet, the Country Music Association is also hosting an Instagram Live broadcast beginning at 6 p.m. ET, with ESPN’s Laura Rutledge taking viewers inside the arrivals.
The red carpet may be free to watch on YouTube and OnTheRedCarpet.com, but Disney+ lets you make a proper evening of it, taking you from the night’s most extravagant fashion statements straight to the star-studded ceremony.
CMA Awards 2026: Date, Time, and Where to Stream the Ceremony
Once the last red carpet interview wraps, the attention turns to the main event. The 60th annual CMA Awards will air live on ABC and Disney+ at 8 p.m. ET/7 p.m. CT, with the three-hour ceremony bringing together some of country music’s most recognizable names.
Beyond Swift’s highly anticipated return, the evening will celebrate the genre’s established heavyweights and rising talent, with performances from Musgraves, Lambert, Langley, and more. The ceremony will also honor Brooks & Dunn with the Willie Nelson Lifetime Achievement Award.
Disney+ subscribers can watch the awards live, while Hulu will make the full ceremony available on demand Thursday, Nov. 19. Hulu + Live TV subscribers can also tune in to the ABC broadcast where the channel is available.
And if the evening’s red carpet proves anything, it’s that country music’s love affair with fashion is showing no signs of slowing down. The trophies may be the official attraction, but a well-executed Western look deserves its own moment in the spotlight.
