Hong Kong considers tougher site operations rules after spike in construction scandals
Task force to review site operations and explore mandatory use of body cameras to record critical work procedures
Hong Kong’s Construction Industry Council will form a task force to review site operations in an effort to address work quality issues following a spike in discoveries of substandard work on buildings and infrastructure across the city.
Secretary for Development Bernadette Linn Hon-ho announced the task force on Wednesday and said that it would explore measures to boost the construction industry’s performance. These could include requiring frontline workers to wear body cameras to record critical work procedures.
The announcement came after the government said it planned to step up construction supervision by stipulating that steel reinforcement arrangements be photographed or videoed before concrete pouring and inspections by an independent laboratory.
