Port Adelaide player Josh Sinn banned for six AFL matches for betting on games
In short:
Port Adelaide player Josh Sinn placed bets totalling $195 on four league matches early in the 2026 season, including one involving the Power, an AFL Integrity Unit investigation has found.
The AFL has banned Sinn, who missed last season because of injury, from the opening six rounds of 2027.
The league said in handing down the penalty it had taken into account the fact that Sinn "was not found to have used inside information".
