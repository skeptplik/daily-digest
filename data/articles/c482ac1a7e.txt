GameCube launch title Wave Race: Blue Storm is now available via the Nintendo Switch Online + Expansion Pack service, alongside a long-awaited new feature.
If you're an Expansion Pack subscriber, you can now update your Nintendo Classics GameCube app to gain access to the cult classic aquatic racer — and also unlock the ability to remove the app's annoying border graphics.
The Nintendo Classic apps have a border gradient that until now you couldn't remove — and it's something that's proven a bugbear from fans who want a cleaner look. For now, only the GameCube app has the option to simply have clear black borders instead, though it seems likely the other console apps will follow. Or, maybe this is something only the technical power of Switch 2 can pull off?
"Storm warning! Prepare to ride the waves!" reads an official blurb for the game. "Take to the water in this watercraft-racing game, released for the Nintendo GameCube system in 2001. Compete in contests of speed and technique—either racing against your rivals or going up against your own records. As you race across the waves, you can see the skies changes from calm and sunny to surging storms... Weather conditions can affect visibility, water levels, and the height of the waves over the course of a race, so you'll need to be prepared for whatever comes!"
Wave Race: Blue Storm becomes the 12th GameCube title available on Switch 2 via the Expansion Pack subscription service, following the previous additions of Chibi-Robo!, F-Zero GX, Fire Emblem: Path of Radiance, Luigi's Mansion, Super Mario Strikers, Pokémon XD: Gale of Darkness, SoulCalibur 2, Star Fox Adventures, Super Mario Sunshine, The Legend of Zelda: The Wind Waker and Wario World.
