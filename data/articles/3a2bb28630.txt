Russian President Vladimir Putin took part in a flag-raising ceremony for the country's newest nuclear-powered submarine, the Perm, in Severodvinsk on Friday. The Perm is the fifth Yasen-M class submarine to join Russia's fleet in six years.
Putin said Russia was among the few countries capable of building such powerful nuclear-powered submarines. He also announced that five missile-carrying submarines, including three Yasen-M class vessels, are scheduled to be delivered to the Navy by 2030.
