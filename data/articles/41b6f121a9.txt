The Joeys have been left facing a slightly nervy final group match before guaranteeing their spot in the knockout stages at the Under-17 World Cup following a frustrating goalless draw against Denmark.
Australia's bright soccer teenagers, who had begun so sparkly with Akol Akon inspiring them to a 2-0 victory over Argentina in Doha, were brought down to Earth somewhat as they dominated possession and chances against the Danes only to draw a blank.
With Argentina bouncing back to beat Mozambique 2-1 later on Sunday, local time, the outcome of group C is not cut and dried going into the final round of pool games on Wednesday.
Australia still leads the way on four points, with Argentina and Mozambique both on three and Denmark on one.
Just a draw against Mozambique would guarantee a top-two place in the group and give Carl Veart's players a place in the last 32, while even defeat to the African side might not be calamitous because the 48-team format awards places to the eight best third-place finishers.
But Australia will still want to deliver a better display than the one it produced against Denmark, which was hammered 4-0 by Mozambique in its opening outing.
Brisbane's 17-year-old box-to-box midfielder Emmett Shaw was named the player-of-the match but he will rue missing the best chance of the match when he should have sunk a free header in the 74th minute while unmarked.
While Australia enjoyed 66 per cent of possession, it took it 55 minutes to get a shot on target, with Shaw's long-range free-kick proving far too hopeful, although Akon did come closer with a curling free kick in the 69th minute.
Impressive young Brighton keeper Charlie Wilson-Papps did not have a great deal to do but, when really called on in the 83rd minute, he got down sharply to make a key stop to thwart a low shot, Denmark's best chance of the match.
