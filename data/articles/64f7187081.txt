After many long years of waiting and a handful of delays, Rockstar's Grand Theft Auto 6 will finally be released on Thursday, Nov. 19. For some players, it's out right now.
GTA 6 has a rolling midnight launch schedule, meaning it's available to play at 12 a.m. your local time on Nov. 19. Due to the power of time zones and a round Earth, midnight local time on Nov. 19 arrives in some territories while it's still Nov. 18 in others. New Zealanders will get first access as GTA 6 gradually unlocks around the world.
The internet is beholden to no time zone. Until it's midnight Nov. 19 in your time zone, you'll have to be aware that spoilers will naturally flood social media and other sites. Depending on where they live, players in New Zealand and elsewhere will have multiple hours to play GTA 6 while the timer on their PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X is still counting down. For example, midnight in New Zealand on Nov. 19 is 6 a.m. EST on Nov. 18 on the east coast of the United States. U.S.-based players will have to be careful to avoid spoilers for essentially the whole day on Wednesday.
As GTA 6 will take players a long, long time to finish, I wouldn't expect any ending spoilers to flood the internet before Nov. 19 hits your time zone. However, GTA 6 could have plenty of surprises in its early game you still might want to avoid. Need I remind you of The Last of Us Part 2's monumental opening hours?
