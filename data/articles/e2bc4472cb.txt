Crimea bridge attacked again, Russian governor says
Damage assessment underway after 3rd such attack this week
Traffic on the Crimean Bridge was temporarily suspended following an attack, Sergei Aksyonov, Crimea's Russian-installed governor, said on Telegram on Thursday, adding that an assessment of the damage was underway.
The attack was the third this week on the bridge connecting mainland Russia to Crimea, which Russia annexed from Ukraine in 2014. It comes as a blow to President Vladimir Putin ahead of his meetings with U.S. and Chinese counterparts on Thursday.
The 19-kilometre bridge, inaugurated by Putin in 2018, is a flagship project and a symbol of Russia's attachment to the peninsula.
Earlier this week, Ukrainian drones and missiles destroyed the central road section of the bridge, causing its main road arch to crumble into the sea and leaving a yawning gap, according to videos and images verified by Reuters.
Russia launched a full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022. The two countries have been at war ever since.
