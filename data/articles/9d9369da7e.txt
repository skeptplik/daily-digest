WARM SLOPES: Italian premium skiwear and bikewear brand Rh+ has linked with Thermore to refresh its seasonal performance gear aimed at the mountains with extra warmth and insulation.
The collection comprises ski suits that could easily transition toward urban environments, crafted from unusual materials such as stretch corduroy, performance velvet and technical loden, engineered to allow movement and withstand low temperatures. These are flanked by more decidedly activewear styles, such as the men’s Bimateric Velvet Jacket and women’s Highline W Jacket.
Thermore supplied the skiwear brand with its signature Classic and Ecodown Fibers stuffing for the jackets, marking the first collaboration between the two players.
While Thermore’s Classic is among the first insulation technologies developed by the company, Ecodown Fibers is an innovative free fiber product made of 100 percent recycled fibers from PET bottles introduced in 2019.
“Thermore and Rh+ share the same vision for technical apparel: starting with the real demands of sport to build garments tailored to athletes. On the slopes, intense descents alternate with long waits for the lifts, so thermal insulation must be able to adapt to these fluctuations. This partnership is essential to us because it brings together two Italian companies with deep technical expertise, giving people the opportunity to experience the difference firsthand,” said Stefano Lagonigro, marketing manager at Rh+.
Laura Beachy, senior vice president of global marketing and communications at Thermore, touted the partnership for “allowing us to show how technologically advanced thermal insulation can enhance both comfort and performance. Our goal is to create meaningful experiences that demonstrate the value of innovation and strengthen the connection between brands and the people who wear them.”
Last October, Thermore linked with Patagonia on the latter’s Heyes Peak collection of five outdoorsy designs, including a jacket, parka, hooded jacket and three-in-one parka.
Rh+ was established in 2001, initially as a performance eyewear brand, and soon evolved into a total look activewear label geared especially at skiing and cycling. It is based in Milan where it also has a flagship store.
Thermore was established in Milan as a premium thermal insulation company for apparel and outerwear. In the 1980s, it introduced its first recycled insulation material made of recycled PET bottles, which evolved into a fully recycled fiber-based padding in 2011 before the introduction in 2019 of the signature Ecodown product.
