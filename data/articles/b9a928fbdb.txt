The DevGAMM Awards took place on Wednesday evening as the finale to the DevGAMM Lisbon conference, which this year was held in a new, more central venue: the Carlos Lopes Pavilion.
The awards centre on AA and indie games, and Phonopolis from Czech studio Amanita Design took home the Grand Prize. Studio founder Jakub Dvorský collected the award, and ended his acceptance speech by imploring the audience to "keep making games and support Ukraine".
Phonopolis – which was released in May and sees a refuse collector squaring up to a dictator in a world inspired by inter-war avant-garde art – took around a decade to make, and the characters and environmental elements were made from paper and cardboard before being scanned into the game.
Amanita Design was founded in Prague in 2003, and is also known for games like Machinarium, Pilgrims, and Creaks.
The other nominees for the Grand Prize were Dead As Disco (Brain Jar Games), Echoes of Mystralia (Borealys Games), Mortal Shell 2 (Cold Symmetry), and Mouse: PI For Hire (Fumi Games).
In addition to taking home the Grand Prize, Phonopolis won the award for Excellence in Visual Art, and Dead As Disco also took home two awards: Excellence in Audio and the Epic Games Store Indie Spotlight.
In total, the awards featured 17 categories, with a total prize pool of $140,000. This year, for the first time, the ceremony was streamed live by IGN (IGN Entertainment is the parent company of GamesIndustry.biz).
The full list of DevGAMM Awards winners is below:
- Grand Prize: Phonopolis by Amanita Design
- Excellence in Game Design: Desktop Explorer by Recurring Dream
- Excellence in Visual Art: Phonopolis by Amanita Design
- Excellence in Narrative: Ledgerbound by OmniMegaSuperCorp
- Excellence in Audio: Dead As Disco by Brain Jar Games
- Best Mobile Game: Gambonanza by Blukulele (Paul Giovannini)
- Most Anticipated Game: Crop by Carbonara Games
- Best Game in Early Development: Hold Your Dragons by Igor Krutov
- Best Small Team Game: Moldrise by Raffaele Picca
- Meaningful Game: As Long As You're Here by Autoscopia
- Best Game from Portugal: War Doctor by Red Pine Studios
- DevGAMM Choice: InKonbini: One Store. Many Stories by Nagai Industries
- Epic Games Store Indie Spotlight: Dead As Disco by Brain Jar Games
- Best Unreal Engine Game: Mortal Shell 2 by Cold Symmetry
- Best Indie Game by TinyBuild: Rewindead by Spellwork AB
- Best Made With Unity Game: Tempus Vitae by Whiteboard Games
- Best Godot Game: Burn With Me by Nozomu Games
