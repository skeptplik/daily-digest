Subway is getting on the GTA 6 hype train with a sandwich themed after the highly anticipated game.
The sandwich chain announced today that it is giving its Miami-exclusive Cubano Pulled Pork Melt a GTA 6 "reskin" in time for the new game. The sandwich is being renamed the Vice City Cubano and, as noted, is only available in the city of Miami. The sandwich comes either as a six-inch or footlong sub and features hardwood-smoked pulled pork, black forest ham, melted provolone, pickles, and mustard on freshly baked artisan Italian bread.
Subway confirmed that the sandwich will be available both in-store and for delivery for those who don't plan to leave their couch when GTA 6 is released. You can see an image of it below.
Subway isn't the only fast food place getting in on on the GTA 6 hype. Popeyes is also releasing a Vice City-themed chicken sandwich that it calls "Spice City." It will be available for one week starting November 19 and is available at participating locations for in-store pick up or delivery. Finally, there are also a bunch of Miami-based dining options and stores that have officially teamed up with Rockstar to provide themed meals and drinks based on GTA. The most notable of the bunch is a partnership with Skinny Louie, a burger chain based out of Florida.
The chain has been turned into Burger Shot, a fast food joint prominently featured in the GTA franchise. The menu, uniforms, packaging, and signange for Skinny Louie has been completely refitted with Burger Shot branding, which will remain in place until the end of the year. Rockstar's marketing power has reached unprecedented levels, even prompting other places that don't have a partnership in place to try and ride the hype waves.
GTA 6 will release this Thursday for Xbox Series X|S and PS5. Players can pre-load the game right now and they absolutely should, as the game is a whopping 210 GBs.
