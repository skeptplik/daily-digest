Macron mulls enshrining fiscal rules in French constitution
Issued on:
Share
French President Emmanuel Macron has suggested reviving a 15-year-old proposal to enshrine a framework for public finances in the constitution, as a possible response to concerns in financial markets over France's fiscal trajectory. "If there is still volatility by January, thinking about a 'golden rule' of the type proposed in 2011 could be an idea worth exploring," Macron said, speaking to Le Figaro.
