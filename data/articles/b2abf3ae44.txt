6 girls filmed vaping in Hong Kong school toilet, investigation under way
Education Bureau says it is ‘highly concerned’ about the incident, reminding the school to step up efforts to instil proper values and behaviour in students
A video showing six schoolgirls puffing on e-cigarettes inside a school toilet cubicle in Hong Kong has gone viral, prompting education authorities to say they are “highly concerned” and contact the school.
The 20-second clip, titled “Six people gathered in one room for hotpot”, was posted on Threads, Meta’s microblogging platform, on Tuesday. The post said the video had been reposted from Snapchat, where it was filmed last week.
It shows six students in school uniforms crammed into a single toilet cubicle, with a Snapchat filter applied.
