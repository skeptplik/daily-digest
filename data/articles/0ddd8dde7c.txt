Walker Scobell and Aryan Simhadri revealed that they would watch Attack on Titan fan edits to help them prepare to film emotional scenes for Percy Jackson and the Olympians.
Attack on Titan is one of the most popular anime series in the world, in no small part due to its tragic storylines and brutal, gory battles. It’s been more than a decade since the anime first released in 2013, and fans have been making AMVs of their favorite characters ever since — but these fans probably didn’t know that their creations would help high-profile actors tap into their emotions.
Speaking at 92NY on a live episode of Josh Horowitz’s Happy Sad Confused podcast, Walker Scobell revealed his unique method for getting himself into an emotional state in preparation for sad scenes while filming Percy Jackson and the Olympians.
Scobell said that he would watch fan-made edits for Eren and Mikasa, two of Attack on Titan’s main characters, set to the opening song of Season 3, Red Swan.
“I was trying to get sad. I was in school, and I snuck my phone in. I was like, ‘What can I do to make myself cry?’ I searched up a bunch of — and he did the exact same thing, and we just figured this out yesterday,” Scobell said, pointing to co-star Aryan Simhadri.
“We didn’t communicate this at all. We just ended up doing it,” Simhardi confirmed.
“I thought of the saddest thing I could, and I searched up Eren and Mikasa edits from Attack on Titan,” Scobell continued. “I started crying at school, watching them to Red Swan. And then I got there [on set], and I couldn’t do it.”

It’s no wonder that actors take notes from Attack on Titan for their own projects; in fact, Eren’s English voice actor, Bryce Papenbrook, revealed that he literally unhinged his jaw while screaming in one of the anime’s most iconic scenes in an effort to really sell the character’s pain and confusion.
