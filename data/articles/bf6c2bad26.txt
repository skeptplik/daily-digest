Splitcreen Croatia, the new industry conference seeking to fill the gap left by Reboot Develop, has opened applications for its Official Selection showcase, which includes free travel, accommodation and exhibition space for developers with unreleased games.
Selected games will receive a free showcase package which includes "a branded showcase station powered by AMD, two All Access conference passes, three nights of accommodation for two people, and access to pitching, networking events and showcase awards," the organisers said. Exhibitors will be able to show their game to publishers, investors and other industry professionals attending the show, which includes speakers from Ubisoft, Landfall, Oro Interactive, Evil Empire and Wings Interactive.
The program is aimed at developers working on unreleased games who are not currently signed with a publisher, although applications will be considered from teams who have existing publishers or funding deals if they "have a clear business reason for attending," such as seeking additional funding or publishing in additional markets like Asia. To be eligible, the games much be unreleased and have a playable build ready to demonstrate.
Applications are currently being accepted through the Splitscreen site, and will be considered by an internal judging panel including Landfall's Rebecca Lautner, Raw Fury's Johan Torensson, former Xbox portfolio lead Nick Zuclich and Rami Ismail. Applications will be accepted until 1st Feb 2027 with winners being announced later that month. The event itself takes place in Split from 20-22nd April.
Splitscreen was announced in October, and is designed to emulate the much-cherished experience offered by the now-ceased Dubrovnik-based Reboot Develop, which became an industry favourite over the ten years of its existence. That event ceased this year, with the organisers being served a bankruptcy notice.
The event is explicitly designed to foster in-person connections, and enable "industry friends getting together by the Adriatic, in a hotel next to the beach, to recharge, connect, and build real relationships," Director and co-founder Hrvoje Mitic previously told GamesIndustry.biz. "You have great talks and meetings, but some of the best conversations happen over lunch, on a boat or just hanging out."
