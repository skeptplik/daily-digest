Over the past few years, denim trends have been a particularly hot topic here at Vogue HQ. From the divisive barrel leg and ultra-low-rise styles, to the return of skinny jeans, there has been no shortage of denim shapes to debate–and, in many cases, internet discourses to fuel. For 2026, however, the denim styles leading the conversation feel, by comparison, reassuringly familiar.
Vogue’s Favorite Wide-Leg Jeans:
Of them all, wide-leg jeans arguably have the most universal appeal. Unlike a straight-leg, which has a more uniform look, this silhouette can be tweaked to create different results. You can go for a baggy skater style, a flared fit that will appeal to aspiring Chloé girls, or even a more tailored pair that resembles formal trousers. This variety also means that the styling opportunities are plentiful. This fall’s street style galleries have been awash with wide-leg jeans, worn in ways that prove there is no one-size-fits-all formula.
When it comes to styling wide-leg jeans, you don’t need to overcomplicate things, as this style arguably looks its best when paired with a boxy white T-shirt and leather jacket–a ’90s formula that continues to define off-duty model style. For a more deliberately oversized take, try balancing baggy denim with a similarly generous poplin or plaid shirt. And, if you’re styling it for a winter storm, a slim-fit base and a faux fur—cropped or maxi, both work—are all you need to stay snug.
Whether you prefer yours on the slimmer side, tailored or extra-wide, scroll on to discover how to style wide-leg jeans now.
The Daily Uniform
For a polished, work-appropriate take on wide-leg jeans, combine a tailored dark-washed pair with a structured white blouse, kitten heels, and ladylike extras.
Color Therapy
Mix sweet saturated colors, like pink poplin and poppy red, with baggy, tomboy cuts for a well-balanced look. Heavy loafers and strong eyewear add a playfully preppy touch.
Elevated Eclectic
Wide-leg jeans from Levi’s gain a bohemian edge when paired with a patterned knit from Dries Van Noten, an oversized trench, and unexpected accessories.
Easy Off-Duty Layers
Take a cue from the models off duty - pair wide leg jeans with low profile kicks, layered tees and an It bag.
Purple Reign
Dress up a trending purple jean with a sleek black blouse and white ballet flats. A wrap coat lets you bundle up without the bulk.
