One of the best two-episode arcs in Star Trek: Voyager, if not the entire Star Trek franchise, is the "Year of Hell" duology. The story from the show's fourth season pitted the crew of Voyager against a unique threat that tested their perseverance more than any previous enemy had.
Now, new DLC for survival game Star Trek: Voyager - Across the Unknown will revisit the iconic storyline. Players won't just be able to relive "Year of Hell," however, they'll be able to change the course of history.
Daedalic Entertainment has announced that the fourth Expansion Pass DLC for Across the Unknown will be inspired by "Year of Hell". The announcement describes the DLC as "a self-contained mini-campaign of three new sectors." The DLC will be released on Dec. 9. As in the show, the game will pit players against the Krenim captain Annorax as he wields a weapon that allows him to alter history and destroy entire species. As a standalone expansion, Year of Hell won't affect any of players' main-game save files.
Unlike the base game, which sees the Voyager trying to rebuild and survive with few resources, Year of Hell will invert the traditional gameplay experience. Players will start with a fully repaired and fully equipped ship before encountering the Krenim. While damaged rooms will be repairable, lost decks will cause players to lose every room, and players can lose up to five decks. Crew members can also die, leaving the Voyager crew scrambling to stay alive. Daedalic Entertainment says that the goal of the DLC "isn't to finish with a perfect ship. It's to see how many of your crew are still standing after twelve months."
Star Trek: Voyager - Across the Unknown will let you ally with the enemy for Year of Hell
If players want to follow the plot of the "Year of Hell" episodes, that is absolutely possible, but Daedalic has given players another option. While Captain Janeway can make alliances with a number of friendly ships, she can also choose to join Annorax. "Help Annorax repair the timeline, once he agrees to stop erasing civilizations. Whether he keeps that promise is another matter. Your senior officers may not share your enthusiasm, and their disapproval grows as the months pass."
Depending on which path players take, the other inhabitants of the sectors will have shifting opinions of the Voyager and act accordingly. The base game is required, but those who own the Expansion Pass will automatically receive Year of Hell when it is released. In the meantime, you can rewatch the "Year of Hell" episodes to prepare for your own fight against Annorax.
