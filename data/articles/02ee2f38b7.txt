There’s no denying that 2026 has been a slower year for the iPad lineup. Only the iPad Air and iPad Mini received updates (with nothing new for the iPad or iPad Pro since 2025), and in June, the entire lineup saw a painful price hike of $100 or $200, depending on the model. But that doesn’t mean you have to abandon your dreams of getting a great deal on a great iPad this year. The best Black Friday iPad deals could be exactly what you've been waiting for.

iPad Mini (2026) for $599 ($100 Off)
I was delighted to see that Apple’s brand-new iPad Mini is already on sale—especially for this much. There’s a lot I loved about the latest iPad Mini, which comes with the A20 Pro chip and an OLED screen, but the price was a tough pill to swallow. But for $100 off, it feels completely worth the money. It’s better than the previous model in every way, with a thinner tablet, a slightly larger screen, and a front-facing camera moved to landscape for better video calls.
iPad for $379 ($70 Off)
If you’re trying to save the most cash, the base iPad is the absolute cheapest. You might also appreciate the larger screen over the iPad Mini. The standard $50 discount has been upped to $70 off for Black Friday, which makes it a great time to buy the base iPad. It’s particularly attractive if you need something for an upcoming flight or to keep kids busy on a long holiday road trip.
iPad Air for $649 ($100 Off)
The iPad Air is the in-between iPad. It’s not as cheap as the base iPad, nor does it have the Tandem OLED screen and high-end features of the iPad Pro. And yet, I think it’s the iPad people should buy if they plan to use it for work. That’s mainly because it has a powerful M4 chip and can connect to a Magic Keyboard with full trackpad support. The most recent versions of iPadOS make windowing much easier, and Apple even made the cursor work more like macOS. After all, most people don’t need an OLED screen for word processing, answering emails, and the occasional Zoom call.
When you’re ready to use it more like a traditional tablet, just pull it off its magnetic cover, and it’s ready for your fingers or an Apple Pencil. The 11-inch model is still small enough to not feel overwhelming in your hands. And while the $649 sale isn’t particularly rare, it’s not a bad price for a tablet that works well as a laptop replacement.
Other iPads to Consider
iPad Pro 11-inch for $1,049: It’s surprising that the iPad Pro hasn’t been heavily discounted at all for Black Friday. At $1,049, it’s cheaper than the 13-inch MacBook Air, which uses the same M5 chip. The difference? You still have to buy the Magic Keyboard separately to pair with the iPad Pro. All in all, the iPad ends up being much more expensive.
iPad Mini (2024) for $499: Like the newer iPad Mini, the 2024 model is also $100 off. The upgrades in the 2026 model are worth the money, but if you just need something simple to take notes or read books on, this previous model will do the trick.
iPad Pro 13-Inch for $1,697: The 13-inch iPad Pro has been fluctuating in price since we started monitoring Black Friday sales. At one point, it was as low as $1,349—a terrific deal for Apple’s flagship iPad. It is a bit of a niche tablet because of the price alone, but if you want an iPad that can properly replace a high-end laptop, the 13-inch model is big enough (and fast enough) for the job. The sale has ended since we last checked, but we’re keeping it on our list—it’s worth checking in case it goes on sale again.
