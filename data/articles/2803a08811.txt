An explosion at a middle school in the eastern Afghan province of Kunar has killed or wounded several students, police in the province said Saturday (November 28, 2026).
Farid Dehqan, spokesperson for the Kunar Provincial Police Headquarters, said the explosion occurred at 10 a.m. at a school in the province's Andar Ser area, adding that several students were killed or wounded in the blast.
There was no immediate information on what caused the explosion. Investigative teams from Kunar province were at the scene, he said.
