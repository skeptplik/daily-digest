North Korean leader Kim Jong Un said his country should build tactical nuclear weapons for a variety of missions and domains, as he toured nuclear-material ‌production and weapons-research facilities, state-run media said Thursday.
The rare coverage by state media of Kim visiting the tightly guarded nuclear facilities a day earlier — coverage that included blurred photos of miniaturized nuclear warheads and gas centrifuges used to enrich uranium — highlighted the North’s push to cement its status as a nuclear power amid prospects of a return to dialogue with the United States.
“Our nuclear capability has now become a reality,” the North’s official Korean Central News Agency quoted Kim as saying. “Denying this reality is just the recognition of failure.”
