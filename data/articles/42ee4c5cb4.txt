It’s a little too early for holiday nail art manicures, and maybe even too late for a fall palette of maples, golds, and pumpkin polish shades. So, what to do in the weeks of November ahead when it comes to your nail look?
“November calls for rich, warming shades such as chocolate brown, mocha, espresso, and caramel, alongside deep cherry, oxblood, and olive green,” says Townhouse founder Juanita Huber-Millet. “Our Flawless collection captures the season beautifully, with coffee-inspired shades like Chai Latte and Mocha, and timeless reds such as Red Apple and Cherry for an effortlessly chic fall manicure.”
Huber-Millet also recommends burgundy, classic red, and deep plum for a timeless winter shade. “This season, expect modern twists with glossy chrome and cat-eye finishes, colored French tips and delicate gems that add a festive touch without compromising on sophistication,” the professional adds.
