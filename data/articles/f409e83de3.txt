Cathay orders 14 more Airbus jets as investment commitments top HK$150 billion
‘These new orders bring our total aircraft deliveries from 2026 to 120, bringing us closer to our plan of 150 new aircraft deliveries,’ group chief executive says
Hong Kong’s Cathay Group has ordered 14 additional Airbus aircraft to support the expansion of its regional services, taking its total investment commitments to more than HK$150 billion (US$19 billion) for the decade to 2035.
The group said on Wednesday that the orders would support growth at both its flagship carrier, Cathay Pacific, and budget airline HK Express, bringing planned aircraft deliveries from 2026 to 120.
“These new orders bring our total aircraft deliveries from 2026 to 120, bringing us closer to our plan of 150 new aircraft deliveries and building a network serving 150 destinations in the next 10 years, if market conditions are favourable,” group chief executive Ronald Lam Siu-por said.
