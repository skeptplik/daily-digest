Crunchyroll has officially unveiled its Black Friday deals for 2026. From now until November 30, both new and select returning subscribers can save on six months of any Crunchyroll subscription.
The anime streaming service is offering discounts on all three of its ad-free plans, offering between 50% and 60% in monthly savings. Fan plans have dropped from $8.99 to $3.99/month, Mega Fan has dropped from $13.99 to $5.99/month, and the Ultimate Fan tier has dropped from $17.99 to $8.99/month.
Crunchyroll has made a number of changes to its subscription model over the past year, with a big emphasis on the brand’s features outside of streaming. Read on for a breakdown of what’s currently included in each plan.
Crunchyroll Black Friday Deals - Each Tier Explained
The big shift in Crunchyroll’s streaming set-up started when the service removed its free streaming tier at the end of last year. Soon after, the service increased prices while moving some features and benefits around to try and justify the hike.
If you’re in it for the streaming, no plan has ads and every plan gets simulcasts. Otherwise, here’s how things stand now:
Crunchyroll Plans, Explained
Crunchyroll Fan basically gets you the Crunchyroll anime library. You get limited offline downloads and can only stream on one device at a time.
Crunchyroll Mega Fan ups your simultaneous streaming limit to four devices and opens up Crunchyroll’s Game Vault, which offers over 100 anime-adjacent games. You also get access to the Crunchyroll Store with free shipping on orders over $50. Yes, store access is now restricted to the most expensive subscriptions.
For reference, the entire Crunchyroll Store is now operating with a 'limited drop' model attached to specific licenses, like a recent Chainsaw Man collab.
Finally, the most expensive Crunchyroll Ultimate Fan tier lets you stream all the anime on up to six devices and tacks in access to Crunchyroll Manga, a relatively new service.
The rest of the perks are all about the Store, where you’ll get early access to drops and free shipping on every order. You also get a swag bag if you subscribe for 12 consecutive months, which, unfortunately, this deal won’t cover.
Note that the deal seemingly removes the option for Crunchyroll’s usual seven-day free trial. If you want to get a better sense of the service before paying up, Crunchyroll is still kind enough to let you at least see its libraries without a subscription. I’d recommend scrolling through the full list of anime, manga, and games before making a decision.
