A diamond’s sparkle might be what catches your attention, but its specifications are what deserve a closer inspection. After all, two seemingly identical stones can carry wildly different price tags, and a higher carat weight doesn’t automatically translate to a more impressive-looking diamond. Engagement ring shopping, as it turns out, involves a little more mathematics than most romantic comedies would have you believe.
That’s precisely where Ritani enters the conversation. The fine jewelry retailer is taking a more information-driven approach to diamond shopping, pairing customizable engagement rings with digital tools that help customers understand what they’re buying.
How to Buy Diamonds Online Without the Guesswork
Its selection spans the classics and then some: four-prong solitaires, oval-cut diamonds, vintage-inspired marquise settings, and three-stone arrangements with baguette accents. Each design can be personalized with a natural or lab-grown center diamond, along with a choice of precious metals and available setting details.
But the real distinction lies in how Ritani approaches the less glamorous aspects of purchasing a diamond. Its AI gemologist, Oscar, helps translate technical terminology into plain English, while comparison tools let shoppers evaluate everything from cut and clarity to physical measurements and price per carat.
The retailer also provides market pricing comparisons and breakdowns of its own markups, offering a degree of visibility that isn’t always easy to come by when shopping for fine jewelry. It’s useful information for anyone trying to distinguish a worthwhile investment in craftsmanship from a higher price tag.
Those who prefer a more tactile experience can arrange complimentary in-person previews for eligible diamonds. Ritani also offers bespoke design services, with engagement rings made to order and handcrafted at its workshop in White Plains, New York.
One important detail to keep in mind: Ritani’s engagement ring settings are priced separately from their center diamonds, so the final cost depends on your selections.
As for the designs themselves, there’s no shortage of beautiful options. Ahead, we’re highlighting the best Ritani engagement rings for different aesthetics, preferences, and budgets, because a little homework can make a rather expensive decision feel much more rewarding.
The Nivea Round Cut 14kt White Gold Petal Solitaire Engagement Ring
Florals for an engagement ring? Actually, quite lovely. Ritani takes the familiar solitaire silhouette and gives it a botanical rethink, swapping conventional prongs for delicate, petal-inspired detailing. The result is a 14-karat white gold setting that feels romantic without veering into overly sentimental territory.	
The Teya Round Cut 14kt White Gold Four-Prong Solitaire Engagement Ring
For the bride whose wardrobe consists of beautifully tailored trousers, crisp button-downs, and an enviable collection of vintage accessories, this solitaire feels like a natural extension of her personal style. Its 1.8-millimeter white gold band and traditional four-prong setting embrace minimalism without making the diamond an afterthought.
The Stella Oval Cut 14kt White Gold Classic Solitaire Engagement Ring with Hidden Halo
An oval-cut diamond already has a certain elegance about it, but Ritani’s Stella takes things a step further with a hidden halo of smaller diamonds tucked beneath the center stone. The 1.8-millimeter white gold band keeps the silhouette beautifully streamlined, while the unexpected sparkle from the side adds a little intrigue to an otherwise classic solitaire.
The Annia Radiant Cut 14kt White Gold Diamond Band Engagement Ring
Minimalism is lovely, but sometimes a little extra sparkle is entirely justified. The Annia pairs a radiant-cut diamond with a white gold band punctuated by smaller stones, creating a design that feels more expressive than a traditional solitaire. It’s an excellent choice for someone who appreciates clean lines but isn’t particularly interested in keeping things too simple.
The Geneva Oval Cut 14kt White Gold Four-Prong Solitaire Engagement Ring
The beauty of a more affordable engagement ring setting is the flexibility it leaves for choosing your center diamond. Ritani’s Geneva starts at $880 for its 14-karat white gold setting, which pairs a graceful twisted band with a four-prong oval diamond mount. That leaves shoppers free to prioritize the carat weight, cut, and quality of their chosen stone.
The Emma Round Cut 14kt White Gold Tapered Baguette Diamond Engagement Ring
There’s something undeniably chic about a three-stone engagement ring, especially when the diamonds aren’t all cut from the same cloth. Ritani’s Emma pairs a brilliant round-cut centerpiece with two tapered baguettes, creating a pleasing contrast between soft curves and sharp geometry. Set in 14-karat white gold, it’s a little Art Deco, a little modern, and entirely deserving of a second glance.
The Merida Round Cut 14kt White Gold Pavé Vaulted Diamond Engagement Ring
A beautifully constructed piece of jewelry has much in common with a well-tailored garment: the proportions matter, the details deserve attention, and nothing should feel accidental. Ritani’s Merida applies that philosophy to bridal jewelry, using a split-shank silhouette and delicate diamond accents to give its round-cut centerpiece a more elaborate frame.	
The Nota Marquise Cut 14kt White Gold Vintage Peony Halo Engagement Ring
If your Pinterest boards are populated by archival runway looks, antique jewelry, and the occasional Victorian-era portrait, Ritani’s Nota might just be your kind of engagement ring. Its marquise-cut diamond sits within an elaborate floral halo, giving the 14-karat white gold design the sort of old-world character that makes contemporary jewelry feel almost too well-behaved.
Design Your Own Custom Engagement Ring
Calling all perfectionists, Pinterest-board obsessives, and anyone with a suspiciously specific idea of their dream engagement ring. Ritani’s custom design experience lets you take matters into your own hands, from selecting a natural or lab-grown diamond to choosing the metal and setting. And for ideas that require a little extra expertise, the brand’s in-house designers are available to help.
The Suri Round Cut 14kt White Gold Hidden Halo Diamond Engagement Ring
A natural diamond that’s been millions of years in the making deserves a setting that knows how to show it off. Enter Ritani’s Suri, which places a round-cut center stone above a discreet halo of smaller diamonds. The arrangement is romantic without being overly precious, leaving the real geological marvel to command most of the attention.
