A leader of the Scattered Spider cybercrime collective has been sentenced to six years in prison in the United States for stealing millions in cryptocurrency and conspiring to hack a dozen companies.
US prosecutors charged 24-year-old British man Tyler Robert Buchanan and four other suspects in November with wire fraud and aggravated identity theft in relation to a series of phishing attacks between September 2021 and April 2023.
According to court documents, their victims include companies across a wide range of industries, such as telecommunications, technology, business process outsourcing (BPO), entertainment, and information technology (IT) suppliers, as well as virtual currency providers, cloud communications providers, and individuals.
Buchanan and his accomplices sent hundreds of SMS phishing messages to victim companies' employees' phones (while impersonating the companies or a contracted IT or BPO supplier) to trick them into entering sensitive information (including credentials and personally identifiable information) into phishing pages that looked like their employers' legitimate websites.
The stolen information was used to steal over $11.6 million of virtual currency after compromising victims' cryptocurrency accounts and wallets via Subscriber Identity Module (SIM) swaps (also known as SIM swapping attacks).
"Those thefts had severe, life-altering consequences for many victims and underscore both the seriousness and far-reaching impact of this conspiracy," the prosecutors argued in a sentencing memorandum.
"In April 2023, on a digital device found at Buchanan's Scotland residence, he possessed the names and addresses of numerous individual victims, including a text file that contained cryptocurrency seed phrases and login information for one victim's account," the US Justice Department added.
Buchanan was arrested in Palma de Mallorca, Spain, in June 2024, has been in US federal custody since April 2025, and pleaded guilty to crypto theft charges in April.
Three of his accomplices (Evans Onyeaka Osiebo, Ahmed Hossam Eldin Elbadawy, and Joel Martin Evans) were also charged in November 2024 with wire fraud, wire fraud conspiracy, and aggravated identity theft and are now serving multi-year prison sentences after pleading guilty.
Noah Michael Urban (also known online as Sosa and Elijah), a fourth conspirator and another Scattered Spider key member, has been sentenced to 10 years in prison after pleading guilty to wire fraud and conspiracy charges in April 2025.
Scattered Spider hacking collective
The Scattered Spider gang (also tracked as 0ktapus, Scatter Swine, Octo Tempest, Starfraud, UNC3944, and Muddled Libra) is a loose-knit group of young, English-speaking threat actors that orchestrates attacks through Telegram channels, Discord servers, and hacker forums.
Scattered Spider has also partnered with several Russian ransomware operations since early 2023, including BlackCat/AlphV, Qilin, and RansomHub.
This cybercrime group was previously linked to breaches at multiple high-profile companies and organizations, including Caesars, Riot Games, MailChimp, Twilio, DoorDash, and Reddit.
According to the FBI, they're using a wide range of tactics to breach corporate victims' networks, including social engineering, multi-factor authentication (MFA) bombing (targeted MFA fatigue), phishing, and SIM swapping.
Some Scattered Spider members have also previously been linked to "The Com," another hacking collective behind violent incidents and cyberattacks.
