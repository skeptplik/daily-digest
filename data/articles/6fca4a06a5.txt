Josh Addo-Carr free to leave Parramatta Eels as club rejects contract extension
In short:
Josh Addo-Carr is free to leave Parramatta immediately after the Eels revealed they would not offer him a contract extension.
Addo-Carr has one year remaining on his deal, and the Eels are happy for him to see out his deal, but will not stand in the way of him joining a rival club.
What's next?
The 31-year-old is due back at pre-season training with the Eels next week, as his management looks to secure the flyer a future elsewhere.
