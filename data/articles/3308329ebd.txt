Amazon's Black Friday sale isn't going live until midnight tonight, but some of the best LEGO Pokémon deals have already been dropping early. That includes one of the most expensive sets in the entire theme, the giant LEGO Poké Ball. The price has dropped by 20%, which is a pretty significant discount for a set that just came out in October 2026.
This Poké Ball is more than just a really big LEGO display, it also houses some of the best minifigures and details from the franchise's 30-year history. The set's design is the classic spherical ball that operates on hinges so you can open it up to the scene inside. Inside the actual Poké Ball you get a miniature diorama recreating some iconic moments from the games. In the lid of the ball you'll see Professor Oak offering you a choice of three starter Pokémon, while the base features a Red minifigure battling a Picknicker's Eevee with Pikachu. At 2,386 pieces, this is an 18+ build that will take you a while to fully put together.
As far as LEGO Black Friday deals go, you're likely not going to see a better price than this any time soon. We expect Amazon to drop more discounts on sets tomorrow, but 20% off one of the most popular new sets of 2026 is going to be hard to beat at any retailer.
