Ousted Bangladeshi Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina plans to return home from exile in India on December 16, the day the country celebrates as Victory Day when it won independence after a war with Pakistan.
Ms. Hasina went into exile in India on August 5, 2024, after a student-led mass uprising forced her to end her 15-year rule. She was sentenced to death in absentia for crimes against humanity involving the uprising in which hundreds of protesters were killed. Ms. Hasina rejected the verdict and has said she is ready fight the case once she is back.

In the statement on Tuesday (November 24, 2026) announcing the planned date of her return, she urged current Prime Minister Tarique Rahman to arrange her return on the national airline.
“Since Sheikh Hasina was flown to India on a Bangladesh aircraft on August 5th, 2024, she now calls upon the government to arrange for her air transport from Delhi back to Dhaka,” spokesman Syed Mozammel Ali said in the statement.
“It is deeply symbolic that she has chosen 16 December, our Victory Day, for her planned return to Bangladesh. We firmly believe that she will celebrate this historic day among her people, honoring the great victory achieved under the leadership of her father, Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujibur Rahman,” Abu Obaidha Arin, an aide to Ms. Hasina, told The Associated Press.
“We are equally confident that, she will definitely return and under her leadership post return, Bangladesh will once again advance towards peace, stability and prosperity,” Mr. Arin said.
Earlier this month, in an interview with The Associated Press, Ms. Hasina said she would return to the country in December at any cost, and even death could not prevent her from returning home.
