On Saturday night, Keke Palmer made an appearance at the 2026 Kids’ Choice Awards in Los Angeles. The “Nope” actress wore a semi-casual outfit for the occasion, sporting a corset top with floral-patterned trousers and a striking pair of metallic gold pointed-toe pumps that added a touch of elegance to the otherwise played-down look.
Palmer’s outfit was chic but also meshed well with the Kids’ Choice Awards quirky, childlike atmosphere. She sported a form-fitted magenta top and accessorized heavily, wearing multiple statement rings and bracelets on both hands, a sparkling chain-link necklace, thick silver hoop earrings, and a pair of studded sunglasses. Her pants were quite eye-catching and slightly eccentric, white trousers embellished with nature motifs including images of birds, branches, and flowers. Some of the gold hues found throughout Palmer’s pants paired well with her footwear. The actress wore platform pumps with a pointed toe and slim, stiletto heel with a glossy, reflective texture in a deep shade of gold.
Palmer is clearly a fan of gold footwear, having been spotted in gold heels multiple times this year alone. Back in June, while attending the 2026 Tribeca Film Festival, she was seen in a pair of Aquazzura’s So Nude Sandal 105 in gold, a slingback stiletto made from a soft-gold specchio material with an ultra-slim, almost imperceptible toe strap. Earlier in November, she wore another pair of Aquazzura shoes, this time opting for gold sandals at the CFDA Awards with thick crisscrossing toe bands, an ankle strap, and a stiletto heel.

The Kids’ Choice Awards are a longstanding hallmark of the Nickelodeon network where the brand’s young viewers are invited to vote for nominees across television, movies, and pop culture in general. Winners receive statues of orange blimps adorned with white Nickelodeon lettering. This year, Palmer attended as a presenter. She gave away the 2026 award for Best Female Creator, which went to the teenage social media personality Salish Matter.
