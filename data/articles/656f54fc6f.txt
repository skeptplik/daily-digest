After launching a well-received Día de Muertos collection for men and women in October, Timberland is keeping its holiday-themed releases coming with a new set of boots that would look right at home under a Christmas tree.
The just-launched Timberland Premium 6-Inch C.F. Stead Waterproof Suede Boot collection includes options for men, women and kids, each of which is decked out in holiday-themed shades.

The men’s, junior and youth sizes of the boot don a dark green premium suede sourced from historic English tannery C.F. Stead. The dark green suede style includes a two-tone midsole in white and gum, two-tone white-and-gold shoelaces, a C.F. Stead-branded hangtag and a secondary hangtag in a mistletoe shape. The green boot also comes packaged with a set of black shoelaces for those looking to switch up the out-of-the-box look.
For women’s sizes, Timberland gave the boot a full burgundy makeover which extends to its signature padded leather collar. Like the green style, the burgundy colorway comes with white-and-gold shoelaces along with an alternate set of ribbon laces in matching burgundy.
The outdoor footwear brand’s latest collection sources its fine suede from C.F. Stead, a heritage tannery founded in 1904 by Charles F. Stead in Leeds, England. It’s among the most venerable tanneries in the world and continues to produce high-quality leathers and suedes more than a century after it was established.
Timberland has previously worked with C.F. Stead on a 2024 Indigo Suede Icons Pack, which included the footwear brand’s 6-Inch Boot, Euro Hiker and 3-Eye Lug boat shoe.
Along with the just-launched holiday pack, a new collaboration between Supreme and Timberland was also announced Tuesday featuring three bandana print colorways of the Field Boot.
The Timberland Premium 6-Inch C.F. Stead Waterproof Suede Boot is available now from timberland.com in men’s ($230, style code TB0A446JAGS), women’s ($190, style code TB0A5Q89AGZ), junior ($130, style code TB0A6BETAGS) and youth ($110, style code TB0A2CTGAGS).
