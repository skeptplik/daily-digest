US strikes Iran’s Qeshm Island ahead of midterms as Trump says deal ‘could happen’
US officials say covert strikes were aimed at deterring Tehran’s attacks on commercial ships in the Strait of Hormuz, according to report
Iran’s Qeshm Island was struck on Monday, The Wall Street Journal reported, citing US officials, some of whom framed the strikes as defensive and aimed at deterring Tehran’s attacks on commercial ships in the critical chokepoint.
Iranian media reported that five blasts were heard in rapid succession across the island and two aircraft had been spotted.
