On Grand Theft Auto 6's launch day, more than half of everyone playing on PS5 and Xbox was playing GTA 6.
Citing new data from Circana's Player Engagement Tracker, analyst Mat Piscatella said on Bluesky that 61 percent of US PlayStation 5 players and 56 percent of global Xbox players who were active on 19th November "engaged with Grand Theft Auto 6".
PS5 players averaged 513 minutes in GTA 6 that day, which is over eight and a half hours, while Xbox players averaged 494 minutes, or around eight and a quarter hours.
"Both the DAU [daily active users] and minutes played engagement rates are absolutely bonkers," Piscatella wrote.
For comparison, Fortnite had the second-highest engagement rate that day, with 10 percent of active players across both platforms playing it.
Piscatella also shared the top five games that GTA 6 players had been playing in the two weeks before launch, between 6th and 19th November: Fortnite, Call of Duty, Grand Theft Auto 5 (Remastered), Roblox, and NBA 2K27.
