Firefighters from the Los Angeles County Fire Department rescued a woman trapped in a flood control channel in City of Industry, California, on Friday.
Footage shows a helicopter crew locating the woman before a paramedic is lowered into the channel to reach her.
The paramedic secured the woman to a rescue winch, allowing both to be lifted out of the channel and brought safely to the ground.
The operation was carried out by the department’s Air Operations team.
