Few hair colors are as iconic and instantly nostalgic as Geri Halliwell's natural red. The hue, synonymous with her nickname “Ginger Spice,” has, of course, evolved over the years, from copper highlights woven through fiery red lengths to a full head of golden blond and lighter strawberry-blond tones.
For winter, Halliwell has swapped her signature brighter tones for a richer shade. Enter dark honey hair. "What I absolutely love about Geri’s dark honey hair is the richness and depth of the color. A beautiful chestnut base with ribbons of warm honey, caramel, and subtle amber runs through the lengths and ends. It’s a wonderful evolution of her signature warmth, but with a softer, more understated finish," says celebrity hairstylist and Remi Cachet expert Adam Reed.
At the premiere of Only Murders in London, Halliwell wore her fresh hair color in a soft side part with dressed-out waves, a combination Reed says helps different tones catch the light.
The rich, luminous color adds subtle dimension that doesn't focus on dramatic placement, but on healthy-looking hair. "To recreate the look, I’d ask your colorist to maintain that beautiful brunette depth through the roots, introducing delicately placed honey and caramel lights through the mid-lengths and ends. A personalized gloss can bring those tones together and create that beautifully reflective finish," he adds.
Alternatively, Reed recommends his “just a little filler” approach, using extensions as color tools placed strategically. This approach uses additional hair to enhance color, density, and movement, rather than focusing solely on length. "By carefully selecting complementary shades of chestnut, honey, and caramel, we can introduce tonal variation without necessarily having to chemically lighten the natural hair," he notes.
Whether you're looking for a complete hair refresh this winter or simply want a subtle way to enhance your natural color without fully committing to a new look, Halliwell's dark honey hair is the perfect inspiration.
