The Nintendo Switch has been out more than a year at this point, which means that some of the coolest Switch 2 accessories are finally getting discounts. I'm not talking about any of the first-party accessories of course, I'm talking about the third-party sellers that have taken it beyond just functionality. More specifically, I'm talking about the Piranha Plant Switch 2 dock.
We discovered the Piranha Plant Switch 2 dock back in April of this year when it got its first discount. Back then, the discounted price was $96.99. It was extremely popular at that price back then, but it's steadily gotten less expensive over the months. The latest discount from Amazon's Black Friday sale brings the price down to $64.33.
It's worth noting that this discount is an Amazon Lightning Deal, rather than a straight-up Black Friday discount. That means there's limited stock available at the price shown. At the time of writing, it was only 50% claimed, so there's a time limit on this deal right now. As this is a third-party seller, rather than Amazon itself, it's certainly possible that the discount could remain once the deal is fully claimed, but I wouldn't count on it.
As for what makes this thing worth your money, you only need to gaze upon its beauty. The Amazon reviews are almost entirely positive and it even lights up. This is a fusion of video games and Nintendo vibes that just works on so many levels.
The PowKong Piranha Dock Gen 2, as it's officially called, works just like the standard dock a new Switch 2 comes with. It can handle 4K 60Hz display output and includes an active cooling system at the base of the Piranha Plant's head to avoid overheating.
The one major thing it's missing compared to the out-of-the-box Switch 2 dock is an ethernet port, but that's really only an issue if you rely on a direct wired connection for internet access. If you're just in it for the aesthetics, you should be perfectly fine.
Nintendo's Sale is Coming Soon
If you're looking for deals on Switch 2 consoles and games, you won't have to wait much longer. Nintendo's official Black Friday sale kicks off on Sunday, November 22 this year. Nintendo has confirmed the sale will include a discount on a Switch 2 bundle and discounts on first-party games like Donkey Kong Bananza.
