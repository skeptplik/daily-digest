The record-breaking launch of Grand Theft Auto 6 drove a combined 2.3 million downloads of the franchise's mobile ports in its first week.
According to data from AppMagic (via PocketGamer), downloads increased from 590,000 to 2.3 million between November 16 and November 22. GTA: Vice City – Definitive Edition was the top installed game during this period, with over 1.1 million installs.
The peak for GTA mobile port downloads occurred in December 2023 after the GTA Trilogy launched on Netflix, reaching eight million downloads between December 11 and December 17.
AppMagic also estimated GTA 6's impact on other games in the franchise on Steam. Daily active users for GTA 5 – Enhanced Edition rose from 407,000 to 718,000 between November 16 and November 21.
DAUs playing GTA Legacy increased from 293,000 to 494,000. GTA 4: The Complete Edition grew from 68,000 to 115,000, the largest increase for a GTA title on Steam since AppMagic began tracking data in 2024.
Other apps benefited from GTA 6's launch as well. The PlayStation App reached 1.3 million downloads, more than double the previous week and triple its typical level. PS Remote Play, the Xbox app, and Twitch also saw increases, with Twitch installs doubling to 1.3 million during launch week.
GTA 6 marked the largest launch in PlayStation history, attracting over 39 million unique players and logging 683 million hours of gameplay. The average playtime per player was 17.4 hours.
New console usage also spiked, with GTA 6 as the first game played on 70% of new PlayStation 5 consoles. Sony reported that GTA 6's launch day saw the highest number of connected consoles in a single day, excluding the console's launch and holiday periods.
Rockstar previously announced that GTA 6 pre-orders reached 32.5 million units, generating $3.79 billion in first-day sales revenue.
