There’s no shame in diving headfirst into party mode with a full sequined gown or embellished suit, but there are plenty of ways to dress for holiday fêtes, cocktails, and gatherings. As a minimalist, I tend to opt for more understated ensembles; however, that doesn’t mean they’re not interesting. In fact, I’d say they’re incredibly chic.
What does minimalist party dressing look like in 2026? Opera-style coats, column skirts, evening jackets, and cummerbunds are a good place to start. Think of it as a thread of the Art Deco–inspired trend we talked about so much this fall. And you needn’t stray from bold jewelry or sumptuous texture to achieve minimalism—in fact, subtly decorative details and rich material choices make monochrome color palettes all the more sophisticated.
Vogue’s Edit of Minimalist, Occasion-Worthy Style
Most recently I wore my black silk column skirt with a satin button-down blouse and tied a thin white fringed sash around my waist to attend a cocktail-party wedding. I usually wear a button-down shirt by day, so opting for one in a more formal material, like satin, for an evening event was a no-brainer. Sometimes just adding an elegant evening bag alone switches an everyday look to evening-ready. Personally, I’m most excited to try one of the statement evening jackets below with my black trousers for the next party I head to.
The best part about taking a minimalist approach to holiday is that your looks can live on well beyond the season. Silky trousers, a feathered jacket, or velvet shoes may lack glimmer, but they’re pieces you can rely on for various occasions. Of course, if you’d rather keep your ready-to-wear look simple and clean, adding accessories is the easiest way to conjure the holiday spirit in the least obvious way: Chan Luu’s black agate drop earrings or one of Liffner’s satin clutches are perfect examples.
Ahead, 10 sleek outfits to master minimalist holiday dressing before the first party invite hits your inbox.
A White Button-Up and Column Skirt
Inspired by one of Carolyn Bessette-Kennedy’s iconic evening looks, a floor-length skirt and crisp shirting are an unbeatable formula for modern party dressing. A skirt in black velvet makes it all the more holiday appropriate.
Opt for a Collarless Opera Overcoat
A long, streamlined overcoat feels especially chic when styled as a top. We suggest wearing one over silk wide-leg pants and a dainty bralette.
Borrow From the Boys: Try a Cummerbund
While undeniably trendy right now, the cummerbund itself is a complete classic when it comes to formalwear. A trouser with a built-in style is a tailored look well suited for any minimalist with a fête-filled calendar. Bring femininity into the mix with a satin tank, gold drop earrings, and bow-adorned sandals.
Pair a Minidress With a Maximum-Impact Coat
For those who want their coat to be the outfit, all you need is a minidress, tights, and T-strap heels peeking out from underneath a fabulous faux-fur wrap coat. Coat check need not apply here.
Let Your Jewelry Do the Talking
Despite its decorative, bold shape, a sculptural collar feels surprisingly minimalist. With a white long-sleeve dress as your canvas, your jewelry becomes wearable art.
A Fluid LBD With a Considered Splash of Red
Take a page from Diana Vreeland’s playbook—or Matthieu Blazy’s pre-fall 2026 collection—and add interest to a black maxi with a red scarf (any will do!) knotted around your waist. Don’t forget to accent with long strings of beads or pearls.
A Statement Jacket—in Black, of Course
Small but significant details like feathers, grosgrain ribbon, or tassels add an element of surprise to a black evening jacket. Pair with burgundy leather elbow-length gloves and an elongated frame clutch for added sophistication.
A Swishy Sash Over Sculptural Tailoring
Using a long, fringe-trimmed sash as a belt is an unexpected but still festive way to dress up black tailoring.
The Draped or Caped Blouse With Cigarette Pants
Going from the office straight to cocktails? Let a silk blouse and cropped dress pants be your recipe. Make sure to leave the laptop bag behind and pack a small evening bag to bring after you clock out.
Play With Textures: Jacquard and Velvet
Floral jacquard is something we saw a lot in the spring 2027 collections, and it also makes a wonderful option for holiday dressing. Plus, what’s more effortless than a coordinating set?
