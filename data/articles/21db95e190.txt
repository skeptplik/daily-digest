From printing menus to educational posters, technology company HP’s DesignJet T250 printer gives businesses the ability to design and print high-quality materials in house.
Described by the brand as the “world’s smallest plotters”, with HP DesignJet T250 users can customise thousands of professionally created templates and print content on-site when required.
The printer can be used for everything from designing large-format posters to advertising a new product or an event.
It can also print branded communications or create retail displays, among other materials. According to HP, this benefits multiple industries, from food and culture to education and design.
HP explained that this flexibility redefines the professional printing workflow by giving businesses control over the creative and operational process, reducing their reliance on external printing providers.
This also results in increasingly flexible timelines, with in-house designers able to tweak their design, then quickly print another copy.
According to HP, the printer also gives brands and companies greater control over costs.
This is particularly useful to smaller businesses, entrepreneurs and creative studios, it explained, where developing professional marketing materials can be expensive and time-consuming.
Instead, HP DesignJet T250 places control into user’s hands by giving them a fast and flexible way to bring creative ideas to life.
According to HP, click software incorporated into the device further streamlines workflow by providing an easy print experience and helping ensure prints are produced as intended.
“By bringing design and large-format printing together in one accessible solution, the HP DesignJet T250 helps small businesses and creative professionals create impactful visual communications without the complexity traditionally associated with professional printing,” the company said.
The HP DesignJet T250 is compact enough to be placed in small workspaces, offices or studios. It was made from at least 40-per-cent recycled plastic and is the first HP large-format plotter to contain recycled metal.
Users can also save 65 per cent energy when using the printer’s on/off auto-scheduler, according to the brand.
For a limited time, users can purchase an HP DesignJet T250 with a year’s worth of Canva Pro included. According to HP, this gives designers everything they need to create and print professional-quality posters from a single workflow.
“The combination of Canva’s intuitive design platform and the HP DesignJet T250 creates a seamless workflow that allows users to move from concept to final print with ease,” said HP.
