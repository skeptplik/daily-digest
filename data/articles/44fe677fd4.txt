Nothing’s new AI device is the Ear Note, a set of earbuds and their microphone-equipped charging case that can record and transcribe conversations, with a connected app to manage the recordings, transcriptions and AI-generated summaries, similar to Apple’s new Watch feature and devices like Amazon’s Bee wearable. Nothing CEO Carl Pei demonstrated the new device to Bloomberg, but said the company hasn’t yet settled on a price ahead of a planned launch on December 8th.
Nothing’s upcoming Ear Note is a pair of earbuds with AI transcription
The new AI device from Nothing can record and transcribe audio with AI from the earbuds or via microphones on their charging case.
The report also says Nothing’s rumored plans for an AI smartwatch and smart glasses are delayed, while it will be “pursuing a more aggressive smartphone release schedule next year than it did in 2026.” In a post on X, Pei said this is “the first of many products building a hardware context layer for AI. Rather than ask you to carry a new product, we took something you already wear and trust and made it intelligent.”
According to Bloomberg, the earbuds will “support interactions with various AI agents, including a more basic voice-control system developed by Nothing, letting users engage in back-and-forth conversations.” Users will also be able to record themselves with a “Talk” button on the case, like Nothing previously included on the Ear 3, which also has recording capabilities. Earlier this year, Nothing introduced an Audio Snapshot recording feature in the Ear 3A. Anker’s Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro Max can also record directly to the earbuds’ case and generate AI transcripts and summaries in an app.
