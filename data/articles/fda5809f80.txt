EU Budget talks: France seeks to protect Common Agricultural Policy subsidies
Issued on:
Share
Leaders from EU countries are meeting in Brussels on Thursday and Friday for another round of talks on the EU's next multi-year budget. For France, the main aim of the negotiations is to prevent a reduction in the funding allocated to the Common Agricultural Policy, a subsidy vital for French agriculture – a position Macron made clear in a meeting with farmers unions in Paris on Wednesday.
