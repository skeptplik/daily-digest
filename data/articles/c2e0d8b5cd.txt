Nature has brewed a wicked cocktail in Galerina indica. It is the first mushroom known to make both the hallucinogen psilocybin and lethal compounds from a family called amatoxins. This group of deadly poisons includes those found in the death cap (Amanita phalloides), one of the world’s most poisonous mushrooms.
