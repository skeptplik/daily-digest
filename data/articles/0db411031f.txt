French far-right leader Bardella faces fresh anti-Semitism accusations in media reports
Issued on: Modified:
Share
French far-right leader Jordan Bardella faced fresh accusations of past antisemitic remarks on Thursday, as French media published an additional trove of comments purportedly made in chats from when he was a teenager in 2013. The initial allegations against Bardella, the president of the far-right National Rally party, were published by investigative outlet ‌Mediapart in September. It reported that he had made a slew of antisemitic and conspiratorial comments. Bardella denies making antisemitic remarks. FRANCE 24's French Politics Editor Marc Perelman tells us more.
