Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO after his resignation earlier this month. The likes of Donald Trump, Elon Musk and Sam Altman welcomed the decision as Nvidia shares surged nearly 35%. The internet has thoughts. And jokes.
Jensen Huang wasn't unemployed for very long, as the divisive chipmaker is back as CEO of Nvidia.
