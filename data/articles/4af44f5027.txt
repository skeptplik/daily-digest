Washington – The posters showed one thing, but U.S. President Donald Trump did his thing.
Fresh off a four-day visit to China and the Philippines, Trump on Friday held a heavily anticipated news conference at the White House, billed as an opportunity to sell the successes of his trip to the American public.
A placard beside Trump’s podium listed a series of broad agreements the president had reached with Chinese President Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin, which included a reaffirmation that nuclear war would be futile for all nations involved. Another poster touted deals the White House said had been inked during the trip.
