Grand Theft Auto (GTA) VI became the largest video game release of all time last week and while it is hard to say exactly how many people are playing the game (current estimates say at least 30 million), there are plenty of other jaw-dropping data points we do know.
Data on streaming the game suggests that only two days into its release a cumulative 364.5m hours have been watched – roughly 41,000 years of content. Put another way, that translates to more than 41,000 years worth of people riffling through cash registers, hanging drywall, birding, diving or hijacking jets. That’s according to streaming data from Stream Charts, a data and analytics company that has been tracking Rockstar Game’s long-awaited title.
Since releasing last Thursday on PS5 and Xbox game consoles, Sony’s reported pre-sales of 32.5m units amounts to the biggest entertainment release across all mediums. It made $3.8bn in its first day alone.
Online streaming platforms like Twitch, YouTube and Kick make up a major share of how people are tuning into the franchise.
Those who grew up playing video games know that watching people play goes hand in hand with the activity. For this writer, that’s a memory of me and my three brothers struggling for the best viewing angle as we took turns huddled around the original Game Boy.
For Gen Z and Alpha, who grew up in the era of social media, it has been watching folks muck about on popular titles like Minecraft, Fortnite or GTA’s previous iterations.
Notably, there are many platforms to watch gameplay in realtime – including Twitch, YouTube or Kick – and for some, there is real money to be made. Since streaming platforms have monetized, the global market size is projected to be $13.3bn in 2026, according to Grand View Research, a business consulting firm.
The exclusivity of the game, which isn’t available on PC or Nintendo Switch at this point, has also fueled some of the novelty. Looking at average viewership since its launch, it’s possible many of the early backseat gamers might have only wanted to see the game that has been in development for 13 years. Some viewers have also likely moved on to becoming players.
When compared against the next top 10 game releases by their viewership, GTA VI stands apart as a cultural phenomenon. It has more viewership than the next top three titles combined. And we aren’t at a full week of data yet.
GTA VI’s launch viewership is more than twice that of Valorant, a team-based and free-to-play shooting game. Or, put another way, more viewers than the next three combined.
When we think of gaming, we think of the immersive experience itself: Donning a new persona and living out an alternate reality. It turns out that in addition to being the best selling video game of all time, it’s also the most watched release.
Maybe you’ll join the other third of Twitch watching a classic heist.
