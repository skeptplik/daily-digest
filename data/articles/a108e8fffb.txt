Yoshinoya Holdings will transfer its ostrich-related business, currently operated by a subsidiary, to Kanoya Ostrich, a company based in Miyazaki that engages in ostrich farming, the firm announced Thursday.
The operator of the Yoshinoya fast-food gyūdon beef bowl restaurant chain said it decided to sell the ostrich business to redirect management resources toward growth areas.
The company had launched the business as it had seen potential in ostrich meat, which is low in fat and high in protein, as a “fourth meat,” following beef, pork and chicken. In 2024, it introduced an ostrich rice bowl called the Ostrich Don.
It established an integrated business structure covering everything from ostrich farming and research to the development and sale of related products.
However, the business posted an ordinary loss in the fiscal year that ended in February.
The transfer is scheduled to take effect on Jan. 1 next year. The financial terms were not disclosed.
Yoshinoya Holdings expects to book an extraordinary loss of ¥123 million ($772,000) in connection with the transfer but said the impact on its overall earnings would be minor.
