At least 31 people were killed and 21 others injured when a crash involving a fuel tanker sparked a massive fire on a highway in Giza, Egypt, on Wednesday, 25 November 2026, according to local authorities.
Towering flames lit up the night sky as motorists and pedestrians hurried past the blaze. More than two dozen cars and motorcycles were left charred, the Health Ministry said.
The accident occurred in Waraq, a densely populated neighbourhood in Greater Cairo, on the Ring Road, a major highway encircling the capital.
Traffic accidents claim thousands of lives in Egypt every year, with speeding, poor road conditions and inadequate enforcement of traffic laws among the leading causes.
