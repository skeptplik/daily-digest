Naha – The interior of the Seiden main hall of Shuri Castle in Naha was recently unveiled to the media following its restoration after a fire in 2019.
The hall will be open to the public from Nov. 23 after a completion ceremony hosted by the government.
The interior consists of three floors, with the Usasuka throne occupying the center of the first and second floors, where kings of the Ryukyu Kingdom conducted political affairs and ceremonial events.
The second floor features decorations influenced by Chinese culture, including golden dragon pillars and glossy black lacquered handrails.
A tablet measuring 1.4 meters high and 3.5 meters wide, bearing an inscription inspired by a letter bestowed by a Chinese emperor, is displayed on the upper wall.
Based on knowledge gained since the 1992 restoration, the tablet’s background color was changed from vermilion to yellow, while the gilded dragon motifs were recreated as sculptures instead of paintings.
On the first floor, the Usasuka throne is adorned with hanging decorations featuring dragons embroidered with a traditional technique and brightly colored ornamental beads.
Guide panels explaining decorative features and how the hall was used have also been installed ahead of its public opening.
Admission to the interior requires advance reservations. According to the Okinawa Churashima Foundation, which manages Shurijo Castle Park, tickets for the opening day have sold out, although reservations remain available for visits from Nov. 24 onward.
