Relax, it’s probably not a cyber attack
Over the past week, a whole bunch of major tech outages have happened — affecting Microsoft, Apple, major banks, the U.S. National Security Administration, and temporarily grounding all flights across the United States.
While officials and companies have repeatedly insisted that these were not cyber attacks, and certainly not coordinated, the incidents have caused a lot of panic.
We’re going to break down what’s really happening (hint: it’s not a cyber apocalypse), and what it says about our fast-changing tech landscape, with Andrew Couts, WIRED’s director of Security and Investigations.
