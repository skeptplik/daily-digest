Looking for a new laptop this Black Friday? If you’re overdue an upgrade, there are some very real reasons to take advantage of these Black Friday laptop deals.
The rise of AI has hit computing (and all consumer tech) hard. Not only do machines now need around 16GB of RAM to be able to comfortably work with many AI-based workloads, but the majority of the world’s RAM supply comes from three companies.
As a result, stock is low, and RAM (therefore laptop) prices are climbing. Buying a laptop in the Black Friday sales might help you avoid further price hikes in the months to come.
With that in mind, I’ve picked some of the best laptop deals I can find right now, and we’ll update the list as we get closer to Black Friday weekend and Cyber Monday.
