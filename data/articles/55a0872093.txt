Rosario Dawson has shared the first look at her return as Claire Temple in Spider-Man: Brand New Day, marking her long-awaited comeback to the MCU.
Warning! Spoilers for Spider-Man: Brand New Day follow...
Posting on Instagram, Dawson revealed a photo of her in costume for her first Marvel appearance in a decade, standing in nurse scrubs next to Tom Holland's Spider-Man. It's a scene that fans thought they'd never get to see, after it was deleted from the final cut of the movie, disappointing those waiting for Dawson's reappearance.
Now, however, it is being reinstated for the film's extended edition re-release, entitled Spider-Man: [Brand New] Brand New Day, alongside a handful of other moments previously left on the cutting room floor.
"It's a brand new BRAND NEW DAY with our friendly neighborhood Spider-Man...!" Dawson wrote. "So excited to enjoy this record-breaking movie on the big screen (starting on December 4th) with an extended cut of new scenes and surprises… Ahhhh!
"Join me in getting tickets NOW!!!" Dawson continued. "PS: mom can finally share her bts photos❣️"
It's pretty obvious when this scene will take place, as Dawson's character stands next to Tom Holland's Spider-Man who is wearing a hospital gown and his Spidey mask. Of course, Spider-Man finds himself in hospital towards the end of the movie, and Dawson's return will likely answer fan questions surrounding how the superhero got patched up with no one else discovering his identity. (The answer, surely, is that Dawson takes care of him directly — having been used to keeping numerous superhero identities secret in the past.)
Debuting in the very first season of Daredevil back in 2015, Dawson’s no-nonsense nurse quickly became an indispensable ally across its sister Netflix shows: Jessica Jones, Luke Cage, Iron Fist, and The Defenders. Whether she was patching up Matt Murdock in a dimly lit apartment, helping Luke Cage navigate Harlem, or standing her ground against The Hand, Claire earned a fan following for her work sewing up the wounds of New York's vigilante community.
However, after her appearance in the second season of Luke Cage in 2018, Dawson stepped away from the role, leaving fans wondering for years whether Claire Temple would ever cross paths with the broader MCU. With Charlie Cox’s Matt Murdock and Jon Bernthal’s Frank Castle re-entering the fold, Dawson’s return felt like a missing puzzle piece — and Spider-Man: Brand New Day is finally delivering on that promise.
Spider-Man: Brand New Day will be back in theaters on December 4, 2026, now with Rosario Dawson included. What else has Marvel added back into the movie? You'll just have to go along and find out.
