These are the stories making headlines in fashion on Monday.
After RCOS’ Reese Cooper and Sage Nation’s Sage Toda-Nation accused Ssense of using AI to alter product photos of their respective brands, a spokesperson for the Canadian retailer responded that the company used “virtual try-on” technology. However, the company declined to comment on whether the try-on technology is AI-powered. Ssense has since taken down the e-commerce photos from its website. {The New York Times/paywalled}
Paolo Bulgari, the Italian jeweler who helped transform his family’s luxury jewelry brand into a global success, has passed away. He was 89 years old. There is no news about the cause of his death. {Reuters}
Frasers Group kicked off its Harvey Nichols restructuring plan by laying off employees and limiting staff perks. For the latter, Frasers has taken away remote work privileges, temporarily ended employee discounts and replaced swipe badges with a new fingerprint check-in system. Anonymous staff members have criticized the changes for creating a strict, difficult work environment. However, the company believes these overhauls will save costs in the long run. {Business of Fashion/paywalled}
Walmart is attracting wealthy shoppers by offering high-speed convenience. The retailer has invested in fulfillment, shipping and supply-chain technology, enabling same-day delivery as fast as 30 minutes (without sacrificing its low-priced products). As a result, Walmart saw 5.2% revenue growth in its third-quarter 2027 report. {Modern Retail/paywalled}
