Italian model Vittoria Ceretti attended the Los Angeles County Museum of Art’s 15th annual Art+Film Gala this Saturday in an elegantly classic ensemble. The star combined a custom black Gucci gown with a pair of Giuseppe Zanotti pumps.
Ceretti opted for an enduringly sophisticated look: the black evening gown. Her floor length dress featured an off-the-shoulder boat neckline with a cinched, hourglass waistline. However, Ceretti incorporated a bit of a modern twist via the increasingly trendy thigh-high slit, which was a red carpet staple at the 2026 LACMA Gala.
She wore a classic pair of Giuseppe Zanotti Guepiere Pump 105, which featured sheer black mesh paneling with a black leather trim. One of the more noticeable aspects of the shoe is the extra elongated, pointed toe coupled with a slender stiletto heel. Ceretti’s choice of footwear added an extra bit of assertive glamour to her entire look.

Ceretti accessorized with a multi-row diamond statement collar and matching diamond drop earrings. She wore her hair up and in a slightly wet, slicked-back style and sported smokey eyeshadow and nude lipstick.
Ceretti has been in a relationship with Academy Award-winning actor Leonardo DiCaprio since 2023. DiCaprio is a long-time supporter of the arts and of LACMA specifically. He has been a co-chair of the Arts+Film Gala since its inception in 2011. Ceretti has attended galas with DiCaprio in the past, including the 2024 gala, where she wore another striking look: a lavender evening gown with a halter crossover neckline and feathered train.
The Los Angeles County Museum of Art’s 15th annual Art+Film Gala brought Hollywood and the art world together on Saturday, honoring artist Vija Celmins and “Dune” director Denis Villeneuve. DiCaprio and LACMA trustee Eva Chow returned as co-chairs, while Gucci continued its presenting sponsorship, a role it has held since the gala’s 2011 debut. Blackpink’s Rosé was tapped to perform. Proceeds support the museum’s film programming, exhibitions and educational initiatives.
