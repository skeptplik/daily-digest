Japan is considering setting its total defense spending for the next five years at around ¥60 trillion, as it revises its three key national security-related documents by the end of this year.
The Defense Ministry estimates that necessary defense expenditures for the five-year period will total ¥70 trillion to ¥80 trillion, while the Finance Ministry is arguing for spending below ¥60 trillion, sources said Friday.
With Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi reportedly reluctant to raise taxes to fund defense spending, a battle between the Finance and Defense ministries over spending levels and how to secure the necessary funding is intensifying.
