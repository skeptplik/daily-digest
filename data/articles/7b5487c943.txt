Chinese consulate in Melbourne demands justice after fatal stabbing of delivery worker
The tragedy has sparked online outrage over delivery platform policies and gig worker safety
A 47-year-old delivery man from China’s northwestern Shaanxi province was stabbed to death while on the job and another rider was injured in downtown Melbourne on Friday evening, after the two confronted a man who was “tampering” with their bikes, sparking an “altercation”, Victoria state police said.
A 51-year-old suspect was arrested on Saturday morning and remains in custody, while the second delivery rider was taken to hospital with non-life-threatening stab wounds, according to police.
