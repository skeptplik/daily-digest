Kate Hudson in Almost Famous, is that you? No—but it is her good friend, Dakota Johnson.
Yesterday, the actor visited her rumored paramour, MGK, during a late-night recording session, where she put her own spin on the Penny Lane jacket. But Johnson didn’t go full boho roadie—instead, she offered a more pop-punk take on the iconic shearling-lined jacket.
Over a white T-shirt and slouchy, light-wash blue jeans, Johnson bundled up in a Nour Hammour tiger-print shearling car coat with a black shawl collar. For an extra dose of attitude, she carried a black shoulder bag riddled with grommets, wore a pair of ultra-chunky lug-sole boots, and embraced power clashing with a leopard-print belt.
MGK—also dressed in a white tee and jeans—and Johnson also built a punky outfit from the same fashion DNA. The singer also leaned into the accessories, adding a bright red beanie to match his stompers and the checked flannel hanging from the pants slung low across his hips (luckily, a studded black leather belt was more than prepared to hold up his pants). MGK finished off the look with a smattering of silver jewelry: a chain, hoop earrings, bracelets, and a watch.
With all the talk of reboots (Glee, Girls, and now, apparently, You’ve Got Mail) Dakota Johnson and MGK are practically begging for an Almost Famous remake. Just trade Stillwater’s “Fever Dog” for “my ex’s best friend.”
