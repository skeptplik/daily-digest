Vaults are locked locations around the map that contain rare chests and a sprite chest in Fortnite Chapter 7's mini season.
To get into a vault, you need to find a vault key, which can be found randomly as chest loot. They're a bit rare, but they're worth holding onto if you're near a vault. However, vaults are a highly sought after commodity, and there are only so many of them.
Here's the location of every vault in Fortnite Chapter 7's mini season, so you know where to go as soon as you get a vault key.
All vault locations in Fortnite Chapter 7 mini season
There are nine vaults in Fortnite Chapter 7's mini season. To open one, you need to have a vault key. With the vault key equipped, the closest unopened vault that is in the storm will appear as a lock on your map. However, vaults in the storm might still be unopened, so if you have a ton of healing items, they still might be worth going to. Inside each vault, you'll find four rare chests and one rare sprite chest.
In the image above, we've marked the locations of every vault, and in the list below, we've detailed where you can find them.
- Head to the south side of the island with Rubber Resort, and you'll find this vault near the middle of the beach.
- Go behind the waterfall at Loot Lagoon to find this vault.
- At Lil' Uzi's Stronghold, you'll find a tall building with a mural of Lil' Uzi Vert. Go to its west side, and head inside the small blue-roofed section to find this vault.
- Head west of Lantern Landing and drop off the cliff. You'll find this vault in the wall.
- Go west of Cursed Cavern, and you'll find a small tower. Go under the west stairs to find this vault.
- Go to the lowest floor of The $.$. Rocky, and you'll find this vault on the south side.
- Head to the northwest side of the island with Windmill Village. You'll find a red house with a blue roof. Drop off the cliff to the north, and you'll find this vault in the wall.
- Go into the canals below Loguetown, and you'll find this vault.
- Head to the southwest area of Busy Bay, and you'll find a shipwreck on the beach. Face east, and you'll see a vault.
