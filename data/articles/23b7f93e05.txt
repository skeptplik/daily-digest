Mexico City – Mexico’s recent string of cartel kingpin takedowns aided by U.S. intelligence suggest closer cooperation with Washington as President Claudia Sheinbaum comes under pressure to advance what is known as the war on drugs.
A swarm of heavily armed helicopters involved in the arrest of cartel boss Fausto Isidro Meza — alias “Chapo Isidro,” one of the FBI’s 10 most wanted men — served as a reminder of that ever-closer relationship last Saturday.
It also marks leftist Sheinbaum’s move away from the “Hugs, not bullets” policy of her predecessor Andres Manuel Lopez Obrador, which avoided direct confrontation with Mexico’s drug-trafficking cartels.
