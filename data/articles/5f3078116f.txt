SHENZHEN – Chinese leader Xi Jinping on Wednesday pledged to stand up against trade protectionism, while his Russian counterpart Vladimir Putin criticized Western sanctions as the two set the stage for a high-stakes trilateral meeting with Donald Trump.
They delivered their speeches at an Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit in the southern Chinese tech hub of Shenzhen, ahead of U.S. President Trump’s arrival from Manila, where he was meeting key regional allies.
The three men are due to meet on the sidelines on Thursday and potentially discuss Russia’s war with Ukraine and the U.S. war in Iran, as well as other flashpoints such as trade, AI and nuclear weapons.
