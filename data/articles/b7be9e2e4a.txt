COP31: Deep divisions persist over energy, finance and science
Issued on:
Share
COP31 climate talks in Turkey have gone into overtime, with deep divisions over fossil fuels, climate finance and global warming targets delaying an agreement. A revised draft dropped all mention of fossil fuels, prompting strong criticism from the EU and a dozen countries demanding a clear commitment to phase them out. Negotiators are also split over a proposed global electrification target, carbon capture and the future of UN climate science reports. Developing nations have voiced anger over the removal of a $120 billion climate adaptation funding target. Australia is now racing to secure a revised deal before delegates leave. France 24 journalist Adria Rocha Cutiller tells us the latest.
