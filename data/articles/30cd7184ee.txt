Mid-air helicopter collision on Nepal’s Everest kills Indian tourist
There are few roads in the Himalayas, and small helicopters are widely used for transport and emergency evacuations
There are few roads in the Himalayas, and helicopters are widely used to transport tourists, climbers and supplies, as well as carry out emergency evacuations. Nepal’s challenging terrain and rapidly changing weather make aviation operations particularly hazardous.
The small helicopters operated by Fishtail Air and Air Dynasty crashed into each other as both came in to land on a snowy site in Solukhumbu district on Tuesday morning.
