Shark attack hero Charlie Verco breaks through for Iron Series win
In short:
Surf lifesaver Charlie Verco has claimed his maiden win in the Iron Series, just five months after saving a woman's life in a shark attack.
Verco paddled towards the danger and pulled the unconscious Leah Stewart from the surf at Coogee Beach.
Verco beat home Ethan Callaghan and Henry Simpson in a breakthrough victory, which comes after the 25-year-old had entered this year's Iron Series as the top seed following the legendary Ali Day's retirement.
