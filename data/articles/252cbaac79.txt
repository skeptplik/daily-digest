Kalyn Ponga eyes miraculous return for Tonga clash 22 days after breaking arm
In short:
Kalyn Ponga is in line to return for New Zealand just 22 days after breaking his left arm.
Ponga must pass some fitness tests during the week to be cleared to return to the field.
What's next?
Ponga and Phoenix Crossland have been named on NZ's extended bench for the World Cup clash against Tonga on Saturday night.
