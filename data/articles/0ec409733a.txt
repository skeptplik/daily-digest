GTA 6 actor Lexie Shoaibi has spoken out about her "bittersweet" newfound fame after her character, Mandy, went viral online.
Warning! Spoilers for Mandy's role in GTA 6 follow.
Lexie Shoaibi did performance capture and voice over for Mandy, a woman you meet early on in the game. Mandy asks Jason to fix a clogged sink in her house, but comes out in a tiny bikini once he’s under the sink with a wrench. It didn't take long for the internet to latch on to Mandy, with countless social media posts discussing the character.
Shoaibi appeared on a Hasan Piker stream a few days ago, chatting about GTA 6 while raising money for charity. During the stream, she spoke about the newfound fame and reaction to her character.
“I don’t know how that happened,” Shoaibi said. “People pretending to be me? It’s thrilling. Please stop, but it’s also so exciting. It’s sort of bittersweet.”
Shoaibi hinted at this issue in a seperate Instagram post in which she warned her followers about fake accounts from people trying to impersonate her on social media. Referring to one X / Twitter account that has popped up in the last week purporting to be her own, she said: "This is fake! I don’t have a Twitter! This isn’t me! Please, someone, make it go away, ew!”
Meanwhile, Shoaibi revealed she spent around 10 days on set playing Mandy, but she also did a few other NPC voices while working with Rockstar.
Warning! Specific Mandy spoilers follow.
Despite Mandy only being a minor character in Chapter 1, Rockstar’s attention to detail put a bit of a spotlight on the character. As well as her basically being nude, the garden outside Mandy’s house hides a dark secret. When Jason is fixing the sink, he finds something clogged in it. You might think it’s a chicken bone, but closer inspection shows it’s a severed human finger. Check the trunk of Mandy’s car and you can find a kill kit. Have a nosey around her back garden and you’ll find vats of acid and a freshly dug grave. The game basically hints that she’s not a harmless stalker — she’s a serial killer. No, you can’t “fix her.”
“She’s a lot of fun, so if you play the game, let me know if you find me,” Shoaibi said in another statement about her character before people made it weird. “Y’all there aren’t words!!!! Thank you so much for the love and support. This is crazy!! Ily forever!”
