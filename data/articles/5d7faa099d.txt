A GTA 6 developer has paid tribute to his late mother with an in-game memorial in Leonida that players can find and visit themselves.
Rockstar Games QA developer Matt Green shared the touching story of how his mother helped launch his career in game development and the way she was honored in the Grand Theft Auto universe with a post on Instagram. Although she passed eight months before GTA 6's launch last week, Rockstar still managed to include her in a wall of other names in a memorial located in Leonida.
"As a kid, I remember practically begging my Mum to let me get GTA: Vice City and even though she was understandably reluctant, she caved," Green said. "I think that decision is likely what set me on the path to eventually working at Rockstar Games and on GTA VI.
"She was super proud and excited to see what I'd been working on but unfortunately I never got to show her. She's been gone almost 8 months now and sometimes it still doesn't feel real."
The memorial, which Green said can be found "just north of Hamlet, shows that Bev Green passed in March 2026, and she's joined by a collection of other names, too. It's unclear if every name on the memorial is tied to another Rockstar developer's lost loved one or who the names may be referencing, but we know at least two other names are.
Production designer Terrance Radcliff took to X/Twitter to share how the team had immortalized his father and daughter, who both passed during GTA 6's development. Elhuntry Radcliff and Jade Radcliff can be seen next to each other in-game.
"To wrap up launch week, what an honor to see my Father and my first Daughter, Jade, both of who I lost during development, forever immortalized in GTA VI," Radcliff said. "To have them be a part of such a huge moment in gaming history means everything to me. Peace & blessings."
