Hong Kong makes further in-roads in AI, aviation at mainland Chinese expo
Executives from start-ups and tech giants highlight cross-border integration across sectors, from AI and aviation to professional services and trade
Hong Kong is forging deeper ties with the mainland across artificial intelligence (AI), aviation and professional services, industry insiders at China’s global import expo have said.
On Saturday, Andrew Chen, founder of technology company Mindverse, told the South China Morning Post that operations spanning both sides of the border were becoming more seamless as physical and business ties grew stronger.
