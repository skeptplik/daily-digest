During the interregnum between the departure of Seán McGirr and the arrival of his as-yet-unnamed successor, McQueen is taking a look at itself. For this pre-fall collection, the studio team used its take on the house codes as a mirror, focusing upon each member’s individual specialisms while ensuring the whole ensemble remained coherently connected.
In both womenswear and menswear, the objective was sophistication stimulated by visceral twists and visual misdirection. The at-first-glance glossy croc leather of pants worn under a sky-blue jacket in womenswear, and as a matching black set in menswear, was denim treated with a raised pigment print. An asymmetric lapel and lighter internal construction relaxed the formality of a gray checked men’s suit worn over a frogged shirt. There was an echo of British countrywear in a subtly snake-patterned brown wool jacquard women’s suit whose integrated leather-strapped harness cinched just below the bust. A trailing-wrap, flat-fronted Prince of Wales check kilt, pleated behind, was rendered in an unexpectedly light fabrication. The full-skirted strapless dress that closed the lookbook was fashioned from a specially developed black lace lacquered to create a sheeny surface flash. Golden viscose suiting shimmered.
In knitwear, the tangled skeins of spider lace—derived from Alexander McQueen’s 1994 Banshee collection—were reinterpreted in a fine openwork jacquard of cotton backed with silk. A closely fitted dress in shades of metallic jacquard was based on a laser-cut lace piece from the house’s spring 2012 collection and featured a grille of serrated-edged rib cage cutouts on the back.
The corsetry that informed some ready-to-wear was also reflected in the finely drawn lacing applied to boots and pumps. Sharply chiseled men’s formal shoes featured gently tarnished and spackled metal hardware. On some women’s pumps, additional spathes of almost patently glossy leather, worked with metal threads to allow their shape to be manipulated, were designed to resemble calla lilies. These also featured in embroidery or print; in pink against a graduated red ground on skirts and shirting, on the sleeves of a biker jacket, and through the blinds of a fitted dress in whose harness structure was slung with strips of sliced leather.
