It's fun to build a PC. This is Tom's Hardware, after all. While the AI boom has forced prices to go up and up, there's still a lot of fun to be had putting a build together. To get to that point, though, you're going to need some kit. You'll need tools to build, but also to maintain, clean, and repair: your PC is going to need it all at some stage. I've built my own toolkit over the years. My go-to right now is a Hoto screwdriver, but I've got electric dusters, flashlights, screw kits, the lot, all ready for a project or two. My toolkit is versatile enough that it's helped me just as much while I renovate my home as it has in putting together my last build.
My toolkit is pretty expansive these days, but if you or a loved one is just starting out, you'll probably be wondering what to buy. I've put together a mix of the must-buys and nice-to-haves below for any PC builder, maker, and hobbyist's toolkit to help you decide.
