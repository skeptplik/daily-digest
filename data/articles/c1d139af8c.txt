A viral video appearing to show China's Xi Jinping "snubbing" Vladimir Putin at the APEC summit has racked up millions of views, fuelling speculation about tensions between Beijing and Moscow. But the footage is fake. FRANCE 24 traced the original video to an earlier summit, where the two leaders did shake hands. The creator, who runs a satire account, admitted to manipulating the footage using Grok Imagine, an AI tool developed by Elon Musk's xAI, as Vedika Bahl explains.
Just hours after the APEC summit in Shenzhen, China, misinformation about the meeting began circulating on social media, including a viral video supposedly showing a diplomatic snub between Chinese President Xi Jinping and his Russian counterpart Vladimir Putin.
The footage appears to show the Russian president approaching Xi and extending his hand for a handshake, only for the Chinese leader to turn away, leaving Putin seemingly humiliated in front of the cameras. The video quickly spread across X, with users describing the moment as a "public insult" and "humiliation" for the Russian president. Others suggested it was evidence of growing tensions between Beijing and Moscow.
The incident however, never happened. The viral clip has been manipulated using artificial intelligence, although it uses footage from a previous meeting.
An old video manipulated with AI
The FRANCE 24 Observers conducted a reverse image search of the viral video, and found it was filmed in August 2026 in Kyrgyzstan, during a meeting of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO). In the authentic footage, Xi and Putin can clearly be seen shaking hands, with no indication of a diplomatic snub.
The viral clip was thus not only manipulated using AI, but also falsely presented as footage from the recent APEC summit. Video from the APEC gathering in Shenzhen also shows Xi and Putin shaking hands.
Creator admits using AI to fake the encounter
FRANCE 24 managed to trace the manipulated footage back to its original creator, whose post has since accumulated more than 10 million views and was flagged as AI-generated content.
The creator also openly acknowledged using artificial intelligence to produce the video, saying it had been made with Grok Imagine, an AI generation tool developed by Elon Musk's company xAI. In exchanges with other users, the creator expressed satisfaction with how realistic the manipulated footage appeared. The account describes itself as satire and regularly publishes provocative content, including AI-manipulated videos of real-world events.
