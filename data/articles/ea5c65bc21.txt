A proposal by the Trump administration to cap the amount of time foreign students and scholars can spend in the country has been struck down in court.
In a Monday ruling, Boston federal Judge F. Dennis Saylor IV wrote that the rule “is arbitrary, capricious, an abuse of discretion, and otherwise not in accordance with law.”
Saylor previously blocked the rule temporarily on the day before the policy was set to take effect. Monday’s ruling is a final decision on the case, though an appeal from the government on the initial ruling is still pending. Universities and research advocates worried that the four-year cap on visas could deter international students from pursuing graduate studies in the U.S., because such degrees often take longer than four years.
