Florence Duvellier, a military fellow at the French Institute for International Relations in Paris, says that it's "technically possible" for Russia to put a nuclear weapon into space. Such a weapon would enable Russia to retain its status as a major space power.
She says that Russia's current focus on the war in Ukraine means it cannot afford to put a large network of satellites into space. However, a smaller nuclear weapon is "not that expensive" and would have a "huge effect".
A nuclear explosion in space would create a huge radioactive field that could cause "a lot of services" such as GPS and weather forecasts to shut down. The radiation would stay in space for months.
