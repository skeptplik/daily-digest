Coles Group Limited, Australia's second-largest retailer, is investigating what it described as a "cyber security incident" that has not impacted the company's stores.
Founded in 1914, Coles now employs over 115,000 people, manages a network of more than 1,800 retail outlets, and operates the Flybuys loyalty program with over nine million active members.
In a statement issued earlier today, the company said that it's now working with external experts to secure its systems and added that it has yet to find evidence that the attackers stole customer, employee, or supplier data from compromised systems.
"Coles Group Limited is currently investigating a cyber security incident. Based on the findings of our investigation to date, we have not identified any evidence that customer, team or supplier data has been compromised or removed from Coles' systems," the retail giant said.
"Coles is working with the Australian Cyber Security Centre and will continue to engage with appropriate agencies and regulators as investigations continue. Coles is also working with global cyber security experts to contain and secure the Coles IT environment."
It also noted that customers can shop as normal, as Flybuys accounts, Coles Plus, Coles Plus Delivery or QuiteLike subscriptions, and Coles stores and online outlets have not been affected by the cyberattack.
However, it added that it is aware of Liquorland click-and-collect issues affecting a small number of customers.
While it didn't link this to the incident, the company advised customers to be suspicious of any messages requesting personal data and recommended using unique passwords.
"We advise everyone to take basic online precautions and recommend remaining vigilant to suspicious links, attempts to solicit personal information from you (including by email, phone, text, or post) and potential scam activity," it added.
"We recommend you regularly update your password with a strong, unique password for your account and not reusing the same password across multiple services."
Coles has not yet said when it discovered the incident or whether the attackers have made a ransom demand, and no major threat group has claimed the attack.
A Coles spokesperson was not immediately available for comment when BleepingComputer reached out for more details on the incident.
