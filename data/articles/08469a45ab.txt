Target has officially launched its main Black Friday sale, which means Amazon is now matching all of the best deals in its own sale. This is especially good news for anyone looking to pad their book or vinyl collections this year thanks to the popular '3 for 2' sale returning at both major retailers ahead of Thanksgiving.
This is actually the second time the two retailers have had this type of sale in November, but this time around there seems to be a lot more included here. That's especially true if you're looking to grab multiple box sets at a discount. This sale is arguably the best time to buy books at a discount if you're willing to spend a bit more to get the extra savings.
Buy 2, Get 1 Free Sale on Books and Music
For those who aren't already familiar with this sale, the way it works is pretty easy to understand. All you need to do is add three eligible items to your cart and the least expensive one will get discounted from the price at check out. Because of this, you'll want to buy three similarly priced items to maximize the discount you're getting. You can also mix and match anything in the sale, so you don't need to just buy three books or three vinyl records.
Our Book Recommendations
The book portion of this sale is quite massive, so it's definitely worth diving into all of the book inclusions to see what's there. If you're looking for some recommendations on where to get started, however, I've picked out some of the most interesting titles I could find.
The Dungeon Crawler Carl books on sale here are always incredibly popular during these sales. Matt Dinniman's LitRPG series has really blown up this year and it's a great opportunity to pick up the full collection for pretty cheap. It's also worth checking out Pierce Brown's Red Rising series, which has been hitting the zeitgeist again after the movie adaptation news earlier this year. And if you're into romantasy, Sarah J. Maas' latest ACOTAR book is even included here.
If you're just looking for some cool stuff to add to your shelf, I'd recommend checking out Hyrule Historia first. This is an art book that details the long and interesting Legend of Zelda history. There are also illustrated versions of Lord of the Rings and Harry Potter included here this year.
Our Box Set Recommendations
The box sets included in the sale are the best overall deal for anyone looking to start their book collection. These are higher-priced items that include either all of the books in a series or a good chunk of them at once. They will often also feature some sort of cool artwork on the spines that look wonderful together on your shelf.
As for what you should check out first, I recommend grabbing The Witcher books on sale here. These books spawned one of the most successful video game franchises of all time and, according to the author, will still be getting new stories. I'd recommend the Game of Thrones box set for similar reasons, but George R.R. Martin may never finish that series so dive into those books at your own risk.
There are also some great fantasy box sets that have since been turned into movies or TV shows. The Hunger Games box set includes the latest prequel, Sunrise on the Reaping, which is now in theaters. There's also the Percy Jackson books, which is worth reading alongside season 3 of the Disney+ adaptation. And if you don't already own all of the Harry Potter books, the paperback set is definitely worth grabbing ahead of HBO's TV show next month.
Our Vinyl recommendations
The sale on music is just as robust as the book selection. So although I've picked out some of the things I personally think are cool, I encourage you to check out the full inclusion list for yourself and search for albums you know you like.
That being said, my overall top pick in the sale is the Lord of the Rings trilogy. Howard Shore's music helped turn Peter Jackson's movies into something truly epic. This is an easy recommendation for anyone looking for a good LOTR gift to buy someone this year.
A few of my other top picks include classics like Michael Jackson's Thriller, Metallica's Master of Puppets, and the Beatle's Abby Road. Noah Khan's Stick Season is also included here as it's been one of the most popular albums of the last few years.
