Shinji Saito, a former member of the popular Japanese comedy group Jungle Pocket, was sentenced to four years in prison by the Tokyo District Court on Monday for sexually assaulting a female co-star on a television production bus.
Saito, 44, was convicted of forcible sexual intercourse and indecent assault for groping a woman in her 20s and forcing her to perform oral sex while the two were alone on the bus between filming in Tokyo’s Shinjuku Ward in July 2024. He was a member of Jungle Pocket until Yoshimoto Kogyo, the comedy group’s agency, terminated his contract following his referral to prosecutors in October 2024.
The case hinged on whether the acts were consensual. Saito admitted to the acts but pleaded not guilty, claiming that the woman, a company employee who also worked as an influencer, had appeared to enjoy the encounter and kissed him back at the end. The defense also argued that he had no influence over her career.
The prosecution countered that the woman had resisted Saito’s advances but was unable to do so fully because he was in a position to influence the woman’s employment, despite the fact that they had met for the first time that day.
The court accepted the prosecution’s argument, finding that a power imbalance existed between the two and that this helped explain why the woman had difficulty resisting his advances and reporting the incident.
“It is hard to imagine that the defendant, without checking with the victim, could have believed that the victim was consenting to sexual acts, or failed to realize that the victim was in a situation where it was difficult for her to refuse to consent,” said Judge Yuko Ito in the court’s ruling.
The court also found that the woman’s statements were backed up by the fact that she had told her mother and an acquaintance about how she felt uncomfortable about the encounter and visited a psychiatrist to discuss it, and said that Saito was fully responsible for the crimes.
“There are no other motives behind the defendant’s crimes other than satisfying his own sexual desires without any consideration for others,” said Ito, adding that Saito made “illogical justifications” for his actions and did not express remorse.
Jungle Pocket was formed in 2006 by Saito along with comedians Hirohisa Ota and Otake, and they specialized in conte, a style of skit-based comedy. The group competed in a variety of comedy programs, and secured a second-place finish in the 2016 edition of TBS’ annual “King of Conte” comedy competition.
