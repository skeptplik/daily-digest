On Friday night, Rosalía showed up at the Los 40 Music Awards in Barcelona in a surrealist outfit that possessed an almost celestial quality. The singer-songwriter commanded attention on the red carpet in a pearly white pleated dress paired with a dove headpiece from Robert Wun’s fall 2026 Child’s Play couture collection.
Rosalía’s dress was a custom made halter gown from Maison Margiela, a floor-length, form-fitted satin number with a plunging neckline and a cinched waist belt held in place with a thin line of fabric. The real star of the outfit, however, was Rosalía’s downright otherworldly headpiece: a semi-realistic rendering of a milky white dove taking flight from the singer’s head, its talons hovering just above a halo-like ring of blond hair.
The unique look had an enchanted quality, which makes sense considering Wun’s inspiration for his 2026 collection. He drew heavy influence from the filmmaker Hayao Miyazaki of “My Friend Totoro” and “Spirited Away” fame. Wun was captivated by how children’s stories, especially those with fairytale-like qualities, could communicate darker, more cerebral themes to an audience.
Wun’s collection was fitting for the thematic undertones of Rosalía’s latest album, “LUX”, which also tackles heavy questions via fantastical elements. The singer uses birds as a recurring motif. In fact, at the end of the music video for “Berghain”, she even transforms into a dove. Doves are traditionally thought of as a meaningful symbol, associated with peace and renewal, and Rosalía’s album has certain spiritual elements. Recently, she’s been seen in a lot of angel-inspired attire to match this tone. At the Latin Grammy Awards, she wore a white gown with feathered shoulders and a large, billowy skirt and the ring of blond in her hair is an intentional choice meant to evoke the otherworldly.
Rosalía was a huge winner at the Los 40 Music Awards, taking home the most awards of the night. The singer won Best Album, Best Music Video, and Best Tour and also received a Global Icon Award.
