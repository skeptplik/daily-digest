Mainland Chinese firm, director admit fraud in HK$52.9m Hong Kong bottled water scandal
Director Lui Tsz-chung and Xin Ding Xin Trade plead guilty to fraud after submitting altered water quality test certificates for government contract
A mainland Chinese company and its director have pleaded guilty to fraud after submitting falsified certificates to win a HK$52.9 million (US$6.78 million) contract to supply drinking water to civil servants in Hong Kong.
The high-profile procurement scandal came to light in August last year after civil servants raised concerns about the quality of products supplied by Xin Ding Xin Trade, which held a three-year contract to provide XinLe-brand bottled water to government offices on Hong Kong Island and some outlying islands.
At a hearing on Thursday, company director Lui Tsz-chung pleaded guilty to seven charges, admitting that between May and August last year he submitted altered documents to the Government Logistics Department, deceiving it into believing that his products had been vetted and produced by Robust (Guangdong) Drinking Water, a prominent food and beverage company on the mainland that manufactures purified and mineral bottled water.
