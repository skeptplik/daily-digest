It’s hard to believe it’s only been seven days since the launch of Grand Theft Auto 6. Still, with the staggering number of screenshots, videos, and everything else filling up social media feeds, anyone out of the loop would never know. I think much of this is due to just how many types of games Rockstar managed to cram into GTA 6. Everything from fixing up Jason’s house in the prologue to racing, billiards, birdwatching, and, of course, all the other gameplay we expect in GTA has created an almost Sims-like experience that is getting more people than ever to try the series. Seriously, though, the early sales numbers are bonkers.
My and many others' favorite things about GTA 6, in addition to just being a general menace to society, have been experiencing all the different kinds of gameplay and Rockstar fitting into it. I’ve been checking in with our IGN community across our various platforms to see how their time in Leonida has been going. I’m still gathering stories for that (If you’re interested in sharing your thoughts, join the IGN Discord); however, in the meantime, I wanted to share the fashion that various members of the IGN Community and Staff have for Jason and Lucia as they make their way through the story.
