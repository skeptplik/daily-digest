Hamas said Israel tried to kill an adult son of the Palestinian movement's chief, Khalil al-Hayya, in an overnight strike on Friday (November 27, 2026), with a Gaza security source telling AFP he survived.

The strike follows a series of killings by the Israeli military in recent days that have targeted Hamas officials, despite a ceasefire in the war-shattered Palestinian territory.
Separately, Gaza's civil defence agency said Israeli forces killed seven people across the territory on Friday (November 27).
"Izz al-Din al-Hayya, the youngest surviving son of Hamas political bureau chief Khalil al-Hayya, along with his family and the family of his late brother Azzam, survived an Israeli assassination attempt early on Friday targeting a residential apartment in the Rimal neighbourhood, west of Gaza City," a security source in Gaza told AFP.
When asked by AFP, the Israeli military said it was looking into the incident.
Bassem Naim, a member of the Palestinian Islamist movement's political bureau, told AFP that "Israel's attempt to target the youngest son of Hamas political bureau chief Khalil al-Hayya... constitutes 'state terrorism,'" while Hamas issued a statement calling the targeting "cowardly".
Four of Mr. al-Hayya's sons have already been killed in Israeli attacks, including one in May and another in an Israeli strike targeting Hamas leaders in Doha in September 2025.
Two others were killed before the war on Gaza.
Israeli strikes continue
Gaza's civil defence agency, a rescue force which operates under Hamas authority, said an Israeli drone strike west of Rafah city in southern Gaza killed three people on Friday (November 27), with nearby Nasser Hospital confirming it received three bodies following the strike.
The Israeli military told AFP it had struck "three terrorists in the area".
Another drone strike west of Jabalia refugee camp in northern Gaza killed one person, the civil defence and Al-Shifa Hospital said.
The Israeli military said it was checking the incident.
The civil defence agency said two more people were killed in an Israeli drone strike near Gaza City's Al-Shifa Hospital, with the medical facility confirming it received two bodies.
The Israeli military told AFP it struck "a terrorist in the area," adding that there was "no harm to the hospital or any medical practice" in the vicinity.
Israeli gunfire in the Rafah area killed another person, the civil defence said. The Israeli military said it was checking that incident.
Israel and Hamas regularly accuse each other of violating the ceasefire that came into effect in October 2025, after two years of devastating war.
More than 1,650 Palestinians have been killed since the start of the truce, according to an AFP tally based on data from the Gaza health ministry, which also operates under Hamas authority and whose figures are considered reliable by the United Nations.
In the same period, the Israeli military says seven of its soldiers have been killed, as well as one civilian contractor.
