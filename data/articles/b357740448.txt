Infinity Nikki players were baffled when Elon Musk’s mother, Maye Musk, appeared in a series of trailers announcing the game’s forthcoming 3.0 update on November 16.
Yep, you read that correctly; Maye Musk appeared as a celebrity guest in commercials for the popular open-world dress-up game Infinity Nikki, which you can watch on the game’s official RedNote account (though you’ll need to make an account to view them). If you weren’t aware of this, you aren’t alone, as it seems that Maye’s advertisements weren’t exactly promoted on the game’s English-language social media profiles — and looking at comments on platforms like Twitter and Reddit, this strategy doesn’t come as a big surprise.
RedNote is a Chinese social media app that's most often compared to TikTok. Infinity Nikki is developed by PaperGames, a studio in mainland China, and boasts a strong fanbase in the country. So does Maye Musk, apparently, judging by the excited remarks on RedNote (a Chinese social app that’s often compared to TikTok).
Maye Musk is something of a celebrity in China. Since at least 2023, the model and famous mum has appeared in all sorts of advertisements and campaigns in the country, representing brands that sell products like makeup, massage devices, and even down jackets, per a report from the New York Times. In that same report, Yang Hu, an expert on China’s beauty industry, explained her popularity: “[Her image] carries an element of successful parenting, attracting a significant number of mothers among her followers.”
These comments are echoed in the remarks that fans left on Billibilli’s live-streamed Infinity Nikki Version 3.0 First-Look Event. Automatic translations show that viewers were equal parts confused and delighted by her appearance as a host, with some writing, “Who the hell?” while others exclaimed, “Bestie, you’ve really made it big!” and, “You’re awesome!”
Given Elon Musk’s controversial nature in the West, comments on English social media sites were decidedly less positive.
“I can't believe what they've done here. I'm in shock. I was so excited to play version 3.0, but now I don't want to play anymore. What were they thinking? Can they genuinely recover from this?” one user wrote on Reddit.
“I’m out. That’ll teach me to sink money into a game. I literally justified the spending because I wanted to get a mental break away from these bulls**t people,” another said.
“I was thinking about trying this game. Now I certainly won't,” yet another admitted.
Overall, this particular advertising blitz seems to have put a sour taste in Western players’ mouths, but, perhaps even more curiously, has exposed the vast differences in opinion toward the Musk family between the game’s Eastern and Western communities.
