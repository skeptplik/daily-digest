David Warner slams Pat Cummins over Sandpapergate denial
In short:
David Warner has hit back at Pat Cummins after the Australia captain's sensational 70-minute press conference earlier today.
Warner stood by Usman Khawaja's revelations about the 2018 Sandpapergate scandal, saying Cummins threw Khawaja "under the bus" in denying them.
Warner also described the blame levelled at him and fellow suspended players Steve Smith and Cameron Bancroft a "life punishment".
