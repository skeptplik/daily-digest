Ukrainian drone attacks killed two men in western Russia’s Kursk and Bryansk regions and injured a woman in St. Petersburg, local officials said Friday.
A 48-year-old tractor driver was killed in a Kursk region village near the border with the Belgorod region, Governor Alexander Khinshtein said, describing the drone attack “monstrous” and “unjustified.”
In the neighboring Bryansk region to the north, Governor Yegor Kovalchuk said a 51-year-old man was killed while standing outside his home in a village on the border with Ukraine’s Sumy region.
Further to the north, falling drone debris reportedly wounded a 44-year-old woman in St. Petersburg after damaging an apartment building. She was said to have been hospitalized for her injuries and was in moderate condition.
Authorities said 55 drones targeted St. Petersburg overnight, according to the local outlet Fontanka.
Elsewhere across Russia, regional authorities reported that three people were wounded in the Belgorod region and that there was property damage in the Tula region.
Russia’s Defense Ministry said it intercepted 318 Ukrainian drones across Russia and annexed Crimea between Thursday night and Friday morning.
