Thanksgiving Day accounts for a long list of food favorites. While the turkey is the star of the show at most dinner tables, trends have shown that it’s the sides that often win the day. In past years, WWD has included a recipe or two to highlight the year’s culinary feast. One of its first Thanksgiving Day side recipes was printed in 1962, a seven-ingredient nutmeg pudding courtesy of the International Ladies Garment Workers Union (ILGWU). Fast forward to 2013, when “Thanksgiving Six Ways” showed how holiday side dishes continued to offer fond memories. This time, the recipes came from a few familiar fashion names and included Nicole Miller’s Southern cornbread from New Orleans chef Paul Prudhomme, Peter Som’s French-y take on Julia Child’s scalloped potatoes, Zac Posen’s own recipe for spiced Thanksgiving cookies and, of course, a cranberry relish. All sure to please then and now.
