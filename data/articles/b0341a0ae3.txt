Meta is planning to price the Muse Charm, a little gadget with a screen that lets you chat with your Muse AI agent, at $300, according to The Information. CEO Mark Zuckerberg revealed the gadget earlier this year and said the company expected to ship the Charm “in time for the holidays in December,” but he didn’t provide a price at the time.
Meta’s Muse Charm might cost $300
The Information has reported a potential price for the Muse Charm, which is expected to ship in time for the holidays.
When he announced the Muse Charm, Zuckerberg said that the company had “packed the whole Muse experience,” including real-time voice and avatar technology, into the gadget. It has a fingerprint sensor, and you can tap it to be able to talk to a Muse AI agent without unlocking a phone or opening an app. The Information reports that the Muse Charm will come with a free 12-month 5G data plan from Verizon.
Spokesperson Tyler Yee wrote to The Information that “We’re charmed by the interest in our AI devices, and we’ll have more to share soon.” When reached for comment by The Verge, Yee provided the same statement.
The company is also working on a smartwatch, The Information says, but a launch for that has been pushed to 2027 as Meta tries to get Muse Charm out the door.
Meta also open sourced code to let users make their own Muse gadgets, which my colleague Sean Hollister used to make his own DIY Muse Charm.
Update, November 19th: Noted statement given by Meta spokesperson Tyler Yee.
