Esque/By, a New York-based women’s bespoke tailoring brand founded by Jennifer “JJ” Lee, this month debuted its first collection.
Although Lee studied computational and systems biology at UCLA and built her career at L’Oréal, where she built and developed product innovations across Lancôme, Clarisonic, Nyx Professional Makeup and SkinCeuticals, she also had a deep relationship with tailoring, which she had done since she was young and was introduced to through her family’s connection to Hong Kong tailoring.
These experiences — combined with her personal relationship with fashion, which came out of adapting her mother Lily’s wardrobe following a breast cancer mastectomy — led to the inception of Esque/By.
Lee began the brand by offering bespoke tailoring styles to clients in the know for two years and has now expanded into ready-to-wear with her first collection and campaign, rooted in thoughtful construction, considered fit and individuality. From start to finish, her principal belief is that, “Your clothes should fit you, not the other way around.”
Prior to the brand’s ready-to-wear launch, Lee teased her new chapter at the 2026 CFDA Fashion Awards, where model Ashley Graham wore a bespoke Esque/By ensemble.
“I think it’s really interesting because of my tech engineering background that I have a very patternmaker and tailor’s eye, and I think that’s different because a lot of time we optimize for aesthetics. But when it comes to suiting, there was so much that we learned in bespoke that half an inch mattered for women’s curves,” Lee said during a preview.
To create the collection, Lee tapped Lauren Matina, who has worked across luxury fashion and editorial at the likes of Net-a-porter, Moda Operandi and Michael Kors for 15 years, as creative director for the brand’s collection development, campaigns and visual identity.
“With vintage clothing, they were really thoughtful about tailoring back in the day, and so that’s why I love our partnership because Lauren comes from Net-a-porter and I have a tailoring/engineering background. She can really optimize for the style and aesthetic, and I’m really thoughtful about construction,” Lee said.
The duo came together to create a capsule of clean, crisp ‘90s-inspired tailoring with sculptural silhouettes, including sharp shoulders, slightly nipped waists and extra seam allowances built into the garments, which they saw missing in the marketplace for women’s bodies. Highlights of the debut collection include a slightly elongated tuxedo dress — great for civil ceremonies — in white; a chic strapless corset top with matching suit, and the fitted blazer with drop-waist belted skirt, to name a few.
The garments are crafted using Italian poplin, Japanese tailoring wool and Italian wool for its wrap dress and pants and offer mix-and-match wearability from day to night. Each piece is produced in New York City’s Garment District; Lee said supporting local manufacturing is a big part of the brand’s ethos.
“I think a lot of tailors see every seam as an opportunity. When we think about curvature in a woman’s body, everything is beautiful as is, but should you want it to be a little bit tighter, or maybe you want to let it out because maybe your body has changed. These [two-inch] seams [allowances] allow for areas of opportunity to address it,” Lee said. The collection also features special details that pay homage to tailoring tradition, including custom embroidery options and custom red buttonholes (said to be their calling card).
Esque/By’s debut collection, priced $250 to $1,495, is available to shop via the brand’s e-commerce. In addition, Lee and Matina hosted a trunk show at Hôtel Barrière Fouquet’s New York on Nov. 19 and previewed the brand’s collection to the likes of Nara Smith, Nicky Hilton Rothschild, Olympia of Greece, Arielle Charnas and more.
