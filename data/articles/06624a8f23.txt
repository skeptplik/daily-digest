The leaves are changing, the temperatures are cooling, and it’s finally time for wool coats. After weeks of feeling like summer would never let up, we’ve officially transitioned into the cozy season—just ahead of everyone’s favorite autumnal holiday. And last night, some two weeks ahead of Thanksgiving, Angelina Jolie got the family time started.
The actor was spotted grabbing dinner with sons Pax and Knox at West Hollywood hotspot Cecconi’s. She looked effortlessly refined for the outing, bundling up in layers that included a loose white button-down shirt and a brown wool coat.
Her wide-leg gray trousers, designed with a slouchy, relaxed silhouette, reached all the way to the ground, covering her black pointed-toe footwear.
Jolie, who shares Pax, Knox, and their four siblings with ex-husband Brad Pitt, wore the classic, comfy ensemble with black sunglasses and an ornate silver pendant necklace crafted with delicate filigree work.
Next to her, Pax—aged 22—wore a varsity-inspired navy-blue jacket over a white T-shirt and dark jeans. His black-and-white sneakers appeared to be a pair of low tops from xVessel’s collaboration with Yohji Yamamoto, recognizable for its chunky, deconstructed sole.
Knox, on the other hand, went for a lighter look with a white T-shirt from Shihiko, embroidered with the phrase, “It’s been a long day.” The experimental piece, worn here without a coat, was printed with a hidden UV reactive graphic of a cat, which only appeared under sunlight. The 18-year-old tucked the tee into brown trousers and finished things off with a pop of yellow, by way of his smiley cap-toe Prospective Flow sneakers.
