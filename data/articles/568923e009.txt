Dakar, Senegal – The crowd’s cheers crescendoed to a deafening roar with every point scored by 16-year-old Senegalese taekwondo competitor Ameth Diop, who kicked and punched his way to a bronze medal at the Youth Games in Dakar, Senegal, last week.
Africa’s first-ever Olympic event, the 2026 Youth Olympic Games, wrapped up Friday after igniting a palpable fervor in the Senegalese capital, a passion normally reserved only for football in the west African country.
Despite taking home 12 medals at the Youth Games and the country’s major investment in Olympics infrastructure, Senegal will need to do more, experts say, if it hopes to maintain the momentum and create Olympians at the main Games.
