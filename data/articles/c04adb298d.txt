Vienna – Germany, Denmark, Austria, Greece and the Netherlands on Monday unveiled a plan to establish a migrant deportation center in Rwanda — the first such facility outside the European Union.
The group has stepped up efforts to open a “return hub” for rejected migrants after the EU cleared the way by toughening migration rules.
The move came despite protests from human rights groups and a warning from the United Nations refugee agency UNHCR. France’s President Emmanuel Macron has also expressed doubts.
