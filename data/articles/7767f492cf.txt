SEOUL – Japanese and North Korean intelligence officials are believed to have had contact in Japan during the Aichi-Nagoya Asian Games between September and October, it was learned Monday.
The North Korean official, who belonged to the National Intelligence Agency, had entered Japan as a member of the North Korean delegation for the Asian Games, a South Korean source said, quoting a person connected with the administration of Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi.
At the time of the contact, the Japanese side said, “North Korea will also benefit if it makes concessions on the abduction issue and the normalization of bilateral relations comes true,” referring to the issue of Japanese nationals kidnapped by North Korean spies decades ago.
