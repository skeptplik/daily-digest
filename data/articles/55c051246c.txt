Maybelline New York Super Stay Icy Tint, $13.99
As much as I report on lip tint/stain supremacy of late, I’ve never been much of a user myself. But I love this concept of a glossy stain. I am a matte-avoidant (satin rising) when it comes to my lips, and so the fact that this formula is durable but keeps my lips feeling plush really seals the deal. So many of the hues fall into my browny-red sweet spot, too — 009 Acai Freeze is my favorite.
