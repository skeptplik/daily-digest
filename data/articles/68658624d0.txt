Pre-loading for GTA 6 begins on November 10, and it will be an absolute beast of a game when it comes to download size. Rockstar has confirmed that the game is 212 GBs, which is nearly a quarter the size of the average PS5 (not counting the required space for the OS and other mandatory elements on the console). Given that fans are anticipating a pretty massive open-world with lots to see and do, the size makes sense. As a result, fans will want to take full advantage of the nine-day pre-load window or hop on this Spectrum deal so they can play as soon as possible.
