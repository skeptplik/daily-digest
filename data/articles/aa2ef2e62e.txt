Adrenaline acts as an emergency boost to all your stats in Grand Theft Auto 6, as it temporarily negates health, stamina, and focus loss. It's meant to get you out of an emergency situation safely or help you burst into a tense situation and mop it up fast. Unlike Zombix and other restoratives, though, you can't buy adrenaline — not from anywhere we've found so far, at least — nor can you expect to find it in abundance throughout Leonida. There is, however, a small set of locations you can reliably get adrenaline from.
Below, we explain how to get adrenaline in GTA 6.
How to get adrenaline in GTA 6
You'll normally find at least one shot of adrenaline where you need it most — illegal operations, which have yellow marked on the map. That's true for the big ones, like the bowling alley the Kolt group operates out of, and the small ones, like the meth labs and grow houses operating out the back of gas stations. Basically, any convenience stores, gas stations, bars, and so on that lets you explore more than just the customer area nearly always has a trauma kit or first-aid kid in a staff area, bathroom, or kitchen. These will always have Zombix, but there's also a chance that they'll contain one shot of adrenaline. The kits might be lying around on a countertop, or they may be little cabinets nailed to the wall. If they're on the wall, you'll have to break them open, which takes a few seconds. Bear that in mind if you've got enemies close behind.
This is not to be confused with the other robbery opportunities that have green markers or stick ups that have gray markers. These areas can have adrenaline, but they're a bit rarer.
There's also a chance to find adrenaline in the back of ambulances, either loose or in a trauma kit. Getting it this way is pretty tough until you reach Chapter 3 and can explore Vice City more freely, though. The city hospital always has a few ambulances around back in the parking lot. They aren't very well attended, so you can break in with comparative ease. They are alarmed, so make sure to slim jim the door first, and then open the back to avoid setting off the alarm.
You might see ambulances driving along the roads of Leonida or the streets of Vice City. If you're really desperate for adrenaline, you can stop them and break into the back. This is a pretty big and unnecessary risk to take if you're trying to keep a low profile, though.
Lucia and Jason can only hold three adrenaline apiece, so you can't stock up on them and let loose in a particularly tough mission. If you want to have it on-hand before taking on a dangerous job, instead of scrambling to find the location's bathroom when the bullets start flying, your best bet is to swipe it from an ambulance before setting out to work.
Just make sure to keep an eye on the stat bars in the upper left corner of the screen. They turn yellow when you use adrenaline, and the yellow gradually fades back to the stats' normal colors as it wears off. Consider it your action timer. By the time it's almost completely run out, you should be finding cover or fleeing for safety.
