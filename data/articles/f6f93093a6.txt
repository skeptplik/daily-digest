Goalkeeper Jesus Owono makes first statement since collision that led to death of Jesus De Miguel
In short:
FC Andorra goalkeeper Jesus Owono has made his first statement since an on-field collision that led to the death of Tenerife forward Jesus De Miguel
The pair came together at midfield, contesting the ball, when De Miguel went into cardiac arrest after the heavy collision
What's next?
Spanish national police have opened a routine investigation into De Miguel's death.
