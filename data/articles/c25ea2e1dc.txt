Halle Bailey placed her Disney princess image on hold with her all-black leather look on the red carpet at the 2026 GQ Men of the Year held at Chateau Marmont on Thursday in Los Angeles. “The Little Mermaid” star went monochrome in a fitted dress with sky-high pointed pumps.
She opted for the daring Le Silla Futura Pumps 120mm, which retail for $1,132, in black. The Futura pump is a contemporary take on the traditional pumps and is made more modern through clean lines and distinctive details.

The shoes are crafted in Italy from semi-gloss lambskin leather, and they feature an ultra-sleek pointed toe and a signature asymmetrical side cut-out that gives the design a light, modern and sexier feel by leaving the inner side of the foot exposed.
The shoes stand tall on a black stiletto high heel that measures 120mm, which is 4.7 inches. The pin-thin style has become a signature for the brand and can be found in many of Le Silla‘s shoe designs. The stiletto starts right in the middle of the heel, giving the foot more support.
She paired the sultry heels with a black croc-embossed leather double-breasted coat dress from the Sergio Hudson fall 2026 collection. She accessorized the look with the emerald green Lila choker and earrings set by Anayah Jewellery, and a Le Vian platinum and vanilla diamond ring.
GQ’s Men of the Year is an annual celebration and award franchise hosted by GQ magazine that honors standout figures in entertainment, culture, music, sports and style who have made a major cultural impact over the past year.
