Ubisoft is laying off staff in its vast Montreal and Quebec studios, with 166 roles being lost "to ensure our resources are aligned with current production priorities and business needs."
GamesIndustry.biz understands that the affected positions were chiefly in production and support roles, and represented less than 4% of the total headcount at the studios. The layoffs impacted employees working on projects that had recently been cancelled or significantly changed. The firm did not refer to any impact on specific games or projects under development at the studios, which work on some of the firm's largest gaming brands.
"Ubisoft is reducing its workforce by 166 positions across its Montréal and Québec City operations," the company said in a statement provided to GamesIndustry.biz. "As our portfolio evolves, our production needs are changing, requiring us to adjust the size of some teams to ensure our resources are aligned with current production priorities and business needs."
"We deeply value the contributions of these teams and recognise the impact this may have on our colleagues. We are committed to supporting them through this process with care and respect. Those affected are being provided with severance, extended health care benefits, career transition services and other resources."
GamesIndustry.biz understands that the cuts include members of Vantage Studios, the joint venture with Tencent that houses the Assassin's Creed, Far Cry and Rainbow Six franchises. Vantage is a Creative House within Ubisoft's new business structure, which was announced at the start of the year alongside a "final" round of cuts to deliver €200 million in savings. The firm has cut hundreds of jobs and closed multiple studios over the course of the year, including Ubisoft Winnipeg, Ubisoft RedLynx, Ubisoft Belgrade and Ubisoft Halifax.
The firm renamed Creative House 2 as Massive Entertainment in September, following the appointment of 2K and Amazon Games veteran Cristoph Hartmann as head of the unit, and cut staff across its German offices in October.
