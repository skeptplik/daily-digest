Searching for a new display to take your gaming to the next level? XR glasses have a lot of potential, packing what looks like a massive display into a compact pair of glasses you can take with you almost anywhere. For Black Friday, Viture has some of its lowest prices ever with discounts on its Luma series of glasses and even its new flagship Viture Beast glasses, which earned a “Great” rating in our review.
Black Friday Deals on Viture XR Glasses
The Viture Beast landed this year at $549, but they’re on sale for $489 through Cyber Monday. Our reviewer, Jacqueline Thomas, said in her review, “the Viture Beast XR glasses are a great way to kick back on a flight and play games on a handheld without having to hold up a bulky device the entire way.”
These are the flagship glasses from the brand, offering some substantial tech inside. The Virture Beast pack in a pair of 1200p Micro-OLED displays that run at 120Hz. If that sounds like a brilliant recipe for gaming to you, you’ve clearly been doing your homework on display technology. The OLED displays give you rich contrast — especially with the 1,250-nit peak brightness — vivid color, and fast pixel response times on top of the fluid visual you already get from that refresh rate. The extra brightness of the displays can help if you’re using the glasses out in the daylight, but if you want to maximize the clarity, Viture uses electrochromic dimming that can cut down on external light.
The Viture Beast supports a lot of different use cases, working with phones, computers, and gaming handhelds alike. With adapters, they can work with consoles as well. You can set them up to run as a heads-up display always front and center or even virtually pin the display in space as if it were a monitor mounted in the real world.
Beyond the Beast, Viture has some of its biggest discounts available on the Luma series, which includes the Luma Pro at $339, Luma Ultra at $449, and standard Luma at $299. These still offer capable 120Hz Micro-OLED displays at 1200p and brightnesses from 1,000-1,250 nits. They also all include electrochromatic dimming to let you see through them or darken your surroundings in a snap. If you wear glasses, this series is probably for you, since they include hardware focus adjustments to adapt for myopia.
The Luma is the simplest, offering a 50-degree field of view and sticking to the basics as a display. The Luma Pro stretches the field-of-view slightly, adds in some stylish lighting effects, and adds in a camera for extra spatial capabilities. The Luma Ultra goes yet another step further, upgrading to brighter panel and bringing even more cameras for additional spatial capabilities and hand tracking.
In addition to the savings on the Beast and Luma series, Viture is offering a free 8BitDo Ultimate 2C controller with the purchase of its glasses. It’s also discounting the Pro Mobile Dock to $99, which gives the glasses extra battery, connectivity options, and can connect to HDMI sources. If you get the Pro Mobile Dock, you can also get a special mount for the Nintendo Switch and Switch 2 for free.
You can find the Viture Beast, Luma series, and more accessories on sale on Amazon, Best Buy, and Viture’s website with these offers running through Cyber Monday.
