Three years after Hamas’s October 7 attack shattered the core security promise of the state, Israel has emerged from a consequential Knesset election that ended Benjamin Netanyahu’s governing majority. Israel at an Inflection Point, published by The Hindu Group, examines how decades of unchecked militarism, a rightward political shift, and the collapse of the peace process have brought the country to this critical juncture.
The e-book brings together incisive essays by leading scholars and experts to offer a realistic picture of the strategic and political landscape surrounding the Jewish nation. Tracing the arc from late-19th-century European Zionism and the 1948 establishment of Israel to the geopolitical shocks of 2026, it probes the central tension between military dominance and durable security.
Across its chapters, the book explores how post-October 7 trauma has hardened Israeli opposition to Palestinian statehood, why tactical battlefield successes and high-profile assassinations have failed to produce a decisive strategic victory, and how rising American public scepticism and regional realignments are testing Washington’s traditionally unconditional backing. It also unpacks New Delhi’s pragmatic “de-hyphenation” policy, as India deepens defence and economic ties with Tel Aviv while maintaining its institutional support for a two-state solution.
For readers seeking a deeper grasp of a rapidly shifting West Asia, Israel at an Inflection Point provides essential historical depth and geopolitical context. Read this e-book to examine the domestic fault lines reshaping Israeli polity, the global economic fallout of the conflict with Iran, and India’s delicate diplomatic balancing act in the region.
