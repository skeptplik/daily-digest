The need-to-know this morning
- Vir Biotechnology said monthly injections of two experimental medicines reduced hepatitis D virus to undetectable levels in 44% of patients compared to none of the patients who received delayed treatment with the same regimen. The result achieved the primary goal of a Phase 3 study.
- Zura Bio said its experimental antibody tibulizumab failed to improve the symptoms of hidradenitis suppurativa, an inflammatory skin condition, compared to a placebo in a mid-stage study.
- Roivant said Lisraya generated $31 million in sales in the five weeks since it was approved to treat dermatomyositis.
- Argenx is acquiring privately held Unnatural Products, a medicinal chemistry company that specializes in the discovery of protein-based drugs.
