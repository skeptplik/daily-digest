Perth Bears to play Manly in first NRL game as round one fixture is announced
In short:
The Perth Bears will play Manly in the first round of the 2027 NRL season.
The game will bring together the two warring parties of the Northern Eagles merger 25 years after its collapse.
What's next?
Parramatta, Gold Coast, Melbourne and Wests Tigers will kick off the season in Las Vegas.
