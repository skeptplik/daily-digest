The last month of the 2026 is set to bring a sneaker many people have been waiting all year for.
One of the most beloved treatments in the Air Jordan 11 franchise is coming back a decade after its last appearance, with the Space Jam stepping in for the annual release. Small but key details set this year’s drop apart from previous ones, and its arrival during the 30th anniversary of the film has been preceded by an unprecedented volume of other sneakers.
Outside of that moment, the slate is also highlighted by a premium, Western-themed Air Jordan 4 Bin23 and an Air Jordan 8 in New York Knicks colors. Not appearing on this list are two sneakers yet to get a confirmed date but nonetheless expected in December: Social Status’ Air Jordan 1 Low Sashiko and the Solefly Air Jordan 5.
