Trump hails 'fantastic' relationships with China's Xi, Russia's Putin after three-way talks
US President Donald Trump played up his "fantastic" relationships with China's Xi Jinping and Russia's Vladimir Putin during a White House press conference on Friday following his return to Washington after a first-ever trilateral meeting with the Chinese and Russian leaders. Here are key takeaways from his wide-ranging briefing.
Trump and his Chinese and Russian counterparts agreed on Thursday at their landmark three-way meeting in Shenzhen, China, that a "nuclear war cannot be won and must never be fought".
The US president, who did not give a press conference in China following the talks, told a White House briefing on Friday that his meeting with Xi and Putin had "great electricity".
'Fantastic' relationships with Xi, Putin
Trump said he had a "great relationship" with China's Xi, dwelling at length on the Chinese leader's rare move to greet him on the tarmac after Air Force One arrived in Shenzhen ahead of the talks.
He later spoke of a "fantastic" relationship with both Xi and Putin.
"I have fantastic relationships with Putin. That's a very good thing. I have fantastic relationships with President Xi. That's a good thing, and our country is safe because of that."
He said the three leaders discussed limiting the proliferation of nuclear weapons, "in particular" with regard to Iran's nuclear programme.
Ukraine, Iran wars
Trump said his Russian counterpart told him he wants a diplomatic end to the Ukraine war, hours after Moscow's forces launched the latest wave of deadly strikes on Kyiv.
In a bilateral meeting, Putin "clearly expressed his desire to see the war between Russia and Ukraine end by diplomatic means," Trump told journalists at the White House.

Touching on talks with Iran, the US president said Tehran had been "bullshitting" the world for half a century.
"They want to make a deal, but they don't want to make a deal that's acceptable to me," he said, before adding: "Watch my lips, Iran will never have a nuclear weapon."
Trade deals 'coming very soon'
Moving onto trade, Trump said he expected the US to secure 10 additional trade deals after his trip to Asia.
"In four days, I met more than 20 world leaders, secured two new trade deals and about 10 others (are) coming very soon," he said.
He added that his administration was also making progress in trade talks with neighbours Canada and Mexico.
Data centres
The US president said he would not allow US firm to build data centres abroad, vowing "tremendous penalties" for those that do so.
The Republican president urged Americans to welcome data centre construction in their communities and extract concessions from the developers, repeating a point he made before the midterms.
"You ought to take advantage of the data centres," he said. "Make them pay you, make them pay your community, but don’t shut them off."
Praise for Anthropic
The US president congratulated Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei for the company's blockbuster IPO, saying he had "a lot of respect" for the AI leader.

The remarks could represent a detente between the White House and Anthropic, after the Trump administration earlier this year ordered federal agencies to stop using the company's AI products and designated the firm a national security risk.
A federal judge later overturned the administration's decision.
Jab at 'political' Fed
Trump reiterated his frequent criticism of the Federal Reserve, claiming interest rates were too high because the Fed is "political".
He added that he did not blame the Federal Reserve chairman.
