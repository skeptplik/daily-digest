Staggs says refusal to sing Australian anthem is due to respect for ancestors
In short:
Kotoni Staggs says he refuses to sing the Australian national anthem out of respect for his Indigenous elders.
Staggs and several teammates have been criticised for not singing the anthem during the Rugby League World Cup.
What's next?
Staggs and Australia will next be in action in the World Cup semifinal against Samoa in Sydney on Sunday.
