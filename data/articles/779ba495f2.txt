Javier Martínez-Cantó, political scientist at the Institute of Public Goods and Policies (IPP–CSIC), says corruption scandals "have been hindering the popularity" of Spain's Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez as he seeks to remain in power ahead of Sunday's snap elections.
A right-wing majority would most likely bring the far-right Vox into a coalition with the conservative Partido Popular (PP), Martínez-Cantó says.
The analyst believes such an alliance would mark a departure from Spain’s “traditional political environment” and lead to “some differences” in areas such as relations with the US, given Sánchez’s confrontational stance towards US President Donald Trump.
