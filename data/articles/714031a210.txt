Shang-Chi is getting ready to return to the Marvel Cinematic Universe in Avengers: Doomsday, but fans hoping to see more of the hero have another reason to be optimistic. Dave Callaham, who co-wrote Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings, has confirmed that he's working on a sequel, offering the clearest sign yet that the character's next solo adventure is moving forward.
Speaking on the Escape Hatch podcast, Callaham, whose writing credits include Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse and Mortal Kombat, said the project is still in its early stages, but that there is "some real movement." He added that he was happy to share the news. Callaham didn't offer details about the story or say when the movie might go into production.
Shang-Chi's co-writer is working on a sequel movie
Shang-Chi first appeared in Marvel Comics in 1973, created by writer Steve Englehart and artist Jim Starlin. His original story revolved around his father, Fu Manchu, a criminal mastermind who raised him to become an assassin. After discovering his father's plans for world conquest, Shang-Chi turned against him and used his martial arts skills to fight the criminal empire. The character became one of Marvel's best-known martial artists, often trading blows with or fighting alongside the likes of Iron Fist and Daredevil. His MCU debut came in 2021's Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings, which replaced Fu Manchu with Wenwu (Tony Leung), providing a more complex reinterpretation of Shang-Chi's origins.
Marvel first announced a sequel in December 2021, with director Destin Daniel Cretton (Spider-Man: Brand New Day, Wonder Man) expected to return. Cretton co-wrote and directed the original film, but has since taken on other projects, including the announced Naruto live-action movie. In the years since the sequel was announced, Marvel has shared few concrete details about its development. Cretton has previously said the project remained in development and pointed to the disruption caused by the COVID-19 pandemic as a factor in the delay.
Callaham's update is encouraging news, but Marvel has not announced a release date, production schedule, or further casting. Still, hearing from one of the original film's writers that work is underway is a meaningful step forward after five years of uncertainty.
Fans won't have to wait much longer to see Simu Liu back in the role. The actor will return as Shang-Chi in Avengers: Doomsday, which is scheduled to arrive in theaters on Dec. 18. It will be the character's first major MCU appearance since his solo debut, giving Marvel a chance to test Shang-Chi's popularity before fully moving forward with its next standalone outing.
