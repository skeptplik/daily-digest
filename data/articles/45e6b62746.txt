Welcome to “Fashionista Face It,” in which practitioners, dermatologists, facialists and other beauty experts answer our rapid-fire questions (quicker than you can say “just a few more units, please?”), revealing their hottest takes, most controversial opinions and the hard truths they wish their clients could come to terms with.
Joseph Carrillo has made a career of being all up in people’s faces. He got his start as an editorial and celebrity makeup artist — a gig he still does — but has also gone on to become a pioneer in “facial sculpting,” launching his Sculpted by Joseph business and developing a signature method for de-puffing, lifting and defining the face to give it a natural “facelift” effect. His focus? Facial posture, and the muscles’ ability to prop everything up and fight the droop-inducing effects of gravity.
Though Carrillo shies away from name dropping (gotta love a modest king), his celebrity client list is a truly impressive one: He regularly works with Mariska Hargitay (Olivia! Benson!), Emmy Rossum, Michelle Williams, Chloe Fineman (with whom he recently went viral for her facelift-adjacent results), Suki Waterhouse, Hillary Clinton (can you think of anyone more deserving of a little tension release?? I can’t.) and many others.
For “Fashionista Face It,” Carrillo shared his most controversial beauty opinions, surprising insights and hard truths. The expert also set the record straight on misconceptions he wishes people would stop believing. (“I don’t know who made up that you shouldn’t be touching your face. You should be touching it all the time,” he quipped.)
