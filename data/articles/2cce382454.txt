The Vogue Business Beauty Tracker, produced in collaboration with Spate, tracks the top five brands, trends and ingredients that are driving the most significant positive change in year-on-year growth that have also demonstrated consistent upward growth historically. Each week, we’ll spotlight a different beauty category and the top five beauty hashtags on TikTok.
Makeup (October 2025 to September 2026)
The makeup products consumers are searching for the most are those combining precise application, long wear, and shimmering finishes. Tools such as winged eyeliner stamps take the stress and guesswork out of creating a sharp, even cat-eye look, while creamy lip liners offer buildable definition through soft, blendable formulas. Gripping primers and longwear lipsticks also reflect demand for staying power, from tacky primers that give makeup a sticky base to lip formulas designed to resist fading and transfer. Meanwhile, the rise of rose gold eyeshadow highlights interest in pink-toned metallic shades that add dimension to eye looks.
When it comes to makeup brands, lasting interest in Burberry Beauty signals the strength of luxury cosmetics from established fashion houses, especially foundations. Nude by Nature highlights demand for makeup powered by natural Australian botanicals. Beauty Creations, a Latina-founded brand, stands out for high-pigment formulas, frequent limited-edition collections, and pop culture partnerships. Sculpted by Aimee, founded by makeup artist Aimee Connolly, reflects interest in multitasking, skin-first products that simplify daily routines. Finally, KeyYou points to demand for lash extension kits and cluster lashes designed for self-application.
Trending on TikTok
There’s a demand for the nostalgic on TikTok, with the #ThrowBackBeauty hashtag showing creators recreating and reviewing makeup, hair, and fashion looks from past decades. Over on #PerfumeTok, the fragrance community is reviewing, ranking, and recommending scents, while #BlackFridayBeautyDeals points to anticipation ahead of TikTok Shop’s official Black Friday campaign. Meanwhile, #FalseLashesTutorial reflects demand for step-by-step guidance on applying false lashes, and #ServerHairInspo highlights low-maintenance hairstyles built to last throughout the work shift — fueled by the viral “server hair theory” that certain styles can earn servers higher tips.
Skincare (October 2025 to September 2026)
Skincare products that combine regenerative ingredients, targeted application, and everyday hydration are on the rise in search. Fresh PDRN formats — creams, face masks, and eye patches — reinforce interest in the ingredient, which is associated with cellular repair and a stronger skin barrier, as well as its use in targeted treatments like the Abib PDRN Retinal Eye Patch. Increased searches for hyaluronic acid face lotion reflects demand for deep hydration in everyday skincare, while liquid pimple patches highlight interest in subtle formulas that directly target blemishes and dry into an invisible film.
Skincare consumers are particularly intrigued in brands combining clinical-grade formulas, advanced delivery technologies, and everyday sun protection. Magic Molecule reflects continued demand for hypochlorous acid, offering a multi-purpose, FDA-cleared spray for everyday skincare. KRX Aesthetics and Meditherapy, both South Korean brands, are known for their professional skin treatments adapted for at-home use. Radical Skincare stands out for its performance-driven anti-aging formulas, powered by its proprietary Trylacel delivery technology. Finally, Invisible Zinc reflects demand for mineral-based sunscreens that use zinc oxide as the primary ingredient instead of chemical UV filters.
Trending on TikTok
Over on TikTok, #DolphinSkin reflects interest in ultra-glossy, smooth, and luminous skin, specifically on the body. #GreenChromeNails and #ColorLashes highlight bold approaches to color, from mirror-like metallic manicures in shades of green to multi-colored lash looks. Content featuring #TTSKbeauty, or TikTok Shop K-beauty, shows the platform’s growing role in K-beauty product discovery. Meanwhile, the rise of #JasmineFragrance is driven by Chun Breeze Xian Jasmine, a Chinese fragrance that creators compare to the jasmine tea scent of tea house chains Molly Tea and Heytea.
Haircare (September 2026 to August 2026)
In haircare, products that combine scalp support, deep nourishment, and targeted cleansing are trending in search. Peptide hair serums cater to those seeking lightweight formulas that support fuller-looking hair, with searches associated primarily with The Ordinary’s Multi-Peptide Serum for Hair Density, while hair thinning serums points to a broader focus on density and scalpcare. Rice water hair spray highlights interest in a convenient leave-in format for an ingredient valued for adding strength and shine. Cholesterol hair treatment reflects demand for rich, deep-conditioning masks that restore moisture and softness, and chlorine removal shampoo signals demand for clarifying formulas that remove chlorine build-up post-swim.
When it comes to brands, the rise of K-beauty player Lilyeve reflects demand for daily scalpcare, with searches led by its Scalp Turn Cica PDRN Brush Ampoule, a cooling leave-in treatment applied through a built-in brush head. Demand for Honeyque products highlights interest in salon-grade Japanese haircare designed to repair dry, damaged and frizzy hair, while Chāmpo stands out for blending ayurvedic principles with modern trichology, particularly through its hair serum. Bare Anatomy underscores continued demand for science-backed, dermatologically tested haircare, from anti-dandruff shampoo to a roll-on scalp serum. Finally, Brickell Men’s Products reflects interest in natural grooming formulated specifically for men.
Trending on TikTok
Beyond traditional beauty content, #MakeupGame on TikTok reflects the growing overlap between beauty and gaming, with posts centered around Makeup Mania, a Roblox game where players compete through makeup and fashion looks. #HowToSilkPress and #4CWashDay highlight continued demand for textured hair education, from step-by-step silk press tutorials to wash day routines tailored to tightly coiled 4C hair. The #GlitterAcrylic hashtag points to interest in sparkling, statement acrylic nail sets, while #EczemaPatch shows the hydrocolloid format popularized by pimple patches expanding to eczema-prone skin.
Wellness (September 2025 to August 2026)
In the wellness world, there’s growing interest in specialized nutrition and brands that prioritize convenience and targeted health benefits. Pure Genius Protein reflects demand for portable, high-protein formats, with ready-to-drink shots providing 23 grams of protein per serving. Sisel highlights a broad approach to wellness through dietary supplements and functional beverages, while Toplux Nutrition emphasizes science-backed supplements at affordable prices. Wip stands out for its nicotine-free caffeine pouches, offering a convenient format for an energy boost. Finally, Triquetra Health reflects interest in gut and metabolic wellness, with searches focused on its GLP-Pro probiotic and postbiotic supplement. Collectively, these brands span everyday protein intake, energy support, and targeted nutritional supplementation.
Trending on TikTok
In the midst of October, #HalloweenHairClips are trending on TikTok, reflecting interest in festive, spooky hair accessories — particularly horror-themed claw clips and seasonal designs. Meanwhile, the rise of #LadybugMakeup highlights whimsical, insect-inspired makeup looks also tailored to spooky season. Across manicure content, #UglyNails are performing well, as more unconventional and unusual nail designs gain traction for allowing artistic expression, while #PinkCateyeNails reflects interest in light-reflecting cateye designs in shades of pink. The #CoilyCurly hashtag celebrates natural hair textures between curly and coily, with content focused on haircare tips and styling.
Bodycare (September 2025 to August 2026)
Products that combine hydration, targeted care and sensory experiences are ruling the bodycare category. Hydrating body mists meet demand for lightweight, water-like moisturizers that deliver lotion-level hydration, while anti-sweat hand creams offer targeted solutions for managing perspiration on the hands. Growing demand for hand mists suggest interest in convenient, fragrant formats that refresh and sanitize on the go, while niacinamide body wash is an example of products designed to support the skin’s moisture barrier. Finally, strawberry body scrub reflects demand for fruit-inspired bodycare that combines exfoliation with appealing scents and ingredients.
Across bodycare brands, there’s growing interest in those offering specialized and efficient approaches to personal care and grooming. Carpe reflects demand for clinical-strength sweat and odor control, while Hanni highlights simplified bodycare through “skip-step” essentials, with a particular rise in searches for its Splash Salve in-shower body balm. Proraso stands out for its Italian men’s grooming heritage. Estée Lauder has evolved its fragrance range by incorporating bodycare into its iconic scent collections, as searches for its Beautiful Perfumed Body Powder grow. Meanwhile, Prequel reflects interest in dermatologist-developed, science-backed skincare centered on skin barrier health.
Trending on TikTok
This week on TikTok, the #DiscountMakeup hashtag is flooded with shoppers unexpectedly discovering Jeffree Star Cosmetics products at Dollar Tree. Meanwhile, content around #PerfumeMists is focused on reviews and recommendations of lightweight, refreshing fragrance formats. #TattooLipLiner content is also on the rise, featuring ink-like liners, peel-off stains, and semi-permanent lip treatments for long-lasting lip definition. #GlowyMakeupRoutine points to interest in luminous, radiant makeup looks and routines, while #FeatheredHair highlights interest in the ’70s voluminous, heavily layered hair look. Together, these trends highlight how beauty content is increasingly shaped by product discovery, makeup routines and nostalgic aesthetics.
Fragrance (September 2025 to August 2026)
There’s growing interest in fragrance trends that combine personalization, identity, and convenience. Alcohol-free fragrances, for example, reflect demand for alternative formulations to traditional perfumes, while custom perfumes point to interest in personalized scents tailored to individual preferences. The rise of masculine fragrances suggests that scent is increasingly functioning as an identity marker for male and female consumers alike, while gothic perfume reflects demand for darker and more mysterious aromatic profiles that evoke the romantic and atmospheric elements of the goth aesthetic. Finally, travel fragrances, the portable and convenient scent formats designed to make fragrance easier to pack and use on the go, are also trending.
This month, there’s particular interest in fragrance brands offering distinctive approaches to scent. Lore’s emotive, transportive fragrances translate specific moods and places into immersive scent experiences, while MCM Fragrance and Miu Miu highlight growing interest in luxury scents that extend recognizable fashion identities into fragrance, with searches for the latter mainly associated with Miutine and its youthful fragrance identity. Ranger Station stands out for its bold, high-concentration, nature-inspired scents, while Maison d’Etto offers perfumes rooted in equestrian influence, contemporary art and architecture. These brands highlight growing consumer interest in fragrance that goes beyond scent itself, offering distinctive stories, identities and sensory experiences.
Trending on TikTok
As we enter October season, #SpookyNailArt is trending on TikTok, reflecting interest in Halloween-inspired nail designs based on playful motifs. Meanwhile, growing interest in the #MakeupColorTheory reinforces the rise of consumers learning about the hues that enhance their features. #ConcertHair highlights demand for hairstyles that are durable, creative and highly accessorized, specifically designed to last throughout a concert, while the #RomanticUpdo reflects interest in soft, feminine hairstyles and intentionally undone styles. #CologneHaul shows interest in discovering and showcasing fragrance purchases and collections.
Nails (September 2025 to August 2026)
When it comes to nails, there’s a growing interest in trends that combine precision, personalization, and visually distinct finishes. The Italian manicure, which creates the appearance of longer slender nails, reflects demand for more precise techniques, while nail art pens and letters on nails point to interest in customized designs and intricate details. The rise of glass nail polish highlights a preference for glossy, translucent finishes, and vibrant nails reflect demand for bold, eye-catching colors.
Among this month’s trending nail brands, there’s a clear preference for distinct approaches to at-home manicures. Reusable, press-on nail line Never Have I Ever incorporates licensed artwork from independent artists and cultural icons into nail designs, while Double Rhythm reinforces interest in accessible, soak-off gel polishes and nail art supplies for DIY and salon-style looks. IPolish stands out for its digital color-changing press-on nails, allowing users to switch between hundreds of shades using a smartphone and activation wand. Heyhae offers sun-cured gel nail strips that harden naturally in sunlight without requiring a traditional UV or LED lamp, while Sparkle & Co. specializes in DIY dip powders with unique glitters, foils, flakes, glow-in-the-dark, and color-changing effects.
Trending on TikTok
TikTok is seeing an influx of content featuring #BumperBangs, reflecting interest in vintage 1940s and ’50s fringe styles consisting of a bold, rounded shape. Meanwhile, the rise of #CurlyHairBride highlights demand for bridal hair inspiration that embraces natural curls and textured styles. #WigsOnTikTok shows the growing role of TikTok in discovering, styling, and showcasing wigs. #UltaFragrance and #SephoraBeautyAdvisor highlight interest in beauty advisors sharing recommendations, product expertise and customer interactions at retailers Ulta and Sephora. Together, these hashtags highlight interest in vintage looks, occasion-specific styling, and behind-the-scenes beauty retail experiences.
Makeup (August 2025 to July 2026)
There’s a growing interest in makeup products that combine ease of use, enhanced appearance, and convenient beauty benefits, according to monthly search data. Growth in creamy lip liners points to demand for smooth, easy-to-apply products that shape and define the lips, while lip volumizers help create the appearance of fuller, smoother lips without invasive treatments. Rose gold eyeshadow is also trending, driven by demand for warm, luminous shades. Gripping primer, which is a gel-based product with a tacky finish to hold your makeup in place, reflects interest in creating durable makeup looks that last throughout the day. The rise of SPF face powder reveals interest in facial sun protection beyond conventional sunscreens, with multifunctional products that both protect and control shine.
Across makeup brands, those offering distinctive products spanning various price points are performing particularly well. Hung Vanngo Beauty reflects demand for makeup artist expertise, while Burberry Beauty signals continued interest in luxury cosmetics from established fashion houses. C-beauty brand Cheeryep and Sleek Makeup highlight demand for vibrant, high-pigment makeup and innovative finishes. Maven Beauty, Fashion Nova’s makeup brand, suggests growing demand for affordable beauty options.
Trending on TikTok
On TikTok, #UVHairExtensions are gaining traction, reflecting interest in innovative UV and LED hair extension techniques offering fast application, while #BrownRedHair highlights demand for rich, warm hair color combinations of brown, red, copper, and mahogany tones. Manicurist content featuring #PracticeNails shows past designs and their training progress, while #CottagecoreNails spotlights whimsical, nature-inspired manicures influenced by cottagecore aesthetics. The rise of #BridesmaidsMakeup reflects growing demand for beauty inspiration tailored to bridesmaids and wedding looks.
Skincare (August 2025 to July 2026)
In skincare, monthly interest in products and treatments that combine targeted skin health benefits with gentle, barrier-supporting ingredients and advanced technologies is rising. Silicone-free sunscreen and its lightweight, ingredient-conscious sun protection is trending, while the retinol sandwich method, where retinol is layered between moisturizer to reduce the risk of irritation, is gaining traction among those looking to safely introduce retinol into their routines. There is continued demand for hyaluronic acid face lotions offering convenient, hydrating properties that support moisture retention and skin plumpness, while ceramide toners are designed to strengthen and maintain the skin barrier. Interest in high-intensity focused ultrasound (HIFU) treatments highlights demand for advanced, non-invasive aesthetic treatments focused on skin tightening and rejuvenation.
For brands, those offering science-backed and targeted skincare solutions are trending. Elm Biosciences, which develops clinical skincare focused on healthy aging and cellular-level care, is gaining traction, while Magic Molecule offers hypochlorous acid solutions that cleanse, reduce inflammation and support skin healing. The rise of TropicSport signals demand for high-performance sun protection during active lifestyles. NewGel+ reflects interest in specialized scarcare and skin repair. The Body Shop, particularly its Drops of Youth line, highlights demand for products targeting early signs of aging while supporting skin resilience against daily environmental stressors.
Trending on TikTok
On TikTok, #SheinMakeupHaul is trending, reflective of the growing interest in discovering and sharing affordable makeup products through beauty hauls. #BestHairOils shows how more consumers are comparing different hair oils and seeking product recommendations. #BakedBlush features content spotlighting new blush launches and shades comparisons through product swatches and reviews. #TinselBraids and #CakeNails highlight playful approaches to self-expression through decorative hairstyles and creative nail designs, with consumers exploring visually distinctive looks.
Haircare (August 2025 to July 2026)
This month’s search data highlights growing interest in haircare products that combine targeted follicle growth with plant-based ingredients. Popular hair serums range from including specific active ingredients, with searches for redensyl and peptide serums that help reduce shedding by supporting follicle health and improving fullness, to concern-specific searches such as hair thinning serum. Rice water hairspray highlights demand for convenient, multifunctional products that can strengthen strands, add shine and reduce frizz, while plant-based shampoos reflect growing interest in products made primarily with vegan and botanical ingredients.
