Beauty technology has officially outgrown the bathroom cabinet. While LED face masks have become something of a skin care status symbol, red light therapy panels are making a case for going bigger (and we mean quite literally). These larger devices bring light-based treatments beyond the face, taking at-home light therapy beyond the complexion.
The Best Red Light Therapy Panels of 2026
What exactly are the benefits of red light therapy panels?
The technology, known as photobiomodulation, uses specific wavelengths of red and near-infrared light to influence cellular activity. Research suggests that red light therapy may help stimulate collagen production, soften the appearance of fine lines, improve skin texture, and reduce certain types of inflammation. Near-infrared light penetrates deeper into tissue, where its potential benefits for muscle recovery and discomfort are also being studied.
Unlike traditional LED masks, panels can illuminate larger areas, including the back, legs, and shoulders. It’s an interesting next step for beauty enthusiasts ready to explore the technology beyond their complexion.
Of course, there’s more to a good panel than an impressive number of glowing bulbs. Wavelengths, LED density, irradiance (the amount of light reaching a given area), and treatment coverage all matter. Some devices incorporate 1064-nanometer near-infrared light, although research into its specific applications is still developing.
The market includes everything from compact, targeted devices to substantial full-body systems. Among the options worth exploring are Helio Cure’s Helio Nova, Mito Red Light’s MitoMega 2.0, Joovv’s Solo 3.0, RLT Home’s Total Spectrum Compact, and PlatinumLED’s Biomax Pro.
Ahead, we’re breaking down five red light therapy panels, examining their specifications, technology, and intended uses to help you find the right fit. Because when beauty technology requires its own designated floor space, knowing what you’re investing in seems like a reasonable place to start.
