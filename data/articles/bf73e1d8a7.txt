As Vogue editors, we often wax poetic on the latest runway must-haves, but not every item we gush about on this website ultimately ends up in our closets. Our discoveries are nice to behold—they’re lovely to look at and inspire fun conversation—however, of all the tempting things to buy, what did we actually end up purchasing? Each month we’ll highlight those special finds our editors welcomed into their lives—all the things that made us buyers rejoice.
Vogue Editors’ Monthly Picks:
The Pop of Color
“Time to make the fall switch: this month I invested in color blocking and protecting my skin from chilly autumn air!” —Chloe Malle, head of editorial content
The Elevated Staples
“Blazers have always been a wardrobe staple of mine. The right tailoring does much of the work for you, instantly making an outfit feel more polished and pulled together; I picked up Cos’s longline style earlier this month. I always have room for one more skirt in my wardrobe, and this Prada one is the most recent addition to my collection. I’ve found skirts to be some of the most worthwhile pieces to invest in thanks to their versatility across seasons. As the days grow cooler, I love experimenting with layers– pairing a skirt with boots and a tailored trench, or playing with proportions by double-layering cashmere knits.
Last but not least, I picked up this charm (along with a few others) at the Celine store in Paris. (Is it even Paris Fashion week without a little shopping between shows?) They’ve quickly become some of my favorite finishing touches.” —Lisa Aiken, SVP, commerce
