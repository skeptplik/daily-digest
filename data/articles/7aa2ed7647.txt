Ultimate Fighting Championship (UFC) boss Dana White declined President Donald Trump's offer to be the next White House press secretary during a briefing on Monday.
The president had asked White to take over the role in a social post over the weekend. Trump later said he was "having a little fun".
During the briefing, White confirmed he won't take the job as he doesn't "want to be in politics."
