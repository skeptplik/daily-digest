PEN PALS: After plenty of reading through its successful Miu Miu Literary Club format, Miu Miu is inviting its fans to rediscover the joy of writing, too.
For the holiday season, the brand is introducing the Miu Miu Correspondence activation, developed in collaboration with Milan’s historic stationery store Pettinaroli.
Inspired by the tradition of handwritten communication, the initiative will see the fashion brand engaging with its community from Nov. 27 to 29, when it will gift bespoke letter-writing sets at the Pettinaroli store nestled in Via Brera. The sets will include specially decorated Miu Miu notelets and envelopes, as well as illustrated holiday postcards, embellished with festive motifs.
Graphics tie in with the Miu Miu 2026 holiday campaign, offering a more naïve and candid take on its playful theme. Released last month and photographed by Tina Barney, the images saw the likes of Gigi Hadid, Louisa Jacobson, XiaoWen Ju and Nyla Singleton portrayed in bourgeois interiors while surrounded by plushies and holding in their arms soft toy bears, rabbits and geese, in addition to the brand’s accessories. The animals are now the faces of the illustrated kits developed with Pettinaroli.
To further mark the collaboration, a special set-up will be conceived for the stationary store’s windows during the activation. At the same time, visitors to the Miu Miu store in Milan’s Via Sant’Andrea will also be able to have their cards customized by a calligrapher. In addition, shoppers visiting the store from Dec. 11 to 20 will have the opportunity to adorn their purchases via a wrapping station reflecting the creativity of Miu Miu Correspondence.
Founded in 1881 by Francesco Pettinaroli, the historic store stands as one of Milan’s most prestigious stationery destinations and a go-to address for locals and tourists alike. An enduring symbol of Italian craftsmanship, for more than 140 years the family-owned business has combined fine stationery, traditional typography, bookbinding and the creation of artisanal paper goods. Alongside its stationery collections, Pettinaroli offers antique maps and rare prints, as well as handcrafted leather goods and refined writing accessories.
