George Lazenby, who formerly portrayed James Bond, is giving Robert Pattinson some sage advice after the Batman actor scored the historic and highly coveted role.
After a lengthy and secretive casting process stacked with some of Britain’s greatest actors, Robert Pattinson was officially revealed as the next James Bond on November 16, 2026. This marks the latest in a string of high-profile parts Pattinson has played lately, which include Batman, Chris Hansen in Primetime, Antinous in Nolan’s Odyssey, and Scytale in Dune: Part Three.
It’s safe to say that Pattinson is no stranger to being the face of a franchise, what with his experience playing Edward Cullen in the Twilight saga and Batman in Matt Reeves’ take on the caped crusader. But James Bond is a different beast entirely, as fans’ reactions to this latest news have already proven this fact.
Luckily, he’s not alone. A slew of other actors have taken up the 007 mantle before him, including Lazenby, who portrayed the character in 1969's On Her Majesty’s Secret Service. Lazenby took the opportunity to give Pattinson some useful tips before he steps into M16.
“I hear they've announced the new James Bond,” Lazenby wrote in a post shared on social media. “Robert, welcome to the most exclusive club in the world. But remember to enjoy yourself. Let's see if you'll have [a] ‘This never happened to the other fella!’ moment. All the best, mate, George xx.”
It’s safe to say Pattinson is already having one of those moments, given the energetic opinions we’ve seen on social media already. Many fans are ecstatic about him being cast as Bond, given his extensive filmography playing the tortured hero; others, however, aren’t so sure that he’s a good fit, while others are joking that he’s being too “greedy” by taking up all the best roles in Hollywood.

It’s certainly been quite a year for Pattinson, who’s had a nice few films that have dominated the box office this year. He, along with Tom Holland and Zendaya, is one of the most recognizable faces in the business right now — and it proves how far he’s come from his days as Edward Cullen.
In fact, Pattinson was apparently so coveted for Bond that he was reportedly offered a more lucrative contract for fewer films than other actors in the running, which included Callum Turner, Jack Lowden, Josh O’Connor, and Nicholas Hoult.
For now, we’ll just have to wait and see what Pattinson’s Bond has to offer. In the meantime, you can look forward to watching him in Dune: Part Three this December.
