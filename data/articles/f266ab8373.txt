On Friday night at the Mother Wolf restaurant in Los Angeles, actress and comedian Janelle Jones attended a celebration of “Abbot Elementary’s” 100th episode. James wore a simple but stylish black dress that she combined with an eye-catching pair of Balmain Ruby Monogram Slingback Pumps.
James wore a classically elegant ensemble for the evening, walking the red carpet in a strapless black midi-dress with some strategic ruching near the bottom half. She paired the outfit with a statement chain-linked collar choker in a gold tone.
Her shoes matched the gown while adding a bit of flair to the classic little black dress look. She opted for the Balmain Ruby slingback pumps in black leather, which feature a gold hardware ankle strap and jacquard slingback strap patterned with a black and white labyrinth motif. The pumps also have a high stiletto heel and pointed toe. James wore her hair down in relaxed waves and sported shimmery eyeshadow, a touch of mascara, and a crimson purple lipstick.

Like many of her “Abbot Elementary” costars, James has been seen out and about lately promoting the show’s sixth season. James is certainly a fan of high-end footwear as evident by many of her red carpet looks. At the 2025 For Your Consideration Emmy event, for example, James wore a striking pair of white Versace slingback pumps. She is also a fan of enduringly stylish red carpet fashion. For the 2024 Emmys, where James was nominated for “Best Supporting Actress in a Comedy Series”, James opted for a red Rodarte gown with delicate shoulder bows.
Created by Quinta Brunson, “Abbott Elementary” is a mockumentary-style sitcom that has been going strong since its 2021 premiere. The show now spans six seasons and James plays the often self-absorbed principal Ava Coleman. “Abbott Elementary” has received critical acclaim throughout its run, receiving multiple awards including four Primetime Emmys. The show’s 100th episode is set to premiere on December 6, 2026.
