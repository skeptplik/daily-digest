Tokyo Filmex, a leading showcase of Asian and Japanese art house films, returns Nov. 20 to 29, with screenings at venues in the Yurakucho and Marunouchi districts.
Launched in 2000, Filmex sources many of its Asian titles from the Venice, Cannes and Toronto film festivals. From 2020 to 2023, it was held concurrently with the more populist Tokyo International Film Festival, which effectively made it a TIFF sidebar, but since 2024 it has resumed its traditional late November dates while reasserting its distinctive identity.
The wide-ranging program comprises three sections — Competition, Special Screenings and Made in Japan — as well as a two-film retrospective devoted to the work of Ukrainian director Kira Muratova. Screenings will be held at Yurakucho Asahi Hall, Marunouchi Piccadilly and Human Trust Cinema Yurakucho.
