The MacBook Neo is on sale (finally), bringing the price down from $699 to $649. Fifty dollars might not sound like a lot, but it's the largest discount the MacBook Neo has ever received. The previous best deal was just $10 off. As Apple's first foray into the truly budget-tier laptop, the MacBook Neo's price has been stubbornly steady, more so than any other MacBook in the lineup. It's one of a few great early Black Friday deals we're spotting.
The MacBook Neo does come with a number of compromises compared to the MacBook Air. The most notable is that it only has 8 GB of unified memory. This is what keeps it from feeling like a truly capable work or student laptop. If you spend most of your working hours sitting in front of a computer, the MacBook Air is worth springing for over the Neo.
While the system balances memory use fairly well, you'll need to be a bit more cognizant of what apps, tabs, and background tasks you have running simultaneously to avoid slowdowns. It also lacks keyboard backlighting and can only support one external display. The base configuration doesn’t include Touch ID, meaning you'll have to type in a password to log in every time. These features are more quality-of-life in nature, but when you add in the extra performance of the MacBook Air, it's well worth the extra money.
Still, if you're after an affordable laptop that doesn't feel cheap, the MacBook Neo is excellent at playing that part. The display is bright and beautiful, and the build quality is as sturdy as you'd expect an Apple product to be. It even comes in a few unique colors. There are a lot of people who could just use a basic laptop, and $649 is a great price for one.
I should note that Apple does sell the laptop for $100 off as part of its education discount, but it requires having an active student ID. That's the way to go if you happen to be a student or teacher. For everyone else, though, this is the cheapest the MacBook Neo has been since late June, when Apple increased prices across the entire MacBook lineup.
