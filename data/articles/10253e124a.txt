The deputy commander of Russia’s Aerospace Forces, Colonel General Yuri Grekhov, has been arrested in Moscow on corruption charges, the newspaper Kommersant reported Thursday, citing law enforcement sources.
A judge at a Moscow military court ordered Grekhov’s detention on Wednesday, the newspaper said. Specific details of the case were not immediately clear, with sources familiar with the investigation saying only that the allegations “pertained to corruption.”
Law enforcement sources separately told the news outlet RBC that Grekhov faces charges of bribery on a particularly large scale. He allegedly pleaded not guilty in court.
Officials have not publicly commented on the general’s reported arrest.
Grekhov has served as deputy commander of the Aerospace Forces since 2021 — the year before President Vladimir Putin ordered the full-scale invasion of Ukraine — and was promoted to the rank of colonel general that same year.
Prior to his current post, Grekhov spent more than a decade holding senior command positions within Russia’s air defense forces.
The European Union sanctioned Grekhov over his role in the Ukraine war, citing his involvement in Russian strikes on civilian infrastructure.
