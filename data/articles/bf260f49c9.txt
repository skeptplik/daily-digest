Measles is endemic again in the U.S.
Such is the verdict of global health experts who just met to consider the country’s measles elimination status, granted in 2000.
Their decision — announced on Nov. 17, but reported over the weekend — wasn’t a shock. Rather, it was a confirmation of what experts have long warned: The U.S. is giving up decades of hard-won public-health gains. The status change “means the virus has circulated for a prolonged period of time, putting families and communities at risk of a preventable illness, hospitalization and death,” explained Dr. Jarbas Barbosa at the press briefing.
