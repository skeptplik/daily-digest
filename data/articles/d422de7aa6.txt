A shift is underway across the fashion industry, and it is not simply a matter of new tools arriving on the scene. Artificial intelligence is reshaping how fashion businesses operate, accelerating content creation, streamlining workflows and transforming the skills expected of emerging professionals.
The question for educators is how to prepare students to lead rather than simply adapt. At Vogue College of Fashion, the answer lies in developing the judgment, perspective and creative confidence that endures, regardless of how technology evolves.
Across programs, Vogue College students combine academic learning with practical industry experience, developing specialist knowledge and the critical thinking, communication, adaptability and individual perspective needed to navigate a changing industry.
As BA graduate Dami Odeyemi explains: “I think the most valuable skill I gained was learning how to effectively market myself in the fashion industry. When I first started my studies, I had ambition but lacked knowledge of how to position myself as a fashion professional. After the program, I not only gained confidence but also practical skills to critically assess my own work, articulate my unique value proposition and offer meaningful services to clients and brands in the fashion industry.”
The students moving through Vogue College of Fashion are entering an industry in genuine transition, where some roles are changing, others are expanding, and entirely new career paths are emerging.
Vogue College of Fashion graduates are building careers across editorial, styling, marketing, management, entrepreneurship and beyond, with some going on to establish brands and agencies of their own. What connects these paths is the ability to bring together industry knowledge with curiosity, adaptability and a clear creative vision.
