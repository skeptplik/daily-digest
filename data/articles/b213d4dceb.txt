On Thursday afternoon, Queen Letizia of Spain made a trip to the Centro Danza Matadero in Madrid to learn about an artistic residency program and show her support for the performing arts. While touring the facilities, the queen sported a chic business casual ensemble complete with a pair of derby loafers from Massimo Dutti, a Spanish brand the queen is frequently seen sporting during public appearances.
With her hair worn down and set in relaxed waves, Queen Letizia wore a blazer with classic lapels and a relaxed silhouette over a crisp button-down white shirt. She paired the look with wide-legged black dress trousers, wearing minimal makeup for the event including a nude lipstick. Her choice of footwear added a little flair to Queen Letizia’s otherwise understated elegance. On her feet, she wore polished black leather Massimo Dutti round-toe derby loafers with finely cut kilty fringe falling over the vamp. The shoes had a block heel and slightly oversized rectangular buckle secured near the outer ankle.
Queen Letizia has a well-documented fondness for the Massimo Dutti brand, often wearing their attire during high-profile appearances. In a 2025 public appearance in Madrid, for example, the queen wore an outfit from Massimo Dutti: a cream-colored sleeveless turtleneck dress with textured fabric and cinched at the waist with a slim black belt. In 2024, Queen Letizia attended a tribute for Fernando Gómez-Acebo, her husband’s first cousin, wearing a pair of Massimo Dutti black leather slingback pumps.

Centro Danza Matadero is a cultural center focused on dance and the moving arts that tries to foster a strong artistic community and encourage creative networking. Two of the center’s directors, choreographer María Pagés and poet and playwright Arbi El Harti, showed the queen around the facilities this past Thursday. She watched multiple stage productions that day, including a rehearsal for a dance production titled “Dónde no hay nada (Architecture of Absence),” and also attended creative workshops and toured backstage areas.
