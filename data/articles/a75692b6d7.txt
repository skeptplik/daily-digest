David Warner accuses journalists of 'perpetuating speculation' as Sandpapergate rumbles on
In short:
David Warner says there is no evidence that he ball tampered before Cape Town, contradicting a report from veteran journalist Gerard Whateley.
Warner said Whateley was "a long-time hater" of his.
Warner said there needed to be a differentiation between "journalism and bullying".
