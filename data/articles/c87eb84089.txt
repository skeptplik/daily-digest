APEC summit: Trump seeks nuclear arms cuts, but few concrete results expected
Issued on:
Share
Donald Trump has arrived at the APEC summit for talks with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin, but expectations for concrete results are low. Nothing substantial is likely to come out of the meeting, as was the case in his previous encounters with the two leaders, says Fraser Jackson, FRANCE 24’s correspondent in Washington. The main issue on the table is nuclear disarmament, with Trump seeking to get the United States and China to reduce their stockpiles. The wars in Iran and Ukraine are also expected to feature in the discussions.
