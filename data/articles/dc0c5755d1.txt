Japan’s government has turned to an unlikely pair of storytellers to explain why the country needs stronger defenses: a cartoon comedy couple and a YouTuber known for videos about urban legends and conspiracy theories.
On Nov. 16, the Cabinet Office’s public relations website launched a feature aimed at people in their 20s and 30s. Titled “This Is Not an Urban Legend,” it features Naokiman, a popular YouTuber, and previews a drama about a world in which food and energy disappear.
It was not the government’s first attempt to explain national security through entertainment. Last month, the same website introduced Kunio and Tamiyo, a cartoon manzai comedy couple whose names play on the Japanese words for “nation” and “people.” Created with entertainment giant Yoshimoto Kogyo, the feature is aimed mainly at women ages 40 and older.
