Singapore adds lustre to bullion hub with gold vault service for central banks
The move comes as the city state seeks to capitalise on growing demand for gold trading and storage services in the region
The Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) said the facility, housed in the city state’s national currency vault, could store 500 tonnes of gold but could expand that as demand grows.
The service will provide foreign central banks, international financial institutions and sovereign entities “with a secure option to hold gold reserves in Singapore”, the MAS said in a statement.
