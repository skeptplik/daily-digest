The Empire State Building is seen illuminated in gold to celebrate NASA’s Voyager 1 spacecraft as it was about to pass one light-day away from Earth, a distance reached by no other human-made object, Nov. 17, 2026. The color gold represents the gold-plated copper phonograph record that is flying on Voyager; the Golden Record contains sounds and images selected to portray the diversity of life and culture on Earth.
