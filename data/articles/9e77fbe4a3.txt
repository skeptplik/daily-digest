India and Egypt have agreed to establish a joint working group to explore the Terms of Reference (ToR) for a Preferential Trade Agreement or Free Trade Agreement between the two countries, the government announced on Monday (November 16, 2026).
During his ongoing visit to Egypt, Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal co-chaired, along with Egypt’s Minister of Investment and Foreign Trade Mohamed Farid Saleh, the 7th session of the India-Egypt Joint Trade Committee (JTC) in Egypt’s New Administrative Capital.
This was the first time this JTC has been convened at the ministerial level.
“As a key outcome of the 7th Joint Trade Committee meeting, both Ministers agreed to establish a Joint Working Group to explore the Terms of Reference for a Preferential Trade Agreement or Free Trade Agreement to facilitate stronger bilateral trade, improve access to both markets and promote investment linkages between the two countries,” the government said in a release.
The release also said that both sides reaffirmed their commitment to expanding bilateral trade, with a target of $12 billion.
“Egypt’s proximity to the Suez Canal and its connectivity with African, Arab and European markets make it a valuable partner for export-oriented manufacturing and regional supply chains,” the release said.
“India, in turn, offers Egyptian investors and exporters a large and dynamic market, increasingly competitive infrastructure and opportunities for technology and production partnerships,” it added.
The committee also reviewed the growth in India-Egypt trade, which increased by 35% to $6.5 billion in 2025-26. Trade during the first half of the current financial year (April-September 2026) also stood at a little more than $5 billion, up 65% over its level in the same period of 2025.
