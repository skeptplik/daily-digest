Drone strikes hit homes, United Nations building in Sudan's capital

Drone strikes damaged residential homes and a United Nations building in Sudan's capital Khartoum early Sunday, with the army accusing neighbouring Ethiopia of launching the attacks. The strikes came as drone warfare intensifies in the country's conflict, now in its fourth year.
