Internet provider Xfinity has reported a massive spike in traffic on Xbox consoles for GTA 6's New Zealand launch.
Xfinity says it saw a jump in Xbox hardware traffic four times more than normal around the time GTA 6 became available in New Zealand. Although Xfinity provides its services in the United States, Xbox users were able to take advantage of a loophole that let them change their console's region to New Zealand, allowing them start playing the game early despite their actual geographic location. It's one of the only real notable advantages Xbox has had over PlayStation with this game, as Rockstar secured a marketing deal to advertise GTA 6 as playing best on PS5.
However, these hours of early access have proven to be very lucrative for Xbox players. As a result, tons of footage for the game has been circulating on social media all day long. Major content creators also took advantage of the New Zealand trick, resulting in record-breaking viewership across platforms like Twitch. As of this afternoon, Twitch reported that tens of millions of watch hours had already been accumulated.
Right now, we still don't know how many copies of GTA 6 have sold, but we'll likely hear something by the end of this week or very early next week, depending on how quickly Rockstar wants to boast. This is expected to be the biggest entertainment launch of all-time, after all, and the official numbers will likely be eyewatering.
From a critical perspective, GTA 6 has received nothing but praise. IGN gave the game a 10 for its impeccable attention to detail and unprecedented depth, but we weren't alone in that analysis. The game is currently tied with The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time for the highest rated game on Metacritic. It's also nominated for Game of the Year, in addition to seven other awards at The Game Awards.
