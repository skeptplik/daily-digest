Slippers are one of those classic Christmas gifts pretty much everyone has received at some point in their lives. They are one of those practical items that everyone is going to use at some point, but rarely do people actually get excited when they unwrap a new pair of slippers. Sure, there are plenty of themed options out there that feel fun, but where do you even put those when you aren't using them?
Well, if it's a pair of Piranha Plant slippers, that problem has been solved for you. Just place them back in their Pipe holder and your footwear is instantly transformed into a little Piranha Plant. Suddenly you've got a fun piece of Super Mario-themed decor that brightens up the space.
The Piranha Plant slippers are getting discounted as part of Amazon's Black Friday sale, dropping the price down to about $20. The deal is only available for Amazon Prime Members, but the typical price is still pretty cheap as far as Nintendo-themed gifts go. They are sold by a third-party seller, but the Amazon reviews are overall quite positive.
The main thing to note before you buy these slippers is that there is only one size available. It's a bit hidden in the product listing, but the page states that they are designed to fit for women with a shoe size ranging from 6-9 or men's shoe size beween 5-8 in the US. So if you're someone with bigger feet, you're probably going to have trouble actually wearing these things around the house.
And while adults can certainly wear these, they seem like an ideal gift for kids. If they had these in my size I would absolutely grab a pair at this price. I would have loved getting these as a kid too, but I don't think these even existed when my feet were still that small. Still, I'm definitely considering picking up a pair for my nephew who has fairly recently discovered Mario games for the first time.
