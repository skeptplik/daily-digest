The 2026 Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix has officially kicked off—and Kim Kardashian brought all her Ferrari reds for support. Cheering on her boyfriend, Lewis Hamilton, who drives for the Italian racing team, the reality star arrived at the Las Vegas Strip Circuit on Friday, November 20 in a vintage, on-theme leather look.
The Skims founder built her WAG ensemble around a pair of bold lace-up red trousers from Dior’s John Galliano era. Pulled from the designer’s Fall 2003 collection for the heritage house, the glossy bottoms featured a mid-rise silhouette and skintight fit, aided by corset-like lacing all down the front of each leg. At the ankle, Kardashian extended the look of the pants with a pair of matching gladiator sandals, designed with a high heel.
As for the rest of her look, she wore a white tank from her own label and bundled up with a black leather trench. The outerwear featured sharp shoulders with epaulettes, wide lapels with pointed edges, and belted cuffs. It was tied at the waist with a matching belt, which cinched the leather to create an hourglass shape. Another pop of red peeked out at the neckline, sandwiched between her Skims top and black coat.
Her long hair was blown out with a deep side part, and she finished things off with a pair of black sunglasses.
Kardashian first sparked relationship rumors with Hamilton in early 2026, after over a decade of friendship. Since then, the All’s Fair star has supported her boyfriend at several F1 races, flying to everywhere from Monaco to Singapore and now to Vegas.
