North Korea on Wednesday (November 25, 2026) declared a senior Swiss diplomat heading the country's newly opened diplomatic office in Pyongyang persona non grata and ordered him to leave the country within 48 hours.
The move came a day after Kim Yo Jong, the powerful sister of North Korean leader Kim Jong Un, threatened to close the office. She condemned Switzerland for imposing sanctions on a youth camp in eastern North Korea over its alleged role in the transfer of children from Russia-occupied areas of Ukraine.
The Korean Central News Agency said a senior Foreign Ministry official summoned Astier Sylvain, who heads the Swiss diplomatic office that opened in Pyongyang last month, and handed him a notice demanding the office's closure and his departure, in protest of the Swiss sanctions on the Songdowon International Children's Camp in Wonsan.
