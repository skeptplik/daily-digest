Four Aussies chasing biggest payday in women's golf history at LPGA Tour Championship
In short:
There are four Australians playing in the LPGA Tour Championship: Hannah Green, Grace Kim, Cassie Porter and Karis Davidson.
The winner of the tournament will earn $US4 million ($5.96 million).
What's next?
The LPGA Tour Championship will be played at the Tiburón Golf Club in Naples, Florida, from November 19 to 22.
