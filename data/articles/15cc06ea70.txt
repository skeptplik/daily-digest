Like many major brands, Xbox is finding all sorts of ways to associate with the biggest game launch of the year: Grand Theft Auto 6. Unlike indie publisher Devolver Digital, however, Xbox has cleverly avoided ever mentioning GTA 6 directly in its marketing campaigns for GTA 6.
As pointed out by multiple people on social media, the Xbox Wire post titled 'How to Get Ready for November 19' is one all about celebrating the big release day of GTA 6, and helping players find ways to maximise their time with their Xbox consoles. Despite that, it does not mention GTA 6 by name a single time.
"Birthdays, anniversaries, and holidays have come and gone, but this day belongs to you and your Xbox console," the post reads, stopping short of saying why the day is important. "We can hardly believe it's almost here, either."
One day prior, Xbox Wire partnered with DJ Khaled to create custom doctor's notes for players to submit to their employers to take the day off. Why would you want to take 19th November off? Because you're apparently having "a serious case of Vitamin G deficiency".
Going even further back, Xbox revealed a custom-made Series X console it called X-Bite, which is a large, sculpted alligator that swallowed a Series X console. Why? Because "this November, Xbox is heading to Florida - home to incredible wildlife, unforgettable places, and every now and then, stories you have to see to believe."
Florida, of course, is the real-world US state GTA 6's Leonida is based on, but in keeping with the theme of not referring to the game - or its made-up state - directly, the blog does not.
Now, Xbox has directly named GTA 6 in places, but only when, seemingly, the post is about factual information. This Xbox Wire post reiterates GTA 6's Xbox Cloud streaming availability, and this one is a look-ahead at games launching the following week on Xbox. But that's about the extent of what you can expect.
So what's going on here? The simple guess is that PlayStation has a co-marketing deal with Rockstar, meaning no other console platform gets to use GTA 6's name in marketing. Sony is allowed to make custom, GTA 6-themed consoles, branded, limited-edition DualSense controllers and blaster the game all over the PS Blog, social media, and even change the PS App's icon colour to match the hue of GTA 6's.
This is all too similar to when, each year, various brands encourage you to buy their products to be ready for The Big Game, which is very distinct from the Super Bowl happening on the same day. Like Xbox, those brands simply don't have the rights to the real thing.
GTA 6 is available now on PS5 and Xbox Series X/S. In Eurogamer's five-star review, Chris Tapsell wrote, "Rockstar pushes back the horizon of video games once again with GTA 6. An irreducible mix of silliness, intimacy, grand ideas and rock 'n' roll action - and a load more after that."
