Disgraced lawmaker pleads guilty to 4 traffic offences over drink-driving crash
Court delays William Wong’s sentencing to November 26 for background and community service reports
Disgraced former lawmaker William Wong Kam-fai will be sentenced later this month after pleading guilty to four traffic offences stemming from a hit-and-run incident while driving under the influence of alcohol earlier this year.
Magistrate Raymond Wong Kwok-fai adjourned sentencing to November 26 on Wednesday, citing the need to obtain Wong’s background report and a community service order assessment.
The former lawmaker, who resigned from the Legislative Council in July days after news of the incident broke, appeared at Sha Tin Magistrates’ Court to enter his plea.
