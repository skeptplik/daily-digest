Dogs and mailmen are natural enemies that have been locked in battle for eons. The dogs view the mailmen as unwelcome invaders of their territory. Meanwhile, the mailmen view the dogs as vicious beasts preventing them from upholding their noble postal worker’s oath. It’s nearly as old as the struggle between good and evil — and just as epic.
Back when cartoonist Gary Larson was publishing his comic strip The Far Side, he regularly depicted this conflict, usually portraying the dogs as the victors and mailmen as the victims. I’m not sure of Larson’s motivations for revisiting this topic over and over again, but I like to think he was trying to resolve the matter for the good of both parties. Sadly, while Larson concluded The Far Side in 1995, the war still rages on.
Here are 10 Far Side comics depicting dogs and mailmen in their everlasting rivalry.
10 Nov. 19, 1986
In this comic from 1986, a mailman is ravaged by a pack of wiener dogs. They don’t seem to be doing too much harm to the mailman, who seems only mildly inconvenienced by the fact that no fewer than seven dachshunds have latched onto him. His wife also seems unfazed by this development, as if it has happened many times before.
9 Apr. 24, 1986
In this comic strip, Larson is parodying Sylvester Stallone's Rambo character with a gun-toting dog named Rexbo, who has blown away a mailman with a bazooka. By 1986, Rambo was already the star of two movies, 1982’s First Blood and 1985’s terribly titled Rambo: First Blood Part II. Stallone would later star in three more Rambo movies, though he has yet to make a film about Rexbo.
It’s not too late, Sly.
8 June 6, 1984
Here, Larson offers insight into the mind of a dog, who dreams of a thick-legged mailman whom he is about to maul.
7 April 29, 1986
Dogs are masters of disguise. At least they are in this comic strip, which depicts an entire pack of disguised canines infiltrating their enemy’s stronghold, the post office. The mailmen here don’t stand a chance.
6 Dec. 29, 1984
In this comic, a couple of prankster dogs are about to torment their friend with a fake mailman. They just can’t wait to see him get yanked back by his leash, which is tied to a tree.
5 July 6, 1985
Do mailmen use mace to fend off dogs? Is this a real thing? If so, it feels like it gives the mailmen an unfair advantage in any dog vs. mailmen matchup. Play fair, mailmen. This isn’t cool.
4 Feb. 24, 1994
This comic strip features a couple of paleontologists discovering the perfectly preserved skeletons of an ancient dog and mailman, with the dog biting the mailman’s leg. It perfectly captures how old this conflict is.
Oddly enough, according to Google Translate, “Canis nipponicus” translates to “Japanese dog,” which I am guessing was unintentional on Larson’s part.
3 June 8, 1986
Larson depicted Hell in many different ways during the 15 years he published The Far Side. For a classical conductor, he depicted Hell as a room full of banjo players. In a comic strip with the caption “Hell’s Video Store,” he showed a video rental store filled only with copies of the 1987 box office flop Ishtar. For dogs, he depicted Hell as a realm where they must pick up their own poop and serve as mailmen, which is an especially cruel fate.
2 July 15, 1986
Besides mailmen, the other biggest enemy of dogs are cats. In this comic strip, Larson depicts two dogs who are so old that they cannot muster the strength to pursue a cat who is employed as a mailman.
1 Aug. 17, 1988
And here is my absolute favorite Far Side comic strip about the rivalry between mailmen and dogs, which features a woman scolding her dog for using a voodoo doll on the mailman. I especially love how sad and broken the mailman is here.
