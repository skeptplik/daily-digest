Part of what makes Lola Blankets so enticing is its understanding of aesthetics. Those who subscribe to a minimalist decorating philosophy will find plenty to love among the brand’s ivory, cream, and chocolate-brown offerings, while maximalists can turn to tiger stripes, bandana prints, and colorful ombré designs.
