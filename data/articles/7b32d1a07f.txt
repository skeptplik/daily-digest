It’s an established social etiquette rule that showing up to a party empty-handed is a bit of a faux pas. Of course, there are more intimate gatherings where a simple bottle of wine or an extra cheese for the board will suffice, but the holiday season runs on a different track. Homes are dressed to the nines, menus are calibrated to merrier flavors, and the stakes somehow feel higher. Arriving with a gift for the hostess not only signals your appreciation, but also adds to the evening’s celebratory air. But what to bring?
There’s zero shame in going the traditional route—no one is going to turn their nose up at a Le Labo candle, after all. If your host is a hardcore holiday enthusiast, why not bring a handcrafted ornament to dress their tree with? Or a fresh stack of illustrated Christmas cards? Something pretty for the pantry will always go to great use, and if you have a strong sense of their taste in interiors, a sleek vase or decorative tray will surely make your host feel extra special.
This year, rather than scrambling to secure the best hostess gifts in the 11th hour, save yourself the stress and stock up ahead of time. Below, shop over 75 picks (starting at just $12) to bring along to all your upcoming holiday fêtes.
For Setting a Stylish Table
For the serial hosts who regularly throw dinner parties, gift them a beautiful tablecloth for the season, or an artisanal set of napkin rings. If you’re hoping they might use your gift the same night of the party, Gohar’s clever crumb catcher could easily be placed on the table for pre-dessert tidying.
For the Powder Room Upgrade
This gift falls squarely into the category of something the host would probably never buy for themselves, but would love to receive. An elegant soap—bar or liquid—or hand lotion transforms their powder room into a mini spa moment, and will make them think of you each time they visit the sink.
For Sleek Snacking
Rather than pouring snacks into an ordinary bowl or repurposing a mug, these sleek snacking vessels will make your host proud to display their assorted nuts and olives. And because they’re crafted in versatile glass, ceramic, and steel, they can be used all throughout the year.
For the Holiday Enthusiast
For hosts who have been known to deck the halls aplenty, get into the spirit of the season with a festive gift. If they celebrate Christmas, an ornament is a winning choice. You could also give them a fresh, handmade wreath to bring their door to life.
For a Pretty Perishable
One of the safest gifts you can bring to a party is food (just check for allergies). Perhaps a tin of artisanal hot cocoa to keep your host cozy throughout the coldest months? Or a limited-run panettone for the dessert-obsessed? If it’s an especially momentous celebration, Flamingo Estate’s manchego cheese wheel is downright impressive.
For the Queen of Cozy
If your host is all about setting the vibes—dim lights, flickering candles, burning incense—bring them a gift that complements their home’s curation of cozy accoutrements.
For the Cooks in the Kitchen
They can whip up a beef Wellington with great finesse and waltz around with a tea towel permanently attached to their shoulder—the hosts who can cook are simply the best. Bring them a gift that befits the kitchen, like a solid-wood cutting board, or something more playful, like a holiday apron.
For the Memories
For a sentimental gift, bring something to help capture memories. A picture frame is always a thoughtful gesture. Or, if you want to give your host something they can use right away, give them an instant camera that can be passed around the party throughout the evening.
For the End-of-Meal Caffeine
For the caffeine addicts in your life, a gift centered around coffee or tea will be well appreciated. If your host keeps saying they want to get into loose-leaf tea, this discovery set—accompanied by easy-to-use filter bags—is an excellent idea. And if coffee is their poison, there’s no better vessel to sip it from than these patterned mocha cups.
For the Discerning Tabletop
For the host who treats their home interiors as an ever-evolving project, a decorative tabletop object is just the ticket. If a candle is always lit in their home, consider a refined tabletop lighter. And if they have a proclivity for the finer things in life, a porcelain tray from Ginori 1735 is a flawless pick.
For the Game Obsessed
Whether they’ve beaten you in too many games of mahjong to count, or they simply love the conviviality of friendly competition, a well-designed game makes for a lovely hostess gift.
For the Elevated Bar Cart
Help your host whip up their signature cocktail with a bit more flair. Whether it’s a sleek shaker (best for those who don’t already own one) or a stunning wine cooler to keep bottles on the table colder for longer, these picks go beyond the basics.
For Post-Party Recovery
After days of tedious prep and a full evening hosting guests, they’re bound to wake up feeling a bit tattered. Help your host ease into the morning with a few wellness gifts, like a luxury face mask and a beautiful Murano glass to encourage hydration.
