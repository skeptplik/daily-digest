Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi arrived Monday in Manila for four days of international summitry in the Philippines and China, where a key focus will be whether she can add some warmth to chilly ties with Beijing.
Just ahead of her departure, Takaichi told reporters that strengthening the Japan-led “Free and Open Indo-Pacific Strategy” would be a crucial element of her trip.
“As the international community faces historic changes, I intend to convey Japan’s efforts to maintain and strengthen a free and open international order based on the rule of law, and to confirm cooperation with other nations toward that end,” she said.
