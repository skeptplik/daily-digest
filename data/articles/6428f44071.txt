The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake is out now, and so we have confirmation of the full English voice cast for the first time.
As fans had suspected following the release of official trailers for the game, Matthew Mercer from the Dungeons & Dragons web series Critical Role, reprises his role as Ganondorf from The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom. Prolific voice actor Erica Mendez plays Zelda, and Navi is voiced by Lisa Reimold.
While the original Ocarina of Time, released in 1998 on the N64, did not feature any voiced dialogue, the remake features voice acting in its cutscenes. Link, of course, remains silent throughout — as he does in all the Zelda games. The full list, which IGN has verified via the credits, is below.
The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake full English voice cast:
- Navi: Lisa Reimold
- Zelda / Sheik: Erica Mendez
- Ganondorf: Matt Mercer
- Great Deku Tree: Richard Epcar
- Saria: Suzie Yeung
- Kaepora Gaebora / Rauru: Frank Todaro
- Impa: Laura Post
- Darunia: Josh Petersdorf
- Ruto: Anne Yatco
- Deku Tree Sprout: Deva Marie Gregory
- Nabooru: Cristina Vee
- Koume / Kotake / Twinrova: Reba Buhr
- Voice support: Daniel Bushey, Rachel Seeley, Danny Hunt, Haley Hanna Hunt
