Ticket seller Fandango Media is notifying customers about a data breach that exposed payment card details entered on its checkout pages.
The company says the details were exposed between October 8 and 11, and it impacts customers who made purchases through its official website.
Card skimmer code injected into Fandango’s checkout page intercepted payment card details entered when placing orders, sending the data to hackers.
Fandango Media is a major U.S. online movie ticketing platform. It sells tickets through its website and app for 31,000 U.S. cinema screens, including at AMC, Regal, and Cinemark.
The company also operates the popular movie review website Rotten Tomatoes.
“Our investigation identified evidence that an unauthorized party could access payment card information associated with certain transactions on our checkout page from October 8-11, 2026,” reads the notification to customers.
“Fandango determined on October 30, 2026 that your information may have been impacted in this incident.”
The company says the following data types may have been exposed:
- Payment card number
- CVV (verification code)
- Expiration date
- Billing ZIP (postal code)
- Email address
Fandango says the incident did not expose names, government-issued identity information, Social Security Numbers (SSNs), or any other account information.
However, the information compromised in the incident could be used for unauthorized purchases on online stores.
To reduce the risk to affected customers, Fandango is offering two years of free IDX credit and dark web monitoring, as well as identity theft recovery services, with an enrollment deadline of February 13, 2027.
Fandango says it has now fixed the issue on its checkout page, investigated with outside cybersecurity specialists, and is currently unaware of any cases of fraud leveraging the stolen data.
The company has not shared the number of affected customers. BleepingComputer has contacted Fandango to learn more, and we will update this post with their response once received.
Users who bought movie tickets from Fandango during the exposure window should monitor their statements and account activity closely, and report any unauthorized purchases to their card issuer.
One way to protect payment card details is to use one-time virtual cards when making online purchases.
