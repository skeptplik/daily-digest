NRL scraps All Stars game for 2027 preseason citing player burnout concerns
In short:
The NRL will not play the All Stars match during the 2027 pre-season.
Burnout concerns following the 2026 World Cup mean the game will not kick off the year.
What's next?
NRL chief executive Peter V'landys says the game could be played later in the year.
