Cricket Australia insists it is not taking sides in sandpaper spat despite Pat Cummins backing
In short:
Cricket Australia insists its backing of Pat Cummins to remain Australia's cricket captain does not mean it does not believe the accounts of other players.
CA boss Todd Greenberg said: "I am not putting someone's version of events over somebody else's".
What's next?
Cummins is expected to train again at the SCG this week ahead of next month's Test series against New Zealand.
