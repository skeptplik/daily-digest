Russian strikes on Kyiv kill one, damage Dutch and Austrian embassies
Issued on: Modified:
Share
A wave of daytime Russian strikes on Kyiv on Thursday killed one person and damaged a historic cathedral as well as the embassies of Austria and the Netherlands. Loud explosions erupted across the Ukraine capital throughout the day as the leaders of Russia, China and the United States held a historic meeting in China that put Russia's President Vladimir Putin in international spotlight. FRANCE 24's Laurent Berstecher reports.
