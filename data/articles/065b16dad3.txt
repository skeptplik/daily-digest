Mamdani questions AI economy as Jensen Huang returns to Nvidia – video
New York City mayor Zohran Mamdani weighed in on the corporate turbulence surrounding Nvidia and the chipmaker’s chief executive officer, Jensen Huang, saying he does not think working Americans 'signed up for their pensions being tied to the question of Huang’s fidelity to his marriage'. The mayor said Huang 'had a massive impact', with Nvidia’s share price dropping dramatically after he stepped down as the company’s CEO on 12 November over a reported extramarital relationship with a suspected Chinese spy
