France awaits verdict in landmark police brutality trial over Michel Zecler assault
Issued on:
Share
A French prosecutor on Thursday urged a court to jail three policemen who beat up a black music producer to four to six years, in a rare case of alleged police brutality to go to criminal court. The assault on Michel Zecler, now 47, triggered renewed outrage in France over police violence, which victims and researchers say is systemic but authorities and law enforcement unions claim is limited to isolated cases. The prosecutor argued the case had caused "immense harm to Michel Zecler but also to society as a whole," and that those on trial had "betrayed" their uniform.
