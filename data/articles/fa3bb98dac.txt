When it comes to her style, Julia Fox has never been afraid to make a statement. Whether turning belts into a bra and miniskirt, tucking her hair into a “Velveeta gold” wig, or putting a futuristic twist on naked dressing, the actor has pushed the boundaries of red-carpet dressing and street style alike. Just this month, she showed up to the 2026 CFDA Fashion Awards in a tulle Miss Claire Sullivan dress that was made using world maps.
For her latest look, Fox turned her attention to designer Sofia Testino, piecing together a preppy purple fit full of the label’s pieces. On top, she wore the Air Hostess Polo, a fitted short-sleeve ivory top with a purple collar and stripe of green. It featured an elongated button placket and a rounded deep V neckline trimmed in purple and green.
The Down the Drain memoir author wore the polo with the coordinating Air Hostess Arrow Skirt—a purple leather midi pencil skirt with arrow motifs across the front—and a reversible jacket. The Work Woman Jacket, as it is called, was crafted with a structured silhouette and padded shoulders. One side was cut from a cream checked wool, while the inverse was made from pale-blue satin with contrast checked panels. The whole thing was finished with brown ribbed cuffs.
Fox added semi-sheer black tights and periwinkle patent-leather wedges, also from Sofia Testino. And for a bag, she brought her go-to geometric black-and-white bowling bag from the Prada Spring/Summer 2000 collection.
