Ayo Edebiri has proven herself to be quite the chameleon on the red carpet. From her cream tank that morphed into a feathery bustle at the BFI London Film Festival last month, to her draping white gown at this year’s Met Gala, Edebiri can pull off almost any look with ease. However, she appears to be following in the footsteps of fashion forebears like Julia Roberts and Diane Keaton, thanks to her particular affinity for suits.
In the past, Edebiri has turned sharp suited looks such as her icy-blue capri set at the Los Angeles premiere of Inside Out 2, and her gray Loewe ensemble at the 2025 Golden Globes, which was notably punctuated with a golden, feather-shaped tie. And it appears she’s still digging through the menswear section—and the results are just as impressive.
On Thursday night in Los Angeles, The Bear star attended the 2026 GQ Men of the Year ceremony, where she wore an eye-popping ensemble pulled from the Conner Ives Spring/Summer 2027 ready-to-wear collection (a choice that, perhaps, served as a nod to the celebrated gentlemen of the evening).
The look consisted of a fitted black blazer and pants, both stitched with a subtle gray pinstripe for a slightly ’80s touch.
Similar to how it debuted on the runway, stylist Danielle Goldberg layered the suit over a seafoam-green dress shirt and a burgundy patterned tie, both visible thanks to the open blazer.
But while the original catwalk fit saw the tie tucked into the pants and cinched under a cummerbund, here, Edebiri went sans the waist accessory, while the necktie hung free.
She accessorized the fit with shiny black loafers and diamond earrings, while her hair was styled in a curly, slicked bob.
Needless to say, this suited realm is clearly where Edebiri thrives.
