Members of the opposition Congress party protested outside Meta’s offices in Delhi on Friday and appear to have entered the building in a video shared by CNN. Members of the party’s youth wing are demanding the company restore accounts of party leaders and warned of more protests to come. The Congress party has criticized Meta for censoring speech that Prime Minister Narendra Modi and his BJP party disagree with, and has placed banners accusing Mark Zuckerberg of favoring BJP around the city.
Protesters storm Meta offices in India
Members of the youth wing of the Congress party are demanding its leaders’ Instagram accounts be reinstated.
While two accounts in question were restored Friday, Manu Jain, who oversees social media for the party’s youth wing, told Reuters that “thousands of accounts of journalists, creators and influencers critical of the government remain restricted.”
In February, India issued tighter rules around the removal of “unlawful” content, changing the timeline from 36 hours to just three. Modi and the Indian government have long been criticized for their restrictive policies around free speech and accused of stifling opposition viewpoints and attempting to bully the press.
Meta did not immediately return a request for comment.
