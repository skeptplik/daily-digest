Fans are expecting a fresh Avengers: Doomsday trailer today, and now have a good idea of the movie's runtime.
With Marvel's highly-anticipated blockbuster less than a month away, viewers are awaiting a chunky new look at the film after what feels like a long wait. Happily, however, that wait seems to be at its end — according to Peggy Carter actress Hayley Atwell.
"There's a new trailer dropping on Monday... am I allowed to have said that?" Atwell told fans in New York over the weekend while signing autographs, according to Deadline. (We're guessing that no, she probably wasn't allowed to say that.)
If Atwell's intel is correct, this would be our first substantial look at Doomsday in months. Marvel began its rollout of Doomsday teasers last December, when it started launching a series of teasers focused on Steve Rogers, Thor, the X-Men and then the Wakandans plus Fantastic Four. A proper trailer then followed in July, showing our first look at Robert Downey Jr. in character as Doctor Doom. Now, with the movie imminent, fans want more.
Over the past year, Marvel has dropped a few more hints and tidbits, and in the past few weeks fans have got confirmation that Elizabeth Olsen is back in the MCU as her fan-favorite character Scarlet Witch, while Kate Bishop actress Hailee Steinfeld has just confirmed her own Doomsday return. (Fans also strongly suspect Ms. Marvel actress Iman Vellani is on the cusp of her own Doomsday confirmation, since she appears to be sitting on the same kind of Doomsday set chair for recent episodes of her official Marvel podcast.)
This year's other MCU releases also featured fresh story beats that connected with Doomsday, with the end of Spider-Man: Brand New Day teasing a Spidey in space, while Avengers: Endgame Encore included looks at what Steve Rogers, the Hulk, and the TVA where up to as Doctor Doom arrives.
"We’ve done a series of tags around the characters because the story actually started [with the Steve Rogers teaser]," Doomsday co-director Joe Russo said during a chat with the Qatar Film Committee. "And you’ve been watching it slowly unfold and these are all pieces of something that are going to equal two and a half hours of your time on December 18," he continued, apparently confirming the movie will run to around 150 minutes.
This would make the film the same length as 2018's similar Avengers movie Infinity War, and shorter than Eternals, Black Panther: Wakanda Forever, and the three-hour Avengers: Endgame.
Here's hoping Atwell is correct and we're just hours away from seeing more of what those 150 minutes will contain. Either way, Avengers: Doomsday launches on December 18.
