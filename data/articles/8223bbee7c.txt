Finding a short hairstyle that is both modern and timeless isn't easy, but Dame Helen Mirren has mastered it. Her softly swept bixie, complete with a side fringe, is a particularly elegant example.
The actor debuted her asymmetric cut, coined the “split” bixie, at the London premiere of her upcoming film, A Talent for Murder. Defined by its pixie-like proportions and bob-length, face-framing front, the bixie is gaining ground as one of this year's most popular haircuts.
"A bixie has all the benefits of a short style, but because the front is kept longer and softer, it has none of the severity of other short haircuts," Sasha Leslie, senior hairstylist at Nicola Clarke, previously told Harper’s Bazaar U.K.. The cut also adds versatility, as the longer front sections can be swept to the side, worn loose around the face, or styled back for a more polished finish.
For the occasion, Mirren opted for a split bixie cut, a more directional version of the classic style, with a deep side parting and a sweeping fringe that frames the face. Of course, age shouldn't dictate your haircut, but for those with mature hair looking for a sophisticated, easy-to-style length, the split bixie makes a compelling case.
If you're tempted to follow suit, consider a few details before your next salon appointment. As Leslie notes, the shorter length does require regular trims to maintain its shape. However, if you're too busy to schedule frequent appointments, she recommends keeping the bixie slightly longer, closer to a bob, so it grows out seamlessly. For thicker hair, she suggests asking your hairstylist to remove excess bulk while keeping length; thinner textures benefit from a more graduated silhouette to create the illusion of extra volume.
When it comes to styling, the split bixie's key feature is its side-swept part. It adds softness and movement to the shorter cut and is also a clever trick to achieve a natural contour. "Wearing a sleek side parting is a great way for one's hairstyle to contribute to a sharper, more contoured appearance, by adding more angles and height to the face," trend forecaster and hairstylist Tom Smith previously told Harper’s Bazaar U.K.. "Most people will suit a side parting more on one side than the other, so it's a good idea to try out a side parting on either side of your face to see which is more flattering for you."
Above all, Mirren's look reminds us that great hair has no age limit. With its flattering shape, soft movement, and effortless appeal, the bixie makes a strong case for embracing shorter lengths at any stage of life.
