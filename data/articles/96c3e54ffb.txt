A school has received recognition for helping support the mental health of pupils.
Blanchelande College in St Andrews, Guernsey, won the Mental Health and Wellbeing Award at the 2026 Independent Schools Association Awards earlier this week.
The independent Catholic school for boys and girls was recognised for its Emmaus Space, a wellbeing room that opened in September 2025.
It was also commended for a drop in school refusal, a rise in attendance and providing a comprehensive network of care.
School leaders said the award reflected their belief in education that develops pupils academically, socially, emotionally and spiritually.
Vice principal Dr Vanessa Mitchell said: "We are incredibly proud to receive this award.
"It recognises our whole college community's commitment to ensuring every child feels seen, heard, understood and supported."
