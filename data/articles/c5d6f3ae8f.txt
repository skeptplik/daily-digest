We’ve been in a bit of a fashion drought ever since Fashion Month ended over a month ago. Thankfully, the 2026 CFDA Fashion Awards arrived just in time to quench our sartorial thirst.
The annual event regularly delivers some of the year’s most exciting red-carpet looks, and Monday night’s step-and-repeat didn’t disappoint: Dozens of stars showed up at the American Museum of Natural History in New York City dressed in the latest collections of the buzziest American designers.
Little black dresses were popular, like Gracie Abrams’ gown by Emerging Designer of the Year Colleen Allen, Pamela Anderson’s Carolina Herrera dress and Aweng Ade-Choul’s Brandon Blackwood ensemble. Others embraced bold colors and more playful silhouettes, such as Julia Fox in a map-print gown by Miss Claire Sullivan and Tinashe in a colorful Christopher John Rogers set.
