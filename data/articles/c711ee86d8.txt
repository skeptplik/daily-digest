At the Game Republic Awards in Wakefield last night, the indie puzzle adventure Rhell: Warped Worlds and Troubled Times beat off competition from the likes of Grand Theft Auto 6, Lego Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight, and Wardogs to take home the prize for Best Game Made in The North.
"This year was our toughest yet with such fantastic nominations and shortlisted nominees, individuals, and studios, and the nominee list of 15 games made in the North is a testament to the growing strength of the network and The North producing incredible games," said Jamie Sefton, MD of Game Republic.
The Best Game Made in The North Award was the only one of the night to be voted for by the public; the winners of the other awards were selected by game writer Rhianna Pratchett, who has previously worked on titles such as Tomb Raider, BioShock Infinite, and Mirror's Edge.
Ste and John Pickford took home the Games Legend Award. The brothers have made more than 70 games, starting on the ZX Spectrum, and worked with Rare developing titles such as Solar Jetman for the NES. Rare bought their studio in 1990, which became Rare Manchester.
They later worked at Software Creations, but would go on to found two more studios: Zed Two, where they created Wetrix, and Zee 3, where they made the strategy game Naked War. The pair currently work at Double Eleven.
The Lifetime Achievement Award was won by Tim Coupe, who has worked on titles including Tiny Toon Adventures, Batman Begins, GoldenEye 007: Reloaded, Dead Space: Extraction, 007: Legends, Crysis, Dirt 4, Harry Potter: Kinect, Project Cars 3, and WRC. Coupe is currently VP of studio at Undisputed developer Steel City Interactive.
GamesIndustry.biz features editor Lewis Packwood presented the Industry Impact Award to publisher Silver Lining Interactive, while the Most Innovative Use of Technology in Games Award went to Sumo Digital and Arm for their work on Neural Dawn, which takes advantage of Arm's neural graphics technology to allow the use of Unreal Engine MegaLights on mobile devices.
The full list of award winners is below:
- Best Game Made in the North Award: Rhell: Warped Worlds and Troubled Times (SlugGlove Games/Yogscast Games)
- Games Legend Award: Ste and John Pickford
- Lifetime Achievement Award: Tim Coupe
- Industry Impact Award: Silver Lining Interactive
- Most Innovative Use of Technology in Games: Sumo Digital/Arm
- Best Small Studio: Miami Avalon
- Best Mid-Sized Studio: Atomhawk
- Best Large Studio: TT Games
- Star Student Award: Zoe Dunn (University of Salford)
- Course Leader Award: Maya Jermy (Leeds Arts University)
- Best Game-Related Research Award: Alex Flint (IGGI, University of York)
- Inclusivity Award: Shiverbug Studios
- Studio Hero Award: Shirley Hoang (Lucid Games)
- Community Manager Award: Wendy Fritscher (Revolution Software)
- Martyn Brown Rising Star Award: Chukwuyenum Opone (Scyte Studios)
