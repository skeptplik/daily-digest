Lauren Sánchez Bezos has gone from television news anchor to talk show host to Bezos bride, and now, she’s making her name in the world of picture books. With the release of her debut children’s story, The Fly Who Flew to Space, back in 2024, Sánchez Bezos became a New York Times bestseller. And today, she released a new holiday-themed sequel and third installment in the series, titled The Fly Who Flew to the North Pole, which she promoted this morning with an appearance in New York City.
It may not be the holidays quite yet, but Sánchez Bezos showed off her unique, femme-fatale approach to yuletide style as she stopped by CBS Mornings to celebrate her book’s release. There, many of her dressing hallmarks were on full display. Luxury vintage? Check. A cinched silhouette? Check. Pops of bloody red? Check and check.
For the talk-show visit, Sánchez Bezos looked like a gothic Mrs. Claus in a black, skintight turtleneck from Wolford and a fuzzy black miniskirt that was cinched along the waist with a red tweed band that simulated a cummerbund.
This skirt wasn’t made by North Pole elves, though. Rather, it stemmed from the Chanel Fall/Winter 1994 ready-to-wear collection by Karl Lagerfeld, acting as the latest addition to Sánchez Bezos’s ever-increasing archival stock.
The two-tone mini also acted as the perfect complement to the former journalist’s inky patent-leather heels from Christian Louboutin, which featured the brand’s signature red bottoms.
To complete her look, Mrs. Bezos popped on a pair of oversize aviator sunglasses and silver, bow-shaped earrings. Plus, she carried a clasp clutch printed with the cover of her newly-released book (a cheeky marketing tactic that she’s employed in the past).
No matter the occasion, Sánchez Bezos will find a way to let her personality shine through.
