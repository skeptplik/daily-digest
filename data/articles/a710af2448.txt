Steam is giving PC strategy fans another opportunity to try a major grand strategy game without spending a cent. This week's promotion is particularly appealing for players who enjoy medieval history, political intrigue, and building a dynasty from the ground up.
Crusader Kings 3 is temporarily free to play on Steam, giving everyone a chance to experience the acclaimed strategy game and see whether they can keep a medieval dynasty alive before committing to a purchase.
The free weekend runs until Monday, Nov. 23, at 10 a.m. PST. Players who enjoy the experience can also purchase the game at a 70% discount, bringing its usual $49.99 price down to $14.99. The sale runs until Nov. 30.
What is Crusader Kings 3 about?
Released in September 2020, Crusader Kings 3 is a grand strategy game that lets players take control of a medieval noble family and guide its dynasty across generations. Rather than focusing exclusively on warfare, the game puts considerable emphasis on diplomacy, relationships, succession, and political maneuvering.
Players can choose from historical starting points, claim territories, arrange marriages, forge alliances, and expand their influence across a sprawling medieval map. Every character has individual traits, ambitions, and relationships, meaning a seemingly harmless family dispute can eventually turn into a succession crisis or an opportunity to seize a neighboring kingdom.
Death also isn't the end of a campaign, as players continue their family's legacy through successive heirs. That combination of emergent storytelling, political scheming, and grand strategy makes every playthrough potentially different.
