Over two million Xbox players in the UK and US accessed Grand Theft Auto 6 before its official launch by changing their location to New Zealand, according to Ampere Analysis.
Specifically, 1.5 million US players and 600,000 in the UK used this method on Xbox. In comparison, 100,000 US players and 25,000 UK players did so on PlayStation 5.
GTA 6 launched globally at midnight local time on November 19, with New Zealand being the first to access the title.
Xbox players in other regions could switch their console location to New Zealand, restart, and start playing the game hours ahead of others on November 18.
This exploit was more difficult on PS5, as an account's country or region is locked after creation. Players needed to create a New Zealand PlayStation account and purchase GTA 6 through the region's PlayStation Store to access the game early.
GTA 6 has unsurprisingly become 2026's best-selling game since its launch, with projected sales exceeding 24.5 million copies and $2.36 billion in pre-order and launch-day revenue.
According to data from Newzoo shared with GamesIndustry.biz, GTA 6's console-only launch could limit its global reach, as previous data indicated consoles would account for only 22% of global games revenue in 2026.
"While GTA 6 is undoubtedly one of the most important releases of the decade, its initial impact will be concentrated in Western consoles," said Newzoo director of market intelligence Emmanuel Rosier.
"That’s still a big market, which we forecast will be worth $36.3 billion in 2026, but it’s only part of the industry. The longer-term global story will depend on what happens next, particularly on PC and online."
