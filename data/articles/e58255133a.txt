Can China champion the Global South without speaking for it?
China’s challenge is to turn its economic relationships into constructive partnerships without expecting political loyalty in return
Travelling through South America earlier this year, I was repeatedly reminded of China’s growing importance. At Ezeiza International Airport in Buenos Aires, I spotted the logo of the Industrial and Commercial Bank of China. ICBC acquired an 80 per cent stake in Standard Bank Argentina in 2012, establishing a significant presence.
