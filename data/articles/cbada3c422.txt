For our Weekly Drop Watch column, Fashionista scours the market to curate the most noteworthy releases from our favorite fashion and beauty brands. Keep scrolling for this week’s highlights.
Anine Bing partnered with Tecovas on a collaboration “born from the spirit of the American West set against the streets of Paris, pairing cowboy attitude with the effortless confidence of the Anine Bing woman,” as described in press materials. The collection features The Loretta, a statement 17″-shaft boot with a snip toe and leather heel, and The Jamie, a round-toe classic with an 11″ shaft and eight-row stitching, both handcrafted with hung leather lining. There are also two cowboy hat styles and two belts, including a co-branded buckle. Shop the full collab — priced from $180 to $525 — at tecovas.com and aninebing.com.
Fashion designer Christopher John Rogers has brought his eye for pattern and color to the home sphere via a collaboration with The Rug Company. The collection features 10 designs ranging in price from $2,050 to $5,750. Shop them all at therugcompany.com.
Denim brand Frame introduced its horse-forward Equestrian Collection this week, bringing “traditional riding style into the modern wardrobe.” The 13-piece lineup features tailored jackets, denim shirting, suede pants, cozy outerwear and horse-adorned knits, priced from $298 to $2,498. Shop it at frame-store.com.
Charleston-based jewelry brand Hart tapped influencer Julia Berolzheimer for a party-ready collab, featuring colorful charms, talismans, gemstone accents and exclusive chains and cords “designed to be mixed, matched and layered.” Inspired by Berolzheimer’s childhood memories of her grandmother’s California garden and her love of collecting Art Deco-era minaudières, the nine-piece collection retails for $55-$375. Shop it at shophart.com.
Accessories brand Dagne Dover dropped its Fall 2026 collection, entitled “Home Team,” this week. Intended as a love letter to New York City, the lineup was inspired by the city’s street style, athleticism and neighborhood spirit, according to the brand. Featuring varsity stripes sporty jersey mesh and rich fall colors, the range includes carryalls, vanity cases, keychains and more. Get them at dagnedover.com.
Jenny Bird introduced its New Estate collection, envisioned as “your nonna’s rhinestones, modernized by JB,” per a press release. It includes oversized earrings, brooches, pendant necklaces and more. Shop the collection at jenny-bird.com.
Hair-care brand Ouai joined forces with jewelry and accessories label Heaven Mayhem for a fragrance-and-jewelry hybrid that launched this week. Available in two distinct designs, the collab’s vase-inspired Amulet Necklaces are “designed to be worn close to the skin,” and hold the perfume of its corresponding Ouai fragrance. The Rue St-Honoré Amulet is “a sophisticated two-tone gold and silver pendant paired with a delicate silver chain, inspired by the floral notes of gardenia, violet, ylang ylang and white musk,” while the Ibiza Amulet is “a striking black and silver pendant finished with a dramatic black tassel, capturing the warmth of cocoa, tonka bean, vanilla and rum.” Shop them for $125 each at theouai.com and heavenmayhem.com.
Eyewear brand Warby Parker teamed up with Selena Gomez’s Rare Beauty on a limited-edition, five-piece collection, with each frame inspired by a shade of Rare Beauty’s Soft Pinch Liquid Blush. Katseye’s Megan Skiendiel stars in the campaign (wearing the Gillian frames inspired by Hope, above). Warby Parker also made a donation in support of the Rare Impact Fund. Shop the frames for $95 to $145 each at warbyparker.com.
Designer Willy Chavarria partnered with with Mexico City-based fragrance house Xinú on a collaboration that “explores the intersection of fashion, fragrance and cultural identity.” The limited edition drop reimagines Xinú’s signature FlordeMusgo candle in a black vessel, finished with a custom Willy Chavarria medallion. Each candle is presented inside Xinú’s signature scented Wool Cocoon, crafted from sustainable wool, in an exclusive black-on-black edition developed specifically for this partnership. Shop the candle for $120 at willychavarria.com.
Soft Services, the body-care brand known for its viral Theraplush overnight hand treatment, introduced its lighter weight daytime counterpart “Deskside” ($58) this week. Described as a “daytime weightless hydrator,” the formula aims to deliver deep moisture without greasy residue or wait time via 1% ectoin, 3% betaine, and 0.5% beta-glucan. It comes housed in a a refillable, desk-friendly vessel and is available in both the debut Lumelo fragrance (“a fresh blend of juicy pomelo, orange blossom, fig leaf, fresh-cut grass and white musk,”) as well as a fragrance-free option. Shop it at softservices.com and sephora.com.
Hair tool brand T3 unveiled its latest device, the CeraBounce ($130), this week. The heated round brush features the brand’s proprietary Korean CeraGloss ceramic technology to add volume, bounce and shine to air-dried hair or freshen up your blowout or curls. It also offers an extended 1 ½” barrel to cover more hair per pass as well as auto world voltage that works around the globe. Shop the tool at t3micro.com.
Soulstice, the new hair-care brand from Nancy Twine, debuted hair tools and accessories this week. Designed to be “beautiful objects that also serve a purpose in your routine,” the lineup includes The Wet Comb ($24) for detangling wet hair, The Dry Comb ($26) for styling and finishing your look and The Styling Pin ($18) a French pin. Shop them at soulstice.com.
Fragrance brand Phlur unveiled its newest scent, Cashmere Frost, available as a hair and body mist ($39 for 8 oz; $26 for 3 oz). Developed by perfumer Alexandra Monet, the cool amber musk scent features notes of iced bergamot, almond, tiare flower, snowdrop, jasmine, silver aldehydes, musk, vanilla cream and cashmere woods. Shop it at phlur.com and sephora.com.
Dibs Beauty and decor brand Mackenzie Childs dropped a limited-edition collaboration ready for holiday gifting this week. The MacKenzie-Childs x Dibs Checkmate Vanity Set ($100) pairs a burgundy check edition of the bestselling Dibs Duo Brush Face with the iconic MacKenzie-Childs Gold Check Capiz Vanity Tray. Shop it at dibsbeauty.com and mackenzie-childs.com.
