Michelle Yeoh is on the press run for “Blade Runner 2099” and has been proving that hot boots work for any age. The actress, who is in her 60s, has worn two daring thigh-high styles this week that added an edge and drama to her looks.
The Oscar winner had some fun with her look for a photocall for “Blade Runner 2099” on the Regis House Rooftop on Thursday. She was dressed in a custom Self-Portrait lime green gradient rhinestone and graphic PVC paneled dress, which she styled with a white fur coat worn draped and off the shoulder.
She opted for what appears to be the dramatic Le Silla Eva Thigh-High Boot 120mm. The thigh-high boots from the brand’s Eva line are a signature style for the Le Silla fashion house. They are crafted from black stretch vegan leather, and they feature a bold 120mm stiletto heel and a snug upper that feels like a second skin.

The black faux-leather thigh-high boots feature a pointed toe and are sculpted by visible seams that highlight the shoes’ sharp lines and structural contours along the shaft and the vamp that help shape the leg silhouette. A single vertical seam runs up the front of the shaft, and it helps the boot stay in place around the thigh. The over-the-knee style retails for $1,149.
Yeoh also attended the “Blade Runner 2099” London premiere at Odeon Luxe Leicester Square on Tuesday, wearing a Givenchy by Sarah Burton fall 2026 look. She opted for a black leather miniskirt, which she wore with a white turtleneck blouse with wide, cuffed sleeves.
She paired the look with the Givenchy Shark Pinch boots, also from the fall 2026 collection. The over-the-knee boots are made in smooth calfskin leather and are part of the brand’s popular Shark boot line. They feature a gold-finish metal pinch with Givenchy engraved on the boot cuff.
Like Yeoh’s most recent pair, the Shark Pinch boots also defined by a pointed toe, but they also have a sculptural covered wedge heel, measuring 3.5 inches in height. The silhouette reinterprets the signature Givenchy 4G padlock silhouette with elongated proportions. The new boots’ shafts are designed with a straight pant silhouette to be paired with skirts, dresses or over pants. They retail for $3,190.
The actress often works with celebrity stylist Dena Neustadter Giannini to craft her youthful and bold style. Yeoh is a fan of making a statement through her shoes, and has paired gowns with edgy boots like the Amina Muaddi Yigit leather platform ankle boots on the red carpet.
