Rare EU-UK summit as Andy Burnham seeks to mend Brexit damage
Issued on:
Share
UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham and the EU's top officials were set to announce deals to ease trade and youth mobility at a Brussels summit Friday, billed as a first step in a push to rebuild ties after Brexit. But in a potential spoiler for Burnham's "reset", Brussels said the deals coming into force would be conditional on reaching an agreement to cut university fees for EU students in Britain. FRANCE 24's International Affairs Editor Rochelle Ferguson Bouyahi tells us more.
