Target has officially revealed its Black Friday plans for 2026. According to the retailer's most recent press release, the official Black Friday sale will kick off this Sunday, November 22. For those planning on doing their shopping in stores on actual Black Friday, though, Target will be giving away gift cards to the first 100 eligible guests on November 27. These gift cards range from $25 -$100 with one guest at each store winning the grand prize of $1,000 in free Target spending money.
In addition to the start date and the giveaway, Target has also revealed its full Black Friday ad for next week. This preview doesn't include everything that will be discounted, but it does give you an idea of what to expect from the sale and when to expect it.
Target has already launched several early Black Friday sales in November, but these upcoming discounts look to be the good stuff. The start of the sale coincides with Nintendo's Black Friday start date, so you can expect the hottest item to be the Switch 2 bundle again this year.
What Deals Will Be Worth Shopping?
Target's Black Friday ad for 2026 leads with the Nintendo Switch 2 bundle discount, which is likely going to be the only console deal of the year. So if you don't already have Nintendo's latest console, this is a good chance to grab it for the same price it was before the price hike happened earlier this year. For PlayStation gamers, the best deal here is on DualSense controllers, but those discounts have been live since the PS5 sale launched earlier this week.
Outside of games, some of the most notable discounts are on toys. The ad claims that select LEGO sets will be up to 40% off during the sale, though we only get a look at a few of the options and they aren't that exciting. Target tends to have some of the best LEGO deals on new sets every year, so you'll definitely want to keep an eye out for those discounts. We're already seeing some pretty substantial discounts on LEGO Pokemon sets that just came out this year ahead of this sale.
Target will also be offering a fairly expansive buy 2, get 1 free on books and music this year. This is one of the most popular sales for anyone looking to add to their book or vinyl collections and Amazon almost always matches the discount.
