Hold on to your hats (and your little blue toggle coats): Paddington is New York City-bound.
Next year, the multiple-Olivier Award-winning theatrical sensation that is Paddington: The Musical opens at the Al Hirschfeld Theatre on Broadway—bringing with it, yes, the four-foot-tall puppet that is Paddington Bear himself, but also a wonderfully star-studded cast.
Performers in the New York production will include Kelvin Dukes as Tony, Drew Gehling (Waitress) as Mr. Brown, Allison Guinn (Operation Mincemeat) as Lady Sloane, Tony Hale (Veep, Arrested Development) as Mr. Curry, J. Quinton Johnson (Hamilton) as Grant, Leslie Rodriguez Kritzer (The Fantasticks) as Millicent Clyde, Zonya Love (The Color Purple) as Tanya, Serena Parrish (Don’t Say Good Luck) as Judy Brown, Karen Ziemba (Contact) as Mrs. Bird, Chip Zien (Into the Woods) as Mr. Gruber, and, as Mrs. Brown, one Jessie Mueller (Waitress, Carousel), making her first appearance on Broadway since starring in Tracy Letts’s The Minutes in 2022.
Additionally, Arti Shah and James Hameed, who brought Paddington Bear to life onstage in the West End, will reprise their joint role at the Hirschfeld.
“This just felt like the right show, with the right message,” Mueller tells Vogue of her grand return to the Main Stem. “Paddington is a character I’ve known and loved all my life! And I’m so excited to be back on Broadway to share this story of love, family, kindness and inclusion.”
“I really hope the Broadway audience can find a place in their hearts to look after this bear,” says the show’s composer, Tom Fletcher. (Paddington’s book has been adapted by Jessica Swale, and Luke Sheppard directs.) “Writing the songs for Paddington: The Musical has been the greatest honor of my life, and it goes without saying that getting to bring our show to New York is an actual dream come true.”
He goes on: “The importance of kindness is at the heart of our story in London, and I hope we can bring that message to New York and spread kindness like marmalade.”
New Yorkers should be on the lookout for a polite little bear from Peru in the spring: Previews of Paddington: The Musical begin on Tuesday, March 30, with an opening night set for Sunday, April 18.
