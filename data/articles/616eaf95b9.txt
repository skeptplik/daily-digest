At least one person was killed after Ukrainian drones struck a number of industrial sites in the Perm region, local authorities said Monday.
Regional Governor Dmitry Makhonin confirmed that drones targeted “several” facilities but did not identify any of them by name. He said one person was killed and two others suffered minor injuries.
The exiled news outlet Astra said it used eyewitness footage to identify one industrial site as a Lukoil-operated oil refinery. Photos shared by the outlet showed multiple columns of smoke rising from the refinery.
Astra reported that the fires appeared concentrated mainly in the refinery’s primary oil processing area.
The Lukoil refinery is one of Russia’s largest and most technologically advanced, processing more than 13 million metric tons of crude oil per year and producing gasoline, diesel, jet fuel and lubricants for domestic and export markets.
The facility has been repeatedly targeted by Ukrainian drones this year, including attacks in April, May, July, August and September.
Russia’s Defense Ministry said it intercepted 192 Ukrainian drones over Russian regions and annexed Crimea between Sunday night and Monday morning.
Also on Monday, authorities said the Novoshakhtinsk oil refinery in the southern Rostov region suspended operations after an earlier Ukrainian drone attack on the facility.
Meanwhile, in Ukraine, authorities said the death toll from a Russian attack on the town of Dymer on Sunday had risen to 11 after rescuers discovered another body beneath rubble.
