Jennifer Lawrence made a splash at the Beauty Inc Awards on Tuesday night, where she and her cofounder Jasmina Aganovic received the Founders of the Year Award for their hair care brand, Mes.
Lawrence walked the red carpet in a tailored Dior jacket and white miniskirt, paired with black pumps and a bird-shaped purse. The “Hunger Games” star slicked back her waist-length blond hair, styling her long locks to tuck behind her ears and tuck in slightly at the nape of her neck. The gelled look added definition to Lawrence’s face, keeping the focus on her features and silhouetted outfit. Slight volume was added at the crown of her head to add shape before falling straight down her back.
For her look on Tuesday night Lawrence tapped hairstylist Jimmy Paul who works primarily for editorial shoots and runway shows. Likely incorporating products from Mes — namely the brand’s hair mask that Lawrence has cited using for styling — the Oscar-winning actress complemented her structured hair with soft, glowing glam designed by makeup artist Nina Park.
Lawrence opted for Park’s signature luminous, skin-first approach to glam, pairing bronzed cheekbones with light pink blush and a simple black cat eye liner. A glossy nude pink lip topped off the look.
Taking to the stage with Aganovic when collecting their award, Lawrence reflected on the journey behind building her brand Mes, and citing Aganovic’s background in biological and chemical engineering as essential to the brand.
Other honorees of the night included Rhode founder Hailey Bieber, legendary makeup artist Pat McGrath, and POV Beauty founder/content creator Mikayla Nogueira.
