Dior Backstage Glow-Up Skin Tint
About the Product: Dior’s skin tint is a triple-threat—complexion coverage, SPF protection, and a skincare-infused formula. To start, it comes in 24 shades, all offering light to medium coverage. Ingredients like fermented collagen, glycerin, and vitamin E hydrate skin for seamless application, while hybrid sunscreen ingredients offer protection up to SPF 20.
More To Know
- Key Ingredients: Octisalate 4.50%, titanium dioxide 4.23%, glycerin, mica, tocopherol, fermented collagen
- Size: 1.35 oz
Hermès Beauty Rouge Hermès Nourishing Lip Care Mask
About the Product: Yep, Hermès does beauty too—and their lip mask leaves your pout soft and supple. Made in Italy with 97% naturally-derived ingredients, Hermes’s lip mask repairs chapped lips with hyaluronic acid, sesame seed extract, ceramides, and astaxanthin (that’s where that orange hue comes from).
More To Know
- Key Ingredients: Hyaluronic acid, sesame seed extract, ceramides, astaxanthin, castor seed oil, sweet almond oil
- Size: 0.29 oz
CurrentBody LED Light Therapy Face Mask: Series 3
About the Product: CurrentBody’s latest LED mask innovation elevates the brand’s existing library of tech. Series 3 has had a major boost in bulbs from 236 to 750, designed to treat everything from hyperpigmentation to fine lines. Board-certified nurse practitioner Natia Rufolo particularly likes its customization features: “It incorporates six wavelengths, including 633-nanometer red, 830-nanometer near-infrared, and 1072-nanometer deep near-infrared, alongside blue, green, and yellow light, so it goes beyond a traditional red-and-near-infrared mask,” she said in our best red light therapy guide.

Jones Road Wide Awake Brightening Concealer
About the Product: Jones Road’s brightening concealer uses a trio of botanical extracts (marshmallow root, rice bran, and licorice root) to help brighten discoloration while offering buildable medium coverage. Meanwhile, shea butter allows the dense, creamy formula to sink into the skin for an entirely natural, non-cakey finish—and for even more coverage, this pick pairs well with the brand’s neutralizer.
More To Know
- Key Ingredients: Marshmallow root, rice bran extract, licorice root, shea butter, vitamin E, sunflower seed wax, beeswax.
- Size: 0.17 oz
TIRTIR Tinted Skin Glow Cushion
About the Product: A follow up to TIRTIR’s viral semi-matte cushion foundation, its Tinted Skin Glow Cushion offers a luminous alternative. Here, the formula features 73% skin-care ingredients and a serum-like texture that just bounces into the complexion. A blend of niacinamide, peptides, hyaluronic acid, and botanical oils deliver hydrating, plumping benefits while offering medium, buildable coverage.
More To Know
- Key Ingredients: Dimethicone, tocopherol, niacinamide, sunflower seed oil, pantheonol, macadamia seed oil, allantoin, multi-weight hyaluronic acid
- Size: 0.56 oz
La Mer The Concentrate Serum
About the Product: La Mer offers some of the most prestigious skincare on the market—in both price and formulation. Aside from the brand’s signature concentrated Miracle Broth (a blend of fermented kelp extract, vitamins, and nutrients), The Concentrate brings together lime tea concentrate and botanic oils to soothe irritated skin—whether you’re combating general redness or post-procedural skin.
- “I liked how this felt on my skin, but it might take me some time to convince myself this $400 serum is worth it compared with my usual glowy, concentrated serums that cost a fraction of the price. It layered really well with my skin-care routine and layered beautifully under makeup, but it’s not revolutionary enough for me to be obsessed.” —Toni Bravo, beauty content creator
- “Open my vanity to see how much I love La Mer—I hoard its elixirs like the fountain of youth. While I quickly fall for the moisturizers, emulsions, and balms, The Concetrate has taken longer to grow on me. As someone with oily skin, its velvet texture isn’t ideal; I find that it feels great on application, but layers well with other often water-based products in my regimen (given my skin type, I try to avoid oil-based products). However, since its reformulation, I’ve been committed to giving it a proper test in the name of research. My texture preferences aside, I am in awe of its reparative benefits. This is for days when skin is looking red or feeling sensitive. I apply it at night and wake with calmer skin by morning. For that reason, she’s staying in my rotation.” — Kiana Murden, beauty shopping editor
More To Know
- Key Ingredients: Algae Extract, glycerin, Isododecane, sea salt, sesame seed oil, lime tea concentrate
- Size: 0.5 oz
Christophe Robin Finishing Perfecting Radiance Oil

More To Know

Carte Blanche Multi-Hyphenate Brightening Skin Tint Mineral SPF 35

Plus, squalane and panthenol soothe and smooth dehydrated skin.

111Skin Precision Peel
About the Product: 111Skin’s rinse-off peel exfoliates with a blend of AHAs, BHAs, PHAs, and botanical extracts. Papain (AKA papaya enzymes) and milk thistle extract help to slough off dead skin and brighten hyperpigmentation. Meanwhile, glutathione helps fight against oxidative stress.
- “Don’t let the word ‘peel’ scare you, this product isn’t more gentle than you think. Balancing three types of chemical exfoliators (read: AHA, BHA, and PHA) with milky thistle, glutathione, and the brand’s NACY², this basically eliminated any congestion, bumpy texture, and blossoming breakouts in one use. Like any exfoliant, I’d recommend spot testing first to target areas to gauge your skin’s resilience.” —Kiana Murden, beauty shopping editor
More To Know
- Key Ingredients: Mandelic acid, salicylic acid, lactobionic acid, papain, milk thistle extract, glutathione

L’Oréal Paris Revitalift Triple Power Night Moisturizer
About the Product: L’Oréal Paris offers an affordable moisturizer that balances active ingredients alongside hydrating humectants. The super-rich texture applies like butter, combining hyaluronic acid to boost water retention, retinol to smooth skin and target signs of aging, and vitamin C to brighten your complexion. The good news, though, is that it’s safe for sensitive skin, given retinol and vitamin C’s relatively low concentration.
- “L’Oreal Revitalift is a great nighttime product with ingredients like vitamin C, PHAs, and glycolic acid, as well as hyaluronic acid. PHAs are actually something I really love, because they act as a gentle exfoliant without irritation. Obviously, vitamin C is a derm-favorite ingredient, but some people have trouble tolerating it—so the gentle formulation here makes a huge difference. In short, this is a nice entry into a nighttime routine with real staying power from ingredients like vitamin C, PHAs, and hyaluronic acid, but it’s not too irritating, so you don’t have to worry. Start two to three times a week, then work your way up. But I have a feeling I really like this.” — Dr. Dhaval Bhanusali, board-certified dermatologist
More To Know
- Key Ingredients: Niacinamide, salicylic acid, caffeine, tocopherol, sodium hyaluronate, retinol, vitamin C
- Size: 1.7 oz.

How We Tested
When we test and review a product, we take a holistic approach to deliver well-rounded product recommendations. First, we lean on Vogue’s vast network of experts—from board-certified dermatologists to celebrity estheticians—to gain professional acumen on the industry’s standout products, ones these specialists would actually use on their clients. We pair their expertise with our editorial best practices to curate the thoughtful edits you read on our site.
We selected the best based on the following characteristics: ingredients, type, texture, packaging, finish, and the concerns they address. To do this, we paired our own personal tests of each formula with expert guidance and reviewer insights to determine which we would recommend to you. Read more about our testing methodology here.
