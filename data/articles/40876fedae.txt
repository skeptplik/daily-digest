Less is more?
In today’s clinical AI landscape, every platform is making a land grab: Epic, traditionally a system of record, now wants to display clinical recommendations. Abridge, which started as an ambient scribe, wants your patient’s insurance information. OpenEvidence, which originally answered clinical questions, wants you to enter patient records and visits so it can give you personalized recommendations.
