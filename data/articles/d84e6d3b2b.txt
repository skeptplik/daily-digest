Key Event
Pick 65: Harper Banfield - West Coast
A tough and willing inside midfielder with the kicking skills to damage teams on the outside too. His clean hands and work ethic impressed in WAFL stints with West Coast.
The son of West Coast premiership player Drew Banfield.
West Coast weren't able to snap up his brother Charlie in last year's draft, with the Saints picking him, but they don't miss out this time.
Key Event
Pick 64: Jamarra Ugle-Hagan - Gold Coast
A similar situation to Himmelberg as Jamarra is delisted and then put back on by the Suns.
Incredible that the No.1 pick in the draft six years ago goes at 64 in this year's draft.
Can he get his AFL career back on track?
Key Event
Pick 63: Elliott Himmelberg - Gold Coast
Himmelberg was delisted earlier in the year to clear up some list spots and now gets re-drafted once again.
The key forward has played 50 games at the AFL level, all of them coming for the Crows before his move to the Suns at the end of 2024.
He is yet to make an AFL appearance for Gold Coast.
Key Event
Pick 62: Koby LeCras - Collingwood
A tall and athletic winger, LeCras looked set for a big 2026 before an ACL strain put him on ice. Has proven to be goal threat from midfield or half-forward and equally dangerous in the air and at ground level.
A real good value pick for the Pies at this point, given LeCras was ranked much higher than this point on a lot of draft boards.
His uncle is 2018 West Coast premiership player Mark LeCras.
Key Event
Pick 61: Mason McGroder - Sydney
A rare, Olympic-calibre athlete who made the late decision to pursue a footy career over the chase for gold. With an incredible speed, power, aerobic capacity and leap, McGroder’s game needs refining but the tools are seriously exciting.
Collingwood places a bid on the 195cm prospect, but the Swans match it.
The vision of this guy running off halfback is incredible. Dare I say he looks like Nick Blakey in the way he carries the ball under the arm out of defence?
Key Event
Pick 60: Zach Stevens - Geelong
A speedy small forward who made a splash with Geelong’s VFL team, Stevens brings the pressure inside offensive 50 and can hit the scoreboard.
Another prospect out of South Barwon being selected by the Cats. Call that the Tom Stewart pipeline.
Key Event
Pick 59: Keenan Boi - Hawthorn
A half-forward type with a powerful frame and a burst of speed. Boi can stand up in tackles and break through them with his strength and is a good finisher in front of goal. Tank is still improving, but the upside is huge.
Another bid from Geelong on a Hawthorn-tied player that is matched straight away by the Hawks.
He earns a comparison to Gold Coast's Leo Lombard on Fox's draft coverage.
That was Geelong's fifth bid on a prospect tied to a rival club.
Key Event
Pick 58: Cooper Hodge - Hawthorn
Tough, contested and uncompromising, there are clear shades of Luke in the way Cooper plays. Can play in defence or midfield and won’t take a backward step despite his size.
Some proper, proper cheeky stuff from the Cats as they place a bid on the son of the Hawthorn legend.
Not a lot of time wasted by the Hawks before they match this one. Hodge was also eligible to join Brisbane as a part of its academy, but opted to go to the Hawks instead.
Key Event
Pick 57: Jake Medved - Fremantle
A dashing general defender who is new to the game of Australian Rules. Excels with the run and carry game off halfback.
The Swans pass on a bid from the Dockers on their academy prospect!
Real shades of Tadhg Kennelly in the way he takes on defenders, and he's got really good size at 190cm as well.
Key Event
Pick 56: Anthony Long - Geelong
A general forward who excels at the ground level and has tremendous speed off the mark.
Geelong has a third straight bid on an NGA/father-son, and this time it's successful as Adelaide passes!
Key Event
Pick 55: Blake Justice - Essendon
A slick and creative midfielder, Justice also tested extremely well in the sprints at the combine. If he can add that electric pace to his all-round midfield game he could become a real weapon.
The Bombers match another bid from Geelong to pick up a player from their NGA, whose mother is from Indonesia.
Key Event
Pick 54: Aidan McCartney - North Melbourne
A key forward with some good size who has the ability to be a good leading target. Huge upside and averaged a couple of goals per game in the talent league.
The Kangaroos match Geelong's bid for the son of Jason McCartney.
He was eligible to be selected as a part of Sydney's academy as well, but opted to go with North under the father-son rule.
Key Event
Pick 53: Sam Devers - Western Bulldogs
A dasher off half-back. Devers is an excellent size and wonderful mover with the ball.
Key Event
Pick 52: Kaie Sullivan - Melbourne
A hands-first distributing midfielder standing at 182cm who averaged 16 disposals and four tackles during the talent league this season.
Key Event
Pick 51: Harry Franz - Richmond
A 193cm key defender who finished in Glenelg's Under-18 premiership-winning side. An intercepting machine that recorded an 83cm vertical jump at the draft combine.
Franz's selection wraps up Richmond's night at the draft!
Key Event
Pick 50: Jack Riding - Western Bulldogs
Another mature-aged pick at 26 years old. James Hird said throughout the year that Riding should be playing at the AFL level after a dominant performance against Hird's Port Melbourne side while playing for Werribee.
Key Event
Pick 49: Ben Forster - Melbourne
Another midfielder who excels at picking up the contested footy. He is ultra-competitive and is a hard-worker.
Forster is the sixth player to be drafted out of the Sandringham Dragons in this year's draft.
Key Event
Pick 48: Mitch Knevitt - Sydney
A second-chance pick from the Swans as they snap up the former Geelong midfielder!
Knevitt was delisted by the Cats earlier this year, but is an absolute ball-magnet at the VFL level.
Definitely a worthwhile free hit at this point for the Swans.
Knevitt played just 23 games in five seasons for Geelong, including just two appearances this season.
Key Event
Pick 47: Angus White - Port Adelaide
A powerful midfield-forward with some explosive acceleration.
White is also in the graduation room with Kodah Edwards and Archie Van Dyk.
Absolutely unbelievable scenes as White is mobbed by his mates before he chats to Fox Footy!
"I'm just absolutely over the moon, emotions are high, watching two of my best mates go before," he says.
"It's the best day ever, I've been waiting for this since I was three years old.
"A little bit [nervous watching Edwards and Van Dyk drafted earlier]. We knew this would happen.
"I've had a pretty tough year with the ACL injury, but just super grateful for the Port Adelaide Football Club."
Key Event
Pick 46: Ethan Herbert - Carlton
A mobile, repeat-effort ruck who is strong in the air and reads the play well. Ruck craft still developing and needs to put on size, but he has the athletic profile to become a genuine AFL ruck.
Richmond has traded pick 46 to Carlton
The Tigers making another move, this time it's with the Blues.
The Tigers get pick 51 and Carlton's 2028 third-rounder in exchange for pick 46.
Key Event
Pick 45: Andrio Sousa - GWS Giants
An exciting medium forward with elite speed and agility, who can also fly for his marks. Sousa is raw and undersized but has an array of forward-50 weapons.
Richmond has traded pick 45 to GWS
The Giants grab pick 45 in exchange for pick 46 and a 2028 third-rounder, which goes to the Tigers.
Key Event
Pick 44: Sam Tassone - Adelaide
A hard-working, hard-running rebounding defender who takes the game on. Tassone is a high metres-gained defender but is willing to do the dirty work down back too.
Key Event
Pick 43: Lewis Houndsome - St Kilda
St Kilda goes big again after taking Tyson Bradley last night by adding a ruckman in Houndsome. He averaged 12 touches, 3.2 marks and 19.5 hitouts per contest in 11 matches for the Northern Knights.
Key Event
Pick 42: Kane Johnston - Fremantle
A massive bolter here as the Dockers grab the key mature-aged key forward ranked 95th on Fox's big board. A match-up nightmare given the agility he has at his size.
Key Event
Pick 41: Jordan Knapp - Collingwood
A hard-working midfielder who is slick in congestion and a link on the outside, Knapp finds the footy easily.
Key Event
Pick 40: Brady Wright - Sydney
Wright is an athletic medium forward who enjoyed a strong season of goalkicking in the VFL. Now the 20th consecutive Fothergill-Round-Mitchell Medallist to be drafted as a mature-ager.
Sydney has traded pick 41 to Collingwood
Sydney has traded pick 41 to Collingwood in exchange for a 2028 second-round pick.
Key Event
Pick 39: Gabriel Patterson - Port Adelaide
A powerful and physical mid-forward who takes on the game and his opponents with confidence. He’s quick, evasive and explosive and is good in the air. Consistency can be an issue, but Patterson has real X-factor.
A player who Collingwood narrowly missed out on adding as a father-son.
Patterson's father, Stephen, played 96 games for the Pies, four short of the 100-game threshold for the father-son rule.
Key Event
Pick 38: Lachlan Hicks - Geelong
Hicks is a fantastic athlete and proved it with elite times across the board at the national combine. He is a hard-working midfielder who uses his running power to get to and influence contests.
Geelong enters the draft with what looks like a real Geelong pick at this point. A player with high footy IQ.
They've made a killing with picks in this sort of range and will hope that they've added another absolute steal here.
Hawthorn trades pick 39 to Port Adelaide
We've got a second trade of the night with Port Adelaide getting its hands on pick 39, with Hawthorn receiving a future pick going the other way.
Key Event
Pick 37: Jackson Watkins - Carlton
A powerful midfield-forward in the Cam Rayner mold. He was a bit of a bolter late in the season and has the ability to hit the scoreboard.
Carlton adding some speed and agility with this pick. The main concern for Watkins is his consistency, but if he is able to string full games of footy together, this pick could be a steal for the Blues.
I'd suspect Watkins starts as a high half-forward, given Carlton's midfield is absolutely chockers.
Key Event
Pick 36: Archie Spencer - Brisbane
A smaller midfielder who is able to ply his trade in defence as well. His versatility is a major plus and he is a good one-on-one player and is prolific grabbing groundballs. Also a good user of the football.
Spencer earns a comparison to Josh Dunkley from Fox Footy's Mick Ablett, and Spencer will have a chance to learn from him first hand.
The Lions end up drafting four midfielders tonight. It'll be interesting to see what other positions those guys play in and whether any of them get a look-in at all next year.
Key Event
Pick 35: Seb Marsic - North Melbourne
A key forward standing at 193cm who kicked seven goals in a single game earlier in the season. He's clean overhead and has good forward pressure for a key position player.
A massive draft bolder as the Kangaroos take a player rated 70th on Fox Footy's big board.
He's compared to Kangaroos skipper Nick Larkey, who also happened to be a very late pick in the draft.
It'll be interesting to see whether Marsic settles in the forward line or down in defence, which is an area of need for the Roos.
Key Event
Pick 34: Xavier Ladbrook - Adelaide
A key-position prospect with stacks of potential, Ladbrook looks set for a life as an intercepting and rebounding key back. His slender frame will need plenty of time to develop, but his height, mobility and aerial ability are enticing attributes for a club willing to be patient.
It'll be interesting to see where Ladbrook eventually settles at the AFL level having played at both ends of the ground.
"Yeah I'm feeling great. I was pretty nervous coming into it," he tells Fox Footy.
"They [Adelaide] spoke to me three times throughout the year, so I'm happy to be here."
Ladbrook says he models his game on Brisbane's defensive maestro Harris Andrews.
Key Event
Pick 33: Khaled El Souki - Western Bulldogs
An electric small forward who is tough to tackle due to a combination of pace, agility and power. El Souki is a strong tackler and brings the pressure inside the forward 50, but most importantly knows where the goals are.
This is the celebration of the draft so far. El Souki is in a warehouse with approximately 100 people and there are fireworks going off in the background.
Khaled has been lifted up like he's scored a winner in the World Cup final. There are also a couple of Lebanese drummers playing in the background as El Souki is hoisted up in the air.

Key Event
Pick 32: Harvey Spawton-Guy - Carlton
A versatile and athletic developing tall, it is too early to tell exactly which position Spawton-Guy will make his own at the top level. Played some senior WAFL games as a tall forward this year and certainly held his own.
A player with real swingman potential at 195cm, who could genuinely play at either end of the ground.
I've got to say Shifter Sheehan loves giving fans of every club hope that they've drafted a superstar. He compares Spawton-Guy to Aaron Cadman this time.
Key Event
Pick 31: Gus Kennedy - Collingwood
Tough, physical and reliable defender who can lock down on opponents and supply on the rebound as well. Kennedy is well built already with a solid frame and knows how to use it.
The son of ABC Sport's very own Paul Kennedy lands at the Magpies!
Paul is a big Magpies man, so he'll be thrilled with that result, no doubt.
Key Event
Pick 30: Benji Van Rooyen - Hawthorn
Strong, big-bodied ruck-forward who is all about the second-efforts. Not a great leaper and is still improving his tank, but van Rooyen’s ruck craft is coming on well and he is a serious competitor in the air and at ground level.
Van Rooyen is the brother of Melbourne forward Jacob and is considered the second-best ruck in this year's class behind Harry Van Hattum.
With Ned Reeves and Lloyd Meek in tow at Hawthorn, Van Rooyen will get some time to develop slowly in the background but will be able to contribute sooner than most rucks given he's got an AFL-ready body.
Key Event
Pick 29: Jackson Phillips - Brisbane
A powerful midfield option who can push forward and hit the scoreboard. Has the speed to evade tackles and strength to stand up in them. Doesn’t rack up big numbers but hurts you with his touches thanks to excellent kicking on both sides.
Phillips becomes Brisbane's third straight selection and looks like he's a proper, proper leader.
The broadcast mentions that he's helped out with some junior coaching and also works at a groundskeeper in the Peninsula.
"It definitely helped take my mind off a few things," he says of the job on Fox Footy.
"When you show up at work at six in the morning and you're mowing the greens, you don't need to think about football."
Key Event
Pick 28: Archie Van Dyk - Brisbane
A tall, big-bodied midfielder, Van Dyk uses his size and strength to dominate the stoppages. He is clean and clever by hand and dangerous in the air, making up for a perceived lack of elite speed or agility.
Incredible scenes as Van Dyk is at the same graduation function as his schoolmate Kodah Edwards.
"This is unbelievable, to be able to do it with one of my best mates from school," he tells Fox Footy.
"I've been waiting for this moment since I was five.
"To be able to go to a club that's just won three premierships in a row, I'm so excited for the opportunity."
Van Dyk says he is prepared to develop a role at halfback at the AFL level as well.
Key Event
Pick 27: Kodah Edwards - Brisbane
A tough but classy midfielder with a bag of tricks inside 50. Captained South Australia and is seen as a strong leader.
Edwards is compared to Caleb Serong by Kevin Sheehan on Fox Footy's coverage. Another comp shown on screen is Connor Rozee.
Edwards checks in from his Year 12 graduation and is suited and booted.
"It's pretty crazy," he tells Fox Footy.
"I've obviously got everyone here and can't really hear much.
"There's actually the ceremony going on right now, so I'm missing that, so I was just sitting here and waiting.
"It was just a crazy wait, so I'm pretty happy."
Edwards has a bunch of his mates behind him, but says his girlfriend is in the graduation ceremony instead. Tough luck.
