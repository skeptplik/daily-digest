Russian air defense systems intercepted 1,246 Ukrainian drones across Russia and annexed Crimea overnight, the Defense Ministry said early Saturday, marking one of the largest air barrages against the country in recent weeks.
More than a third of the drones were intercepted in the wider Moscow region, according to local officials. Moscow Mayor Sergei Sobyanin said air defenses destroyed most of the incoming aircraft, though he did not provide details on any damage.
In the southern Samara region, Governor Vyacheslav Fyodorishchev said drones targeted industrial infrastructure without causing any damage or injuries. Earlier in the week, one person was killed in the Samara region during a Ukrainian drone attack on a major Rosneft oil refinery.
At least three people were killed in attacks on the border regions of Bryansk and Kursk over the past 24 hours, regional authorities there said, while 14 people were wounded in the neighboring Belgorod region.
Authorities in the Bryansk region said separately that at least nine employees of the meat producer Miratorg were wounded on Saturday morning when a Ukrainian drone attacked a company bus.
Saturday’s claimed interceptions come days after the Russian Defense Ministry said it intercepted a record 1,441 Ukrainian drones in a single night.
Meanwhile, in Ukraine, officials said early Saturday Russian attacks killed 10 people and wounded 55 others across the country over the past day.
Correction: An earlier version of this article incorrectly stated that Saturday’s drone interceptions marked a new single-night record claimed by Moscow. The record was actually set on Nov. 17, 2026.
