Are you ready to return to the Capitol? On November 20, the latest installment of the Hunger Games franchise—subtitled Sunrise on the Reaping—arrives to theaters in the United States. To celebrate the premiere, cast members old and new walked the red carpet at Alice Tully Hall in New York City on Monday evening, in what turned out to be an array of fantastical looks.
Longtime fans of the Hunger Games movies were treated to appearances from original stars like Jennifer Lawrence and Josh Hutcherson, who even posed together on the carpet, sending Everlark shippers into a frenzy. Lawrence wore a silky black coat from Dior with a shearling collar and floral embroidery, while Hutcherson stepped out in a sharp black suit from Balenciaga.
And newer members of the cast pulled off equally show-stopping fits. Rachel Zegler, who starred as Lucy Gray Baird in the former Hunger Games prequel, The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes, was magnificent in an emerald gown from the Zuhair Murad Fall/Winter 2026 haute couture collection.
Glenn Close embraced the avant-garde label of the moment, Matiéres Fécales, with a diaphanous black dress with a dramatic tulle train. And Elle Fanning and Elizabeth Banks each brought their own striking approaches to the character they both portray onscreen: Effie Trinket. (Fanning did so in a Givenchy by Sarah Burton dress embroidered with vintage compacts, while Banks chose a lace-up floral mermaid skirt and velvet cropped jacket from Christian Siriano.)
