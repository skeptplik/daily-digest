Baidu’s new AI simulator gives robotaxis a crash course in rare road hazards
Baidu is using an AI-driven simulator to train its Apollo robotaxis to deal with hazards such as reckless cyclists running red lights
For autonomous vehicles, the hardest lessons to learn are the ones that can test the skills of even the most experienced human drivers: an errant cyclist darting through a red light, or an animal bolting into oncoming traffic from behind a corner.
The Chinese tech giant Baidu has developed a new AI-driven simulator to prepare its Apollo robotaxis for just such rare but high-stakes road hazards, as the firm looks to gain an edge in the fast-growing self-driving taxi market.
The system, Apollo World, can build complete virtual journeys from scratch, allowing the company to prepare vehicles for scenarios that are difficult to capture safely in real-world testing, according to executives.
