US Democrats nominate Jeffries to be first Black House speaker
Issued on: Modified:
Share
Hakeem Jeffries is on the cusp of becoming the first Black speaker of the US House of Representatives after Democrats elected him their leader Wednesday following a sweeping midterm election triumph. Jeffries faced no declared challenger, although opposition from some newly elected progressives had exposed divisions over how Democrats should wield their newfound power. FRANCE 24's Fraser Jackson reports from Washington.
