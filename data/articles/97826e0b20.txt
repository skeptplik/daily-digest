Michael Jordan’s most popular Air Jordan is going punk rock for 2027.
Set for a 2027 release is the all-new Air Jordan 4 Retro “Punk,” which will be available exclusively in women’s sizing. With a full colorway of White/Silver/Sail, the sneaker is expected to feature an all-white leather upper that displays its punk rock inspiration through a set of silver studs adorning the mudguard. A Sail outsole adds an off-white accent to the otherwise colorless look.

While the full-on punk inspiration is new for the Air Jordan 4, the concept of adding studded adornments to Jordan’s classic sneakers has been seen on a few occasions. Most notable is Supreme’s 2019 Air Jordan 14 collaboration, which was released in black and white-based colorways. Various Air Jordan 1s, including a recently released mid-top version for women, have also featured studded details.
An in-hand look at the Air Jordan 4 Retro Punk has yet to surface but in the meantime, a speculative mock-up was shared by @zsneakerheadz and Sneaker Files which is said to accurately depict the upcoming release.
In its Big Facts: 2026 Trends report issued in November, resale marketplace StockX’s data confirms the Air Jordan 4 is still Jordan’s most popular model, trailed closely by its successor, the Air Jordan 5. In the third quarter, the Air Jordan 4 was the second-most searched term on the platform.
And while the launch of the punk-inspired Air Jordan 4 is still a ways off, there’s plenty for fans of the model to look forward to this holiday season. The sneaker’s original Bred style will be reissued on Black Friday, Nov. 27. A football cleat Air Jordan 4 Retro TD version of the colorway will also arrive at retail on Black Friday. Later, in December, the Air Jordan 4 will be released in a new women’s “Pink Thunder” colorway and a premium Bin 23 iteration inspired by Jordan’s love of Western films.
The Air Jordan 4 Retro Punk women’s sneaker is scheduled to be released during fall 2027 for a retail price of $220. The style code for the upcoming release is JV3877-104.
