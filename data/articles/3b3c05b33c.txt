Ukrainian drones sink Russian warship at naval base in Libya
Issued on:
Share
Ukraine carried out an attack on a Russian warship moored at a port in Libya, President Volodymyr Zelensky said Monday, claiming that sea drones sank the navy vessel in a surprise assault the previous day. Ukrainian forces deployed seven domestically made sea drones in the operation, which struck what was one of Russia’s newest vessels, commissioned a year after Moscow launched the 2022 full-scale invasion of its neighbour.
