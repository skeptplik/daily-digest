Hong Kong’s malls must evolve into green sanctuaries to stay relevant
Visitors go out of their way to praise Hong Kong’s natural environment, so it stands to reason that our shopping malls should also reflect a love of nature
Retail is making a comeback in Hong Kong. Deloitte China reported an encouraging HK$203 billion (US$25.9 billion) in sales volume in the first half of 2026, beating expectations. Accordingly, they forecast the retail market to reach HK$412 billion by the end of this year.
