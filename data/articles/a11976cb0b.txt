Rishabh Pant century helps India break record for highest Test score on New Zealand soil
In short:
India has registered the highest team total in a Test played in New Zealand after declaring at 9-720 in Wellington.
Centuries from KL Rahul (136) and Rishabh Pant (128) allowed India to attain a 352-run first-innings lead over New Zealand.
The innings fell 39 runs short of India's all-time highest Test score of 7-759, scored against England in 2016.
