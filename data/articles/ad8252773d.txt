Officially, GTA 6 releases on Thursday, the 19th November. In practice, the first players in New Zealand, or claiming to be in New Zealand, fired it up on the 18th in the UK and US, and dozens of reviews have been published worldwide. Mainstream media coverage has been running all day, and will continue into the morning as the game goes on sale. In response to this highly anticipated global event, marketeers across the globe have deluged the world's media with uncertain claims of product relevancy.
Our inbox has filled with opportunistic press releases attempting to catch a sliver of the game's huge cultural impact, and direct it to everything from mattresses to car tyres to domestic help. Some of them are seeking to get their client coverage, others are trying for the SEO benefit of links from a media outlet. Most of them have offered expert comment, and many of them have offered data, invariably based on surveys conducted specifically to support their pitch.
We don't have time to write up all these pitches, and it's doubtful that most of them would be relevant to you anyway. The games industry is well-informed on the impact of Grand Theft Auto, as demonstrated by the number of releases that have fled its arrival. But there's a lot of data in there, and perhaps you need something to drop into a presentation, so we have collected some highlights.
- Almost a third (30%) of players who work from home said they may use working hours to play the new game. (Uswitch)
- 8% of US adult gamers will take holiday or adjust their work schedule to play the game (PCMag)
- 887,515 UK workers may take sick leave to play the game (Uswitch)
- 46% of GTA 6 players have graduated from school since the previous GTA game released (Unwind Media)
- 47% of Gen Z survey respondents "report being excited about the upcoming release" (Ipsos)
- 36% of them are worried about missing out on conversations if they don't play it
- 16% intend to wake up earlier than usual to squeeze in extra gaming time (MattressNextDay)
- 12% of UK players saw the gameplay leaks over the summer (YouGov)
- Of those, 46% said that seeing the leaked footage made them more excited about the game, and 4% said it made them less excited
- 1% of players said that $100 for the Ultimate Edition of the game was too cheap (firstlook.gg)
- 37% of players would prefer to purchase a major release like GTA on a physical disc (G2A)
- 38% preferred digital
- There was a 25% increase in total data downloaded on the day that the preload started (GigaClear)
- The download volume peaked at 1.2 terabytes per second (CityFibre)
- Retail purchases of Take-Two Interactive stock have increased 150% WoW (Freetrade)
- GTA 6 has been the most-wanted game in Nielsen's weekly ratings for over 150 weeks (Nielsen)
- Annual leave bookings for the 19th in the UK are 40% higher than would otherwise be expected (Timetastic)
- 22% of gamers are prepared to cut back on takeways to afford a new console to play GTA 6, and 13% would buy cheaper Christmas presents (Virgin Media O2).
Use this information as you will. We will report the game's performance figures as soon as we receive them; given Sensor Tower's projection of $1.4B in pre-order revenue, and Take Two CEO Strauss Zelnick's prior description of the pre-order volumes as "unprecedented", it seems likely we'll have sales figures before the week is out.
