Washington – A researcher at AI company Anthropic said Friday that he had used the firm’s Claude chatbot to prove the Riemann Hypothesis, a problem that has eluded mathematicians for more than 160 years.
First proposed by German mathematician Bernhard Riemann in 1859, the hypothesis concerns the distribution of prime numbers and is widely regarded as the most important open question in pure mathematics.
“I believe I have, working with Claude, discovered a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis,” Levent Alpoge wrote in a four-page note released Friday.
