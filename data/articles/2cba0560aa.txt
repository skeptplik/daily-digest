Trump recounts pilots’ ‘very strange’ UFO stories but says he remains sceptical
US leader throws spotlight back on reports of extraterrestrial encounters during interview on Sunday news programme 60 Minutes
“I’m not too much of a believer,” Trump said, while describing the pilots as credible people whose accounts he took seriously.
“They’ve told me things that are very strange. They said that, in certain cases, they said, ‘Yes, there’s something going on out there’.”
