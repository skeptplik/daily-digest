Raheem Sterling has avoided jail for dangerous driving while inhaling nitrous oxide, receiving a 12-month sentence suspended for 18 months at Basingstoke magistrates court on Wednesday.
The former England international was also ordered to do 100 hours of community service. His MBE and charitable and foundation work was accepted in mitigation, together with his decision to voluntarily submit to addiction treatment.
Sterling pleaded guilty in September to dangerous driving after driving his Lamborghini for more than an hour on motorways during rush hour while inhaling nitrous oxide in May.
The court heard in September that the former Chelsea and Manchester City forward embarked on a prolonged period of reckless driving on the M25 and M3, inhaling nitrous oxide balloons, before crashing his £270,000 car.
Sterling also admitted possession of nitrous oxide and refusing to provide a specimen and was disqualified from driving.
Simon Jones, prosecuting, told the court one witness had “genuine concerns the driver might kill someone” and described Sterling’s driving as “absolutely horrendous”.
The 31-year-old Sterling has been without a club since he his short-term contract with Feyenoord expired at the end of last season.
