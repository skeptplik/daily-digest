Whether it’s a wardrobe-related gift, a new cologne, a cozy gift for the homebody, a techno treasure for your favorite audio-head, or a new knife set for the chef who loves to make all those delicious meals, these Vogue editor-approved holiday gifts come from the heart.
Vogue’s Top Picks of Best Gifts for Men
The best gifts for any man in your life—whether it be a father (or father-in-law), grandfather, brother, husband, fiancé, or boyfriend—are ones that are individual to them. Whether it is a portable lamp for the homebody, a cap for the tennis player or golfer, an espresso maker for the coffee connoisseur, or portable speakers for the adventurer. We’re here to take the guesswork out of holiday shopping by finding the best gifts for all the men in your life.

Under-$100 Gifts
Looking for men’s gift ideas that won’t break the bank? Send him a no-fail ideas that aren’t, you know, an iPhone charger, a cap, a leather notebook, a sleek deck of cards, a sock set, hand soap, and a chili sauce set.
Best Gifts for the Cook
He watches Top Chef and Ciao House in his free time, likes to commit recipes to a book, rather than the Notes app on his cellphone, and always smells a bit like seasoning, but in a good way. The home chef comes alive in the kitchen and finds no greater joy than feeding others a home-cooked meal. Go the everyday essentials route with a set of essential knives with a sleek storage box, a Staub dutch oven perfect for cooking and entertaining, or a set of Flamingo Estate essentials—perfect for bringing that New York Times Cooking recipe he just bookmarked to life.
Best Timeless Gifts for the Classic Guy
Being practical doesn’t mean you have to forgo the element of surprise. For something classic and elevated, provide them with a great gift that’ll transcend the test of time— a Charvet tie, a pair of cufflinks, the classic pair of denim, and an everyday watch are just a few can’t-go-wrong-with bestsellers.
Best Gifts for the Homebody
Shopping for the introverts, cozy kings, and homebodies? You’re in the right place. Make home feel like a sanctuary with Loewe’s luxe candle or plush slippers. Does he swear by a morning ritual? There’s a unique gift for that—a sterling silver coffee maker. If he likes his games analog, a luxe chess set is the kind of anti-high-tech gift he’ll appreciate over, say, a PlayStation.
Best Gifts for the Wellness-Minded
Some of the best gifts for dad are those that keep him on his A-game. A Sauna blanket for good recovery. For everyday workouts and runs, A Satisfy windbreaker, a pair of Oakley sunglasses to keep him looking sharp on runs, or a pair of Nike running sneakers.
Best Gifts for Tech and Gadget Lovers
If audio is his forte, he’ll be thrilled to unwrap a Bang & Olufsen portable Bluetooth speaker for entertaining indoors or outdoors. Looking for a cozy addition to movie night? A projector adds a vintage-inspired feel, and can be used in any space—the perfect gadget! For the one who always wears Wayfarers, update his sunglasses with a pair of Meta that records and captures special moments or simply listens to music.
Best Travel Gifts for the Adventurer
For the one who is always on the go and whose vacations are mapped out months in advance. The avid traveler craves adventure, and he needs the gear to support the obsession. Get him an AirTag to track his suitcase, a carry-on, a dopp kit for the grooming essentials, and a camera to capture those special moments. For those who love hiking on a trip and love bird watching, a running vest and a pair of binoculars.
