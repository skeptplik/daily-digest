Adding to its roster of premier retail partners, Van Dang Fragrances is officially launching at Saks.
The scent brand, cofounded by former beauty editor and fragrance distributor Kim-Van Dang and her husband, Anthony Roberts, will debut online at saksfifthavenue.com and in-store at two major locations: Fifth Avenue and Brickell, Miami.
The lineup features its full assortment, including its home fragrance line — comprising candles, reed diffusers, room and linen mists, and crystal meditation sets — as well as its extraits de parfum collection, which debuted in April 2025 with its nationwide wholesale launch at Neiman Marcus. Van Dang Fragrances first debuted at Bergdorf Goodman in 2021.
“For an indie brand, our strategy is very different from most indie brands,” Dang said. “They usually start in small boutiques and then build up, whereas we started at the top because that’s just what we know. Those were our partners for a long time.”
Before Van Dang Fragrances existed, Dang and Roberts operated their own sales and distribution company, KVDAR Inc., for about two decades, during which they distributed and grew both Maison Francis Kurkdjian and Cire Trudon in the U.S. When it came time to market and take on national wholesale for their own brand, the duo went straight to the retailers they’d already formed relationships with.
These retailers, Dang continued, are where their target consumers shop, even though interest and opportunities in the category are rising on TikTok. “A lot of TikTok influencers kicked this off, and surprisingly enough, it’s bringing a whole new, younger audience into the realm, and that stigma of perfume is gone,” she said. “[That said], they are going into the department stores. I’m meeting them at Neiman and at Saks.”
As for what’s in store — pun intended — for Van Dang Fragrances next year, Dang said the brand has inked its first international distributor, On.Brand, a company in Germany. “We’ll be starting up with Mexico as well, and we are in talks with a lot of other European countries right now, as well as China,” she continued. “So, global expansion is the next big thing.”
The brand is also planning to enter the Middle East soon with three rich, oud-based scents, marking its first product expansion after its extraits de parfum collection.
