Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi emphasized the need to pursue “constructive and stable ties” with China after two brief but warm encounters with Chinese leader Xi Jinping at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit in Shenzhen on Thursday.
“We intend to comprehensively advance our strategic and mutually beneficial relationship ... pursuing dialogue to safeguard the national interests of both sides, and build a constructive and stable relationship,” she told reporters at a news conference following the summit.
Earlier that day, the Japanese leader appeared to speak to Xi for some length during a pre-summit greeting. This followed a cordial handshake between the two at a welcome banquet on Wednesday, their first brush with each other since Takaichi’s comments last November about possible Japanese military intervention in a Taiwan crisis.
