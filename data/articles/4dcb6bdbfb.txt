Key issues for voters
Surveys suggest that the economy will be a crucial factor for Americans voting in the midterms.
The US' war in Iran will also likely be on people's minds, as will Trump himself.
Congressional elections take place every two years, with midterms coming halfway through a sitting president's four-year term. They are often regarded as a referendum on the president's performance in office.
Democrat Jon Ossoff wins for US Senate in Georgia
Democrat Jon Ossoff won re-election to the Senate in Georgia, defeating Republican Mike Collins.
Ossoff, who was first elected in 2021, was the only Senate Democrat running for re-election in a state that Trump won in 2024. Holding his seat was critical for his party to reclaim control of the Senate.

What you need to know
Voters across the US are heading to the polls to decide the balance of power in Congress for US President Donald Trump's final two years in office.
Around a third of the Senate's 100 seats are up for grabs, with 51 needed for a party to hold a majority. The Republicans currently hold 53 to the Democrats 45, while two are with independents.
Eyes will be on six key Senate races in Alaska, Iowa, Kansas, Maine, Ohio and Texas. In the House of Representatives, 218 seats are needed to gain control of the chamber, the exact number held by the Republicans.
