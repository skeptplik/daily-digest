South Korea apologises for classing gay teens as delinquents
A counselling manual put homosexuality in the same bracket as theft and drug abuse, triggering a hasty climbdown
Homosexuality was grouped alongside sexual violence, theft and drug abuse under “delinquency-related issues” in the manual, the Korea JoongAng Daily reported.
Published by the Korea Youth Counselling and Welfare Institute – a body affiliated with the Ministry of Gender Equality and Family – the guide was produced for “youth companions”, or specialists tasked with supporting at-risk young people.
