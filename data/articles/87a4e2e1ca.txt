Thousands march during International Day for the Elimination of Violence Against Women
Marches, protests and other events took place worldwide, including in Canada, Europe and South America
Thousands of people marched at events worldwide on Wednesday to mark the International Day for the Elimination of Violence Against Women.
"Everyone deserves to feel safe in their home, in their community and in their relationships," Rechie Valdez, Canada's federal minister of women and gender equality, said in a statement. "Yet for too many women, girls and 2SLGBTQI+ people, that safety is still not guaranteed."
Here's a visual tour of some of the protests and marches worldwide. All photos are from Wednesday and commemorated the international day.
A man walks past a large mural bearing the inscription 'No More Femicide' on the side of a building to mark the day, also known as Orange Day, in Gelsenkirchen, Germany. (Martin Meissner/The Associated Press)
Activists from the Not One Less feminist group protest outside a conference hall in Rome where Catholic groups were holding a conference on birth rates. (Alessandra Tarantino/The Associated Press)
A woman dressed as a bride marches in Quito, Ecuador. (Dolores Ochoa/The Associated Press)
A woman holds a flare as she takes part in a protest in Bogota, Colombia. (Luisa Gonzalez/Reuters)
Women stage a street performance dressed in traditional dress and displaying the names of victims on their skirts, during a march against gender-based violence in El Alto, Bolivia. (Claudia Morales/Reuters)
A woman performs a traditional dance from northern Chile during a march in Barcelona, Spain. (Nacho Doce/Reuters)
Protesters scuffle with Turkish police officers during a demonstration in Istanbul. (Berk Ozkan/AFP/Getty Images)
Protesters dressed in black carry a mock coffin reading 'not one more' next to a mock tombstone reading '1,352 women killed since 2017, start of [Emmanuel] Macron's presidency,' during a demonstration in Bordeaux, France. (Philippe Lopez/AFP/Getty Images)
People take part in a ceremony at Niagara's regional headquarters in Thorold, Ont., near a flag calling for an end to gender-based violence. Similar ceremonies were held in 11 of the Niagara region's 12 cities and towns. (Paul Forsyth/CBC)
