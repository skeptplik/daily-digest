Dozens of photojournalists died while covering conflicts in Southeast Asia in the 1960s and 1970s, including several Japanese. Two became the subject of films: Kyoichi Sawada (1936-1970) with the 1997 documentary “Sawada: From Aomori to Vietnam” and Taizo Ichinose (1947-1973) with the 1999 biopic “One Step on a Mine, It’s All Over.”
Now, former NHK director Atsunori Kawamura has made “Battlefield Bohemian: Photojournalist Bunyo Ishikawa,” a documentary about a contemporary of Sawada and Ichinose who survived to tell his own tale.
“(Ishikawa’s) unique perspective was really appealing to me as a director,” Kawamura tells The Japan Times during an interview at K’s Cinema in Shinjuku, where the film opens on Nov. 28.
