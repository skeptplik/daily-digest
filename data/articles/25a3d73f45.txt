Lisa Rinna is living out a dream — “a dream that I didn’t even know I had,” she says.
The multihyphenate — actress, reality star, author, personality — is back on Broadway some 20 years after her last appearance, starring in “It’s a Wonderful Life” at the Broadhurst Theater alongside Andrew Rannells, Megan Hilty, Titus Burgess, and her husband, Harry Hamlin.
It’s a week out from opening night, and Rinna is sipping a hot tea in sweats in a Midtown restaurant, just before she’s due at the theater for rehearsals.
“It’s just a pinch-me moment, to be honest,” she says. “When I first talked to Jerry [Zaks, the director], I said, ‘Jerry, who came up with this casting? How do you cast Andrew, Megan, Titus, and then you think of myself and Harry?’ No one still has given me an answer, to be honest with you.”
The call for the production came at the end of August, when Rinna and her family were at their place in Muskoka in Ontario.
“My daughter had just gotten a Jet Ski there, and I wanted to go out on it. Everybody had been on the Jet Ski but me,” Rinna, 63, recalls. “So I went out and I was buzzing around and all of a sudden I see Harry on the dock [waving his hands]. And I was like, well, what’s wrong? Why is he calling me in? I mean, I’m out having a ball.”
She docked the Jet Ski as Hamlin handed her the phone, with both her acting and theater agents on the other line, who delivered the news that Zak wanted her for the show.
“I couldn’t believe it. I screamed, to be honest,” she says.
She said “yes” immediately.
“I love ‘It’s a Wonderful Life’ as a movie. We watch it every single year on Christmas Eve. The kids grew up watching it,” she explains. “So it wasn’t even a question. I said ‘yes’ and I didn’t quite understand the concept. You’re going to do a radio play of ‘It’s a Wonderful Life’? I didn’t even care. I just said ‘yes.’”
Prior to the call about “It’s a Wonderful Life,” Rinna’s return to the stage was already confirmed: In the new year, she’ll appear in the revival of Neil Simon’s “Rumors,” produced by the Obamas.
Rinna hadn’t been on Broadway since her turn as Roxie Hart in “Chicago” in 2007, but inspiration struck when she was in the audience of “The Rocky Horror Show.”
“I was thinking to myself, ‘I could maybe play the Juliette Lewis role. Wouldn’t that be fun?’” she says.
She rang her theater agent after the show to plant the seed, and soon after had a similar experience seeing “Oh, Mary!” She even filmed a TikTok about how she one day wanted to play Mary Todd Lincoln in the hit Broadway show.
“I think because I started to think about it, talk about it and manifest it, that’s when we got the call for ‘Rumors,’” she says. “I’m a big believer in manifestation and I was definitely thinking, talking and manifesting it.”
Rinna has loved theater ever since seeing “Rent” 30 years ago, which was “one of those life-changing theater moments.” Recent favorites include “Oh, Mary,” which she’s seen with both Cole Escola and her friend Bowen Yang in the lead, “Ragtime” and “MJ the Musical.”
“I’m so moved by theater. I just think it’s one of the greatest forms of art and expression,” she says. “I love live and I love an audience and I love the rehearsal process and the community of it and all that it takes. It’s so herculean to put a show together like that. And I also love that it’s live so that there’s no cuts, there’s no close-ups, it’s instant gratification.”
“It’s a Wonderful Life” will have the Hamlin/Rinna family in New York for the holidays for a change (they’re usually home in Beverly Hills with their model daughters Delilah Belle Hamlin and Amelia Gray Hamlin), but they’ll continue their traditions of going to the movies on Christmas Day and doing “those stupid pajama photos.”
“I’m excited about what being in New York the holidays will bring,” Rinna says. “I think there will be new traditions.”
Ahead, Rinna is keeping up her manifestation, with new goals including hosting “Saturday Night Live” and attending the Met Gala.
“I mean, I just feel like I’ve just begun again, which is really exciting,” Rinna says. “There’s so much that I would like to do.”
