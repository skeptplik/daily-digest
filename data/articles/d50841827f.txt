Ballon d’Or winner Harry Kane has signed a contract extension with Bayern Munich until June 2029, the Bundesliga club has announced.
“I feel right at home at FC Bayern,” Kane said in a statement announcing his extention with Bayern Munich on Thursday.
The England captain joined Bayern from Tottenham Hotspur in 2023 and has since helped the German side win two Bundesliga titles, the German Cup and two German Super Cups.
The 33-year-old striker has scored 156 goals in 161 appearances in all competitions for Bayern and capped his prolific spell in Munich by winning the Ballon d’Or last week.
He was the first Englishman to win the prestigious award since Michael Owen in 2001.
“I love the club, the fans and the city of Munich. We have a fantastic team and, in Vincent Kompany, a fantastic manager,” the star forward went on to add.
“It makes me very proud to be part of this club. My whole family is very happy here. I was keen to stay. I’m now looking forward to the future and I want to win many more titles with this great club.”
Kane has frequently been linked with a move back to England, where he remains 47 goals shy of Alan Shearer’s Premier League record of 260 goals.
However, the striker said in August that the Premier League no longer holds the same appeal for him as he is focused on his career in Germany.
“Since joining us in 2023, he has achieved extraordinary things with his goals, his influence on the game and as a role model both on and off the pitch,” Bayern CEO Jan-Christian Dreesen said.
“His contract extension is a commitment to FC Bayern and to a story that is far from over.”
Bayern did not disclose financial details of the agreement. However, German media reported that the extension is the most lucrative contract in the club’s history and part of a long-term commitment to the England captain.
Kane has finished as the Bundesliga’s top scorer in each of his seasons with Bayern and was named Germany’s Footballer of the Year in 2026.
He has also won the European Golden Shoe twice, awarded to Europe’s leading league goalscorer, and captained England to a third-place finish at this year’s World Cup.
