Streaming deals aren’t as plentiful as they used to be, but they’re still around if you know where to look. One that caught our eye is an offer for Apple TV for those with an Amazon account in the US. Apple’s streaming service usually costs $14.99 per month, but following this page will let you get it for $6.99 for your first six months (totaling $42 versus $90 without the deal). Amazon hasn’t shared how long this offer will be available for, so I’d strike on it sooner than later.
Amazon is knocking almost $50 off six months of Apple TV
Instead of paying $14.99 per month, you can pay just $7 for six months when you subscribe through Amazon.
The subscription includes exclusive access to Formula 1 races and Major League Soccer matches, not to mention a bunch of acclaimed shows like Severance, Silo, The Morning Show, Pachinko, Pluribus, Slow Horses, and many more. (Yo Gabba GabbaLand is amazing for kids who enjoy music.) The service also somewhat recently added movies made outside of Apple’s studios, including classics like Arrival, Dead Poets Society, and Anchorman, to name a few. While the service is technically free of ads, Apple advertises plenty of its own original programming as skippable preroll ahead of content.
