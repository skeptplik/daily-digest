Thu, 24 Sept, 11:00 pm, Final score, IAW 6/364, AAW 9/362
New South Wales hold on for thrilling Sheffield Shield draw at SCG against Queensland
In short:
New South Wales and Queensland shared a thrilling draw after four, rain-affected days at the SCG.
Kurtis Patterson batted out 122 balls in a superb rear-guard action as Queensland fell two wickets short of victory.
Leg spinner Mitch Swepson took 6-73 and 5-55 in a player of the match performance for Queensland.
