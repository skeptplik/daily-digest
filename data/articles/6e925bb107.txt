The Biggest MacBook Neo Discount Ever
The MacBook Neo was already cheap, so Apple hasn't been eager to give much in the way of price cuts—despite the laptop having been out for seven months. But finally, the MacBook Neo's price has dropped by $50, bringing it down to $649. That's a great deal, especially if you're looking for an affordable laptop that feels premium, with a metal chassis and quality display. The 8 GB of RAM is limiting, but if your needs aren't overly heavy, the MacBook Neo should do the trick—especially at this price.
An Excellent 15-Inch Laptop for $900
These days, you'll be hard-pressed to find a laptop for under $1,000 that doesn't have any major compromises. That's even more uncommon for a laptop with a larger screen. But the IdeaPad Slim 5x is just that. It's a 15-inch laptop with great performance, long-lasting battery life, lots of ports, a solid trackpad, and a high-resolution OLED screen. It even comes packed to the brim with 16 GB of RAM and 512 GB of storage. It's not as thin as the 15-inch MacBook Air, no, but then again, it's hundreds of dollars cheaper.
Microsoft Surface Laptop for $900
It might be well over two years old, but I still find myself recommending the Surface Laptop from 2024. The price is down to $900 right now, which is a good deal for these specs, performance, and battery life. You're limited to only the blue color option, but it's the best price I've seen on this laptop this year so far. My favorite feature will always be the 3:2 aspect ratio screen, which gives the display a taller shape for more vertical space to work with.
There is a newer version, which comes with the Snapdragon X2 Plus/Elite chips, but I've found that it's still quite expensive. The IdeaPad Slim 5x listed above is the better option if you're OK with a larger device, since it uses those next-generation Snapdragon chips (for less money). But if you're sold on the design of the Surface Laptop, this is the one to get.
HP OmniBook 5 14 for $750
With the MacBook Neo selling for $649, you might look at something like the HP OmniBook 5 with suspicion. Even on sale for $750, being $100 more than the MacBook Neo doesn't seems like a great position to be in for an entry-level Windows laptop. And while this laptop is on sale often, this is $100 cheaper than I've seen it over the past few weeks.
The HP OmniBook 5 is a special device, with its OLED display, very thin chassis, and 16 GB of RAM. It very much looks like a laptop that should cost hundreds more. Battery life is great too, thanks to the Snapdragon X chip inside. The 16 GB of RAM is still the main selling point here—if it was any less, I would recommend the XPS 13 in nearly every scenario (but it's not on sale right now). It's only real downfall is the touchpad. It feels frustratingly clumsy one to use, making precise movements difficult.
