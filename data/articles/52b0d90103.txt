Catch up with the World This Week with Gavin Lee. In just 45 minutes, make sense of seven extraordinary days in global affairs, as Gavin and his handpicked panel of four leading international correspondents unpack the stories shaping the world — and the questions behind the headlines. This week: Spain’s snap election; the presidential plotting unfolding over lunch at the Brasserie Concorde in Paris; Ukraine, Kushner and the ‘Kremlin Trap’; and the question hanging over Iran: whose side is time on? Joining Gavin are New World Magazine columnist Emma-Kate Symonds; Constant Méheut, The New York Times correspondent in Ukraine; Venetia Rainer, Daily Telegraph Foreign Editor and host of Iran: The Latest; and Sarah Morris, France 24’s correspondent in Spain.
