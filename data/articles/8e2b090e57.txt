The GX Pro 8100, like the SN8100 it replaces, is one of the fastest drives you can buy for your gaming PC. We tested the 8TB model earlier in the year and loved it: it's a top-tier drive with brilliant performance and good power efficiency. The Sandisk Optimus GX Pro 8100 2TB is the rebadged version of the trusty WD Black SN8100, with the same sequential read and write speeds of 14,900 MB/s and 14,000 MB/s each.
The discount matters. PCIe 5.0 SSDs, as our SSD price tracker will show you, command a price premium in the current market. The AI boom has forced prices for drives through the roof and beyond over the last 18 months, so a deal like this is one of the best ways to upgrade your storage without paying the extra premium. Specs-wise, you're getting a drive powered by a Silicon Motion SM2508 controller with dedicated DRAM cache for multitasking, paired with 218-layer SanDisk/Kioxia 3D TLC NAND and SanDisk's nCache 4.0 write buffer. To put that into context, you'd need to write to a third of this drive every single day for five years to wear out that condition on your warranty's five-year coverage.
This is the sort of drive you'd want if you want no bottlenecks, whether it's intensive AI workloads or in-game load times. A high-performance drive in the current market makes sense if the price is right, which makes the discount extra important.
