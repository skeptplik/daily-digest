Former Pakistan Prime Minister Imran Khan appealed to the country's Supreme Court in a bid to receive urgent medical attention for himself and his wife.
According to an open letter conveyed to the court through Khan's lawyer, the former leader's doctors had warned him of a risk for brain hemorrhage and cardiac arrest and that his blood pressure had risen sharply.
He also said his wife Bushra Bibi required further eye operation, and that she is at risk of losing her eyesight should she not be granted the surgery.
The letter was released to the public by Khan's Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf party and lawyer Salman Safdar.
"Today, I ask only that my wife and I be treated,” the letter reads, asking the judges to give him "justice" before it was too late.
"I will only remain able to prove my innocence if I stay alive," Khan wrote.
Government contests account
An earlier court order ordered Khan's transfer to a hospital in Pakistani capital Islamabad, with Khan alleging that the authorities had not implemented it.
However, cabinet minister Tariq Fazal Chaudhry said Khan was receiving medical care, while also receiving food and books of his liking.
According to Chaudhry, the former leader has been taken to medical examinations at a hospital in Islamabad eight times, with his health and blood pressure being found to be in order.
Still a popular figure
Despite being in prison since 2023 for corruption offenses, Khan is still considered a popular figure in Pakistan, with his party demanding his release. His wife is alo serving jail time for graft offenses.
Khan argues the charges against him are politically motivated, which the government denies.
The government, led by Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, also denied Khan was in solitary confinement. More than 900 visits to Khan have been registered since he started his jail time in 2023, according to the authorities.
In a previous case, Khan's attorney told the court that his client had lost about 85% of his right eye's vision. However, his sister later said his situation was improving.
