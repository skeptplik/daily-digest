Pat Cummins maintains Sandpapergate comments after Cameron Bancroft allegations
In short:
Australia Test cricket captain Pat Cummins has maintained he did not know sandpaper was being used on the cricket ball during the infamous Test match in Cape Town in 2018.
Cameron Bancroft, the player caught ball-tampering, said last night the entire team knew what he was doing.
What's next?
Cricket Australia has announced a forum for players and staff to discuss the Sandpapergate saga, but no firm details of how it will work have been announced.
