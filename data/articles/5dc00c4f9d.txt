Harman's portable speakers are stylish and well-built, and the Onyx Studio 8 is no exception. Its fabric-wrapped rounded profile and sleek anodized aluminum handle resembles a ringed planet. The main body houses two 120mm tweeters and a 200mm woofer with 50W of RMS power to deliver powerful, balanced stereo sound with excellent bass performance.
A wireless portable speaker doesn't need to be tethered to a home theater system or a power cable in order to work. Instead, the Onyx Studio 8 has an internal rechargeable battery inside that allows up to 8 hours of tetherless audio with USB Type-C recharging. It connects to your smartphone or music streaming device via Bluetooth.
Note that although the Onyx Studio 8 can be moved around from room to room, it's designed to be an indoor speaker, since it has no explicit water or dust resistance rating. It's also considerably larger than your typical outdoor speaker, but on the flip side, it looks nicer and the larger szie allows it to sound miles better.
