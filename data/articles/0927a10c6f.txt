China’s EV exports surge 145% as Iran war drives up overseas demand
China’s electric vehicle exports soared to a record high in October, as high petrol prices lead drivers around the world to go electric
Chinese brands sold a total of 582,000 pure electric and plug-in hybrid vehicles outside mainland China last month, a 144.7 per cent jump from a year earlier, according to data from the China Passenger Car Association (CPCA).
The figure – which broke the previous monthly record of 558,000 set in September – includes cars exported from China and EVs assembled by Chinese brands’ overseas plants.
