Manni L. Perez, the actress who plays Lucia in GTA 6, has issued her first statement since the game came out.
Perez’s role as Lucia Caminos was confirmed when she was nominated in the Best Performance category for this year’s The Game Awards, a day before GTA 6 launched on November 19. She’s now considered the favorite following the game’s critically acclaimed, record-breaking launch.
Now, a week later, Perez took to social media to thank fans for the reception to GTA 6.
“Life has been such a whirlwind these past couple of days and I just want to take a moment to say thank you,” an Instagram Stories post began.
“Thank you to all the new followers, to all the beautiful messages, and the positive reception the game and my character have received.
“I've dedicated almost a decade bringing Lucia to life. She's been such a significant part of mine and I am beyond grateful she now gets to be a small part of yours.”
“Happy Holiday's y'all.”
Perez is an actress with a smattering of credits to her acting resume, including TV guest roles in Jessica Jones, Chicago P.D., and Law & Order: Special Victims Unit. While she hasn't been up to much else over the past few years, it's easy to know why — GTA 6 sounds like it was a pretty involved project. Indeed, Lucia isn’t Perez's first GTA role. In GTA 5, she plays the background character of "Clubber #2," while in GTA Online she voices a blackjack dealer.
Meanwhile, Jason actor Dylan Rourke also posted a message of gratitude on Instagram Stories, saying: “Thankful for this wild ride. Gratitude won’t cut it. Make today about the ones you love.”
Manni L. Perez shouldn't be confused with Manny Perez, who plays Raul Bautista in GTA 6. This week, Manny Perez bought a PS5 just so he could play the game, saying in a video posted to social media: “Guy, I’m playing and this s**t is crazy! Look how real that looks. I’m learning, I’m loving this, it’s crazy.”
Other GTA 6 actors who've started to talk about their work on the game include Lexie Shoaibi, who recently spoke out about her "bittersweet" newfound fame after her character, Mandy, went viral online, and Cocoteo FM radio host Issa Lopez, who described the wild non-disclosure agreement she had to sign before working on the game.
