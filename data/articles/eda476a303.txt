Darktide already has a religious fanatic on deck in the Zealot class, but the upcoming Sister Novitiate will play differently to that melee-charging maniac. As Fatshark explains in its recent overview, "while the Zealot is more unbound fanaticism, the Sisters Novitiate brings a more ordered and militaristic feel to this aspect of the Imperium."
That includes some unique weapons. The Condemnor Stake Thrower is a semi-auto crossbow with a little of that Painkiller Stake Gun juice as it can "pin enemies to walls if they are close enough when hit." The Ministrorum Hand Flamer is a lighter version of the Zealot's Purgation Flamer with a special that fires at higher pressure for the cost of more fuel, the Penitent Eviscerator is a heavy chainsword, and the Mace of the Righteous is an incense-bearer that sets enemies on fire.
She also gets access to a Novitiate Sword and Shield, with the shield both blocking ranged attacks and having a bash attack, and can wield the Novitiate Sword by itself with its heavy attack working as a block. "If you played the Grail Knight career for Markus Kruber in Vermintide 2 and get a familiar feeling from using this," Fatshark says, "know that this was very intentional! Our way to pay homage."
Finally there's the Meltagun, a short-range armor cooker that's an iconic weapon in Warhammer 40,000 and will be available to the Veteran class as well as Sisters Novitiate. "Firing a ray of superheated energy that ignites the air," Fatshark says, "it's an extremely potent short range weapon."
Where other classes have blitz abilities, Sisters have Acts of Faith, and can choose two for each mission. They cost Faith to activate, which regenerates over time and can also be spent to empower some weapons. The headliner here is the Sanctification Grenade, which covers enemies in holy water that applies the Sanctification debuff, making tougher enemies take more damage.
Other Acts of Faith are the staggering Neural Whip, the ally-buffing Icon of Purity and Shield of Aversion, the enemy-debuffing Auto-Broadcaster, and finally the healing Chirurgeon's Tools. The healing they provide is only temporary, however, ticking away over time. Presumably that means they won't interfere with the Zealot's Martyrdom ability, which boosts them when they're injured.
The other thing marking the Sisters out will be their lack of access to Boltguns. While full-fledged Sisters of Battle typically wield bolters and wear power armor, the Sisters Novitiate are aspirants of the Adepta Sororitas who only get to take up holy arms as the final step in their training. I guess access to the holy hand grenade cupboard is another matter. The Sisters Novitiate DLC will be available from December 7.
