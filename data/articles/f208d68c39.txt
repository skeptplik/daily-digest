These are the stories making headlines in fashion on Friday.
Gap Inc. appointed Ashley Kechter, formerly Vuori’s global president, as brand president and CEO of Athleta, effective Dec. 14. Kechter previously led Fabletics and spent eight years early in her career at Gap Inc., including as chief business officer of Banana Republic. The move comes after Athleta’s comparable sales fell 15% in the third quarter (its eighth straight decline) and after its previous CEO, Maggie Gauger, left for Lululemon. Gap also lowered its full-year guidance, now expecting roughly flat net sales instead of 1% to 1.5% growth, as sales fell 3% at Banana Republic and were flat at Old Navy. {Gap Inc.; Gap Inc.; Bloomberg/paywalled}
Sportswear giant Nike has agreed to a multiyear deal to become Formula One’s official apparel and uniform supplier from 2027. F1 Academy, the series’ all-female driver pipeline, is also included in the deal. The first look comes this weekend, as a limited Las Vegas Grand Prix collection goes on sale at the F1 Hub and Nike’s Las Vegas stores ahead of the Saturday night race. {Reuters/paywalled}
Demand for wigs and hair extensions is rising as shoppers with thinning hair seek fixes. Many pieces cost more than £250 ($327), while wigs at the top of the market sell for tens of thousands. Additionally, sourcing is a growing concern as an ITV News investigation found some sellers pass off salon-floor and drain hair as premium product, and a BBC report said Indian temple hair donors often receive none of the proceeds. As a result, some brands are skipping human hair altogether, with plant-based and synthetic brands rising in popularity as alternatives. {Vogue Business/paywalled}
Swiss sportswear brand On is expanding its U.S. store fleet to 23 locations by the end of 2026, adding nine new stores this year, including three in October and one in December. With only 30% brand awareness in the U.S., On is counting on stores to showcase its apparel, accessories and expansion into sports beyond running, including football and, in 2027, golf. Dan Schade, On’s vice president and general manager of the Americas, said stores “play a huge awareness-building role” and often lift e-commerce and wholesale sales nearby. {Modern Retail/paywalled}
