In Malaysia, a shadow trade in DIY weight-loss jabs takes hold
Priced out of official prescriptions, Malaysians are buying unregulated slimming jabs online – with no guarantee of what’s inside
When her prescription for Mounjaro became too expensive, Bey* did what a growing number of Malaysians are doing: she found a cheaper vial online.
The 34-year-old from Kuala Lumpur started on the weight-loss drug about a year ago and hasn’t looked back since.
“I didn’t feel like eating, I didn’t feel hungry,” she said, recalling flying to Jakarta for a “food trip” recently during which she barely ate.
