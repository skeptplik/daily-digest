Ukraine live: US envoys Witkoff and Kushner arrive in Russia to meet Putin
US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner arrived in Russia for a meeting with President Vladimir Putin after two days of talks in Abu Dhabi, Russian news agencies said Thursday. When they leave Moscow, the US negotiators plan to fly to Ireland where they will meet with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky, due to attend a meeting of European leaders there. Follow our liveblog for the latest updates.
• Kyiv and Moscow agreed to a large-scale prisoner exchange as the final day of peace talks in Abu Dhabi came to an end, though neither side gave a concrete figure.
• NATO Secretary General Mark Rutte said the US-mediated Ukraine-Russia peace talks showed that the US, Ukraine and Europe were "aligned and united".
• A Russian strike on a busy market in Ukraine's southern Odesa region killed three people and injured 11 others, the regional governor said.
