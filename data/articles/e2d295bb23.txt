Jakarta wants Russia not to recruit Indonesians in its war. Should Prabowo call Putin?
Almost 300 Indonesians have reportedly been involved in combat in the war, including a taxi driver who was lured by ‘easy money’
On Sunday, Indonesia’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs spokeswoman Yvonne Mewengkang said: “The Indonesian government asked the Russian government to take all necessary steps to ensure that no Indonesians will be recruited into the Russian military or any other military-related position in Russia through any avenues.”
The request was expected to be implemented “effectively and immediately”, Mewengkang added, according to a report by state news agency Antara.
