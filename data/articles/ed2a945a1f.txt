Adelaide club baseballer loses sight in one eye in 'horrific freak accident'
In short:
Adelaide Angels Baseball Club member Tyler Forney was hit in the eye by a foul ball during a double-header against Woodville on Saturday.
Close friend and former teammate Chris Congdon said, as a result of that, Mr Forney had "permanently lost vision in his right eye".
What's next?
The local baseball community is continuing to rally around Mr Forney, and is helping to gather funds to cover his medical fees.
