Crocs and designer Simone Rocha are returning for the sixth and final installment of their popular and successful partnership. Their new drop, which arrives on Nov. 17, culminates a collaboration that added a high-fashion couture aesthetic to the casual footwear brand.
The shoe styles from the collection have featured romantic detailing and sculptural forms, elevating the Crocs‘ signature utilitarian comfort with the Irish designer’s signature romantic aesthetic, embellished with elements like pearls, crystals, bows, and translucent or platform designs.
	Those same elements will be present in the new collection, and have been added to three of Crocs’ iconic designs for one last time. The line will feature the Simone Rocha x Crocs Ballerina Platform, which will be available in both latte and black colorways. The style features pointe shoe-inspired accents, ribbon embossing and signature embellishments.

The Classic Lined Clog has been made more whimsical yet still appropriate for everyday wear. The silhouette features a plush shearling footbed for added warmth and luxury, while the vamp is decorated with crystals, jewels and gems. The Quick Trail Racer is the collection’s most utilitarian silhouette. The functional shoe received a high-fashion update with Rocha’s signature flair.
The first Crocs and Simone Rocha collaboration launched in April 2024. It was officially previewed during the Simone Rocha spring 2024 runway show at London Fashion Week in September 2023. The collection featured adorned Siren clogs, Classic clogs and Quick Trail shoes.
The initial launch sold out in just about 15 seconds and immediately drove high resale prices on reseller sites. Due to high demand, the partnership expanded across multiple chapters and drops, including a fifth collection in May. The sixth collection launches on Nov. 18 and will be available at select Simone Rocha locations and within the Crocs app. See more photos of the final collection below.
