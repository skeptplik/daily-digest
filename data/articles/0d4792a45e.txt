Chinese accessories brand Songmont founder Fu Song on Thursday shared photos with LVMH Louis Vuitton Moët Hennessy chairman and chief executive officer Bernard Arnault and his eldest son, Antoine Arnault, on her Xiaohongshu account.
The images dropped as Louis Vuitton staged a concert beside its boat-shaped store in Shanghai’s HKRI Taikoo Hui, triggering a fresh round of online speculation about an interest from the French group in Songmont.
Vuitton built a glass-domed concert hall next to its experiential “The Louis” store, to host a series of public-facing performances that start Friday.
On Thursday night, it kicked off the music rendezvous with Shanghai residents with an exclusive performance by house ambassador Yuja Wang and the Mahler Chamber Orchestra. Global brand ambassadors including Liu Yifei and Jackson Wang were in attendance alongside Vuitton CEO Pietro Beccari and LVMH China chairman Andrew Wu.
It’s understood that those images were taken in New York when the LVMH boss was in town to receive the John B. Fairchild Honor for lifetime achievement at the 2026 WWD Honors gala, and were also posted by Fu on WeChat on Nov. 11.
Arnault previously visited a Songmont store in Shanghai in 2025, prompting investment speculation at the time.
Fu, who was also invited to attend the concert, told WWD in a September interview that the company is cautious about outside involvement but open to evaluating opportunities.
Songmont, founded in 2013, draws on Chinese landscapes, architecture and craft traditions in its designs, which has recently extended into footwear. Fu described the brand’s approach as rooted in creating products the team genuinely likes rather than chasing trends.
