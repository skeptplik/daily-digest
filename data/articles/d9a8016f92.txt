The only answer we have right now is that Rockstar has not announced GTA 6 Online. But we all know it's totally going to have a multiplayer mode, right? GTAO has been a constant presence over the past decade and an important part of keeping GTA 5 relevant this whole time. It's successful enough that Rockstar ditched doing updates to RDO to focus on it, which I'm definitely not bitter about. So despite official word on the subject, a GTA 6 online mode does seem pretty inevitable.
GTA 6 online hasn't been announced… yet
Just like how Rockstar hasn't confirmed a GTA 6 PC release date, it's not unusual to be left waiting to hear about multiplayer. It is out of the ordinary that we've arrived at launch week without any confirmation of GTA 6 online, though. Rockstar announced the live service parts of both GTA 5 and RDR2 about a month before the singleplayer game release dates and launched the online portion about a month after.
Rumors about GTA 6 online
- July 2025: Rockstar was rumored to be testing 64-player servers for with a goal to hit 96 players for launch, which is a ton more than the 30 player max in current GTA Online lobbies
- September 2026: Twitch CEO Dan Clancy implied that GTA 6 online would launch in 2027 based on conversations the streaming platform had been in with Rockstar.
