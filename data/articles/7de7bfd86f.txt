In the version of this article initially published, the reagent information for the DNA2 inhibitor was incorrectly reported as DNA2i; Sigma-Aldrich, SML4192 and should instead have read DNA2i; NSC-105808. The change affects the Methods “Thymidine analogue incorporation” section and the Supplementary Table 7 reagents sheet. The error has been corrected in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
