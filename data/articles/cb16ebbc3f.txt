Vincent van Gogh's painting "Chestnut Trees in Bloom", has fetched a record €173 million at Sotheby's in New York, the most expensive sale for a work by the Dutch artist. It is the fourth most expensive artwork ever sold at auction.
A masterpiece by Vincent van Gogh has sold for a record-setting €173 million at an auction in New York.
The Dutch artist painted "Chataigniers en fleurs" (Chestnut Trees in Blossom) in 1890 in Auvers-sur-Oise, France.
The work dates from an extraordinary period of creative intensity that marked the final two months of van Gogh's life.
According to Sotheby's the sale was organised to settle a long-running row between the seller and the heirs of a previous Jewish owner who sold it in the Nazi era.
Influential German publisher, Samuel Fischer, acquired the painting in Paris in 1909 and his family sold it in 1936. His heirs had previously opposed the sale, saying Fischer's family parted with the painting only because of financial losses suffered under the Nazis.
The Blaquier family acquired the painting in 1975 and has owned it for more than half a century.
The heirs' Berlin-based lawyer, Gunnar Schnabel, had called on Sotheby's to prove the painting was sold at its market value and that the family would have sold it regardless of the political context.
But on Tuesday, Sotheby's said "an agreement has been reached between the Blaquier-Arrieta family and all of the legal descendants of Hedwig and Samuel Fischer regarding the sale.
The previous auction record for a painting by Van Gogh was €106.5 million for the 1888 "Orchard with Cypresses" at Christie's in New York in November 2022.
