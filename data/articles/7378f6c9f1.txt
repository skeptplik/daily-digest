Fukuoka – A 70-year-old man was fatally stabbed at a bicycle parking lot in the city of Fukuoka on Wednesday, police have said.
Yoshiaki Ishikawa, a worker for the parking lot, was found bleeding and unconscious at around 12:25 p.m. and was pronounced dead at a hospital later. A knife believed to have been used in the attack was found near the scene.
Fukuoka Prefectural Police arrested Norio Kawatani, 49, on Wednesday night on suspicion of assaulting a station attendant in Fukuoka. He is being investigated over possible involvement in the stabbing of Ishikawa.
Security camera footage showed a man believed to have stabbed Ishikawa running away inside a nearby shopping mall. The mall operator evacuated customers and employees.
