DIAMOND LIFE: Eugenie Niarchos, founder of fine jewelry label Venyx, finds beauty in the unusual, so for her debut diamond collection she’s focused on rocks with old-fashioned, unconventional cuts and shapes.
Next week the designer will debut First Light, a diamond collection that explores how light is “captured, transformed and revealed” through a stone’s geometry.
The collection showcases white and colored diamonds with old-style cuts set in 18-karat yellow or white gold. The designs come with brushed and polished surfaces, black rhodium and diamond pavé details meant to highlight the central stones’ shape and character.
Niarchos has always been fascinated with the natural universe, which is why there are references to “dawn, lunar phases and celestial alignments” throughout the collection.
The Refraction pendant is centered on a 3.57-carat, shield step-cut diamond; the Eclipse pendant features a 1.54-carat hexagon-cut diamond; and the asymmetrical Selene earrings showcase pear- and marquise-cut diamonds with different yellow tones.
The designer said she’s always worked with unusual cuts and types of stones, “so it felt natural to continue this into my first diamond collection. Sourcing them was a challenge but it was interesting, and enjoyable.”
She relished finding stones with “individual character, intriguing color and cuts that speak to you for their beauty, and not just for the technical 4C’s,” said Niarchos, referring to the diamond grading system that ranks color, cut, clarity and carat weight.
Niarchos said she was so taken with the unusual diamonds she came across for her various bespoke commissions that she started to collect them, and later worked the stones into First Light.
“The natural world has so much inspiration to offer, from the beauty of the Fibonacci sequence seen so perfectly in shells, to the beauty of how light refracts in diamonds. That’s what has inspired this collection,” she said.
The pieces are handmade in Venyx’s workshops in Italy or London, with colored gemstones and diamonds all hand-selected by Niarchos, for their shape, history and symbolism.
Niarchos added that while the new pieces may be special, she wants people to wear them every day.
She’s previewing the collection in London from Nov. 30 to Dec. 3, and from Dec. 7 to Dec. 10.
