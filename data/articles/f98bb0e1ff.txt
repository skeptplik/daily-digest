Spoilers follow for The Rings of Power Season 3, Episodes 5-6.
The Rings of Power Season 3 loses some forward momentum this week with two very talky episodes where several characters are literally waiting around for something to happen, whether it’s Sauron trying to pry secrets out of Celeborn or the rival Númenórean factions camped across from each other and poised for battle. The closing moments of Episode 306 definitely pick up the pace, though, promising an eventful finish to this season in next week’s final two episodes.
Gil-galad and Elrond’s mission to Númenór to coax Pharazôn into their war against Sauron sees them weighing their options, including the possibility of asking Miriel to rescind her claim to the throne. Ultimately, though, it’s Pharazôn himself who offers to cease hostilities with Miriel by proposing marriage to her. Ick.
That certainly sucks for Miriel – we saw how things went earlier this season for Pharazôn’s previous wife, Eärien – but, for the story, it seems like the only way to resolve this plodding subplot and finally get all the players on the battlefield against Sauron.
Elrond and Gil-galad haven’t been particularly compelling characters this season, but it was nice to see Elrond revisit his past and what his brother Elros meant to him and to the Númenóreans. Indeed, complicated feelings between loved ones and things that had long been left unsaid is a throughline in these episodes not only with Elrond and Elros but also with Galadriel and Celeborn and Durin and Disa as the latter two married couples wrestle with mistakes from the past.
Poor Marnûkh, meanwhile, learns there is a fate worse than death as Sauron slays his children and then leaves him to be tortured. Torture is also used on Celeborn after he’s captured, with the Elf lord getting the Giles Corey treatment. Ultimately, Celeborn is tricked into revealing to Sauron what the missing ingredients are needed to successfully forge the One Ring. That Galadriel-to-Sauron kiss with Celeborn will surely become a gif in no time.
Gandalf’s mission to help Pallando see the light sure didn’t work out well for him, did it? Pallando violently rebuffs Gandalf and sends him away strapped to the back of an oliphaunt. Although I know Gandalf hasn’t reached his full power and potential yet, it was still a bit disappointing to see the Dark Wizard make such quick work of him. I hope the storyline between them can be resolved this season but I suspect it will probably get dragged out into Season 4.
He’s only been a Ring-servant for like a weekend but Theo is already suffering from buyer’s remorse. Galadriel taking pity on him sure is big of her considering he killed Arondir, which makes me suspect Theo will be given a redemptive death in the season finale.
That wouldn’t shock me since this season seems intent on killing off original characters who were introduced in Season 1, whittling the cast down to just the core Tolkien-created characters as the war against Sauron picks up. By doing so, the show is signaling to audiences, “Don’t worry. We know many of you didn’t like these characters, so we’re just gonna make the show more like the movies now.”
