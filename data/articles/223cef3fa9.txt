Middle East live: Kuwait, Bahrain report drone and missile attacks
Kuwait said Wednesday it was countering hostile drone attacks, while Bahrain reported intercepting Iranian strikes, as US allies in the Gulf remained on alert following an American attack on Iran's Qeshm Island. The incidents came amid a standoff over the Strait of Hormuz. Follow our liveblog for the latest updates.
Yesterday's key developments:
• President Donald Trump said that US authorities take any possible threats to Washington "very seriously", following reports that Iran was planning to target senior Trump administration officials, possibly with a radioactive bomb. Tehran dismissed the reports as "fabricated".
• The Israeli parliament elected as new speaker Matan Kahana, an ally of Gadi Eisenkot, the party leader currently working to form the next government and replace current Israeli Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu.
• Photographs published by Iranian local media suggest that US forces may have used graphite bombs, also known as ‘blackout bombs’, against infrastructure in Iran in recent days.
(FRANCE 24 with Reuters, AP and AFP)
