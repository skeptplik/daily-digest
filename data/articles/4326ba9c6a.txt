STOCKHOLM – Having agreed to new peace talks with Ukrainian and American representatives earlier this month, Russian negotiators suddenly wanted to discuss the European security order, too.
It was an obvious attempt to divert attention from the urgent need, after almost five years, for a ceasefire in Russian President Vladimir Putin’s brutal war against Ukraine.
While the inept U.S. negotiating duo of Jared Kushner and Steve Witkoff seemed to go along with the Russian proposal, the Europeans saw right through it. They pointed out that there is already an agreed European security order, to which Russia is a signatory. The only problem is that Russia is blatantly violating it. That is what must be discussed.
