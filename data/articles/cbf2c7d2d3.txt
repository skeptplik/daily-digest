Frequently Asked Questions
When is Black Friday?
Officially, Black Friday is November 27, the day after Thanksgiving. However, Black Friday sales typically begin well before; I've seen some retailers just declaring November as Black Friday Month. Black Friday sales end on November 30, after which it's Cyber Monday.
However, I'd advise you to snap up these deals while you can, because they will go fast. I was eyeing up a Black Friday deal on one of the best smart telescopes, only to find it had sold out already! Don't risk missing out!
Which star projector should I buy?

Are star projectors scientifically accurate?
The Pococo Galaxy Star Projector is an accurate star projector, featuring images of actual space. You can also buy additional image discs to expand the Pococo's library. The Rossetta 3D Galaxy Star Projector is scientifically accurate to some extent; the video of the moon rotating, for example, matches the real moon. But others are computer-rendered images of planets and stellar phenomenon. And laser star projectors, breathtaking as they can be, usually don't reflect the real cosmos.
