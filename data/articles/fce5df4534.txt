Anthropologie has revealed the 2026 recipients of the YoungArts x Anthropologie “Leading With Creativity” Awards.
Now in its fourth year, the partnership provides emerging artists with professional mentorship and hands-on commercial experience, furthering Anthropologie’s commitment to arts education and empowering the next generation of creative voices.
Anthropologie will showcase and sell the five exclusive designs, available as made-to-order prints on anthropologie.com, starting Monday. Each recipient will receive an unrestricted grant of $10,000 to continue to pursue their creative endeavors. The five artists partnered with the Anthropologie Home team to develop an exclusive gallery wall art collection. Through virtual design and buying workshops, the artists collaborated with Anthropologie merchants and product developers to create the artwork. The resulting collection features one original commissioned design from each recipient.
The 2026 recipients are:
- Daniel J. Watts, a visual artist and tap dancer specializing in African-diasporic movement. Watts creates rhythm-driven abstract paintings by dancing directly onto the canvas with his feet.
- Demetri Burke: An Atlanta-based artist who creates works that serve as personal examinations of his past and present life.
- Isabel Oliva: A Miami-based artist who creates multifaceted textile works through a layering of drawing, painting and weaving.
- Sara Wagner Miller: A painter and photographer whose work focuses on intimate scenes of the people and places closest to her.
- Spencer Vazquez: A New York-based artist whose work explores the intersection of memory, digital and the everyday image.
“Collaborating alongside these five exceptional artists has been an inspiring journey for our Home team,” said Katherine Finder, chief merchandising officer at Anthropologie Home. “Through our design and buying workshops, we worked hand-in-hand to translate each artist’s unique mediums, from rhythm-driven movement and intricate textile weaving to intimate photography, into an exclusive gallery wall collection. By offering these commissioned works as accessible art pieces, we are able to preserve the integrity of each artist’s original vision while seamlessly inviting their distinct creative perspectives directly into our customers’ homes.”
Clive Chang, YoungArts president and chief executive officer, said, “Artists thrive when they have the freedom to create and the tools to turn that creativity into a lasting career. Our partnership with Anthropologie invests in emerging artists and gives them a unique way to connect with new audiences We’re grateful to Anthropologie for championing these artists and for furthering YoungArts’ mission of supporting them throughout their lives.”
As part of the brand’s continued support of YoungArts, Anthropologie will also invite customers to round up their in-store purchase across their more than 200 North American stores to support YoungArts throughout the holiday season.
YoungArts is the only national organization that champions artists throughout their lifetimes and across all art forms. Artists gain lifelong access to critical resources, including funding, incubation and presentation opportunities, mentorship and professional development, beginning with early recognition as YoungArts Award winners.
