'Out of control': ICE arrests at Seattle airport spark outcry
Issued on:
Share
A father and daughter detained by masked ICE officers on an airport jet bridge in Seattle has sparked a renewed outcry over the Trump administration’s immigration policies, with the governor of Washington state calling US federal immigration agents "out of control". Other passengers boarding the Alaska Airlines flight at Seattle-Tacoma International Airport on Saturday used their cellphones to film the father and daughter being detained by the officers, who were wearing unmarked jackets and khaki pants. The recordings were widely viewed on social media.
