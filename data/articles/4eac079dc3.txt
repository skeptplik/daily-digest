Chinese dad whose daughter died in crash visits junction daily to catch traffic violators
Wu calculates the dump truck was 92 per cent overloaded, carrying 2,000 times his elder daughter’s weight
A grieving father in southeastern China became a citizen watchdog after a collision killed his nine-year-old daughter.
On August 1, Wu Zhisheng’s wife was riding an electric bike with their two young daughters through an intersection in Jiangxi province when a turning tipper truck struck them.
Their elder daughter, Zijin, died at the scene, while her mother and five-year-old sister were injured.
