Some couples wouldn’t dare mix love and work, but Lisa Rinna and Harry Hamlin beg to differ. “I love working with Harry because he’s professional, hardworking, and he knows his lines,” Rinna tells Vogue. “But the best part of working with him is that I get to sleep with him, and it doesn’t cause a scandal!”
Last night, Rinna and Hamlin celebrated the opening night of It’s A Wonderful Life: A Live Radio Play on Broadway (she’s Violet; he’s Mr. Potter), joining Broadway legends Andrew Rannells, Megan Hilty, and Tituss Burgess on the call sheet. “Our family watches It’s A Wonderful Life every year on Christmas Eve. We love the movie so much, so when we got the call that Broadway legend Jerry Zaks was directing, it was a no-brainer,” Rinna says. “The first thing I asked Jerry when he cast us was: Who thought to put Broadway icons in a cast with Lisa Rinna and Harry Hamlin? He didn’t really have an answer, but he likes us all together.”
While she delights in sharing the stage with her husband, Rinna also enjoys the solitude of her backstage getting-ready routine. “I am someone who loves to have music playing, and I am a bit superstitious, so I have specific songs that I have to listen to before each show,” she says. On the It’s A Wonderful Life playlist? “While I am getting my wig put on, I listen to the La Bola Negra soundtrack. Then we go to Whitney Houston’s Greatest Hits—we listen to ‘Heartbreak Hotel’ and ‘It’s Not Right, But It’s Okay.’ Finally, right before the five-minute call, I listen to ‘Party’ by Troye Sivan.”
Rinna wanted to convey the holiday spirit through her premiere night look. “I had a vision of myself wearing a Willy Chavarria men’s suit,” she says. “[My stylist, Daniela Romero] found this one for me, and I’m absolutely in love with the color. She took my vision and added a nod to the holidays. I am a cunty Grinch, how perfect!” For the after-party, Rinna opted for a look from her longtime friend Sergio Hudson. “I wanted to wear American designers for opening night,” she says. “I am excited to have a piece of [Sergio] with me tonight.”
