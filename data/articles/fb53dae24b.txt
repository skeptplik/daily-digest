Why is putting trees on buildings controversial? What’s behind the current trend for food-oriented streetwear? And what’s wrong with Snøhetta’s remarkable new Shanghai Grand Opera House? Listen to the latest episode of Dezeen Weekly now.
In this week’s episode, Jennifer Hahn and Nat Barker dive into the debate surrounding one of 21st-century architecture’s major movements: buildings festooned with trees.
Then, they consider the design significance of a new hoodie created to help the wearer smuggle a burrito into the cinema.
And lastly, they reflect on the revelations that visitors to the Shanghai Grand Opera House must pay to access its dramatic spiral staircase and rooftop.
