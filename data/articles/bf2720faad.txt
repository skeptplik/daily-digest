Because there is no doubt your teen thinks they know better than you, we took them at their word and asked what they think makes the perfect gift for Christmas (or other religious festival they celebrate).
To ensure the selection was truly tried and tested, we asked them only about presents they have already received, love and use constantly. We also made sure to ask a broad range of teens – from book lovers to tech fiends and plenty in between – to try to represent the myriad tastes of this age group.
The best Christmas gifts for teens in 2026

Honestly, anything from Elf makes a perfect gift; I got a selection of products for my birthday, and there’s still so many I want to try.
I usually ask for Byoma as a gift too. I think it’s one of the best skincare brands out there for teenagers. I love how it’s designed for layering, and it’s easy to use.
