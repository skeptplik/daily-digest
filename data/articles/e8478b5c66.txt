Someone get Teyana Taylor on the race track. This weekend, the “Gonna Love Me” artist headed to Las Vegas for the Formula 1 Grand Prix—and she showed up looking ready to race, thanks to an on-theme deconstructed leather jacket from Alpinestars.
The Alpinestars label, best known for its motorsport equipment, has been creating protective essentials for racers for over 60 years now. With its new RSRV line, however, the company has expanded into ready-to-wear—blurring the lines between performance gear and everyday statement pieces.
For its Spring/Summer 2027 collection, Structures of Speed, this meant creating eight leather jackets made from authentic MotoGP crash suits that were actually worn by riders. The Crashed Leather Jackets, as they’re called, preserved real scuffs and signs of wear, and came with documents identifying the original riders.
Taylor’s piece featured a black base with blue patchwork, red details, and zipper accents. It carried her from the Ferrari garage—where she met up with All’s Fair costar-slash-F1 first lady Kim Kardashian, whose boyfriend, Lewis Hamilton, drives for the Scuderia Ferrari team—to the Raising Cane’s suite to watch the qualifying race, and finally to a SkinCeuticals activation.
The singer paired her boxy jacket with a black tee embellished with a red-and-orange flame motif. She grabbed oversize sunglasses, and for shoes, she went with thigh-high leather boots from Balenciaga—a house that has previously collaborated directly with Alpinestars. In addition to Kardashian—who wore Ferrari red for the occasion—Taylor was joined by the likes of Nina Dobrev and Adam Sandler.
