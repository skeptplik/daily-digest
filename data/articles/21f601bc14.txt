Ubisoft has launched Motherbrain, a dedicated subsidiary focused on developing proprietary generative AI technology for gameplay.
Announced in the firm's H1 financial results published yesterday, Motherbrain consists of the team behind Ubisoft's generative AI project Teammates, which began playtesting last year.
Ubisoft said the subsidiary is currently focused on developing "smart NPCs with memory and AI game masters that manage large-scale systemic worlds."
"[Motherbrain] aims to serve creators globally, both within Ubisoft’s Creative House ecosystem and beyond, and form partnerships with selected technology actors."
Ubisoft CEO Yves Guillemot added that Motherbrain aimed to "empower creators and open up new possibilities for richer, more immersive and engaging player experiences."
The launch of Motherbrain comes as Ubisoft laid off 166 staff at its Montreal and Quebec studios. Production and support roles were eliminated "to ensure our resources are aligned with current production priorities and business needs."
As GamesIndustry.biz understands, the cuts accounted for less than 4% of the studios' total headcount and included members of Vantage Studios.
"As our portfolio evolves, our production needs are changing, requiring us to adjust the size of some teams to ensure our resources are aligned with current production priorities and business needs," said Ubisoft.
