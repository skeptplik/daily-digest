In celebration of its 50th anniversary, Beams is partnering with Adidas and throwing it back to a time when loafers were just loafers.
The Beams x Adidas Loafer is essentially a classic shoe that just so happens to have two classic elements from Adidas uppers. While maintaining a traditional build from its upper to its sole, the loafer employs Adidas’ Three Stripes motif along with a mustache overlay channeling the Samba and Gazelle.
These add-ons stand out in white atop a black upper for the “Preppy” colorway, but they’re all but hidden on the all-black “Yankee” counterpart that uses pony hair for all parts of its upper. Both variants then get a healthy among of fringe with a kiltie and a pair of tassels sitting above the foot.

Adidas is no stranger to the sneaker loafer trend, having transformed its Handball Spezial with a variety of treatments that have included an array of animal prints. The brand has also left behind its athletic soles on several distinct takes on the Superstar made in collaboration with Clot.
For those not familiar with Beams, the department store and label played a prominent role in popularizing preppy American fashion in Japan. Its first store, in fact, was modeled after a UCLA dorm room — making Los Angeles a natural destination for its first permanent U.S. location that opened in 2025.
On the shoe front, Beams’ 50th anniversary series has featured most of the biggest players in the industry, including Crocs, Salomon, Red Wing, Ugg, Birkenstock, Converse, Hoka, Vans, Puma, New Balance and Timberland as a non-exhaustive list. Adidas was also involved in a September drop that turned the Campus 00s into a golf shoe.
The Beams x Adidas Loafer will first release Friday through a preorder at Beams’ Harajuku store before a traditional release Saturday at other locations and on its website. Pricing converts to roughly $250. Although no U.S. launch has been announced, a proxy service can get a pair to you Stateside.
