Russian ship attacked near Benghazi in suspected drone attack
Issued on: Modified:
Share
Authorities in divided Libya's east said a Russian ship docked at the Benghazi port was hit on Sunday in a suspected drone attack, causing a blast but no casualties. There was no immediate claim of responsibility for the strike, but it comes as Ukrainian forces have stepped up their long-range barrages against Russia far beyond the front lines.
