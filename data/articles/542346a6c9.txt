Gold Coast Suns AFLW player delisted just weeks out from giving birth
In short:
Gold Coast's AFLW side has delisted Georgia Clayden just weeks out from her giving birth.
Clayden spent this season on the club's inactive list and was informed on Tuesday that she would not be offered a contract for the 2027 season.
The club's Instagram account had posted in celebration of Clayden's pregnancy just weeks before her axing.
