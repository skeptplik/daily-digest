The MacBook Neo was released on March 11, 2026. This is the only MacBook that's equipped with the A18 Pro chip, the same processor found in the iPhone 16. Note that it still uses the macOS operating system, not iOS found on Apple's mobile devices. It's not as powerful as the M5 chip found in the MacBook Air, but as a result the Neo is nearly half the price. It's certainly capable enough to handle all of your daily tasks and it still supports AI and Apple Intelligence features. After all, no one's getting a MacBook for gaming anyway.
The MacBook Neo features a 13" 2408x1506 (219ppi) Liquid Retina display, Apple A18 Pro chip with 6-core CPU, 5-core GPU, and 16-core NPU, 8GB of unified RAM, and a 256GB SSD. It measures about 0.5" thin and weighs in at 2.7 pounds. The chassis is 90% aluminum. Connectivity options include two USB-C ports, headphone jack, Wi-Fi 6E, and bluetooth.
IGN's Jackie Thomas gave the MacBook Neo a glowing 9/10 review, stating that it is "without a doubt, the best budget laptop you can buy today." It's basically an iPhone in a laptop form factor, and really there's nothing wrong with that.
