Let’s face it—all too often, travel is not so glamorous. With flight cancellations, long TSA lines, and schlepping luggage, you may not feel chic throughout it all, but your travel look can still be. Peak holiday season is here, and some of the busiest travel days are still ahead; we drew inspiration from a few of our favorite celebrities to bring you the best travel outfits for women, making your next excursion all the more stylish.
Comfort is often top of mind, but that doesn’t mean you have to settle for just leggings. Katie Holmes showed how to wear a simple white button-down shirt and baggy patchwork jeans on a flight, adding comfortable soft loafers and a stylish houndstooth jacket—a cool spin on a relaxed travel outfit that still feels put together. Kendall Jenner went for something polished, wearing a silky cream matching set, while Naomi Watts sported a leather bomber jacket, T-shirt, wide-leg sweatpants, and bright-red sneakers, showing the many different directions you can take to fit your own personal style.
Ahead, shop the 9 best travel outfits for women that will ensure your journey is just as good as the looks you packed.
Simple Yet Polished
As Katie Holmes proves, you don’t always have to rely on leggings for the airport; instead, elevate your look with a white button-down shirt and baggy patchwork jeans. Styling those with a houndstooth jacket, gray cashmere knit, and soft leather loafers, you’ll look incredibly polished throughout your travel journey.
Matching Sets
Everyone knows matching sets are the easiest wardrobe choice when traveling, but take yours a step further with a silky cream version, as Kendall Jenner did. A sleeveless top and wide-leg pants instantly look stylish in the luxe fabric. Add classic accessories like a suede duffel bag, the Row’s leather flats, and a watch as small but impactful touches. For a little extra warmth, throw on your favorite wrap, cardigan, or coat.
On-the-Go Essentials
Sometimes your tried-and-true capsule wardrobe essentials are all you need. Naomi Watts paired a leather bomber jacket with a gray T-shirt and coordinating wide-leg sweatpants for a relaxed take. Bright-red sneakers add a playful touch, balancing the neutrals. From curbside to the arrivals gate, you’ll be comfortable throughout the duration of your trip, even after a red-eye.
Denim on Denim
The Canadian tuxedo works just as well on the street as it does in the air. Take inspiration from Isabelle Huppert’s chic attire, styling a denim button-down shirt with coordinating bootcut jeans (and a tonal handbag). The actress broke up all the blue with a khaki car coat and white sneakers—clean finishes with plenty of jet-set appeal.
French Flair
As a seasoned globe-trotter, Julianne Moore is constantly moving from one city to another and certainly knows how to look pulled together while doing so. Take notes from her minimalist look with a French twist. Layer a black suit with a striped Breton top, both easy foundation pieces you’re sure to wear throughout any journey. From there, keep accessories classic with optic white shoes and an oversize shopper.
Standbys on Call
When Alexa Chung steps out, we take notice. Here, she puts her signature spin on all the travel classics while traveling. A khaki trench coat, a lace-trimmed camisole, and straight-leg white jeans are all you need. By adding oversize black sunnies, leather slip-on shoes, and the iconic Boat and Tote bag by L.L.Bean, she brings a laid-back, cool sensibility to airport fashion.
All Black
Why make things overly complicated when travel already is? Priyanka Chopra shows how simplicity and monochrome dressing can be a winning outfit formula. Her straightforward tank top and loose wide-leg trousers demonstrate how comfort can be a priority without sacrificing style. Don’t forget to bring a long cashmere cardigan to ward off in-flight chills and an oversize travel tote bag to complete the look.
Trench Dressing
When you’re looking to travel with a little more style, look to Mikey Madison for inspiration. By using a trench coat as her focal piece, she creates an effortless travel look by layering a sweater underneath and pairing it with slinky black pants. Suede flat slides, a bright handbag, and a trustworthy carry-on create the perfect combination for your next getaway.
Clean Classics
Nothing beats a white T-shirt and blue jeans, the American style staples that just keep giving. Channel the ease of Margot Robbie’s airport outfit with this similar combination, accessorizing with comfortable ballet flats and a stylish flap bag for added flair. A clean leather belt and tortoise sunglasses are all you need to ace this look.
