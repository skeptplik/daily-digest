Tabletop gaming can be an expensive hobby if you're not careful. Some of the best modern board games can get pretty expensive, even with a Black Friday discount attached. If you're just looking to add some fun family games to your collection, however, it can be really quite affordable. Amazon has a number of Lightning Deals going on right now that drop prices well under $15 for some great games.
There's a nice mix of old and new board games getting discounts here. You've got classics like Clue, Candy Land, Connect 4, and Chutes and Ladders as well as some wild cards like Pigs on Trampolines and Taco Cat Goat Cheese Pizza. These are all games that the whole family can play, though many of them do lean more toward younger kids.
More Family Board Game Deal Recommendations
If you're willing to spend a little more, there are also some great family board games for under $25 right now as well. One of IGN's top picks for the category is Harmonies, which is a delightful habitat building game up to four players can play. We gave it an 8 out of 10 in our review, calling it a "satisfying blend of drafting and spatial puzzle-solving wrapped in a beautifully designed package."
For those more interested in trick-taking games, there's also a great deal on The Fellowship of the Ring version of the genre. We gave it an 8 out of 10 in our review, saying: Its simple rules and working together gameplay make it great for families, but its twists on the trick-taking formula and storytelling beats make it deserve a much wider audience."
