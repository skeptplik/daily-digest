Famous Footwear will have a new president early in the new year.
On Thursday, Caleres disclosed that Brian Costello will retire as division president of Famous Footwear in the first quarter of 2027 as part of a planned leadership transition.
In his place, David Law, currently general manager of Allen Edmonds, will succeed Costello as division president of Famous Footwear. Costello and Law will work closely together over the coming months to support an orderly transition.
Costello joined the St. Louis-based company in February 2025 as chief merchandising officer where he assumed responsibility for all buying and merchandising, including women’s, men’s and kids’ athletic and fashion footwear and accessories, and for the brand’s U.S. and Canada stores and e-commerce sites. He transitioned to the division president of Famous Footwear role in August 2025.

The executive has nearly 30 years of fashion retail leadership at Nordstrom and Macy’s, including leadership roles in the women’s shoe business at Nordstrom and all footwear and women’s accessories at Nordstrom Rack.
“I want to thank Brian for his leadership and progress strengthening the brand and merchandise assortment and the team at Famous Footwear,” Jay Schmidt, president and chief executive officer of Caleres, said in a statement. “David is a results-driven leader who brings an extensive retail background and a strong track record of success at Caleres to the role at Famous Footwear.”
As for Law, he joined Caleres in 2021 as senior vice president and general manager of Allen Edmonds, and in 2026 he also took on leadership of the company’s specialty stores center of expertise.
He brings over 25 years of footwear, retail and merchandising experience to the role. Prior to joining Caleres, he spent a decade at Lord & Taylor, where he worked his way up from buying to chief merchant. He also held leadership roles at Hudson’s Bay, PVH and Vida Shoes.
In conjunction with this announcement, Caleres is providing an update on third quarter earnings expectations. Results will be released on Dec. 3.
Caleres expects to report third quarter sales of $792.2 million and adjusted earnings per diluted share two cents below the low end of its previously issued guidance range of 62 cents to 70 cents.
By segment, Famous Footwear sales declined 3.3 percent with comparable store sales down 2.6 percent. Caleres’ brand portfolio division reported a net sales increase of 3.0 percent.
The company noted that based on current trends, it still expects to achieve its most recent annual adjusted earnings per diluted share guidance of $1.50 to $1.65.
The company will report its full third quarter earnings results on Dec. 3.
