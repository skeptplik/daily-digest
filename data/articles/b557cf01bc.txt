Rockstar has announced that GTA 6 achieved $3.79 billion in retail sales in its first day on sale, "setting a new record for entertainment across any medium, and becoming the biggest entertainment launch in history."
The game sold 32.5 million units pre-release, the firm said, which it claimed as a new record across all platforms. The revenue total comfortably exceeds the estimated development budget for the game, which was pegged at $2 billion by some reports. Neither Rockstar nor parent company Take-Two have revealed any development costs.
“It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI,” said Sam Houser, President and Founder of Rockstar Games, in a press release. “We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you.”
The revenue and sales tally comes in well ahead of the expectation of analysis firms. Earlier today Sensor Tower pegged the title at $2.36 billion in revenue from 24.5 million copies sold; last week Analyst Joost van Dreunen projected "opening-weekend sales above $2 billion".
Enders Analysis had previously estimated 30-35 million units sold in the game's first year, although it also highlighted a "breakout scenario" that would lead to sales in the 50-60 million range for the same period. That now seems a more plausible outcome, although the game's potential remains somewhat constrained by the elevated cost of consoles; the as-yet unconfirmed PC release will be a major opportunity for growth.
