Italian architect Carlo Ratti has been named the curator of next year’s Seoul Biennale of Architecture and Urbanism, for which he plans to take “an unconventional, AI-based” approach.
Taking place from 24 September 2027, the biennale will be used by Ratti as an opportunity to question the role of a curator today through the lens of architecture.
However, the event’s title, theme and full curatorial team will not be unveiled until 8 December.
“One assistant curator isn’t even human”
What Ratti has revealed so far is that he plans to take “an unconventional, AI-based” approach to the event.
Specifically, he will use an AI assistant, aiming to challenge traditional approaches to developing exhibitions by highlighting emerging tools that can help curators.
“I am delighted to take on the direction of the Seoul Biennale and to explore the future of cities together with the Seoul Metropolitan Government,” says Carlo Ratti.
“Seoul is perhaps the best place in the world to ask how artificial intelligence and the collective wisdom of its citizens might shape the city together,” he continued.
“At the upcoming pre-biennale event, we will begin to introduce the people who will join us, and I think there will be a few surprises – one assistant curator isn’t even human!”
Ratti is an Italian architect and engineer, and director of his eponymous studio Carlo Ratti Associati. He is also a professor at Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT).
Ratti succeeds Thomas Heatherwick
He was appointed by Seoul mayor Oh Se Hoon, succeeding British designer Thomas Heatherwick, who curated the 2025 event.
Heatherwick centred the event on a long, four-storey-high wall displaying images that reflected the ideals of his Humanise campaign.
The Seoul Biennale was established in 2017 by the Seoul Metropolitan Government as a festival of international architecture and urbanism.
“The Seoul Biennale is a place where architects and citizens from around the world think together about the future of cities and explore new possibilities,” said mayor Oh.
“Together with general director Carlo Ratti, who has connected architecture, cities, and technology, we will prepare so that the sixth Seoul Biennale offers a new perspective on cities and architecture and a meaningful experience for citizens.”
Ratti’s previous curatorial experience includes the Venice Architecture Biennale in 2025, for which the theme was Intelligens. Natural. Artificial. Collective. It explored how architecture can be adapted to increasingly unstable living environments through cross-disciplinary collaboration.
At the time, Ratti said, “Dezeen killed the traditional form of the biennale“.
The architect’s other recent projects include the French Pavilion at Expo 2025 Osaka and a proposal for a children’s hospital housed in a one-kilometre “green ring”.
