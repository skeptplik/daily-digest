Everything You Need to Know
What type of eyeliner is best for mature eyes?
“Because of its rich color payoff, a smooth gel or cream eyeliner pencil is a great choice for enhancing mature eyes,” says Wagener. “Eyeliner with a creamy texture glides on effortlessly, providing a defined line that won’t tug at delicate skin.” On the other hand, Wagener tells her mature clients to steer clear of most liquid felt-tip liners. “They can be less forgiving and very difficult to use,” she says.
What to Look for in an Eyeliner for Older Women
When it comes to choosing an eyeliner for women over 50, makeup artists keep a few key factors in mind.
- Water-Resistance: “Since so many women get either dry eyes or watery eyes as we age, my preference is always to use a water-resistant liner or a long-wearing application technique by layering shadow over the liner,” Patinkin says.
- Ease of Removal: While Patinkin recommends long-wearing or water-resistant formulas, “I stay away from most waterproof eyeliner products on mature skin since it’s easy to rub too hard when you’re removing them at the end of the day,” Patinkin says. “The last thing mature skin needs is all that tension and pressure on the delicate eyelid skin.”
- Texture: For mature lids in particular, makeup artists gravitate toward eyeliners with a smooth, creamy texture, such as those made with kajal or kohl. “Avoid overly dry pencils as they can skip or pull on delicate skin,” Perisco notes.
- Ingredients: Additionally, consider what’s inside the formula. Beyond the ingredients, keep in mind if the liner is hypoallergenic, cruelty-free, or paraben-free, as these factors can ensure it's safe for sensitive eyes.
- Tip Size: Eyes can become more hooded with age. According to Patinkin, a liner with an ultra-fine tip is ideal for this scenario, as it can help accentuate the eyes without taking up too much lid space.
- Color: There’s always room to play around with color, and artists gravitate toward different shades for their mature clients. For Perisco, it’s hard to beat shades like deep brown, bronze, or plum to enhance mature eyes. “These enhance eye color while keeping the overall look soft and elegant,” she says. Patinkin’s take? “I love a navy, grey, or plum liner for tightlining,” she says, reiterating her preference for cooler tones on older eyes. “A blue undertone makes the whites of the eyes pop and look nice and bright,” she says.
How should you apply eyeliner to mature eyes?
“Depending on the eye shape, I often line only the outer third or outer half of the upper lash line and then tightline the whole length of the lash line from underneath,” says Patinkin. “This is a great technique for lots of reasons.” First, she says, it contributes to a lifted look at the outer corner of the eyes, where the shadow is thicker. The technique also helps define the shape of the eyes without “eating up lid space,” she says. 
Tightlining is another technique makeup artists favor for mature eyes. “It makes the lashes look thicker because you’re filling in gaps between the roots of the lashes,” Patinkin says.
