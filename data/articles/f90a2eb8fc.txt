Zoë Kravitz has been going for gold with her recent string of red-carpet looks, all thanks to Anthony Vaccarello’s final collection for Saint Laurent.
On Tuesday evening, the actor and director attended the New York City premiere of her new heist comedy, How to Rob a Bank, where she turned heads in a lustrous, ab-baring set from the Saint Laurent Spring/Summer 2027 ready-to-wear collection.
The ensemble consisted of a cropped bolero jacket and a pair of straight-leg slacks, both of which were made from a gold, foil-like material with paisley designs embroidered all over.
Kravitz’s midriff-baring jacket was trimmed with chocolate fur at the wrists, while the neckline was decorated with a wreath of golden florals. To mirror the neck, her slightly-darker pants were cinched with a textured, studded belt.
To complete her monochromatic fit, Kravitz slipped into some golden sandals with curling heels and had her hair pulled into a messy top bun with baby strands dangling in front.
It was just last week that Kravitz pulled from this same collection, when she and fellow Saint Laurent muses Hailey Bieber and Rosé took the stage at the 2026 CFDA Fashion Awards to present Vaccarello with the International Designer of the Year award.
The prize served, in part, as a recognition of Vaccarello’s 10-year tenure at the French fashion house (he announced his impending departure early last month). And to commemorate the special moment, Kravitz and her co-presenters all dressed in looks from his final YSL offering.
The unforgettable collection was comprised of over 60 outfits, almost all in shimmering gold, that acted as the creative director’s glorious, gilded, swan song. It was presented in Paris, with a sparkling Eiffel Tower as its backdrop. No wonder Kravitz doesn’t want the moment to end.
