Singapore – To commemorate her visit to the country, Princess Aiko, the daughter of Emperor Naruhito and Empress Masako, had a new orchid variety named after her at a ceremony in Singapore on Thursday.
At the Singapore Botanic Gardens, Princess Aiko placed a nameplate in a pot containing the newly named orchid, “Dendrobium Aiko Naishinno Denka,” which bears white flowers with a faint purple tint.
After receiving the naming certificate, the princess posed for a commemorative photo with a smile and said, “It’s beautiful.”
She also viewed “Dendrobium Naruhito Kotaishi Denka” and “Dendrobium Masako Kotaishi Hidenka,” which were named to commemorate the 1993 marriage of her parents. The new variety shares the same lineage as the two orchids, according to Singapore.
Singapore has a tradition of “orchid diplomacy,” in which new orchid varieties are named after foreign dignitaries to welcome them to the country.
“Renantanda Akihito” and “Dendrobium Michiko” were named to commemorate a 1970 visit by Emperor Emeritus Akihito and Empress Emerita Michiko, Princess Aiko’s grandparents.
The botanic gardens opened in 1859 when Singapore was under British rule. It is registered as a UNESCO World Heritage site.
Princess Aiko was invited to visit by Singaporean President Tharman Shanmugaratnam as this year marks the 60th anniversary of diplomatic relations between Japan and Singapore.
