Marvel Comics may employ a lot of artists, but it's safe to say there's no one quite like Peach Momoko. Momoko has made a huge impression through her work on books like Demon Days and Ultimate X-Men, combining painted watercolors with a manga-influenced aesthetic. Momoko's unique style makes her a natural choice to join Clover Press' ever-growing series of Marvel art books.
With the Kickstarter campaign for The Marvel Art of Peach Momoko now live, IGN can exclusively reveal some of the posters included as optional bonuses. First, check out this gorgeous Daredevil cover image:
Next, there's Momoko's rendition of Laura Kinney as Wolverine:
The Marvel Art of Peach Momoko is a 200-page, 9" x 12" hardcover book featuring numerous illustrations and comic covers. The Kickstarter includes optional extras like these above posters, as well as 11” x 17” print portfolios, stickers, a puzzle, and autographed books.
“Stunning watercolors and gorgeous compositions set Momoko’s work apart from anything else in comics,” said Clover Press Publisher Hank Kanalz in a statement. “We’re excited to add her collection of work to our line of books and prints.”
The Kickstarter campaign for The Marvel Art of Peach Momoko is live now.
Clover Press previous Marvel art books have included The Marvel Art of Alex Maleev, The Marvel Art of David Mack, The Marvel Art of Russell Dauterman, and The Marvel Art of David Nakayama.
