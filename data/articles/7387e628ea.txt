While October saw only a few new releases, the launch of Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 significantly increased revenue for game launcher Call of Duty HQ, moving it to first place on Newzoo's PC charts and second on console within a week.
Apart from Call of Duty, annual sports titles continued to dominate. EA Sports FC 27 held first place on console for a second month, with NBA 2K27 in fourth and EA Sports Madden NFL 27 in fifth.
Newzoo noted that EA Sports FC 27 and NBA 2K27 are leading the January to October rankings for new games by revenue, despite only having launched in September.
Among new PC releases, five entered the Top 20 for revenue. Planet Zoo 3, Star Wars: Galactic Racer, and Aion 2 rounded out the Top 10, while Gears of War: E-Day and Phantom Blade Zero ranked No.17 and No.18 respectively.
On console, only Phantom Blade Zero (No.8) and Star Wars: Galactic Racer (No.9) reached the Top 10. Nintendo Switch Sports Resort, BeamNG.drive, and Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve were also in the Top 20.
Updates pushed three games into the Top 20 for monthly active users (MAU). Valve's invite-only playtest Deadlock was the only one to enter the Top 10 after its City Never Sleeps update.
Arc Raiders jumped 32 places to No.18 on the PC MAU charts and entered the Top 20 for console revenue, driven by the Frozen Tail 2.0 update and discounts.
For PC revenue, Cyberpunk 2077 climbed 51 places to No.13 after the Edgerunners 2 update on October 22. It also rose 22 places to No.19 on the MAU console charts and 20 places to No.19 on PC.
