The queen of Spain makes outfit repeating look so good. Today, Queen Letizia attended the 30th Ibero-American Summit 2026 held at the Royal Collections Gallery in Madrid, Spain, and for the occasion, she brought out a favorite dress of hers: a one-shoulder midi from Madrid-based brand Delpozo.
The navy dress with a bare shoulder, midi sleeves, and a pencil detail has the most elegant silhouette—one which is made even more chic by the large rosette that adorns the covered shoulder.
Letizia, who regularly supports the Spanish heritage label, has pulled the dress out several times before, including to the 50th anniversary celebration of Spanish sports newspaper AS in 2017, on a trip to Paris in 2018, and for the Princesa de Asturias Awards in 2020, at the peak of the COVID-19 pandemic.
She styled the midi with a different pointy pump, clutch, and elegant updo every time before, but today, she changed it up. Letizia let her hair down in glamorous waves, and instead of slipping into classic heels, she went for a pair of black patent-leather slingbacks with kitten heels.
For some extra pizzazz, she accessorized with her aquamarine-and-diamond drop earrings. The special pieces were commissioned by her husband, King Felipe VI (then Prince Felipe), from Joyería Aldao, and gifted to Letizia on the day of the birth of the couple’s second child, Infanta Sofía.
The queen paired her earrings with her striking diamond-and-sapphire bracelet—the one which was gifted to her by her in-laws, former King Juan Carlos I and Queen Sofia, upon her engagement to Felipe in 2003.
