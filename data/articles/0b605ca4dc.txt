Robert Pattinson was watching an upcoming horror movie when the news of his casting as the next James Bond was announced today.
IGN knows this thanks to filmmaker Robert Eggers, who directed Pattinson in The Lighthouse and just so happened to be at IGN’s Los Angeles studio today to do an interview about his next movie, Werwulf.
When I jokingly asked Eggers if he ever thought while watching Pattinson kill that seagull in The Lighthouse, “There goes the next James Bond,” he chuckled and responded:
“No, but I’m not surprised that it panned out the way that it did. And also, crazily enough, he was watching a screening of Werwulf when the news broke. And I was just texting with him about it this morning.”
So what did Pattinson think of Werwulf? “Oh, he loved it, of course,” Eggers responded with a grin.
