Hong Kong must build ‘reassuring’ environment amid political criticism: Paul Lam
Justice chief says city must stay focused on ‘practical work’ while dealing with inevitable criticism of national security cases
Hong Kong must focus on the “practical work” of building a safe and convenient environment and reassuring the international community amid criticism of its legal system, the justice minister has said, after 10 countries issued a joint statement decrying the latest jail sentence on opposition activist Joshua Wong Chi-fung.
“It is inevitable that their criticism will continue … Political realities have to be dealt with, but I do not think we should allow them to distract us from our focus,” Secretary for Justice Paul Lam Ting-kwok told a radio programme on Sunday.
“We must get practical work done, because concrete results are what will attract people to Hong Kong, to support us through action.”
