Beaver supermoon lights up skylines around the world – video
A stunning supermoon rose over skylines around the world. Supermoons occur when the full moon coincides with the moon being at or near its closest point to Earth, making it appear larger than usual. The November full moon is also known as the beaver moon, a name thought to date to when hunters traditionally set traps for beavers in autumn to secure a supply of warm fur for the winter
