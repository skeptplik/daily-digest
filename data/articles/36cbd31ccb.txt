Switch 2 Memory Card Deals
One of the most worthwhile Black Friday buys for new Nintendo Switch 2 owners is a memory card. Nintendo's latest console comes with just 256GB of onboard storage, which might sound like plenty at first, but it can disappear surprisingly quickly once you start downloading games. And if you're planning to reuse a MicroSD card from your original Switch, I've got some bad news: it won't work.
While MicroSD Express cards might look almost identical to the older format, they're considerably faster and are the only type of memory card compatible with Switch 2. Samsung's P9 range is one of the options we've tested and recommended, with transfer speeds reaching up to 800MB/s.
Thankfully, there are some pretty decent discounts on Switch 2-compatible memory cards this Black Friday, including Samsung's P9 range.
Amazon currently has the highly rated Samsung P9 256GB MicroSD Express Card down to $44.99 with free shipping. That's a further $3 off the price we saw earlier this month, and one of the better offers I've spotted recently. The 512GB version is also on sale for $99.99.
Are these the lowest prices we've ever seen? No. Can you find cheaper alternatives? Absolutely, including this 512GB option from Onn. However, if you'd rather stick with a memory card we've actually put through its paces and recommend, the Samsung P9 is a solid choice. Our Samsung P9 MicroSD Express review is worth a read if you want the full breakdown.
And there's a pretty good reason to consider expanding your storage sooner rather than later. Switch 2 games are getting increasingly chunky, with Final Fantasy 14 currently taking the crown at around 117GB. That's almost half of the console's entire onboard storage gone on a single game.
Other particularly hefty downloads include Final Fantasy VII Remake Intergrade at 90GB, Split Fiction at 70GB, Cyberpunk 2077 at 60GB, and Hyrule Warriors: Age of Imprisonment at 44GB. Even The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time comes in at around 27GB.
Put simply, downloading just three or four particularly large games could leave you scrambling for storage space. That's why I'd argue a decent MicroSD Express card is one of the more sensible Switch 2 accessories to pick up during this year's Black Friday sales.
