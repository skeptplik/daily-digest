Jack Clayton draws on AFL draft experience to chase Test cricket debut
In short:
Jack Clayton is edging towards Test contention after scoring 449 runs at 74.83 for Queensland in the Sheffield Shield this season.
Clayton believes his time in the Brisbane Lions academy and missing out on the AFL draft in 2017 helped him deal with the pressure of fighting for a baggy green.
What's next?
Australia will name its squad for the New Zealand Tests series on Saturday.
