Julian Klausner and Zane Li are among a new cohort of kings of color painting the runways in brights that kept us talking about color all summer long. Street stylers were on the same wavelength as they peacocked at the shows in September and October. Purple was one of the main trends of the season, but photographer Phil Oh collected a rainbow of hues in the crowds. Red has become a fashion neutral on everything from crewnecks to sexy dresses; bubble-gum pink was the go-to color for button-ups, while chartreuse added a savory accent to knits and wovens.
