France: Senior officials investigated over suspected leaks to far-right National Rally
Issued on: Modified:
Share
Two senior figures in France have been caught up in an investigation into suspected leaks to the far-right National Rally. The prefect of the Loiret and former top official at the Interior Ministry, Hugues Moutouh, has been removed from his post after being suspected of passing on confidential information about investigations. A deputy prosecutor in Paris, who was found at the party's headquarters during a police search, is now also under administrative inquiry
