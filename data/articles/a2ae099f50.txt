Amazon's Black Friday sale is a great time to find discounts on things you actually need. It's also a great time to buy expensive gifts for yourself that you absolutely don't need. Somewhere in between those two extremes is where I like to do most of my online window shopping, which is what led me to this discount on the official Lord of the Rings Pizza Wheel.
Amazon is currently offering a 20% discount, which drops the price down to $159.99 for the first time ever. It's a very expensive pizza cutter for sure, but it's also one of the coolest LOTR gifts for your kitchen you'll find anywhere.
As someone who makes a lot of pizzas at home, I can attest to how important it is to have a good pizza cutter at your disposal. If you don't have a sharp enough blade, toppings get crushed and pepperonis do that thing where they disappear into the cracks of every cut. You're only going to get good pizza slices if you have a knife capable of cutting good slices.
Dalstrong, the brand who makes this product, uses high-quality German X50CrMoV15 steel for their knives and pizza cutters. This gives them solid toughness and corrosion resistance that will help keep the blade sharper for longer. This type of steel isn't going to hold an edge nearly as long as the high-end Japanese variety, but it's fairly easy to sharpen and hone at home if the edge does get dull. Just don't go putting it in the dishwasher!
As for the LOTR stuff that actually makes this pizza cutter cool, there's a lot to love in the design. The handle is made of ebony wood and copper accents with the Leaf of Lórien carved into it. The blade itself is left bare to ensure drag-free cutting, but both sides include different designs etched into the steel. On one side you get silhouettes of the members of The Fellowship of the Ring and on the other side you get an inscription written in Elvish. I can't actually read Elvish, but I hope it just says pizza multiple times.
The pizza wheel also comes with a collector's pin and a leather sheath to help protect the blade. The whole thing ships in the kind of premium gift packaging that you'd expect from a nearly $200 pizza cutter.
