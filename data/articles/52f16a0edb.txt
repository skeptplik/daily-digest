If you preordered GTA 6 and head to the "store" page in the main menu, you'll see that you have a free month of GTA+, Rockstar's subscription service. If you didn't, you can subscribe for $7.99 a month.
Yep, weird as it sounds, there's a subscription option for a singleplayer game.
Subscribers to GTA+ for GTA Online get some decently sweet perks (and if you subscribe via GTA 6, you'll get them for GTAO too) like $500,000 of in-game cash every month, access to exclusive cars, multipliers on missions, discounts on in-game items, and more. But what does the GTA+ subscription get you in GTA 6?
Well, how does free rides on public transportation sound? Great, right? Can I sweeten the pot with 10% off haircuts? Huh? Huh? How about if your $10 scooter rental was comped. Are you in?
OK, you do also get a nice exclusive car and an extra garage to store it in. But there are already more than 500 vehicles in the game, so unless you're a diehard completionist, I don't think one more coupe is going to be missed, especially if you have to pay $7.99 to claim it.
And it's not like you're gonna show it off to anyone. This is a singleplayer game. Remember Bethesda's infamous horse armor DLC? Imagine that, but you keep paying for it month after month.
Here's a full breakdown of what GTA+ will get you for GTA 6:
- A 1990 Annis Venturas Blvd Elegy (a coupe)
- A single-car garage to store it in (or store other cars in)
- Free rides on public transportation (woo!)
- Free scooter, bike, and Seashark (jetski) rentals (gosh!)
- 10% more on owned cars you sell at Pay 'n' Spray
- 10% discount on clothing, tattoos, weapons, hairstyles, etc
- Two more vehicle delivery credits per day from Scooter Bros (car delivery app)
- Discounted fares on RydeMe (taxi/rideshare app)
I have no doubt that when (if) GTA 6 gets an online component, GTA+ will be more substantial, but right now it's not the meatiest offering I've ever seen for a monthly bill on a singleplayer game, unless you want those GTA Online perks, too.
It's a cool looking car, and I can't dispute that those are definitely bonuses. But midway through the game you're not exactly going to be sweating rideshare costs or how much you're charged for new hairstyles. For now, at least, you might want to save your real money for something better.
