Yuja Wang has played Carnegie Hall, the Salzburg Festival and the Hollywood Bowl. On Thursday night, she played inside a glowing glass globe next to a giant faux cruise ship in Shanghai.
The Beijing-born pianist, a Louis Vuitton ambassador, performed Brahms’s Piano Concerto No. 2 in B-flat major, Op. 83, on a stage built inside a luminous glass sphere erected beside The Louis, the luxury megabrand’s boat-shaped landmark at Taikoo Hui on Nanjing West Road. It was the first classical music performance hosted at The Louis.
Among the 300-plus guests in attendance were Hélène Mercier-Arnault, the wife of LVMH Louis Vuitton Moët Hennessy chairman and chief executive officer Bernard Arnault, Pietro Beccari, chairman and CEO of LVMH Fashion Group and Louis Vuitton, house ambassadors Jackson Wang, Liu Yifei, Ouyang Nana, and more.
Livestreamed on Louis Vuitton’s official social platforms, including WeChat, Xiaohongshu, and Weibo, state-owned media publications including CCTV.com and The Paper, and QQ Music, the viewership of the 1.5-hour concert reached 160 million at the time of publication.
As the clock struck seven, Yuja Wang strode on stage with her signature power walk, before joining the Mahler Chamber Orchestra for the one-hour long concert. Her spirited performance, marked by high-energy flourishes and fierce passagework, captivated the audience, while her emerald green minidress and Christian Louboutin stilettos, lent the pianist a distinctly rock-star edge.
Returning to the stage five times — she has previously held a record of 13 — Wang’s encore repertoire, with scores including Dance of the Four Swans Pas de Quatre arranged by Earl Wild, Maurice Ravel’s La Valse, and Vladimir Horowitz’s Variations on the Gypsie Theme from Carmen, showcased the pianist’s supreme virtuosity.
On Friday, Louis Vuitton’s glass globe will host a day of classical music from noon to 7:30 p.m. as a part of its “The Louis – Music for the City” program. The lineup includes over a dozen local young musicians and professional artists. To sign up for a free slot, visitors can visit the “MyLV” mini program on the WeChat app.
