Kazem Gharibabadi, Iran’s deputy foreign minister for legal and international affairs, says Tehran “will not wait for the US to attack first” and suggests Washington might be buying time through diplomacy to prepare for another attack.
The US Embassy in Bahrain issued a security alert for its citizens in the country, warning them of the risk of attacks from Iran and the Houthis. The alert came after Manama said its air defences had repelled Iranian attacks it branded as being part of Tehran’s “systematic aggression“.
