Black Friday is next week, but this weekend is LEGO Insiders weekend. That’s a special event where anyone who has a free LEGO Insiders account can access exclusive promotions (you can become an Insider here for free). This weekend, November 21 - 22, LEGO Insiders can get exclusive discounts (including on Nike LEGO sets), plus up to 4x Insiders points, and free LEGO sets as Gifts With Purchase, which aren’t available to anyone else.
LEGO Insiders Weekend Deals
In addition to all that, you can get 10% off by entering promo code INSIDERS10 at checkout. That’s a decent reason to check out the weekend promotion if you’ve had your eye on a LEGO Store-exclusive set.
The deals are live now, with discounts on some sets that almost never get discounts otherwise. The LEGO Maersk Dual-Fuel Container Vessel is an awesome set, IMO, and it's on sale for 20% off. So are the LEGO Nike collabs. You can see all of the discounts and promotions here.
All of the sets that aren't discounted on that page have 4x loyalty points, which helps you rack up lots of options for freebies. LEGO Insiders accrue loyalty points with every purchase on the LEGO Store. You can spend these points at any time on the Rewards page. You can spend them on things like art prints, exclusive LEGO sets, hats, keychains, and more. Insiders can also get some free digital items and enter sweepstakes for bigger-ticket items on this page for zero points.
Two new sets are exclusive Gifts With Purchase over Insiders Weekend. These are LEGO Retro Jukebox (free with orders of $250 or more) and LEGO Winter Snow Globe (free with orders of $170 or more).
All of these promotions are exclusive to the official LEGO Store. If you’re a LEGO fan, it’s a good idea to sign up to be an Insider, because 1.) it’s free, and 2.) many of the most sought-after sets are exclusive to the LEGO Store, so you’ll probably be shopping there anyway. Might as well stock up on some free Insiders points to spend on whatever rewards catch your eye.
Black Friday 2026 Set: LEGO Ideas Godzilla
Among the LEGO Store-exclusive sets is this year’s Black Friday LEGO set. Each year on Black Friday, LEGO releases a major new set exclusively on the LEGO Store. This year it’s LEGO Ideas Godzilla, a towering recreation of the King of Monsters that looks pretty cool if you ask me (we’ll have a review of the set at launch).
