If you’ve found yourself torn between square and oval nails this winter, consider the celebrity-approved “squoval” shape. Practical and elegant, the hybrid silhouette has grown in popularity thanks to its effortless look that elongates the nails while staying durable for everyday wear.
Most recently, Zendaya, Penélope Cruz, and Florence Pugh were spotted at the 17th annual Governors Awards, all opting for a soft milky nude finish in the flattering nail shape. "What makes this manicure so beautiful is its simplicity. The long squoval shape paired with a soft, sheer pink polish creates a really understated look," says Susan Carroll, founder of Notting Hill beauty destination Young LDN. "We’re seeing a continued move towards refined, natural-looking manicures that feel luxurious without needing intricate nail art. This is the perfect example of that quiet luxury aesthetic, where the shape, finish, and attention to detail really do all the talking."
To recreate the look, Carroll recommends asking your nail technician for a long squoval shape with softly rounded corners. "The key is ensuring the sides remain relatively straight while the edges are gently softened. Regular cuticle oil and maintenance appointments are essential, particularly with longer nails, to keep the manicure looking healthy, elegant, and perfectly shaped," she adds.
And though celebrities have been leaning on glossy, sheer nude finishes, plenty of hues and nail designs are great for experimenting with the silhouette. Ahead, five ideas to bookmark for your next appointment.
