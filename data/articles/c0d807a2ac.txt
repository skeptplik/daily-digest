Australia batting coach Graeme Hick weighs in on Usman Khawaja sandpaper story
In short:
Australia's batting coach from the 2018 Test tour of South Africa, Graeme Hick, has labelled claims about ball tampering in Usman Khawaja's new book "absolutely false".
Khawaja's friend and former opening partner, David Warner, replied on social media to take shots at Hick as not being popular with the playing group.
Warner has previously backed Khawaja's claims that the ball tampering was more widely known than just the banned trio Warner, Steve Smith and Cameron Bancroft.
