An explosion near a middle school on Saturday in Afghanistan's eastern Kunar province killed at least seven students and injured nearly 100 others, according to the Interior Ministry.
Early reports cited by the ministry's spokesperson indicate the blast was caused by negligent storage of explosives at a depot near the school belonging to a mining company in the Andir Sir area of the provincial capital, Asadabad.
No further details were provided on what caused the explosives to ignite.
Earlier, Kunar Provincial Police Headquarters spokesperson Farid Dehqan said the explosion occurred at around 10 a.m.
Unverified video footage posted on social media showed injured children being taken to the provincial hospital.
Samiulhaq Haqbayan, provincial director for information, told Germany's DPA news agency that the explosives were intended for use in mining operations and for clearing hazardous rocks from mountain slopes along the nearby Kunar-Nuristan highway.
Third blast near a school this week
The ministry said an investigation into the incident is ongoing and further details will be announced once it is completed.
Saturday's incident is the third explosion near educational facilities in Afghanistan in the past week.
Two separate blasts near religious schools in Kapisa and Farah provinces killed a total of five students, with local officials attributing both incidents to unexploded ordinances left behind from previous conflicts.
