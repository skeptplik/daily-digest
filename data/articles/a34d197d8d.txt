Chinese delivery driver divorces American teacher he married 4 months after love confession
Lifestyle differences heightened the strain, with Liu favouring northeastern Chinese dishes while Harris preferred pizza and sweets
A food delivery driver from northeastern China, whose whirlwind marriage to an American teacher captivated social media, has announced their divorce.
Liu Hao, 28, from Liaoning province, met Hannah Harris, 31, an English teacher from Alabama, in November 2024 after delivering food to her flat.
Charmed by Liu, who greeted her in the lift with the simple English phrase “Hello, I love you,” Harris soon bonded with him over their shared love of food, sport and pets.
