GTA 6 has already set records for critical response and streaming audience, even before formally releasing in much of the globe including Europe and the USA.
The review embargo lifted the moment the game became available in New Zealand, at midnight local time on the 19th November and 11am on the 18th November. It was rapidly streamed by hundreds of creators worldwide, exploiting a widely-known exploit that enabled them to set the Xbox system clock to New Zealand time.
The game hit 6.38M concurrent streaming viewers 90 minutes after release, according to data from monitoring service Streams Charts, over three times the previous record of 1.74M for the closed beta of Riot's Valorant in the pandemic heights of 2020. YouTube lead with 2.66M viewers, with 2.5M on Twitch and 1.31M on Kick. That figure continued to climb over the course of the day.
Ahead of release the game had been made widely available to reviewers globally, resulting in 167 reviews listed on long-standing aggregator Metacritic within the first few hours of the embargo lifting. The majority of these were perfect scores, delivering a "Universal Acclaim" rating of 99 that is listed at the top of the site's list of the best games of all time, ahead of the original 1998 release of Legend of Zelda: The Ocarina of Time which has a score of 99 from only 22 reviews.
The game's availability to press had previously enabled its inclusion in the nominations for The Game Awards, which were announced yesterday and in which it is listed for eight categories including Best Game Direction, Art Direction, Narrative, Score and Music, Audio Design, Performance, and Action/Adventure. It was selected by "a global jury of over 160 media publications and creator outlets."
