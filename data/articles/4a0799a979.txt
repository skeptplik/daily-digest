Watch: Truck hangs in the balance after losing control in wet conditions
A semi-truck carrying mail was left straddling an off-ramp and an overpass in Toledo, Ohio on Thanksgiving Day during severe weather in the US.
No injuries were reported, and authorities were able to pull the truck back onto the road, according to the BBC's US partner, CBS News.
The Ohio Department of Transportation described it as a "very unusual sight" before thanking emergency crews for helping "even on the holidays, even when it's cold and rainy".
