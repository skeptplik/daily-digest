A truck was left dangling between two elevated roads after losing control on a highway ramp in Toledo, Ohio.
CCTV footage released by the Ohio Department of Transportation shows the container truck skidding across the gap between an off-ramp from northbound Interstate 75 and an elevated ramp leading to the eastbound Anthony Wayne Trail.
The incident happened on the afternoon of 26 November. Authorities later managed to pull the truck back onto the road before towing it away.
