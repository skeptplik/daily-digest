On Friday night, Jessica Alba attended the Baby2Baby Gala at the Pacific Design Center in Los Angeles. For the evening, Alba wore a black evening gown from Brandon Maxwell Ready to Wear Fall/Winter 2026 collection.
Alba’s look was fittingly formal for the occasion, a classic little black dress with a slight twist. The dress featured a floor length column silhouette and a straight hemline falling just above Alba’s ankles. The architectural bodice is where the design got creative. Alba’s dress had a plunging v-neckline stretching down and stopping just above the ribcage.
The neckline was flanked by flared, high-standing and slightly asymmetrical lapels that folded downward to create a cap-sleeve effect. Alba accessorized with multiple bangled bracelets on her left wrist, statement diamond rings on both hands, and a pair of huggie diamond hoop earrings.
Alba’s appearance at the Baby2Baby gala is no surprise given the actress’s entrepreneurial turn over the past few years, many of her endeavors focusing on the well-being of children. In 2022, she founded The Honest Company, which provides parents with baby products with less synthetic materials. In conjunction with The Honest Company, Alba hosts the Roku makeover show “Honest Reservations.” Back in 2024, Alba promoted the show on “Today” in an outfit that, much like her Friday night attire, made use of interesting sleeves. For the appearance, Alba wore a floral-patterned Sabina Bilenko dress in a light blue shade with a beaded, plunging neckline and short puff sleeves.
The Baby2Baby Gala is an annual event to raise money for Baby2Baby, a nonprofit that brings vital supplies such as diapers and clothing to more than a million children living in poverty across the United States. The gala features catering from top Los Angeles chefs, high-end auctions, and a lineup of uplifting entertainment that promotes the organization’s mission. Each year, one celebrity mother receives the Giving Tree Award. This year, the award went to Demi Moore. Past recipients have included Drew Barrymore, Vanessa Bryant, Gwyneth Paltrow, and Serena Williams.
