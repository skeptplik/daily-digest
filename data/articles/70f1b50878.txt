If you’re making a list and checking it twice, then don’t forget to add in some of the travel gift options from our round-up.
From packing to itineraries, travellers just love a list. And at Christmas what better than making a list of all the nice things you’d like to receive as a present?
In our round-up below, we’ve picked out some great gift options for travellers. Though to be totally honest with you, the items are really what ***we’d*** like to receive rather than what we’d like to give.
Here are the travel gifts to add to the wish list this Christmas.
The Luxury Collection x Laila Gohar Advent Calendar
A gift for the Christmas countdown, The Luxury Collection has released a limited-edition advent calendar created in partnership with artist and designer Laila Gohar. Inspired by 24 locations across the Marriott brand’s global portfolio, the calendar pairs original illustrations by Joana Avillez with bespoke chocolates by Casa Bosques. Price: $218 (€199.15)
Little Worlds Alpine Ski Village Advent Calendar
We’re tempted to buy this ski-themed advent calendar for ourselves. It features 24 Alpine ski village ornaments, including skiers, chalets, and ski lifts, which can either be hung on a Christmas tree or used to create a mini winter village display. Price: £5.19 (€6.21)
Envami Scratch World Map
Forget ticking off the destinations from your bucket list, why not scratch them off instead? With this world map that’s exactly what you can do once you’ve been to a place. The map features icons and landmarks from around the world and comes in gold, rose gold, and silver. Price: £16.99 (€20.30)
Jastown Adventure Archive Box
If you’re anything like us, then you probably still have all your ticket stubs, postcards and general keepsakes from an unforgettable adventure. Now, instead of keeping them all in a forgotten drawer, display them in this archive box, which can sit on a table or hang on a wall. Price: £16.99 (€20.30)
Grupo Erik Travel Wall Calendar 2027
As readers of Euronews Travel you of course don’t need inspiration from elsewhere for where to go on holiday in 2027. But, if you actually do, then this calendar could also help you decide on your next travel adventure. Price: £8.99 (€10.74)
A&K Sanctuary x Globe-Trotter Sundowner Trunk
Crafting cases by hand in England since 1897, Globe-Trotter has a range of stylish carry-ons, totes, trunks and gifts on its website. One item that really took our fancy, however, is the Sundowner Trunk, created by Globe-Trotter to mark the opening of Abercrombie & Kent’s Kitirua Plains Lodge, an A&K Sanctuary in Kenya’s Amboseli region. Available to order by commission only, the bespoke portable bar gives a nod to a cherished safari tradition, “unwinding at sunset and soaking in memories of an incredible game drive with an ice-cold drink”. Price: £18,995 (€22,682)
The Little Shop by Raffles
Luxury hospitality brands don’t just offer a place to stay – you can also buy their fashion and design collections. The latest company to join the hotel-retail crossover is Raffles Hotels & Resorts, which recently launched The Little Shop by Raffles. Inspired by Raffles Singapore's 1940s boutique, the shop has a bespoke ready-to-wear collection and limited-edition partnerships. Items include Frette slippers, Christofle coffee cups, Globe-Trotter carry-on cases, Orlebar Brown swim trunks, Vanessa Sposi swimwear, Amambaih bags, Z Lab sleep masks, Smythson leather goods, and Ginori 1735 tableware.
Art of Travel - France: In the Footsteps of the Great Artists
The second volume in the “Art of Travel” series written by Nick Trend and illustrated by Cassandre Montoriol, “France: In the Footsteps of the Great Artists” invites readers to experience the cultural hot spots of France, with its best-loved artists as tour guides. With 12 chapters, each devoted to an artist or group of artists, the book has accompanying itineraries and bespoke illustrations. Price: £50 (€59.71)
Apple AirTag 2nd generation
Once you’ve bought your swanky new suitcase or carry-on, then you need to know of its whereabouts. The Apple AirTag makes it easy to keep tabs on your luggage as it makes its way through the airport or if it gets left behind. Either pop the tag into your suitcase as it is or buy the keyring case to clip it to something. Check your airline’s rules before putting an AirTag in checked luggage. Price: £29.00 (€34.65)
Airplane Pockets Tray Cover
If you like to keep all of your things to hand on a long journey to avoid disturbing other passengers by getting up and down for your bag, this gadget from Airplane Pockets is for you. It’s so simple, yet so ingenious. The polyester sleeve effortlessly slips onto your tray table, providing a clean surface to work and eat on and various pockets underneath to keep your magazines, phone, water, chargers and snacks organised. Price: £34.41 (€41.09)
The Big Blue 28W Solar Charger
One for the adventurers, this solar charger is essential for keeping in touch after hours hiking on the hills. When your gadgets need a boost, this panel will deliver a substantial charge in a variety of conditions. The panel is simple, with two USB ports and a classic fold-out design. Price: £89.99 (€107.40)
Language courses with Gymglish
If you’re travelling to a new destination then there’s nothing better than learning a bit of the lingo before you go. Gymglish offers a range of courses which are made to explore a language and its culture – and there’s “humour included”. Every lesson is made for the user: It goes back over your past mistakes, helps you memorise new vocabulary, continues your series and tests what you've learned. Two months of courses offers up to 43 lessons. Price: from €57
Perilogics universal phone mount
Whether you’re in a car, bus, train or plane, this little gadget enables you to watch a film on your phone hands-free. Simply clamp it to the back of a seat or tray table and relax. No more craning your neck staring down at your phone. Price: $13.99 (€12.79)
Charmast Mini Power Bank
Power banks can be clunky and eat into your hand luggage allowance. So when we saw Charmast’s mini pocket of power (which actually fits into your pocket), we just had to try it for ourselves. Being cordless, it’s easy and convenient to continue using your device as it charges. At just 90g, it won’t weigh you down if you’re taking a holiday selfie. Charmast offers three colours: black, pink and white and boasts speedy charging. Note, this model is only compatible with USB-C devices but there are other brands on the market which cater to different port sizes. Power banks must travel in cabin baggage. Price: £14.99 (€17.90)
Lonely Planet’s Where to Cheat on Your Cat
Described as the "ultimate travel guide for cat lovers", Lonely Planet’s new book highlights destinations where travellers can experience the charm, character and cultural significance of cats across the globe. Price: £13.99 (€16.70)
The Bon Voyage Traveler Kit
An easy choice for a stocking filler, the Bon Voyage Traveler Kit is ideal for anyone about to embark on a journey. The unique novelty kit is filled with handpicked items. Price: £3.99 (€4.76)
