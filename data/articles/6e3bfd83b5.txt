Cop31 failed. What happens now? - video
The longest ever Cop climate talks have ended in failure after countries were unable to agree a deal. The key sticking point was the omission of any mention of fossil fuels in the final draft. To find out how it all went so wrong and what could come next, Madeleine Finlay speaks to Guardian environment editor Fiona Harvey who has been in Antalya, Turkey covering the talks.
