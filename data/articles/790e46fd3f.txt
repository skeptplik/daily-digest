There's no doubt that 2026 has been a great year for Illumination. It had one of the biggest releases of the year with The Super Mario Galaxy Movie in April, earning over $1 billion. Then, in June, it released another hit Minion movie, Minions & Monsters, which made $531 million. It's no surprise that fans were looking forward to more from the animation company.
In late June, Illumination announced its next animated feature film, Not Alone, with a trailer that unveiled a one-of-a-kind sci-fi rom-com. With its April 2027 release date fast approaching, Illumination has begun promoting Not Alone. It began with a Thanksgiving Day Parade promotional teaser, which gave us a better look at the Pikmin-like alien creatures that crash-landed on Earth in the first trailer.
Not Alone gets Thanksgiving-themed teaser
Not Alone follows Joe, an introverted rocket mechanic voiced by Timothée Chalamet (Marty Supreme, Dune), as his romance with astrobotanist Fran, played by Selena Gomez (Only Murders in the Building, Hotel Transylvania), blossomswhile they build a rocket ship together at work. Their budding romance is interrupted by small, colorful, humanoid aliens. Out of all the places on Earth, their space voyage ends at Joe's house, where he is forced to deal with their presence.
In the new teaser, we see the three creatures, Dunk, Welly, and Shirm, voiced by Bob Brydon (Barbie), Diane Morgan (Mandy), and Jamie Demetriou (Cruella), attend Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade. Because of their diminutive size, they can't see much of the parade, even when stacked on top of each other atop two plastic cups. A gust of air from a vent sends them flying, but they hold onto a paper cup, which propels them higher, causing them to land on the musicians. The air from a trombone blasts them onto a Minion float, giving them the best view of the parade.
Although we didn't get to see Joe and Fran this time around, the comedy clip makes the movie look like a guaranteed good time. If you're looking forward to it, you won't have to wait long, as Not Alone hits theaters on Apr. 16, 2027.
