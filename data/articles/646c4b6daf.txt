Manila – Southeast Asian leaders reaffirmed the need to keep engaging with Myanmar while sticking with a plan calling for an immediate end to violence in the war-torn country, Philippine President Ferdinand Marcos Jr. said Sunday.
Chairing the annual summit of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations in Manila, Marcos said the 11-member bloc agreed to pursue “constructive and principled engagement” while adhering to its five-point peace plan, ASEAN’s framework for responding to the crisis in Myanmar since a 2021 coup.
The summit is under pressure to ease a five-year ban on Myanmar’s military-backed leaders attending high-level meetings, after Thailand and several other members stepped up engagement despite Myanmar’s civil war.
