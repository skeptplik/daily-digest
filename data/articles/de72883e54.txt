If you’ve ever spent time in and around Miami, Grand Theft Auto 6 is bound to delight you. Rockstar Games spared no expense in bringing the diverse world of Florida to life in digital form. Walk down the street in Vice City, and you’re sure to find an approximation of a bar you’ve actually been to. Head to the Leonida Keys, and you’ll find rows of houses on stilts. The attention to detail is impressive, but it also makes one odd detail about the map a little odd.
Go all the way north in Leonida, right up to Lummox County, and you’ll be able to go hiking and hunting in the mountains. As any Floridian will tell you, that’s not exactly reflective of the real Florida’s topography. Why take such a major creative liberty there when so much else about Leonida is grounded in realism? In an interview with Polygon, Rockstar North co-head Rob Nelson explained why GTA 6 takes players to the mountains.
Nelson said that one of the biggest challenges the team faced when designing the game was figuring out the map. Rockstar’s map team began working on the game in 2015, Nelson said, well before anyone else at the studio. While creating another city was easy enough for a team that has mastered that art, expanding to surrounding Florida locales was a tougher proposition. That’s because Florida is a very flat state.
The problem with depicting it as is, though, is that GTA players likely would have some expectations about what should be in a modern GTA map. GTA 5, for instance, built off of Los Angeles to create Los Santos. Because of that, the map has a lot of variety in elevation. That wouldn’t be possible if Leonida stuck too close to reality, so Rockstar ultimately decided to include some mountains to keep GTA 6 a little more consistent with GTA 5.
Though it may not be accurate, it’s a welcome addition to the map. As fun as it is to drive around Vice City, weaving around the same highways can get old. Lummox County offers a lot more open space for off-roading, biking, and other activities that just wouldn’t be possible in a city. For instance, when I found a bicycle perched atop a hill, I found myself racing down a natural BMX course that was built into the world. Every time I head up to Lummox County, it feels like I’m on vacation.
Crucially, it’s also the best date spot in the entire game. You can take Jason and Lucia out on impromptu dates throughout GTA 6, letting the couple play some mini-golf together or blow off steam at a shooting range. One of my favorite play sessions so far simply happened when I got the two in a truck and took them up north to a part of the map I’d never seen. We got out when we found a hiking trail and wound up stumbling into some ruins high up on a hill. It was a peaceful moment of discovery that the two got to share together. Moments like that are what make GTA 6 special, and Leonida’s intricate design is the thing that powers it all.
