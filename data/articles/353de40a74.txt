Herzliya, Israel – The parties of caretaker Israeli prime minister Benjamin Netanyahu and his rival Gadi Eisenkot concluded a new round of talks on Sunday without reaching an agreement on a possible coalition government, which would require concessions from both sides.
Delegations from Eisenkot’s Yashar party and Netanyahu’s Likud met for four hours at a hotel in Herzliya, near Tel Aviv, following a first round of discussions last Sunday.
“The latest round of coalition talks ... has concluded. The two sides outlined their positions on a range of substantive issues and discussed them in detail,” the two parties said in a joint statement.
