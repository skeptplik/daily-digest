Spanish goalkeeper barred 12 games for collision leading to death of Tenerife striker
Jesús de Miguel was chasing high ball in midfield when he was struck in the chest by Jesus Owono
Andorra goalkeeper Jesus Owono has been suspended 12 games after he received a red card for a reckless on-field collision with Jesus de Miguel that resulted in the death of the Tenerife striker.
The Spanish soccer federation's disciplinary committee issued the suspension for Owono on Friday, 10 days after De Miguel died a day after his clash with Owono.
The disciplinary committee said Owono used "excessive force" in the collision.
De Miguel was chasing a high ball in midfield when he was struck in the chest by Owono, who rushed far outside his area, near the end of the Nov. 2 match in Tenerife. The 30-year-old De Miguel suffered cardiac arrest following the collision. He died the next day.
Owono issued a statement through his club expressing his remorse and offering his condolences to De Miguel's family.
Spanish police opened an investigation into possible involuntary manslaughter. Under Spanish law, deaths from non-natural causes, including accidents, are subject to judicial investigation, though prosecutors have said that criminal proceedings are unlikely.
On Thursday, Andorra coach Carles Manso said it will be up to Owono to decide when he feels ready to play again.
"He is deeply affected by the situation," Manso said. "We will give him the space to feel completely free to decide whether, and when, he might want to return."
