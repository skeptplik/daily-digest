Kobe – The Kobe Municipal Government on Friday submitted to the city assembly a draft ordinance to introduce a tax on vacant condominium units in central areas, aiming to reduce investment-driven vacancies and promote the effective use of housing.
If enacted, it would mark the first tax in Japan targeting vacant condominium units specifically. The city aims to introduce the tax in fiscal 2030.
The levy would be imposed on owners of units without resident registration in condominiums located in such central areas as the Sannomiya and Motomachi districts.
The tax would consist of 0.7% of a condominium unit’s assessed value plus an additional amount based on its location and floor area.
It would be collected as a non-statutory ordinary tax, whose revenue use is not restricted. Tax reductions or exemptions would be available when resident registration is temporarily absent due to relocation, inheritance or other reasons, or when the period since the unit was put on the rental market has not exceeded a year.
The city is concerned that purchases of condominium units for investment or asset-holding purposes are increasing the number of units without actual residents despite growing housing demand in central areas.
It set up an expert panel in 2025 to discuss measures for the proper management of condominiums.
