News2u Holdings Inc. (Headquarters: Chiyoda-ku, Tokyo; President and CEO: Minako Suematsu) today announced that it will implement a group business restructuring, effective January 2027, to enhance the competitiveness and operational efficiency of its media and publishing businesses.
Overview of Business Restructuring (Effective January 2027)
1. Consolidation into The Japan Times, Ltd.
- Transfer of the businesses of “The Japan Times Alpha” and “Alpha J” (currently under The Japan Times Publishing, Ltd.)
- Transfer of the advertising business (currently under The Japan Times Agency, Ltd.)
By consolidating these key businesses into The Japan Times, the group will strengthen coordination across editorial, sales, and advertising and promote cross-functional media operations.
2. Focus Areas of Group Companies
- The Japan Times Publishing will focus on its language publishing business as well as the rollout of an online Japanese-language learning platform.
- The Japan Times Agency will specialize in its education business, centered on education-related events and educational information platform services.
Through this restructuring, the News2u Group will optimize its management resources and advance the delivery of high-quality journalism and revenue generation supported by a strong business foundation.
