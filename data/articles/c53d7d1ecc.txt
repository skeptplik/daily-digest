$100 is a sweet spot in gift-giving. You can really buy something that will make your giftee smile for that price without breaking the bank too much. To help, we spent countless hours testing all manner of gizmos and gadgets to bring you expert advice on what is worth buying.
If you don't mind spending more, check out our Best Tech Gifts guide, and if money is tight, we've also made a list of the Best Gifts Under $25. For giftees with a particular passion, you can find tailored options, like gifts for bird lovers, home chefs, book lovers, and more.
Updated November 2026: I added a few new entries and updated links and prices.
- Portable Sound The perfect tiny speaker, the JBL Go 5 fits in the palm of your hand but produces a surprisingly punchy sound with deep bass and clear mids. It's also tough, with an IP68 rating, offers plenty of connectivity options, and boasts battery life of up to 50 hours. It even supports stereo pairing if you have more than one. This is the budget pick in our Best Bluetooth Speakers guide.
- Light It Up Most light strips are designed to be tucked away in recesses, but this Govee strip has a diffuser, so you can happily stick it anywhere you want without it being an eyesore. The adhesive-backed metal brackets make for easy installation too. The Govee app allows you to schedule, cycle through colors, play animations, and even create your own scenes. Some transitions are blocky, and the app can be confusing, but this is a great way to introduce your recipient to smart lights. This is our favorite diffused light strip from our Best Smart Lighting guide.
- Tiny Tunes Stupidly cute, this mini Stylophone on a keyring is a fully functional electronic synthesizer with a pop-out stylus, so you can noodle around with tunes wherever you are. Based on the original 1968 design, it comes in white, black, or pink, and the necessary AA batteries are included.
- Power Up Sling a good portable charger in your bag, and you will never be stranded with a dead phone again. One of our favorites is Nimble's Champ, which is compact, light, and packs a 5,000- to 20,000-mAh capacity, capable of recharging most phones and laptops at least once, fully at its smallest size. It comes in snazzy speckled colors with a handy lanyard strap and a small USB-C cable. We are also big fans of Nimble’s environmental efforts (using bioplastics and plastic-free packaging). This power bank appears in our Best Portable Chargers guide.
- A Smart Speaker The Google Home Speaker is our favorite smart speaker right now. It boasts impressive sound for its small form, the AI is a little more human than competitors, and it comes in a range of fun colors. Gemini is also helpful and smart, though some of the best features require a subscription. This speaker is the top pick in our Best Smart Speakers guide.
- Cyberpunk Original It’s hard to believe Neuromancer was released in 1984. William Gibson’s sci-fi novel was foundational to the cyberpunk scene, and the hit 2020 video game Cyberpunk 2077 borrows heavily from it. Ahead of Apple TV’s new show, which arrives in January 2027, Penguin’s Ace Books has released this special edition with fresh cover art and sprayed edges. It can be a challenging read, but it’s an incredibly inventive and spookily prescient masterpiece.
- Desk Tidy We love multifunctional gadgets like this headphone stand from Oakywood, which offers a place to hang your cans and a spot to wirelessly charge your phone. The angular base in solid walnut or oak is super stylish, and the steel stand keeps your headphones neatly out of the way. This headphone stand appears in our Best Wireless Chargers guide.
- Game On Comfy to hold for long gaming sessions, with a subtle texture that adds grip, this mobile controller combines responsive analog sticks with clicky buttons and an excellent D-pad. It cradles your phone with a built-in USB-C connector for a speedy connection and includes pass-through charging, a screenshot or screen-recording button, a mute button, and a headphone jack. There's also a handy companion app with an optional subscription. This is a top pick in our Best Mobile Controllers guide.
- Motion Sensor This versatile motion sensor can flag access to a cabinet, drawer, or safe. You can easily configure it in the app to set exactly what triggers an alert, set the cooldown period, and choose how you want to receive alerts. Unlike many other smart home devices, it keeps everything completely private too. This gadget appears in our Best Motion Sensors guide.
- Forty Winks I have a constant whining tinnitus sound in my ears after a misspent youth of loud gigs and clubs. A white-noise machine helps me tune it out at night so I can drift off to sleep. This is what I have on my nightstand right now, and it works great. Compact, easy to use, and with 22 non-repeating sounds (I like the ocean surf) and a timer function, it's all you need. This gadget appears in our Best Sound Machines guide.
- Wireless Keyboard One of the best scissor-switch keyboards available, the Keychron B2 Pro offers a comfortable, quiet typing experience. Its fully customizable (you can use Keychron’s Launcher software to remap keys and set up macros), and it looks pretty stylish, too. You’ll also find this deck in our Best Keyboards guide.
- Gaming Mouse You don’t have to spend big to snag a great gaming mouse, and the SteelSeries Rival 3 proves it. This mouse is lightweight and responsive, with a lovely rubbery scroll wheel and a couple of handy side buttons. It’s everything most people need without the unnecessary frills. This is the budget pick in our Best Gaming Mice guide.
- Excellent Earbuds These full-featured earbuds have it all. Solid sound, active noise cancellation that works well, a stable Bluetooth connectivity, and a robust app with adjustable EQ and programmable controls. It even has a respectable IP55 dust- and water-resistance rating. These are the cheapest option in our Best Wireless Earbuds guide.
- Cordless Vac Crumbs in your keyboard, dirt in your window frames, those dusty crevices in your car: Our lives are filled with mucky, but hard-to-clean places. My own personal vacuum has a bag of attachments, but it’s heavy and a hassle to pull out and set up. Hoto’s angled Penvac is the cordless solution. The battery doesn’t last long, suction drops as it fills, and it’s a pain to empty, but my nooks and crannies have never been so dust-free.
