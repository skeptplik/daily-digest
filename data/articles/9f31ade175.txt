The UK’s competition watchdog has called for a shake-up of the £8bn private dentistry market, after identifying a “catalogue of concerns” including inflation-busting price rises and signs of targets to sell more expensive treatments and plans.
The Competition and Markets Authority said patients faced substantial differences in prices in different parts of the country and “commercial practices, targets and incentives [that] may not operate in patients’ best interests”.
