Hunter Schafer just proved that hoodies—when done right—can earn a rightful place on a red carpet. Balancing comfort with avant-garde style, the actor selected a one-of-a-kind, upcycled ensemble from emerging label Nihl for an appearance during the Wired World Fair in Miami. And it featured a sleeveless lime-green sweatshirt.
The look was pulled from by New York City-based label Nihl’s Spring/Summer 2027 collection. A semi-finalist of the 2018 LVMH Prize, designer Neil Grotzinger founded his brand after working for houses such as Marc Jacobs, Prabal Gurung, and Diane Von Furstenburg. At Nihl, he has focused on subverting traditional ideas of masculinity, creating gender-fluid collections that exist outside of binary stereotypes. Reflecting these themes, his practice has often included transforming everyday, functional pieces into couture creations, with hand craftsmanship and unexpected, off-kilter detailing that make a viewer pause.
In this case, that meant turning a hoodie into a singular masterpiece, with studded gemstones creating an organic medley of blue, black, gold, purple, and red flowers. The motifs covered the whole piece, from hood to hem, and were supplemented by magenta beading placed around the waist. The accompanying skirt was repurposed from a pink-and-white striped dress, and adorned with blue and purple crystals that mirrored a splatter-paint effect. It reached just below the knee, and was finished with a raw, ripped hem.
Schafer, who was promoting her forthcoming Blade Runner 2099 series, kept her hood up. Stylist Dara Allen, known mononymously by her first name, grounded the runway fit with a pair of timeless pointed-toe heels in white.
