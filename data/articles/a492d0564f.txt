NATO assessing intentionality of Russian violation of Polish airpsace, Rutte says
NATO allies are meeting to discuss the violation of Polish airspace by Russian missiles last week.
The alliance chief, Mark Rutte, said they were still assessing whether this was an intentional act by Moscow or not.
"It is clear that this is a dangerous incident. It comes from Russia," Rutte said.
"Whether it is intentional or not has to be decided, of course, by authorities. I will never speculate," he added.
He said a full comment would only come once Poland and NATO had fully assessed what had happened.

Russia warns against conflict expanding in Baltic Sea
Following a drone attack on a Russian cargo ship over the weekend, Moscow has warned against a an "escalation fo tension" in the region.
"It is indeed a very dangerous trend, the expansion of terrorist activity in the Baltic region... This is very dangerous. It is, of course, leading to an escalation of tensions in the region," Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said.
Finland said the cargo ship strike had been carried out by Ukraine.
Peskov declined to comment on the drone strike that sunk a Russian ship off the coast of Libya which Ukraine claimed responsibility for on Monday.
Oil refinery in southern Russia suspends operations after attack
The Novoshakhtinsk in the Russian state of Rostov has suspended its operations after a barrage of attacks overnight, the state governort Yuri Slyusar said on Telegram.
Slyusar said the attack, which involved both drones and missiles, lasted over six hours.
He added that emergency services were working to contain the aftermath.
Ukraine has repeatedly targeted Russian refineries as a means to hamper the Russian economy.
The Novoshakhtinsk refinery lies close to Ukraine's eastern border with Russia. It mainly refines oil for export and has a capacity of 100,000 barrels per day.
UN calls for investigation into attack on Russian ship off Libya
The UN Support Mission in Libya (UNSMIL) has expressed concern over the Ukrainian attack that sunk a Russian ship off the coast of Libya, saying in a post on X: "Libya’s sovereignty, territorial integrity and the security of its citizens and assets must be fully respected."
"UNSMIL calls for an immediate, independent and transparent investigation to establish the facts and hold those responsible to account," it added.
Libya has been split between two competing powers claiming to be the sovereign government of the country for several years.
"UNSMIL calls on all actors to act responsibly to preserve the country’s stability and build on the hard-won gains in the political process towards a durable settlement," the statement said.

Ukrainian attack kills 1 in Russia's Perm, governor says
The governor of Russia's Perm region, Dmitry Makhonin, said one person was killed in a Ukrainian drone attack that hit and damaged several industrial sites.
Two others were injured by shrapnel, Makhonin said in a post on Telegram.
The governor said 56 drones had been shot down. It wasn't clear how many drones were involved in the attack.
Perm lies some 1,700 km (1,050 miles) from Ukrainian-held territory and is home to an oil refinery that has repeatedly come under attack.
Zelenskyy says Ukraine drones sunk Russian ship off Libya
Ukraine on Monday claimed attacks on Russian vessels off the coast of Libya, with President Volodymyr Zelenskyy lauding the nation's defense forces for the "exemplary Ukrainian operation."
"Our warriors deployed seven Ukrainian-made naval drones. Despite all the enemy's efforts to counter the attack, the Russian corvette Merkury was sunk. It's one of their newest ships, commissioned in 2023, during the full-scale war," Zelenskyy said in a post on X.
"A Ka-27 combat helicopter was also destroyed along with the corvette. Our second target was the Russian dry cargo vessel MV ANGARA, which the enemy used to transport weapons. Result: target hit," he said.
This comes a day after a Russian vessel, docked at the Libyan port of Benghazi, was attacked by a suspected drone.
Zelenskyy further said Ukraine was continuing "operations in designated Waters," adding that it was in response to Russia's aggressions.
Ukrainian drones attack Russian oil facilities, reports
Ukrainian drones attacked oil storage facilities in Russia's Tatarstan region, Russian news agency Interfax reported on Monday, citing local authorities.
As per the report, the strikes damaged several facilities but did not lead to any deaths or injuries.
Russia's oil industry is particularly important to its economy and generates revenue that helps fund its war in Ukraine.
Russia and Ukraine have been regularly targeting each other's energy infrastructure. Russian oil is especially key to the warring nation's economy and supports much of its military operations in Ukraine.
UN urges probe after Russian ship attacked at Libyan port
The United Nations on Monday called for an independent probe into suspected drone attacks on two ships off the coast of Libya, in what could be spillover from the war in Ukraine. One of the two vessels was Russian while the other was sailing under a Maltese flag.
The UN mission in Libya expressed "serious concern" over the attacks, calling for "an immediate, independent and transparent investigation".
"Libya cannot afford, and must not become a battleground for foreign conflicts," the UNSMIL wrote on X.
Libya's eastern administration did not identify who was behind Sunday's attack but said Libya was "not a party to the Russian-Ukrainian war".
NATO to discuss Russian violation of Polish airspace
Representatives of NATO's 32 member states are set to discuss the recent Russian violations of Polish airspace on Monday in Brussels.
No special meeting has been called for this purpose and the discussion will take place during regular consultations, a spokesperson said.
Three Russian missiles entered the Polish airspace on Friday during airstrikes on Ukraine, the Polish government has said. They crashed in the southeast of Poland which is both a member of NATO and the European Union.
NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte, who is currently in Berlin for the 72nd annual session of the NATO Parliamentary Assembly, earlier said Europe would do everything possible to identify where the risks to Western deterrence and defense systems lie.
Russian strikes kill at least 14 in Ukraine
At least 14 people were killed as Russia pounded Ukraine's Kyiv region with airstrikes overnight, local authorities said.
In Dymer, at least 11 people died after a Russian strike hit a five-storey building, regional official Tymur Tkachenko said over messaging app Telegram. "Search and rescue operation is ongoing," he said.
Ukraine's State Emergency Service had earlier said that the dead included a teenager and a woman in her forties. Before that, it said rescuers were searching for 11 people, including two children, who were "not responding or cannot be reached."
It is not yet clear how many people remain trapped in the rubble of the collapsed building.
In the city of Vyshneve, a Russian strike hit a supermarket, killing three people, Mayor Illya Dikov wrote on Facebook.
Ukrainian human rights ombudsman Dmytro Lubinets called the attack in Dymer part of "Russia's genocidal war against Ukraine....The world must stop looking away. Condemn this terror, hold Russia accountable, and act now to stop the killing of innocent Ukrainians."
Moscow has been pummeling Ukraine with nightly aerial barrages as the war-torn country braces for another winter, as the invasion grinds through its fifth year.
