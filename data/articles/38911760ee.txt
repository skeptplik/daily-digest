Marvel Rivals, the Overwatch-like hero shooter from NetEase and Marvel Games, is just a couple of weeks away from its second anniversary. To celebrate, The Lego Group is releasing a new set based on the game's take on an iconic Marvel character. This will be the first-ever Marvel Rivals set, though plenty of Marvel sets already exist, and to mark the occasion, it will turn the beloved Jeff the Land Shark into a brick-built figure.
Jeff the Land Shark debuted in Kelly Thompson's West Coast Avengers in 2018 and has since become a mascot for Marvel across comics and games since.This includes Marvel Rivals, where he is playable as a Strategist hero. The Jeff the Land Shark lego set will be released on Jan. 1, 2027 and will cost $49.99. You can pre-order it now at the official Lego store.
In a press release (via GameSpot), Lego Marvel design lead Jesper C. Nielsen said that, "Jeff brings together everything players love about a breakout gaming character: an unforgettable silhouette, a personality you understand instantly, and the ability to turn any match into a story.”" He continued that the Lego team "wanted this Lego model to capture both sides of him, from the lovable companion fans want beside their screen to the unpredictable strategist they meet in battle."
Lego Marvel Rivals: Jeff the Land Shark set details
The Jeff the Land Shark set will be a modest 495 pieces in total and is rated for ages 12 and up. The store page states that once fully built, Jeff's head, legs, tail, and fins are all movable, allowing Jeff to be posed in multiple stances. You can also detach his tongue and close his mouth. The fin can be detached as well, so you can move it around on the ground as if it were about to pop up on an enemy.
NetEase Games will celebrate the second anniversary of Marvel Rivals by bringing its year-long Path to Doomsday event to a close. At the end of the year Marvel fans can catch Avengers: Doomsday in theaters on Dec. 18. However, you won't be able to see it on IMAX screens, since Dune: Part 3 has those locked down.
