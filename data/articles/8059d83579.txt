‘A symbol of hope’: Pakistan celebrates as astronaut enters orbit

Muhammad Zeeshan Ali has become the first Pakistani to reach orbit after blasting off on board a Chinese mission to the country’s Tiangong space station on Tuesday. The Pakistan Air Force pilot and payload specialist’s feat was celebrated by cheers and rapturous applause by those watching in his homeland.
