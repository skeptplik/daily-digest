The Vespera design is very portable and great for taking to dark sky sites or just setting up in your backyard. This is part of the appeal of this model; the outer shell is the same, whilst the insides have been upgraded to capture better-quality images of your chosen astro targets.
From a smartphone, we managed to capture the two galaxies M81 and M82 in Ursa Major, along with the great globular cluster in Hercules. These were captured with the boosted 12MP resolution sensor, just one of the many upgrades the Pro version has over the regular Vespera or Vespera II, also including bigger internal storage, better battery life and a built-in hygrometer for dew control.
