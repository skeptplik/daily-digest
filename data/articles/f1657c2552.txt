Inspiring Senegal's next generation: Free tickets bring children to Dakar 2026
Issued on: Modified:
Share
As part of the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games, 200,000 free tickets will be distributed to schools and community organisations. The aim is to give as many young people as possible the chance to experience the Games. FRANCE 24’s team on the ground reports.
