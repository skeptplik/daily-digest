Nine Inch Nails bandmates and award-winning film composers Trent Reznor and Atticus Ross are once again partnering up to provide the score for David Fincher's new film, The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth. This marks the sixth collaboration between the musical pair and the director.
Reznor and Ross' involvement was revealed when Netflix debuted the official poster for the film on its social media accounts earlier today, and you can seem them listed in the credits at the bottom of the image below (if you squint hard enough).

Reznor and Ross previously featured on the soundtracks to Fincher-directed films including The Social Network, Gone Girl, and Mank, with the former receiving the Academy Award for Best Original Score in 2011. More recently the pair won the Golden Globe Award for the Best Original Score for Challengers in 2025, and earned a Grammy nomination earlier this year for one of its tracks from the Tron: Ares soundtrack (credited as Nine Inch Nails).
The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth is a sequel to Quentin Tarantino's Once Upon a Time in Hollywood, written by Tarantino and featuring Brad Pitt in a reprisal of his stuntman role, but directed by Fincher. In a recent interview with Empire Magazine, Fincher revealed that Tarantino's original draft "was essentially two movies" and that he had to cut 60 pages from the screenplay in order to get the film down to a runtime of around two hours and 20 minutes.
The Further Mis-Adventures of Cliff Booth is set to open in IMAX theaters globally on November 25, before it launches on Netflix on December 23. Alongside Brad Pitt, the film is also set to star Elizabeth Debicki, Scott Caan, Carla Gugino, Yahya Abdul Mateen II, and Peter Weller. Tarantino is set to release a novelization of the film on December 8, which will serve as a literary follow-up to 2021's Once Upon a Time in Hollywood: A Novel.
