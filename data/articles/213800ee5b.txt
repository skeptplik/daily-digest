Anitta, JT and Sexyy Red joined in on the “Grand Theft Auto VI” craze by attending the launch party for the video game, which was hosted by Rockstar Games and David Grutman at Gekko Miami on Wednesday in Miami ahead of the release.
The musicians matched the event’s celebratory vibe and the energy of Miami with form-fitting looks, paired with towering and super feminine heels. Anitta showed off her figure in a sheer dark brown, espresso-toned top paired with a matching sheer maxiskirt. On her feet, the Brazilian star wore the Retrofête Naomi Sandals in a rich metallic gold.

The minimalist sandals are crafted with a laminated gold leather upper, leather lining, and a clean black footbed that provides contrast. The shoes feature an ultra-thin, delicate front strap that sits across the lower foot, paired with sweeping, wrap-around ankle straps anchored securely at the heel counter. The sandals have an open, pointed-toe box paired with a tall, slender, striking 110mm stiletto heel, which is approximately 4.3 inches tall, that creates a clean, architectural line. They retail for $398.
JT, who is a fan of extravagant heels, wore the Roberto Cavalli metallic sandals tiger heels, which the brand describes as being “synonymous with glamorous and sensual femininity.” The bejeweled sandals are made of fine silver metallic snake scale-effect leather and are decorated at the ankle with jeweled chains and crystal-studded tiger heads on the strap.
The super-high spool heel elevates the style. The shoes feature a zip closure and a monogram mirror snake on the back. The sandals feature a Roberto Cavalli signature on the inner sole with a black leather outsole. The shoes retail for $2,395 and are available in gold, black, fuchsia and white.
Sexyy Red dressed for the occasion in a black and pink Vice City #6 basketball jersey paired with a glossy black jacket and black mini shorts. She paired them with the statement I.AM.GIA Anya Peep Toe Boot, which is a knee-high design. She opted for the style in black, which is made from a snakeskin-textured faux leather, featuring fixed ruching over the top of the foot. They also feature a peep toe, back heel cutout and stiletto heel. The shoes are currently listed on sale for $149.40.
“Grand Theft Auto VI” is a long-awaited and major cultural event because it marks the return of the blockbuster open-world franchise after 13 years. The video game, which is available on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, has an estimated launch revenue of around $1.84 billion and has already received universal critical acclaim. The video game follows an open-world action-adventure crime story centered on a modern-day Bonnie and Clyde-style criminal couple — Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval — as they try to survive and pull off scores in the state of Leonida, which is Rockstar’s fictionalized version of Miami.
