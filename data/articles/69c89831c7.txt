The story so far: Software giant Microsoft is facing a widespread outage affecting users of its Outlook email service, as well as its other workplace products. According to DownDetector, over 100,000 users had initially registered complaints about Microsoft Outlook.
- November 13, 2026 13:22Microsoft outage enters second day; company says not aware of malicious activity Microsoft’s workplace services, including Outlook and Teams, remained disrupted for users in India and elsewhere on Friday (November 13, 2026), a day after a global outage began, with the company saying it was still working to pinpoint the cause and is not aware of any malicious activity behind it.
