Cunha helps Manchester United seal comeback win after fans' tennis ball protest
In short:
Manchester United has come back from 0-2 down to beat AS Roma in the UEFA Champions League, ending a major winless run.
Matheus Cunha scored goals in the 83rd and 86th minutes to deliver a 3-2 win, after fans had protested against the Man United owners in the first half using tennis balls.
Elsewhere, Bayern Munich, Barcelona and PSG also recorded wins.
