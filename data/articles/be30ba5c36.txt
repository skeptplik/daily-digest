With “Grand Theft Auto 6” less than two days away from its blockbuster launch, official release information has come in for the game’s highly anticipated collaboration with Travis Scott and Nike.
The “Grand Theft Auto 6” x Travis Scott x Nike Air Force 1 Low won’t drop on the same day as the game as previously reported, but will instead be made available to purchase through two opportunities not long after. Nike‘s Snkrs app will host the initial launch Saturday, followed by another release Monday on the Cactus Jack website.
Official image leaks suggest only the “Triple White” and “Triple Black” colorways will be listed on Snkrs. It’s possible the multi-color pair seen only in rougher photos will be sold in the second drop alongside one or both of those treatments. And then there’s fourth gradient patent leather variant Scott showed off last week that will only be doled out to fans by the rapper himself.

Both the white and black-dominant versions share “GTA” in place of the “Air” mark on the lateral midsole, six stars repping the highest Wanted Level in “Grand Theft Auto” on the opposite and lace dubraes featuring Rockstar’s logo. Scott’s signature backward Swoosh takes its place on the upper’s lateral side, and mismatched tongue tag but the “VI” logo on the right shoe and an upside down “Nike Air” mark with six stars beneath on the left. Matching the pink on those tags are a removable elastic money band for the toe box and six more stars on the outsole.
Setting the black pair apart is a crocodile texture running throughout the most of the upper and mismatched insoles with camouflage on left shoe and solid black on the right. The white pair’s upper is uniformly smooth in patent leather, and its mismatched insoles have purple on the right shoe and a map on the left.
A range of apparel also includes T-shirts and an assortment of colors, patterns and iconography from the three parties. Additional products not yet shown will be exclusive to the Cactus Jack release.
Pricing for both Air Force 1 Lows shown here is set at $130.
