LEGO revealed a new lineup of Winnie the Pooh sets back in March in anticipation of the bear's 100th anniversary. What you may not know, however, is that LEGO had already released an Eeyore set back in 2025 as part of the BrickHeadz theme. Now that it's been on the market for a while, LEGO has officially marked it for retirement at the end of the year.
Before that day comes, LEGO is currently offering a discount on the set ahead of its own Black Friday sale. You can get 20% off the set, which drops the price down to just $10.30 for as long as stock remains.
LEGO Eeyore is made up of only 156-pieces and is fairly easy to put together. The BrickHeadz line is essentially a Funko Pop competitor, so this set is entirely meant to be displayed on a desk or a shelf somewhere. It's the only Eeyore set that exists right now, but there are rumors that LEGO is planning a 1,600-piece Eeyore set for March 2027. As with most LEGO rumors, it's most likely true. The rumored price for that set is $150 though.
If you want to grab a cool Disney gift for the Winnie the Pooh fan in your life, it's a lot cheaper to buy this set before it retires than wait for the expensive version. At $10.39, this is an easy recommendation as a stocking stuffer worth buying for the holidays.
I make this recommendation knowing full well how many secret Eeyore fans exist in the world. My 2-year-old son brings his beloved Eeyore plushie with him everywhere and often refers to him as his "good friend". Because of this, I regularly get strangers talking to me about how Eeyore was their favorite Pooh character too. If you would have asked me a few years ago who I think most people's favorite Pooh character was, I would have probably said Tigger or Pooh. But the times have changed.
Now that I've rewatched all of the Winnie the Pooh movies and read the books again, though, I have to agree that Eeyore is the best one. He's a loyal friend, he's surprising up for anything, and his self-depreciation only adds to his lovability. He deserves all the love he gets.
