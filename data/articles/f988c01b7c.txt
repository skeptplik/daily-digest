First Indonesian POW fighting for Russia in Ukraine sparks Jakarta crackdown
With hundreds lured to the battlefield by bogus job offers, Jakarta steps up online policing and warns citizens of lost nationality
The 49-year-old contract soldier from Java was captured in the frontline Zaporizhzhia region after entering Ukrainian-held territory following a drone strike on his Russian unit, The Guardian reported, citing Kyiv’s state-backed “I Want to Live” surrender initiative.
According to the project, roughly 283 Indonesian nationals were recruited during the first nine months of this year to support Moscow’s war effort.
