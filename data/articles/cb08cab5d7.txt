Following a sold-out European tour earlier this year, A Painted Symphony is coming back in 2027 with even more dates and locations. The concert series for Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 will get an encore performance next year with a global tour including the first ever performances in North America. The world tour was announced today with tickets going on sale Sept. 15. It will include Europe, the United Kingdom, and the United States. Fans in Asia, Africa, Australia, and South America are out of luck this time around.
The concert series brings Lorien Testard's critically acclaimed and award-winning score for the 2025 RPG to life. Having attended video game concerts in the past, I can attest to the power of hearing a good soundtrack performed live. It'll be a show worth attending.
