Only a fraction of World Heritage sites are in Africa. China wants to fix that
Beijing is offering funds and training to help nations win Unesco listings, a move analysts say boosts its soft power
Africa is home to ancient cities, rock art and striking natural landscapes, yet its sites are sorely under-represented on Unesco’s World Heritage List.
According to data released by the UN body, of the 1,273 sites on the list – which are formally protected under the World Heritage Convention treaty – just 115 are in Africa.
