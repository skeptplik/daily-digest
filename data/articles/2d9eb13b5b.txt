Here in the United States, Harper’s Bazaar hosts its annual Icons event, where this year’s honorees included none other than Céline Dion, Anna Sawai, Gabbriette, Charlotte Rampling, Cara Delevingne, Chris Fleming, Alysa Liu, Julie Mehretu, and Kim Gordon. But across the pond, our sister publication, Harper’s Bazaar UK, hosts its own awards ceremony, called Women of the Year, which celebrated its 2026 iteration last night at Claridge’s in London.
Like our “Icons” designation, the Women of the Year ceremony highlights figures who have inspired the world through art, entertainment, fashion, sports, and more. And the big winners of the evening included names like Oscar-winning actor Mikey Madison and All’s Fair star Glenn Close, the latter of whom was chosen as the Icon recipient for the WOTY ceremony.
Other prizewinners included Chicken Shop Date host Amelia Dimoldenberg, who was given the Innovator Award, while the co-CEO’s of the National Theatre, Kate Varah and Indhu Rubasingham, were honored with the Changemakers Award. Givenchy’s Sarah Burton was celebrated for her design, while Stephanie Cavalli was recognized for her modeling. Cate Blanchett, Sam Taylor-Johnson, Claudia Winkleman, Dina Asher-Smith, and Bella Maclean all walked home with statuettes as well.
Plus, a few other starry figures—like Isla Fisher, Elizabeth Debicki, and Letitia Wright—made surprise appearances.
