Houthis launch strikes on Saudi Arabia
Issued on:
Share
Iran-backed Houthi rebels in Yemen continue to exchange strikes with the Saudi-led coalition. The coalition said it intercepted and destroyed three ballistic missiles fired towards Riyadh early on Saturday. It marks the second Houthi attack on the Saudi capital in recent days, following Saudi-led strikes on Houthi targets in Yemen. Yuka Royer has the latest.
