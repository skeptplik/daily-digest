It doesn't take long for one of your cars to end up at the police impound in Grand Theft Auto 6. You might have abandoned it near a crime scene, or they arrested you while you were driving it. While the circumstances may vary, you have only two ways to proceed if a car is impounded: you forget about it or you retrieve it.
If you are not ready to abandon your favorite car and want it back, we've got you covered. Here's how to get your car out of the impound in GTA 6.
How to get your car out of the impound in GTA 6
There are two methods to retrieve a car from the police impound: you can either steal it back or pay for it. Both are valid, but the second one becomes available only in Chapter 2.
Stealing the car is the most plausible and cheapest option to execute. You just need to head to the impound lot, enter, and drive your car away from there. Some impounds, like the one in Port Gellhorn, have openings that you can use to enter unnoticed. Regardless of how you get inside, the challenge is escaping from the police once you leave. You must be prepared to run away, so try to come up with an escape route by setting a destination far from the city.
Now, the second option requires the Scooter Bros app, which you unlock in Chapter 2 after completing Moses' mission. If you have it already, open the app and select the impounded car (the one with a police badge icon). You will see that the first option in the menu is to pay a fee to retrieve the car.
The price varies depending on how far you are from the police impound. For example, I would have to pay $699 to get my car back from the Port Gellhorn impound while in Vice City. However, if you have upgraded your Scooter Bros account to Premium, you can use your remaining daily credits to request an impounded vehicle. Since this will essentially consume all your credits for the day, you should wait until you have only one left before using this option.
