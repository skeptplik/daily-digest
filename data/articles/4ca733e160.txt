BBC Sounds Re-Architecture
The project I'm going to focus on for this talk is a large-scale re-architecture within BBC Sounds, which was started around a year ago. This is part of wider org changes to move to the product operating model and start to embrace platform engineering. Why is this project important? Because the BBC needs to evolve its digital offering to stay relevant. The BBC's audio offering is a key part of the digital estate and we need to ensure that it's in a fit state to serve the audience. First, I want to cover the project context and the organizational changes at the heart of it. The previous team structure was organized around a product vertical for BBC Sounds. Within that vertical, a catalog team provided core metadata for available audio content. A business-led team consumed that metadata and defined the business logic which powers the BBC Sounds experience. That business logic was used to serve the audience-facing experience across clients on web, mobile, TV devices, and smart speakers.
With the move to platform engineering, this structure needed to change. Under the new structure, the catalog team would sit in platform and the previous business-led team would be split into two halves, one half providing platform APIs. The remaining part of that team would sit within the experiences area of the org, and the audience-facing client teams would also sit within experiences. What would that mean for the split of responsibilities for those new teams? The platform team would own core and personalized metadata with responsibility for creating APIs that describe the catalog of on-demand content, including podcasts, previously aired radio programs, and audiobooks, and live content, the BBC's stable of local and national radio stations. The experience team would own personalization and user experience concerns, building personalized responses based on user context, and owning the logic around experience-specific concerns, such as what content rails are presented in clients, and experience variants, such as the international mode which users are presented with when using the app on holiday.
