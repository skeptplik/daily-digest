Best known for its viral bangles, fitness brand Bala is entering the weighted vest game.
The Obsessed Vest, Bala’s first weighted vest, launched Tuesday direct-to-consumer, with prices beginning at $129 for the lowest weight.
The adjustable, one-size-fits-most design comes in Bala’s signature core colors: Charcoal, Blush, Sea, Sage and Sand and four weight variants with respective pricing: 8-pound, 12-pound, 16-pound and 20-pound.
Cofounder Natalie Holloway told WWD the vest has been four years in the making and is something customers request daily.
“We never launch anything that is not truly unique to the market and Bala’s take. We’re not just going to put something out because someone else did,” she said. “So that’s what took us four years to get this right. It’s the sexiest, cutest weighted vest on the market because everything that exists now is either combat ready or really expensive so ours is that like sweet spot.”
Additional weight inserts for the vests will be available next year, said Holloway, while more wearables are on the product roadmap.
“Wearables is definitely part of the our core identity,” she continued.
In June, the brand launched its first activewear collection, called Everyday Active. The collection includes a cotton and spandex with polyester blend sweatshirt and shorts, as well as nylon and spandex blend biker shorts, a flared legging, a square-neck bra and streamlined tank dress.
