Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi has moved forward Japan’s financial contribution to the International Criminal Court, ICC President Tomoko Akane said Tuesday after her first meeting with Takaichi since the U.S. announced sanctions on Akane in August.
With the early payment, the Takaichi administration plans to safeguard the ICC’s funding for the next fiscal year, starting April 2027, and address any shortfall if U.S. sanctions take effect.
Japan currently contributes the largest share among ICC members toward the court’s budget, equating to approximately ¥4.6 billion out of a roughly ¥34.5 billion budget.
