Taylor Swift is one of the biggest music artists in modern times. From 2023 to late 2024, the Eras Tour took her all over the world to play nearly 150 shows for millions of fans. It was a monumental moment in her career, and it earned $2 billion in ticket sales, becoming the highest-grossing tour of all time. Grand Theft Auto 6 grossed double that amount, in just four days.
GTA 6 was released on Nov. 19, 2026, and it has been truly unprecedented, breaking records left and right. According to a report by Alinea Analytics, by the end of its launch weekend, GTA 6 sold a total of 44.9 million copies and amassed $4 billion in gross revenue — as Alinea head of market analysis Rhys Elliot pointed out, within days, GTA 6 made twice as much as Taylor Swift's entire Eras Tour.
GTA 6 smashes entertainment milestones as earnings numbers rise
GTA 6 wasn't just the biggest launch in PlayStation history but also the "biggest entertainment launch of all time," according to developer Rockstar Games, and easily surpassed astonishing entertainment milestones in other industries. The game's success is even more impressive when compared to other giants in the entertainment industry.
This summer, two movies broke records, but even still, their box-office performances can't hold a candle to GTA 6's earnings. The theatrical runs of The Odyssey, Christopher Nolan's highest-grossing movie, and Spider-Man: Brand New Day, the highest-grossing movie in domestic box office history, generated $1.2 billion and $2.5 billion, respectively. Put together, they weren't able to surpass GTA 6's opening weekend numbers.
These numbers reflect only Grand Theft Auto 6's launch window and are bound to grow further. Once the game becomes available on PC, it will reach millions more players. Additionally, it'll get even more attention when the highly anticipated GTA 6 Online mode releases. Only time will tell just how far GTA 6 will go, but if Swifties are lucky, this record will motivate the artist to kick off a new worldwide tour.
