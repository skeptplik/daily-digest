A Neuralink participant has shared a video showcasing how the technology has allowed him to drive boats, fly planes, and get in shootouts in GTA 6 with his mind.
Neuralink shared a video of one of its few participants playing Rockstar Games' latest on social media yesterday. The two-minute highlight reel showed how the individual can use the brain implant tech to take Jason through Leonida with ease, saying that previous methods would often leave him sore.

"Before the old chip and surgery, I was using a mouth stick and an iPad," he explained. "I was essentially like a bird, just pecking at an iPad. There was a few games I could play, but obviously, it made your jaw sore and everything sore.
"So then, I get a chip, and it's like, 'What does this let me do?' Immediately, controlling a computer is like, 'boom.' Portal to the world. You just immediately get your portal to the world back."
Rockstar spent 13 years painstakingly crafting the world of GTA 6 to ensure every blade of grass and every line of dialogue was given an unprecedented amount of detail. Although it's maybe truer to life than some may have hoped, there's no denying that the amount of freedom the studio gives players to do everything from robbing banks to diving for sunken treasure has attracted tens of millions of players.
Despite guiding Jason and Lucia through GTA 6 by different means, the Neuralink participant shown today is one of those millions. In a follow-up post, the tech company, which was co-founded by controversial figure and billionaire Elon Musk, reiterated that the participant and others like him are part of an extremely limited clinical trial.
"Neuralink devices are investigational and have not been approved by the FDA or other regulatory authorities," it said. "This content features voluntary clinical trial participants sharing their personal experiences, which may not reflect all participants or future outcomes."
The first human patient to enter the Neuralink program used it to stay up all night playing Civilization 6 in 2024. A few months later, he was playing Slay the Spire.
