Starting on Sunday, eBay Live is hosting a six-day series of shopping streams of luxury resale ahead of the holiday season.
Featuring top sellers alongside fashion and cultural tastemakers to give shoppers a chance to seek out, consumers can discover and shop pieces that are in high demand, as seen in its latest 2026 fall Watchlist.
Bringing the report to life, the company is bringing the archival fashion, designer handbags and luxury watches that are thriving in the secondhand market to connect shoppers with coveted pieces through the livestream event.
Notable pieces featured in the upcoming eBay livestreams from the Watchlist Edit include an Hermès crocodile Birkin, archival Chanel and Louis Vuitton bags alongside Rolex and Cartier watches. Bidding for items starts at $99 and $1 for luxury timepieces.
The six-day lineup is Reserve Luxury with Aimee Kelly on Sunday, Bolt Luxury with Jake Fleming on Monday, Happy Jewelers and Sayeh Soltani on Tuesday, Watch by Juls and Cassie Thorpe on Wednesday, Swiss Ice and Albert Muzquiz on Thursday and Vookum on Friday.
Across eBay Live’s programming thus far this year, the company has seen a platinum Rolex Daytona sold for $170,365 and an Hermès crocodile Birkin sold for $31,376 — and Vookum’s “5M Auction” drew nearly 50,000 viewers. The seller, who hosted 2026’s highest-value stream, will return for a final night of the holiday series with Rolex Daytona models.
Reserved Luxury’s livestream will feature the rare Hermès crocodile Birkin, a Balenciaga City bag, Bottega Veneta Andiamo, Fendi Baguette and Dior Saddle bag. The live shopping selection reflects some of the most notable pieces being sold on eBay, with global listings for the Balenciaga Le City bag surging 936 percent year-over-year, followed by the Bottega Veneta Andiamo up 43 percent, Fendi Baguette up 42 percent and Dior Saddle up 30 percent.
A rare 2026 Matthieu Blazy Chanel runway flap bag will also be up for auction during Reserved Luxury’s livestream alongside archival Chanel and the limited-edition Louis Vuitton Artycapucines bag by Tschabalala Self.
Chanel has continued to be a force on eBay and has been the most-purchased luxury brand in the past five years. Searches for Matthieu Blazy jumped 291 percent and Karl Lagerfeld‘s era of Chanel listings has risen 87 percent year-over-year.
Watch by Juls will feature some of the coveted items within the chronograph space: Rolex’s Datejust and Submariner, Cartier Tank alongside Audemars Piguet and Patek Philippe timepieces. According to eBay’s Watchlist, resale values for luxury watches continue to rise — average sale prices for the Cartier Tank Louis Cartier are increasing by 151 percent and the Cartier Baignoire is increasing by 99 percent. Yet still, Rolex models continue to dominate as the marketplace’s most-purchased luxury timepieces.
“EBay has always brought people together around the things they love, and eBay Live builds on that by making shopping more interactive and community-driven. As consumers embrace new ways to shop, Live brings the excitement of discovery to life, connecting shoppers with expert sellers and unique finds just in time for the holidays,” said Jesse Kiefer, general manager and vice president of eBay Live.
