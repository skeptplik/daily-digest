A U.S. Congressional panel has recommended establishing a senior-level channel with India for succession contingency planning for the 14th Dalai Lama, arguing that China may turn the process into an international legitimacy contest.
The U.S.-China Economic and Security Review Commission, in a report to the U.S. Congress, made a strong pitch to recognise the Gaden Phodrang Trust as the sole legitimate authority to identify and recognise the successor to the 14th Dalai Lama.
The report by the bipartisan Commission said China was likely to use coercive tools to shape the outcome of the succession process.
It said that the U.S. Congress should adopt a policy of not recognising any successor to the Dalai Lama who is selected or installed by China.

“To carry out this policy and ensure U.S. government readiness for the Dalai Lama’s succession, Congress should direct the Secretary of State to… establish a senior-level channel with the Government of India for succession contingency planning, including intelligence sharing on Chinese influence operations,” the report said.
It also asked the U.S. Secretary of State to lead an internal interagency tabletop exercise on succession scenarios, with participation by the National Security Advisor, the Director of National Intelligence, and the Secretary of Defence.
The commission also asked the U.S. Secretary of State to conduct Track 1.5 and Track 2 dialogues and tabletop exercises with Indian counterparts on the political and security implications of succession scenarios.
The Commission also wanted the U.S. to coordinate with allies and partners on a diplomatic response that denies international legitimacy to any successor selected by China.
It also wanted the U.S. to counter succession-related influence operations by strengthening the Central Tibetan Administration’s cybersecurity and resilience against China’s cyber and artificial intelligence (AI)-enabled disinformation campaigns.
Set up by the Dalai Lama in 2011, the Gaden Phodrang trust will have the sole authority to recognise his future reincarnation.
