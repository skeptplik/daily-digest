Nike has plans to get creative with its classic Air Force 1 sneaker in 2027 as seen on upcoming square-toe and polygonal variations. And at the Beaverton, Ore.-based company’s Investor Day event Tuesday, it previewed another unexpected, yet on-trend spin on the retro basketball sneaker.
Shared by Jacques Slade on social media is the Nike Air Force 1 Mule. The latest spin on the sneaker features a number of key changes while maintaining the Air Force 1’s overall profile and signature details. Previewed in pink and off-white suede styles, the mule variation removes the model’s shoelaces and midsole, wrapping its suede upper all the way to the rubber outsole. A suede insole and liner further the premium feel of the updated build, while the shoe’s traditional Swoosh logos and “Air” branding are left unaltered.

While this particular Air Force 1 Mule is a new addition to the sportswear brand’s lineup, it’s not the first time Nike has given the beloved sneaker a backless update. In 2018, Nike introduced the women’s Air Force 1 Lover XX Premium, a modification which kept the Air Force 1’s thick midsole but trimmed down the upper even more than the upcoming mule. Nike, Inc.’s Jordan Brand has also issued mule versions of the classic Air Jordan 1 built for golf.
The new Air Force 1 Mule won’t be released until next year, but there’s plenty of other noteworthy Air Force 1s that are closer on the horizon including a camouflage-covered collaboration with Supreme, a Chanel-esque quilted iteration and more shaggy pony hair makeups in bold colors.
Specific release details for the Nike Air Force 1 Mule have yet to be confirmed, but the model will make its retail debut in 2027. Readers can expect pricing information and further colorways in the coming months and in the meantime, a full look can be found in the gallery below.
