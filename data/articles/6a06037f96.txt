Fashion designer Rebecca Minkoff has teamed up with model and television personality Grace Ann Nader on a three-piece capsule collection spanning the outerwear, footwear and accessory categories.
The collaboration sees Minkoff and Nader take their friendship into the fashion space. The project was born organically after the two attended the 2025 CFDA Awards, where Nader was the designer’s guest. The capsule served as an opportunity to work toward Nader’s goal of broadening her role in the fashion industry beyond modeling, aiming to become more involved in the creative and design processes. The statement pieces in the collection bring together Minkoff’s downtown-cool approach with Nader’s free-spirited style.
The Grace Ann cropped jacket, priced at $248, is a black bomber-inspired piece featuring a contrasting brown faux-fur collar, button closure and overall relaxed shape.
The $239 Carson boot is done in rich black suede, with a silhouette distinguished by relaxed ruching and accented by beads for an edgy-meets-free-spirited vibe. It features a pointed toe and stiletto heel as well.
Lastly, the “Holland” Nolita hobo, marked at $398, is a slouchy genuine-suede leather bag named for Nader’s mother. The bag features stud detailing, antique silver hardware and a metal handle. Functional aspects include a detachable strap as well as interior and exterior slip pockets.
The capsule launched on Monday and can be purchased from Rebecca Minkoff. It is currently planned as a limited drop with the potential for future collaborations.
Rebecca Minkoff’s business, acquired by Sunrise Brands in 2022, signed multiple licensing deals last year in a significant brand expansion reported exclusively by WWD, with deals for categories including handbags, footwear, loungewear, sleepwear and jewelry. It was also announced last year that the designer would have a 15-piece apparel collection appear on QVC, called RM Studio x Rebecca Minkoff for QVC.
Nader, represented by Ford Models and WME, stars on the reality docuseries “Love Thy Nader” alongside her sisters Brooks, Mary Holland and Sarah Jane; the second season is set to premiere in the first quarter of 2027. In addition to her modeling and TV endeavors, Nader is passionate about philanthropy. The Louisiana native graduated from the University of Georgia and earned a master’s in public health from New York University.
