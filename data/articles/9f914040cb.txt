Sometimes a movie's spectacle is so great that it really does need to be experienced in a theater, preferably in the best possible format available to you. Project Hail Mary, this year's stunning Andy Weir adaptation starring Ryan Gosling, is one such movie. It may have been difficult to catch in IMAX, however, as IMAX screens are a hot commodity and the film has been out of theaters for a while. If you missed it the first time around, Amazon MGM Studios has an end-of-year treat for you.
As announced on the film's official X account, Project Hail Mary will return to theaters in all formats this December. That includes IMAX and 70mm. The special event begins on Dec. 11, but it's not clear if it's a one-day-only event or if Project Hail Mary will have IMAX screens up until Dune: Part 3's release on Dec. 18.
The film adaptation of Andy Weir's 2021 novel of the same name follows Ryland Grace (Gosling) as he wakes up on a spaceship without his memory. Grace encounters the alien Rocky and the two embark on a shared mission to save their species, while Grace slowly begins to recall the events that led him to go on the mission in the first place.
Project Hail Mary became an instant critical and commercial success when it was released earlier this year. Much of that success was due to Gosling's heartwarming performance and the incredible puppetry behind Rocky. The film has already garnered some awards buzz, and its December theatrical re-release — right around when awards voters begin considering end-of-year-awards contenders — is a smart move. Of course, the movie is also currently streaming on Amazon Prime Video if you want to stay in the comfort of your own home.
Ryan Gosling is returning to sci-fi in 2027 after Project Hail Mary
If Gosling manages to secure an Oscar nomination for Project Hail Mary, which is a big if, then it'll come ahead of another big sci-fi turn for the actor. Next year, Gosling is set to star in Star Wars: Starfighter, which is helmed by Deadpool & Wolverine director Shawn Levy. The film is set to take place five years after the events of The Rise of Skywalker, which means it will go into some uncharted territory for the franchise. The movie is slated to be released on May 28, 2027.
After that, Gosling is set to team up with Levy once again in 2028 for Marvel's Ghost Rider reboot. After years of openly saying he wanted the role, Gosling's casting was finally revealed at San Diego Comic-Con. It is also rumored that Heated Rivalry breakout Connor Storrie will play the film's villain. Gosling sure is busy.
