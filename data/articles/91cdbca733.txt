Trump says ‘taking seriously’ reports Iran planning Washington dirty-bomb attack
Issued on:
Share
President Donald Trump said on Tuesday the US was taking seriously the possibility that Iran could be planning an attack involving a so-called dirty bomb in Washington, after being asked about a CNN report citing US intelligence assessments. Iran's Foreign Ministry spokesperson denied the allegations in a post on X on Tuesday, calling them "baseless."
