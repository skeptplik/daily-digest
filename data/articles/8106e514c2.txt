Live: Russian attacks target bridges in Kyiv, knocking out heating and water
Russian strikes hit two major bridges in Kyiv late Monday, part of an overnight attack that damaged critical infrastructure and disrupted heating and water in parts of the capital, its mayor said. Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelensky had warned of an attack by Russia in the coming days even as negotiators representing Kyiv as well as Europe and the United States held talks on future negotiations with Russia aimed at finding a path to end the war.
- Ukraine for the second day in a row hit a symbolic bridge that links the annexed Crimean peninsula to Russia, a Moscow-installed official said Tuesday.
- Russian President Vladimir Putin could meet North Korean leader Kim Jong Un if he also attends this week's APEC summit in China, Interfax news agency quoted Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov as saying on Monday.
- Zelensky says intelligence shows that Russia is planning new strikes on Ukrainian targets Monday evening, overnight and Tuesday, according to a report by Reuters.
(FRANCE 24 with AP, AFP and Reuters)
