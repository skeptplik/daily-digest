For our latest roundup, we have gathered five unusual streetwear designs by big brands, from a hoodie designed to smuggle Chipotle burritos to Crocs shaped like giant Lego bricks.
Supermarket chain Aldi and London fashion brand Agro Studio teamed up to design a puffer jacket that resembles a giant baked potato.
The cosy jacket has a mottled brown exterior that looks like potato skin and features silver cuffs that resemble the tin foil that baked potatoes are wrapped in.
American fast-food chain Chipotle has also created streetwear with a food connection – a hoodie with a hidden, insulated pocket built specifically for people to smuggle burritos into the cinema.
“Movie nights deserve real, craveable food,” Chipotle senior director of social and influencers Kirby Ann Connell said. “And since our fans were already sneaking Chipotle to theatres, we figured we’d make it a little easier.”
Toymaker Lego and footwear brand Crocs launched a pair of oversized-Lego Crocs as part of a multi-year partnership.
The shoe, which is shaped like a clog, was made to the scale of Lego’s two-by-four bricks and has four branded studs on the upper.
Aptly named Trolley Bag, this handbag by designer Nik Bentel was created to resemble supermarket Lidl’s shopping trolleys.
It is made from industrial stainless steel and has a gridded rectangular shape, as well as a handle in Lidl’s yellow-and-blue hues.
Perhaps the most innovative of all of these designs is fast-food brand KFC’s Pickle Puffer, a transparent coat filled with gherkins.
Filled with sliced pickles and acid-green brine, it’s “a bit unhinged, but that’s the point,” said KFC UK & Ireland brand manager James Channon.
