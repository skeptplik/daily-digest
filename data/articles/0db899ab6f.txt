South Africa was once a welcoming country; a refuge for those fleeing conflict and persecution. But for thousands of foreigners, the dream of a new life in South Africa is turning into more of a nightmare. Against a backdrop of unemployment and economic hardship, anti-immigration movements are gaining ground. Our correspondents report.
Refugees living in South Africa are being driven from their homes, businesses are being vandalised and foreigners are even being targeted in hospitals and universities. Behind this growing wave of xenophobia lies deep social anger, but also political rhetoric that blames migrants for the country's problems.
For those who once saw South Africa as a land of hope – an El Dorado – that era now seems firmly in the past.
