Roblox has launched a new creator program that lets developers convert existing games to its platform for free.
As Roblox Game Solutions senior director Jim Greer explained, Roblox has "built agentic conversion tools that bring games to Roblox, and today we're opening the program to everyone."
Developers with games built on any engine, including Unity and Unreal, can apply to have Roblox convert their titles to the platform.
The program will initially include a limited group of developers and games across mobile, PC, and console. Approved developers will be asked to provide their source code and assets.
Roblox clarified that while it "may use AI-assisted tools" to work with a developer's code, "the code and assets provided won't be used to train or fine-tune AI models."
Roblox will handle the conversion at no cost, and developers will retain ownership of their game, intellectual property, and the Roblox version. Nothing will be published on Roblox's platform unless a developer chooses to do so.
In September, Roblox introduced the Everywhere initiative, enabling creators to publish games as standalone apps across multiple platforms.
Roblox also announced that creators will soon be able to enable offline solo play, with early access available before the full release in mid-2027.
