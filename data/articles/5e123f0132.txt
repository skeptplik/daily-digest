‘Sharper, longer slowdown’ predicted for Australian economy as fifth interest rate hike looms
Australia’s economy is set for a “sharper, longer slowdown”, major bank economists say, as the Reserve Bank is forced to keep interest rates higher to rid the nation of persistent inflationary pressures.
Belinda Allen, the head of Australian economics at CBA, said the extended Middle East conflict, collapsing house prices and a more aggressive central bank would drive real GDP down to just 1% by the middle of next year, nearly half the previous estimate of 1.8%.
Growth through 2027 would be barely stronger at an estimated 1.1%, according to the updated projections.
