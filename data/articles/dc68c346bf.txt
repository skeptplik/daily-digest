Indie developer Studio Bravada has announced its closure after its second title, Extremely Powerful Capybaras, failed to recoup development costs over three years.
In a statement shared on social media, the Lisbon-based studio said it had "reached a point where we cannot continue operating in the long term."
"We have been trying to recover from Extremely Powerful Capybara's release, and it has now snowballed into an unsustainable situation. We weren't able to support online gameplay with the stability that we needed in all regions on launch day, especially China."
Extremely Powerful Capybaras launched in December 2023 to mixed reviews on Steam. It was the studio's second game, following its debut title, Sky Caravan, released in May 2022.
"We're not blaming anyone; we're responsible for the game's state on release," the developer said. "We wish we'd done better. It's just the reality."
Studio Bravarda stated it pursued other projects and transitioned game engines from Unity to Godot, but "funding opportunities [became] incredibly hard to get" as an indie developer. "Our industry has been a mess for some time [and] our new games made in Godot were not able to secure funding."
"Work-for-hire projects became our lifeline. [But] at a certain point, you realise you are just moving forward for the sake of moving forward. All the while, time goes by, personal sacrifices pile up, and money problems accumulate. Together, we realised that it is time to accept the journey for what it is, not for what we'd like it to be."
The developer concluded: "Throughout six years, six friends fulfilled their childhood dream together, partnered with amazing people around the world and released two games on multiple platforms that have been played by hundreds of thousands of people. We will be thankful for the rest of our lives to everyone who helped make this a reality."
Studio Bravarda was founded in 2020 by a group of Brazilian developers. Extremely Powerful Capybaras won Best Multiplayer at the inaugural Gamescom Latam Awards in 2024.
