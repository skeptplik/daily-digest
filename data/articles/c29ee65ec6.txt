“It’s so stupid and it changed my voice”- that’s what Selena Gomez recently said about vaping nicotine. She’s now stopped, but what could the long term affects be for people who use vapes? There aren’t many scientific studies yet with proof about how vaping may impact your health and your vocals. But anecdotally, there are examples of singers who say they are losing vocal range and their tone is being affected after vaping or smoking e-cigarettes. We speak to Cypriot singer and vocal coach Alex Panayi about changes he’s noticed in singers who vape, and Los Angeles-based medical doctor Reena Gupta tells us how it may impact the voice.
