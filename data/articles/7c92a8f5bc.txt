After being embroiled in a legal dispute for a few years, the Estée Lauder Cos. and travel retailer DFA formed a global partnership in 2025 to launch ELC brands across DFA stores.
At the time, this was viewed as a move by Lauder to boost and diversify its travel retail offering, which had been impacted in the years following the pandemic, especially in Asia.
Now, they’ve renewed their partnership and are ready to debut the first store featuring three of ELC’s prestige beauty brands — Le Labo, Tom Ford Beauty and Jo Malone London — at Miami International Airport.
Olivier Dubos, senior vice president and general manager, travel retail worldwide at the Estée Lauder Cos., said: “This new store at Miami International Airport reflects ELC’s continued investment in the Americas and our commitment to expanding the reach of our prestige beauty portfolio. By leveraging the respective strengths of these three brands, we are committed to creating meaningful connections with passengers and unlocking new opportunities for growth.”
Duty Free Americas chief executive officer Jerome Falic added: “The introduction of these brands at Miami International Airport marks an important first milestone in our collaboration and reflects our shared ambition to elevate the travel retail experience for all passengers. This is just the beginning, and we look forward to building on this partnership with further store openings across our network in the months ahead.”
