With its large L3 cache, the AMD Ryzen 7 9800X3D is a brilliant 8-core, 16-thread gaming CPU for the very best in desktop performance.
As per our benchmarks below, the 9800X3D is second only to the 9850X3D in terms of gaming performance when running on our testing suite. Notably, the X3D SKUs have very good 1% low scores when it comes to frame rate thanks to the aforementioned cache. At this price, this 9800X3D is a fantastic option for the beginnings of a 4K gaming rig, or an AM5 upgrade.
Naturally, this is a gaming-focused CPU, so it lags a little behind Intel's offerings when it comes to more productivity-focused apps that require heavier threading. This is first and foremost a gaming CPU, but it can more than handle other desktop applications, too.
The 9800X3D offers boost clocks of up to 5.2GHz, but doesn't come with a cooler, so you'll need to provide your own solution, either an air cooler or an AIO. As noted, this will slot easily into any existing AM5 setup, and is especially enticing if you've already got a powerful GPU or DDR5 RAM ready to go.
