Live: Ukraine, Russia hold second day of US-mediated talks in Abu Dhabi
Washington called for a "new" security framework on the first day of US-led talks between Ukraine and Russia in Abu Dhabi, as Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said he was "happy" with the changes in approach by US mediators. Negotiations are set to continue Wednesday. Follow our liveblog for the latest updates.
- Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said Europe should this month choose its representative in Ukraine-Russia peace talks.
- Top diplomats from the Group of Seven industrialised democracies gathered in Paris with clear differences on global crises and questions about how US President Donald Trump will approach foreign policy after his Republican Party’s heavy losses in the midterm elections.
- If Russia is allowed to win the war in Ukraine it is likely to also take land from other countries, including NATO member states, Danish Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen said Tuesday.
