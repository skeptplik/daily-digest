From self-cleaning vacs to bots that also mop, you can save hundreds on many of our favorite models.
This year’s sale has made some pricier robot vacuums a lot more affordable. The Eufy Omni C28, for example, now costs about what you’d expect to pay for a budget bot, while the Matic — which impressed us with its almost humanlike navigation — is getting a rare discount in the form of a bundle. If none of those are quite what you’re looking for, we’ve also found good deals on models from Roborock, Dreame, and more, with picks below for a variety of budgets and cleaning needs.
Roborock’s first roller-mop robot vacuum, the Curv 2 Flow, features flagship specs at a midrange price, including excellent mopping, a retractable mop cover, 20,000Pa suction, AI-powered obstacle avoidance, and a compact design. Read our review.
Despite lacking a multifunction dock, the budget-friendly robot vacuum and mop offers an impressive set of features for the price, including AI-powered obstacle avoidance, sonic mopping, automatic mop lifting, and a self-emptying dock.
The Matic robot vacuum and mop is ideal for cluttered homes with pets, multiple floors, and a mixture of carpets and hardwood. It offers almost humanlike navigation, tackling high-pile rugs and mopping up wet spills. The quiet bot also requires less maintenance than many rivals thanks to its onboard water tank and dust bag. Read our review.
The Eufy Omni C28 combines excellent mopping performance with a small self-cleaning dock that’s well suited for apartments and other smaller spaces. It navigates around furniture and into tight areas with ease, while its roller mop does a great job cleaning hard floors.
The Dreame X60 Ultra is a powerful robot vacuum and mop that does a great job of cleaning carpets thanks to its dual roller brushes and 35,000Pa of suction. It can climb over tall thresholds, squeeze under low furniture, and automatically detach its mop pads when vacuuming carpets.
The Narwal Flow 2 is an excellent robot vacuum for hardwood floors. A superb mop with a wide flat roller pad and a good vacuum, the Flow 2 also has top-notch navigation and obstacle detection. Its sleek base station is also less of an eyesore than most models. Read our review.
Roborock’s Saros 20 stands out for its ability to climb tall thresholds, avoid obstacles with impressive accuracy, and clean under low furniture thanks to its slim design. It also combines powerful 36,000Pa suction with great spinning mop pads, which is why we recommend it for homes with multiple floor types and tricky transitions between rooms.
Ecovacs’ robot vacuum and mop can pretreat stains, keep carpets dry with a smart mop cover, and deliver powerful 22,000Pa suction. It also includes an all-in-one dock that automatically empties the dustbin, refills the water tanks, and washes the mop with heated, pressurized water.
The Matter-compatible SwitchBot K11 Plus is ideal for small apartments, bedrooms, and home offices. It can easily navigate around chair and desk legs and into tight spaces that most other bots miss. It also features a commendable 6,000Pa of suction power — which is great for this price point — and works well on both hardwood and carpet.
With high suction power, an improved brush system, and the ability to scale thresholds up to 6cm, the X50 Ultra stands out as a great robot vacuum-mop hybrid. While its 20,000Pa suction isn’t quite as powerful as the X60 Ultra’s and it can’t tackle especially high thresholds as well, it’s still an excellent option. Its edge cleaning also isn’t quite as effective as the X60 Ultra’s, but you’re otherwise getting many of the same features for a lot less.
The Dreame L40 Ultra Gen 2 delivers powerful 25,000Pa suction, AI-powered obstacle avoidance, and a self-cleaning dock that empties the dustbin, washes the mop, and refills its water tanks. Unlike the X50 Ultra, however, it lacks the ability to climb high thresholds or stairs.
The Freo X Ultra features spinning triangular mop pads and can swing slightly to reach baseboards. Its large charging dock holds two big water tanks, while Narwal’s disposable onboard bin compresses the dust. It’s also relatively quiet when it returns to the dock, and its anti-tangle brush and 8,200Pa of suction perform well on most carpets.
