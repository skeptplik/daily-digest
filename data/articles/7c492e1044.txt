Technology has changed rapidly over the last few decades. While we've got hardware that's so much more powerful than what came 10 or 20 years ago, there's still a lot of enjoyment to be had in the classics. That's why there's a huge market for retro hardware, from game controllers designed to look like classic gamepads from old consoles, to full re-releases of old machines, with plenty of fun (and well-priced) accessories in between.
If you're buying for a retro aficionado, you're going to want to pick up something that's fun to use and has a serious nostalgic edge. There's a whole host of gear out there for those who prefer an '80s or '90s aesthetic, rather than the products we live with today.
You can relive the glory days of yesteryear today with these retro gift ideas. We've put together some of the best ideas for a wistful techie who can upgrade their setup, with old-school designs ready for today's hardware. Check out the options we've hand-picked for you below.
This miniature recreation of a classic NeoGeo arcade cabinet includes 40 games (the average price of a Neo Geo game in the 1990s was $200, $8000 for all 40 games!) and includes classics like Metal Slug, The King of Fighters and of course, Fatal Fury. The console has scaled versions of the original controls, a 3.5-inch screen, and runs from a USB-C power supply.
Need a bigger screen? You can connect via HDMI. External controllers and NeoGeo Mini Game controllers (available separately) can be used to retro game from the sofa!
This Game Boy-style console is tiny (0.7 x 0.3 x 1.2 inches!) and is powered by the Raspberry Pi Pico's RP2040 microcontroller. Programmable via a web interface, you can make your own games and demos to share with others.
This gorgeous, retro-inspired N Edition Retro Cube 2 wireless speaker from 8BitDo offers stereo sound, with support for 2.4 GHz and Bluetooth connectivity, along with USB for wired use, and includes a wireless charging dock.
A 1:1 scale replica of the gaming handheld that dominated the 1990s. This is a glorious piece of retro tech, preserved in Lego form for your shelves.

It has multi-function connectivity and a unique look that's perfect for a gaming enthusiast. It also comes with two separate 'super buttons' that you can map to keys or macros.
