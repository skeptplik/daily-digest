Tonight, the 2026 LACMA Art + Film Gala is underway at the Los Angeles County Museum of Art in California. The night, co-chaired by actor Leonardo DiCaprio and trustee Eva Chow, will bring together influential figures from the worlds of art, film, fashion and entertainment to support the museum’s mission to make film more central to its future programming. Artist Vija Celmins and filmmaker Denis Villeneuve will also receive special honors.
Before the ceremony begins inside, however, the red carpet beforehand will see loads of Hollywood A-listers arrive in grand style. Every year, the LACMA Gala—which is supported by Gucci—produces memorable (and extremely glamorous) formal attire moments, and this evening is sure to be no different. (Last year, for one, saw some of newly-installed creative director Demna’s first big red carpet moments, like stars Elle Fanning and Doja Cat appearing in their boldest looks by the Italian label.)
