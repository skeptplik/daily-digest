As Shopbop’s Senior Fashion Director, Caroline Maguire does a lot: Her responsibilities span buying, merchandising, creative, marketing and PR (among others), and she plays a lead role in shaping the Amazon-owned retailer’s point of view and assortment strategy. With more than 20 years of fashion industry experience — including a long tenure in ecommerce, and a decade at Shopbop in particular — she’s an expert on the state of shopping today, as well as emerging designers and what’s driving consumer behavior across luxury and contemporary fashion. Plus, she has impeccable personal style. (Just check out Shopbop’s “Caroline’s Closet” section for her amazing hand-selected curation.)
“Day-to-day is not ever the same,” Maguire said of her role, adding, “I’m constantly collaborating across all teams at Shopbop, whether it’s from the buying team, the merchandising team, the styling editorial team, to the creative content team. My team is actually kind of the glue that centers and pulls everyone together.”
Maguire also shared insights from her own career trajectory, the emerging indie designers she’s excited about right now, the major fashion trends she expects to see through the holidays and into 2027 and more. Watch the full interview on YouTube below.
