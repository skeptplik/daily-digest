Ask Mikayla Nogueira if she knew her brand, POV Beauty, would explode prior to launching and her answer is simple: Yes.
“I’ve done so many successful collaborations with other brands in the past, and that was a good pre-test for me to see if I could create something and if people would want to buy it,” said Nogueira, who rose to fame via her signature TikTok beauty tutorials in 2020 and now counts 20 million followers across platforms. “But also, bringing on the right people early on gave me the confidence that I would be successful.”
Led by Nogueira and cofounder and chief executive Ani Hadjinian, POV launched in 2025 with backing from Imaginary Ventures and has since taken a slow-and-steady approach to expanding its range of skin prep essentials and color cosmetics. Its rise has been meteoric: This fall, the brand entered Sephora — even headlining the retailer’s launch on TikTok Shop in September — and raised a $15 million funding round led by Silas Capital.
“A lot of [purchases] comes from the trust within Mikayla’s community that we would create something that earns their adoption,” said Hadjinian, adding that the brand takes a high-touch approach to product education, a key differentiator for POV. “It is [about] consistently coming back with a message and not veering off from it, because people have to hear it multiple, multiple times.”
In terms of POV’s product development — the brand most recently launched Trace It lip markers — Nogueira draws from her own ongoing experience as a reviewer and tutorialist to find the white space.
“We started creating Trace It right around the time that lip stains were exploding and everyone was talking about them, and I thought, I would love to do this in a bit of a different way,” she said, adding the final result is “more of a liquid lipstick in a marker that is extremely long-wear…I basically took everything about lip stains that I didn’t enjoy and changed it.”
The brand also actively seeks out community feedback with each new launch.
“When we look at the pipeline, it really is about discipline and doing things that matter, not necessarily chasing trends,” said Hadjinian.
Nogueira added that her own following acts as something of a supersized focus group (“they don’t hold back,” she said) and the brand has its own app called My POV designed specifically for product reviews, photo sharing and fostering discourse. “We also have a channel on there just for future launches — what do you want to see? What should we create? What’s missing? It’s cool to be able to get that feedback directly from the POV customer,” she said.
The founder said that maintaining her integrity as a creator who people can come to for broad-ranging beauty advice and reviews is critical even as she continues to operate just as firmly in a founder role.
“You’ll never see me solely using POV — it just doesn’t make any sense for me. I’m obsessed with makeup. I’m obsessed with beauty,” Nogueira said. “Rather than me saying you should only use POV, I want to say prep your skin with POV and then go in with your favorite makeup products.”
