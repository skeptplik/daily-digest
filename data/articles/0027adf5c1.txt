These are the main developments from Russia's war in Ukraine on November 17, 2026:
Ukraine holds new talks with US, European negotiators
Ukraine held fresh talks with US and European negotiators on ending the war with Russia on Monday evening, President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said.
Ukrainian officials held online talks with US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner, as well as representatives from Ukraine's European partners.
Kushner said progress had been made towards an agreement, although significant challenges remain to finding a path to peace.
The online talks come less than a week after delegations from the US, Ukraine and Russia met in Abu Dhabi for their first trilateral talks in nine months.
"Diplomatic processes are actively underway and we hope that these efforts will not be in vain," Zelenskyy said. "Our team is working in the most constructive manner possible."
In his nightly video address, Zelenskyy also warned that Kyiv had intelligence that Russia would launch major strikes on targets across Ukraine on Monday evening and into Tuesday.
The Ukrainian leader warned that Russia "must know that for every act of evil they commit, there will be a just response from us."
In his remarks, Zelenskyy cited a Ukrainian intelligence report that said North Korea was supplying "dozens of ballistic missiles to Russia each month."
"This is extremely dangerous and not only for Ukraine," he said. "By using its ballistic missiles in this war, North Korea is making its weapons more effective. This creates additional challenges for the entire region around Korea."
Ukraine hits the Crimean Bridge for second day in a row, Russian authorities say
Ukraine struck a bridge connecting the annexed Crimean peninsula and the Russian mainland late Monday.
The same bridge over the Kerch Strait was also struck a day earlier, Russian-installed authorities said.
"At the moment, traffic is suspended," Crimea's Moscow-installed governor, Sergey Aksyonov said Tuesday.
Russian officials also said Ukraine had struck oil refineries on Monday.
Monday's attack was the largest such assault on Russia since it launched itsfull-scale invasion of Ukraine in 2022. Russia says it intercepted over 1,300 Ukrainian drones during the attack.
Overnight into Tuesday, Ukraine targeted Moscow with nearly 200 drones.
Moscow mayor Sergey Sobyanin said early Tuesday morning that Russian air defenses had downed 20 drones heading towards the capital.
Russia hits two major bridges in Kyiv: What we know
Russia struck two bridges over the Dnipro River in Kyiv late Monday, Mayor Vitali Klitschko said.
"According to preliminary information, there have been hits on the North and South bridges in the capital," Klitschko said on Telegram.
In a later post, Klitschko said the attack "caused damage to critical infrastructure," adding that there were "disruptions to water and heating supplies" because of strikes.
Earlier this month and in October, a series of Russian attacks hit several bridges in the Ukrainian capital, causing major disruption.
Earlier on Monday, Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelenskyy had warned Russia was planning strikes for Monday night and into Tuesday.
"Tonight, overnight, and throughout tomorrow, please pay attention to missile danger alerts," he said.
"And they there, in Russia, must know that for every one of their evils there will be our just response," Zelenskyy added.
