U.S. flight disruptions stretched into Sunday as airlines worked to return to normal operations following the four-hour nationwide stoppage on Friday as authorities continued to investigate what caused it.
After canceling more than 6,600 flights on Saturday, airlines scrapped nearly 1,900 on Sunday, with more than 6,300 others being delayed, as of 3 p.m. New York time, according to data from tracker FlightAware.
American Airlines Group Inc. is showing the most canceled flights so far on Sunday, with 613, while JetBlue Airways Corp. has shed more than a quarter of its schedule.
