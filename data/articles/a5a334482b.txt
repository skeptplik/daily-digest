Kathryn Hahn stopped by “Good Morning America” on Wednesday in New York City in an all-gray look. She broke up the monochrome ensemble with a pair of sharp snake-print pumps.
The actress, who was on the daytime talk show promoting her new movie “Madden,” opted for a shoe design from Hagelstam, wearing the Neely Pump 95 Snake, which retails for $562. The animal print pump is designed with an architectural, ultra-elongated, pointy-toe structure.

The shoes feature a 95mm slim block heel, which measures 3.7 inches. The shoes are made with calf leather on the upper, with an embossed beige-and-black snake print. The high heels have a rubber outsole for support and comfort, which is a key element for the brand’s heeled designs.
The “Agatha All Along” star wore a look from the Tove Studio fall 2026 collection for the TV appearance. She opted for the Hope Skirt in gray, which is crafted from 100 percent virgin wool and is defined by a sculptural knot detail at the waist. She paired the mid-length skirt silhouette with the brand’s Efa Shirt.
Hahn was on the show to talk about “Madden,” which is a sports film directed by David O. Russell and follows the life of American football coach and commentator John Madden, portrayed by Nicolas Cage. The cast also features Christian Bale, John Mulaney, Sienna Miller and Shane Gillis, and will be available on Prime on Nov. 18.
Hagelstam is a luxury leather footwear brand founded in 2024 by Finnish designer and former blogger Sandra Hagelstam. The London-based designer’s goal is to make sure every pair she creates is a modern reinterpretation of a classic silhouette that is designed to transcend seasons.
