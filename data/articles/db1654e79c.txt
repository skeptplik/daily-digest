Congratulations are in order for Formula 1 driver Charles Leclerc and his wife Alexandra Leclerc, who just became parents!
On November 4, the couple took to Instagram to announce the birth to their first child: a baby girl named Bianca. They shared the first photo of Bianca, showing the baby’s little legs and feet in light pink knitwear and socks with tiny bows. According to the post, she was born on November 3.
Alexandra and Charles were first linked in 2023, and married in a small civil ceremony at Mairie de Monaco, (Monaco City Hall) in February 2026. Together, they already share a beloved miniature dachshund named Leo, who comes with the Leclercs everywhere—F1 paddock to modeling jobs. “We bring him everywhere. He’s always with us! We treat him like a child,” Alexandra previously told Vogue.
While the couple chose to keep their privacy when it came to sharing much about their pregnancy, Alexandra donned many fun maternity looks across the last nine months, including Christian Dior’s vintage fall 2000 newspaper print dress by John Galliano, at the Spanish Grand Prix in Madrid in September. To attend the Global Gift Gala at the beginning of October, she wore a shimmering blue custom Antoin Guerin gown. For more low-key affairs, she opted for Realisation Par slip dresses and her favored Hermès Oran sandals. Throughout, Alexandra stayed true to her ultra-feminine personal style with a healthy dose of diamonds, candy colors, and glitter.
In a September fan Q&A session, Charles revealed his joy and pride about becoming a father, saying: “I am going to become a dad and I'm very proud of that.”
Congratulations once again to Charles and Alexandra Leclerc!
