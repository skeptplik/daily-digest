Black Friday sales are finally upon us, and while we don’t celebrate the turkey-related holiday origins of the sales event here in Aus, Black Friday / Cyber Monday is increasingly the best time of the year to get our hands on powerful tech at tidy discounts. Adding to the festivities, Gigabyte are offering up to $602 off the GAMING A16 Laptop range, along with a host of other deals across Australian retailers. Here are our picks for the sale that you can pick up right now at Officeworks.
GIGABYTE GAMING A16
The i7 model offers both the largest dollar reduction and arguably the best value of these three laptops (purely for gaming performance bang-for-buck). An Nvidia RTX 5060 provides 8GB of VRAM to keep up with the latest AAA games, and the inclusion of a 1TB SSD allows for a decent handful of games to be installed without impacting performance. At sub-A$1,800, it serves as the practical sweet spot for 1200p gaming.
GIGABYTE GAMING A16 – Flagship Configuration
The flagship configuration of Gigabyte’s A16 range, featuring an Intel’s Core Ultra 9 processor, an Nvidia RTX 5060 and 16GB of DDR5 RAM. It’s a powerful gaming laptop and daily driver that can also handle CPU-heavy creative workloads, content creation, AI workflows or code compiling. It’s a beast of a laptop, though for pure gaming its performance is likely to be similar to the Core i7 variant. Consider the upgrade if you have broader ambitions than gaming or plan to multitask with a second monitor.
GIGABYTE EAGLE 16"
As the budget option of the three, this configuration pairs an AMD Ryzen 5 processor with an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4050 GPU and a 165Hz WUXGA panel. It should handle most titles at medium-settings, allowing for reliable 1080p/1200p gaming, but the hardware has lower performance ceilings than the other two options. With only 6GB of VRAM, and a 512GB SSD that will fill up quickly with a couple of AAA games. Still, it’s a great entry-level gaming laptop for the price.
