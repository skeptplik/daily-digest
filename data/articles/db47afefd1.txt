China moves to simplify forex settlements and make cross-border business easier
Draft measures would expand forex services, ease compliance requirements and simplify overseas trade payments, Safe says
China plans to expand foreign exchange settlement services and streamline cross-border trade payments, in a move to lower transaction costs and facilitate international commerce.
Among the key measures is a plan to roll out nationwide, from January 1, a scheme facilitating foreign exchange receipts and payments for cross-border trade. The programme has so far operated on a pilot basis.
