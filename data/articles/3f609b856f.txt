Israel’s president taps Eisenkot to form government, in setback for Netanyahu
The main challenger to the PM will have the first opportunity to build a ruling coalition after neither bloc won a clear majority
Israeli President Isaac Herzog said on Saturday night that he was giving opposition candidate Gadi Eisenkot the first opportunity to form a governing coalition following elections on October 27.
Eisenkot, a former military chief who ran as the main challenger to Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu, is now a step closer to ousting Israel’s longest-serving premier.
“This is the time to establish a national emergency government. I will do anything possible to prove us worthy,” Eisenkot posted on social media just after the presidential announcement.
