Mexico City – The San Francisco 49ers overwhelmed the Minnesota Vikings 35-10 on Sunday at Azteca Stadium, as the National Football League made a thrilling return to Mexico City after a four-year absence with its first international Sunday Night Football game.
More than 85,000 fans, most of them backing the 49ers, filled the stadium, underlining the popularity of the sport in Mexico, which is the NFL’s largest market outside the United States.
NFL Commissioner Roger Goodell had described the occasion as a “mini-Super Bowl,“ underlining the importance of a country that has hosted five previous regular-season games since the league first played in Mexico in 2005.
