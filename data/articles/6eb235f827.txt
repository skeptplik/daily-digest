Traditional HDDs are much slower than modern SSDs, but beyond the speeds, the main difference is price. You can buy much larger amounts of storage in the HDD form factor than the more expensive SSDs. If you need a lot of room for your files, or want to back up your data for cold storage, then 20TB can hold a fair amount of files, from video to photos to plain old system backups.
This 20TB WD Elements external drive is mains-powered, with a 5Gb/s Micro-USB Type B to USB 3 Type-A interface to connect to your PC. It's not a lightning-quick transfer rate, but it gets the job done and is perfectly fine as long as you're not in a hurry.
The WD Elements 20TB external HDD operates over USB 3.2 Gen 1 via a Micro B USB-B to USB-A, includes an AC adaptor, comes preformatted for Windows, and includes a two-year limited warranty.
The unit is not too large, with a height of 6.53 inches, a depth of 5.30 inches, and a 1.89-inch width. So, it shouldn't take up too much room on a desk.
