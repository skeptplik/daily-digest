A long-awaited judgment from the U.N.’s highest court on Thursday said that killings, destruction of homes and widespread sexual violence by Myanmar’s security forces amounted to state-sponsored genocide of the Rohingya Muslim minority population.
The decision comes nearly a decade after a 2017 military crackdown drove more than 730,000 Rohingya into Bangladesh, and as conflict, shrinking humanitarian aid and dangerous sea crossings continue to leave many members of the community displaced and vulnerable.
