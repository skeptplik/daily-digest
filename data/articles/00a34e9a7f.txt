As advanced and lifelike as the animatronic characters at Disney parks have become, they typically either stick to predefined movements and routines, or there’s an out of sight puppeteer helping make their behavior appear more spontaneous. That could soon change as Walt Disney Imagineering R&D has revealed three new Audio-Animatronic prototypes that rely on Amazon’s AWS cloud and AI capabilities to respond in unique ways to guest interactions in real-time.
Disney’s new Star Wars animatronics interact with guests using AI
The three Anzellans each have distinct personalities and respond to guest interactions using hidden cameras and mics.
The three animatronics, named Vilo Vele, Kado Bre’a, and Jibu Bre’a, are based on the Star Wars alien characters called the Anzellans that were featured in The Mandalorian and Grogu film released earlier this year. The tiny bots will make their first public appearance in Las Vegas later this month where attendees of the AWS re:Invent conference will have a chance to interact with them, although the characters, and the technology powering them, could eventually find its way into Star Wars: Galaxy’s Edge and other attractions at Disney’s parks.
Unlike Disney’s Olaf robot that recently debuted, there’s no one secretly using a Steam Deck gaming handheld to bring the Anzellans to life. The animatronics instead each have hidden cameras and microphones they use to capture guest movements and verbal interactions which are processed by multiple AI models running on AWS cloud servers. One “fast-decision model” generates quick responses such as the animatronics quickly making eye contact so it appears as if they’re paying attention, while larger models plan out longer interactions.
However, the Anzellans movements and verbal responses aren’t generated on the fly by AI. Every movement and line of dialogue that’s selected and triggered by the models is prewritten by Disney Imagineers to ensure the animations stay true to each character, including unique personality quirks for each one. Although AI helps guide their actions, the Anzellans never deviate from the script.
