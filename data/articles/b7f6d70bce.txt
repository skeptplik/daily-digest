Supply chain security startup StepSecurity has raised $20 million in a Series A funding round, which brings the total raised by the company to $23 million.
The new investment round was led by Elephant, with additional support from Runtime Ventures, Inner Loop Capital, and SaaS Ventures.
Founded in 2021, Seattle-based StepSecurity has built a platform that combines developer-machine protection, repository checks, a hosted upstream proxy and policy gate, and a CI/CD security agent to identify and block malicious components.
According to the company, its platform can secure every stage of the software development process, including developers’ systems, AI coding agents, repositories, package registries, and CI/CD runners.
The startup offers a free community tier of its CI/CD runtime security agent Harden-Runner, already used by over 20,000 open source projects, which now also integrates a free Secure Registry proxy and policy gate tier to help open source maintainers block compromised packages.
StepSecurity will use the fresh funding to accelerate the development and distribution of its platform by growing its engineering team, enhancing the security research team’s capabilities, and expanding go-to-market efforts.
“We believe independent controls at all control points in agentic software pipelines are the strongest defense for agentic software development,” said StepSecurity co-founder and CTO Ashish Kurmi.
“We have seen our customers adopt one product, find value, and ask us to protect the next part of their development process. This funding gives us the resources to respond faster,” Kurmi added.
