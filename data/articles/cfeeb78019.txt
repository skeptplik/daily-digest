The UK’s National Health Service is giving players advice to help them avoid straining their hands once GTA 6 finally releases on November 19.
The service’s humorous PSA was posted to Instagram on November 16, just three days before Rockstar’s highly anticipated game launches. The post includes several photos taken by the NHS’s social media manager, whose hand has been wrapped up tight in a cast.
The poor employee explained that they underwent surgery to “remove a lump” from their hand, which they said is particularly “bad timing” due to GTA 6’s imminent release. To help others avoid injuring themselves and being unable to play, they shared some tips to keep your ligaments in tip-top shape.
For starters, the NHS is urging players to “try and take regular breaks.” This helps avoid eye strain, and getting up from your gaming chair or sofa every couple of hours is good for circulation, relieving muscle stiffness, and providing mental clarity. (That’s right — you might actually play better if you take regular breaks.)
The NHS’s post was particularly concerned with RSI, or repetitive strain injury, which it described as “pain that can be caused by repeated movement of part of the body, such as hands and fingers.”
RSI can happen for a number of reasons; sports like tennis or golfing can be culprits, as can jobs like hairdressing (using scissors and other tools), working an assembly line, and even typing at your computer.
While RSI is a general umbrella term for these types of injuries, carpal tunnel is a more specific condition that falls under the RSI label. Gamers are prone to this type of strain, due to pressing buttons quickly and repetitively, moving a joystick back and forth, and gripping one’s controller or mouse tightly while playing.
While a repetitive strain injury might legitimately be something you could call out of work for, you might have a hard time getting the time off, given how many players are already planning on taking time off from their jobs to roam around Vice City. There's already been a 17% increase in time-off requests from employees for November 19, and even world governments like Germany and Australia are talking about the game’s upcoming impact on the workforce.
In fact, estimates show that GTA 6 is likely to drain $1 billion from the US economy, since so many people will be staying at home to play on November 19. Some companies are getting a jump on the amount of ‘sick’ employees they’ll likely have by offering the time off ahead of time, with tech giant Logitech giving staff the option to take personal days for major video game releases. Just remember to take regular breaks — Vice City will be just fine without you for a few moments.
