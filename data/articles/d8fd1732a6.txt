I'd just stolen my very first airplane in GTA 6 and was happily cruising over the city when I looked out my window and saw an enormous purple building. I banked, slowed down, and flew a bit closer to read the huge sign on the roof.
VICE CITY CASINO
Oh, hell yes. I was so excited to throw my carefully accumulated three hundred dollars away on poker and slots that I immediately attempted my very first landing. After the explosion and my miraculous resurrection, I rushed back to the casino and crashed again, this time with my body into the locked front doors.
The casino in GTA 6 is closed. Trust me, I tried every door, and the only one that opened was a utility closet.
I was early into my playthrough, still in Chapter 1 of the GTA 6 story missions, so I thought maybe there'll be some mission that unlocks the casino so I can play some poker, try my luck at the slots, and maybe bet on the also-closed dog track—I assume it's a dog track because I've seen dogs in GTA 6, but no horses—that's right next door to the casino.
Nope! I'm now 100% done with the main story and that casino is still closed. And that's not the only business in GTA 6 that isn't open for business. Not even a half-mile away from the casino there's a go-cart track called Hot Skidz sporting a huge "Coming Soon" sign. I saw that early in my game as well, and checked back later. Still closed. I also found a second, smaller casino. Also closed.
What a letdown. I managed to get my motorcycle onto the go-cart track, and as I drove glumly around it, alone, having no fun, I perked up a bit. Maybe this just gives me something to look forward to. A hint of more singleplayer content that'll be coming to GTA 6 next year. Hopefully next year.
There's already a lot of seemingly unfinished stuff in GTA 6, partial systems that could be added to and completed, like:
- I can throw a frisbee but no one will catch it—not even dogs—or throw it back
- I can go to a tennis court, pick up a tennis racket and tennis ball, and hit the ball with the racket, but I can't actually play tennis
- Speaking of dogs, who would put so many dogs in a game and not let me have my own dog?
- There's a baseball bat and baseballs, but I can't play baseball
- The dog thing is madness. Gotta let us have dogs at some point
- There are so many cool houses but I can only live in the one currently assigned to me by the story
- There are hotels everywhere but I can't rent a room
- You can play eight-ball pool but not nine-ball? Pure insanity, even moreso than the dog decision
- There's tons of construction sites, so many it can't just be satire about America's horrible infrastructure, they've gotta at least build a new few things for us to play with
- Like, no offense to the snake, but I want a damn dog
I felt better after that, but as I went back to stare at that huge, closed casino—which is all lit up at night like it's actually open, even though it is not open, and I know this because I rechecked all the doors—I remembered how the casino in GTA 5 worked. It came to the game post launch, but only as part of GTA Online. It never opened in singleplayer.
But surely, why include the building at all in GTA 6 singleplayer if it wasn't also going to be in singleplayer? They wouldn't do that to us, would they? They wouldn't open it in GTA 6 Online and not open it in GTA 6 singleplayer.
That would be like putting dozens of dogs in a game and not letting us have our own dog. Madness.
