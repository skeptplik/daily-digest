Apple has officially revealed its streaming deal for 2026 and it's the best discount we've seen so far on an ad-free plan. New and eligible returning subscribers can get Apple TV for $6.99 per month for the first six months of their subscription. This is half the price of what the service usually costs, thanks to a fairly recent price increase bumping monthly plans up to $14.99/mo.
This promotion is available now and is currently set to end on November 30, 2026 at precisely 8pm PT. If you're hoping to save on streaming subscriptions heading into next year, this deal is a great way to do so. Just make sure you grab it before the end of Cyber Monday.
Apple TV Black Friday Deal Details
Apple TV has seen a pretty drastic price increase since it first launched, but it makes sense when you look at the number of high-quality original shows are on the platform now. You've got massive hits like Severance and Ted Lasso as well as some of the best new sci-fi shows like Silo and Murderbot. If you've never had an Apple TV subscription, there's a lot of really good TV you could watch in that six-month window.
If you're a returning subscriber, there's plenty to look forward to on the service as well. The slate of November releases includes Owen Wilson's golf series and a feel-good Chris Pratt movie. The highly anticipated Neuromancer series is also set to arrive in January 2027. Apple is also relatively good about consistently putting out new seasons of its most popular shows, so every month has at least something decent to watch.
More Black Friday Streaming Deals
Not all of the Black Friday streaming deals have gone live, but a number of other services have also kicked off their discounts alongside Amazon's official sale. Crunchyroll is discounting all of its subscription tiers by up to 60% and Paramount+ is offering the first two months of it's plans for $2.99 per month. These subscriptions are also available at a discount via Prime Video add-ons, though the same restrictions apply there too.
