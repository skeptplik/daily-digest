Florence Pugh coordinated her custom Valentino dress with Aquazzura sandals at the Governors Awards in Los Angeles on Sunday, where lifetime achievement and honorary awards were given out.
Pugh stepped out in Aquazzura‘s Love Struck sandal, which features dainty crystal-embellished straps. A triple-strap design intersects across the toes, while two more thin straps meet in an X-shape toward the top of the foot and ankle. The style is elevated by a 105 mm stiletto heel, providing a significant boost in height. Pugh wore the barely-there Love Struck sandal in a metallic silver colorway, which added extra shine to the look alongside its light-catching crystals.

Pugh’s footwear choice was the perfect complement to her custom Valentino dress, which was blush pink and embellished with a constellation of crystals concentrated most heavily along the bodice. The sleeves fell off the shoulder and the skirt transitioned into a dramatic train, textured with undulating lines of ruffles. A slit in the front of the skirt allowed her statement footwear to be seen.
Another striking footwear look displayed by Pugh recently was a pair of strappy black satin Vivienne Westwood platform pumps worn with a black satin Harris Reed look featuring large sculptural shoulders at an “East of Eden” FYC event.
