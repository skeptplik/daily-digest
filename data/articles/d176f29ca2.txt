Anyone who pre-ordered GTA 6 is entitled to one free month of GTA +, Rockstar’s subscription service. It's been around for a while now, proving worthwhile for fans of GTA Online who get exclusive items and opportunities in the game through it, as well as access to a library of the developer’s games, including the likes of GTA 5, Red Dead Redemption, and Bully. But it also comes with fresh benefits for those playing GTA 6.
These bonuses include store discounts, exclusive vehicles, and ways to make earning in-game cash easier. It’s not quite pay-to-win due to the small advantages it gives and GTA 6’s nature as a single-player game, but it will certainly give a leg up to those with a GTA+ subscription.
All of the listed benefits, which can be found on the in-game store page, can be found here:
- Venturas BLVD Elegy & Garage – A free ‘90 Annis Venturas BLVD Elegy, specially tuned and featuring an exclusive widebody design, as well as access to a single-car garage to store it, located in the Crosstown area of Vice City. This garage also includes a weapon locker and the ability to directly fence stolen items. These are only available from Chapter 3 of the story onward.
- Transit Services Bonuses – Scooter Bros Premium vehicle care, RydeMe rideshare app premium membership, free scooter, bike, and Seashark rentals, plus free rides on all Leonida public transport.
- Increased Sell Value – Benefit from a 10% increase on all eligible owned vehicle sales.
- Store Discounts – Get a 10% discount at select businesses, including weapons and clothes shops, hairdressers, tattooists, vehicle mod shops, and more.
Crucially, if you have signed up for GTA+, but want to experience the purest form of GTA 6 possible, these benefits can be switched off in the game’s settings menu. The pre-order bonus must be redeemed before March 31, 2027, and will renew automatically unless cancelled, costing $7.99/£6.99 per month after that. Of course, we are yet to hear anything about GTA 6’s potential online component, and how GTA+ may play into that equation.
All GTA 6 pre-orders also come with the Vintage Vice City, which can be purchased separately if you’re planning to buy the game after launch. Here are the details on what’s included, all of which become available in Chapter 3 of the story.
- ‘55 Vapid Stanier & Garage – A classic yellow and white sedan that can be stored in the Shore Court garage near Ocean Beach. You can collect it from the iconic Ocean View Hotel, which features prominently in GTA: Vice City.
- Outfits & Hairstyles — Jason’s vintage pastel coloured linen suit and Lucia’s red sequin mini dress can be paired with two 80s-inspired hairstyles for the couple. These become available once you’ve picked up the Stanier and can be found in any wardrobe or at any hairdresser.
- Exclusive Weapon Pattern — Most guns can be wrapped in a tropical pattern inspired by Vice City protagonist Tommy Vercetti’s iconic palm tree shirt. Available from any Ammu-Nation store once you’ve picked up the Stanier.
