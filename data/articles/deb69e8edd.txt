Now that Halloween has come and gone, we can officially put spooky season to rest—and the festive holiday season has come in to fill the void. And who better to ring in this cheery time of year than with Mariah Carey—the undisputed queen of Christmas? (Because, as she would say: “It’s tiiiime!”)
Consider her defrosted: This week, Carey was announced as the new star of Gucci’s 2026 holiday campaign, and last night the singer attended the Italian label’s private store event in New York City to kick off the festive party season.
In the new campaign video, Carey arrives at a holiday party decked out in full Demna-era Gucci—mix-and-mingling with guests, set to the tune of—what else?—her enduring hit “All I Want for Christmas Is You.” Last night, Carey kept the celebrations going by handing out (Gucci!) gifts to all of the party attendees. A full choir even sang renditions of some of her best Christmas hits.
Pre-soirée, however, Vogue couldn’t resist the chance to catch a private moment with Carey and ask her some of our burning questions. From her festive styling tips to her party hosting no-nos, read on for the superstar’s hottest holiday takes.
Mariah Carey: It was so fun shooting the campaign. I had a lot of favorite moments—mainly the one with the dog. Did you see the dog? So cute. That was festive.
I love everything about this time of year. It’s just … something that I can’t explain. I’ve been in love with Christmas my whole life, and now when we celebrate and we do all of the things, it opens up a whole other moment of festivity.
What are my traditions in the morning? I’m not a morning person, that’s the thing. I wait till mid-afternoon, and then we start with the opening of the gifts and all the things. It’s fun and festive.

It’s one of my favorite things. And then I’ll just eat that for the next few days.
