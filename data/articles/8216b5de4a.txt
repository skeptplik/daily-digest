What’s the opposite of gatekeeping? Welcome to “Payment Processing,“ in which we ask stylish, generally cool people who’ve tried it all about their favorite fashion and beauty buys.
Alexandra Nechita has been making art since she was literally 2 years old. She started painting with oils and acrylics at age 7 and held her first solo exhibition at 8. (Casual!) Millennials might remember her 1990s-era “Scholastic News” cover or when she played herself on a “Boy Meets World” episode in 1998. The Romanian-born, Los Angeles-based artist has since expanded her practice to include large-scale sculptures and public art installations, with works featured in prominent collections and public spaces internationally.
Outside the art world (or one could argue within it, too), Nechita has been dabbling in equally creative fashion content. Fans love her OOTDs at the whimsical pink figurative mirror she herself designed. (For those who covet the mirror but aren’t in the market for one that size, the artist launched a tabletop frame version as part of Nechita Studio, a collection of “functional art objects.”)
We caught up with Nechita to find out what luxury beauty and fashion items she has her eye on, the fashion creator who influences her the most and the best gifts to give for the holidays.
What’s a luxury beauty item you’re obsessed with right now?
I’m skin care-obsessed. I spend my money on skin care, lasers, anything I can get my hands on. That is the high-end part of my beauty routine. But I also love makeup and I’m loving everything from Fara Homidi.
Fara’s lip liner is one of my favorites. And then I use her foundation compact religiously, especially on days where I’m running around, just quick under-eye touch-ups, a little bit of that nice little sheen on the cheeks and you’re ready to go.
What’s a drugstore beauty item you love?
I buy Aquaphor in bulk and I can’t live without it. It’s on my nightstand; it’s in every one of my bags. My kids carry it in their backpacks. It’s like H20-equivalent to me at this point in my life.
What’s a luxury fashion item you’re eyeing right now?
I’m so glad that my husband is in the room to hear this. Anything from Sherman Field. I love jewelry, so I will never say no to anything that’s special and different. I’d love another Schiaparelli bag.
What shoes do you want to buy right now?
So, I wear a size 42 for anyone who wants to send me shoes. Size 12. I have giant feet and shoes are always difficult for me to find. It’s fall/winter, so I’m on a boot hunt and there’s a pair of Khaite flat boots that are calf-high. And then there’s this chunky-heeled Saint Laurent black boot that’s also knee-high that I’m dying for.
What’s a fall-fashion must-have you swear by?
I love a nice cozy cashmere sweater just to replenish and go through seasonally. I’ll find sometimes a really nice quality Zara one or Cos.
What’s the last product an influencer or celeb inspired you to buy?
I have a major girl crush on Courtney Grow. Basically everything is add to cart, add to cart, add to cart on a daily basis. I’ve had to mute her because it’s getting out of hand, but I just bought, and unfortunate returned because of my giant feet, this beautiful pair of Dôen flats that she recommended with the flower. So chic and so nice. I was thinking for holiday parties when I don’t want to wear heels. My big-ass feet didn’t fit in them properly, so that didn’t work out.
What’s a gift you give a friend who has everything?
Because I do love hosting and I love a nice table setting, I decided to create a collection of dove candlestick holders. Also, I’m an avid reader, a total bookworm. Duh, had to make a bookmark. This is a bookmark referencing a sculpture of mine and it’s this sexy little lady that hangs over your page.
