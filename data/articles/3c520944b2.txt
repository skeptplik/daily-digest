Warning: Spoilers for GTA 6 ahead!
A character in GTA 6 named Mandy has been going viral for being a total smokeshow, but fans are also noticing that she seems to have a dark secret.
Early on in the game, Brian sends Jason to go retrieve rent from a tenant named Mandy, who refuses to pay until her sink is fixed. Once you get there, it's clear she has staged a maintenance emergency so Jason would turn up. She's madly obsessed with him, but Jason pays no mind, since he's still pursuing Lucia. She eventually tries to seduce Jason with a skimpy bikini, to no avail. He ends up pulling out whatever is lodged in her sink, which she quickly takes and runs off with.
Mandy eventually gets irritated with Jason's disinterest and kicks him out, but if players linger around the property, they may start to realize Mandy isn't just obsessive because she has a crush... but because she may be a killer. If you wander into her backyard, you will see a freshly covered-up, body-sized hole next to a few tubs of acid. But hey, maybe it's just a coincidence! If you pop the trunk of her car, you will also see more acid and tools someone might use to kidnap and kill a person. And if you take a quick look at what was in Mandy's sink, it appears to be a severed finger.
While it's unclear if she's actually a serial killer (it takes three victims to be classified as one, by the way), she does appear to have killed at least one person. It's unclear who the other guy is, but it's possible Jason could've met a similar fate. Does she seduce people in order to kill them? Or does she kill them because they reject her advances or hurt her emotionally? It's hard to say. Either way, it seems clear that there's definitely a screw loose, and it's good that Jason didn't fall for her seduction.
