Wizz Air confirms Milan-Warsaw flight affected, says 'not the only airline targeted'
Wizz Air has confirmed that its Milan Malpensa – Warsaw flight has been diverted to Vienna after “a bomb threat received during the flight” (10:52).
“The diversion was initiated in accordance with established security protocols and procedures, with the safety of passengers and crew as the highest priority,” it said.
The airline added:
“This incident comes amid a series of similar bomb threats affecting several airlines across Europe in recent days. Wizz Air is not the only airline targeted, with other carriers also experiencing threats resulting in flight diversions and security checks.
Wizz Air is cooperating fully with the relevant authorities and is taking all necessary steps to ensure the safety of everyone on board. The safety and security of our passengers and crew remain our highest priority.”
LOT Polish Airlines confirms 'no suspicious items found' after earlier bomb threat
Meanwhile, LOT Polish Airlines has confirmed the incident involving their plane earlier today (10:48) – but said the reported threat was not confirmed.
In a statement sent to the Guardian, the airline said:
“After receiving information about a potential threat to the safety of flight LO235 from Warsaw to Brussels, the flight crew was informed of the situation and conducted an onboard inspection in accordance with applicable procedures. No suspicious items were found.
The plane landed at Brussels Airport, where the authorities carried out an additional inspection of the aircraft, which also did not reveal the presence of any dangerous or suspicious items.”
Bomb threats reported against four planes heading to Prague
The Czech police has just confirmed that it received reports of “an improvised explosive device” against four planes heading to Prague.
“We are preparing … for their landing. We assess the credibility of threats as low,” it said.
“We do not underestimate the threat, which is why all necessary security measures have been activated with the least possible impact on passengers and airport operations. Upon landing, pyrotechnic inspections will be conducted on all aircraft.”
Two more planes affected by bomb threats, Italian media report
And the Italian news agency Ansa reported that two more planes with links to Italy were affected by bomb threats this morning: that’s a WizzAir service from Milan Malpensa to Warsaw, which reportedly landed in Vienna, and a Finnair flight from Helsinki to Rome Fiumicino.
This morning’s incident will once again raise questions as to whether there is any coordination behind the bomb warnings in recent days.
Four out of eight incidents in the last few days targeted planes flying to or from Italy: three to Rome – two from Dublin and one from Lisbon, and one from Milan Bergamo.
Flightradar24, one of the largest flight tracker websites in the world, also said that “no aircraft have departed from Rome Fiumicino Airport (FCO) in the past 30 minutes.”

An Aer Lingus flight from Dublin to Rome has been diverted to a major military base in Evreux in France after a bomb alert, the French prime minister’s office said.

The flight, EIN40G, is operated by an Airbus A320. It’s currently circling above France and should start its descent to Evreux shortly.
