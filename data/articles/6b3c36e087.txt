Marnus Labuschagne axed from Australian Test squad ahead of New Zealand series
In short:
Marnus Labuschagne has been axed from Australia's Test squad ahead of the upcoming series against New Zealand.
Australia's chairman of selectors, George Bailey, has revealed the 32-year-old won't be considered for the four-Test series this summer, but remains a possibility to return to the squad for next year's tours to India and England.
What's next?
Australia will reveal its squad for the New Zealand Tests after the fourth round of Sheffield Shield. The Test series commences on December 9 in Perth.
