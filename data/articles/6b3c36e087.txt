Marnus Labuschagne axed from Australian Test squad ahead of New Zealand series
In short:
Marnus Labuschagne has been axed from Australia's squad for the four-Test series against New Zealand beginning December 9.
The chairman of selectors says he won't be considered this summer but could return for the tours to India and England.
What's next?
Australia will reveal its squad for the NZ Tests after the fourth round of the Sheffield Shield.
