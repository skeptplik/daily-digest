China’s freewheeling tech capital of Shenzhen has embraced an unusual quiet as it prepares to host world leaders this week, with car horns banned, delivery drones grounded and residents urged to leave vehicles at home.
The meeting of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) forum, which U.S. President Donald Trump and Russian leader Vladimir Putin will attend, is a chance for Shenzhen to show off its transformation from an agricultural backwater into the high-tech — and orderly, officials hope to show — city it is today.
More than 1.5 million local car owners have pledged to stay off the roads in exchange for APEC car stickers and, if they keep their word, transit cards worth up to 100 yuan ($15).
