Educational technology company Turnitin disclosed that it detected unauthorized activity involving certain customer-facing systems and deployed its incident response plan.
In a public statement, CEO Chris Caren says that the incident is being investigated with help from external cybersecurity specialists, and law enforcement and cyber regulators have been notified.
Turnitin is a major education technology firm with a global presence, best known for its AI-generated writing detection, digital grading, academic research integrity checks, and examination proctoring.
According to its website, its software is used in over 16,000 institutions across 185 countries and serves 71 million students.
“We are writing to let you know that we recently identified unauthorized activity involving certain customer-facing Turnitin systems,” reads the letter from the CEO on the platform's website.
“Upon identifying the matter, we implemented our incident response plan, initiated an investigation with external cybersecurity specialists, and notified law enforcement and cyber regulators.”
Turnitin says its systems are fully operational and safe to use, noting that the affected systems do not collect government identification credentials or institutional financial information.
The company said its investigation into the incident remains ongoing and has not disclosed any details about the nature and scope of the unauthorized activity.

Turnitin continues to monitor its systems for further unauthorized activity, indicating that it is still early to guarantee that the threat has been eliminated completely.
The company has not disclosed whether any user data was accessed or stolen, when the incident occurred, and how many individuals might have been impacted.
Regarding Turnitin service availability, the platform’s status page shows no incidents for November 2026, and the uptime history logs no disruptions.
As of writing, no ransomware groups have publicly taken responsibility for the attack at Turnitin.
BleepingComputer has contacted the firm with questions about the incident, but a company representative said they have no additional details to share at this time.
