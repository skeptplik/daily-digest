Ukraine sank a Russian naval corvette in a Libyan port using naval drones on Sunday, the day after a drone strike on a Russian military-linked cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland, President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said.
Zelenskyy said Monday morning that Ukraine used seven naval drones in Sunday’s attack, and had also hit the Russian dry cargo ship MV Angara, which he said Russia had used to transport weapons.
“Despite all the enemy’s attempts to counter the attack, the Russian corvette ’Mercury’ was sunk,” Zelenskyy posted on Telegram, the first official acknowledgement that Ukraine has carried out an attack in Libya.
