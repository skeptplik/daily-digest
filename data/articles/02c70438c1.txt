American fast-food chain Chipotle has launched a hoodie with a hidden, insulated pocket built for one highly specific purpose.
The novelty streetwear garment was designed to keep burritos both warm and concealed while sneaking them into the cinema for illicit mid-movie snacking.
Accessed via a subtle zip at the top of the hoodie’s front pouch, the pocket can also squeeze in an entire burrito bowl or a set of tacos, Chipotle claims, although the logistics of that might be trickier.
Kirby Ann Connell, the company’s senior director of social and influencers, said the launch was inspired by real stories of people smuggling elaborate Chipotle meals into the cinema.
“Movie nights deserve real, craveable food,” she said. “And since our fans were already sneaking Chipotle to theatres, we figured we’d make it a little easier.”
“Sometimes innovation is as simple as putting a burrito pocket in a hoodie,” she added
The limited-edition Sneak It In hoodie features an oversized fit to conceal any bulky contraband, plus a dedicated napkin pocket for any spills.
It comes in three colourways: Nothing To See Here Black, No Burrito Here Brown and Got the Goods Gray.
Chipotle is the latest brand to create novelty streetwear as a marketing stunt.
Other offerings include KFC’s Pickle Puffer, Lego-shaped Crocs and Aldi’s Potato Jacket.
