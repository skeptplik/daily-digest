Yemen's Houthis claim attacks on Riyadh financial district, Saudi refinery
Issued on:
Share
Yemen's Houthis on Wednesday claimed attacks targeting the financial district in the Saudi capital and the sprawling Ras Tanura refinery in the kingdom's oil-rich east, in retaliation for strikes on their territory. Houthi military spokesman Yahya Saree described the attack targeting the refinery as "accurate and direct", saying it "caused fires to break out at the facility". He said the attack on Riyadh was "carried out in three phases, with strikes hitting their targets accurately".
