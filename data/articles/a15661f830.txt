Gunman kills six in Krakow, three more bodies found at his house
Issued on: Modified:
Share
A chilling new discovery in Poland, where three bodies have reportedly been found inside the Krakow gunman's home. That's in addition to six people killed and sixteen wounded when the attacker opened fire in the city's historic square. Prime Minister Donald Tusk has now declared the shooting an act of terrorism, saying Poland has never experienced anything quite like it. The 25-year-old suspect, described by authorities as holding radical far-right views, was shot by police.
