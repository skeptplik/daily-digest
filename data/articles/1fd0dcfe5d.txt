Parenting is exhausting. If there’s a new parent in your life, whether it’s your friend who is the new parent of the group or a family member who just had a baby, there are a lot of ways to show some love. Showing up, checking in on how they're surviving, and bringing something that can improve their life are all great ways to support the new parents in your circle.
There’s a lot of parenting and baby gear out there, and not all of it is created equal. Not all of it will suit what the parents in your life might need, either. This guide is organized into a handful of categories of parenting gadgets, smart home gear, little luxuries, and even food deliveries you can send to their door. All of these are tried and tested by WIRED’s reviewers, many of whom are parents and can confirm the difference it’s made in their lives.

Great Parenting Gadgets to Gift
It’s easy to remember to register for things like the car seat and baby clothes. But there are a lot of small gadgets that make parenting a little easier, things that new parents don’t realize they might love using. Here are some of the favorites from the parents at WIRED.
Something to Eat
Early parenting is survival, and bandwidth to go to the grocery store or cook becomes a hard zero. There’s a reason friends and family often run food trains for new parents. The following food gifts would be a great option to contribute to a food train, and being shippable packages means you can send them to a friend or family member you may live too far from to visit often.
Some Daily Life Upgrades
I didn’t put anything fun on my firstborn's registry. I was so overwhelmed with how many must-haves I needed (including an infant baby tub device I never used, but every registry site claimed I'd need). Baby registry websites will constantly tell you that you haven’t added things from every single category their site has deemed important. There are a lot of things parents need, but there are lots of fun things you can gift that aren’t necessities but will still make parenting a little easier, or at least feel like a thoughtful gift.
