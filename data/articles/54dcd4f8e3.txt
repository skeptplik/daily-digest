Love was in the air at the 2026 Baby2Baby Gala on Saturday night.
Billy Ray Cyrus and Elizabeth Hurley made their first public appearance since reportedly getting engaged in October at the West Hollywood fundraiser. Taking to the red carpet to show their support for the non-profit, the celebrity couple was joined by Hurley’s son, Damian.
Hurley wore a sheer red ensemble, Monique Lhuillier‘s scarlet embroidered tulle gown, with floral appliqués and a red plunging one-piece underneath. The high collar and long sleeves provided the 61-year-old actress with coverage, while the bright hues remained in line with the dramatic colors the “Austin Powers” star tends to favor.
When it came to beauty, Hurley opted to part her “bronde” locks down the middle and styled in loose waves. Hurley completed her look by accessorizing with a bedazzled clutch that matched her light gunmetal eyeshadow.
Cyrus — who chose not to wear his signature cowboy hat — stuck with his go-to black monochromatic look, paring a velvet suede jacket with dress pants. The 65-year-old singer wore aviators on the red carpet, along with several heavy silver rings and two chain necklaces with crosses on them.
In a sweet coincidence or a planned decision as their first appearance as a family, Damian also wore a cross pendant necklace that lay against his exposed chest. Dressed in an ivory velvet jacket with broad lapels, the 24-year-old model also chose to wear black dress pants and loafers.
Damian is the son of Hurley and her ex-boyfriend Steve Bing, who died in 2020. Hurley and Cyrus first met while filming “Christmas in Paradise” in 2022 and later went public with their relationship in the spring of 2025, reportedly getting engaged in mid-October of this year.
