After delivering premium leather versions of its classic Air Max 90 sneaker in black and olive, Nike is adding to the set with a previously unseen third colorway that’s just been released from its Snkrs platform.
The Nike Air Max 90 SP Dark Russet/Mesa Orange follows the same premium construction and stripped-back branding design language established by the previous two colorways, offering a new spin on the 36-year-old retro running shoe. Rather than its typical mesh and suede builds, this SP variation features a full leather construction which bypasses the sneaker’s usual paneling for a puffy premium look. Stitching gives the sneaker’s overlays depth, while perforated leather panels on the tongue, eyelets and heel tab add an even more elevated feel.

Materials aside, the most intriguing part of this Air Max 90 SP construction is its reduced branding. The shoe’s traditional heel Nike Air branding and Air Max badge have been completely stripped of their wordmarks, letting the model’s design and premium materials do the talking instead.
This unbranded approach is reminiscent of releases from the sportswear label’s now-defunct NikeLab footwear line, which was reintroduced as a performance running apparel collection in September.
While the black and olive colorways had been seen weeks prior, Nike managed to keep the newly surfaced Dark Russet/Mesa Orange looks under wraps until its recent unveiling. As of publishing, the Air Max 90 SP is currently available in the aforementioned black (style code IV4504-001) and Medium Olive/Sequoia (style code IV4504-201) makeups, both of which retail for $170.
The Air Max 90 SP Dark Russet/Mesa Orange is available now from nike.com for $170 under style code IV4504-200. A full look at the remastered sneaker can be found in the gallery below.
