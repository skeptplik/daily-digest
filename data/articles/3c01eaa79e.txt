A couple of years ago, reports emerged that Challengers and Call Me By Your Name director Luca Guadagnino was working on his own reimagining of American Psycho, the 2000 film adaptation of Bret Easton Ellis' novel. Unfortunately (or fortunately, depending on your point of view), it doesn't appear his new take on the cult-hit psychological thriller will see the light of day.
Luca Guadagnino's American Psycho is not happening
Luca Guadagnino was first linked to a new American Psycho adaptation written by Contagion's Scott Z. Burns in October 2024. We had not heard much more about the project since then, so Deadline asked Guadagnino about it during the director's recent appearance on its Crew Call podcast. Sadly, Guadagnino did not have a positive update on the project.
“Scott Z. Burns wrote a fantastic script. Unfortunately for me, as we speak, it’s not happening," Guadagnino succinctly explained.
American Psycho is a horror novel originally released in 1991, but it's mostly remembered for Mary Harron's 2000 film adaptation starring Christian Bale. The movie, which explores themes of toxic masculinity and materialism, was a subversive horror comedy for its time. Despite its very grim subject matter, Bale's over-the-top performance and key scenes from the movie have nestled their way into pop culture and become memes in the 26 years since the original's release. Even official accounts for kid-friendly franchises like Pokémon have referenced it.
Any new adaptation will have lofty expectations to live up to and will likely have to face the reality that many of the things the book lampoons have only gotten worse in the decades since its original release. Sadly, it looks like we'll never quite know how Guadagnino and Burns planned to reinterpret American Psycho for the modern day.
This new rendition of American Psycho isn't the only Luca Guadagnino project that hasn't come to fruition in recent years. He was attached to direct a movie based on Sgt. Rock for DC Studios, but that project was shelved before it could start shooting in 2025. Artificial, a movie about the founders of OpenAI, will be the next Guadagnino film to hit theaters. It'll be released on Dec. 25.
