Dave Täht given the Jonathan B. Postel Service Award
	Täht's legacy can be found in community hubs in Nicaragua, where he
	worked to expand connectivity, and first discovered the urgency of
	solving bufferbloat. It can be found in every lag-free video call
	with a loved one. It can be found in outer space—Täht helped
	Starlink solve its latency challenges. And it can be found in the
	countless people he mentored and befriended over the years, who
	will not stop until bufferbloat stops getting in the way of band
	practice. 
Dave is still deeply missed by those of us who knew him; this is an award
he richly deserved.
