On Monday evening, Lincoln Center was temporarily transformed into the Capitol of Panem for the highly anticipated world premiere of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping. The purple carpet, flanked by flames, was thronged with fans cheering as three generations of Hunger Games stars reconvened for a long-awaited reunion.
The dress theme for the evening, Make Them Remember You, encouraged attendees to dress in vintage-leaning silhouettes and rich textures, and to embrace a touch of theatricality. It was an assignment that the cast and attendees alike took seriously, donning looks that were hard to forget. Elle Fanning and Elizabeth Banks took on a battle of the Effies, with each actress indulging in an avant-garde approach to the mood board. McKenna Grace’s Mockingjay gown came complete with wings as a nod to Katniss’s costume from Catching Fire. The horde of onlookers surrounding the area also displayed their own interpretations of Capitol-inspired garb.
As the final guests were ushered into Alice Tully Hall, director Francis Lawrence invited what he described as “one of the most impressive casts in cinema” to rise one by one. Each familiar face from the original films—including Woody Harrelson, Josh Hutcherson, and Jennifer Lawrence—was greeted with thunderous applause. Francis even invited the audience to wish his assistant a quick “Happy Birthday” before the lights dimmed and the room erupted into more cheers as each member of the cast appeared on screen.
Following the screening, guests journeyed across Central Park to the historic Metropolitan Club for an opulent after-party. Indeed, the space felt reminiscent of the ball held in Catching Fire as people sauntered into a marble room complete with a sizable ice sculpture carved in the symbol of the film: a spiked sun rising over a songbird and snake. Attendees were also spoiled with their choice of Haymitch-style cocktails: a sweet floral vodka cocktail named “The Rascal” or the spicy, tequila-infused “All Fire.”
The stairs were decorated with extravagant floral arrangements, nodding to the flowers seen in the film’s arena, while mirrors on the wall were inscribed with quotes like “All machines can be broken” or “I love you like all fire.” Flags of Panem hung below a balcony filled with what appeared to be Capitol citizens inspecting guests from above. As servers strolled through the many decorated rooms distributing hors d’oeuvres of tuna tartare and ceviche, guests were truly made feel as if they were transported straight to the heart of the action.
The DJ played a wide variety of dance floor-filling tunes while decked out in full Effie Trinket attire, much to the delight of stars who’d arrived to join their fans. Some of the cast had changed into more comfortable clothing to relax and unwind—no one more so than Joseph Zada, who arrived in the infamous set of pajamas Woody Harrelson wore to the premiere of Mockingjay — Part 2. Meanwhile, Elle Fanning opted for a full in-character transformation as she bopped and laughed alongside her sister, Dakota, switching into a latex bodysuit and a brand-new metallic purple bob.
The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping hits theaters on Friday, November 20.
