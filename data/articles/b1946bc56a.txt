During July and August 2025, approximately one in 10 U.S. adults reported taking a GLP-1 medication during the previous year, according to a report from the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Among GLP-1 users, approximately one in five reported taking a compounded version and 9% of those taking a GLP-1 medicine were uncertain whether it was compounded, suggesting difficulties in distinguishing compounded versions from medicines approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration. Approximately two in five GLP-1 users modified their use of the medication from the way it was prescribed, often citing cost and availability as barriers to prescribed use.
The pharmaceutical industry wants an audit of how the World Health Organization is spending millions of dollars that drugmakers contribute to a program designed to prepare the world for the next flu pandemic, Devex reports. The International Federation of Pharmaceutical Manufacturers is pushing for one of the so-called Big 4 accounting firms to audit industry contributions to the WHO’s Pandemic IInfluenza Preparedness Framework, citing concerns over how the funds are used. Some manufacturers are even withholding funds. However, the WHO says it already has an external auditor and that it spends all funds in accordance with its rules.
