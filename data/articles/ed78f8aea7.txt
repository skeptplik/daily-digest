Why Use A VPN?
A VPN hides your device’s web traffic by routing it through servers in other locations worldwide.
The big focus here is privacy, since you can prevent snoopers from getting a look at your internet traffic (particularly useful in locations like airports, train stations, and your favorite coffee shop).
And with built-in Kill Switch functionality, if your VPN drops out, your internet access will be cut, too. That means no traffic can squeak through.
While rerouting your traffic, websites (and streaming services) will see you as coming from another location, so you can browse different versions of your favorite sites and streaming platforms, unencumbered by geo-blocks.
Want to see what’s on Disney Plus in the US while you’re based in the UK? This is how to do it.
You can also sidestep surge pricing. This is when a website spots you looking at a product regularly and adjusts the price to entice you to make a purchase before it climbs further. With a VPN, you’re just a random passer-by that’s checking in.
