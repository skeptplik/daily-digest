There is growing traction for Arc’teryx footwear on TikTok.
Truist analyst Joseph Civello concluded that one shoe in particularly is clicking — the Postour Boot, a two-in-one boot with a hidden slipper.
Arc’teryx sibling Salomon was no slouch either. According to Civello: “Growth in TikTok engagement for Salomon is tracking atop its peer group over the past three months and near the top year-to-date.” He added that there is still “very low” awareness in the U.S., an indication that the sneaker brand has a long runway for growth ahead of it.
And with social media traction fueling brand heat, Civello is bullish on the two brands’ opportunities to “outperform through the Holidays and into ’27.” He expects the company to report a modest third quarter beat on earnings. Amer Sports is scheduled to post third quarter earnings results Tuesday morning.”

“In our view, Arc’teryx and Salomon are differentiated brands that consumers will likely stretch for around the Holidays, and we see opportunities to outperform amidst a challenging environment,” he noted.
Civello isn’t the only one on Wall Street who thinks highly of Amer, which is receiving FN’s 2026 Company of the Year award.
“Big picture, Amer Sports stands out as a multi-brand sportswear platform delivering strong 20 percent-plus top-line growth near-terms, and low double-digit to mid-teens percent growth over the medium term,” Janine Stichter, consumer retail and lifestyle brands analyst at U.S. Bancorp | BTIG, said. She expects growth in the third quarter report to be led by Arc’teryx and Salomon.
Jefferies Randal Konik sees Arc’teryx as a key growth engine, noting that women’s — now front and center in stores — is “one of the brand’s most under appreciated growth levers.”
Moreover, a dedicated footwear wall showing the brand’s broad range of trail and hiking styles indicates that “Arc’teryx sees footwear as a real category with its own growth path, not just an add-on.”
