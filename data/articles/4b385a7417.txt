Canberra Raiders coach Ricky Stuart upset over Hudson Young's money-driven move
In short:
The Canberra Raiders withdrew its $1.1 million per year offer to Hudson Young as the Blues forward said he wanted to pursue an opportunity with PNG.
Coach Ricky Stuart said Young had been given the "highest offer that's ever been offered to a player" at the club.
What's next?
Stuart said any decision on whether Young would play at a different club in 2027 was up to the player's management.
