China, US defence officials agree to improve ‘communication’ to reduce risks
Military officials from two countries to work on promoting ‘effective’ crisis management after constructive meeting in Washington
US and Chinese defence officials met in Washington for the first meeting of a Crisis Communications and Prevention Working Group established under an agreement announced in September when leaders of the two nations met.
The two sides agreed on the importance of “advancing practical communication measures” to reduce risks between the US and China, according to a Pentagon readout of the meeting released Tuesday. Delegates also discussed “additional channels” to promote “effective” crisis management and ways to communicate “expediently and effectively”.
