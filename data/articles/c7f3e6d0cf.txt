Iraqi forces strike compromise with powerful pro-Iran militia
Issued on:
Share
Iraq has reached a compromise with the powerful Iran-backed militia Kataib Hezbollah, three senior security officials said, after Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi readied security forces to move against the group. Iraqi officials had accused the group of running a smuggling ring that siphoned off hundreds of millions of dollars' worth of state-owned oil. Under the deal brokered by senior Shi'ite political leaders to avert a confrontation, government forces were expected to move into the group's stronghold of Jurf al-Sakhar on Friday.
