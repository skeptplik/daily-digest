After reading your report (‘They’re struggling for oxygen’: children pay the price as countries allow measles to surge again, 20 November), I was struck by the statement that proposed cuts to foreign aid from wealthy countries are likely to exacerbate the problem, particularly for those within developing countries.
The UK intends to cut foreign aid spending from 0.5% to 0.3% of national income by the end of 2027. Worryingly, in its 2025-26 report, the Foreign Commonwealth and Development Office proposed a cut of 35% on funding provided to the Democratic Republic of Congo. As your article pointed out, the DRC is already experiencing a deadly Ebola outbreak; with the spread of measles, thousands more will be lost to a different, and largely preventable disease.
We could be at risk of another serious worldwide epidemic. Recent cases of measles in the US, Mexico and Bolivia, and the spread of harmful misinformation surrounding vaccination, may only worsen the problem. I will be writing to my local MPto highight the potentially disastrous results of further cuts to the UK’s foreign aid budget. I urge your readers to do the same.
Colette McIvor
Cookstown, County Tyrone, Northern Ireland
