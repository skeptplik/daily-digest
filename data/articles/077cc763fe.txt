Ariana Grande is back! The superstar has been absent from the spotlight since she wrapped her Eternal Sunshine Tour on September 1, and stepped away from public roles and duties (including the West End revival of Sunday in the Park With George) due to harsh online scrutiny regarding her physical appearance.
But now, she’s bopping back onto the stage—even if that stage is a late-night show set rather than a concert arena.
Last night, Grande made her big comeback with a surprise appearance on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon. She joined the show alongside her Focker-in-Law costars Robert De Niro and Ben Stiller to promote the upcoming holiday comedy film (which comes out November 25).
The “Hate That I Made You Love Me” singer looked so chic as she glided onto the stage in a sharp suit from Givenchy by Sarah Burton (look 20 from the Spring/Summer 2027 collection). The double-breasted wool blazer was slightly oversize, and paired with matching cigarette pants.
Grande, styled by none other than Law Roach, kept the look sleek and monochromatic. The duo opted to pair the black suit with pointy suede heels and a velvet choker, while the musician’s little bob was curled upward at the ends for an extra sweet, classy touch.
The singer also brought some of her on-stage persona with her to the late-night show via her makeup, which was rosy and glittery, matching her glossy pink lip and powder-pink manicure. Tiny gold hoop earrings completed the look.
