Nepal Prime Minister Balendra Shah will pay a state visit to India from November 22–23 at the invitation of Prime Minister Narendra Modi, Nepal’s Foreign Secretary Amrit Bahadur Rai announced on Friday (November 20, 2026). This will be his first visit to India after assuming office in March 2026.
A 34-member government team will depart from Kathmandu for New Delhi, Mr. Rai said. Prime Minister Modi will hold bilateral talks with Mr. Shah on Monday (November 23, 2026) to review the full range of India–Nepal relations and exchange views on regional and international issues of mutual interest.
As part of his official itinerary in Delhi, Mr. Shah will call on President Droupadi Murmu.
Various projects relating to energy will be inaugurated by the two Prime Ministers. After the delegation-level talks, the two Prime Ministers will hold a joint press conference.
India and Nepal share close relations, rooted in historical, cultural and civilisational links. The visit continues the tradition of regular high-level exchanges between the two countries and reflects the importance India attaches to its relations with Nepal under its ‘Neighbourhood First’ policy.
