Firaxis, the 30-year-old studio behind the Civilization and XCOM franchises, is laying off a third of its staff, totalling 60 people, as reported by Bloomberg's Jason Schreier. Studio head Heather Hazen, who has lead it since 2023, will be leaving as part of the cuts.
“Firaxis is restructuring and recentering around the studio’s core strength – designing world class strategy games that have set the industry standard for the genre for the last 30 years,” a spokesperson said in a statement provided to the site.
“As part of these changes, the size of the studio is being reduced to focus on design, technology and the player experience, which means saying goodbye to talented colleagues who helped build Firaxis. These decisions are never easy, but they’re essential to build the next chapter of Firaxis.”
The firm previously laid off staff in September 2025.
The studio is reportedly working on a new XCOM title which has "faced development struggles and missed a significant milestone over the summer," according to Schreier, citing internal sources who said the game was still in development but may "face a shakeup" as a result of the staff departures. The last XCOM release, Enemy Unknown 2, was released in 2016; Firaxis shipped Civilization VII last year.
Firaxis parent Take-Two Interactive posted net revenue down 16% YoY, but reported GTA 6 pre-orders "far in excess of expectations". A report today from Sensor Tower suggested pre-orders had already topped $1.4B, meaning the title has already exceeded its predecessor's initial three days of revenue in advance of going on sale.
