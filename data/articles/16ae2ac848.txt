Speaking with FRANCE 24's Sharon Gaffney, Nobel Peace Prize laureate Oleksandra Matviichuk, Director of the Centre for Civil Liberties, says that today's war in Ukraine "is a war of unmanned machines, AI, total transparency of the battlefield, mass drones attacks and long range weapons, and this means we have to rethink how to protect human beings".
