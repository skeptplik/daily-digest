A second U.S. judge on Wednesday blocked enforcement of President Donald Trump’s executive order limiting the number of people eligible for birthright citizenship, which Trump signed after the U.S. Supreme Court rejected his initial effort to impose restrictions.
U.S. District Judge Christopher Cooper in Washington, D.C., issued an injunction in a class action lawsuit filed on behalf of children who would be denied citizenship under the August executive order.
The ruling will stymie Trump’s efforts to implement a flagship piece of his administration’s hard-line immigration crackdown. Another federal judge in Maryland in September also blocked Trump’s order, and the administration will now need to overturn both decisions in order to ever enforce it.
