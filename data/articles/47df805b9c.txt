Gold Skulltulas act as a collectible in The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake, but they're also needed to cure a cursed family in Kakariko Village.
The poor family have been turned into Skulltulas, and the only way to cleanse them of their curse is to hunt down the Gold Skulltulas scattered across Hyrule. However, that's easier said than done, as there are 100 of them in total, and they're extremely difficult to find.
Here's where to find every Gold Skulltula in Ocarina of Time remake, and the rewards for curing the family in Kakariko Village.

                        All Gold Skulltula rewards in Zelda Ocarina of Time
As you collect Gold Skulltula tokens, you can turn them in to the cursed family in Kakariko Village for rewards. Every 10 Gold Skulltulas you collect from 10 to 50 will lead to a reward, but then you won't receive anything until you turn in all 100 Gold Skulltulas. After you collect 20 Gold Skulltulas, make sure to get your reward, as the Echo Gem is extremely helpful for finding the rest of them.
Below, we've listed all the Gold Skulltula rewards, and how many you need to turn in to receive them.
