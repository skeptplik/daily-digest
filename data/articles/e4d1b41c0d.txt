A Chinese Long March 2-F rocket carrying three astronauts, including a Pakistani crew member, launched on Wednesday from the Jiuquan space centre in northwestern China.
The Shenzhou-24 spacecraft lifted off at 8:18 am local time, heading for the Tiangong space station. Footage from Chinese state broadcaster CCTV showed the rocket rising into the sky in a cloud of flames and smoke, while people watched from the launch site.
Astronauts already aboard the space station were also seen watching the launch on a screen.
