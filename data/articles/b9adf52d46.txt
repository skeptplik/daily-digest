Trump lost Congress. 3 presidents before him did, too
Donald Trump just joined a rare presidential club of U.S. presidents to lose both the House and Senate in the midterms. CBC's Mike Crawley looks at the former presidents who also faced similar congressional busts. CORRECTION (Nov. 4, 2026): In this video, there is a reference to Donald Trump being the third U.S. president to lose both the House and Senate in the midterms. In fact, he is the fourth.
