Carolin Schmidt has been missing since 6 September 2026. Last seen around Mittenwald and Garmisch-Partenkirchen on hikes near the Bavaria–Tyrol border. No trace of her for over ten weeks.
Carolin Schmidt is regarded as close to nature, sporty and experienced in mountain hiking.It is known that the 25-year-old went mountain hiking twice in the days before she disappeared, including in the border area with Austria. Her relatives filed a missing person report (source in German) with the Garmisch-Partenkirchen police station about seven weeks ago.
The search with several police patrols and a police helicopter has so far been unsuccessful. The search is focused on the areas around the Erlspitzmassiv, the Karwendelgebirge, the Nordkette, the Inntal, the Sellrainer Berge, the Gleirsch-Halltal-Kette, the southern Wettersteingebirge and the Mieminger Gebirge.
The last contact with her relatives was on 6 September. Since then her whereabouts have been unknown.
The parents' desperate search
The relatives of the missing Carolin have set up their own search page (source in German). It documents how the search has progressed so far. In addition to current information and photos of the missing woman, there are also pictures of the clothing Carolin may have last worn. The family hopes to receive further information about her whereabouts in this way.
Carolin's father has also been heavily involved in the search. For several weeks he combed the mountain region around Garmisch-Partenkirchen and checked possible hiking routes his daughter might have taken. Various television channels have reported on this.
Carolin's father, himself an experienced mountaineer, is convinced that Carolin would not take any unnecessary risks. Speaking to the newspaper "Augsburger Allgemeinen" he stressed that his daughter is sporty and a seasoned mountain climber. Despite his intensive efforts he has not been able to discover any new traces so far.
On 13 November he told the "Allgäuer Zeitung": "Unfortunately there are no reliable new leads." Despite intensive search operations, the fate of the 25-year-old therefore remains unclear.
Police and relatives still hope
According to the police, Carolin Schmidt is about 1.75 metres tall, very slim and has medium-length blond hair and blue eyes. She is believed to have last been wearing a grey and pink fleece jacket by The North Face and grey trail running shoes by Scarpa. She also had a grey and red rucksack made by Deuter with her.
The police told the "Allgäuer Zeitung" on request that the Oberbayern Süd regional police headquarters stated on 13 November: "According to the current state of the investigation there are no indications of third-party involvement."
Information is also being sought in Tyrol. Referring to Carolin's mobile phone, Engelbert Thum of the Seefeld police station told the "Tiroler Tageszeitung" on 4 November: "The phone has not been in the network for a long time." There had previously been reports of possible sightings of the missing woman in the region. The investigation into the young woman's whereabouts is nevertheless continuing.
The Garmisch-Partenkirchen police station is asking the public for help in the search for the missing woman. Witnesses who can provide information about the current whereabouts of the 25-year-old are asked to call (08821) 9170.
