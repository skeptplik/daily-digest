Sam Konstas of NSW and Queensland's Jack Clayton miss out on big One-Day Cup runs ahead of Test selection
In short:
Two of the next men in line for Australia's Test team have missed their opportunity to impress selectors with big runs in white-ball cricket.
Sam Konstas scored six for NSW against South Australia in the One-Day Cup, while Queensland's Jack Clayton made 24 against Tasmania in the same competition.
What's next?
Australia's Test series against New Zealand begins at Perth Stadium on December 9.
