As Usman Khawaja and Mitchell Starc's Sandpapergate recollections differ, Adam Gilchrist urges Australia to move on
In short:
Cricket great Adam Gilchrist has urged Australia to "move on" from the 2018 Sandpaper incident.
Despite Usman Khawaja's claims that the whole team knew about the incident, Mitchell Starc said the bowlers had no knowledge.
What's next?
Australia's four-Test series against New Zealand begins on December 9 in Perth.
