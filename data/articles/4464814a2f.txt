A decade ago, many of us wouldn’t leave the house without a full-coverage beat. As Instagram selfies and YouTube tutorials took over pop culture, meticulously sculpted, perfected complexions reigned supreme—until the pendulum swung in the opposite direction, ushering in an era of barely-there coverage and skin-care-infused formulas. And while the early 2020s were defined by ultra-dewy, almost damp-looking skin, today’s complexion ideal strikes a more nuanced balance: coverage without the weight, radiance without the shine.

“Modern complexion formulations have come such a long way, and the new-Gen complexion products give medium to buildable coverage that glides and melts into skin, while giving a soft-focus matte finish that reads radiant rather than shiny,” says celebrity makeup artist and Vogue Beauty Board member Fara Homidi. “We can now get more coverage, while the final look is breathable and has flex to it, never heavy and cakey.” Nowadays, lightweight texture, skin-friendly ingredients, and flexible coverage are non-negotiable. The ideal product perfects your complexion without looking or feeling like makeup.
“I want the complexion product to be able to be great, without the help of a concealer or bronzer,” says content creator and fellow Board member Toni Bravo. “It also needs the right undertones that bring out dimension naturally,” she says, to create a second-skin effect.
Mastering your complexion, however, is both the science of formulation and the art of application. While formulas are more advanced than ever, technique and placement have simultaneously evolved. Homidi points out that it’s not about wearing less makeup, but about how it’s placed on the skin. Her best advice is to put the product only where you need it like under the eyes or around the nose, with lighter coverage around the the rest of the face. She prefers pinpointing blemishes and discolorations with fuller coverage, while letting the rest of the skin breathe.
Looking ahead, beauty lovers and experts alike can expect complexion makeup to keep evolving. “Hopefully, complexion products continue to evolve shade-wise,” says Bravo. “We’ve seen brands truly expand the meaning of inclusivity in the last few years, in ways 14-year-old me wouldn’t believe. Folks understanding undertones, and how to apply them to our complexion products, will make for a more conscious consumer and intentional brand.” Meanwhile, Homidi expects further advancements in texture and innovative application methods (roller ball foundation applicator, anyone?). In short, the future looks more inclusive, with advanced formulations and better shade matches for everyone.
Below, a look at some of the latest foundation and skin tint launches that we believe are pushing the category forward; with some helpful insights from the Vogue Beauty Board members who have tested them.
Dior Backstage Glow-Up Skin Tint
- Why We Love It: Dior’s skin tint offers surprising complexion coverage and SPF protection, plus a wide range of shades to choose from (24, to be exact). Not to mention, the brand really nails its neutral undertone shades. Plus, the formula features skincare-grade ingredients like fermented collagen, vitamin E, and glycerin to keep skin hydrated.
- Key Ingredients: Octisalate 4.50%, titanium dioxide 4.23%, glycerin, mica, tocopherol, fermented collagen
- Coverage: Buildable; light-to-medium
- Finish: Natural
- Size: 1.35 oz
I love the glow and the wearability of this skin tint. I prefer a glowy finish, so this one was right up my alley. I have combination skin, so going forward I’d wear this with my favorite mattifying primer to keep it from getting too shiny. I used shade 8N. It pulls fairly neutral, so if you have a warmer undertone, I’d pay close attention to the swatches before applying, especially since it includes SPF.
“I love complexion products that still look like skin and what I like about this formula is the natural glow and the ability to build a little more coverage where you want it. It gives your skin a more even look without feeling overly heavy and perfect for light makeup days.”
I was surprised by how much I liked this skin tint—I’m not usually fond of tints because they tend to look too shiny on my oily skin and don’t provide much coverage. The real draw here was the special attention to shades for neutral undertones (me!). I went with shade 2N, and it was a perfect match—plus, this gave a surprising amount of coverage, blurring away any light hyperpigmentation marks I had. The finish is definitely radiant, but I found it natural and lightweight-feeling. My only hesitation is that the formula isn’t totally non-comedogenic, and I’m acne-prone, so I’d reach for this when my skin is in maintenance mode (AKA clear, and upkeeping that) rather than actively breaking out.
Ommage First Base
- Why We Love It: Ommage just launched this past October—but really, the brand is a grand return for Australian model and beauty industry vet Laura Worthington, who previously founded a makeup brand in 2014 called Base. Over a decade later, First Base, a tinted cream in pump jar format, is an ode to that. Here, mineral powders create a subtle soft-focus effect to smooth the appearance of fine lines and counteract redness—meanwhile, calendula extract and vitamin E calm skin irritation.
- Key Ingredients: Calendula flower extract, sunflower seed oil, mica, vitamin E
- Coverage: Sheer, buildable.
- Finish: Soft focus
- Size: 30 mL
First of all, I love that this is called a ‘tinted cream’—it feels like a modern reimagining of the tinted moisturizer. I tried this for the first time uptown at Violet Grey’s NYC boutique, and was so pleased with the shade match. I couldn’t believe how incredible my skin looked. When I got home, I put the tub front-and-center in my vanity (where I keep the things I reach for most often), and have used it for most makeup days since. Lightweight, sort of gel-like, and hydrating with a natural finish, this is a winner in my book.
Carte Blanche Multi-Hyphenate Brightening Skin Tint Mineral SPF 35
- Why We Love It: Another newcomer to the beauty world, Cart Blanche, offers a hybrid skin tint and mineral sunscreen. Beyond its barely-there feel, the formula is packed with plant-derived, skin-loving ingredients: Exosomes derived from licorice and flax plants help to plump skin, while squalane and panthenol keep skin smooth. Meanwhile, vitamin C brightens, and bakuchiol boosts antioxidant protection.
- Key Ingredients: Exosomes, licorice extract, vitamin C, bakuchiol, squalane, panthenol, tomato extract
- Coverage: Sheer, buildable
- Finish: Luminous
- Size: 1 fl oz
I love formulas that bring skincare and makeup together in a thoughtful way. I like that this combines mineral SPF with brightening ingredients and it has a bit more coverage than a skin tint. This is perfect for mornings when you are in a rush and want to just apply one thing that combines multiple steps.
Hung Vanngo Red Carpet Skin Luminous Serum Foundation
- Why We Love It: Hung Vanngo’s serum foundation delivers highly-concentrated coverage yet feels totally weightless. That’s thanks to a blend of microsphere pigments and hydrolyzed hyaluronic acid for equal parts hydration and seamless blending. Meanwhile, niacinamide and licorice extract help to brighten hyperpigmentation over time.
- Key Ingredients: Hydrolyzed hyaluronic acid, microspheres, niacinamide, licorice extract
- Coverage: Medium-to-full
- Finish: Soft focus
- Shades: 38
- Size: 1.01 fl oz
If you see me on a stage or at a photoshoot, this is probably the secret to my skin looking good. In fact, I’ve decided to leave it in my beauty closet makeup bag because I get ready for most weekday things from our office. Celebrity makeup artist Hung Vanngo’s serum foundation is easy to love. While many newer formulas are sheer or light coverage, this is medium-to-full. Fortunately it achieves such without the weight—blurring skin without feelign heavy, pilling, or caking. A feat!
TIRTIR Tinted Skin Glow Cushion
- Why We Love It: K-beauty favorite TirTir already had a viral cushion foundation with a semi-matte finish; and now, the brand’s giving a luminous finish alternative with its Tinted Skin Glow Cushion. For medium, buildable coverage, the formula features 73% skin-care ingredients—a blend of niacinamide, peptides, hyaluronic acid, and botanical oils for hydrating, plumping benefits.
- Key Ingredients: Dimethicone, tocopherol, niacinamide, sunflower seed oil, pantheonol, macadamia seed oil, allantoin, multi-weight hyaluronic acid
- Coverage: Sheer-to-medium, buildable.
- Finish: Radiant
- Shades: 30
- Size: 0.56 oz
I’m not typically a fan of cushion foundations. I’ve tried the other varieties of the TirTir cushion and never found one that I loved. I have very dry, dehydrated skin, so some cushions can accentuate dry spots, especially if there’s any peeling—but I really loved this one! The finish is dewy without looking oily, and the shade match (23N for me) was spot on. Another big issue with cushions for me has always been the cleanliness of the puff, but I like this formulation so much, I’m committed to a regular puff-washing routine and getting extra puffs to have on hand.
