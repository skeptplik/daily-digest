Chinese men risk lives to support girl stuck between window bars outside 12th-floor home
Though some of the men were terrified of heights, the moment they saw the girl dangling outside the window, all fear vanished
Six individuals in southeastern China were named Good Samaritans by local authorities for risking their lives to save a little girl who was in danger of falling from a high-rise building.
The incident unfolded on the morning of October 17 in a residential community in Zhangping, Fujian province.
According to Xinhua, a resident shared a picture of a young girl in the community’s online chat group and urged security guards to help. The girl, about three years old, was shown hanging outside the window of her 12th-floor flat, with her neck caught between the window’s wire mesh.
The crying girl was facing inwards and was gripping the wires, meaning she could fall at any moment.
