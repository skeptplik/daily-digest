After dropping a black version of its flame-adorned Field Boot collaboration with customizer Loso in March, Timberland is following it up with another release in the outdoor brand’s signature Wheat Nubuck style.
The Loso x Timberland Waterproof Field Boot in Wheat Nubuck/Gold features the Bronx-based designer’s familiar flame motif on the toe, giving the otherwise rugged boot some fashionable flair.

Aside from the added flame graphic, the Waterproof Field Boot features all of its usual functional components including a premium Timberland nubuck leather upper, fiberglass shank, 200 grams of PrimaLoft insulation, TimberDry waterproof technology, an anti-fatigue removable insole and a polyurethane cushioned midsole. In addition to their traditional two-tone boot laces, the Loso collaboration will also come packaged with alternate red laces.
Loso has been applying his flame graphics to the Timberland Field Boot for years, frequently hosting customization workshops and selling his custom boots at pop-up events in New York. The collaboration became official in March with the aforementioned release of the black Field Boot, which marked a shift from the handmade custom designs to more mass-produced offerings featuring the designer’s signature graphics.
With the official start of winter still a month away, Timberland is already off to a hot start with its fourth-quarter product releases. Recent highlights have included a Christmas-themed collaboration with English tannery C.F. Stead, a Peanuts collaboration peppered with Snoopy references and a recent restock of its popular woven full-grain leather Premium 6-Inch Waterproof Boot.
After an early launch at ComplexCon in October, the Loso x Timberland Waterproof Field Boot in Wheat Nubuck/Gold will receive a wider release Monday from Timberland’s e-commerce store and select retailers including Snipes. The boots will retail for $180 and their style code is TB0A6DSWAEB. Readers can find a full look at the boot in the gallery below.
