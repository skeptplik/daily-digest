Is this the worst COP summit on record? With limited political leadership, no official US delegation and mounting frustration over bureaucratic delays, questions are being raised about the effectiveness of the climate negotiations. Juan Carlos Beldsoe, a former White House climate adviser under Bill Clinton, described the process as labyrinthine and ultimately dispiriting. France 24's Gavin Lee speaks with climate advocate and six-time COP delegate, Henna Hundal about what this means for the future of global climate action.
