Huge explosion at oil depot in Bangkok sends fireball into sky
An explosion at an oil depot in Bangkok sent a large fireball and thick black smoke into the sky, journalists said Thursday, as local authorities urged people nearby to evacuate.
A huge explosion happened at an oil depot in Bangkok on Thursday, authorities said, with AFP journalists reporting a large fireball and black smoke rising into the air above the Thai capital.
"An explosion has occurred at the Bangchak oil depot. For their own safety, members of the public in the vicinity are urged to evacuate the area immediately," Bangna District Office said in a statement on its Facebook page.
Seven people were injured, four of them critically, a local medical centre said.
The Erawan Emergency Medical Centre, which is operated by city authorities, reported four "red" cases and three less severe "yellow" cases after the blast.
This is a developing story and will be updated.
