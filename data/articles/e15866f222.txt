Russian attacks damaged two of Kyiv's bridges, the mayor of the Ukrainian capital said on Friday (November 20, 2026), as Moscow launched a fresh wave of missiles and drones.
"As a result of the ballistic attack on the capital, the Podilsko-Voskresensky Bridge crossing has been damaged. Traffic from the right bank to the left bank is currently closed on it. The Darnytsky Bridge has also been damaged," Mayor Vitali Klitschko said on Telegram.
He added that the Southern Bridge was hit but did not specify if it was damaged.
Russian president Vladimir Putin vowed "much more painful" retaliatory attacks after Ukraine this week damaged the Crimea bridge, a symbol of Putin's rule over the annexed region.
Ukraine's Suspilne broadcaster reported multiple explosions in Kyiv in the early hours of Friday (November 20, 2026).
The country's air force command said on Telegram that ballistic missiles were heading for the capital.
A building in the Obolon district and a building in the Podil district were set ablaze after Russian attacks, the capital's military administration said.
In the northeastern city of Kharkiv, Mayor Igor Terekhov said on Friday (November 20, 2026) that a Russian drone damaged a hospital.
"Four people were wounded, and more than 50 windows were blown out," he said on Telegram.
Mr. Terekhov also reported a fire at a residential complex in the city's Shevchenkivsky following an attack.
