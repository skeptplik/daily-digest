Johnny Depp-infused A Christmas Carol adaptation Ebenezer is off to a strong start, and has already earned back its production budget.
Ebenezer earned $19 million domestically over its first weekend, and conjured up $42 million internationally for a $61 million opening gross. According to Entertainment Weekly, Ebenezer had a $38 million production budget, though that does not include the film's additional marketing and promotional costs.
While it's certainly a promising start — and seemingly a vote of confidence in Depp after his recent Hollywood hiatus — it remains to be seen how well the movie will perform over time. Ebenezer has received a decidedly mixed response from critics, including for Depp's typically eccentric take on the Dickensian antihero.
Could this damp down the film's performance over time? Perhaps. And next weekend will see the arrival of The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, which looks set to become a blockbuster. Though as Christmas itself gets ever closer, it's easy to see audiences still turning up to Ebenezer as the season gets more festive.
Next up for the star is Day Drinker, a supernatural revenge film coming next year with Madelyn Cline and Penélope Cruz. And then there are the long-discussed potential plans for Depp to return in a fresh Pirates of the Caribbean entry. Whether that actually happens or not, however, remains to be seen.
Is Depp's big return worth your valuable time this holiday season? IGN's Ebenezer review returned a 6/10 score, and described it thus:
"Ti West’s Ebenezer does a respectable enough job taking a more horror-forward approach to A Christmas Carol, but the Johnny Depp performance at the heart of the film pulls more focus than is necessary, even for a story about a man who goes around yelling 'bah, humbug' at children and adults alike."
