The new U.S. tariff policy, which is set to restore duties on most Japanese imports to 15%, was met with questions and a degree of concern in Tokyo.
“Given that the tariffs will be imposed by the world’s largest economy, we cannot say that there won’t be a significant impact,” said trade minister Ryosei Akazawa during a news conference on Friday.
The United States said Wednesday that it plans to impose 15% tariffs on most Japanese imports, citing manufacturing overcapacity. It said that the rate could be taken to 20%.
