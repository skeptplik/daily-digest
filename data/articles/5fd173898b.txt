GTA 6 leaker Cyberleek has reportedly released the executable file for the leaked Xbox build of the game, prompting concern that an unofficial PC port could follow.
The leak comes via the same group that shared leaked footage and threatened to release additional videos unless Rockstar changed its all-digital launch strategy a week before Rockstar released its extended gameplay teaser on Netflix. The group then threatened to release a full build of the game, including its full story mode and end credits.
Now, as reported by IGN, Cyberleek has released the .exe for the incomplete Xbox build, with some predicting that AI-assisted recompilation of the build could get GTA 6 running on PC within days.
The file was released alongside a new statement in which Cyberleek claimed their actions were about "fighting anti-consumerism in the games industry."
They also referenced Take-Two's efforts to stop the leaks and identify whoever is behind them, saying: "Months of takedowns, subpoenas and wallet tracing later, the hunt is still on and the uploads keep arriving."
Cyberleek also pushed back on claims that the leaks are little more than a crypto scheme, insisting the crypto was used to raise money for a secret project unrelated to the game. "There is no other way to move funds without names," they said, "and this work requires no names."
According to IGN, the leaked Xbox build runs counter to the idea that Cyberleek only had access to an early build.
Take-Two previously issued subpoenas to companies including Discord as part of its search for those responsible for the leaks. It remains unclear how this build got out of Rockstar.
