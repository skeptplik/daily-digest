Avengers: Doomsday star and Captain Carter actor Hayley Atwell appears to have let it slip that a new Avengers: Doomsday trailer is set to premiere this Monday.
The longtime Marvel Cinematic Universe actor volunteered the information to a group of fans following a performance of her new Broadway play with Tom Hiddleston, Much Ado About Nothing (via @HayleyAtwell_CN on X/Twitter). When one fan shared their hopes to see the short-lived Agent Carter show return, Atwell admitted that "we'll need to see what happens" with Doomsday first.
"There's a new trailer, I think, dropping on Monday," she said with some hesitation. "Am I allowed to have said that?"
Uncertain or not, MCU enjoyers have been on the edge of their seats waiting for what is expected to be the final trailer as Marvel enters the home stretch to the film's premiere. A few short teasers here and there have helped with the wait, but really, the last full-length trailer actually arrived all the way back in August.
As Doctor Doom's assault on the multiverse looms, we're overdue for another look. That said, it has become increasingly clear that Marvel wants to keep most of the movie's big reveals for those who actually make the trip to theaters to see it. While another trailer likely may not be worthy of a slew of new YouTube reaction videos, it's still sure to have a few goodies to entice viewers to make the trip.
Atwell said that, as far as she knows, the new Doomsday trailer will premiere at some point Monday, November 23, 2026. If, for some reason, Marvel decided to push this alleged trailer back, she was able to report that whatever she saw was "amazing."
Hayley, "There's a new trailer I think, dropping on Monday. I watched the trailer. I was like, this is amazing."
Fan: "I heard very sad though. We're going to all cry."
Hayley, "There's going to be some feelings."
