The amount of money that players spent on video games this October fell 14%, and it’s little surprise that this coincided with the release of GTA 6 in November.
A new report from market research firm Circana shows that gamers were a bit more frugal this October than they were last year, despite two major games releasing that month. Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 launched on October 23, and Gears of War: E-Day released on October 1. Both games enjoyed reasonable critical reception, though reviews for Modern Warfare 4 were rather mixed.
It’s worth noting that sales for E-Day paled in comparison to the rave reviews it received, with analysts estimating that the game only sold 400,000 copies across both console and PC as of October 30. And last October, Battlefield 6 rocked players’ wallets as one of the best-selling games in the entire series — and, indeed, the best-selling game of the entire year — so it’s not a head-scratcher that this was a tough act for MW4 and E-Day to follow.
Circana’s report found that consumer spending on console, specifically, fell 18% from last year, and compared this trend to sales from Battlefield 6 and Pokémon Legends: Z-A. The report pointed to a 16% decline in mobile spending as the primary driver of this drop in addition to Battlefield's booming sales from last October.
However, it’s hard to ignore the impact that GTA 6 has had on the industry at large. Earlier this month, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick revealed that the release of the world’s most highly anticipated game likely had an impact on the lower-than-expected sales for NBA 2K27, as well as other titles.
“I do think that this timing may have affected performance so far,” he told IGN in an exclusive interview. “It has been a crowded period, and we do think that GTA 6 coming has had an influence on the consumer. That said, we're thrilled with how NBA 2K27 is performing.”
The launch of GTA 6 has proven to be a massive and unprecedented moment in pop culture history, to the point where analysts saw a 17% increase in time off requests for its release date on November 19. Even world governments were hopping on the bandwagon, with Australia’s workplace regulator reminding employees of their rights to PTO in a viral post last month. Germany also boasts a program that allows workers to take time off for professional development to play the game.
If that wasn’t wild enough, a U.S. Army Battalion even advertised a special reenlistment incentive that gave service members four days of paid leave that directly coincided with GTA 6’s release, which 43 soldiers ended up taking. It remains to be seen if GTA 6 will really save the average gamer $5,000 by staying indoors, but so many people have hopped online to play that internet service providers reported a massive spike in traffic from Xbox consoles.
