After two years at the creative helm, David Koma is leaving Blumarine, the company announced today.
“I would like to thank David for the personal commitment and creative energy he devoted to Blumarine, and for the value he brought throughout our collaboration. I appreciated his interpretation of the brand and the passion with which he approached this experience, leaving a meaningful mark on our shared journey. I wish him every success in his future endeavours,” said Marco Marchi, Founder and Chairman of Exelite S.p.A. which owns Blumarine.
Blumarine has had a turbulent recent history. From 2019-2023, the label was somewhat reinvigorated by former creative director Nicola Brognano, who brought a popular Y2K aesthetic to Blumarine, including the viral butterfly camisole, low-slung denim and metallics. Following Brognano’s departure in October 2023, Blumarine appointed Walter Chiapponi, who’d previously served as creative director of Tod’s, setting the label on a more mature, classic course. He exited the label after just one season.
Under Koma “Blumarine shed its girlish skin and stepped into a darker, more dangerous light,” wrote Tiziana Carzini in her Spring Summer 2026 review.
“I am grateful to Marco Marchi for his trust and for the opportunity to contribute to Blumarine’s creative evolution. I am proud of the work we have accomplished together and deeply appreciative of the talented teams and collaborators who brought this vision to life. I will carry with me the value of this experience and look forward to the next chapter of my creative journey,” said the designer.
Blumarine didn’t indicate when a successor would be announced.
