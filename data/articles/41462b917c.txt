People affected by a pollution incident that turned a brook bright blue are being asked to share their experiences as part of an ongoing investigation.
The Environment Agency has launched a survey following the contamination of Littleham Brook in Exmouth, Devon between 11 and 25 September.
It prompted warnings against swimming and other water activities at Exmouth beach while officials investigated the cause.
The survey, external closes on 18 December.
Clarissa Newell, regulatory manager at the Environment Agency, said: "We want information from people, businesses and organisations who may have been affected by this incident.
"This could be specifically during this period or you may still be experiencing ongoing impacts."
The Environment Agency said the anonymous feedback could help investigators understand the wider effects of the pollution and may lead to witness statements supporting the inquiry.
It said possible impacts included illness, cancelled water activities, disrupted visits and reduced income for businesses.
No information could be shared at this stage on the source of the pollution as it could affect any future legal action, it added.
Paper copies of the survey are available from Exmouth Town Hall.
