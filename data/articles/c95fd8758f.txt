Is Sara Duterte’s impeachment case being sped up due to Filipinos’ ‘growing impatience’?
The prosecution’s move to proceed on just one charge against the vice-president shows its ‘overwhelming confidence’, a lawyer says
Beyond the trial, the decision could have significant political implications for the 2028 presidential race, with a swift verdict potentially removing a strong candidate, according to analysts.
On October 13, a prosecution panel from the House of Representatives dropped three of the four articles of impeachment against Duterte-Carpio.
