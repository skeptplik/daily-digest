Malaysia’s Najib barred from politics under house arrest rule ahead of Melaka election
If the former leader takes part in political activities or makes media statements, he will be returned to prison, the home minister says
The announcement will remove any ambiguity about Najib’s possible role in the Melaka state election on Saturday and help shore up the credibility of Prime Minister Anwar Ibrahim’s government, already facing recriminations for not being able to stop the house arrest, an expert says.
