The late model Veruschka was a towering figure in the fashion world — both figuratively, and literally, at six-feet tall — but in a 2014 book, the woman dubbed the “original supermodel” says she initially approached the industry with wide-eyed naivety.
“I was in my twenties, but felt like a little girl who did not yet know how to cope with reality,” she wrote in From Vera to Veruschka, a hardcover coffee table book from publisher Rizzoli. “It was this incapacity to accept things as they were that propelled me to enter the fashion world, which was in itself, a very strange universe. It was an escape into a new life,” she continued, “using my body as an instrument, an avatar for metamorphosis.”
This book compiles more than 3,000 never-before-seen images of Veruschka, unearthed when Moncada unlocked a trunk he had left sealed for more than 40 years and discovered thousands of unpublished film negatives.
“Capturing the romance and beauty of la dolce vita, this volume features intimate and rare moments of Veruschka, the iconic face of 1960s glamour,” reads the publisher notes, showcasing the German-born model in “both beautifully staged and informal poses. Moncada’s work also presents a rare glimpse of a young woman, known to friends as Vera, transforming in front of the camera into the image of perfection that we know as Veruschka.”
“My most treasured recollection is Johnny’s presence,” wrote Veruschka. “I felt that I was almost part of his family.”
In addition to words from Veruschka, the book also features contributions from Franca Sozzani and Hamish Bowles, among other notables in the fashion world. As Sozzani writes in the intro, the young girl from Germany was a “model who was destined to become an icon.”
You can pick up a copy of From Vera to Veruschka on Amazon and on sale for 50 percent off right now at Barnes & Noble. There are only a limited amount of copies remaining on the heels of Veruschka’s passing, with fans and collectors no doubt purchasing the coffee table tome to commemorate the late model.
Veruschka’s death was announced by her former partner and photographer Franco Rubartell on Wednesday. She was 87.
