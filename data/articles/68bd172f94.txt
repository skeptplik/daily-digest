Rockstar Games employees have voted against official recognition of the Rockstar Game Workers Union, with 93.2 percent of staff participating in the vote.
25 percent of those who participated voted to recognise the union, while 75 percent voted against. If it had been approved, it would have been the largest recognised union in the UK video game industry by a large margin. While the union will still exist and organise, Rockstar Games will not have to recognise the union as a representing body for staff at the studio, and is not obligated to attend regular meetings with the body.
Jennifer Kolbe, head of publishing at Rockstar Games told Eurogamer: "We are extraordinarily proud of our team, the culture we've built together and the launch of Grand Theft Auto 6. This result reflects our team's strong support for continuing the direct relationships, collaboration and open dialogue that have helped make Rockstar Games so innovative and strong. As we look ahead, we're excited to build on all we've accomplished together."
The Rockstar Union also released a statement following the outcome of this vote, saying Rockstar employees will continue to organise despite the lack of recognition. It reads:
"For anyone at Rockstar who believes in a fairer workplace, a fairer sector and a fairer society - this isn't a sign to retreat, it's a sign to double down on what we stand for. Hundreds of our coworkers voted in favour of our union at Rockstar, yet sadly we recognise we have more work to do to build understanding and win the trust of more of our colleagues. We're ready to put in the effort to make that happen.
"Whilst we haven't emerged with a recognition deal, this ballot has put our union on the radar of every worker, and opened the door for new kinds of conversation to start happening. People entirely unfamiliar with unions were for the first time debating employment conditions, collective bargaining rights, and our contracts. We heard from many more colleagues about what mattered to them, and we hope people will continue to engage so that we can see where these new discussions can lead us.
"A recognition agreement would have marked a path towards greater workplace democracy. But it's one route of many. At Rockstar we have already won, and will continue to win, tangible changes that improve our everyday lives. We'll keep fighting for a say. For pay transparency, contractual overtime pay, and protections against Al and layoffs.
"The power to shape our working lives has always been, and will always be, in our hands."
Rockstar employees represented by the union are still awaiting the verdict from the Rockstar Games vs IWGB employment tribunal legal case, of which the final verdict is still pending. This case was kicked off by the sudden firing of employees present in a union Discord server. The employees claim this was an attempt at union busting by Rockstar, while the company denies this, saying it was for breaking confidentiality agreements.
