Dark Eden, the November read for the New Scientist Book Club, is set on a planet drifting alone outside the Milky Way, where the descendants of two astronauts struggle to survive. Author Chris Beckett talked to Alison Flood about how he dreamed up the flora and fauna on this sunless world, how the genetics of this community work – and how, once he’d built his alien world, he set about telling his story.
