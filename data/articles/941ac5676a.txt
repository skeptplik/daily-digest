1 Blade Runner: 2099
- Available on Prime Video
- Genre: Sci-fi
- Episode count: 8
- Cast: Hunter Schafer, Michelle Yeoh, Katelyn Rose Downey
Set 50 years after Blade Runner: 2049, the series completely shuffles things around. After exploring the dynamics with humans and replicants in two beloved films, the new TV show finds humans as the underclass. The story follows Cora (Hunter Schafer), a human on the run pretending to be a Blade Runner in order to secure a future for herself and her brother.
Per our Polygon review:
                        While Blade Runner 2049 was a direct sequel that continued the story of Harrison Ford’s Rick Deckard, Blade Runner 2099 creator and showrunner Silka Luisa focuses more on exploring the franchise’s themes than advancing its plot. Fifty years after the events of the 2017 film, the Replicants once used as slave labor out in space have led an uprising and now rule Los Angeles. Rather than searching for rogue Replicants, Blade Runners brutally enforce order amongst the humans unlucky enough to be trapped on the barely habitable planet. Desperate human fugitive Cora (Hunter Schafer) is offered a chance at a fresh start if she can impersonate a Replicant to steal something from the police evidence room. When Cora discovers a rogue Blade Runner already took her prize, she has to team up with his former partner Olwen (Michelle Yeoh) to find it.

4 Fool Night
- Available on Netflix
- Genre: Anime
- Episode count: 12
- Cast: Koki Uchiyama, Minako Kotobuki, Junya Enoki
Netflix’s new dystopian anime is set in a world without sunlight, where humanity relies on an optional medical intervention called transfloration. Volunteers are paid handsomely to have a flower implanted in them; it blooms two years later and provides oxygen for others. The series follows an impoverished boy who agrees to the procedure, gaining an ability that allows him to hear the voices of Spiriflora. He soon finds himself drawn into a complex conspiracy.
Per our Polygon review:
                        Based on the manga by Kasumi Yasuda and directed by Atsuyuki Yukawa, Fool Night is a surprisingly intimate story set against an enormous environmental catastrophe. Rather than throwing viewers into an expansive science fiction mystery, Yukawa takes his time introducing its main characters and how their lives become intertwined by circumstances neither could have anticipated. It's a more methodical introduction than some might expect, offering plenty of room to establish the people at the center of a world literally steeped in darkness and comforted only by the discarded remains of the volunteers transformed into grotesque flora.

8 Blue Exorcist
- Available on Netflix
- Genre: Anime
- Episode count: 12
- Cast: Nobuhiko Okamoto, Kana Hanazawa, Yuki Kaji
Blue Exorcist follows a teen raised by an exorcist priest who discovers he's the son of Satan and a human woman. His journey of vengeance against his father begins when Satan tries to claim him as a vessel, with the priest and caretaker sacrificing himself to protect him. The series’ third season is now available on Netflix.
Per our Polygon review:
                        Any shōnen that leverages religious mythology and symbolism to flesh out its world-building is already off to a great start, and Blue Exorcist makes good use of this premise. Two mirror dimensions exist: Assiah, the material world for humans, and Gehenna, the demonic realm ruled by Satan. The demons often breach through the earthly realm via human possession, which is why we have Exorcists, who secretly work for the Vatican and take down these threats. Our protagonist is Rin Okamura (and to an extent, his twin Yukio), who learn that they’re sons of Satan after witnessing their human father’s death.
