Tehran – Bahrain accused Iran of striking a civilian industrial facility on Saturday, causing a fire and damage but no casualties, while two tankers were hit in the Strait of Hormuz, at the heart of the Middle East conflict.
The Gulf kingdom suffered several attacks last week, ending a weekslong lull in the conflict that began with U.S.-Israeli strikes on Iran in late February.
“As a result of the heinous Iranian aggression, a civilian industrial facility was targeted, causing a fire at the site. Civil Defense is working to bring the fire under control,” the interior ministry said on X, without specifying the location.
