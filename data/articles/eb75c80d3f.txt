Training firm Mastered has secured a Skills Bootcamps contract for studios in the West of England, covering 90% of funding for upskilling staff.
Awarded by the West of England Combined Authority (WECA), the contract will provide a minimum of £25,000 in funding for developers in Bristol, Bath and North East Somerset, and South Gloucestershire.
The contract funds 60 places on a first-come, first-served basis. Training is based around each studio, with a mentor assigned to design a "curriculum around [its] tools, pipeline, and production schedule."
Last year, Mastered secured funding to support studios and publishers in London, specifically for leadership coaching. It recently offered programmes in advanced Unreal Engine 5 training, intermediate and advanced C++, Houdini and Blender, and the impact of AI on these disciplines.
"We're delighted to, once again, be able to support the West of England’s games sector with funded training that moves the needle on strategic objectives – whether that's upgrading pipelines for greater efficiencies to upskill existing teams in hard-to-hire roles," said Mastered CEO Perri Lewis.
Mastered has previously utilised Skills Bootcamp funding with over 30 studios, including D3T, Sumo, Avalanche, and Radical Forge.
"Mastered are exceptional collaborators who genuinely understand the games industry’s challenges," said Radical Forge COO Alison Lacy. "They designed bootcamps that perfectly met our needs and improved both individual and team performance."
Earlier this week, the government announced the UK gaming sector will benefit from a funding package of over £45 million as part of the Creative Industries Sector Plan, with eight studios receiving grants for projects costing between £110,000 and £500,000.
The UK Games Fund also revealed its largest group of funded games companies in July, with 29 studios receiving grants of up to £100,000.
