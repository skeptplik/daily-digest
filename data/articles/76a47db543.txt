Mary Fowler headlines big names returning to Matildas squad for Colombia friendlies
In short:
Mary Fowler headlines a host of big names recalled by Joe Montemurro in his squad for the upcoming friendlies against Colombia.
Fowler returns alongside Alanna Kennedy and Teagan Micah, while Kyra Cooney-Cross is also back for her first games since March's Asian Cup.
What's next?
The Matildas will take Colombia on in Canberra on November 28 and in Geelong on December 2.
