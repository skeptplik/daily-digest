Manila – A key regional summit opens on Sunday in Manila, with Southeast Asian heads of state facing questions on trade, security and an ongoing energy crisis while playing host to a who’s who of global leaders.
U.S. President Donald Trump is scheduled to arrive on Tuesday near the summit’s conclusion, a visit expected to include a sideline meeting with fellow “Quad” countries India, Japan and Australia — the first of his second term.
The meeting of the Quad — a grouping broadly aimed at counterbalancing China’s regional influence — would come days before a historic sit-down with Chinese leader Xi Jinping and Russia’s Vladimir Putin in Shenzhen.
