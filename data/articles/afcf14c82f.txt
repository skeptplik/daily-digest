Usman Khawaja has continued on the Fox Cricket broadcast, discussing the new forum announced into the Sandpapergate saga.
The former Test batter, whose book Sabr was the catalyst for the infamous 2018 ball-tampering episode to be thrusted back into the spotlight, says if there is no accountablity from the forum, then the saga will continue to "go i na circle".
My questions are: what are the outcomes from this?
I agree there shouldn't be any sanctions. The price has been paid, and it was a hefty price.
It's never been about that. The biggest question I have is the board makes the decision about what comes out at the end. How is that an actual way of trying to present what people have said?
I could go in and say something and the board decide they aren't going to release that. I just feel like then we go in a circle.
I appreciate where their heart is and I'll support the board in this. I just wanted to take accountability for the three guys who I think were unfairly treated.
But for me there are still a few questions to be asked.
