Electronic Arts is bringing back the voice of the late football commentator John Motson in EA Sports FC 27, recreated using a combination of archive recordings and generative AI.
EA told the BBC the commentary will be added to the game tomorrow (November 26). It's the first time the series has featured a commentator who has died.
Motson, who died in February 2023 aged 77, was the series' original commentator. He was heard in FIFA 96 through to FIFA 2005, and returned briefly for FIFA 19.
EA said Motson's widow and son gave their permission, and that the family was paid for the use of his voice and likeness, though it wouldn't say how much.
"There was no way we would have moved forward with this in any way, shape or form if they weren't fully on board," said Mat Prior, senior game design director at EA Sports FC. "He was the very first voice of our game - so we really wanted to do him justice and bring him back."
Prior said AI was "the only way" to make it happen. Motson never recorded lines about today's stars, such as Bradley Barcola and Chloe Kelly.
Motson's son Fred said it "means an awful lot" to hear his dad in the game again.
"My dad was the least technological person in the world," he said. "So I don't think he would have quite understood what the AI was doing technologically - but I think, on the other hand, he was always really keen to get involved in these kinds of projects."
Peter Caulfield, founder of the anti-AI voice-cloning campaign Save Our Voices Now, was less convinced, and raised concerns that Motson himself never consented.
"If someone has signed a deal allowing their likeness to be used after they die, I have no issue with that," he said. "But in life or in death, unless you have specifically agreed to it, AI cloning of any kind should not be allowed. Being a family member shouldn't automatically give you that right either."
EA insisted the technology isn't there to replace its commentary team. "It's not there to replace commentators in any way shape or form... there's certainly no future plans to replace anyone with AI," Prior said.
Derek Rae, who has commentated on the series for several years, said AI could handle more routine lines, but the "more creative things have to be done with the human voice because they have to have the personality."
In September 2025, a consortium led by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund (PIF) offered to acquire EA. The consortium includes Silver Lake and Affinity Partners and is backed by over $20 billion in debt financing from JPMorgan. The deal was completed in August.
After the deal was announced, EA assured fans it would "maintain creative control" and "creative freedom" under the PIF-led consortium.
