Apple gear goes hand-in-hand with holiday sales. WIRED readers love opportunities to save on MacBooks, iPads, AirPods, and other gadgets—and while discounts on Apple products aren't especially rare, Black Friday deals do usually drop these sought-after items to their lowest prices of the year.
I've rounded up the best discounts on Apple products and accessories below. Everything you see has been hand-tested by someone on the WIRED Reviews team, and every price has been hand-checked ahead of publishing. We'll keep this story updated throughout the weekend.

Best Black Friday Apple Device Deals
AirPods Pro 3 are finally, actually on sale. The best wireless earbuds for iPhone owners are usually slightly discounted, but this Black Friday price is a truly good deal worth considering if you're in the market for an earbud upgrade. They're comfortable, have great noise cancelation, and have never been this cheap before.
For over-ear headphone enthusiasts looking to keep thing Apple, the AirPods Max 2 are the best option thanks to their clear sound and top-notch noise cancelation. They're also eye-wateringly expensive and basically never drop very far in price. If you've been hemming and hawing trying to figure out a smart time to buy, we think it's right now.
We especially like these earbuds for working out. They fit around your ear so they'll stay in place while running—something AirPods definitely do not do (at least not for me). They have punchy sound and solid noise cancelation, plus dust and water resistance.
We love this MacBook and think it's the best option for most people looking to buy a laptop of any kind. The only real drawback is its price, but at $250 off for Black Friday, now is a great time to buy. It strikes a balance between price and performance that WIRED editor Luke Larsen calls “unbeatable,” and it manages to do it in a sleek, high-quality build that inspires confidence.
The inexpensive MacBook Neo doesn't drop in price very often, but Black Friday has shaved just a bit off the top ($50). We wish it had more RAM, but otherwise, the Neo is totally adequate for most basic use cases: think internet browsing, office software, video calls, and watching your favorite shows.
The base iPad is the model we recommend most often, and while it is no stranger to sales, it's absolutely worth considering if you're thinking about getting someone an iPad as a gift. It isn't powerful enough to be a work machine, but for browsing, content streaming, and “games on your phone,” it'll do the trick.
In my personal and professional opinion, the Apple AirTag doesn't get enough love. I became an AirTag enthusiast this year and now I have one on my keys, in my car, in my purse, and on my luggage. The four-pack price isn't as enticing as it could be, but picking up a single AirTag for under $20 is a steal. The batteries are replaceable and the Bluetooth tracker can play a loud chime if you're looking for it and can't find it.
Best Black Friday Apple Accessory Deals
You can never have enough wall chargers. This option is tiny and comes in colors that match with many iPhones. It has folding prongs and a single USB-C port. I always keep one around for when my power bank inevitably dies.
This MagSafe AirPods case is rugged and protective, with a magnetic closure that keeps your earbuds extra protected. The optional lanyard comes in handy if you don't have space to spare in your bag or pockets.
These privacy protectors are dead simple to install, and we like that the edges of the glass are slightly rounded. Getting two for this price is a steal—both the clear version and the privacy version are discounted for Black Friday.
This MagSafe wallet doubles as a tripod and is great to have around for emergency fit checks or group photos. I also like it for hands-free FaceTime calls. There's a version with Apple Find My compatibility that is also slightly discounted.
MagSafe wallets can be tricky to use, but this one makes it easy to get your cards out. Simply press them in and the wallet's mechanism will pop them out for you to grab. The look isn't that special, but the functionality can't be beat.
