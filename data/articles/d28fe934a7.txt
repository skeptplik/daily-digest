Sad news dinosaur lovers. Exoprimal - Capcom's third-person sci-fi shooter with a Jurassic twist - will be shutting down next year. With its closure, the game will no longer be playable, even for those who've got it downloaded.
In a new post to the official Exoprimal website, it's revealed the game will be shuttered on 29th September 2027, at 8am PDT across all regions. Until then, all Survival Passes (battle pass equivalents) are no longer available for purchase, with the game and all its DLC stripped from stores on 30th March.
If you do fancy hopping in for a bit to send off Exoprimal in style, you'll still be able to re-download the game after it's taken off stores if you originally downloaded the game in the past. Tragically, the game will no longer be playable at all once shuttered, as there's no offline support. As an always-online game that requires a constant internet connection, it'll be one for the history books, at least in an official capacity.
Exoprimal was an interesting game for certain. A spin on the popular hero shooter, the game blended staples from the genre with some fairly cool twists. For one, the PvE elements were immediately interesting: seeing waves of raptors race towards you and your allies was critical to its appeal. On top of that, the PvPvE elements where two teams would race to complete objectives was pretty neat, thanks in large part to a variety of enjoyable exosuits at your disposal.
Even so, the game never really caught on to the extent that its peers did. Many pointed to the premium price tag paired with in-game microtransactions as a core factor to this, believing it would be hard to convince someone to play the game over something like Overwatch, which was free. Still, it had nearly a million players soon after launch, with Capcom stating it expected the game to be profitable in a financial report following the game's release. Whether or not it actually was remains unclear.
With that in mind, pay some mind to the Exoprimal players who don't have too long left with their game of choice. All the Krieger players out there have my sympathies. All the Vigilant players do not.
