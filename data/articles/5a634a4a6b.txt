After a series of delays, the Nitro Deck 2 finally launched for the Nintendo Switch 2 this year. Just like its predecessor, it's proving to be one of the Switch 2's hottest accessories for anyone looking to elevate their gaming experience while maintaining comfort over extended periods of time. And with Amazon's Black Friday sale on now, this new and improved hardware from CRKD has dropped to a new low price of $79.99.
For those who already owned this product for the Switch 1, it's worth getting excited about its suite of upgrades over the new version has compared to the original Nitro Deck.
The Nitro Deck 2 is a step up in essentially every meaningful way, providing a far more premium experience over the original. Some of the most notable are improved ergonomics (including an offset right stick), swappable D-pads and stick tops, a sturdier kickstand, and an updated sliding mechanism for a better fit.
Not only does the Nitro Deck 2 offer considerable improvements over the Switch version, it also sports full backward compatibilty with the Switch and Switch OLED consoles. So you'll have the freedom to swap back and forth between Nintendo's newer and older systems if you ever needed to. It can even be disconnected and used as a pro-style controller for your Switch or Switch 2, PC, or mobile device.
For those who value aesthetics as much as the ydo functionality, the Nitro Deck 2 comes in three standard color variations you can choose from: Smoke Black, Pal Grey, and White. Every variation will run you $79.99 (25% off) while Amazon's sale lasts.
If you want add-ons, you can find them directly from the CRKD website. There are variety of accessories for the Nitro Deck 2, such as a pack of swappable D-pads, an 8-pack of stick tops, and more. There's also the Nitro Deck 2 Hardshell Carry Case for $39.99.
