'Coalition of the Willing': Ukraine's allies meet in Berlin after Trump-Putin-Xi talks
Issued on:
Share
Ukraine's allies met Thursday for a "Coalition of the Willing" summit in Berlin with President Volodymyr Zelensky to stress solid wartime support for Kyiv and to push for a European role in any future peace talks. The meeting – co-hosted by the leaders of Germany, France and Britain – was being held shortly after unprecedented three-way talks in China between US President Donald Trump, Russia's Vladimir Putin and China's Xi Jinping.
