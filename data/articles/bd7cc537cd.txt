Nvidia’s board of directors “isn’t considering” having Jensen Huang return as CEO, according to a report from CNBC. A source tells the outlet the board “is focused on an investigation into Huang, and is not in discussions to bring him back.”
Nvidia’s board is reportedly ‘not considering’ hiring Jensen Huang back
Nvidia’s board of directors is still focused on an investigation into Huang, CNBC reports.
Since Huang’s resignation last week, tech executives, along with current and former Nvidia employees, have called for his reinstatement. An online petition to bring Huang back as CEO has amassed more than 180,000 signatures, including from major figures like Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney, SpaceX CEO Elon Musk, and Replit CEO Amjad Masad.
President Donald Trump said on Monday that it “certainly isn’t a bad idea” for Huang to return to Nvidia. A report from the Financial Times also suggests that Huang floated the idea of returning as CEO with some of his colleagues.
Nvidia appointed its chief financial officer, Colette Kress, as interim CEO. The company has also hired Wachtell Lipton to investigate Huang’s conduct.
