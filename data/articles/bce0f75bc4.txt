24-month matured Comté crowned world’s best cheese
Issued on:
Share
With its nutty flavour and smooth, supple texture, it was aged for two years in a former military fortress in eastern France. Comté, an unpasteurised cow’s milk cheese from the Jura Mountains, has beaten more than five thousand challengers to the top prize at the prestigious annual 'World Cheese Awards'.
