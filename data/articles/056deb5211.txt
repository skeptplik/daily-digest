Euronews launches its new season on 16 November with more live journalism, new weeknight programmes and a brand-new studio. Its journalists and editorial expertise take centre stage, with more perspectives and deeper insight into the stories shaping Europe. More Europe than ever, in 12 languages.
Live from morning to night
Every morning at 8:00 am, 9:00 am and 10:00 am CET, Stefan Grobe presents Europe Today live from the new Euronews studio.
Every evening from 6:30 pm CET, the Euronews Verify team checks the facts behind the news.
As the day comes to a close, Mared Gwyn Jones presents Europe Tonight at 6:00 pm, 7:00 pm and 8:00 pm CET, bringing viewers the biggest stories and making sense of the day.
Five new programmes every weeknight at 9:10 pm CET
Euronews takes a step back from the headlines with a new programme every weeknight:
- Monday: The Big Question. Hannah Brown sits down with some of the biggest names in business and industry.
- Tuesday: On the Record with Maria Tadeo. The voices and leaders that matter from the world of politics, business, culture and the arts go on the record.
- Wednesday: The Ring. Two MEPs, two sides, one debate.
- Thursday: The World Conversation. Jane Witherspoon travels the world to challenge political leaders, business figures and other influential voices.
- Friday: Made in Europe. The successes, ideas and innovations shaping the continent.
The Road to Enlargement: A new monthly series
The new season also brings The Road to Enlargement, a monthly series presented by Marina Stoimenova exploring the stories, ambitions and decisions shaping Europe's future.
More live. More conversations. More Europe. This is the new Euronews.
