Is Russia preparing to launch a nuclear-armed satellite?
Issued on:
Share
German media reports, citing unnamed German military and BND spy agency sources, have claimed that Russia may be preparing to launch a nuclear-armed satellite in the coming weeks, giving it the capability to destroy other satellites. In response, NATO chief Mark Rutte said Monday the alliance would defend against threats in space. France 24’s correspondent in Berlin Nick Holdsworth has the details.
