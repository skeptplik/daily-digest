Financial leaders tiptoe around Beijing’s asset scrutiny, hail Hong Kong’s wealth boom
Industry leaders centre discussions on Hong Kong’s soaring asset inflows and safe-haven appeal at major summits this week
Global financial leaders who gathered in Hong Kong this week largely sidestepped Beijing’s offshore trust tax crackdown, a lingering near-term hurdle for the city’s otherwise expanding wealth management industry.
Industry heavyweights instead focused on the city’s appeal as an alternative safe haven amid global market volatility and its growing strength as a cross-border wealth hub.
