Over the years, Taylor Swift has changed her hair often, switched up her style, and gone through many eras, but one constant has been her iconic red lip. Swift has worn the notable blue-red color for decades—through multiple tours, relationships, and possibly even at her wedding—but more recently, she’s branched out and ditched her signature red for something a bit juicier.
On Monday, the Recording Academy announced the 2027 Grammy nominations, and Swift made a surprise appearance on the livestream to announce two of the categories, “Album of the Year” and “Song of the Year,” but her appearance wasn’t the only surprise. Instead of her classic red lip, Swift wore “cinnamon latte lips”—a burnt-orange shade that may be our favorite hue for the fall season. One might assume “cinnamon latte lips” would skew more brown, but the way Swift wears it, the color has a bit of orange. The lipstick shade also resembles the one Swift wore when announcing her recent single “Patient Zero” back in September.
Although Swift swapped her red for a warm brown-orange hue for the Grammys announcement, she still wore her classic winged eyeliner and bangs—two other staples of her beauty look. This isn’t the first time she’s shaken up her lip look. More recently, Swift has leaned more neutral, with a petal-pink lip at the sixth annual Academy Museum Gala and the 2026 MTV Video Music Awards. But she’s not completely forgoing her red lip, as it still seems to be a staple look, especially at her husband Travis Kelce’s Kansas City Chiefs games.
Swift hasn’t explicitly shared what lipstick she is wearing in the video. However, her makeup artist, Lorrie Turk, has previously confirmed that she often wears the Nars lipstick in Morocco, a warm, neutral cinnamon shade with an orange undertone, so she may have worn the same shade during the Grammys announcements.
