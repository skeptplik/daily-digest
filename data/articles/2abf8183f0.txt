If you’re a fan of discrete minimalism or stealth-wealth, this week’s red carpets were not for you. Instead, the best dressed stars of the week leaned into extra opulence—choosing voluminous silhouettes or slightly OTT details (think: Feathers! Crystals!) that instantly turned heads. And the timing makes total sense: Given we are now entering the season of glitzy holiday style, now is certainly not the time to hold back.
A few of the week’s most noteworthy fashion moments? Jennifer Lawrence hit The Hunger Games: Sunrise On The Reaping premiere in New York City wearing in a full Dior look from the pre-fall 2026 collection, including an embroidered black robe coat with a sweeping train. Chic. In Nashville, Taylor Swift returned to her country roots at the 2026 CMAs, wearing a bright-yellow, strapless Oscar de la Renta gown. Talk about a color that will get you noticed.
Even the week’s more subdued moments still had a major impact. At the Focker-In-Law premiere in London, Ariana Grande chose a black vintage Vivienne Westwood gown with a tulle shoulder shawl. Also in the city was Hunter Schafer, who slipped into one of Prada’s killer bra top-and-Velcro skirt combos from the recent spring 2027 show and fall 2002 respectively. Wearing a full look from fashion month’s buzziest collections then and now? A total fashion flex.
