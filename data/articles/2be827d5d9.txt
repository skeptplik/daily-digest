You need to find a way to steal the BF Bandito in Grand Theft Auto 6 to help out Moses. After completing the first batch of requests, Moses will contact you about other cars he wants you to retrieve for him. Among them is this off-road beast. Unlike the Declasse Vamos, you won't need to figure out how to move the BF Bandito out of a garage. The challenge this time is to actually make the target stop.
Below, you learn how to steal the BF Bandito in GTA 6.
How to steal the BF Bandito in GTA 6
You will find the BF Bandito moving around the northern part of Buckskin Swamp, on the southeastern side of Kelly County. As you approach the area, you will see a few cars and motorcycles driving around an off-road circuit. The BF Bandito is a white vehicle that's always followed by a motorcycle riding alongside it. You can also check the gallery to see a picture of it.
The challenge when it comes to stealing this car is that you need to make its driver stop so you can get them out and drive it back to Moses. The best strategy here is to simply block its path, which you can do by parking a car in the middle of the track where the vehicles are racing. If you aren't driving a large car when running this mission, don't worry. You can find a pickup truck at the intersection on the east side of the area.
Once the driver stops the BF Bandito, you can steal it. Since they were already driving it, you won't need to break in or hotwire it. We do advise dealing with the person on the motorcycle, as they will shoot at you and pursue you after you take the car.
A different approach to completing this request is to pursue the BF Bandito and try to kill the driver. However, this is a dangerous method because there's a chance of hitting the car, which may explode depending on how much damage it takes.
To finish the request, drive the BF Bandito back to Moses's garage, and he will pay $3,210 for this car.
