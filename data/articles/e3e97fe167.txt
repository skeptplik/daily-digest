Will China’s maritime moves push Japan and South Korea closer together?
A sharp rise in naval transits could give Tokyo and Seoul good reason to put historical rancour aside, analysts say
A reported sharp increase in Chinese warships and coastguard vessels passing through the strait that separates the two countries, some operating alongside Russian forces, has raised concerns about Beijing’s expanding maritime reach.
The strait, which links the East China Sea with the Sea of Japan, or East Sea, has become a growing focus of Chinese naval activity, according to a Japanese security institute.
