No matter the occasion or season, there are always plenty of opportunities to wear a dress and, of course, an overwhelming number of options from which to pick. We’ve put together an edit of the best dresses of the month; check back every two weeks for a new selection of styles, handpicked by Vogue.
There’s no better time than mid-November to embrace a little holiday cheer. Based on this month’s standout dress finds, the key elements are texture, color, and fabric. Art Deco influences were plenty on the fall runways, and drop-waist styles like Tory Burch’s deep red midi make for excellent party attire. ‘Tis also the season for autumnal textures in wool, velvet, jacquard—try a brocade number for an evening occasion, or pick up a sweater dress from either Me+Em or Proenza Schouler to wear throughout the winter. And for all fall weddings and holiday festivities on the horizon, there are simply too many good options—from Toteme to Staud—that will make you the best-dressed guest.
Whether you’re shopping for new essentials, vacation, or a destination wedding, these are November’s best dresses.
