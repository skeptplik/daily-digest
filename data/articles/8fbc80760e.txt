Live: Zelensky confirms Ukraine’s military struck Russian ships in Libyan waters
Ukraine’s military was responsible for drone attacks on two Russian vessels at the Libyan port of Benghazi Sunday, President Volodymyr Zelensky confirmed Monday. The president alleged that the Russian corvette Merkury had been sunk in the attack. Follow our liveblog for all the latest updates.
- Libyan authorities alleged that Ukrainian drones hit two ships docked in Benghazi, including a Russian warship.
- Poland alleged that the three objects that crossed over into its airspace Friday were Russian missiles.
- Ukraine's President Volodymyr Zelensky said that Russia had launched 2,700 drones and 1,460 aerial bombs over the past week. Russian President Vladimir Putin last week vowed a "painful" response to Ukrainian strikes on Russia's bridge to Crimea.
