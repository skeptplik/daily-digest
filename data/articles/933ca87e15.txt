Kenya discharges last Ebola patient, 42-day countdown to 'Ebola-free' begins
Issued on:
Share
Kenya has discharged its last Ebola patient, who has now officially recovered. The 11 other people who had been in quarantine were also released from hospital on Wednesday, November 11, as the country began a 42-day countdown to being declared Ebola-free. FRANCE 24's Nairobi correspondent Bastien Renouil reports.
