Indonesia doubles down on climate goals despite surging wildfire emissions
Environment Minister Jumhur Hidayat defends growth agenda and outlines six areas for Indonesia’s green economy push as Cop31 talks near
However, Environment Minister Jumhur Hidayat has doubled down, arguing that his country’s climate goals should not come at the expense of national development and that emissions reduction efforts must be fair.
On Wednesday, he outlined six priority areas for Indonesia’s negotiations, The Jakarta Post reported. The priorities include promoting a just energy transition, expanding access to international climate financing, energy affordability, workers’ welfare and protection of local communities.
