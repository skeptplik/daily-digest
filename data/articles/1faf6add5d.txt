On is expanding in the U.S. with four new store openings in the fourth quarter, taking the retailer’s store fleet to 23 locations across the country.
The company’s openings this quarter started with a 3,250 square-foot location inside Aventura mall in the Miami area. Grounded by On’s signature materials like polished concrete and industrial metal finishes, the store contrasts the aesthetic with local coral stone textures that celebrate the coastal South Florida landscape.
Next, was the opening of a 5,700 square-foot location at Suburban Square shopping center in Ardmore, Pa., just outside of Philadelphia. The store brings the neighborhood’s historic textures inside, pairing natural stone with a concrete core and aluminum fixtures. There’s also a freestanding limestone wall and continuous overhead canopy.
Then, the company moved down to the Washington, D.C. area to open a 3,400 square-foot at Tysons Corner in Tysons, Va. Inspired by the region’s hilly terrain and the Potomac River valley, a light-filled curved ceiling and tiered olive leather seating bring the natural contours of Virginia indoors.

Finally, On will open a 3,200 square-foot store at Southlake Town Square in Southlake, Texas in December. The location, just outside of Dallas, features warm red sandstone accents and native Texas pecan wood bring natural local texture indoors, balanced by polished terrazzo and clean aluminum fixtures.
Each location features a full assortment of On footwear, apparel and accessories for running, tennis, training, outdoor and all-day lifestyle. Select products from partner collaborations are also available in limited quantities.
“2026 has been a milestone year for U.S. retail expansion in key cultural hubs with active communities,” Dan Schade, vice president and general manager of the Americas at On,” said in a statement. “Each new store strengthens our multichannel strategy and gives communities distinct yet consistent opportunities to connect with our brand. Across every location, our focus is to create premium, elevated experiences for the communities we serve that reflect the quality of the On brand.”
On’s 2026 retail growth path began in Boston, opening its Newbury Street store days before the city’s well-known road race. Throughout the year, the sportswear brand opened locations in new and existing markets, including Dallas, San Diego, San Francisco and Los Angeles.
The news comes just weeks after the Swiss athletic company issued its most recent earnings report earlier this month.
For the period ended Sept. 30, On’s sales rose 17.2 percent over a year earlier, hitting 931.4 million Swiss francs, or $1.1 billion at current exchange. Net income jumped 36.9 percent to 162.8 million Swiss francs.
Sales through the direct-to-consumer channel rose 25 percent to 393.3 million francs, while wholesale sales increased 12.2 percent to 538.2 million francs.
By category, sales of shoes rose 15.4 percent to 843.7 million francs; apparel sales rose 38.7 percent to 69.5 million francs, and accessories were up 39.2 percent to 18.1 million francs.
