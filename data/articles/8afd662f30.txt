Iran’s top security official Mohsen Rezaei said there are currently no negotiations taking place between Tehran and Washington, in an interview with pro-Iran broadcaster Al-Mayadeen.
Iran has insisted the U.S. must meet seven conditions set out in a June interim agreement before the Strait of Hormuz can reopen and negotiations resume.
Mr. Rezaei said Iran had asked the U.S. for a “clear and explicit” response to its conditions, but had yet to receive one, adding that Washington could respond in the future.
After meeting with mediator Qatar on Monday (November 9, 2026), U.S. President Donald Trump played down the likelihood of major warfare restarting soon but remained unwilling to accept the existing Iranian truce plan.
