Shani Darden is expanding her beauty business beyond skin care.
The celebrity aesthetician has launched Shani Darden Aesthetics within her Beverly Hills flagship studio, bringing together injectables, regenerative treatments, medical dermatology and plastic surgery concierge services alongside her signature facials.
The move highlights the growing consumer demand for comprehensive aesthetic care and marks a new chapter for Darden, who opened her Beverly Hills studio in 2019 and has built a namesake skin care brand sold at Sephora and a celebrity clientele that includes Jessica Alba and Shay Mitchell. While Darden previously offered injectables at the studio through a practitioner from The Things We Do, the new endeavor brings together a team of specialists she has personally assembled to offer a broader range of services.
“I’ve always wanted to have a nurse. It’s a one-stop shop,” Darden said. “People don’t want to go all over the place.”
She’s bringing together aesthetic nurse practitioner Quynh Dao-Payne, dermatologist Dr. Sheila Farhang and L.A. Beauty Connect founders Julie Obst and Alana Ungvari, who specialize in guiding clients through plastic surgery, from surgeon selection to recovery.
“More than ever, people are getting injections,” Darden continued. “It’s what everyone’s spending their money on.”
Dao-Payne, who has more than 20 years of health care experience, offers neuromodulators, fillers, biostimulators including Sculptra and Radiesse, and regenerative treatments such as platelet-rich fibrin matrix, or PRFM. She currently sees clients Wednesdays and Saturdays, with plans to expand her availability.
Farhang, who operates a dermatology practice in Tucson, Ariz., and makes regular visits to Beverly Hills, brings medical and cosmetic dermatology expertise, including advanced lasers, prescription treatments for acne and hair loss, and regenerative procedures. She plans to be at Shani Darden Aesthetics monthly, while also doing virtual consultations.
The broader strategy is to address growing demand for skin longevity, Farhang said: “It’s really a shift from antiaging to supporting your skin and skin health and skin longevity.”
The approach focuses on treatments that stimulate collagen production and support the skin’s natural regenerative processes, she added.
The L.A. Beauty Connect partnership adds another dimension to the business. Obst and Ungvari will offer in-person consultations at the studio, with Darden’s clients receiving expedited access to their network of plastic surgeons and specialists.
“Anyone that’s a Shani client gets access to us more quickly,” Ungvari said.
The partnership will allow for a more coordinated approach to care, from skin preparation ahead of surgery to post-procedure recovery and ongoing maintenance, they said. Asked about emerging trends in plastic surgery, the founders noted a growing interest in eyelid surgery, natural-looking results and alternatives to traditional fillers.
For Darden, the expansion comes as she reconsiders the pace of product launches in an increasingly crowded beauty market. Her hero products include Retinol Reform, Triple Acid Signature Peel and Rescue Serum.
“I’ve been more focused right now on the studio,” she said of product development. “Beauty right now is so overwhelming, and there’s so many brands.”
Her priority is building out the studio’s services, with eyebrow microblading specialist Audrey Glass also potentially joining.
“I just think it’s great to have everything all under one roof,” Darden said.
