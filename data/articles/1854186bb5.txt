Lance Morris's Test hopes dealt another blow after hand injury at party
In short:
Australian Test hopeful Lance Morris has suffered an injury to his non-bowling left hand during a party.
It continues a horror run for the 28-year-old, who has managed just 26 Shield appearances since making his first-class debut in 2020.
What's next?
Morris is set to consult a hand specialist to determine the extent of the injury. There is no timeline on his return to playing.
