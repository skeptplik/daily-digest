Australia's Test batting line-up could be decided by crucial Sheffield Shield round
In short:
A full round of Sheffield Shield matches kicking off this week will help determine the make-up of the Australian Test side to face New Zealand in the first Test.
Key players will miss the matches because of injury and one-day international duty, but others have a chance to impress national selectors.
What's next?
The first Test against New Zealand begins in Perth on December 9.
