On Monday evening in New York, a starry crowd gathered to toast the crème of American fashion at the 2026 CFDA Awards.
Hosted at the American Museum of Natural History by one Keke Palmer, the event saw a teeming group of creatives honored for their contributions to the industry. These included Peter Marino, winner of the Fashion Icon Award; Tony Burch, who received this year’s Positive Change Award; photographers Inez van Lamsweerde & Vinoodh Matadin, fêted with the Geoffrey Beene Lifetime Achievement Award; and Anthony Vaccarello, whom the CFDA recognized with the International Designer of the Year Award as he wraps a decade-long tenure at Saint Laurent.
