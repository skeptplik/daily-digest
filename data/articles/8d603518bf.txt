With the various Black Friday console deals still requiring you to part with over £400 in one go, Raylo (an affiliate partner with IGN) is offering a rather different way to finally jump into GTA 6 — with both PlayStation and Xbox consoles bundled with the game available via a subscription for less than £20 a month.
Amongst the highlights of Raylo’s Black Friday offers, you can currently subscribe to a brand-new 825GB PS5 Digital Edition with Grand Theft Auto VI from £19.49 a month at Raylo, whilst an Xbox Series S with Rockstar's latest blockbuster will set you back as little as £18.79 a month.
Pay Monthly for a Console With Raylo
The best part of all about these Raylo subscriptions is that there’s £0 to pay upfront, with next-day delivery included, meaning you can explore Grand Theft Auto 6’s Leonida, or try any other current-gen games you’ve been aiming to try, before the Black Friday/Cyber Monday season is even over.
For anyone more interested in catching up on Nintendo's biggest releases of 2026 — like The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time or Fire Emblem: Fortune’s Weave — Raylo’s Nintendo Switch 2 has the lowest starting monthly price of the bunch, from £10.99 a month for a refurbished model. There are some decent options for revamping the rest of your gaming setup, too, including LG's 2026 OLED TVs and HP's HyperX OMEN gaming laptops.
How Do Raylo's Gaming Subscriptions Work?
Unlike buying a console outright, subscribing through Raylo gives you access to your chosen hardware for either 12, 24, or 36 months, or on a monthly rolling basis, with both brand-new and refurbished devices available across its range.
Once your chosen term is up, you can either carry on subscribing, return your console, or upgrade to another device — especially handy if you're already eyeing up what the next generation of gaming hardware might bring, but aren’t keen on the bigger investment to buy one outright if you’re not 100% sure.
Mind you, since you won't own the console at the end of your subscription, it's very important worth checking the overall cost across your preferred term before signing up.
That said, if your top priority is to play GTA 6 and you aren’t bothered about playing other games once you’ve beaten the campaign, this is the cheapest way to get on board.
