Should You Wait for Black Friday?
These deals are pretty good right now, but it isn't even Black Friday right now. Amazon hasn't announced it's official sale dates just yet, but they traditionally kick off the best Blu-ray and DVD deals closer to Thanksgiving. So with that in mind, you may want to wait a couple more weeks to buy.
Amazon is overall the best place to buy Blu-rays at a discount during Black Friday. It's there that you'll usually find the lowest price and the retailer will sometimes have BOGO promotions that multiply the savings.
