Europe may be home to three of the world’s fashion capitals, but it has just six cities in Time Out’s top 20 shopping destinations ranking, with only London and Copenhagen making the top 10.
For many of us, a trip to a new place isn’t complete without a little shopping. Around the world, tourists love to snap up a bargain which provides a long-lasting memory after the holiday is over.
Now, Time Out has ranked the best global shopping destinations, with Shanghai in China taking the top spot.
Despite being the home to three out of the “big four” fashion capitals, Europe only has six entries in the top 20, and just two – London and Copenhagen – are in the top 10.
That was decided by a combination of Time Out’s global network of editors, local experts and data from the publication’s annual Best Cities survey, which gathered the views of more than 24,000 local residents across 150 cities worldwide.
The scoring model combines 70% local sentiment and 30% expert evaluation, based on five key pillars:
- quality and variety of shopping, affordability, city vibe
- how safe and welcoming locals consider their city
- wider city experience
- the quality of restaurants, public transport and walkability
- expert evaluation, which is made up of Time Out editors’ assessment of each city’s shopping offering in 2026
These are the best cities for shopaholics to add to their bucket list.
Shanghai, China
Shanghai’s shopping scene is a mix of international names and a growing crop of homegrown fashion and lifestyle brands.
From rummaging for antiques at Juqi Antique City to browsing beauty boutiques along Anfu Road and exploring immersive retail spaces on Middle Huaihai Road, there’s plenty to discover.
Local labels including An Ko Rau, The Beast, Pane and Short Sentence are among Time Out’s recommendations.
London, United Kingdom
One of the four main fashion capitals – alongside Paris, New York and Milan – London is world-renowned for a hugely varied shopping scene.
It certainly caters to all tastes, from the famous department stores of Harrods, Liberty and Selfridges to the vintage shops of Brick Lane, designer boutiques on Bond Street and independent record stores in Soho.
For something quintessentially British, Time Out recommends stopping by the original Daunt Books in Marylebone, just north of shopping paradise Oxford Street.
Tokyo, Japan
It seems like Japan is on everyone’s must-visit list, judging by the number of viral shopping haul videos on social media.
That’s no surprise as Tokyo’s shopping districts offer everything from high-end fashion and vintage designer finds to quirky stationery, manga, cute trinkets and traditional crafts.
Ginza is the place for luxury labels and grand department stores, while Harajuku, Shibuya and Omotesando have a mix of independent boutiques and trend-led brands.
For vintage shopping, head to Shimokitazawa or Koenji, or visit Beams Japan for a curated selection of Japanese fashion, design and crafts.
New York City, United States
New York is the only non-European fashion capital, and its shopping credentials are legendary, far beyond Fifth Avenue and its iconic department stores.
Across the five boroughs, shoppers can discover independent designers, rare vinyl, vintage fashion, antiques, artisan goods and specialist retailers.
Time Out recommends Grand Bazaar on the Upper West Side, the city’s longest-running market, for discovering local makers and taking home a unique piece of the Big Apple.
Melbourne, Australia
Melbourne’s shops reflect the city’s creative, independent spirit, with everything from international labels and luxury fashion in the Central Business District (CBD) and Chadstone to vintage finds and emerging designers in Fitzroy and Collingwood.
Armadale’s High Street is a good place to discover Australian fashion, while beauty fans should make a beeline for Mecca’s flagship on Bourke Street.
