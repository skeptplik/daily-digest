Next is the Ryzen 9 9950X3D processor. Before the X3D2 was released in April of this year, this was the flagship X3D SKU and offered not only a lot of cores and threads (16c/32t) but also X3D cache (128MB) on one of the CCDs, which is great for gaming. Clock speeds start at 4.3 GHz, with turbo boosting a couple of cores up to 5.7 GHz. With its clock speeds, core count, and extra cache, this processor is not only great for gaming but also awesome for any PC work that needs more cores and threads, like video editing, 3D rendering, and even running VMs.
The third part of the combo is the Samsung 990 Pro M.2. This PCIe 4.0 x4 SSD delivers up to 7,250/6,450 MB/s sequential read/write speeds and up to 850K/1,200K IOPS for random reads/writes. This is a very speedy PCIe 4 drive, only eclipsed by more costly PCIe 5-based drives. We reviewed this drive when it was released and loved its strong all-around performance, excellent software (Samsung Magician), and consistent, efficient, cool-running performance.
Last but certainly not least, we reviewed the Asus ROG Strix X870E-E Gaming Wifi motherboard and gave it four stars as one of our best options around the ~$500 price point. This premium offering gives users everything they want out of a motherboard. It has strong power delivery to support the 9950X3D processor, five M.2 sockets (three PCIe 5.0) and four SATA ports for storage, fast networking with 5GbE and 320MHz Wi-Fi, ample USB ports on the rear I/O including dual USB4 (40 Gbps) Type-C ports, and a premium audio solution using the Real ALC4080 codec along with an integrated amplifier. On top of that, it’s packed with “AI” features (Overclocking, Cooling, Cache Boost) and sports a DIY-friendly design.
