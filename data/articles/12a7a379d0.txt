The fashion calendar might insist that fall and winter are two separate seasons, but a truly good handbag has little interest in such technicalities. After all, why retire a perfectly good suede shoulder bag in December when it looks even better tucked beneath an oversized wool coat? If 2026’s handbag trends have taught us anything, it’s that the right arm candy deserves a longer shelf life than your pumpkin spice latte.
Best Fall to Winter ‘It’ Bags of 2026
This year’s It-bag conversation has been anything but quiet. Slouchy silhouettes are challenging the reign of structured totes, chocolate-brown leather is giving classic black some healthy competition, and oversized carryalls are making those barely-there microbags look increasingly impractical. The common denominator? Bags with personality, preferably enough room to accommodate more than a lipstick and wishful thinking.
Chloé‘s bohemian sensibility fits neatly into the equation, with relaxed shapes and vintage-inspired details that complement winter’s heavier textures. Marc Jacobs brings a more playful approach to proportion, while Vince makes a case for understated leather goods that don’t need a monogram to announce their arrival. Damson Madder, meanwhile, offers a more whimsical alternative for anyone whose wardrobe could use a little less seriousness.
And because hunting down six different handbags across six different websites sounds like an unnecessary exercise in patience, Nordstrom makes the shopping part considerably easier. The retailer’s designer selection offers a range of aesthetics, price points, and silhouettes, meaning there’s something for the minimalist, the maximalist, and the person who insists they already own enough bags (we’ve heard that one before).
Below, six designer handbags worth carrying from fall straight through winter 2026, and perhaps several seasons beyond.
Donna Karan New York Bridgehampton Tote
There’s something particularly satisfying about a chocolate-brown handbag against a camel wool coat, and this Donna Karan number understands the assignment. Its spacious interior and structured silhouette make it an equally attractive companion for morning commutes and weekend outings.
Marc Jacobs The Dakota Large Suede Satchel
Fashion’s love affair with suede shows no signs of cooling off, and Marc Jacobs is giving us another reason to stay committed. The Dakota’s slouchy silhouette, belted detailing, and oversized proportions make it particularly well suited to winter’s chunky knits and tailored coats.
Vince Vivian Leather Crossbody Bag
For anyone who finds oversized logos and elaborate hardware a little exhausting, Vince offers a welcome change of pace. This compact suede bag relies on an elegant curved silhouette, rich texture, and an earthy green colorway to make its point.
Damson Madder Organic Cotton Gingham Canvas Tote
Gingham might conjure images of summer picnics and countryside escapes, but Damson Madder has other plans for the familiar check. Rendered on a generously proportioned canvas tote, the print feels surprisingly at home alongside winter’s heavier layers.
