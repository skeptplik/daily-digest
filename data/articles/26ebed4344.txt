Annexed Crimea suffered a widespread power outage on Thursday, local utility providers said, following reports of explosions and air raid alerts across the peninsula earlier in the day.
Krymenergo and Sevastopolenergo, the regional electricity suppliers for Crimea and the port city of Sevastopol, respectively, attributed the blackout to an “accident” at energy facilities “outside” the peninsula.
Utilities advised residents to unplug electrical appliances until service stabilizes. Krymenergo said repair crews were working without a clear timeline for full restoration.
Earlier on Thursday morning, local Telegram news channels reported explosions across parts of the peninsula, though it remained unclear whether Ukrainian strikes had directly hit power infrastructure.
Kremlin-installed authorities issued air raid alerts in the morning, but they have not commented on potential damage or the cause of the blackout.
Crimea, which Russia annexed in 2014, has faced repeated power disruptions this year as Ukrainian forces intensify strikes on the peninsula.
Earlier this month, a Ukrainian strike destroyed a major road span of the Crimean Bridge linking the peninsula to mainland Russia, though the parallel rail line remains operational.
