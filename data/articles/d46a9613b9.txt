Not too long ago, the phrase “Mormon wives” evoked nothing but lukewarm apathy, and for most, confusion. Now, the words are synonymous with one of the largest reality television brands in the world. Among said wives, one has gone on to become the Bachelorette (although her season never aired), while another landed the lead in Broadway’s “Chicago.”
Finalists on “Dancing with the Stars,” hair care empire billionaires, bestselling authors: the group of Utah moms that began filming TikToks in 2021 has exploded into popularity. In the span of five short years, life has turned around completely for these women — most of whom have now left the Church of Latter-day Saints, and also, are no longer wives.
Regardless of the misnomer, the business of being a Mormon wife is a highly lucrative one, with most cast members becoming millionaires thanks to Hulu‘s reality show, “The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives,” which premiered in 2024. With the increasing limelight, the core cast — Taylor Frankie Paul, Whitney Leavitt, Mikayla Matthews, Mayci Neeley, Layla Taylor, Jessi Draper, and Jen Affleck — has undergone quite a few changes, beginning with their beauty.
From embracing vs. escaping the infamous “Utah curls” to makeup application, WWD is breaking down the beauty evolution of the cast of “Secret Lives of Mormon Wives.”
2021
At the beginning of the group’s ascent to fame, Frankie Paul wore her waist-length brown hair loose and streaked with lighter shades of gold. The reality star wore her eyebrows dark and arched while usually keeping the rest of her look minimal save for self-tanner and various shades of skin-toned pink lipstick.
Leavitt, pictured here with husband Connor, was an ash blonde in 2021, straying away from her natural hair color of a caramel brown. The “Chicago” star sported thin, plucked eyebrows and dark cluster lashes, usually paired with a pale pink lip.
Neely has remained loyal to her platinum blond locks, just below her shoulders in 2021. The then 26-year-old often sported the infamous “Utah curls” which combine a loose barrel curl with straight ends. Like the rest of the women, Neeley favored long black lashes and tinted brows.
The youngest of the group, Taylor had chocolate-brown hair in 2021, which was often styled similarly to Neeley’s. Taylor opted for full, brushed-out brows and cluster lashes along with light pink blush and lipstick regularly.
A hairstylist by trade, Draper is the most prone to switching up her hair among the group. The Utah-based business owner often sports extensions in addition to dyeing her hair, as pictured here, with Draper donning 24-inch brunette waves. Draper favored curved eyebrows in 2021, along with bright lipstick shades and matte foundation.
Affleck — who recently announced her divorce from husband Zac, pictured above, on Nov. 3 — wore her hair in an ombré style in 2021. Affleck’s blond waves appeared toward the ends of her hair while her natural brunette coated the top half. Affleck often donned full cluster lashes, plenty of blush on the apples of her cheeks and a darker shade of pink on her lips.
2022
Now infamous thanks to a viral swinging scandal, Frankie Paul began switching up her look in 2022, swapping in some cooler-toned highlights and wearing her long locks straight and free-flowing. Her brows took on a lighter, more brushed-out look while staying consistent with the light pink lipstick.
Leavitt’s brown locks made a return in 2022, styled in layers and often worn straight. Leavitt’s arched eyebrows took on a touch darker shade while the reality star began opting for lighter, spaced-out lashes. Pink blush and lipstick remained classics for Leavitt as well. Later in the year, Levitt dyed her hair red for the first time, opting for a fiery shade for a brief period.
Matthews — who did not post on social media in 2021 — had waist-length hair in 2022. Her long brown tresses were left loose with a slight natural wave and blond streaks placed throughout, including one framing each side of her face.
Also growing a bigger following in 2022, Neeley grew out her cool-toned blond hair, and added a slight arch and tint to her brows. Neeley also began favoring a shimmery brown-taupe eye shadow and blush applied in a W shape across her face, switching out creamy lipsticks for shiny lip gloss.
Taylor’s took on a more cool-toned shade of espresso in 2022, usually styled in loose curls and with a middle part. Then 21, Taylor maintained the same beauty from 2021, favoring cluster lashes and pink blush on her cheeks.
Draper continued to experiment with hair colors in 2022, this time opting for a “bronde” shade, styled in the favored Utah curls. Draper also changed her eyebrows, going from a curve to an arched shape, adding definition to her face.
Affleck had the most dramatic hair transformation of the group in 2022, dyeing her hair a melting pot of warm brunette tones. Affleck styled her layered cut in a style reminiscent of Jennifer Aniston’s hair on “Friends,” but longer and flowing over her shoulders. The rest of her beauty remained similar to 2021, with emphasis on blush, cool-toned pink lipstick and natural brows.
2023
Now filming for Hulu’s “Secret Lives,” Frankie Paul dyed her hair darker, getting rid of most of her lighter-toned highlights. The former “Bachelorette” also cut face-framing pieces, complementing the heavier glam she sported for high-profile appearances which included gold eyeshadow, blush, and mauve lipstick.
Leavitt returned to blonde while filming Season 1 in 2023, this time chopping her hair into a wavy blond shoulder-length bob. Leavitt left her eyebrows dark and arched in contrast to her lighter locks, and was often seen barefaced or with minimal makeup during the season.
Leavitt wasn’t the only one to switch up shades — Matthews donned a light shade of brown in 2023, styling her waist-length hair in loose waves. The reality star kept her brows brushed out and brunette, adding a natural look to her beauty, which featured blush and long, dark lashes.
Neeley maintained her mid-length hair in 2023, opting to brush out her layered tresses with a light bounce at the end. The reality star swapped out her full tinted eyebrows for a more natural, brushed-out look that bracketed her blue eyes and upturned lashes.
Ash blond highlights made their way to Taylor in 2023, appearing primarily toward the bottom half of her dark brown waves. Similar to Neeley, Taylor began favoring brushed-out eyebrows, pairing them with kohl-rimmed eyes. Later in 2023, Taylor transitioned to having a full head of ash blond hair, staying darker at the roots with face-framing pieces cut into her layers.
Draper opted for a Barbie shade of blond in 2023, this time cutting her hair to just above her shoulders. The new shade added contrast to her dark brows and heavy black lashes, while the rest of Draper’s glam focused on even skin and a pop of color with her pale pink lipstick.
Affleck’s hair took on a brassy-gold hue in 2023, styled in voluminous beachy curls and paired with dark, defined eyebrows. The former “Dancing with the Stars” contestant stayed true to her use of warm blush, incorporating faux freckles and shimmery gold eye shadow to add a fun flair to her everyday looks.
2024
Pictured at the Country Music Awards in November, Frankie Paul added warm, golden highlights to her hair, now ending well below her shoulders. The Utah native’s brows took on a fuller, straightened shape while falsies and a nude pink gloss remained steady in Frankie Paul’s favor.
Leavitt sported her natural hue of light brown with a few warm blond highlights in 2024, cutting the wavy locks into a chin-length bob. Leavitt opted for dark, straight eyebrows and usually favored minimal makeup, allowing the pink details of blush and lipstick to serve as the stars of the show.
Matthews turned to a darker shade of chocolate brown in 2024, likely incorporating a few extensions into her waist-length locks. The reality star has been open about her struggle with psoriasis and her skin, tending to keep makeup minimal and natural looking.
Neeley sported an ombré of blond in 2024, with her hair resembling a shade of “bronde” at the roots and lightening to platinum by the ends. Lighter streaks of blond were incorporated into the then 30-year-old’s hair. Neeley continued to brush out her brows, this time taking on a more curved shape and pairing with her typical icy glam in pale pink shades.
Taylor’s hair took on its darkest hue yet in 2024, with her black waist-length locks styled in loose barrel curls. She began favoring pops of highlight in the corner of her eye and along her nose and cheekbones. Long lashes and kohl remained favorites for Taylor who completed her looks with blush on the apples of her cheeks and nude pink gloss.
A warm, honey-toned shade of blond was Draper’s go-to in 2024, as she began straightening out her brows, allowing for a softer arch. The JZ Styles founder continued to favor pale pink lipstick and false eyelashes.
Affleck returned to an ombré in 2024, this time opting for a more honey-toned hue like Draper. Her roots remained dark brown while the golden ends were often styled in loose barrel curls. Affleck continued to experiment with faux freckles and blush, often pairing her glam with a raspberry pink lip.
2025
Not much changed in terms of beauty for Frankie Paul in 2025. Well into filming Season 3, Frankie Paul kept her hair a mixture of blond and brunette streaks, but opted to go lighter still, cutting more defined layers. Her brows returned to their natural shade and shape as the reality star began brushing them out.
After a brief return to her natural caramel brown, Leavitt took the plunge and became a redhead in 2025, sporting a warm, coppery shade. The former “Dancing with the Stars” contestant thinned out her eyebrows and tinted them a darker shade, with the new straight shape adding dimension to her beauty. Leavitt also began favoring a flick of black liquid eyeliner and a bolder lip for high-profile experiences.
Matthews stayed a brunette in 2025, warming up the shade considerably and adding delicate gold strands to the front and throughout her long waves. Her lighter, brushed-out brows framed her long wispy eyelashes and paired well with the bright blush on her cheekbones and rose lip Matthews favored for public appearances.
Neely began styling her platinum blond hair in softer curls reminiscent of a brushed-out blowout during 2025. Her eyebrows were also thinned and styled with a more triangular arch, setting off the drama of her long black lashes and icy blue eyes. Neely continued to favor a matte finish for her makeup, adding darker eye shadow for red carpet events.
Taylor opted for a straight, shoulder-length bob in 2025, maintaining the jet-black hue from 2024. The reality star also began incorporating bronzer into her glam routine while moving toward a warmer-toned blush and lip combination.
Draper landed on the “bronde” mix that she sported for the majority of Hulu’s show in 2025. The reality star also began applying her blush higher on her cheekbones and matched her brow tint to the roots of her hair, creating a cohesive and complementary beauty. Around the same time, Draper began to gravitate toward cool-toned lipstick along with nude shades of gloss.
Affleck’s hair reached its longest length yet in 2025, when she dyed her waist-length curls a rich shade of golden brown. Similarly to Taylor, Affleck grew increasingly drawn to warm-toned products, including an apricot blush and warm pink lip gloss.
2026
Pictured at the 2026 Oscars in March, Frankie Paul took a break from the public purview later in the year due to her legal proceedings. Frankie Paul often opted to wear her hair straight and loose with sparse blonde highlights. Her eyebrows remained straight with a soft arch completed by Frankie Paul’s usual peachy glam.
Leavitt continued to favor slightly winged eyeliner, thin brows and a pink lip for glam in 2026. Growing increasingly rooted in the theater scene, Leavitt opted for a darker hue of red that paired well with the soft light pink blush the performer began to use.
Matthews returned to a dewy, no makeup-makeup look in 2026, placing emphasis on glowing bare skin and minimal glam. The 26-year-old cut her chocolate brown just below her shoulders and tends to wear it straight and in loose layers. Straight light brown brows and a pink lip complete most of Matthews’ looks.
While continuing to keep her blonde hair cool-toned, Neeley moved toward warmer makeup in 2026, favoring peach blush placed high on her cheekbones and nude lip gloss. The reality star continued to favor smoky and dramatic eye makeup to create a visual contrast against her near-gray eyes.
Taylor experimented with a few hairstyles in 2026, donning long brown hair with honey-blond highlights and bangs at the beginning of the year, before later moving to a rich auburn and then settling on a dark, rich brown. She also briefly wore blonde braids and stuck to the bronze, warm glam of 2025.
Draper became a brunette for the majority of 2026, donning the same rich shade of brown many of the other “Mormon wives” have dabbled in. The dramatic hue paired well with the smoky eye makeup and taupe lip Draper increasingly gravitated toward this year.
Sticking with the deep brown hues of 2025, Affleck trimmed her hair to below her shoulders and often sported tighter curls. Gold eyeshadow entered her rotation while she continued to favor blush placed high along her cheekbones and a pink lip.
