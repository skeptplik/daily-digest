MILAN — Italian fine fabric company Successori Reda SBpA and io.Global LLC (Io.), the global holding company that provides ecological and ethical solutions for six sectors, have come together to raise more awareness about natural fibers.
On Thursday the companies unveiled a joint venture bringing together Reda’s textile expertise, research and innovation and io.’s commercial, cultural and scientific ecosystem. The idea is to create a shared platform to explore the role natural fibers, particularly merino wool, can play in the relationship between what people wear, their well-being and the environment around them.
“The most important collaborations begin with a shared vision, even before a business objective,” said Francesco Botto Poala, chairman of Successori Reda.
“In Io., we have found the same values, the same long-term perspective and the conviction that the future of fashion depends on a more authentic relationship with nature and people. By bringing together complementary expertise, we have the opportunity to build something greater than either of us could achieve alone,” said Botto Poala.
The agreement between the two companies is based on the concept that this initiative will help to change the way clothing is conceived, designed and experienced through innovation and responsibility.
“For me, this has never been simply a financial transaction. It began with a genuine admiration for the values and consistency with which Francesco Botto Poala and his family have run their companies, learning from nature rather than working against it,” commented Suzy Amis Cameron, founder and chief executive officer of Io. “Through this joint venture, I want to strengthen the work already underway to support younger generations and those to come.”
The joint venture is backed by an aggregate 5 million euros in new capital investment, alongside significant noncash contributions that extend well beyond this amount.
As a first move, the new company will focus on the strategic repositioning and subsequent international expansion of Rewoolution, the Reda Group’s merino wool performance base-layer brand, starting with the appointment of Matteo Ward as chief executive officer.
Drawing on his experience in responsible business development and brand building, Ward will lead the brand’s strategic evolution and international growth. He succeeds Botto Poala, who will continue to support the brand as chief operating officer and adviser.
“Leveraging a vertically integrated chain, this joint venture gives us an extraordinary opportunity to expand the applications of one of the most advanced technological materials available to us: merino wool. The projects we have in the pipeline are built on culture, research and innovation, to expand our understanding of the impact clothes have on our physical and psychological health,” said Ward, CEO of Io. fashion, textiles and home.
The first new collection developed by the joint venture will debut for fall 2027.
