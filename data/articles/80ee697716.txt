British singer Raye, with the genre mixing hit "Where Is My Husband!," landed at the top of the list of Grammy Award nominees on Monday, followed by Spanish musician Rosalia and "Choosin" Texas" artist Ella Langley.
Raye earned 11 Grammy nominations including nods for song and record of the year for "Husband," and for album of the year for "This Music May Contain Hope."
She now ties Beyonce's record at the 2025 Grammys for the most nominations in a single year by a female artist.
For her part, Rosalia received nine nominations, just days after sweeping the Latin Grammys with her album "Lux."
First-time nominee Langley secured seven, as did Miranda Lambert, Olivia Rodrigo and Phoebe Bridgers.
Megastar Taylor Swift received five nominations.
Only one male artist nominated for Best Album
Besides Raye, competitors in the album race include Taylor Swift's "The Life of a Showgirl," Langley's "Dandelion," Rosalia's "Lux," Bridgers' "Lost Weekend," Olivia Dean's "The Art of Loving," and Rodrigo's "you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love." Bruno Mars was the only male artist nominated in the category, for his album "The Romantic."
The record of the year category is made up of Dean's “Man I Need,” Langley's “Choosin' Texas,” Mars' “I Just Might,” Raye's “Where Is My Husband!”, Rodrigo's “The Cure,” Rosalía’s “Berghain” featuring Björk and Yves Tumor, Swift's “The Fate of Ophelia” and Tame Impala and Jennie's “Dracula.”
K-pop sensation BTS declined to submit any music for consideration in protest over a new Asian pop music category. Members of the South Korean band said they did not believe music should be categorized by region.
Dolly Parton, the country legend who died in August, received three posthumous nominations.
Winners will be announced on February
The final Grammy voting round will run from December 10 to January 7, with winners selected by around 16,000 Recording Academy members.
Winners will be announced at a red-carpet ceremony in downtown Los Angeles on February 7.
The event will be broadcast live on Walt Disney's ABC and streamed on Disney+ and Hulu.
