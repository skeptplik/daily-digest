Wasting less and looking after our physical and mental health were among the themes of this year’s Qatar Sustainability Week. Schools, businesses and communities explored how the choices we make when shopping, eating and going about our day can affect both the environment and our wellbeing.
Qatar Sustainability Week explored what households and businesses could do differently, from shopping habits to managing waste. At LuLu, shoppers were encouraged to use reusable bags and return empty bottles and old electronics for recycling. Schoolchildren have also helped spread the message by speaking to customers about replacing single-use bags.
At Qatar Foundation’s Green Island in Education City, sustainability programmes for schools and companies run throughout the year. Enbat’s permanent food waste exhibition shows the resources needed to get a meal onto the table and what is lost when food is thrown away. It also demonstrates how discarded ingredients can be used again, with banana peels turned into taco filling, potato skins into crisps and carrot tops into pesto.
At Qatar University, researchers explained how digital tools could help organisations measure carbon emissions and report their environmental impact. Youth Fest also explored the social side of sustainability, with young organisers combining environmental activities with a space focused on physical and mental wellbeing.
