Anime style has been the main focus in Fortnite Treasure Hunters due to its crossover with One Piece. However, Epic Games is now changing lanes and bringing a beloved new IP into Fortnite. As confirmed by series creator Bryan Lee O'Malley, Scott Pilgrim is coming to Fortnite soon.
Scott Pilgrim is a coming-of-age series that focuses on the adventures of Scott, a bass player in a local rock band, as he takes on his crush Ramona's evil ex-boyfriends. The franchise developed from a graphic novel series, Scott Pilgrim, to a cult movie, Scott Pilgrim vs. the World, and further expanded to include video games and an animated show produced by Netflix and Science Saru, Scott Pilgrim Takes Off.
Scott Pilgrim creator reveals Fortnite collab
O'Malley revealed the Fortnite collaboration in an 18-image post on his Instagram. The first image shows Ramona Flowers holding Scott Pilgrim's hand as the two seemingly fall from the sky. Each slide offers a closer look atthe strange object in the distance. Eventually, a familiar outline comes into view; it becomes clear that it is, in fact, the Fortnite battle bus that drops players onto the island.
In the latest Fortnite update, dataminers were able to dig into the game files and find out which type of cosmetics are coming as part of this collab. Leaks reveal that there will be two skins, one emote, two pickaxes, and two instruments: a bass and drums. From the teaser, we expect the skins to be Scott and Ramona. However, while Scott is known for playing bass in the band Sex Bob-Omb, Ramona doesn't play any instruments; Kim Pine is the drummer. This could mean that the crossover will include even more characters from the series.
For now, information is scarce, and this is all we know about the Fortnite x Scott Pilgrim collaboration. Since official teasers have already been published, it shouldn't take long for the collab to launch.
