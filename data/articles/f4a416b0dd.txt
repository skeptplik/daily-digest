From Glasgow teenagers to global rock stars, Simple Minds have spent nearly five decades filling stadiums and creating some of the defining songs of their generation. Now, as the band celebrate 50 years with new music "Sacrosanct" and a major 2027 tour, Jim Kerr tells Eve Jackson about the music, the memories and the moments that shaped Simple Minds, including the night he discovered they were number one in America by telegram in a hotel in Nice.
