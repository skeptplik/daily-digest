As the tabletop industry has blossomed and grown in popularity the past number of years, making digital versions of beloved games has taken off as well. And right now, Steam users have the perfect chance to stock their digital Kallax shelves with some amazing deals on digital cardboard.
Lasting until November 15, Steam is currently running the Digital Tabletop Fest - hosted by Nomad Games & Monster Couch. The sale features a wealth of these digital adaptations but if you are more of a physical-only type of player, check out our 2026 Black Friday page for more upcoming sale dates.
Take a quick peek through the shelves of the sale and you will find some wonderful offerings, including some that we have featured on our “Best of” lists. What better time too, as we enter the season where everyone will be inside more! A perfect time to roll some digital dice.
