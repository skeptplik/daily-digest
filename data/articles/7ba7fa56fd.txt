After delivering its Foamposite sneaker boot hybrid in a golden “Green Goblin” style in November, Nike is running it back with another staple colorway as we enter the holiday shopping season.
The Nike ACG Air Max Goadome Low Foam is set to arrive in December in a new Black/Dark Smoke Gray colorway. The new look sees the entire sneaker covered in a blacked-out aesthetic, with its Foamposite shell leaning ever-so-slightly lighter in a Dark Smoke Gray shade.
The Foam-infused variation of the ACG Air Max Goadome Low was introduced with the aforementioned Green Goblin colorway and is a continuation of the standard Goadome Low model brought to market by Nike in February. After a series of popular in-line releases and a fan-favorite Taller de Rafa collaboration, the model recently received a three-sneaker collaboration with Carhartt WIP.

The ACG Air Max Goadome Low is the latest in a string of Foamposite sneaker boot releases which include a reissue of the high-top ACG Air Max Foamdome, which was released in November in its Eggplant colorway.
Ahead of its official launch, the sneaker’s Green Goblin colorway received an early release from NYC-based luxury resale store Luke’s. In recent years, Nike has increasingly engaged with popular resale shops for marketing and product launches, including numerous collaborations with vintage store Procell.
Nike’s Goadome line was first released in 2000 and was intended to compete with the street-style boot segment of the market that had been dominated by Timberland throughout the ‘90s.
Nike’s latest ACG Air Max Goadome Low Foam in Black/Dark Smoke Gray will be released on Dec. 3 at 10 a.m. ET from Nike’s Snkrs app and select Nike Sportswear retailers for $200. The sneaker’s style code is IV7693-001. Readers can find a full look at the new colorway in the gallery below.
