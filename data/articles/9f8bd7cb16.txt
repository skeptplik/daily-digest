It’s not very often that the stars of Vogue’s Beauty Secrets open up their routine with a caveat. But Sienna Miller, actor and mother of three, is here with a confession.
“I’m just going to preface this entire thing with saying I have a black eye,” she says while lying up her signature blonde waves. “And, no, I did not have surgery. I was breastfeeding in the middle of the night, bent down, and whacked it on the bedside table.” The entire anecdote is leading to her first product recommendation of the video, Arnicare Bruise Gel. “Arnica cream is a miracle,” she adds.
From there, she walks us through her entire routine from cleanser (a gentle foaming variety for sensitive skin from SkinCeuticals) to her 3-step under eye routine to battle both that bruise and any tiredness from long nights with her kids. And, yes, she does give us a glimpse into how she maintains that dreamy boho texture: A spritz of Sam McKnight’s aptly-named Cool Girl Barely There Texture Spray. It’s almost as if the product was named in her honor.
