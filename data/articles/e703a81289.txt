What is it? PNG says it’s an artillery shell. China says it’s a wine bottle
4 Chinese nationals arrested, bomb squad deployed following discovery of suspected artillery round in a commercial shipping container
The embassy said in a social media post on Tuesday it was awaiting the official findings of an investigation by the Royal Papua New Guinea Constabulary (RPNGC).
It cited an on-site verification by a company involved in the case, which said the object was a wine bottle. “It poses no danger at all,” the embassy said.
The embassy also noted that China had some of the world’s strictest firearm control laws and it was “virtually impossible” for an illegal military-grade artillery shell to be smuggled out of China to PNG.
