Alan Ritchson has had a busy year. Between seasons of Prime Video's Reacher, he's taken starring roles in numerous film and TV projects, including Netflix's sci-fi thriller War Machine and the action comedy Runner. But of course, his best-known role is the wandering renegade Jack Reacher, and the role has earned him praise from Amazon and MGM Studios' top executives. In a 2024 interview, Jennifer Salke, head of Amazon and MGM Studios, told The Hollywood Reporter, “We’ll have Alan as long as he will play Jack Reacher. No end in sight for that, hopefully that’s for a very, very, very long time.”
As fans eagerly await the next seasons of both Reacher and its spin-off, Neagley, the series is proving that, like its title character, it refuses to back down on the streaming charts.
Reacher officially hits major streaming milestone on Prime
Reacher has hit a major streaming milestone: the show has been ranked among the 10 most-watched TV series on Prime Video for 100 consecutive days in the U.S., according to data aggregator FlixPatrol. That's just over three months of Alan Ritchson and Jack Reacher dominating the Prime Video streaming charts, and as of Nov. 27, 2026, the show is still holding steady in ninth place.
That's an impressive milestone for Reacher following its fourth season, which garnered more than 66 million total views and became one of Prime Video's most successful original series to date. On Rotten Tomatoes, the fourth season was very well received by critics, and currently holds a 92% score.
Ritchson, for his part, has been recognized time and time again for the work he puts in both on and off screen to bring Jack Reacher to life. Jennifer Salke told The Hollywood Reporter in 2024: "What makes him unique is that he is very brave and open in talking about mental health. He’s a modern leading man in that sense, and he comes to it all so honestly, through life experience. Though it’s been tough for him, he will share his story with anybody.”
What do we know about Season 5 of Reacher?
The fifth season of Reacher will be a season-long adaptation of Make Me, the 20th novel in Lee Child's Jack Reacher series, which begins with Reacher arriving in the tiny Midwestern town of Mother's Rest. In true Jack Reacher fashion, Ritchson's character will find himself at the center of a mysterious, deadly investigation that involves the whole town. Other cast members are set to include Amanda Ip as Michelle Chang, Jay Baruchel as Chief Buck Bauer, Ciara Bravo as Eunice and Kevin Durand as Nokes.
The fifth season is currently being filmed in Toronto, and is scheduled to wrap during the first week of December 2026. A date for Season 5's debut on Prime Video has not yet been announced, but given that Reacher has already been renewed for a sixth season, it's likely that Reacher will return to the top of Prime's "newly added" list sooner rather than later.
