Hong Kong temperatures to fall to 18 degrees next week as cold front hits
Observatory says strong monsoon to sweep Guangdong province’s coast over weekend will bring cooler weather starting on Tuesday
Temperatures in Hong Kong are expected to drop to as low as 18 degrees Celsius (64.4 Fahrenheit) on Tuesday as a cold front hits the city early next week.
According to the Hong Kong Observatory, a strong monsoon over the weekend will bring cooler weather.
“A strong northeast monsoon will affect the coast of Guangdong over the weekend. A cold front is expected to move across the coast of southern China at first on Monday,” the forecaster said on Friday.
