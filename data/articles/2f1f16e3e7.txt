This Baseus power bank boasts a generous 20,000mAh, or 74Whr battery capacity. If you factor in 80% power efficiency, here are the approximate number of times you can fully recharge each gaming handheld:
- Nintendo Switch (16Whr) about 3.7 times
- Nintendo Switch 2 (19Whr) about 3.1 times
- Steam Deck (40Whr) about 1.5 times
- Apple iPhone 18 Pro (17Whr) about 3.5 times
- Apple iPhone 18 Pro Max (22Whr) about 2.7 times
The power bank has four total USB outputs: not one, but two built-in 45W USB-C cables that clip together to form a mini lanyard loop when not in use, another 45W USB-C port, and a 33W USB-A port. The 45W Power Delivery output can charge the Nintendo Switch, Nintendo Switch 2, and Steam Deck at their maximum rate, which means you can play and charge simultaneously without draining the battery. The built-in cable is a popular feature on newer power banks because you no longer have to bring along your own USB Type-C cable.
