Jenga: Super Mario Edition for $12.49
This Super Mario version is like regular Jenga, but with a fun set of Super Mario-themed twists. For one, its blocks are modeled after those in Mario games. It also uses a spinner to determine the number of blocks you need to stack, how many layers you need to jump, and whether or not you can collect coins. Each player chooses a character: Mario, Luigi, Peach, or Toad, and the goal, of course, is to defeat Bowser by racing up the tower. Like any game of Jenga, it's all over if the tower falls, so you have to defeat Bowser before it's too late.
This sale won't last long, and for just over $12, it's hard to beat what could be a great addition to your board game collection.
