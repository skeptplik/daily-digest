Can Elon Musk really put AI datacentres in space? – The Latest
The Trump administration has approved Elon Musk’s plan to create datacentres in space. US federal regulators have authorised the launch of one million Starmind orbital satellites, which are built by Musk’s SpaceX, to process data in space. Supporters argue the project will be more energy-efficient and reduce the environmental impact of datacentres on Earth as the AI industry grows.
