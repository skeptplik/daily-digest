From multi-brand salons to designer showrooms, the best bridal shops in NYC are worth making appointments at to find every piece of your wedding wardrobe.
Whether you live in the U.S. or beyond, New York City is the premier destination to shop for a wedding dress. The best bridal shops in NYC run the gamut from singular showrooms for locally based ateliers, flagship stores for international brands, and salons featuring curated selections from a number of top bridal designers. To-be-weds looking for their own wedding wardrobe can always benefit from a trip to multiple bridal shops around New York to help them discover some of the most beautifully made and innovative designs in the bridal industry.
As a New York local who tried over 100 dresses ahead of her own wedding, wore five looks over her wedding weekend, and regularly attends bridal fashion week both in New York and Barcelona, I am constantly asked where to shop in the city. I myself went to 15 different salons over the course of my search for the perfect wardrobe (and had repeat visits at multiple). While I always advise brides to do a little research to understand the styles they like and what brands they are drawn to, I usually give the same advice. First, try out one or two multi-brand salons with styles within your budget that carry designers you are interested in. This can give you an opportunity to try multiple silhouettes and see options from a variety of designers’ perspectives. Then, choose a few individual brand salons and showrooms to visit.
It’s important to take into consideration that many stores require an appointment fee—and that those can book out months in advance. Also, some stores might offer trunk shows showcasing larger, new collections from certain designers seasonally. So you should invest your time and money strategically into the shops that align with your style and will have the selection you are interested in. However, don’t be afraid to try something different—often, brides fall in love with a wedding dress they never expected.
Ready to start your wedding shopping journey? Ahead—and in no particular order—see our selection of the best bridal shops in NYC.
Multi-Brand Bridal Shops
Mark Ingram Bridal Atelier is often the first stop we would recommend for a bride seeking a wedding gown. You can start your journey trying a wide range of silhouettes from legacy luxury designers and up-and-coming fashion-forward brands. Store owner Mark Ingram is a designer himself, and carries his own brand with classic-leaning silhouettes in store. Those looking for a party dress can also find shorter silhouettes in stock at the Midtown bridal salon.
Designers in Stock: Mira Zwillinger, Dana Harel, Elie Saab, Elly Sofocli, Naeem Khan, Lela Rose, Ines Di Santo, Marmar Halim, Valentini, Mark Ingram Bride, and Vera Wang Haute.
Dress Price Starting Point: $7,500
Appointment Fee: Complimentary for a standard appointment. Room services are available starting at $450, which include additional guests, extended appointment times, and beverage service.
Alteration Services: In-house alterations available.
For brides with a slightly more bohemian sensibility, give Spina Bride a try. The luxury bridal salon carries chic designers with a dreamier flair that aren’t often found in most major cities. With a mix of avant-garde and more traditional finds, we often advise this Chelsea salon as a must-stop location when taking a trip to NYC for dress shopping. The multi-boutique salon has also recently partnered with bridal brand Lihi Hod, which opened its flagship on the ground floor. Appointments must be made separately to explore the brand’s selection.
Designers in Stock: Lihi Hod, Floure Studio, J. Andreatta, Yellow by Sahar, Ferrah, Francesca Miranda, Santos Costura, Costarellos, A La Robe, and Chana Marelus.
Dress Price Starting Point: $7,000
Appointment Fee: $100 fee, which is applied to a purchase.
Alteration Services: Spina recommends two independent tailors for off-site alterations.
