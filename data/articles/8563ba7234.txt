The first time I watched The Hunger Games, I was 20 years old and spending my junior semester abroad in St. Petersburg, taking in the 2012 film with subtitles and extremely poor dubbing. Safe to say, a lot has changed over the last 14 years, and now that The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping is officially in theaters, it seems like an opportune time to revisit Jennifer Lawrence, Josh Hutcherson, and Liam Hemsworth in the epic that started it all.
