Here is a roundup of headlines from India from Wednesday, November 18:
William and Kate honor Earthshot winners in Mumbai
The Princess of Wales made a rare speaking appearance at the Earthshot Prize awards in Mumbai on Tuesday. "Their work will inspire and create seismic change that will improve lives for generations to come," Catherine said of the winners.
Prince William, who founded the prize, said in his keynote speech that history is shaped not only by challenges but also by the people who rise to meet them. The prize is now at the midpoint of its planned 10 years.
Each winner received £1 million ($1.3 million, 1.2 million euros). They included Mahindra Last Mile Mobility, the maker of the electric rickshaw William drove earlier with Catherine as a passenger.
Catherine presented the Protect and Restore Nature award to Bhutan, which has written nature protection into its constitution and keeps nearly 70% of its land under forest.
Actor Rami Malek and cricketer Sachin Tendulkar were among the presenters. The royal couple wore outfits by Indian designers: a Mahima Mahajan "anarkali" for Kate and a Manish Malhotra "bandhgala" jacket for William.
Rahul Gandhi warns of economic crisis during talk in New York
Congress leader Rahul Gandhi, during a conversation at Columbia University in New York City on Tuesday, said that India faces a looming economic crisis.
Answering a question about India's youth, the opposition leader said: "The blunt thing is that India is sitting on an economic disaster. That's the reality. One can brush it aside, the media can brush it aside, but India's got a massive unemployment problem. India's got a massive problem with its education system."
Prime Minister Narendra Modi's ruling Bharatiya Janata Party responded sharply, calling him the "leader of anti-India propaganda."
Gandhi also reiterated his vote theft charge.
"140 million people who would have voted in 2024 have been removed from the electoral roll. We ask the Election Commission: how can an election be considered valid under these circumstances?" he was reported in Indian media as saying.
The Election Commission has rejected allegations of wrongdoing over the voter-roll revision.
In response, BJP spokesperson Pradeep Bhandari took to social media to say: "His inherent hate for India's progress and Indian democracy makes him speak ill about India every time he goes abroad!"
Gandhi, on a two-day US visit, is also due to visit the University of Chicago on Wednesday.
