With the vast majority of French high schools reopening despite calls for new blockades, and the number of people attending weekend demonstrations down for the third week in a row, are the protests in France beginning to die out? We spoke to one political expert who says he believes they will, saying it is difficult to see who people are protesting against, given that the government has no majority in parliament.
Renaud Foucart is senior lecturer in the Department of Economics at Lancaster University. He notes, however, that the sheer number of people taking to the streets over the past month shows how disappointing the political debate in France has become. He spoke to us in Perspective.
