The New York Knicks defeated the Indiana Pacers last night for their fifth consecutive win (Knicks in five!) and there to see it all were the orange-and-blue’s biggest celebrity cheerleaders.
Celebrity row at Madison Square Garden saw regulars like Timothée Chalamet and Ben Stiller, as well as rarely seen faces like Callum Turner (aka Mr. Dua Lipa), Obsession star Inde Navarrette, tennis pro Ben Shelton, and our girl Katie Holmes.
Others in attendance included Sombr, Rachel Zegler, Ziwe, Sean Evans, Kevin Bacon, Luke Wilson, Mark Ronson, Nick Kroll, Steve Schirripa, and comedians from the cast of Saturday Night Live.
“The stars are shining brighter than ever 🤩” the Knicks captioned an Instagram post featuring photos of their celebrity fans.
Chalamet was not accompanied by girlfriend Kylie Jenner this time around, but he sure stole the spotlight in his bubblegum-pink puffer jacket from Goldwin and Kith & ’47 Knicks snapback. As the game wrapped, the actor was also seen holding another piece of team merch: a black hat from Sportiqe (the sports merch brand that dropped the popular Billy Joel x Knicks collection centered around this spring’s playoffs).
Navarrette skipped the merch and went for a sharp gray coat and loose jeans. Turner went for a cool black leather-jacket look. Shelton opted for all-black layers and chain necklaces. Ziwe reached for double denim. Zegler was casual in an Adidas zip-up. And the SNL cast did a little bit of everything.
When in doubt, look no further than the Knicks’ star-studded celebrity row for your game-day look inspiration.
