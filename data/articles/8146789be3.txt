This year's lineup of LEGO Advent Calendars have started going out of stock ahead of Black Friday. Unsurprisingly, the Star Wars-themed calendar is one of the first to have completely disappeared from major retailers. One of the only places you can still buy the set is at Target, and somehow it's still being offered at a nice discount.
If you've been hoping to pick up the 2026 version of this calendar, this is probably your last chance to do so at a discount. Here's a quick rundown of where you can still find this set in stock online.
LEGO Star Wars Advent Calendar Stock Update
- Barnes & Noble - $44.99
- Macy's - $44.99
- Walmart - $52 (3rd-party seller)
- Amazon - Out of Stock
- LEGO Store - Out of Stock
- Best Buy - Out of Stock
Target isn't the only retailer that still has stock available, but it is by far the best deal you'll find online right now. Both Barnes & Noble and Macy's are great places to check for stock after a LEGO set disappears from all other shelves, but it's pretty rare to find discounts on such sets there. Once Target officially sells out of its own inventory, those are the next best places to shop if you want to avoid paying a mark-up.
This year's LEGO Star Wars Advent Calendar is themed after The Mandalorian and Grogu movie, featuring minifigures of The Mandalorian, Grogu, Moff Gideon, Carson Teva, and a Jawa. Most of these characters are also dressed in festive sweaters. You'll also get to build miniature versions of Mando's N-1 Starfighter, The Razor Crest, a Sandcrawler, and more. The set comes with a total of 293 pieces, which is actually 30 more than what we got with the 2025 edition.
When Do LEGO Black Friday Sales Start?
Although most of the LEGO advent calendars will likely sell out before Black Friday actually arrives, we're still expecting better deals on other sets closer to Thanksgiving. Amazon has officially announced that its Black Friday sale will kick off on November 19, which is when most of the really good LEGO discounts will start to arrive. You can also expect the official LEGO Store Black Friday sale to launch on November 27 alongside the new Godzilla set.
