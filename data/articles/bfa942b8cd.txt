Rockstar Games' UK workforce has voted firmly against unionization the week after the studio's latest game, Grand Theft Auto 6, demolished sales records.
The vote was 25% in favor of and 75% against a recognition agreement with the IWGB Game Workers Union. It's a decisive result, especially considering a 93.2% turnout for the vote, with 1,544 employees from Rockstar's offices in Edinburgh, Leeds, Lincoln, Dundee, and London participating.
The two-week ballot coincided not just with GTA 6's launch, but with an employment tribunal hearing over the firing of 31 Rockstar workers in 2025. Rockstar argued the workers had leaked company secrets, while the IWGB characterized the action as union-busting. A judgment in the case is pending.
The union can continue to represent individual members, and Rockstar staff remain free to organize over pay, overtime, and other issues, but Rockstar management will not be formally required to meet or negotiate with union reps.
Rockstar Games head of publishing Jennifer Kolbe said in a statement that the company was "extraordinarily proud of our team, the culture we've built together and the launch of GTA 6."
"This result reflects our team's strong support for continuing the direct relationships, collaboration and open dialogue that have helped make Rockstar Games so innovative and strong," Kolbe said. "As we look ahead, we're excited to build on all we've accomplished together."
In its own statement, the IWGB vowed to fight on. "For anyone at Rockstar who believes in a fairer workplace, a fairer sector and a fairer society - this isn’t a sign to retreat, it’s a sign to double down on what we stand for," the union said. "Hundreds of our coworkers voted in favour of our union at Rockstar, yet sadly we recognise we have more work to do to build understanding and win the trust of more of our colleagues. We’re ready to put in the effort to make that happen."
