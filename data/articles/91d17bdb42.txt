Fri, 11 Sept, 7:50 pm, Final score, Rabbitohs 10, Knights 20
Jillaroos claim fourth-straight World Cup as Jaime Chapman scores hat-trick
In short:
The Jillaroos defeated New Zealand 34-10 to claim a fourth-consecutive Rugby League World Cup.
Winger Jaime Chapman scored a hat-trick as Australia all-but assured the victory with a dominant first-half performance.
New Zealand's Mackenzie Wiki scored the only points of the second period in sweltering Brisbane heat.
