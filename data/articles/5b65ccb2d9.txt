Grand Theft Auto 6 received eight nominations for The Game Awards 2026, including Game of the Year.
The highly anticipated game, launching tomorrow (November 19), was also nominated for Best Game Direction, Best Narrative, Best Art Direction, Best Score and Music, Best Audio Design, Best Performance (Manni L. Perez as Lucia Caminos), and Best Action/Adventure.
Remedy's Control Resonant and Capcom's Resident Evil Requiem each earned six nominations, including Game of the Year.
Nintendo's The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, IO Interactive's 007 First Light, and House House's Big Walk were each nominated in four categories. Zelda and First Light were also recognised for Game of the Year.
Nintendo led all publishers with 12 nominations, followed by Capcom with 11, and Annapurna Interactive and PlayStation with nine each.
This year, 112 games, individuals, and teams were nominated, selected by "a global jury of over 160 media publications and creator outlets."
The Game Awards 2026 will take place on Thursday, December 10 at the Peacock Theatre in Los Angeles and will be broadcast on 40 digital streaming platforms, including Prime Video, Disney+, Twitch, and YouTube.
At last year's ceremony, Sandfall Interactive's Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 won nine awards, including Game of the Year, after receiving 12 nominations.
