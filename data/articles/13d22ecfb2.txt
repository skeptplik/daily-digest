Top-to-toe color coordination is not always easy to master. And when you throw beauty into the mix? Even trickier—after all, not everyone is about to experiment with, say, a navy lip. But Jennifer Lopez delivered something of a masterclass in head-to-toe merlot over the weekend. The trick? Layering different textures, all in the same shade—and that applies to beauty, too.
Lopez wore a buttery leather trench with a matching silky minidress with a pussy-bow detail at the neck, plus lace tights and patent leather stilettos in the same fall-ready shade. For good measure, she added glossy burgundy nails, a dusting of claret eyeshadow, flushed cheeks, and even a touch of deep red lipliner.
While summer time for J Lo heralds pretty French manis and flush, creamy pinks, fall and winter is when she really hits her nail shade stride. Consider last year’s vampy Bordeaux red, for one. As Bachik previously told Vogue: “We want a little something something when we’re doing an event nail.”
“The nails are their own thing, but they’re definitely the key accessory,” he said. “It’s almost like part of the jewelry, or if she doesn’t have her handbag or whatever, the nails are that little extra something.”
Whether you’re up for color coordinating or not, rich, deep reds are a classic cold weather shade to add into your manicures and makeup routine. The moody hue lends itself perfectly to cozy nights and log fires, and pairs nicely with other seasonal colours like mocha brown, black, and even cream. If a singular shade’s not exciting enough, there are plenty of wintry nail designs, like candy stripes or a moody floral, that you can add into the mix.
In need of more inspo? A deep dive on the IG feed of Tom Bachik (the go-to nail artist for Jennifer Lopez, Selena Gomez, and more mani purveyors) will sort you out for the season; but expect to see sultry, seductive reds in deep wine and burgundy tones in regular rotation.
