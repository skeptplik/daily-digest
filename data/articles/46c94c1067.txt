In the version of this article initially published, there was an error in Fig. 3n, where the bottom-right panel was a duplicate of the panel to its left. In the Extended Data Fig. 3 caption, the “cluster 1” and “cluster 2” designations in the description of panel h were transposed. Finally, in Extended Data Fig. 10c, the label now reading “Tuber 29” was originally “Tuber 26”. The corrections have been made in the HTML and PDF versions of the article.
