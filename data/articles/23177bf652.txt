As Spain readies to head to the polls in snap general elections on 29 November, Prime Minister Pedro Sánchez is going head-to-head with his conservative rival, Alberto Núñez Feijóo, in a televised debate on Thursday.
Both men will be looking to consolidate a lead, with most opinion polls suggesting the government majority could swing to the right.
Joining Euronews' weekly evening show Europe Tonight, Antonio Roldán, a former member of parliament and professor at IE School of Politics, Economics and Global Affairs, discussed the upcoming elections with our presenter Mared Gwyn Jones.
Among the topics discussed were whether Sánchez's major risk in triggering this election, hoping to unite the left, will pay off, as well as what a coalition government between PP and Vox could mean for Spain.
