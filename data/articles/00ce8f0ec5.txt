The GTA 6 Kit Car locations are spread all over Leonida, making your search to repair Wyman's fourth car significantly more involved than the Transgressor, Riata, or even the Mamba GT. There are five whole cars to scan with the WAiNK It app, but it's well worth it, because the Kit Car is super fast and stylish.
It's worth noting that you'll need to complete the Chapter 3 mission, Meet the Heders, to acquire the cut-off tool (since one of the Kit Cars is in a locked shipping container). After the Kit Car, there's one additional vehicle in the Classic Car Collection, the Deviant, but it doesn't actually require a collectible hunt as Wyman will simply offer it to you, making this your last one.
At Willie's Service Center in south Vice City
The first Kit Car is inside the garage at Willie's Service Center in south Vice City, southeast of Salton, and northwest of Tequesta. Just north of the purchasable 7th Avenue Garage and west of Vice City Firearms,you'll spot a building on the left labelled as Willie's Service Center with a brown top half and blue lower half.
Enter the ground floor via the door around the left side by the stairs. You'll have to break in, which sounds an alarm, so be quick scanning the car inside.
In a garage on the north side of Casanova Island
The second one is on Casanova Island, the rightmost of the Rialto Islands that connect west Vice City with east Vice City. When you reach the island, driving west to east (coming from Vespucci) head along the northernmost road and look to your left about halfway across the island for a big white villa with a small gated garage next door—it's next to a big vacant lot. Push the gate open to find the kit car right in front of you.
In a shipping container at Wild Hog Point
The third one is in a shipping container on Wild Hog Point, directly to the southwest of Port Gellhorn, on the big peninsula that sticks out where the airfield is located. For this one, you'll need to have completed the Meet the Heders mission in Chapter 3 to acquire the cut-off tool to open it.
It's on the westernmost edge of the point, around the middle of the pools that look a bit like a seahorse on the map. Due to the end of Chapter 2, similar to Grassrivers, this area is also a hot spot for police, so be careful while driving around. Look for the yellow scoop container, crack it open, and scan the car inside.
At Donnie's Auto Repair in Watson Bay
The fourth one is around the back of Donnie's Auto Repair Watson Bay in Watson Bay town, west of Hamlet, southwest of Port Gellhorn. This garage (which has Donnie's Auto Repair written on it) is next door to the Pay 'n' Spray - Watson Bay. Just head around the left side of the building and you'll find the Kit Car to scan immediately on your right.
In a garage in Capri
The fifth Kit Car is in a fancy garage on the west side of Capri on the little curving northern peninsula of houses. Capri is south of Port Gellhorn on the coast. Drive along the road until you spot the building site on your right and the green villa on your left—just after it, you'll find the driveway of the house you want, also on the left. Follow the path and break into the garage on your right to find the car.
Collecting the Kit Car
Same as always, wait for Wyman to finish the car and call, then click the payment request he sends in his following text to Buck him $1,500 before collecting the car from the nav point at his scrapyard.
This is actually the final car hunt, as later Wyman will call you and straight-up ask if you want the final car, a Deviant. You then pay him $1,000 for labour, and it's immediately yours to collect, so with this, you've basically finished the Classic Car Collection.
