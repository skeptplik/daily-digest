In his World view, Marc Schmitt argues that if universities teach students to argue with artificial-intelligence systems and assess their debating skills, and if developers offer learning modes, then students will use such tools as a sparring partner rather than as an oracle (see Nature657, 575; 2026). Concerns about loss of critical-thinking skills are, he says, “aimed at the wrong object”: as with the calculator, students’ abilities will “come out sharper”.
