Japan targets viral ‘Shorinji racing’ social media pranks harassing police
Authorities sound the alarm as online dares prompt primary and middle school pupils to vandalise police boxes and disrupt emergency lines
Known as “Shorinji racing”, the trend involves minors shouting “Shorinji-ja” (“We are Shorinji”) at police officers in person or through false emergency calls from public telephones while peers record them, according to Jiji Press.
Some have kicked doors and torn down posters at local police boxes, or koban. The perpetrators then flee or hang up before uploading the footage to social media.
