Director Luca Guadagnino has confirmed his American Psycho remake is officially dead.
The Challengers and Call Me by Your Name movie maker delivered the disappointing news when asked for an update on Deadline's Crew Call podcast. He admitted the script for the project that he read, which was penned by the writer behind The Bourne Ultimatum and Side Effects, was "fantastic" but not something he can continue working on.
"American Psycho, which is a fantastic script from Scott Z. Burns, unfortunately, is not happening for me, as we speak," Guadagnino said.
Guadagnino has more than proven himself as a name capable of handling the weight of a property on the level of American Psycho, which is exactly why movie fans were so excited to hear he could be attached to the project in 2024. Although fans have referred to what would have become his slice into the well-established movie universe as a 'remake,' at the time of its announcement, Lionsgate teased that the project would have been more of an adaptation of Bret Easton Ellis' American Psycho novel, rather than a remake of Christian Bale's 2000 movie.
Updates on Guadagnino's American Psycho have been few and far between in the years since its announcement, though. Elvis star Austin Butler was once in the running for the role of Patrick Bateman, as was Euphoria's Jacob Elordi. It was reported in October 2025 that the film would not gender-swap its lead role.
Bale eventually went on to comment on Guadagnino's reimagining and another actor bringing his iconic character to the big screen. He said "all the best to 'em" just earlier this year, adding, "I like brave people."
The director didn't explain why his version of American Psycho won't be moving forward, just that it's "not happening for me." While that means any version of American Psycho won't be coming to movies anytime soon, it's unclear if Lionsgate has completely closed the door on Patrick Bateman. With Burns' script finished, there's always the chance a different filmmaker could take the reins.
For now, though, it's best to assume the American Psycho remake/adaptation is well and truly dead. In the meantime, Guadagnino is gearing up for the release of his Sam Altman biopic, Artificial. The film is gearing up for a limited premiere Christmas 2026 before getting a wider release January 8, 2027.
