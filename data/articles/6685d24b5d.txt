Burgundy is having a moment (as evidenced in our ballet flat and handbag trend reports), but now it’s footwear’s time to shine. On the runways, deep, wine-colored boots walked the Hermès and Lanvin fall 2026 shows, and appeared off-duty in Fashion Week street style looks captured in London, Paris, and Milan.

High or low, scrunched or structured, oxblood-colored boots look great styled a multitude of ways: pair the knee-high version with a long leather coat for a Carmen Sandiego–inspired look, or wear with jeans tucked into the shaft for an equestrian-minded moment.
The shade’s strong presence on the runways and in street style serves as a testament to burgundy’s staying power, just in time for the colder seasons. From deep oxblood tones to bordeaux and brighter cherry reds, it’s a hue that’s essentially a neutral, serving as an alternate stand-in for black, with a subtle pop of color.
