Japan’s latest gross domestic product statistics have raised concerns among some analysts that the slump might be worse than it at first seems and not just about temporary factors like bad weather.
Government data released earlier this week show the Japanese economy shrank an annualized 0.1% in the July-September quarter — its first contraction in a year — as private consumption fell 0.3% from the previous quarter.
A large earthquake in Kumamoto Prefecture, heavy rains and a pullback of demand for air conditioners in the previous quarter were mentioned in discussions about the decline.
