Trade association predicts over 1M EV sales next year
The German Motor Trade Association (ZDK) predicts that more than 1 million electric vehicles will be sold in Germany in 2027.
The prediction was based on its estimate of 840,000 sales in 2026.
From January to October 2026, the Federal Motor Transport Authority recorded around 698,000 new all-electric cars.
The forecast also included a total of around 3.05 million passenger cars sold, up by 2.5%.
ZDK President Thomas Peckruhn said the electric car market would gain "further momentum" in 2027. "The range of models is growing, and government subsidies for electric cars can provide additional incentives to buy."
Job market unlikely to recover until summer, employment agency warns
Andrea Nahles, the head of the Federal Employment Agency (BfA), has warned that it could take months to see any noticeable recovery in the German job market.
She told the Funke media group that there would be little change between spring and early summer. Even if there's continued economic growth, its impact on the job market would be delayed.
"We've reached a tipping point," Nahles said. "For the first time, we're not seeing any growth in the number of employees subject to social insurance contributions, which currently stands at around 34 million."
She predicted that "demographics and the resulting worsening shortage of skilled workers will be the biggest problem facing the German labor market."
At the same time, there is no lack of unskilled workers. "There are currently 13 unemployed people for every open position for unskilled workers," she added.
At the same time, there are two jobs for every skilled worker.
The employment agency chief said it was necessary to attract more skilled immigrants.
