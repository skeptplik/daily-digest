JAKARTA – Indonesian President Prabowo Subianto met with Chinese President Xi Jinping on Friday to discuss the economy and investment partnerships as well as developments in cooperation in the South China Sea, the Indonesian government has said.
Both countries saw prospects for partnership in the strategic waterway of the South China Sea, including progress in discussions on areas for joint development, Prabowo’s office said on Friday.
“Our relationship, we think, keeps growing stronger,” Prabowo told Xi in Shenzhen on the sidelines of the APEC summit. “We have reached a more aligned understanding.”
