The LEGO Store Black Friday sale has officially kicked off, which means Amazon is dropping some of its best LEGO deals today too. Of the new discounts, The LEGO Beauty and the Beast Castle is the most likely to sell out quickly. Unlike many of the other LEGO Disney sets we've seen over the years, this set is very specifically targeted more at adults than kids. The massive LEGO Beauty and the Beast Castle is a whopping 2,916 pieces of pure Disney magic filled with little scenes from the classic film. The LEGO Store has already sold out of this set and it's currently up for retirement at the end of the year.
This isn't the first adult LEGO set from Disney, but it certainly is a break from the norm when it comes to LEGO castles. Usually these types of sets come with a lot fewer pieces and more play-friendly elements, but the Beauty and the Beast Castle was meant to be displayed in all its glory. If you're an adult fan of the animated movie and LEGO, this set was meant for you.
The LEGO Beauty and the Beast Castle Deal
The LEGO Beauty and the Beast Castle has never been discounted at a major retailer before, so this is kind of a big deal. Amazon has dropped 30% off the price of the set, dropping it down under $200. It's still a pretty expensive set, but this is likely the best discount we're going to see on this build before it's officially retired forever. It's a great gift idea for Disney fans at this price.
The set itself is massive and filled with fun features you'll discover as you build. Most importantly, this set comes with a total of 10 minifigures from the classic Disney animated movie. The full list includes Maurice, Belle, Cogsworth, Chip and Mrs. Potts, Lumière, Fifi, the enchanted rose in a case, the Beast, Gaston, and LeFou. Priced at $279.99 for the set, it's definitely an expensive purchase, but one well worth your time if you love Beauty and the Beast.
We actually had the chance to build this set ourselves and our reviewer, Kevin Wong, loved putting it together. Here's what he had to say about the experience: "As a fan of LEGO and a fan of Disney (I proposed to my wife at Disneyland), I can say that the cottage and the new castle succeed both as LEGO builds and as homages to two Disney classics."
