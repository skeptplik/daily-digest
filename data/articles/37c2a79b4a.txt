Luxury car brand Ferrari brought its street-savvy fashion to New York City — with a little help from supermodel Adriana Lima.
Held at the Jack Shainman Gallery in TriBeCa, the brand’s spring 2027 ready-to-wear collection consisted mainly of gender-neutral, silhouetted looks, with a particularly familiar face appearing among the mix of models
Lima — best known for her time as a Victoria’s Secret angel — took to the catwalk in a slicked-back braid, dramatic smoky-eye makeup and a bold red lip.
Dressed in brown textured leopard print pants and a yellow tank top, Lima strutted down the catwalk donning heavy silver jewelry, including an oversize chainlink necklace and a bracketing cuff on each earlobe.
Crafted by makeup artist Lucy Bridge, Lima’s glam was reminiscent of a vintage high-fashion editorial, combining shape and color for maximum visual drama. Black eye shadow extended around Lima’s orbs in a smoky wing shape, nearly reaching the halfway point to her hairline. The “Black Swan”-esque eye look was bracketed by Lima’s thin arched brows. Traveling south on her face, a glossy ruby shade coated her lips while strategically placed blush and contour brought out the definition in Lima’s cheekbones.
For the hair care needs of their various models, Ferrari tapped hairstylist Guido Palau, who works primarily with major fashion houses and editorial magazines. Palau opted to slick back Lima’s long, near-black hair into a thin cord-like braid. Emphasizing her bone structure, the hair swung behind her back as Lima completed her walk.
Although Lima announced she was retiring at the 2018 Victoria’s Secret Show, the Brazilian model made her return to the pink glitter stage in 2024, continuing to walk for the 2025 and 2026 shows. Her recent appearance at the 2026 show in October marked her 21st runway for the brand, making Lima their longest-running model.
