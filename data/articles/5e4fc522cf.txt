Paris – Tens of thousands of people marched in cities in France on Saturday, on the sixth weekend in a row of protests to demand better public services and express anger over the rising cost of living and inequality.
The protest movement, sparked in mid-September by high school students demanding better learning conditions, fueled a nationwide campaign against soaring prices, inadequate public services and police brutality that spread across Europe, even as turnout in France has dwindled.
Across the country, organizers reported 150,000 protesters, compared to 200,000 last week and 800,000 on the first one held October 17.
