As we hit peak fall, Footwearnews has seen a slew of celebrities, from actors to athletes, adopt a myriad of footwear styles.
No shoe is more popular this season than the suede boot, a staple silhouette that just so happened to appear on the famous feet of Michelle Pfeiffer while the actress was at a Q&A and screening of her show “Margo’s Got Money Troubles.” The event took place in West Hollywood on Nov. 20. Pfeiffer’s suede style was a chestnut hue that featured pointed toe boxes that transitioned into those classic textural suede uppers. Black block heels rounded out the set. Suede is a unique textural fabric that we often see utilized a ton in shoe designs.
The fabric is moderately warm and works well for keeping the chill away on moderately cold days, like the climate in the fall. Suede is a type of leather, made from the underside of an animal’s hide. The fabric is soft to the touch, stretchy, and thin, working well for footwear designs like boots or sneakers. While shoes have to be our favorite application for suede, the material is also used to make jackets and accessories like hats or belts. Similar styles to Pfeiffer’s own can be found from brands like Manolo Blahnik, Tacovas, Ralph Lauren, Jimmy Choo and more.

Along with her fall-ready boots, the “Scarface” actress wore a certified fall classic look surrounding a gray blazer in an oversized style. The oversized plaid outerwear was worn over a cream-colored blouse with lace trim and boot-cut dark-wash denim. The blouse featured a ruffled accent that imparted a boho-inspired touch. The actress’s look was cool and casual, featuring closet staples like blazers and denim that make this look easy to duplicate. For fans of Pfeiffer’s style, this is pretty great news. The layered look is a great way to get the most mileage out of their staple pieces.
“Margo’s Got Money Troubles” is a television show available to watch via Apple TV, created by David E. Kelley. The show is currently in its first season and features an all-star cast, including Pfeiffer, Elle Fanning, Nicole Kidman, Nick Offerman and Greg Kinnear, among others. The new show was adapted from a popular 2024 comedic novel by Rufi Thorpe and is a comedy-meets-drama.
