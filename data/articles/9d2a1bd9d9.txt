How did Anthropic's stock market debut unfold?
Anthropic, the company behind AI chatbot Claude, closed 17% higher on its first day as a publicly traded company. Shares closed at $97.17, giving the company a market valuation of around $1.77 trillion, after briefly surpassing $2 trillion during trading.
The debut came as the wider technology sector fell 2.4%, raising questions about investor confidence in the AI boom.
