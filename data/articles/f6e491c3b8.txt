Puff, puff, didn’t pass.
It was a night of relieved exhaling for weed consumers and industry workers across Massachusetts, as voters decisively shot down Question 8, a ballot initiative that would have dismantled the state’s recreational cannabis industry.
The rest of the country was engulfed in a blue wave, but for those who cared about this race, it was more of a green one: Around 76% of voters cast their ballot against the repeal, while about 24% voted in favor, as of Wednesday morning, with close to 90% of votes counted.
