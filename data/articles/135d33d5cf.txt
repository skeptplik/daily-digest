A Sudanese Army drone strike killed 15 people in a West Kordofan village including four children playing football, a survivor told AFP Monday (November 16, 2026).
The double-tap strike on Sunday (November 15, 2026) on the village of Abu Qalb also "injured others and burned a number of homes", reported local rights monitor the Kordofan Observatory, who gave the same toll.
The attack came as the Sudanese Army leads a counter-offensive through West Kordofan State to recapture territory from the paramilitary Rapid Support Forces (RSF), with whom it has been at war since April 2023.
"We lost 15 people. Two of the children had already lost their father earlier in the war, and nine of those killed were my own relatives," the survivor said, using a satellite internet connection to circumvent a communications blackout.
Requesting anonymity for his safety, he said the drone struck the village twice. The first hit a public square, where the children were playing football, while the second hit local homes, setting several on fire.
The war has killed more than 200,000 people, aid workers estimate, and has triggered the world's largest hunger and displacement crises.
The United Nations has repeatedly criticised both sides' rampant use of drones, which killed more than 1,100 people in the first half of 2026 alone.
Of the hundreds of child casualties this year, two-thirds were killed or injured by drone strikes, the UN said this week.
