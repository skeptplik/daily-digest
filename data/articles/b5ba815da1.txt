What goes around, comes back around and the spring 2027 season—with its historical fashion references—proved the point. Or, as designer Pierpaolo Piccioli once told Vogue, respecting what came before “is cooler, and more revolutionary than to deny what’s been before you arrive.”
Six months is a long time to wait around for some of the spring 2027 season’s hottest items to hit the stores, be it Miu Miu’s python leather bag or the candy colored button-ups from Jil Sander. If you’re like us and don’t subscribe to the old axiom that patience is a virtue and have a hankering for next season’s trends right now, vintage is your best friend. If you search hard enough, you might almost find a close enough vintage approximation, like the pleated flared skirts from Prada or the collar shirts from Polo Ralph Lauren. And if you’re lucky (or particularly dogged), you might snatch up these items at an incredible price point on one of the internet’s many vintage retailers, from eBay to Poshmark.
