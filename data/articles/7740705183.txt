A new survey of Grand Theft Auto 6 players has found some particularly interesting results. 30 percent came away from the game with the desire to buy someone special flowers. Nine percent punched cats.
Per Games User Research's director Steve Bromley, 303 players from across the UK and US were polled on their experiences. They had to have at least two hours of playtime to contribute, which led to an informed sample of how folks are finding the game.
Those polled gave the game an 8.4/10 (that's 4.2/5 Eurogamer readers), which is glowing praise. 42 percent loved the narrative, as well as Jason and Lucia's relationship. 38 dug the lifelike and believable world, while 35 percent were keen on the freedom to explore and the variety of activities.
As for what they didn't like, 28 percent weren't a fan of the slow pacing and long cutscenes, 19 percent didn't like the difficulty in stealing cars and losing a wanted level, while 18 percent were put off by the game's technical performance.
Now for some fun findings: 41 percent expressed an interest in improving their real life after playing the game, with 30 percent of those looking to buy a loved one some flowers. Nice! 14 percent were at least once confused or stuck, due to confusion with the stealth, identifying time-sensitive missions, or experiencing control issues.
There's some bad news for the cats out there, too. Nine percent accidentally punched a cat while trying to pet it, while 61 percent had the same experience with human NPCs. At least it wasn't intentional, that has to count for something. Right?
