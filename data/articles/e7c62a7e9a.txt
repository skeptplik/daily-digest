Jason Horne-Francis prepared to recruit Nasiah Wanganeen-Milera during AFL Origin camp
In short:
Jason Horne-Francis says he is prepared to recruit Nasiah Wanganeen-Milera to Port Adelaide during next year's State of Origin camp.
Horne-Francis and the off-contract Wanganeen-Milera were named as two of South Australia's first five players, joining Callum Wilkie, Jordan Dawson and Lachie Neale.
Western Australia's first five players revealed were Patrick Cripps, Charlie Cameron, Kysaiah Pickett, Luke Jackson and Tom Barrass.
