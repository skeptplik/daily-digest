François Picard is pleased to welcome Phil Clark, professor of international politics at SOAS University of London. The diplomatic crisis between Rwanda and the Democratic Republic of Congo spilling out at the Francophonie summit in Phnom Penh for all to see has exposed an unexpected shift in their relations. Clark says that "nobody expected Rwanda to back down in this way" following President Macron's request that Paul Kagame withdraw Louise Mushikiwabo's candidacy for a third term.
Clark argues that Congo is now "winning the diplomatic battle", pointing to growing international pressure on Rwanda over its backing for M23 in eastern Congo. He also questions the effectiveness of the Washington and Doha peace processes, arguing that neither has made substantial progress and that future mediation is likely to be led by the African Union. The dispute comes as Clark sees organisations such as La Francophonie "waning in their global influence".
