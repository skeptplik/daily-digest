Cricket Australia chief Todd Greenberg says there be no new Sandpapergate investigation
In short:
Cricket Australia chief Todd Greenberg says there will not be another investigation into the 2018 Sandpapergate scandal.
Usman Khawaja has claimed in his book, Sabr, which is released today, that the entire team knew about the ball-tampering.
In his book, Khawaja says the team started using sandpaper on the ball in the Test prior to the match in Cape Town, where Bancroft was caught on camera tampering with the ball.
