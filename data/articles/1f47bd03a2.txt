Tobu Department Store launched a facial recognition payment service at a Tokyo store on Thursday jointly with its parent company, Tobu Railway.
Customers can register their facial data and credit card information in advance, allowing them to make purchases without a wallet or smartphone.
The Tobu store became the first department store in Japan to introduce a facial recognition payment service, according to the companies. The service uses Sakulala, a biometric authentication system jointly promoted by Tobu Railway and Hitachi.
Most customers continued to use conventional payment methods on Thursday rather than the unfamiliar new service on its first day.
A man in his 50s shopping at the store said, “It seems very convenient, but I’m a little concerned about whether the payment will go through properly.”
