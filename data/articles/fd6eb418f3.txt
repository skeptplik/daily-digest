Satisfactory publisher Coffee Stain Group has agreed to acquire the IP rights and related assets for Chronicles: Medieval from the bankruptcy estate of Raw Power Games.
According to a regulatory filing published on November 14, a newly established company will hold the assets, with 75% owned by Coffee Stain and 25% by "a small group of key members of the game's original development team."
Coffee Stain is paying around $5.5 million (DKK 36.75 million) in cash, excluding transaction costs, and will also fund the game's continued development. The deal is expected to close in the coming weeks, once the purchase price has been paid to the bankruptcy estate.
Copenhagen-based Raw Power Games was declared bankrupt on October 30. Around the same time, the studio told fans that "the necessary funding for Raw Power Games is no longer in place," and that it was at risk of closing before the game's planned early access launch.
"Chronicles: Medieval made a strong impression at its reveal at Summer Game Fest last year. We're happy to step in and help bring it to the players after the recent developments in Copenhagen. Chronicles: Medieval ticks many of the boxes that we believe to be fundamental for a great game, and we think it has a good chance of becoming a strong long-term IP for the group," said Anton Westbergh, CEO of Coffee Stain.
"Things have been moving fast around this deal, and our short-term focus will be on working out how best to complete the game, together with the new company's management team."
Chronicles: Medieval is a sandbox game about building and commanding armies, and has been in development since 2022. Coffee Stain said it ranks among the top 30 most wishlisted games on Steam.
