A suspected drone attack caused an explosion aboard a Russian ship docked at the Benghazi naval base in eastern Libya, the country’s eastern-based government said on Sunday after a preliminary investigation.
No one has claimed responsibility for the suspected attack, and there was no immediate reaction from Russian authorities.
Since Russia’s full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022, Ukrainian forces have repeatedly used naval drones and missiles to target Russian warships and naval facilities, mainly in the Black Sea.
