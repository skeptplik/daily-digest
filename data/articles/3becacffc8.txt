Nuclear arms at the center of the Shenzhen trilateral meeting
Issued on: Modified:
Share
Nuclear weapons are at the top of Donald Trump’s agenda at the trilateral summit in Beijing. The US president wants the United States, Russia and China to reduce their nuclear stockpiles, and FRANCE 24’s Philip Turle expects him to push hard for a three-way agreement on the issue. Iran is also a key part of the discussion. The three leaders share an interest in preventing the Iranian regime from acquiring nuclear weapons. Regional conflicts and oil prices are also se to be central issues.
