The actor behind one of the best GTA characters of all time has started working his way through GTA 6. Manny Perez, who plays Raul Bautista, bought a PS5 just so he could play.
“Guy, I’m playing and this s**t is crazy!” he said in a video posted to social media. “Look how real that looks. I’m learning, I’m loving this, it’s crazy.”
According to Perez, the biggest issue as a non-gamer has been getting used to the DualSense controller, which he called “the stick,” but he’s “getting there.”
The actor sent a follow-up tweet after a fan shared the video. “Will take a while but I will finish this game,” he said. “Thank you all for the support. I’m loving the comments and appreciations.”
Warning! Spoilers for GTA 6 follow.
Raul Batista is a big character in GTA 6 from Chapter 3 onwards, and one of the highlights of the game in terms of performance. Batista steals the show in some really cool scenes, which I won’t spoil, but I can see him becoming a fan favorite as people work their way through the story.

Perez isn't the only GTA 6 actor who's started to talk about their work on the game and fan reaction to their character. For example, Lexie Shoaibi has spoken out about her "bittersweet" newfound fame after her character, Mandy, went viral online. And Issa Lopez, who plays a radio host on GTA 6’s fictional Cocoteo FM radio station, described the wild non-disclosure agreement she had to sign before working on the game, calling the project “crazy secretive.”
