In writing this guide on gift ideas for friends, I learned that there really is no catchall gift that will please everyone. Choosing these gifts, I thought back to some of the best self-care products and tech I’ve tested recently. I included the gadgets I reach for regularly, as well as those I’ve bought for family and friends after testing and loving them. So please use this guide as a starting point, keeping your friend’s personality, needs, and hobbies in mind as you shop for the right gifts.
After testing hundreds of products during my years on the WIRED Reviews team, these are the items I’d recommend to the friends on my own list. I’ve included self-care gifts like massagers, cozy gifts like an ultrasoft throw blanket, kitchen staples like a chef’s knife, and everything in between. There are many gifts at different price points, and they’re also categorized by gift type below. Be sure to check back often, as I will be updating this guide with versatile new picks as we get closer to gifting season.

Best Tech Gifts
It’s my belief that everyone could use a small, portable Bluetooth speaker. Whether you want to sing along to your tunes in the shower or get the party going around a campfire, having a compact, Bluetooth-enabled speaker at the ready makes you everyone’s favorite party guest. At less than $60, I promise this li’l guy will be a hit as a gift (without breaking the bank). Plus, you can fully customize the design with images, text, and stickers if you want to make it more personalized for the giftee.
If your friend wants a great home speaker for sound (not voice assistant tasks), the Sonos Era 100 SL is an excellent, microphone-free option. It is a huge step up in terms of sound quality—it can easily fill any smaller room (think apartment living) with rich, detailed instrumentation. And if they decide they love it, they can easily pair it with other Sonos speakers. —Harry Rabinowitz
Best Self-Care Gifts
This nifty gadget from wellness tech company Chirp is a godsend for anyone who’s always on their feet. It has two sides: One features a textured surface that targets pressure points, and the other has a heated, centralized oval that acts like a hot stone for targeted arch relief. The end has a USB-C charging port and buttons to adjust heat, vibration, and preset recovery programs, which use different heat and massage patterns for relief. I use this gadget almost daily, especially if I’ve been on my feet a lot that day. This is the perfect gift for someone who’s always asking for a foot massage.
This surprisingly affordable skin care box set is the perfect gift for a friend who loves facial products or could use a little self-care. It includes a hydrating serum, energizing gold eye masks, a face mask, rose spray, face balm, and lip mask. Its focus on hydration is especially ideal for the dry winter months.
Best Cozy Gifts
WIRED reviewer Boutayna Chokrane was sold on Hatch’s newest sunrise alarm clock, which also doubles as a sleep tracker. If your giftee doesn’t want to sleep with a wearable to get their sleep stats, this device uses motion-based tracking to provide passive sleep monitoring from their nightstand. It functions as a sunrise clock, a dimmable bedside light, and a sound machine, and it now captures data like sleep duration and stages, presented in interactive visuals. Boutayna loved the refreshing minimalist design, with no cameras or microphones watching you sleep. Best of all, your data is not locked behind a Hatch+ membership. If you have a friend who struggles with sleep or waking up, consider joining in the sunrise alarm clock trend and getting them this aesthetic bedside machine.
Best Kitchen Gifts
Everyone—I mean every single person—needs at least one great chef’s knife. This is probably the most important multipurpose kitchen tool—it can slice, dice, and chop ingredients with speed and precision. Your friend most likely could use a better knife for everyday cooking. Great chefs’ knives range from less than $100 to several hundred dollars, but I really like this mid-range, 8-inch knife from Material. It’s made from luxe Japanese high-carbon stainless steel for extra durability, with a matte, stain-resistant composite handle. Plus, if you buy from Material’s site, you can add an engraved message for a fun, more personal touch.
If your friend is a wine enthusiast, or you want to impress at the next dinner party, I love these easy-to-use, accessible wine opener sets. This set includes an electric opener and aerator, plus a carrying case. WineWhiz also has its flagship ready-to-wrap box set, which I tested and liked, that makes a great gift and includes a foil cutter and vacuum sealer. But I love the idea of bringing a great bottle of wine and a fancy new opener with a cute carrying case to your holiday party and gifting both.
