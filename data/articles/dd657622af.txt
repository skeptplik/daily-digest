You may have recently read that CD Projekt Red's in-development Cyberpunk 2 may be trying to revive the original game's online ambitions. But, if you don't want to wait the many years it'll likely be before the Cyberpunk 2077 sequel arrives, then I've got good news for you: Cyberpunk 2077 multiplayer mod CyberMP is now out in open beta.
"After almost six years of development, countless reworks, and code rewrites, we're finally launching the public beta," the modders on the CyberMP Discord channel wrote (thanks RPS). "Anyone can jump in and play now - no invites or applications needed." The team notes, however, that this is "true beta", meaning "Crashes, desyncs, and server drops under heavy load are completely normal for now. That's exactly why we need testers."
Alongside the potential shakiness, the devs say it's also possible the mod could ping your anti-virus software, since it "doesn't have a digital signature yet". Whilst the priorities for the team lie in "polishing the foundations" and "fixing crashes and stability", more features are eventually planned. But for now, they stress, "let's see how the project handles real player traffic!"
So, what exactly is it? CyberMP is a dedicated multiplayer modification for Cyberpunk 2077 that allows you and your friends to explore Night City together: drive vehicles, engage in combat, and play custom community-created game modes on high-tick dedicated servers. You need an official copy of the game to play it (obviously), and the mod is completely free to download. You can download the mod, and read a fair amount of how to get it working, what to expect from it, and how to troubleshoot any issues, over at its dedicated website.
If you're thinking 'Hang on, why does Cyberpunk 2077 multiplayer sound so familiar?', it's because the game was supposed to receive an official multiplayer mode after its original release. Thanks to its shaky launch, however, plans changed, and CD Projekt Red later announced that multiplayer would be a separate "triple-A release", but, eventually, that too was cancelled. The studio is still keen to honour the idea, though, and recently stated it's "focusing on bringing online into all of our franchises one day".
This isn't the first big bit of news we've had about Cyberpunk 2077 mods this week. Just yesterday, a new mod called 'Torrent' added 3D rain, lightning storms, and tyre spray to Night City - all rendered and made lovely with a new lighting system that seems to play nicely with other weather mods that have been created for the game.
