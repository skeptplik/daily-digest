Every year, the art, film, fashion, and entertainment industries head to the Los Angeles County Museum of Art (LACMA) for an evening of glamorous fundraising, celebration, and live music. The Art+Film Gala, as it’s called, has seen everyone from Kim Kardashian and Kendall Jenner to Charli XCX and Troye Sivan to Cindy Crawford and Kaia Gerber, welcoming models, actors, philanthropists, musicians, athletes, and more.
Since its inaugural edition back in 2011, the star-studded event has supported the museum’s efforts to expand its programming, making film more central to its exhibitions and education. With sponsorship from Gucci, it has placed a focus on the intersection of art and film, honoring painters, cinematographers, and directors alike for their global contributions. Tonight, it recognized the work of Vija Celmins, known for her photo-realistic paintings exploring the complexity of nature, and Dune filmmaker Denis Villeneuve. Blackpink’s Rosé was tapped for an intimate performance, and, for the 15th year, Leonardo DiCaprio and LACMA trustee Eva Chow were appointed co-chairs.
As for the guests, Chris Burden’s public Urban Light installation—a collection of oversize street lamps that doubled as a red carpet for the evening—saw the likes of Alex Consani, Nicole Kidman, Kylie Jenner, and Paris Hilton. The familiar faces were joined by Anya Taylor-Joy, Alix Earle, Andrew Garfield, Benito Skinner, Candice Swanepoel, Chloe Kim, Emily Ratajkowski, Jordyn Woods-Towns, Julia Garner, Lindsey Vonn, Monica Lewinsky, Odessa A’zion, Rosie Huntington-Whiteley, Salma Hayek, Viola Davis, and more.
