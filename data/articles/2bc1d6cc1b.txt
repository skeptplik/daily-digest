The midterms didn’t just bring a blue wave — it was a biomedical blue wave, with several doctors and scientists joining Congress. This moment presents a unique opportunity to remove a major American public health problem.
I’m talking about impeaching Health and Human Services Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. once the next Congress begins in January.
Making America and the world less healthy
Kennedy’s whole approach to public health is upside down, and it’s hurting the country. Perhaps most notably, he wrongly thinks there’s a connection between autism and vaccines, which are proven safe and effective. Meanwhile, he seems enamored with unproven stuff like sketchy autism treatments that might include stem cells.
