The last few years have been marked by the “Lip Balm Wars.” That is, every brand—from Hailey Bieber’s Rhode to prestige labels like Chanel and Dior—has launched more sheer, glossy, and hydrating formulas than classic lipstick bullets. But even though we all own more than 10 emotional-support lip balms in various shades, flavors, and finishes, there's still nothing more powerful than a statement-making lip. This is especially true for women over 40.
Last night, supermodel Christy Turlington stepped out at the Museum of Modern Art’s 2026 Annual Film Benefit in New York, wearing a knit dress from Matthieu Blazy’s fall 2026 Chanel collection. A dress this graphic and eye-catching needs makeup to balance it, and Turlington paired it with black eyeliner and a glossy ruby-red lipstick.
While we don’t know the exact shade she wore last night, it’s clearly a formula that sits somewhere between a satin lipstick and a gloss-balm, with pigment that doesn’t sacrifice moisture or shine. Turlington is a Lancôme ambassador, so it could be the brand’s Lip Idole Butterglow Hydrating Lip Balm or L’Absolu Rouge Cream Lipstick. Otherwise, Chanel makes a gorgeous option in both the Rouge Coco Flash and Rouge Allure Extrait formulas. These formulas subtly plump and moisturize dry lips while blurring lip lines and imperfections.
The best part of this look is that it proves you don’t need to match your lipstick exactly to your outfit, as long as they’re in the same color family. While the Chanel dress is more of an orange-red knit, Turlington’s lipstick is a more neutral ruby that harmonizes with the warm tones and shades in the rest of her look. Plus, raspberry or ruby-red lipstick can brighten your face in seconds, even with little to no other makeup.
Bold lipstick is a bit outside Turlington’s day-to-day comfort zone; she told Bazaar recently that she’s “definitely an old-school Carmex or ChapStick kind of person, because I’m constantly reapplying it to my dry lips, especially in the winter.” The rest of her routine is equally as effortless: “I wash my face in the morning and moisturize a lot. Sometimes I even double-moisturize because, especially at this time of year, I get dry skin. And then, I’ll add a nice little layer of foundation, some blush, and mascara, and that’s kind of it, I’m on my way.”
