Amazon’s Black Friday sale is officially live, making it a great time to pick up some serious bulk of whatever your drink of choice is. Mine happens to be AriZona Iced Tea, because who can hate cheap caffeine, and I’ll certainly never complain about a deal dropping those prices even further.
The 15oz Fallout edition teas only come in 12-can packs, priced at around $2/can. Don’t worry, AriZona still maintains its 99-cent MSRP for non-licensed drinks — founder Don Vultaggio makes sure of that. The Amazon discount only knocks off a couple of bucks, but it brings every Fallout can closer to that beautiful standard price.
Amazon Discounts Fallout AriZona Green Tea Packs for Black Friday
The 15% discount is available on 12-can cases of individual flavors as well as the bulk variety pack. All packs have the same base price, so the decision really comes down to your flavor preferences.
Your Fallout options consist of three Vault-Tec reimaginings of AriZona classics – Atomic Red Apple, Wastelander Sweet Peach, and Power Pomegranate – and Apocalyptic Orange, a new flavor unique to the collaboration.
The flavors are tied to specific characters from Amazon’s live-action series, depicted in playing card illustrations on the packaging of each case. Lucy gets apple and the Ghoul gets peach, while Dogmeat and pre-war Ghoul adorn the low-cal versions.
The collaboration also doubled up on Maximus drinks, with orange dedicated to the man and pomegranate dedicated to his Power Armor. I guess it could be someone else in the suit, but I leave that up to your interpretation.
Amazon’s Fallout TV show has brought a whole new audience to Bethesda’s post-apocalyptic world. The series aired its second season earlier this year, and a third season should be coming to Prime Video at some point in 2027.
Bethesda, on the other hand, has turned its attention back to the world of Elder Scrolls. The company announced Sentinel as the name of the next game, though it likely won’t arrive for several more years. Bethesda confirmed Fallout 5 won’t be coming until after the next Elder Scrolls, so it might be quite the wait until we get a new journey in the Wasteland.
