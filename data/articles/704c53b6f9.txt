Traffic was temporarily suspended over the Crimean Bridge after an attack, Sergei Aksyonov, Crimea's Russian-installed governor, said on Telegram on Thursday, adding that assessment of the damage was underway.
The attack is the third this week on the bridge that connects mainland Russia with Crimea, which Russia annexed from Ukraine in 2014, in a blow to President Vladimir Putin ahead of his meetings with U.S. and Chinese counterparts on Thursday.
The 19-kilometer (12-mile) bridge is a flagship project inaugurated by Putin in 2018 and a symbol of Russia's attachment to the peninsula.
Earlier this week, Ukrainian drones and missiles destroyed the central road section of the bridge, forcing its main road arch to crumble into the sea and leaving a yawning gap, according to videos and images verified by Reuters.
