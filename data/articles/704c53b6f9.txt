Traffic was temporarily suspended over the Crimean Bridge after an attack, Sergei Aksyonov, Crimea's Russian-installed governor, said on Telegram on Thursday, adding that assessment of the damage caused was underway.
The attack is third this week on the bridge which connects mainland Russia with Crimea which it annexed from Ukraine in 2014, in a major blow to President Vladimir Putin ahead of his meetings with U.S. and China counterparts on Thursday.
