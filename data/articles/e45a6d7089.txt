Crocs launched its first Animal Crossing-themed clog last year. The official Nintendo collaboration sold out in minutes, and only the teeny-tiny kid's edition has been popping in and out of stock ever since. Until now.
Right after launching its official Black Friday sale, Amazon has brought back a few select sizes of the adult Animal Crossing clog. The retailer has also knocked over $30 off the price of each shoe, bringing the Crocs well below their previous lowest price.
Animal Crossing Crocs Are Back and Cheaper Than Ever
The Animal Crossing clog captures plenty of the New Horizons charm in its design. The clog generally sticks to soft pastel tones, with ocean wave details along the platform foundation. A collage of Animal Crossing’s many trees coat the front of the shoe, while fluffy clouds and a little red balloon float on the ankle strap.
The base clog also comes with seven exclusive Jibbitz. Four of the Jibbitz depict characters: Isabelle, K.K. Slider, Tom Nook, and the joint tanuki Timmy and Tommy. Three more charms depict a classic villager house, an apple tree, and a good ol’ bag of Bells.
Additional villager Jibbitz are on sale directly through Crocs, which is also hosting its own Black Friday sale. For whatever reason, Crocs itself didn't pull off the full clog restock.
Amazon has also dropped the 'little kids' edition from $54.95 to $33, the same 40% discount as the adult size on an already cheaper shoe.
