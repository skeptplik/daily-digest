What the Democrats' 'blue tsunami' midterm victory means for the Republican party
Democrats won at least 7 Senate seats held by Republicans, taking control of both the House and Senate
The Democrats took control on Tuesday night, securing the majority in both the Senate and the House of Representatives during the U.S. midterm elections, according to projections by three American networks.
The Democrats were able to flip major Republican strongholds, including Texas, where Democrat James Talarico defeated Republican state attorney general Ken Paxton in the Senate race.
These results may reduce U.S. President Donald Trump's power, though the president has shown an ability to take action without Congress, through tools such as executive orders.
Washington correspondents Katie Simpson, Willy Lowry and Paul Hunter break down what this says about American voter sentiment, what the next two years of Trump's presidency could look like and how Canada-U.S. relations may change.
