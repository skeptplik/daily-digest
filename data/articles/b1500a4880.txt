FIFA unveiled its final world ranking of 2026. France remain third in the world, behind Spain and Argentina. Les Bleus have now finished in the top three for the ninth consecutive year. Morocco climb two places to fifth, equalling the best-ever ranking by an African nation, previously achieved by Nigeria in 1994. Brazil drop to seventh, while Senegal remain 18th and Ivory Coast sit 28th.
In the Women's Champions League, PSG lost 1-0 away to Chelsea after Veerle Buurman scored in the 87th minute. The Paris club, currently 10th, will play Juventus on 16 December, with a place in the play-offs at stake.
Laurent Bonadei has also named his 24-player squad for France's next two friendlies. Lyon forward Liana Joseph receives her first senior call-up, while Delphine Cascarino returns to the squad.
In tennis, at the ATP finals in Turin, Carlos Alcaraz beat Daniil Medvedev 6-2, 6-4 before securing his place in the semi-finals, thanks to Frances Tiafoe's victory over Flavio Cobolli (7-6, 6-0).
In the EuroLeague, Real Madrid lost 83-79 to Besiktas, despite Théo Maledon's 22 points.
Finally, Killian Hayes has signed for FC Barcelona until the end of the season.
