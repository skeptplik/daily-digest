Kith is learning the power of the dark side of the force with a collection focused fully on the villains of “Star Wars.”
Headlining the assortment is the “Star Wars” x Ronnie Fieg x Asics Gel-Lyte 3 “Castlerock.” Now featuring an official sign-off from the franchise, the sneaker is actually a reissue of the sneaker Fieg designed in 2008 as an informal homage to Darth Vader.
Black patent leather overlays bring a glossy effect similar to Vader’s suit, and sparse hits of red then rep his lightsaber and helmet lenses on the eyestays, lace striping and heel branding. Nubuck is then used for the shoe’s base and Tiger Stripes motif in a more washed out-shade of black, and the two insoles come with graphics splitting up the likeness of the Dark Lord of the Sith and a “Star Wars” patch on the left shoe. Each pair will also come in special packaging that looks like hardware from the galaxy far, far away, complete with text in the Aurebesh alphabet.

A larger capsule of apparel brings in more Sith and their acolytes through an Avirex leather jacket with embroidery of Vader, Darth Sideous and General Grievous and a leather moto jacket embossed to look like Vader’s helmet. Stormtrooper helmets are replicated on another leather jacket and a windbreaker, and more graphic iconography includes a Vader bomber jacket, longsleeve jersey and short-sleeve button-up.
There’s still yet more product with a range of collectables and accessories adding co-branded action figures, lenticular movie posters, keychains, mousepads, skateboards and more.
This latest assortment marks the third time Kith has partnered with “Star Wars,” with the first capsule coming in 2021 and a second tied to the 40th anniversary of “Return of the Jedi” in 2023. The brand is also no stranger to focusing on the villains with Asics‘ involvement, as four sneakers formed a Marvel villains collection in 2024.
The “Star Wars” x Ronnie Fieg x Asics Gel-Lyte 3 “Castlerock” and the rest of the collection will release will first release Friday at Kith London, which will be transformed with a dedicated experience, with a global launch following Wednesday through Kith’s other stores, website and app. Pricing for the various items has yet to be confirmed.
