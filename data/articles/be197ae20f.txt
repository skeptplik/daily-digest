Scientists are racing to restore people’s ability to communicate. Powerful AI-based methods to decode speech signals and predict words, along with advances in technology, promise success.
This episode of The Deep End: Talk to Me takes listeners through the breadth of scientific efforts to restore words, emotions and even gestures to people who can’t move or talk.
We’ll hear from Casey Harrell, a man with amyotrophic lateral sclerosis who can no longer talk. A user of today’s technology, Harrell has a unique perspective on what he’d like to see improved for the technology of tomorrow.
And as season two wraps up, listeners will be left with a reminder of the power of a voice: a piece of comedy from Jules Rodriguez, performed with his voice clone in front of a live crowd.
“Being backstage and then seeing him come out, like, roll out his wheelchair, and everybody goes nuts because they’re like, ‘What is going on here? This doesn’t make sense,’” says Jules’ wife, Maria Aleandra Fernandez. “And every time I was like, ‘This is life. This is love. This is passion. What else is there?’ I don’t know that there’s anything else more powerful than what this guy is doing here.”
