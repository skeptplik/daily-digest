Exiled Thai critic mocks authorities after textbook banned on national security grounds
The book, edited by Pavin Chachavalpongpun, contains chapters on Thailand’s monarchy and the government’s use of the lese-majeste law
The royal family holds revered status in Thai society and is shielded from criticism by a strict defamation law that critics say is used to muzzle dissent.
The book, a second edition of the Routledge Handbook of Contemporary Thailand published in English in August, was edited by Pavin Chachavalpongpun, a prominent academic and critic of the Thai monarchy who is based in Japan.
