Expanding your Nintendo Switch 2’s 256GB of built-in storage with a microSD card is pricier than it was with Nintendo’s original console. That’s because microSD Express cards, while physically identical to ones you might have lying around, have faster read and write speeds. Thankfully, Samsung’s 256GB model has dropped to $44.99 at Amazon ahead of Black Friday, which is a few bucks off its usual price but still well below its $79.99 MSRP. Sandisk’s 256GB card, meanwhile, is available for $49.99 ($10 off Amazon’s typical list price) from Amazon and Sandisk, if you prefer one brand over the other for some reason.
Multiplying your Switch 2’s storage costs less than usual right now
SSD costs are still sky-high, but at least there are decent deals on microSD Express cards.
For those who don’t want to think about upgrading their Switch 2’s storage again, both brands’ 512GB microSD Express cards are selling for a fair $99.99 on Amazon. That gives you double the storage for roughly twice the price of a 256GB card, as it should be.
I suspect the 256GB option will be enough for most Switch 2 players, unless you buy a lot of games. The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake is 24.9 gigabytes, for example. Other Nintendo-developed games tend to be similar in install size, though it’s all over the place for third-party game makers; Resident Evil Requiem (which also recently dropped to $39.99 on Amazon) is relatively small at 28.7 gigs, while The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Remastered is about 50. Some games take up even more storage.
