Spanish workers stage huge general strike, first in 14 years, angry over housing
The nation needs at least 750,000 more homes but fewer people can afford to buy one, meaning more pressure on rentals
Less than three weeks before a snap national election that could catapult the far-right to power, millions of Spain’s workers were striking on Wednesday to demand that the government remedy the nation’s housing crisis.
They set up picket lines despite stormy weather in a mass protest against housing affordability.
The general strike – Spain’s first in 14 years – was causing delays in the public transport system and slowing down factories across the euro zone’s fourth-largest economy.
