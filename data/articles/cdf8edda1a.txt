On Saturday night, Camila Alves attended the Baby2Baby gala at the Pacific Design Center in Los Angeles. The Brazilian-American model was shimmering for the occasion, sporting a black sequin column gown from the Stella McCartney Resort 2027 collection.
Alves’s dress was appropriate for the high-profile annual gala, easily falling into the category of black-tie evening wear while also incorporating some modern, mildly edgy flair. The dress was form-fitted with a column silhouette, but was made unique with an asymmetrical one-shoulder neckline with a diagonal strap over Alves’s right collarbone creating a striking keyhole cutout.
Just above Alves’s right hip was another cutout, this one in a curved, abstract shape. The artfully fragmented look added a playful twist, as did the tiny sequins dotting the outfit, creating a glittery, night sky-like backdrop. Alves wore minimal makeup and accessorized with a pair of diamond drop earrings.
Alves has a documented history with Stella McCartney. Back in 2022, Alves coordinated with McCartney in Austin, Texas for charity work where they put on a runway in the middle of a soccer field for an audience of over 700. Ever since, she’s been spotted out and about in the brand. In 2025, for example, Alves wore Stella McCartney to the SXSW premiere of husband Matthew McConaughey’s film “The Rival of Amziah King.” Alves wore a double-breasted suit from the 2025 collection that was covered head-to-toe in metallic embellishments. To accentuate the impact of these gleaming accents, Alves wore silver statement earrings.
The Baby2Baby Gala is an annual event to raise money for Baby2Baby, a nonprofit that brings vital supplies such as diapers and clothing to more than a million children living in poverty across the United States. The gala features catering from top Los Angeles chefs, high-end auctions, and a lineup of uplifting entertainment that promotes the organization’s mission. Each year, one celebrity mother receives the Giving Tree Award. This year, the award went to Demi Moore. Past recipients have included Drew Barrymore, Vanessa Bryant, Gwyneth Paltrow, and Serena Williams.
