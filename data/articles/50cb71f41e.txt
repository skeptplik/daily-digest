Macy's Thanksgiving Parade postponed to Saturday due to expected high winds
Annual event was scheduled for Thursday morning in New York
Macy's 100th annual U.S. Thanksgiving Day Parade has been postponed from Thursday to Saturday as powerful winds are expected to bear down on New York during the annual event, organizers said Wednesday.
In a statement, NBC cited conversations with the city and concerns over the safety of participants and spectators.
"Due to the extreme weather conditions, the city of New York, Macy's and NBC have decided, in the interest of safety for both the participants and spectators, to move the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade to Saturday," the statement said.
NBC, which airs the parade, will instead broadcast a rerun of the 2025 edition Thursday. The 2026 parade will kick off at 8:30 a.m. ET Saturday, Macy's said in a statement.
"Our team is now working through the complex operational logistics required to bring the Parade to life on Saturday, and we will share additional details as they are finalized," Macy's said.
"We are incredibly grateful to the thousands of volunteers, performers and partners who make this extraordinary tradition possible. We can't wait to celebrate 100 years of Parade magic together on Saturday."
A balloon of Buzz Lightyear rests under protective netting Wednesday. (Timothy A. Clary/AFP/Getty Images)
High winds and rains for holiday
New York City is expected to get blasted with wind gusts between 80 and 96 km/h on Thursday, along with steady, sustained winds between 48 and 61 km/h, according to city emergency management officials. Moderate to heavy rain is also expected.
Separately, the U.S. National Weather Service has issued a high wind warning and a coastal flood warning for Thursday into Friday.
The city has long had measures in place that dictate whether to lower the iconic balloons that are such a big part of the parade, or cancel their flights entirely, if the wind gets too strong, due to concerns that strong winds can knock them off course and into spectators along the parade route.
A balloon of Buckaroo Bob The Minion was being inflated Wednesday before the parade was postponed. (Timothy A. Clary/AFP/Getty Images)
The balloons were well on the way to being inflated when the postponement was announced. Macy's representatives had repeatedly put off interviews with media on-site.
City rules have long barred large balloons from flying if sustained winds exceed 37 km/h or gusts exceed 55 km/h.
The city's balloon rules were put in place after high winds sent a Cat in the Hat balloon careening into a metal pole near Central Park during the 1997 parade, leaving a woman in a coma. The parade has seen other snafus since then, including a 2005 incident in which a balloon hit a lamp post and hurt a woman and her young sister.
Bad weather in the past has caused balloons to be flown a bit lower than usual, with each inflatable character having a distinct risk profile that includes its size and weight and optimal altitudes for differing wind speeds.
