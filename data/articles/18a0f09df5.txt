CVS Health is one of the largest companies in the U.S. It serves the health care needs of hundreds of millions of Americans annually, has a hand in nearly every part of the drug supply chain, and has more than $400 billion in annual revenue.
So when it announced last Wednesday that it will split into two entities, the news made waves.
Here’s the thing: Much of the media coverage frames this move as CVS unwinding its vertical integration in response to ongoing regulatory pressure. That is not what is happening here.
