Sydney Swans investigate poor culture after Tom Papley incident
In short:
Chief executive Matthew Pavlich said the club was "bitterly disappointed" in having to address reports of a poor culture at the Sydney Swans.
Pavlich said the Swans "need to be better" amid allegations of a poor team culture.
Tom Papley allegedly returned a positive roadside test for methylamphetamine on Monday.
