Watch: Cruise ship seen sinking after collision with cargo ship in New Orleans
A cargo ship has collided with a docked cruise ship in New Orleans, Louisiana, with reports suggesting more than 120 people were on board the cruise ship.
Eyewitness video shows the moment the cruise ship is seen sinking and the aftermath of the collision as rescue efforts are under way.
New Orleans mayor Helena Moreno has said all passengers have been accounted for.
