Can DeepSeek challenge OpenAI, Anthropic in AI agents as Harness adoption takes off?
Hangzhou-based start-up ramps up efforts to monetise its powerful foundation models as AI agent platform gains traction
Users of Harness through the company’s official application programming interface were consuming around 92 trillion tokens a day as of November 4, the Hangzhou-based start-up said. That is equivalent to about 1.1 billion tokens consumed per second.
A “harness” is the software infrastructure that turns a large language model into an AI agent that can autonomously execute tasks on behalf of users, rather than simply respond to prompts.
