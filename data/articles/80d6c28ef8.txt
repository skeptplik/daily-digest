Why A Standing Desk?
While it’s been memed for years, a standing desk does offer serious health benefits. Shifting your posture regularly throughout the day helps you burn a few extra calories, and improves circulation, and can improve your cardiovascular health because you’re spending less time on a long day sitting in a sedentary position.
