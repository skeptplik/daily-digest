‘Hypocritical’: Hong Kong hits back at criticism of Joshua Wong’s sentence
Ten countries, including US, UK and Australia, say the national security law is being used to ‘target individuals for political expression and participation’
Hong Kong has hit back at a joint statement from 10 countries over the latest sentencing of opposition activist Joshua Wong Chi-fung, accusing them of distorting facts and smearing the city’s national security law.
The rebuttal came after the US, Australia, Canada, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, the Netherlands, New Zealand and the UK issued a joint statement on Friday saying the sentencing of Wong, together with the earlier jailing of many others, showed how the city’s national security law (NSL) was being used to “target individuals for political expression and participation”.
