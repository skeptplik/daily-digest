Seniors rock the stage: Greek theater seeks to show that 'art has no age'

A recent Greek National Opera production afforded a group of seniors the chance to channel their young-at-heart energy into onstage performances, proving that no one's too old to learn something new. It challenged the premise that retirement means surrendering to a sedentary lifestyle and extinguishing one’s creative spirit.
