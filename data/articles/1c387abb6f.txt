When it comes to shoes, I’m loyal to my favorite pairs. You’ll rarely find me switching things up too often—that’s why I take great pride (and care) in my footwear purchases. In the summer, it’s a tight edit of ballet flats and flip-flops. Come winter, I make the switch to pumps and boots exclusively—sorry, sneakers, I have a strict gym-only policy. However, as each year passes, I find it harder and harder to discover my next great boot purchase… at a reasonable price, that is.
Vogue’s Favorite Boots Under $500:
Which is why I made it my mission to curate the season’s very best boots under $500. (A task harder than you might imagine, when you want both lasting quality in a fairly timeless shape). Whether you gravitate towards ankle boots, flat styles, knee-highs, or a style with a little more personality, there’s something for every wardrobe in here. Each is ready to be worn with your favorite jeans, a wintery dress, or a printed maxi skirt. And for the adventurous types, there are weatherproof styles to brave the rain or snow—all with a fashionable twist, of course.
Below, discover our edit of the best boots under $500.
The Ankle Boot
For a pair you’ll reach for daily, the ankle boot is as straightforward as it is versatile. Opt for a classic Chelsea style, a sleek pointed toe, or something a little more playful—think croc-embossed leather, a rich burgundy hue, or a sumptuous suede. There are no wrong answers!
The Knee-High Boot
A knee-high boot brings a little extra attitude to winter dressing. Style your favorite pair with a sweeping maxi skirt or a printed dress, or tuck your jeans into them for a Princess Diana-approved styling trick.
The Flat Boots
For those keen on getting their 10,000 steps in, a flat boot is the way to go—and these chic knee-high styles from Reformation, Banana Republic, and Staud will look good doing it, too.
The Personality Boots
If you’re looking to bring a little flair to your winter rotation, reach for a boot that steps outside your comfort zone. Croc-embossed booties, buckled moto boots, or Western-inspired styles each bring on personality to your look; Reformation’s Remy boot in snakeskin is your way into the animal print trend spotted across the runways and street style scene.
The Weather-Proof Boot
Whether you’re packing for a spontaneous weekend upstate, planning your annual ski vacation, or doing the Sunday shop in stormy weather, a weather-proof is essential. Braving the rain or snow doesn’t mean sacrificing style, however, and favorites from Ancient Greek Sandals, Hunter, Ugg, and Moon Boot bring flair to a sensible shoe purchase.
