Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey sparked a fresh surge in back-catalog sales for 2018's Assassin's Creed Odyssey, Ubisoft has said.
In its latest financial results briefing, the Assassin's Creed publisher noted how a "major summer movie" had "materially benefited" the continued sales of its brilliant Ancient Greek game. Over the summer, Ubisoft re-promoted Assassin's Creed Odyssey alongside the launch of Nolan's film, inviting players to go start their own epic journey for themselves after returning home from theaters.
Once again, Ubisoft used its financial update to praise the evergreen sales of its Assassin's Creed back catalog. Also, once again, it failed to provide any further detail on how last year's Assassin's Creed Shadows had fared. There's a sense that the long-awaited feudal Japan blockbuster has been a commercial disappointment for Ubisoft, and it's been a while since we had any formal update on sales numbers. No further content for the game is on the way, either — even though 2020's Valhalla just got a New Game Plus mode.
Indeed, Shadows is not mentioned at all in the results, despite Ubisoft commenting on Odyssey, Valhalla (which also just passed 40 million players), and this year's Assassin’s Creed Black Flag Resynced — the latter of which has clearly had a successful launch.
Ubisoft attributes an outperformance in its latest financial results to Resynced, which it describes as having been "among the industry's most successful Action-Adventure remakes." How did its launch compare to any all-new Assassin's Creed games? If it did surpass any of those, Ubisoft perhaps understandably isn't saying — but it's clear the game has been a hit.
"The franchise will celebrate its 20th anniversary next year," Ubisoft said, reiterating what fans have heard already — that 2027 should be a more exciting year for the series than the past 12 months have been. "This is a major milestone for the brand, which is preparing a year of reveals, updates, rewards, and community
moments." 
It seems likely that next year will be when we get a first glimpse of the upcoming Assassin's Creed Hexe, the series' next blockbuster game, as well as Netflix's upcoming Assassin's Creed TV series set in Ancient Rome and a peek at Codename Invictus, a new multiplayer game spinoff. Until then, well, Assassin's Creed: Odyssey is really worth a go if you haven't played it already.
