AFL draft hopefuls haven't put a foot wrong since ACL injuries
In short:
After Gus White did his ACL during a game this year, the 18-year-old sought advice from another AFL draft hopeful who has experienced the same injury.
Like White, Memphis Webb suffered an ACL rupture — but both have worked hard on recovering.
What's next?
Both White and Webb are prepared for all eventualities as they set their sights on the draft, which will be held over two nights later this month.
