Jordan Peele has become one of the biggest names in modern horror, with his films Get Out, Us, and Nope helping establish him as one of the genre's most recognizable filmmakers. Through his production company, Monkeypaw Productions, Peele has also helped bring several other horror projects to the big screen, including Candyman, Monkey Man, and Him.
Now, Monkeypaw might be getting involved with another iconic horror franchise, judging by a cryptic new social media post.
The official Monkeypaw Productions TikTok account recently shared a video featuring footage from Wes Craven's 1984 horror classic A Nightmare on Elm Street. The clip has prompted speculation that Peele and his company could be involved with the franchise's next installment, which is already in development.
Neither Peele nor his production company has confirmed any involvement with the upcoming movie, so it's possible the video is simply a tribute to Craven's original film. Interestingly, Monkeypaw Productions also shared a cryptic video featuring footage from 1996's The Craft earlier this week, prompting speculation that the company could be considering multiple horror revivals.
What we know about the new Nightmare on Elm Street
Paramount is developing a new A Nightmare on Elm Street movie after securing a deal with the estate of franchise creator Wes Craven to adapt his original screenplay.
The film will launch Paramount Primal, a new genre label led by Barbarian, Weapons, and Companion producers J.D. Lifshitz and Raphael Margules. They will executive produce, while Iya Labunka and Jonathan Craven, Wes Craven's widow and son, respectively, will produce alongside Marc Toberoff.
The project remains in early development, with no writer, director, cast or release date announced.
The original 1984 film spawned six direct sequels, a television series and the Freddy vs. Jason crossover. Its last theatrical installment was the 2010 remake starring Jackie Earle Haley.
