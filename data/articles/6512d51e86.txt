Few fashion purchases carry as much weight, both literally and financially, as a designer handbag. The right one can make an outfit, become a daily fixture on morning commutes, and, in some cases, retain its value long after checkout. But in a market where a new It bag seems to emerge every season (or with every scroll through your feed), figuring out which styles are worth the splurge isn't always straightforward.
Fashion history offers clues to sound investments. Some of today’s most coveted bags have been around for decades: Gucci’s Jackie traces its roots to the 1960s, while Dior’s Lady Dior became an icon of ’90s style thanks to Princess Diana. Fendi’s Baguette, meanwhile, outlived its early-aughts It-bag heyday and found a new generation of fans more than two decades later. Their enduring appeal speaks to the power of great design, which can transcend changing tastes, evolving trends, and creative director shake-ups.
Vogue’s Guide to the Best Designer Handbags:
Of course, a handbag doesn’t need decades of history behind it to become a wardrobe staple. Sometimes, an unexpected silhouette, celebrity endorsement, or clever design detail can turn a newcomer into a cult classic overnight. Identifying the forever pieces among today’s endless stream of new arrivals is then part of the fun.
Beyond the initial buzz, the most promising designs have some things in common: practical proportions, distinctive details, and versatility—all the more important when you’re considering a four-figure purchase. The best designer handbags should fit seamlessly into your wardrobe rather than demand an entirely new one. They should be flexible enough to carry everywhere from dinner to work and the grocery store, beautifully crafted to withstand years of wear, and stylish enough to elevate even the simplest of outfits.
Below, Vogue’s guide to the best designer handbags, from bona fide icons to established favorites and newer arrivals with the potential to become tomorrow’s most treasured pieces. Whether you're drawn to a heritage house signature or something less ubiquitous, these are the bags worth getting to know—and keeping close for years to come.
The Boho Bag: Chloé Paddington
The Paddington was the bag of the mid-2000s, instantly recognizable for its slouchy shape and oversized padlock hardware. Worn by every It-girl from Kate Moss to Sienna Miller, it defined an era of bohemian glamour. In 2025, the cult bag returned under Chloé creative director Chemena Kamali, who has revived the padlock-topped icon in various sizes and colors.
The Noughties Throwback: Balenciaga Le City
Balenciaga’s Le City bag was a favorite of Noughties It-girls—think the Olsen twins, Nicole Richie, and Lindsay Lohan. It made a full-fledged comeback in 2024 with refreshed proportions and multiple colorways. Today, the archival icon endures as a house staple—equally tempting in its new iterations as vintage styles. While plenty of fashion lovers are reaching for the new model, which comes in a range of colorways, others, including British Vogue’s Naomi Smart, are securing their wishlist pieces second-hand on preloved sites such as eBay and Vinted.
The New Icon Bag: Gucci Jackie
Gucci’s Jackie is one of fashion’s true icons. Originally beloved by Jacqueline Kennedy Onassis, the hobo-shaped shoulder bag has been reissued countless times, each wave balancing retro charm with contemporary relevance. Modern versions—sleek, compact, and available in lush hues, as well as go-with-everything neutrals—make it just as covetable today as in the ’60s.
The Sculptural Style: Loewe Puzzle Bag
First introduced as Jonathan Anderson’s debut bag at Loewe, the Puzzle quickly became a fashion-insider favorite for its origami-like panels and clever, shape-shifting construction. Now, with Proenza Schouler founders Jack McCollough and Lazaro Hernandez as Loewe’s creative directors, the Puzzle remains a house cornerstone—offered in multiple sizes and colorways, with the classic full-size in black or beige still the chicest bet.
The Modern Muse: Miu Miu Aventure
Miu Miu’s Aventure may feel in step with fashion’s current appetite for ladylike top handles, but the bag’s roots go back. Introduced for fall 2024, the softly structured style reimagines a doctor bag from the 2009 archives, complete with curved lines, a snap-hook closure, and vintage appeal. Since its debut, the Aventure has become one of Miu Miu’s most popular styles, offered in everything from nappa leather to suede and an ever-changing lineup of colors.
The It Bag: Prada Passage
The Prada Passage bag may have only made its debut on the spring 2026 runway, but its architectural, slightly off-kilter silhouette already has the makings of a modern classic. The elongated style plays with contrasting textures and a clever bag-within-a-bag effect. With its folded sides, protruding flap, and slender handles, it’s practical, minimal, and guaranteed to become a must-have for collectors of the brand.
The Understated Icon: The Row Agnes
The Row has a knack for transforming familiar handbag silhouettes into instant must-haves, and the Agnes is no exception. Inspired by vintage bowling bags, the structured top handle is marked by its gently curved shape, tubular handles, flat base, and sweeping zip closure. Devoid of flashy logos or hardware, the Agnes features a quietly distinctive design that sets it apart—and feels destined to get even better with age.
The Modern Crossbody: Khaite Blake
Khaite is renowned for its arsenal of timeless, investment-worthy accessories. The brand's Blake bag reimagines the classic box silhouette with a softly structured, flap-front shape that combines clean lines with sculptural curves. The result is a contemporary crossbody that offers form and function. More recent Soft Blake styles loosen things up, translating the same understated elegance into a supple, slouchier shape designed for everyday wear.
The Edgy Bag: Balenciaga Rodeo Bag
Spotted on just about every cool girl, the Balenciaga Rodeo has quickly become one of fashion's defining It bags. Its slouchy silhouette draws inspiration from vintage briefcases, while a structured top handle and removable, adjustable leather strap allow it to be carried by hand, over the shoulder, or crossbody.
The Roomy Holdall: Coach Empire
It was clear that the Empire bag was an instant hit upon walking into the Vogue offices—you can spot at least three perched on an editor’s desk at any given time. The appeal of this popular Coach style lies in its marriage of aesthetic and accessibility—its roomy design and mid-level price make it hard to ignore. Opt for the larger 40 model for daytime and the slightly more compact 35 for evenings.
The Minimalist Hero: Bottega Veneta Campana
First introduced in 2004 under then-creative director Tomas Maier, Bottega Veneta's Campana bag has been relaunched for fall 2025. The softly curved shoulder bag retains its signature Intrecciato weave and double handles, now with even sleeker lines. Its spacious silhouette and lack of hardware make it an effortless everyday option, while its two-decade history proves that some designs are timeless for a reason.
The Party Bag: Fendi Baguette
When Carrie Bradshaw slung a whipstitched Fendi baguette over her shoulder in Sex and The City, she helped turn the already-iconic bag into a worldwide sensation. And while the style has never really gone away, it has enjoyed a serious renaissance over the last decade, with a new generation discovering its Noughties charm. Recent re-editions, from tactile pony hair to richly embellished iterations, prove there’s still plenty of life left in the silhouette.
The Modern Doctor Bag: Loewe Amazona
Originally introduced in the 1970s, Loewe's Amazona bag returned from the archives to mark the house's 180th anniversary. This medium-sized version is crafted in Spain from supple leather and finished with reinforced corners for added structure. True to its effortless appeal, it's designed to be carried slightly open—what Jonathan Anderson described as looking "like a chic mistake"—while a concealed closure keeps the contents secure. Removable shoulder and crossbody straps add to its versatility.
The Quiet Luxury: Toteme T-Lock Bag
When Toteme introduced the T-Lock in 2022, the Swedish label proved its minimalist sensibility translated just as well to accessories as to ready-to-wear. Defined by a softly structured silhouette, curved flap, and instantly recognizable T-shaped clasp, the bag has since become a signature of the brand—and a favorite of celebrities like Katie Holmes. Available in everything from buttery leather to earthy suede, and in a range of sizes and colors, the T-Lock feels just as relevant today as it did four years ago—and promises to remain so for decades to come.
The Evening Favorite: Saint Laurent Le 5 à 7
Saint Laurent’s Le 5 à 7 has become one of the house’s quintessential everyday bags, offered in both a sleek, subtly curved shoulder silhouette and a more spacious bucket shape. Its name nods to the Parisian “after-work to before-dinner” window, telegraphing day-to-night ease. Available in smooth or grained leather, suede, and seasonal finishes, both styles are discreet staples that read polished without trying.
The Cult Archive Bag: Dior Lady Dior
Another iconic bag named after another iconic woman, Dior’s Lady Dior bag owes its fame to Princess Diana. Originally called the Chouchou, the boxy top-handle bag was gifted to Diana by then-First Lady of France, Bernadette Chirac, in September 1995. The princess quickly made it a signature, carrying it frequently to public engagements and requesting a version in blue to match her eyes. In 1996, Dior renamed the bag the Lady Dior in honor of its most famous fan. It remains one of the brand’s most popular bags.
The Slouchy Crossbody: Lemaire Croissant
Named for—and inspired by—the curved shape of its namesake French pastry, Lemaire’s Croissant has quickly become one of the house’s signatures. Curved panels give the crescent bag its memorable shape, allowing it to mold against the body when worn crossbody, while knotted strap ends and a looped zipper pull make for recognizable, playful finishes. Since launch, the line has expanded to include a variety of sizes and colorways.
The Travel Tote: Louis Vuitton Neverfull
Louis Vuitton’s Neverfull tote is often spotted on the arms of airport-bound celebrities, no doubt thanks to its practical, capacious interior (the clue is in the name) and timeless logo leather design. Introduced in 2007 as a beach bag, the style has experienced a revival in recent years.
The Do-It-All Mini: Miu Miu Wander
Launched in the fall/winter 2022 collection, Miu Miu’s Wander bag has already secured its place in the designer bag hall of fame, thanks to its distinctive matelassé detailing and compact size. Beloved by the likes of Emma Corrin, Alexa Chung, Gigi Hadid, Hailey Bieber and Jaime Xie, to name but a few, its star shows no sign of fading.
The Tasteful Top Handle: Savette Soft Symmetry
Fashion-insider favorite accessories brand Savette built its name on retro-tinged ladylike handbags, but the Soft Symmetry takes the label’s codes in a more relaxed direction. Introduced in 2026, the roomy top handle trades the original Symmetry’s structure for a slouchier silhouette, generous interior, and dangling zipper tails that lend it an appealingly undone quality. Founder Amy Zurek designed it with longevity in mind: recognizable enough to feel distinctly Savette, but restrained enough to transcend a trend cycle.
The Personality Pick: Prada Wish Pouch
Leave it to Prada to turn a humble, dust bag-like drawstring pouch into one of 2026’s most coveted accessories. Introduced on the spring 2026 runway, the Wish Pouch’s pared-back construction takes on a different look depending on the material: Silk duchesse versions come in jewel-box shades, while leather, nylon, and neutral iterations offer a more understated take on the whimsical silhouette.
The Stylish Shoulder Bag: Coach Chelsea
Coach knows its way around a buzzy bag (see: the Bella Hadid-beloved Brooklyn), and Chelsea is already emerging as another hit. The roomy shoulder bag pairs a relaxed, soft shape with a flap closure and minimal hardware. Available in leather and suede, it has the easy, throw-it-over-your-shoulder appeal that makes a bag more likely to become a wardrobe mainstay than a one-season wonder.
What is the most popular handbag right now?
Well, it shifts from season to season. For fall 2026, Prada is back on top: The Lyst Index crowned it the world’s hottest brand in Q3, with the house reclaiming the number one spot for the first time since 2023. Elsewhere, Gucci remained in the top five, Chloé climbed two places, and Bottega Veneta rose three spots following Louise Trotter’s much-anticipated debut for the house.
The season’s most-wanted bags offer an equally varied snapshot of what shoppers want right now. Prada’s silk duchesse Wish Pouch was Lyst’s hottest product of Q3, with searches surging 498 percent. Meanwhile, Balenciaga’s slouchy Rodeo landed at number eight, with the fashion platform calling out “a spectrum of slouchy top-handle bags” among the quarter’s strongest performers.
It bags like Toteme’s T-Lock Bag and Miu Miu’s Aventure have proved they have staying power beyond their initial buzz, while perennial signatures, including Saint Laurent’s Le 5 à 7 and Gucci’s Jackie, continue to make the case for investing in a silhouette with history behind it. At the same time, a new class of contenders is emerging: Savette has softened its signature polish with the relaxed Soft Symmetry, and The Row has put its typically minimalist spin on the vintage bowling bag with the Agnes.
The takeaway? There isn’t one handbag (or even one handbag aesthetic) dominating right now. The most compelling styles range from softly slouching carryalls to structured top handles and fanciful evening pouches. So ultimately, the smartest investment is the bag that works with your wardrobe and your life—and that you’ll still want to carry long after its moment on the Lyst Index has passed.
