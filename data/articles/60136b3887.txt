China’s HQ series intercepts 2 new missile types as PLA tests weapon platform
State broadcaster highlights exercise by ground-based air defence unit, though it is unclear what was being tested
The CCTV report on Monday said it was a rapid response operation by the ground-based Shenwei Missile Battalion – under the PLA Central Theatre Command Air Force – which is equipped with Hongqing surface-to-air missiles.
It did not disclose the type of missiles intercepted or any details about the air defence interceptors used, nor did it give the timing or location of the exercise.
