Welcome back to Beauty Marks: Vogue’s weekly edition of the best moments in celebrity beauty, from Vogue editors’ IG feeds, and all the glam of the fashion and pop culture landscapes. Each week, we curate the nail art to pin for your next nail appointment, new recruits to ‘Team Bob’, and major red carpet moments from the week’s most glamorous affairs. Phoebe Bridgers punctuated her impossibly sleek platinum lengths with a single black bobby pin, while Nicole Warne brought a flash of pearlescent shimmer to the inner corners of her eyes. Chappell Roan, never one for half measures, took things a few steps further with an elaborate flame-red braid and icy blue lids. Elsewhere, Rosie Huntington-Whiteley showed off her sculpted cheekbone, Pauline Chalamet embraced the charm of a tousled bob, and Demi Lovato served up a golden honey-inspired manicure. Consider these the finishing touches worth stealing.
