Why You Should Still Buy Physical PS5 Games
I'll admit I'm a little biased here, but I really do think there's still plenty of life left in physical games. That's why Sony's plans to stop producing physical discs for new PlayStation releases from January 2028 are so disappointing. While existing physical releases won't suddenly disappear, the prospect of future PlayStation games becoming digital-only is a worrying one for anyone who enjoys building a collection.
Sony has attributed the decision to changing consumer preferences, so if we want physical games to stick around, we need to make our voices heard where it matters most: our wallets.
Buying physical gives you options that digital downloads simply don't. You can lend games to friends, trade them in, sell them when you're finished, or keep building a collection that you can actually see and hold. Physical retailers also compete with one another on pricing, meaning you're not entirely at the mercy of the PlayStation Store when hunting for a bargain.
There's a wider ecosystem worth supporting, too, from independent game shops and second-hand retailers to collectors and gaming preservationists. An increasingly digital-only future means fewer choices over where we buy our games, how much we pay, and what we can do with them afterwards.
So, if you've been sitting on the fence about picking up a physical copy of a game you've been eyeing, perhaps now's the time. Keep building that collection, support the retailers still selling physical games, and show Sony there's still an audience for discs. Buying a few games won't single-handedly reverse its decision, but if we stop buying them altogether, we're hardly going to convince anyone to keep making them.
