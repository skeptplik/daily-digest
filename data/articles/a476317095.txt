NEW ARRIVAL: Isabel Marant has appointed Therese Lundström chief marketing officer as it maps out its strategy under new management.
The executive, whose CV includes stints at Courrèges, Dries Van Noten and Givenchy, will oversee the brand’s global marketing strategy, including image, global communications, press relations, celebrity relations, events and visual merchandising, Isabel Marant said in a statement Wednesday.
She succeeds Sophie Condroyer in the position and will report to chief executive officer Catherine Jacquet.
A graduate of Paris ESLSCA Business School, Lundström previously served as communications director at Dries Van Noten, following several years at Givenchy overseeing corporate image and global media.
She joins Marant from Courrèges, where she was chief marketing officer in charge of the brand’s marketing, press and communications strategies during a period of rapid expansion under then-creative director Nicolas Di Felice.
Her arrival at Marant comes on the heels of Jacquet’s appointment in May. Most recently chief operating officer at Fear of God, she succeeded Anouck Duranteau-Loeper and has been charged with supporting and accelerating the brand’s next phase of development.
Marant launched her label and had her first show in 1994, quickly becoming a byword for a hip, womanly brand of boho chic.
The house is now under the creative purview of Kim Bekker, who took over as sole creative director a year ago and pressed pause on the usual rock ‘n’ roll edginess of its runway shows to reveal a softer, sunnier side to the brand with her spring 2027 collection.
