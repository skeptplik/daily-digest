The GTA 6 Ultimate Edition has a lot of stuff in it, but quite a few of these bonuses only become available at specific points in the story, similar to the Vintage Vice City Pack.
Below I'll lay out each bonus in order of when it becomes accessible in the story, though once you complete mission 11, you should be able to open the main menu store and view these details in each specific entry.
Chapter 1 unlocks
Jason's Vehicles
- Only available from Jason's House during this chapter.
These include the Dinka Endura military motorcycle and the Crest Kayak.
Ganado Retro Build
- Available at Coastal Auto Care in Key Lento inside the Pro Builds/Full Kits section of the mod menu.
This is an upgrade from Jason's Vapid Ganado vehicle with exclusive mods and classic stylings.
One-Eyed Willies
- Find this shop in the Vehicles/Mod Shops section of the map index.
This Lake Leonida mod shop specialises in off-road modifications and hand painted customisation.
Rideout Customs
- Find this shop in the Vehicles/Mod Shops section of the map index.
Another mod shop, specialising in detailed interiors, exquisite rims, and "donk stylings".
Stock 305 Clothing Store
- Find it in the Style/Casual & Street section of the map index.
A clothing store in Stockyard that includes various unique looks for Jason and Lucia.
Keep up to date with the most important stories and the best deals, as picked by the PC Gamer team.
Electric Fang Tattoo Parlor
- Find it in the Style / Tattoos section of the map index.
A tattoo parlor, also in Stockyard, with over 50 signature tattoos for Jason and Lucia, designed by real-world art collective, FAILE.
Vice City Merch
- Available from the mannequins at The Wonder Whale in Key Lento. Find it in the Style /Souvenir & Swimwear section of the map index.
These are apparel and accessories featuring Macca the Gator, a character from a hit Leonida TV Show who you likely will have seen in the game's marketing.
PTT Youngin$ Compound
- Available once you've explored Southside. Find it in the Robberies/Illegal Operations section of the map index.
This is a raid on a gang compound that can score you special items and contraband.
Personalized Weapon Variants
- Find these in the Variants section of the weapon locker at Ammu-Nation. Lucia's design unlocks in Chapter 2.
These are special skins for Jason's Girardi ES9 Pistol and Lucia's Klose K17 pistol, with engravings. As mentioned, you can get Lucia's in Chapter 2.
Sara's Unisex Salon
- Find it in the Style/Hair & Beauty section of the map index. Signature Looks are available in Chapter 2.
A salon in Hamlet that has signature styles, including facial hair options for Jason, plus makeup and nails for Lucia. More styles unlock in Chapter 2, plus even more in Chapter 4.
Chapter 2 unlocks
Dominator Buggy and Garage
- Available after visiting the Mud Club with Wyman in Chapter 2. Find a task in the Exploration section of the map index.
This is a specialised off-road vehicle for the backwoods of Mount Kalaga and beyond, plus the Paradise Garage in Watson Bay, which features a weapon locker and a secure place to deposit stolen goods that need fencing.
Classic Car Collection
- Wyman will contact you on WhatUp once you've collected the Dominator (the entry above) in Chapter 2.
This special set of activities revolves around tracking down and restoring abandoned classic cars and work-in-progress project cars for Wyman.
Morgan Revolvers
- Available from any Ammu-Nation weapon locker in Chapter 2.
A Morgan Revolver variant for both Jason and Lucia, including palm-tree etched grips, engravings, and a scope.
Chapter 4 unlocks
Vice City Style
- These outfits become available in your wardrobe, tattoos appear in the Special section of most tattoo parlors. Hair and beard styles are also available at Sara's Unisex Salon.
This is an additional set of cosmetic options for Jason and Lucia, including basically all aspects of their style; whether outfits, tattoos, or hair and beard options.
Shitzu Squalo
- Available in Chapter 4 from a Washington Beach Canal dock. Find it in the Personal section of the map index.
This speedboat with a pink and blue palm tree motif comes with an explosive-laden weapons crate.
Chapter 5 unlocks
'95 Grotti Cheetah
- Available from the Pegassi Residences building in Chapter 5. Find a task in the Exploration section of the map index.
This signature 90s red and white sports car comes with a retro futuristic livery.
