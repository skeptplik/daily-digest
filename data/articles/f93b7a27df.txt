China puts DF missiles on display as flight bans fuel speculation about ICBM test
State media showcase the nuclear-capable DF-61 and the rarely seen DF-100 as Beijing marks anniversary of the first Dongfeng-series missile
The developments came as China marked the 66th anniversary of the DF-1, the first missile in the Dongfeng series, amid growing emphasis on the rocket force’s combat readiness and strategic deterrence capabilities.
