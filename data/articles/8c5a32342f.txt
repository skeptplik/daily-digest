We are headed right into the thick of ski season; when warm, wool knitwear is the uniform du jour, and the temperature is the kind of cold that’s completely inexcusable unless accompanied by plates of melty raclette at après-ski. This is your sign to book your winter getaway in advance. And while there are tons of great ski resorts you could jet off to, nothing quite beats an Alpine retreat—whether freeskiing in Zermatt or trialing bunny slopes in Megève, you have options. The best Airbnbs in the Alps, from Austria to France to Italy, are the perfect place to stay if you need more flexibility and space than a hotel.
Vogue’s Guide to the Best Airbnbs in the Alps:
- For an Off-the-Beaten Path Cabin: Cozy Chalet in Riddes, Switzerland, from $389 per night
- For the Ultimate Mountain Views: Maria 1936 in Colle Santa Lucia, Italy, from $608 per night
- For a Stay on a Working Farm: La Baita nel Bosco in Pieve d’Alpago, Italy, from $269 per night
- For Restored Charm in the Village Center: Chasa Noth in Scuol, Switzerland, from $700 per night
- For Ski-In/Ski-Out: Panoramic View Apartment in Megève, France, from $1,061 per night

It’s a vast region—the Dolomites alone are an 18-peak range—so there’s a lot to choose from. Go for stately chalet elegance with a multi-bedroom option if you’re traveling with lots of friends and family; book something closer to a cozy cabin if you prefer a low-key holiday. These 15 options span the French, Swiss, Austrian, and Italian Alps, as well as a range of prices and styles. Your next decision? Which Fair Isle sweater will make it into your carry-on.
Those in search of the most traditional of Alpine vacations will find everything they’re looking for in this peaceful chalet—right down to the Savoyard pottery. It looks out onto the Aravis mountains, just five minutes away from the Croix-Fry ski resort and 40 minutes from the busier spots of Annecy and Megève. It has a wood-burning fireplace that’s ideal for relaxing after a day on the slopes, and a spacious balcony for those brave enough to wrap up and take their coffee outdoors in the winter chill.
- Rooms: 2 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms
- Amenities: Bathtub, washer/dryer, HDTV, high chair, wood-burning fireplace, balcony, pet-friendly, long-term stays allowed
- Location: Manigod, France
Situate yourself in the heart of one of the most sought-after ski resorts, close to all of Gstaad’s main shops. The apartment, which can sleep up to six, has heated underground parking and a ski cellar to facilitate winter trips. The interior is simple but well-appointed—the dining table being a major plus for cozy dinners—but the real draw is the balcony. Head outside for gorgeous mountain views whenever you want them.
- Rooms: 3 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms
- Amenities: Washer/dryer, HDTV, sound system, crib, indoor fireplace, free parking, balcony, pet-friendly

If you’re not looking for a fully remote stay, this charming mini-chalet gives you a taste of quaint mountain village life. Sleek chrome furniture, a chef-grade kitchen, and whitewashed walls bring light in.
