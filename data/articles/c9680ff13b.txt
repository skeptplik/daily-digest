Cornell faculty vote no-confidence in leadership after gang rape case
The resolution reflects unhappiness over the Ivy League school’s handling of allegations that a female student was assaulted at a fraternity
Faculty members at Cornell University on Friday passed a no-confidence resolution against the elite college’s leadership over its handling of an alleged gang rape that has sparked outcry in the US.
The vote is not binding but marks a strong symbolic rebuke of administrators at the Ivy League college in New York state.
According to a lawsuit, a female student was assaulted by seven male students at a fraternity house on campus.
