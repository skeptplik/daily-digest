Josh Hazlewood backs Australia captain Pat Cummins as sandpaper saga continues
In short:
Josh Hazlewood has publicly defended Pat Cummins amid speculation about his future as Australian men's Test captain.
Cummins has come under fire following the resurfacing of the 2018 ball-tampering scandal.
Hazlewood says Cummins has done a "remarkable job" as Australia's captain.
