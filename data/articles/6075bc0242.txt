Britain’s sex slump, nuclear weapons in space, key suspect in bowel cancer rise – podcast
Madeleine Finlay and co-host Ian Sample discuss the three science stories you need to know about this week, including a breakdown of what the average Briton is – or isn’t – up to in the bedroom.
