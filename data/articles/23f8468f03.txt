From diving deep into the dark waters of your mind to feeling like life is one big adventure park, the switch from Scorpio season to Sagittarius season (November 22 to December 21) can feel like quite a drastic one. Indeed, feel your spirits lift as the cosmos heads toward a much-needed “let’s give life a real shot” sort of energy—just in time for party season. Take a deep breath as the mood lightens, the pace picks up, and possibilities begin to look exciting again. Just remember to keep your feet firmly on the ground as you make the most of everything coming your way, because it can be easy to get swept up in all that excitement.
But while Sagittarius season is usually fun for everyone, this year it’ll hit some signs more than others. Below, see if you’re among the luckiest zodiac signs of the season.
Sagittarius
November 24 – December 21
Your season is here, and it means increased luck, serendipity, and cosmic blessings. As the sun moves into your sign, you find that classic Sag fire surfacing once again. You’ll feel more motivated and driven, with that go-getter energy making a comeback. Do you care about the spoils of war that had you weeping last season? Not exactly. Your gaze shifts towards those solid relationships and blessings that keep your life moving in all the stable ways. You are no longer waiting for someone else to show you the way ahead. You are back with a bang, and you know exactly where you are headed.
Aries
March 21 – April 19
Ideas: check. Finances: check. Self-confidence: check. Aries, you are thriving this Sagittarius season! Life may have thrown you some lemons lately, but you’ve picked up the pieces and turned them into something creative that excites you, mostly because it pushes you to explore life through a fresh lens. Keep your childlike wonder and belief in your superpowers intact—don’t be afraid to climb that mountain and keep your feet firmly anchored in what sustains you in the long run (the activities and people that truly matter). You could get used to life feeling like a blessing.
Libra
September 24 – October 23
You may have found yourself circling back to the same old hiccups: an unavoidable, strained relationship, a lingering lack of self-confidence, a fear of failure… What shifts for you this Sagittarius season is that you decide enough is enough. You take things into your own hands and slowly tend to what you deemed as your unchangeable fate, shaping it into a malleable version of your desired destiny. It will take some practice, patience, and consistent effort. But with your instinctive need for balance guiding you towards steady progress, success becomes predetermined.
Leo
July 23 – August 22
You made some strong choices in your recent past. You chose to focus on all that is working for you instead of wallowing in self-pity. You got extremely articulate about your goals and desires. You made peace with the tug-of-war between your heart and mind and found a more purposeful, diligent way forward. This season, you turn that fire up a notch. Keep your eyes locked on your long-term goals, fire your way through with laser-sharp focus, and build and grow with others as you move along. And remember, your relationships are not only a strength, Leo—they’re also a foundation.
Scorpio
October 24 – November 23
You used your season well, Scorpio. You mulled over challenges, ruminated over choices, and tried your best to look at the larger picture. Sagittarius season simply gives wings to all those ideas and shifts you have been plotting silently in your head. You may not be leaping onto the other side just yet, but what you are definitely doing is laying rock-solid foundations for what you are here to build. You’ve set your timelines, fixed your goals, and are now ready to set them into motion.
Virgo
August 23 – September 23
From conflicting goals to emerging victorious, life has come full circle for you. You stuck to your guns, bit through the challenges, and found your way to the other side. Now that you are here, you feel a renewed sense of energy to build once again, but with greater clarity, sharper vision, and a stronger sense of alignment. Whether it’s in work or relationships, it’s time to play your best hand and give it your all. It’s time to clear the deck and open up to receive. If things feel a bit topsy-turvy along the way, don’t be too quick to read it as a setback.
