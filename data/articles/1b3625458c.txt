Over half of players on console in the US played Grand Theft Auto 6 on launch day, according to Circana.
According to the firm's Player Engagement Tracker, 61% of US PlayStation 5 players and 56% of global Xbox players engaged with GTA 6 on November 19.
GTA 6 ranked first in minutes played, with users averaging over eight hours. PS5 players averaged 513 minutes, while Xbox players averaged 494 minutes.
Fortnite ranked second, with 10% of active players across both platforms on launch day. Circana noted that the top five games people who played GTA 6 also played on November 19 were Fortnite, Call of Duty, Grand Theft Auto 5 (Remastered), Roblox, and NBA 2K27.
More than two million Xbox players in the UK and US accessed GTA 6 before its official launch by switching their location to New Zealand.
On Xbox, 1.5 million US players and 600,000 UK players used this method. By comparison, 100,000 US players and 25,000 UK players did so on PS5.
GTA 6 set new records across all platforms, generating $3.79 billion in retail sales on its first day. Rockstar confirmed this figure and reported 32.5 million units sold during pre-release. It was the most pre-ordered game on PlayStation and Xbox storefronts.
Revenue and sales exceeded expectations from analysis firms. Sensor Tower had predicted 24.5 million copies sold and $2.36 billion in pre-order and launch day sales.
