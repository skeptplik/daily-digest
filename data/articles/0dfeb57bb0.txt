Revealing your map in Grand Theft Auto 6 is no easy task, as the map is huge. Defogging the map isn't necessary to complete the game, but it's nice to see what each area has to offer. By uncovering it, you can see every clothing store, gas station, Pay 'N' Spray, and more.
However, as even getting across the map once can take a very long time, you'll need to make numerous trips across the entirety of Leonida to reveal the whole thing. Luckily, there's an easy way to get rid of the fog that also lets you see the sights at the same time.
Here's the best way to reveal the map in GTA 6
Best way to reveal the map in GTA 6
Uncovering the entire map of Leonida will take a long time. Even using the method we list below can take a couple hours, but given how big the map is, it's still one of the quickest ways to do it. The best way to reveal the map in GTA 6 is to ditch the land and sea, and take to the skies. Using a plane or a helicopter is the best method for a multitude of reasons:
- You don't need a key cloner, and they don't have trackers that you need to worry about.
- There are a lot of planes and helicopters scattered across the map, so you can always swap to another one if you're running low on fuel.
- Boats can't drive on land, cars can't drive on water, but a plane can fly over all terrain.
- There's no traffic in the skies.
However, there are a couple of downsides:
- You can't turn on auto drive for a plane or helicopter.
- There's no way to refuel planes or helicopters at the time of writing, so whenever you're running low on fuel, you need to make your way to another plane or helicopter.
As we said before though, there are a lot of planes and helicopters at your disposal, so you just need to make a quick pit stop before you're back up in the air. Below, we've marked locations where you can safely find and steal planes and helicopters around Leonida:
Planes can be found near the purple pins, helicopters can be found near the orange pins, and both planes and helicopters can be found at the pink pins. It's important to note that helicopters are mostly found at the top of towers, so you'll need to find the building's elevator and take it to the roof.
Additionally, there are more locations out there, but these locations generally have at least a couple planes or helicopters for you to take. Locations like the Mile Zero Air Station in the southwest and VCIA southwest of Vice City also have a lot of airborne vehicles that are faster, but if you try to steal any of these vehicles, you'll be met with a strong military force. It's best to avoid these as they will take you down eventually.
In our experience, the best vehicle to use is the Mammoth Dodo, which is a seaplane. The Dodos are fast and fuel efficient, so you can quickly circle around the map, defogging a bunch of it in the process. Additionally, it allows you to land not only on flat ground, but also on any water source. Dodos are usually found near hangars by the coast, so make sure to keep an eye out for them.
Tips for defogging the map in GTA 6
After you find your first airborne vehicle, follow these tips to reveal your GTA 6 map as fast as possible:
- Press down on the D-pad to check your fuel. When you're low on fuel, avoid flying over islands and start making your way to another plane or helicopter location.
- You do not have to travel far into a covered area to remove the fog. As soon as you cross over its border, the area will get revealed on the map. If you're a pro at flying, you can even fly directly in the middle of two regions, and uncover to areas at once.
- Use markers to mark the boundaries of the fog. The markers will appear on your minimap, so this will help you know if you've entered a covered area and removed the fog.
- There are areas covered in a yellow tint, like Mile Zero Air Station and VCIA. Avoid flying in these areas for a long period of time, or else you'll be hunted down.
- Find your favorite radio station or something to watch (or better yet, don't reveal the entire map at once!). This can take a couple of hours, so you'll need something to pass the time.
- Because this can take so long, flying solo can deteriorate your relationship. Avoid the Mallards, and bring your partner with you if you want to keep your relationship in good standing.
- If you’re not well acquainted with flying, get some safe practice in before you start, as crashing the plane will result in death (and loss of money).
