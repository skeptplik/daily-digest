Kermit the Frog once famously declared that “it’s not easy bein’ green,” but Jennifer Lawrence seems to disagree.
Over these past few weeks, the Oscar winner has eschewed moody fall tones for fluorescent, chemical-level greens, putting her stamp of approval on the surprise lime hue for the season. Just the other day, she layered a chartreuse Colleen Allen coat over a matching chartreuse cardigan from Miu Miu, while popping on a neon hair scrunchie to match. Prior to that, she stepped out in a bright-green puffer jacket from Dries Van Noten, with an orange floral jacquard that added even more citrusy power. And as for her new favorite bag? Oh, it’s just a slime-green Hermès Bolide, which would’ve looked right at home in the peak of “brat summer.”
For a dinner at Fouquet’s in New York City over the weekend, Lawrence brought out her statement bag once again, an accessory so bright that it basically doubled as reflective safety gear.
The Bolide bag (which Lawrence also owns in a burgundy croc) sharply contrasted her jet-black, double-breasted Aris coat from the Row, which she layered over a pair of straight-leg blue jeans and a lemony top that peeked out from the front of her jacket.
She complemented her outerwear with a pair of pointy black flats, and popped on a pair of dangly drop earrings for jewelry. Her hair, meanwhile, hung down in tight waves along her shoulders—perhaps a purposeful styling choice as she promotes her new haircare line, Mes.
Whether she’s mixing and matching prints or sporting flip-flops in the fall, Lawrence has simply never prescribed to standard fashion rules. Now, I just need her to really go full-send and put together a lime-green ensemble this Thanksgiving.
