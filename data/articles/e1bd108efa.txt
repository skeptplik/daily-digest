How Japan’s 660 most wanted stay hidden: slums, day labour and forged IDs
In a society ruled by red tape, police are still hunting murderers and radical bombers who slipped off the grid decades ago
The nationwide drive, officially designated the “Month for Strengthening Investigations into Wanted Suspects”, began on November 1. Through official websites and social media appeals, authorities are urging the public to report any information on the whereabouts of roughly 660 fugitives currently on the national wanted list.
“There is a long list of arsonists, murderers and radical activists who are still wanted, with some of the cases going back to the 1970s,” said Shinichi Ishizuka, a former criminal lawyer and founder of the Tokyo-based think tank Criminal Justice Future.
