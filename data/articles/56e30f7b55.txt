Addis Ababa – Heavy artillery attacks and cross-border incursions are threatening a new escalation between Ethiopia and Eritrea, analysts say, as the two neighbors remain on the verge of all-out war.
The two Horn of Africa countries fought a brutal border war in the late 1990s, and tensions have been rising again in recent months as Eritrea fears landlocked Ethiopia plans to invade and seize access to the sea.
Eritrean troops backed an offensive by rebels in Ethiopia’s Tigray region that began in September but was eventually pushed back by the Ethiopian army, leaving the two countries in a tense face-off.
