Israeli strikes on Gaza kill senior Hamas security official and six other people, medics say
Israeli air strikes on the devastated Gaza Strip killed Brigadier General Naeem Abu Nahel, director-general of the Palestinian territory's Internal Security Service on Tuesday, the ministry said. Another six people, including two police officers and a 17-year-old teenager, were killed in Israeli strikes across the enclave.
Israeli air strikes killed a senior Hamas security official and six other people, including two police officers, in the Gaza Strip on Tuesday, medics and police said.

Israeli strikes continue through ceasefire
An October 2025 US-backed ceasefire halted major fighting in the shattered territory, but has not stopped Israeli attacks on Gaza or secured Hamas' disarmament.
Israel, which accuses Hamas of trying to regroup and rearm, has stepped up its attacks in Gaza in recent months. Hamas denies the Israeli allegations, saying it is committed to the ceasefire agreement and accusing Israel of trying to derail it.
At least 1,600 Palestinians and five Israeli soldiers have been killed since then, according to respective Palestinian and Israeli tallies.
Israeli troops now control around two-thirds of Gaza, up from more than half at the time of the ceasefire, with Hamas controlling a sliver of territory along Gaza's coast.
Israel's assault on Gaza has killed more than 74,000 Palestinians since 2023, caused a hunger crisis, internally displaced Gaza's entire population and led an independent United Nations commission as well as scholars to label Israel's actions a genocide.
Israel has maintained that its actions were carried out in self-defence after Hamas-led militants killed 1,200 people and took more than 250 hostages in an attack on Israel in October 2023.
Most of Gaza's population of 2.1 million now live displaced in tents or makeshift shelters in the devastated territory.
