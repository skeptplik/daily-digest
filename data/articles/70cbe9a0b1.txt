Street cricket with a legend then metro ride as William and Kate begin India visit
The Prince and Princess of Wales have played street cricket and taken a trip on the metro during the first day of their three-day visit to India.
In the coming days, the royal couple will be attending events for the Earthshot Prize, which William founded, as well as meeting with Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi.
It marks the first time in four years that William and Catherine have been on an official overseas trip together - following Catherine's treatment for cancer.
The BBC's senior royal correspondent Daniela Relph explains more.
