Millions of us play on consoles, laptops and phones - it's big business. Grand Theft Auto 6 is the latest blockbuster release. But is gaming having an impact on the environment? Our BBC World Service colleagues at The Climate Question have been looking at gaming and the effect it has on the climate. Jordan Dunbar presents the latest edition, and he joins us on Tech Life to talk about the issues. Also in this episode, we hear from the huge Chinese tech company Tencent about their investment plans. And we find out about the specialised computer chip designed to process AI on your devices.
