High voter turnout as first US midterm results test Trump’s grip on Congress
Will Donald Trump become a lame-duck president? With the Republican Party unpopular and the Democrats resurgent, it may happen
This live article is freely available to our registered users. Please log in or create an account.
Americans are awaiting the results of midterm elections that will determine control of Congress and shape the next two years of Donald Trump’s presidency, with consequences for US-China relations, global trade and the war with Iran.
His Republican Party is fighting to retain its majorities in the House of Representatives and Senate as Democrats seek to capitalise on dissatisfaction with Trump’s leadership and the economic fallout from the war.
Democratic control of either chamber would give the opposition greater power to block legislation and scrutinise the administration. Trump would retain significant authority over foreign policy, but face greater congressional resistance to his agenda.
Governorships and state legislatures are also at stake, with implications for how states implement or challenge the administration’s policies.
