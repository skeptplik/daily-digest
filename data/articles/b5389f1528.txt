There's a chill in the air and a snowman (plastic) at the end of the street, meaning winter is firmly upon us. So what better way to make it way through the long dark nights than a spot of festmas gaming. And hark, just in the nick of time, PlayStation Plus' monthly games for December have been revealed, with some skating, spooking, and dragon baubles.
PlayStation Plus subscribers can claim December's gaming trio starting Tuesday, 1st December. They'll remain available to add to your library until early in 2027: Monday, 4th January to be exact. Here's the list in full:
- Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 3+4 - Cross-Gen Edition (PS5, PS4)
- Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2 (PS5, PS4)
- Amnesia: The Bunker (PS4)
Not the most festive bunch, then, but some decent stuff all the same. Amnesia: The Bunker is a real winner if you like it spooky (and The Bunker is very spooky), setting players loose in a labyrinthine underground WW1 bunker where something awful prowls. It's a "truly unsettling adventure" as we wrote in our Amnesia: The Bunker review.
As for the other two, Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 3+4 is well worth your time, taking the beloved early 2000s skating games and giving them a successful modern-day makeover. We called it a "properly glorious collection of two classic games" in our Pro Skater 3+4 review. As for Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2, the critical consensus seems to suggest it is 'okay'.
And that's your lot. It is, though, worth noting that hellos also mean goodbyes, and PlayStation Plus subscribers have until 30th November to claim the current batch of monthly games. And if you need a reminder, they're Dead Island 2, Tomb Raider 4-5 Remastered, and Monster Train 2.
