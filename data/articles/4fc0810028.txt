The clocks have gone back. The mornings are cold and the evenings are dark. The world seems to be doing its level best to discourage any fashion ambition beyond the need to stay alive and warm enough to function.
But clothes – even in winter – are not just about staying alive. They are about staying alive alive. Alive inside. So this month’s edit is about finding the fun in getting dressed when the weather is trying to discourage you. There is a pink jumper that manages to be both cosy and, well, slutty; a £16 henley that looks considerably more expensive than it is; and a skirt I have been stalking since the summer and can finally report back on. There are also ballet pumps with exactly the right amount of grownup elegance, a sweatshirt that makes a persuasive case for elevating the everyday, and a necklace to bring a bit of sparkle back to the proceedings.
An excellent value henley
There is something so classy about a henley. Like, when a character in a film is wearing a henley, you know it’s there to tell you this person has a good soul. When done with finesse – a superfine rib, delicate buttons – there is something quite sensual about it, in an old-timey kind of way. Anyway, this M&S version is amazing value.
A delicious handbag
Polène Cyme bag
Polène handbags are exquisite, which is why there are queues outside the Paris stores when new bags arrive. If you get a chance to swing by the Regent Street store in London, you will see what I mean.
A Christmassy pigeon
Skirts are the new everything
Silk blend lace detail skirt
Wear a skirt. This is not a drill: Miuccia Prada says so. Skirts are so back, baby. I spotted this one, with its snazzy lace insert, back in the summer, when M&S womenswear head Lisa Illis was wearing it in the showroom, but it wasn’t yet on sale. I’ve been stalking it ever since; it’s now arrived, and I can confirm it is excellent.
Everyday elegance, yes please
Gathered back sweatshirt
The White Company strapline is “everyday elegance”, which feels like a policy we can all get behind. It has long been a go-to for chic home bits, but the new-ish clothing director Sheila McKain (ex of Jaeger and Donna Karan) is doing excellent womenswear too. The drawstrings at the waist here are great – I never like how a sweatshirt hem hits at the hip – and the gathering elevates it out of the ordinary.
Over a crew-neck sweater or with an open-neck shirt …
Lapis and blue sapphire necklace
Necklaces are having a moment again. I do find that a lot of pendant necklaces look a bit … babyish? Cutesy? Anyway, this gold-plated one is fab. Here in blues, but also comes in greens (aventurine and onyx) and reds (jasper and garnet).
Not all ballet pumps are created equal
Pointed leather ballet flat
I don’t like ballet pumps with bows on. So much chicer without. Massimo Dutti makes great shoes; I know I am repeating myself here, but I don’t think enough people know about the brand. These have an almond-shaped toe and a high narrow vamp, both of which give an elegant line. Available in black or burgundy.
