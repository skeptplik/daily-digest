A 39-year-old man has been arrested on suspicion of stabbing a 24-year-old woman to death on a street in Tokyo’s Nakano Ward earlier this week, police said.
Kazunori Kiyotake, who was arrested Monday for allegedly stabbing Nanami Aoki, told investigators he had thought of killing her and then himself if she refused to enter into a relationship with him, investigative sources said Wednesday.
Police believe he had developed one-sided romantic feelings for the woman and grew increasingly frustrated when she did not reciprocate.
The sources said Kiyotake of Tokyo’s Nakano Ward first met Aoki of Tokyo’s Suginami Ward four years ago as a customer at a restaurant where she worked, and he continued to frequently visit her even after she began working at another establishment. He said he began going out for meals with her about two years ago.
“I spent about ¥8 million over four years,” he reportedly told investigators. “We were not in a romantic relationship, but I had feelings for her on my own.”
On the day of the incident, Kiyotake allegedly asked Aoki to meet him, saying he wanted to talk. The two had a meal together for about 40 minutes at a restaurant in Nakano.
He is suspected of becoming angry after she reportedly told him that the outing had been boring, and he stabbed her on their way to Nakano Station. Aoki died at a hospital roughly 90 minutes after the incident.
Police are investigating Kiyotake on suspicion of robbery-murder, alleging that he stabbed Aoki in the neck with a kitchen knife, which he is believed to have brought from home, and then fled with her bag.
While fleeing the scene, Kiyotake called emergency services himself and said he had also tried to end his life. However, he had no visible injuries when police apprehended him.
Police officers spotted him near Higashi-Nakano Station as he was walking while talking on the phone.
When he was apprehended, he was carrying a bag believed to have been taken from Aoki and a kitchen knife with an approximately 15-centimeter blade, which appeared to have bloodstains and was kept in a homemade sheath. Police are investigating whether the attack was premeditated.
Kiyotake has admitted to stabbing the woman but partially denied the allegations, saying, “I didn’t steal her bag. I just took it so that someone else wouldn’t steal it,” according to police.
