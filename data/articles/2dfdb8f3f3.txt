Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce are fully in their newlywed era. After getting married in New York’s Madison Square Garden over Fourth of July weekend, the two are getting ready to celebrate their first holiday season as a married couple—and the Kansas City Chiefs tight end is beyond looking forward to it.
“I’m excited for my first holiday as a married man,” Kelce told Women’s Wear Daily. “I love all the old traditions and can’t wait to start some new ones. I start celebrating as that tree goes up.”
In a promotional video for the Tommy Hilfiger winter 2026 campaign, shared on Instagram, Kelce spoke on the personal traditions that make up his “perfect holiday.”
The NFL star said, “My mother, [Donna Kelce], baking blueberry muffins.” He later joked, “You got any Tommy Christmas onesies somewhere?”
Last year, the couple attended a pre-Christmas family party in Kansas City, Missouri, with members of the Kelce family. They then spent the actual Christmas Day at Arrowhead Stadium, where Kelce and his Chiefs played the Denver Broncos. The pop superstar’s parents, Scott and Andrea Swift, joined her for the game to make it a full family affair. And after the game, Swift and Kelce traveled to the mountains for a cozy winter getaway. Who knows what they have planned for this year, now that they are officially Mr. and Mrs. Swift-Kelce.
While the “Opalite” singer has always maintained that her favorite holiday is Fourth of July, it seems the Swift-Kelces’s holiday of choice may now be Christmas.
