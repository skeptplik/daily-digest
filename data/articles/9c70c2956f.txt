Is Levi Alves McConaughey Hollywood’s next rising star to know? Matthew McConaughey and Camila Alves’s eldest son turned 18 earlier this year and seems to be following in his father’s footsteps, gradually stepping into his own spotlight.
Over the years, Levi has joined his famous parents on red carpets and at industry events, most recently at last night’s Airbnb.Org Gala in Los Angeles, where he even had his own solo moment on the red carpet. With two acting credits, a runway debut, and several Fashion Week appearances under his belt, he’s beginning to build a public persona of his own. And in the world of celebrity offspring, all signs certainly point to a career in the making for Levi.
Here’s everything to know about the young McConaughey, who’s just getting started.
He was born in 2008.
Matthew McConaughey and Camila Alves McConaughey welcomed their first child, Levi Alves McConaughey, on July 7, 2008. He’s the eldest of the Hollywood couple’s three children. They also share a daughter, Vida (born in 2010), and a son, Livingston (born in 2012).
As a kid, he worked small jobs on set with his dad.
Over the years, Matthew has spoken about the importance of instilling a strong work ethic in his three children. During a 2020 appearance at The History Channel’s HISTORYTalks, the actor called his kids his “living legacy” and said he taught them the value of hard work by giving them small jobs on his sets.
“I have my kids come out, we put them to work,” the Dazed and Confused star said. “We give them a little cash for catering.”
Levi has also joined his parents in giving back. In 2023, Alves brought Levi and daughter Vida to Refettorio Paris, where they helped cook and serve meals to people in need. Levi later reflected on the experience in an Instagram post, writing, “I got the chance to cook at @refettorioparis for some people that don’t normally get to have a really nice meal. Refettorio Paris serves first class meals to the homeless. They made a great restaurant with high-end chefs to cook—using produce that was going to be thrown away.”
He added, “It was really cool to serve some people that don’t normally get to have it.”
He made his acting debut alongside his dad.
Levi made his acting debut in the 2025 film The Lost Bus, starring opposite his father. The up-and-coming actor plays Shaun McKay, the son of Kevin McKay, who is portrayed by his real-life dad.
But as he tried out for the role. Levi didn’t want his famous last name to give him a leg up. In an interview with People, the father-son duo revealed that Levi auditioned for the role anonymously, with his last name removed so he could earn the part all on his own.
“Can you pull the last name?” Matthew asked the film’s casting director. “She goes, ‘Yeah.’ And I got a call back and she said, ‘Our director Paul Greengrass said, ‘That’s the kid. That’s the son, Levi.’ And then she goes, and ‘I told him that’s actually Matthew’s son in real life.’”
For Levi, the role was an opportunity to explore his interest in acting. “This role wasn’t me going, ‘Oh, I’m going to become an actor,’ It was me saying, ‘I’m really interested in this.’”
He’s stepped into the fashion world.
Levi has entered the fashion scene on a few occasions, including sitting front row at the Dior Homme Spring 2026 show during Paris Fashion Week.
He also made his official catwalk debut during Milan Fashion Week this season, walking the Vogue World runway.
He’s reflected on his parents and potential fame.
In 2025, Levi opened up to Interview magazine about his mom, dad, and budding Hollywood career. The teen shared why he idolizes both of his parents: “My dad for the man he is, because of the work he has been able to create, and how he’s handled it all,” he said. “My mom for the woman she is, her patience, and her want to help others.”
When asked if he wanted to be famous, he said, “Famous in a lasting way. I want to continue to build on a legacy that I have the privilege to carry on.”
