3 hikers injured after being hit by landing paraglider in Hong Kong country park
A 42-year-old woman, her son, 14, and another boy, 15, were transported via helicopter to Ruttonjee Hospital in Wan Chai for treatment
Three hikers have been injured in Hong Kong after being hit by a paraglider landing at the Ngong Ping campsite in Ma On Shan Country Park.
Police received a report at 12.36pm on Sunday that three people, including two teenagers, had been injured at the campsite after being hit by a landing paraglider.
The Government Flying Service deployed a helicopter to transport the trio to Ruttonjee Hospital in Wan Chai for treatment.
