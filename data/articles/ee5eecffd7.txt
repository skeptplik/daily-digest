France vows tough response to freezer cyberattack blamed on Iran
Issued on:
Share
France's Foreign Minister Jean-Noel Barrot said Tuesday, November 10, that Paris ruled out "nothing" to respond to a major cyberattack on hundreds of supermarket fridges in October that it has blamed on Iran. The goal, Jean-Noel Barrot told France Info, is "to deter anyone who might be tempted, in Iran or elsewhere, to do it again".
