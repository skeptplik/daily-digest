It’s an instantly recognized vignette: the San Jacinto Mountains rising in the distance, palm trees in the foreground, and a sea of mid-century modern homes dotted along the valley floor. Palm Springs is unmistakable, and its charms undeniable. It’s no wonder the glamorous desert destination has been a favored escape for Hollywood royalty for nearly a century. Beyond its numerous famous admirers (a roster that includes the likes of Elvis Presley, Lucille Ball, and Frank Sinatra) Palm Springs is also home to world-famous events, including the Coachella Music & Arts Festival, Modernism Week, and the city’s namesake International Film Festival.
