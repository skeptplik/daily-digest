Demerit system for contractors ‘not ruled out’, Hong Kong development chief says
Government also considering new conditions for homeowners under subsidy scheme, including having a long-term maintenance plan and funds for it
Hong Kong authorities have not ruled out introducing a demerit points system for contractors, the development chief has said, after an independent inquiry into last year’s deadly Tai Po fire identified unscrupulous consultants and building professionals as the main culprits of the tragedy.
Secretary for Development Bernadette Linn Hon-ho said on Sunday that the government was also considering introducing new conditions for homeowners who applied for its building maintenance subsidy scheme, including a long-term plan and fund for property maintenance.
“I do not rule out the possibility of introducing a demerit points system in the next stage if we can formalise the mechanism further. However, we have not considered it at this stage,” Linn said in a television interview.
