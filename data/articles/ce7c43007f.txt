A Russian military transport plane crashed in western Siberia on Tuesday (November 17, 2026), killing all 19 people on board, the Defence Ministry said. Preliminary findings pointed to equipment failure.
The An-12 aircraft went down in an unpopulated area near the city of Kemerovo, about 3,000 kilometres (about 1,860 miles) east of Moscow. Air force experts were investigating the cause of the crash. The An-12 typically has a crew of six.
Kemerovo Gov. Ilya Serdyuk said the plane crashed near the village of Yagunovo, which is close to Kemerovo airport. “Emergency responders put out a fire on the site of the crash,” he said.
The Investigative Committee, the country’s top criminal investigation agency, launched an inquiry into possible violations of flight safety rules, a standard practice following crashes.
The An-12 is a Soviet-built four-engine turboprop transport plane that has been in service since the late 1950s. Over 1,000 were built and some still remain in operation in Russia and other nations.
