Black Friday is one of the best times of year for those looking to upgrade their fall and winter wardrobes—especially if you’re in the market for a new bag. Today, retailers including Nordstrom, Amazon, and Net-a-Porter are offering dozens of markdowns on sought-after styles from Staud, Polo Ralph Lauren, and more.
Below, find all the best Black Friday handbag deals from Bazaar-loved labels. Whether you’re looking to pick up a sleek new shoulder bag from celebrity-favorite Freja or invest in a new office tote from Toteme, act with urgency before they’re no longer in stock.
Tory Burch
Nordstrom and Amazon both have massive Black Friday sales, making them our top destinations for Tory Burch bags. Staples like the Romy hobo and Perry tote fit all your work essentials if you’re looking for a new commuter-friendly bag, while the Kira and Perra shoulder bags can be worn with all your holiday looks (and make excellent gifts for a loved one too).
Coach
Whether shopping for a tote bag or a petite clutch, Coach stands out for its timeless designs and celebrity-loved silhouettes. Shop markdowns up to 30 percent at Coach’s website or at Nordstrom, including fan favorites like the Empire Carryall or the Kisslock barrel bag.
Staud
Staud fans have plenty of markdowns to choose from at Nordstrom and Fwrd, including signature silhouettes like the Maude and Harlow.
DeMellier
DeMellier’s sitewide Black Friday sale is here, with 20 percent off sitewide. We’re predicting that celebrity-worn styles like Anne Hathaway’s New York shoulder bag and Kate Middleton’s Nano Montreal bag will be the first to go.
Toteme
Net-a-Porter is the best place to shop for Black Friday deals on Toteme bags—but at 40 percent off, stock will go fast. Signature silhouettes like the belted bucket bag and oversize totes are part of the offerings. And those needing an elegant party bag should pick up the cocktail shoulder bag.
Polo Ralph Lauren
Polo Ralph Lauren bags are always timeless. This Black Friday, shop Neiman Marcus’s selection of markdowns on fall-ready suede styles like the popular Polo ID or Polo Play bags.
Verafied
Looking for a new handbag label to obsess over? Verafied’s current markdowns will quickly convert you to a fan. Shop the editor-loved Club Bag in suede for under $300.
Freja
Freja’s sleek handbags have been worn by Hailey Bieber, Katie Holmes, and Jennifer Lawrence, making them the ultimate fashion gift for the celebrity-obsessed. The New York–based brand is currently offering holiday shoppers 25 percent off sitewide.
Marc Jacobs
Marc Jacobs is another smart choice for gift shoppers. The beloved New York designer is offering steep markdowns at Nordstrom on totes and shoulder bags that make a great first designer bag for a stylish loved one.
