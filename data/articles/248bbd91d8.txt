Hims and Noom take peptides further into mainstream
Hims & Hers and Noom are getting into the synthetic peptide business, bringing treatments marketed for muscle maintenance, intimacy, and longevity to a much larger audience — often with little evidence to support those uses. Both are already offering compounded drugs and eyeing BPC-157 and TB-500, two of six unapproved peptides covered by a new HHS policy easing enforcement against compounding pharmacies, STAT’s Sarah Todd and Katie Palmer write.
