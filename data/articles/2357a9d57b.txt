Why Malaysia may still face trade uncertainty after US midterms
The Democrats may face stiff opposition from Trump over his tariffs and scrutinise China’s trade partners such as Malaysia, analysts say
Trump’s Republicans suffered their worst rout in recent times in the US midterm elections on Tuesday, losing control of both Congress and the Senate by wide margins as voters made clear their anger at Trump over surging living costs caused by the Iran war.
The results are expected to force the president to go on the defensive and double down on his controversial tariff regime, with little indication that a Democratic Congress would be of much help to smaller trade partners such as Malaysia, according to experts.
