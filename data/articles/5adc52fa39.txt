Sonos speakers and soundbars aren’t exactly cheap, but thankfully the company’s Black Friday sale has already begun, making everything a little easier to afford through November 30th. Most notably, you can pick up the Arc Ultra soundbar for a new low of $849 ($250 off) from Sonos, Amazon, and Best Buy, or the newfangled Sonos Play for $239 ($60 off) from the same retailers (Sonos, Amazon, Best Buy).
Sonos is taking up to 30 percent off speakers and soundbars right now
Whether you want a portable speaker or a new home theater setup, Sonos is offering great deals through November 30th.
If you’re looking to give your home theater setup a big upgrade, the Arc Ultra is an excellent soundbar. It supports Dolby Atmos and delivers more immersive sound than the original Arc, while adding Bluetooth connectivity. Dialogue also sounds noticeably clearer, and the new Quick Tune feature automatically adjusts the soundbar’s audio to suit your room using built-in microphones, so you no longer need an iPhone to fine-tune the sound for your environment. For more powerful bass, you can also pair it with a compatible Sonos subwoofer, like the Sub 4, which is also available from Sonos as part of a discounted bundle for $1,499 ($499 off).
If you’re looking for a more affordable soundbar, the second-gen Sonos Beam has also hit a new low price at Amazon, Best Buy, and Sonos’ online storefront, where you can buy it for $339 ($160 off). The sound isn’t quite as immersive as that of the Arc Ultra, but the latest Beam can still fill a room with rich, clear audio. It also supports Dolby Atmos; however, unlike the newer Beam Ultra, it lacks side- and upward-firing speakers and instead relies on software to replicate surround sound effects. It also supports HDMI eARC, providing high-quality audio from a compatible TV.
If you’re shopping for a smart speaker instead of a soundbar, the Era 100 SL is available for an all-time low of $149 ($40 off) from Sonos, Amazon, and Best Buy (the standard Era 100 is also down to $179 at Amazon and Best Buy). The feature-packed speakers are nearly identical, but the SL model lacks a built-in mic, and thus voice controls. Both offer excellent stereo sound and rich bass, with dual tweeters and a large woofer that delivers clear, detailed audio despite its small size. They also support Bluetooth and Apple’s AirPlay 2, and you can connect a wired audio source like a turntable using an optional USB-C adapter. For a more immersive experience, you can even pair two for stereo sound or use them as rear surrounds with a compatible Sonos soundbar.
If you want a speaker you can take on the go, the newer Sonos Play is available from Sonos, Amazon, and Best Buy for $239 ($60 off), which is the first notable discount we’ve seen. The portable speaker delivers clear, detailed sound that can fill both larger rooms and outdoor spaces. It also offers up to 24 hours of battery life and an IP67 rating for water and dust resistance, making it a great companion for long days at the beach or other outdoor adventures. Plus, its removable carrying loop makes it easy to bring along, and you can even use it to top off your phone. Like other Sonos speakers, it supports both Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, and you can also group it with other Sonos speakers over Bluetooth when you’re outdoors and away from Wi-Fi.
Last but not least, you can also buy the Sonos Ace for $229 ($120 off) at Amazon, Best Buy, and directly from Sonos, which marks a new low price. The newer Ace Ultra offer better sound and improved noise cancellation, but the Ace is still a solid option, especially if you already own a Sonos soundbar. The gorgeous headphones deliver powerful, detailed sound, solid noise cancellation, and an exceptionally natural-sounding transparency mode, along with up to 30 hours of continuous playback on a single charge. Unlike the newer Ultra, though, they can’t transfer music from your other Sonos speakers to your headphones. However, their TV Audio Swap feature still lets you transfer audio from a compatible Sonos soundbar to your headphones, so you can watch movies or listen to music playing on your TV without disturbing anyone else.
