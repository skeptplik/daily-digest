Minjee Lee set to miss LPGA Tour Championship, ending nightmare season early
In short:
Minjee Lee is set to miss the LPGA Tour Championship for the first time since 2014 after a horror season.
Lee's world ranking has dropped from third to 24th, her equal-lowest standing in 11 years.
What's next?
Cassie Porter, Grace Kim, Karis Davidson and Robyn Choi are all hoping to join Hannah Green at the Tour Championship in Naples from November 19-22.
