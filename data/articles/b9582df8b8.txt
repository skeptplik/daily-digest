Fri, 11 Sept, 7:50 pm, Final score, Rabbitohs 10, Knights 20
Reece Walsh disappointed some Australian fans wanted Kangaroos to lose World Cup final
In short:
Reece Walsh wants to see more support for Australia at the international level after some fans wanted the Kangaroos to lose the World Cup final.
Walsh defended team culture after starring in the 32-12 win over New Zealand.
He was disappointed after seeing some fans say, "I hope Australia loses just to make the game better."
