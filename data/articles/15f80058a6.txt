Former Test player Chadd Sayers says 'everyone knew' about sandpaper, David Warner doubles down on Cricket Australia
In short:
Chadd Sayers, who was part of the 2018 touring squad, said he was comfortable in claiming all members of the team knew sandpaper was being used on the ball.
David Warner has doubled down on social media, claiming he was not giving all the information before taking the brunt of the punishment for Sandpapergate.
Usman Khawaja sent the cricket world into a spin last week when he claimed in his memoir, Sabr, that the ball-tampering scandal in Cape Town in 2018 was everyone in the team's responsibility.
