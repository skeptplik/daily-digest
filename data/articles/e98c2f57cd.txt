On Saturday night, Kim Kardashian showed up the Las Vegas Grand Prix in Paradise, Nevada to support her Formula 1 driver boyfriend Lewis Hamilton. The entrepreneur and reality star arrived in style, appearing in two different leather ensembles throughout the day.
Kardashian’s first look was a custom red leather look from Chrome Hearts. She wore a zip-up form-fitted jacket with a popped collar and plunging v-shaped neckline paired with flared leather pants in a matching shade of red with four silver buttons prominently displayed. Kardashian accessorized with a matching red handbag and a pair of oversized sunglasses and wore burgundy pumps that paired well with the outfit’s bolder, brighter color.
Later that day, Kardashian switched into a similar but darker outfit, a black leather ensemble with a top nearly identical to the red version worn earlier that day. The pants deviated slightly, these one straight-legged in almost leggings-like style, and worn with black pumps.
Kardashian has been dating Hamilton since this last February, when the pair were seen out and about in Paris together. They’ve been making public appearances ever since, with Kardashian frequently showing up for Hamilton’s races in fashionable outfits. This past October, for instance, Kardashian attended the Singapore Grand Prix wearing a dusty pink vintage Roberto Cavalli maxidress with cap sleeves, a plunging neckline, and detailed oil painting-like patterns of peacocks and flowers. She paired the dress with a semi-casual pair of strappy Manolo Blahnik sandals.
The Las Vegas Grand Prix is a motor racing event that takes place each year in a temporarily constructed racing circuit that includes portions of the famous Las Vegas Strip. The event tends to attract high-profile attendees, with the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix attended by the likes of Beyoncé, Jay-Z, Keanu Reeves, and Chris Pratt. Kardashian was seen hanging around with costar Teyana Taylor and “Vampire Diaries” star Nina Dobrev. This year, Hamilton placed seventh in the race and was also involved in a crash with racer Charles Leclerc. Dutch racer Max Verstappen came in first place, followed by Lando Norris in second and Oscar Piastri in third.
