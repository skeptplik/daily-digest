More than two years have passed since Biogen announced results from a Phase 3 study in lupus. The drug was dapirolizumab and the outcome was positive, in case you were wondering. In the first half of December, Biogen will hope for a repeat, and once again, it’s another lupus drug, litifilimab, that is being evaluated in twin Phase 3 studies.
The looming readout from the TOPAZ-1 and TOPAZ-2 studies is an important test of the R&D revamp and growth strategy that Biogen CEO Chris Viehbacher implemented when he joined the company in 2022. It’s also risky.
