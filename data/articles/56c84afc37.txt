Shopping in a cost of living crisis means more of us are looking for bargains. But ahead of Black Friday and the Christmas shopping rush, experts are warning consumers to be cautious. Tests on fake make-up, perfumes and skin creams have been shown to contain dangerous substances such as mercury and nicotine. Laura Foster speaks to toxicologist Rani Ghosh and Helen Barnham from the Intellectual Property Office about the health risks linked to fake cosmetics including blistering, blindness and kidney damage. After research by the University of Cambridge and University of East Anglia suggested females with autoimmune diseases are less likely than men to be believed, more and more women have been coming forward to share their experiences. We hear from Beth Smith from East Yorkshire about her long, diagnostic journey and speak to the research lead, Dr Melanie Sloan, about what can be done to stop the gender bias and reduce the likelihood of anyone being misdiagnosed, no matter their gender. Plus, BBC Radio 4 presenter Claudia Hammond pops in to talk about this year’s All in the Mind awards which celebrate people helping others with their mental health. She also shares some of the tips she’s learned from previous winners on how best to support people who are struggling.
