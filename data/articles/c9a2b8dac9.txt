Armaf, the Dubai-based fragrance brand, has partnered with Brazilian soccer player Neymar Jr. in its efforts to tap into sport and contemporary culture.
The company framed the deal as a meeting of “sport, style and fragrance,” and the plan is to release a new fragrance together next year. Armaf did not disclose financial terms of the deal.
“Neymar Jr. is a true icon, and his confidence and style embody what Armaf stands for,” a brand spokesperson said. “This is a significant moment for Middle Eastern and Dubai perfumery. He brings the style, we bring the excellence, and together we intend to lead the way. This is only the beginning, with more exciting developments underway.”
The spokesperson added that Neymar Jr.’s influence extends “well beyond football, reaching into fashion, entertainment and contemporary culture. His creativity and self-expression align closely with Armaf’s philosophy of creating fragrances that embody personality and distinction.”
Founded in Dubai, Armaf has a presence in 140 countries, and said it sells one fragrance every second.
Positioned mainly in the accessible luxury space, the brand has made a name for itself as one of the top-selling Middle Eastern fragrance brands on Amazon and TikTok Shop, with lines including Club de Nuit and Odyssey.
With more than 240 million Instagram followers, the soccer player, whose full name is Neymar da Silva Santos Jr., is no stranger to the world of fragrances.
In 2019, he teamed with Diesel to co-create a men’s scent, Spirit of the Brave, and added Spirit of the Brave Intense the following year.
Neymar Jr. plays as an attacking midfielder and a forward for the Campeonato Brasileiro Série A club Santos, which he also captains. He has also played for Barcelona, Paris Saint-Germain, Al Hilal and Brazil’s national football team.
He is the son of Neymar Santos Sr., a former soccer player who now serves as his son’s commercial agent, business manager, and advisor.
