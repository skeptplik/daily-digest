Australia's Cricket World Cup preparations for 2027 to be affected by nine-month gap between games
In short:
A heavy Test schedule and the IPL will mean that Australia will have just six games to prepare between the end of the England series and the Cricket World Cup.
Australia's next ODI after the England series will be games against New Zealand in August 2027.
Then three warm-up games against Zimbabwe will be all the team has before the start of the World Cup in October.
