Warner backs Broad's comments that Lehmann 'got away lightly' over Sandpapergate
In short:
David Warner said he agreed with comments that former match referee Chris Broad made regarding Darren Lehmann.
Broad suggested the former Australian coach got away lightly in the wake of the Sandpapergate scandal.
Lehmann has continuously denied he knew about the use of sandpaper on the ball during the 2018 South Africa tour.
