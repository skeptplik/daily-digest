Amazon has kicked off its latest '3 for 2' sale on books ahead of Black Friday and there are some terrific new releases in there for LitRPG fans. If you haven't already scooped up the Dungeon Crawler Carl books, for example, now is a great time to do so for cheap. And if you're a fan of Travis Deverell's (AKA Shirtaloon) He Who Fights With Monsters series, you can also grab the first two hardcovers at a discount.
The first He Who Fights With Monsters hardcover was released earlier this year and the third book will be arriving next week. This series is my overall favorite LitRPG and a great read if you love Dungeon Crawler Carl.
He Who Fights With Monsters is fairly well-known in the LitRPG community already. There have been a total of 13 books released so far and they are all terrific. Similar to Dungeon Crawler Carl, there's a lot of humor and pop-culture references sprinkled throughout a relatively fast-paced story. It definitely leans more fantasy than sci-fi, so expect a lot more magic and a less actual science.
The story follows Jason Asano as he wakes up inside a hedge maze in a magical world with new powers and absolutely no hair on his body. His determined and off-balance attitude to his strange new predicament helps him to survive the likes of cannibals, monsters, and even personal relationships with ease. All-in-all, a fantastic book with a morally conflicted main character that you can’t help but love the whole way through. His dark and mysterious powers only make the story all the more interesting.
Also check out the Dungeon Crawler Carl books in the sale
Seeing as there are only two He Who Fights With Monsters books included in the sale, you will have to purchase one more eligible book to actually get the discount. I'd recommend checking out the Dungeon Crawler Carl hardcovers and the graphic novels, both of which are also buy 2, get 1 free. The first graphic novel is an easy recommendation if you already own these books, I gave it an 8 out of 10 in my review.
