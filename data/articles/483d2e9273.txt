NEW YORK — If you’re blind, Bill Ackman wants to help you see again.
That is the ambitious plan the billionaire is pursuing with the Ackman Oxman Institute, a new institution in New York City that will act as a hub for brain injury research, including brain-computer interfaces that can help people move, speak, and connect to digital devices through brain activity.
It’s a new dream with a tragic backstory.
