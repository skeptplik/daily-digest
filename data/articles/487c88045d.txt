Former Australian cricket coach Darren Lehmann has once again denied knowledge of a plan to use sandpaper in the Cape Town Test against South Africa in 2018.
Speaking on ABC Radio, Lehmann apologised for his role in the team's culture at the time, admitting that the team's attempts to find reverse swing "went too far".
"No. I didn't know sandpaper was being used," he said when asked of his knowledge.
"I was coach at the time, I've got to take that responsibility. It was the best job in the world.
"I'm sorry for what happened. It's my fault it went too far."
