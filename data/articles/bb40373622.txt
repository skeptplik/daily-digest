Sat, 7 Nov, 1:05 pm, Final score, Cats 52, Demons 56
The AFLW is on the cusp of history as an all-conquering North Melbourne side looks to claim its third consecutive premiership in this year's grand final.
Standing in the Kangaroos' way will be Melbourne, a team that has been one of the most consistent over the last couple of years.
Here is everything you need to know ahead of tonight's decider between the Roos and Dees.

Where is the AFLW grand final being played?
This year's AFLW grand final will once again be hosted by Princes Park in Melbourne.
Princes Park hosted the last three AFLW grand finals and also the 2018 decider between the Western Bulldogs and Brisbane.
Where can I watch the AFLW grand final?
Fans can watch the game on free-to-air TV on Channel 7 as well as its streaming services.
Kayo Sports will also broadcast the grand final.
ABC Sport will run a live blog where you can follow all the action and analysis, while you can also listen to the match live on ABC Sport via the website, local radio or the Listen app.

Who are the key players for each team?
North Melbourne will once again rely on its star duo of captain Jasmine Garner and midfield maestro Ash Riddell.
Riddell led the league in disposals per game with 33.8, while Garner added 30.1 touches herself, with both players making 14 appearances this season.
Melbourne's back line will also have to be wary of Kangaroos forward Tahlia Randall, who has slammed home a team-high 27 goals this season, while Garner has also hit the scoreboard with 14 goals.
The Demons also have their own dynamic duo in the midfield, with Olivia Purcell's 30.8 touches per game good for fourth in the league, while Tyla Hanks has added 25.5 touches of her own.
Mia Austin is Melbourne's leading goalkicker this season with 17 goals in just eight appearances, while skipper Kate Hore has kicked 10 goals and averaged 19.1 touches per game.
Who are the coaches for the AFLW grand finalists
North Melbourne is still coached by Darren Crocker, who has led the team to the last two AFLW premierships.
Crocker, who played 165 games for North Melbourne's men's team, has coached the women's side since 2021.
Before his AFLW coaching career, Crocker led the Kangaroos men's side as caretaker coach in 15 matches during three separate seasons.
Melbourne is coached by Tom Wilson, who is in his first season in charge of the Demons after taking over from the club's inaugural AFLW coach Mick Stinear.
Wilson joined the Demons after spending two seasons with Collingwood as the club's midfield coach and head of development.
He played 176 games for the Northern Blues/Bullants and Collingwood's reserves team between 2011 and 2025.

How many AFLW premierships have these teams won?
North Melbourne has won two premierships over the last two years and hopes to be the first team in AFLW history to claim three straight wins.
This grand final appearance is North Melbourne's fourth, with the previous three coming over the last three seasons.
Melbourne has won one AFLW premiership during S7 (2022).
The Demons were runners-up during S6 (2021) and are making their third grand final appearance.
Who is doing the pre-match entertainment?
The Jungle Giants, an indie rock band from Brisbane, will headline this year's AFLW grand final entertainment.
The group's four members are Sam Hales, Cesira Aitken, Andrew Dooris and Keelan Bijker.
Their opening act will be Super Disco Club, who will also play music during breaks in play.
Kate Miller-Heidke will sing the national anthem.
