DR Congo's Tshisekedi signs referendum law amid fears of third-term bid
Issued on:
Share
DR Congo President Félix Tshisekedi paved the way for a constitutional amendment on November 10 by signing a law authorising a referendum. The move comes amid concerns that he could seek a third term in office, with opposition figures accusing him of manoeuvring to remain in power.
