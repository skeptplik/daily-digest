Thanksgiving calls for comfort, but it’s also an opportunity to show off your fashion prowess to relatives you haven’t seen all year. Focus your sartorial efforts from the waist up. Ruffled skirts and statement shoes won’t earn compliments while seated, but artful jewelry pieces, interesting layers, and trending prints certainly will.
The 12 Thanksgiving outfits below will fast-track you to the evening’s best-dressed list, whether you’re hosting a casual get-together or attending a more formal affair.
Comfort in Disguise
Prioritizing comfort doesn’t have to be overt. This printed wrap dress from the Senegalese brand Diarrablu will earn you compliments all night, while allowing for subtle fit adjustments throughout the evening.
Elevated Casual
A casual Thanksgiving gathering is the perfect place to test-drive this season’s sweatpant-jeans hybrids. Elevate your laid-back bottoms with a buttery cashmere knit and Tory Burch’s new Bunny Knot bag, and no one will be able to tell the difference.
Effortless Olive
A tonal Thanksgiving outfit in fall’s trending olive green reads sophisticated, without the added effort. Chestnut brown bags and boots make a reliable pairing for olive colored skirts and dresses. Complete your look with classic stud earrings, which will serve you well during the holiday party season.
Merlot Monochrome
Deep wine-red tones work especially well for holiday gatherings. Add interest with on-trend details like croc embossing and fringe trims. Then, top your monochrome burgundy look with a classic tan Burberry coat.
Kitchen-Conscious
Draped sleeves and flowy skirts can prove a hassle while cooking, so consider your pre-dinner tasks when choosing an outfit. Stick with comfortable pieces that allow you to move around easily. This playful LaDoubleJ apron is worth planning your look around if you’re fully in charge of this year’s meal.
Modern Family Matching
Hill House Home’s range of coordinating dresses is perfect for those hoping to snap Christmas card photos while waiting for the turkey to cook. Minimalist shoulder bags, flats, and hoop earrings give your holiday outfits timeless appeal.
Temperature-Savvy
A wisely layered look will keep you at a comfortable temperature while prepping side dishes over a hot stove, then easily transition to watching a quick game of flag football in the backyard. An androgynous button-up can peek out from under J.Crew’s retro Rollneck Sweater in the morning, then act as a solo layer on a warm afternoon.
Informal Elegance
Even the most casual gathering can be a stylish affair. Éterne’s cashmere sets are just as comfortable as any sweatsuit, but look much more put-together when paired with luxe accessories, like suede flats and a tennis necklace.
Autumnal Plaid
Here’s one way to master plaid for fall. Style a checked button-up with coordinating trousers and keep the rest of the look comfortable and chic with elevated basics and a cozy scarf coat.
Sophisticated Brown Suede
A sleek charcoal-grey is an unexpected complement to richly textured brown suede jackets, boots, and bags. Mixing multiple mocha tones makes this trending fabrication feel even more elegant.
Festive Accessories
If you’re feeling extra festive this year, consider accessorizing with a statement necklace, such as this sculptural Sézane necklace. It may help fill awkward silences and give you and your mother-in-law plenty to talk about while preparing this year’s turkey.
Corduroy Textures
Employ corduroy textures for a seasonal take on a Canadian tuxedo. Then, complete the autumnal palette with dark-wash denim, saddle leather, and tan suede. Those Frame Swing Trousers will be a useful addition to your work wardrobe, which you can style in a multitude of ways throughout fall and winter.
