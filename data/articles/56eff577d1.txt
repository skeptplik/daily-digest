The white truffles sparking bidding frenzies in northern Italy

In northern Italy, the white truffle capital of the world, the small round fungi are selling at auction for tens of thousands of euros. And as the climate changes, truffles are becoming increasingly rare, with less supply and ever more demand pushing up prices even further.
