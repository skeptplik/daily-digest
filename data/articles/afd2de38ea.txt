Last night, The Game Awards 2026 nominations were officially announced, and fans immediately noticed the prestigious Game of the Year category - previously won by the likes of Astro Bot, Clair Obscur, and Elden Ring - does not feature a PlayStation-published game this year. It's a rare sight for PlayStation Studios, which has secured a Game of the Year nomination every year since 2015.
PlayStation has published several games this year that might usually be shoe-ins for a Game of the Year nomination. Marvel's Wolverine is the company's highest profile first-party release in 2026, but it's also responsible for this year's Saros, as well as the externally-developed but PlayStation-published Marvel Tōkon: Fighting Souls.
Despite missing out on the Game of the Year slot, however, PlayStation still features in other categories. All three of the aforementioned games are up for various, non-GOTY awards, while Marathon is nominated for Best Multiplayer, and the upcoming God of War Laufey for Most Anticipated. Here's all of PlayStation's 2026 nominations:
- Marvel's Wolverine: Best Performance - Liam McIntyre, Innovation in Accessibility.
- Saros: Best Performance - Rahul Kohli, Best Action Game.
- Marathon: Best Multiplayer Game.
- Marvel Tōkon: Fighting Souls: Best Fighting Game.
- God of War Laufey: Most Anticipated Game.
And if you're wondering, here are the 13 Game of the Year nominations PlayStation has secured over the last 11 years:
- 2015: Bloodborne.
- 2016: Uncharted 4: A Thief's End.
- 2017: Horizon Zero Dawn.
- 2018: God of War, Marvel's Spider-Man.
- 2019: Death Stranding
- 2020: The Last of Us Part 2, Ghost of Tsushima.
- 2021: Ratchet & Clank: Rift Apart.
- 2022: God of War Ragnarök, Horizon Forbidden West.
- 2023: Marvel's Spider-Man 2.
- 2024: Astro Bot.
- 2025: Death Stranding 2: On The Beach.
2027's PlayStation slate of games might fare better at next year's The Game Awards, what with a new entry in the often-nominated God of War series in Laufey, and potentially Naughty Dog's Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet.
