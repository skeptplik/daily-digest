Abby Choi killed by ex-husband, his brother tells Hong Kong court
Slain socialite’s ex-brother-in-law says he knew Alex Kwong fatally attacked her, but denies the killing was planned
Slain Hong Kong socialite Abby Choi Tin-fung was killed by her ex-husband, his elder brother has told a court.
In a tearful confession in the High Court on Wednesday, Anthony Kwong Kong-kit said he knew his younger brother, Alex Kwong Kong-chi, had fatally attacked Choi on February 21, 2023, but stressed that there had been no plan to kill her beforehand.
Anthony, 35, is contesting a joint count of murder alongside Alex, 31, and their father, Kwong Kau, 68, who are accused of conspiring to kill the model.
