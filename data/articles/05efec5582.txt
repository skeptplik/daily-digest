There are two sorts of Parisian holidays. The first, an exercise in blissful escapism, where luxury hotels, gallery hopping, and lazy apéros à la terrasse all help you lean into the romantic undercurrent of the city. The second, an exercise in make-believe: You would not be the first person to plot a move to Paris halfway through your stay there, earmarking local boulangeries and wine shops as favorites and spending mornings chit-chatting with market vendors. If you lean toward the latter experience, this is where the best Airbnbs in Paris enter the picture. After all, what better way to cosplay a Parisienne than via a cozy flat of your own (albeit a temporary one)?

Our edit is an evolving one—there’s no shortage of stately Haussmann buildings available for rent—grouped by arrondissement. (There are 20 total, and while we haven’t hit on all of them, the below list includes several of the most popular for visitors.) Each spot has its own distinct flair, depending on what sort of Parisian you wish to embody. The chichi traveler with high-brow tastes will love the classic charm of the Left Bank; the arty fashion-lover will prefer the cozy studios of the Marais (as well as its proximity to some of the best vintage shops); the low-key bohemian will find respite in the hills of Montmarte, above the city’s lights. Grand architecture and bistro balconies, you’ll be glad to discover, have no loyalty to any particular arrondissement. They’re all good; you really can’t go wrong.
For anyone planning a trip to the City of Light, consider the roundup of properties ahead for a true immersive experience in one of the most stylish cities on the planet.
