Japan’s military build-up hit by US missile delays and domestic parts shortages
Stalled Tomahawk arrivals and low patrol fleet readiness leave Tokyo struggling to realise its deterrence goals as regional threats mount
The issues have cast doubt on Tokyo’s ability to meet ambitious force expansion targets as the government prepares to overhaul its defence and security spending priorities.
Observers say these setbacks highlight the need for Japan to build up its domestic defence industry and reduce reliance on US suppliers. However, persistent maintenance and supply issues affecting Japanese-made aircraft suggest that greater self-reliance brings its own set of challenges.
