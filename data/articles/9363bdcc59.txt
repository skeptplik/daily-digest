The LEGO Black Friday sale is now live and will run through Cyber Monday. As is tradition, LEGO is now taking orders for the exclusive Black Friday set, which just so happens to be Godzilla this year. If you're not trying to queue up to buy that set, though, the LEGO Store also has a number of worthwhile discounts you can check out now.
The overall best deals here are on sets that LEGO is planning on retiring at the end of this year. Once these sets sell out, they aren't going to get restocked, so this is your last chance to buy them at a discount.
LEGO Black Friday Deals on Retiring Sets
It's worth noting that once LEGO officially runs out of a set it has marked as 'retiring soon', you can essentially consider that set to be retired. That being said, you can usually still find those sets at MSRP somewhere else. So it's not unheard to find a discount on a set that is no longer available at the LEGO Store, but it is uncommon. Some of the sets here are available elsewhere for Black Friday, sometime with a better price
As for what retiring sets are worth grabbing before they're gone, it's difficult to recommend anything else above Wicket the Ewok. He's arguably one of the best parts of Return of the Jedi and is just as adorable as you'd expect in LEGO form. Since it is a Star Wars set, it's also likely to appreciate in value after it is officially retired. The Disney Beauty and the Beast Castle set is also a great pick for similar reasons. This is the first time it's ever been discounted and the LEGO Store is already out of stock.
The other set that's likely going to sell out pretty quick is the Volvo L120 Electric Wheel Loader. LEGO construction sets are always some of the most popular builds during these sale times. I actually got sent to a queue when I first tried to click on that page. People just love to build toy versions of heavy machinery.
Last but not least, my personal recommendation is the BrickHeadz Eeyore set. It's currently the only Eeyore build you can buy and it's under $10 right now. There are some rumors swirling that a much larger and more expensive Eeyore set will be arriving next year, but for now, this is worth grabbing at this price. It's the perfect stocking stuffer for any Winnie the Pooh fans out there.
