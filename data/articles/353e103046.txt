Matt Renshaw reveals check-in with Usman Khawaja amid Sandpapergate storm
In short:
Aussie batter Matt Renshaw says he is concerned for the wellbeing of Usman Khawaja amid the Sandpapergate firestorm.
Renshaw revealed he checked in on Khawaja after Pat Cummins rubbished some of Khawaja's claims.
Khawaja has been backed up by Chadd Sayers, who was a member of the 2018 South African touring party.
