“Stay hydrated”, “keep moisturised”, “go for a walk”, “be social”: while these are definitely good things for us to do, they are hardly the top of the list for avoiding winter ailments (Get vaccinated, don’t vape – and moisturise liberally! 10 doctors on how to avoid and treat winter ailments, 18 November). I am glad you have included vaccinations, but the two most useful things you can do don’t get a mention: wear a mask and ensure ventilation.
It’s flu season and airborne viruses such as Covid are still harming people, although as it is hardly ever reported you would not know it. People are being told that “it’s fine”, but as a long-Covid sufferer for 2.5 years, I can tell you this disease is vile: I wouldn’t wish it on my worst enemy, certainly not my loved ones. I am unable to go for a walk, be social or stay active. If people feel ill (even “just a cold”) they should stay at home and avoid spreading their germs to everyone they meet, because they probably won’t know who is vulnerable.
Sally Billenness
Hounslow, London
Caroline Clitter
Retired dental hygienist and dental therapist, Lymington, Hampshire
