Amazon founder Jeff Bezos, who purchased MGM Studios in 2022 and assumed full control over the James Bond 007 franchise last year, shared details on the casting process and how the creative leads, including the director of the next 007 film Denis Villeneuve, ended up choosing The Batman and The Odyssey actor Robert Pattinson as the next James Bond.
Speaking at an event with media company Semafor, Bezos spoke for a couple of minutes on the Bond casting process (hat tip: Steve Jurvetson), saying he was "heavily involved" before detailing how it came down to Pattinson. "We ran a really tight process," he said. "We made the final decision on Friday. We down-selected to five finalists and then we did real old-fashioned screen tests of those five finalists. And it was really interesting. Full costume, real sets, other actors with them. We did three different scenes with each of the five finalists and then we got together on Friday to spend a couple of hours looking at all of the screen tests.
"All of the actors were extraordinarily talented. By the time we got to this list of five, they were all amazing. But there was something special about Rob Pattinson and his performances. And it was immediately undebated. Denis, we all gravitated towards Rob."
Bezos and his team did question it for a bit, though. "Initially, I would say we were slightly biased a little against him just because he's already so famous," he explained. "We sort of thought maybe we should have somebody a little more unknown. But in the screening tests it was so obvious: he is James Bond."
Interestingly, after Bezos asked fans who they'd like to see as the next James Bond last year, Superman actor Henry Cavill won the popular vote. Instead, of course, an actor who has played DC's other ultra-famous superhero got the gig. Unfortunately, though, it stands to reason that Pattinson might not continue as Bruce Wayne and Batman after 2028's The Batman Part II.
It's been a heck of a year for Pattinson, certainly, between starring in The Odyssey, Primetime, The Drama, and next month's Dune: Part 3; filming The Batman Part II; and landing the role of Bond.
