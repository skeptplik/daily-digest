Nintendo Switch 2 Digital Game Deals
What games are on sale? For Nintendo Switch 2, you can save a little on very recent releases, including the phenomenal The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake. It’s only $5 off, but it just came out. I beat it the other day, and I have to say I found playing it to be close to a religious experience. It’s that good. It’s the best-looking game Nintendo has ever released, and the 1998-era puzzle design holds up impossibly well. I loved every second of that game, and I look forward to replaying it in a year or so.
Other Switch 2 digital games on sale include the incredibly fun and destructive Donkey Kong Bananza. The open-world Mario Kart World is on sale, in case you didn’t get it bundled with your Switch 2. And a handful of other worthy releases are also on sale.
Nintendo Switch Digital Game Deals
On the OG Nintendo Switch side of things, you have a number of games to choose from. My top recommendation would be Metroid Dread if you missed it when it originally launched in 2021. It’s getting a sequel in January called Metroid Ravenous, so you have plenty of time to catch up on Samus Aran’s previous adventures.
Other digital Switch games on sale include Kirby and the Forgotten Land, a 3D platformer that’s tons of fun. And then there are two souped-up ports of Wii games, if you can believe it. One is Donkey Kong Country Returns HD, which originally came out in 2010. The other is Xenoblade Chronicles: Definitive Edition, which was first released in the US in 2012. Don’t let the long-in-the-tooth age of those games fool you – they both still rule in 2026.
