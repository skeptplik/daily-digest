Democrats have won control of both chambers of the U.S. Congress in a midterm election landslide that amounted to a rejection of U.S. President Donald Trump following widespread voter discontent with the economy and the war in Iran.
Across the country, Democrats scored victories in Republican strongholds, flipping at least seven Senate seats and winning House races in districts the GOP easily carried in 2024. Several news organizations projected that Democrats would win the House, while Decision Desk HQ projected they would take the Senate.
The Democratic rout sets up the first real check on Trump’s power in Washington since he returned to the White House last year, as the party rode a wave of voter dissatisfaction driven by the cost of living. The incoming Congress is sure to investigate Trump, his administration and his businesses, and stymie his legislative agenda.
