Alleged Islamic State group propagandist Emily Konig on trial in France
Issued on:
Share
Accused of being one of the Islamic State group's most active French propagandists, Emily Konig went on trial in Paris on Thursday. Originally from Brittany, Konig spent a decade in Syria before returning to France in 2022. She is charged with criminal association with a terrorist organisation and faces up to 30 years in prison if convicted. FRANCE 24's Luke Shrago is following the trial in Paris and joins us to explain the defence's strategy.
