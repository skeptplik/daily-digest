Let’s say you’re Jensen Huang. You (allegedly) cheated on your wife with an alleged Chinese spy, allegedly had an affair with not one, but two subordinates, and (allegedly!) “terrorized” your employees, a series of indiscretions that led to your ouster from what was once the world’s most valuable company. (The stock price has recently taken a tumble, for obvious reasons.) And now you’re back, baby — and all your friends are so eager to congratulate you.
Tech leaders are celebrating Jensen Huang’s return
Huang is getting by with a little help from his friends.
Elon Musk, for one, is thrilled.
And why wouldn’t he be? As a fellow leather jacket enthusiast who has a checkered history with women — including those who work for him — it’s clear that he and Huang have a lot in common beyond their positions as tech CEOs. Shivon Zilis, who has two children with Musk, also got in on the fun.
Gavin Baker, founder and chief investment officer of Atreides, is so impressed by Huang’s rise from the ashes that he’s going to buy a leather jacket of his own.
Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeny chimed in with an oblique reference to Huang’s outerwear.
Shopify CEO Tobi Lütke took what one could call a moral stance.
And then there’s billionaire investor Bill Ackman, a real wife guy if there ever was one, but one who, for better or worse, seems to understand that the market comes first.
Sequoia Capital partner Shaun Maguire also pointed out the obvious market-bending powers of Huang’s reinstatement.
Of course, no one has been more effusive than the man who may have orchestrated it all. President Donald Trump posted his lengthy congratulations to Huang on Truth Social. “I have gotten to know Jensen, and he is a Great Patriot and National Treasure,” Trump wrote. He commended Nvidia’s board for making the “correct and proper” decision in reinstating Huang — a decision that, the board admits, was made with input “from senior levels of the United States government,” who determined that “Huang’s reinstatement poses no security risk.” Isn’t that great news?
Trump expects Huang to make him proud. “I think you’re going to see some tremendous numbers out of this company,” Trump said on CNBC. “I talked to him last night. I said, ‘Jensen, just promise me one thing: You’re going to work hard.’ I spoke to him last night because I knew what was going on.” In the same appearance, Trump said “we need to protect our geniuses.”
Marc Andreessen didn’t officially comment on the reinstatement, but the a16z cofounder changed his X bio after the news broke. It now reads: “We have to protect our geniuses.”
Trump also seems to be relishing in Huang’s alleged affairs. In another Truth Social post, he superimposed Huang — red bra in hand — into the iconic photo of his reaction to being shot at a rally in Butler, Pennsylvania.
For Canadian venture capitalist Chamath Palihapitiya, Trump’s intervention is just further proof of Huang’s innocence. Certainly an interesting take!
The market’s reaction may be the most jubilant of all. Per Odd Lots host Joe Weisenthal, NASDAQ-100 futures jumped so quickly that trading was halted. Congratulations to everyone who bought the dip, I guess.
