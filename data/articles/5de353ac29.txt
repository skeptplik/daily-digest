PRACHINBURI, THAILAND – Father of four Adisorn Lakonburee used to catch fish for his family’s dinner, but stopped after floodwaters brought oil slicks and foul chemical smells into his village in eastern Thailand.
“Now I’m too afraid to catch or eat them,” the 43-year-old homebuilder said at his home in Prachinburi province, adding that he and his family are also too scared to drink the tap water.
Located between the capital Bangkok and Thailand’s flagship industrial zone further east, Prachinburi has long faced concerns over waste pollution.
