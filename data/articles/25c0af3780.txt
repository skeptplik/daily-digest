Denim is one of the most widely worn garment types on the planet. This common fashion denominator runs across different demographics, including age groups.
Holy Stitch is using denim and manufacturing as tools to engage with the community. Based in San Francisco—the birthplace of denim—the nonprofit runs classes in sewing and garment assembly, including an eight-week course covering the creation of a pair of jeans. Its location in San Francisco’s Mid-Market neighborhood also houses a small-scale manufacturing facility, a retail store and a tailor shop, and it serves as the official denim clothier of its district in San Francisco.
