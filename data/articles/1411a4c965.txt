A man has been charged with causing death by dangerous driving after two people were killed when their car was hit by a vehicle being chased by police.
A 22-year-old man and 23-year-old woman, both from York, died when their car was struck by a BMW being pursued by police in Leeds in the early hours of Sunday.
Leigh Ross, 39, will appear in court on Monday charged with causing death by dangerous driving and other offences.
West Yorkshire police said Ross, from Harrogate, had been charged with two counts of causing death by driving a vehicle while unlicensed or uninsured, failing to stop a mechanically propelled vehicle when required by a constable, and aggravated vehicle taking.
Two boys, aged 16 and 17, and a 16-year-old girl, who were also arrested on suspicion of causing death by dangerous driving, have been released on bail pending further inquiries, the force said.
West Yorkshire police have referred themselves to the police watchdog, the Independent Office for Police Conduct, which has launched an investigation into the incident.
The crash happened shortly after a blue BMW 123d allegedly failed to stop for officers in Gelderd Road and then collided with a white Volkswagen Scirocco.
The driver, a 22-year-old man, was pronounced dead at the scene while his passenger, a 23-year-old woman, died later in hospital. Specialist family liaison officers are supporting their families, who have asked for privacy.
Detectives are appealing for any witnesses who saw the collision, or has dashcam, mobile phone or CCTV footage, to contact West Yorkshire police via 101 or online.
