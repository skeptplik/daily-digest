On the surface, the premise of espionage drama “Vivant” is straightforward: Inept businessman Yusuke Nogi (Masato Sakai) is actually an elite spy for the covert organization Beppan, which is tasked with protecting Japan from terrorist threats and promoting world peace.
But the second season of the TBS series has incited a number of real-world controversies, exacerbating Japan’s already fraught relations with China while inviting scrutiny for its depictions of LGBTQ+ characters and even AI.
