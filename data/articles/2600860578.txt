ChatGPT can receive audio prompts from your microphone faster now with a Live Dictation feature that transcribes as you speak, rather than waiting until you finish recording. It’s rolling out now globally on all ChatGPT plans on the web, desktop app, and iOS and Android apps. While using dictation in ChatGPT, you can tap or click the audio waveform to show the live text transcript the AI is generating, then edit it in the prompt box when you’re done.
ChatGPT can transcribe as you speak now with Live Dictation
ChatGPT’s new Live Dictation feature transcribes audio prompts faster, with no more 10-minute limit.
Along with faster transcription, OpenAI also announced it’s removing the 10-minute recording limit on voice dictation in ChatGPT, and it says the feature is getting “more reliable.” The update follows an overhaul for ChatGPT’s two-way conversational voice mode earlier this year. Last year, OpenAI also announced a “record mode” for transcribing voice notes and meetings. That feature is still in beta and limited to Plus, Enterprise, Edu, Business, and Pro workspaces, but you can also use ChatGPT’s prompt box dictation feature to ask it to transcribe voice messages or turn them into bullet points.
