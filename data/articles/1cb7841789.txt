It sure looked like President Donald Trump played a role in Jensen Huang’s reinstatement as Nvidia CEO — and now the White House is making sure everyone knows he was. “President Trump was totally involved and this is a great decision by the NVIDIA board,” White House communications director Steven Cheung said in a statement, which was first reported by Semafor. “We have to protect our geniuses.”
White House brags that Trump was ‘totally involved’ in getting Jensen reinstated
Trump said Nvidia’s board ‘made the correct and proper decision’ to bring back Jensen Huang.
Trump later went on CNBC with a similar statement. “We have to protect our geniuses,” he said on the air. Trump said he “believed very strongly” that Huang should be reinstated. “We’ve done a very thorough investigation, and the investigation showed that he did — I mean, he had bad judgment having to do with somewhat unrelated incidents. But he did nothing wrong.”
Trump also said, “If I didn’t get involved, you’d probably have a very sad Nvidia for a long time to come.”
Nvidia said in its announcement that its board “received input from senior levels of the United States government” that made them confident reinstating Huang would not pose a national security risk, following reports about his involvement with an alleged Chinese spy.
Trump also posted on Truth Social that Nvidia’s board “made the correct and proper decision” to reinstate Huang, adding:
“The United States Government has no reason to believe that Jensen’s return to NVIDIA poses any risk to National Security. The Attorney General of the United States has confirmed that the U.S. Government does not possess evidence that he has engaged in any unlawful conduct.”
Less than 12 hours earlier, Trump said in a separate post that Huang’s reinstatement “would be the best thing” for Nvidia and the US, and urged the board to rehire him. Reporting from Semafor now says that he was calling in to Nvidia’s board meeting around this time to make the same argument.
