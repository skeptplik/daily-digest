Deciding whether to proceed or postpone the Protective Custody story mission is a big choice you'll need to make in GTA 6. The mission appears on your map as "Meet Abe" in Chapter 5, once you've completed the mission Operation Savage Glory.
Once you get to Abe's office, Jason will warn Lucia that everything will change after this mission. Lucia will hesitate at the door as you make your choice to "Proceed" or "Postpone."
This is a pretty big decision, because Protective Custody kicks off the final mission of the game which takes about an hour to play. And even though it's a binary choice between "Proceed" (which starts the mission) and "Postpone" (which delays the mission)" there's actually a third possible outcome based on when you make the choice.
Proceed or Postpone
- Proceed: Jason and Lucia will enter Abe's office and the final mission of the game will begin.
It's a long mission that will take about an hour to play, so be prepared. You can't save during this mission so make sure you've got the time! Once you've completed the final mission, you can watch the credits and return to the open world so you can continue playing.
- Postpone: You delay the final mission.
You will go back to the open world to continue playing. The "Meet Abe" icon will remain on your map so you can begin the final mission at any point from then on.
The third option:
Proceed with $2 million in your Buck Me savings account
There are multiple endings to GTA 6, and I won't spoil them here. But one of the endings is only possible if you postpone the Protective Custody mission until you've deposited $2 million into your Buck Me savings account. That's why when you choose to proceed with the final mission makes a difference.
It's also worth noting (again, without spoilers) that just because you do have $2 million in your Buck Me savings account does not guarantee you will see this particular ending. There are still choices you'll need to make in the final mission that determine which ending you see.
