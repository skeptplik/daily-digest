Truancy has continued to rise among elementary and junior high school students in Japan, with a record 359,424 students not attending school fully in fiscal 2025, according to the education ministry’s annual report on student issues.
The ministry counted in its report for fiscal 2025, which ran through March 2026, students who missed at least 30 school days during the school year without a stated cause, such as illness or financial issues. Over half of the students did not attend school for at least 90 separate days, including over 10,000 who did not attend a single day.
The overall figure, which includes those at special needs schools, was up 1.1% from the previous year, marking the 13th consecutive year with an increase. However, the rate of the rise shrank significantly compared to the COVID-19 pandemic years, when the number of such students increased by as much as almost 25% at one period. Due in large part to these steep increases, the number of such students has nearly tripled in the last decade even as the number of children in the country continues to decline.
