We may be knee-deep in a literacy crisis, but one could argue that makes books more important than ever. This holiday season, just be sure to gift titles that are tailored to the recipient’s interests — if you’re shopping for someone interested in fashion, you’ve come to the right place.
This year was packed with fashion-forward book launches, including a personal deep dive into Sandy Liang‘s girlish universe, an anthology of key moments in Bergdorf Goodman’s history and Kristen Bateman’s exploration of New York’s boldest fashion icons. “Doll Dressing: Fashion from Barbie to Bratz and Beyond” explores how dolls have long influenced fashion (and vice versa), while “Prada in the Nineties“ delivers a nostalgic glimpse into the Italian fashion label’s transformative era. You also can’t go wrong with gifting a chic coffee table book à la “Marc by Sofia” or “Schiaparelli: Anglomaniac“.
