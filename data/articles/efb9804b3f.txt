Live: Trump says leaders of US, China and Russia are 'making history' at Shenzhen summit
US President Donald Trump told his Chinese and Russian counterparts on Thursday that the three leaders were "making history" as they kicked off their first trilateral talks in Shenzhen. Beijing has said the meeting is aimed at "safeguarding world peace", but it has raised fears of a carve-up of the globe into "spheres of influence". Follow our liveblog for the latest developments.
Donald Trump, Vladimir Putin and Xi Jinping are meeting Thursday on the sidelines of the APEC summit in the Chinese city of Shenzhen for talks that have echoes of the US-UK-USSR February 1945 "Big Three" summit in Yalta.
The unprecedented trilateral summit of the leaders of China, Russia and the US will provide startling images and unsettle Europe this week, but all three presidents are approaching the meeting with starkly different agendas.
