Xi-Trump-Putin talks live: China, US and Russia leaders to hold landmark three-way meeting
The leaders of China, the US and Russia are set to hold a first-of-its-kind trilateral meeting on Thursday in the Chinese city of Shenzhen, on the sidelines of an APEC summit. The meeting between Xi Jinping, Donald Trump and Vladimir Putin, which Beijing said would involve "safeguarding world peace", has raised fears of a carve-up of the globe into "spheres of influence". Donald Trump, Vladimir Putin and Xi Jinping will meet Thursday on the sidelines of the APEC summit in the Chinese city of Shenzhen for talks that have echoes of the US-UK-USSR February 1945 "Big Three" summit in Yalta.
The unprecedented trilateral summit of the leaders of China, Russia and the US will provide startling images and unsettle Europe this week, but all three presidents are approaching the meeting with starkly different agendas.
