The Melbourne Renegades have pipped Brisbane Heat by three wickets in a last-ball thriller highlighted by a bizarre diamond duck to Deandra Dottin.
Chasing 138, Dottin came in at 4-97 and was adjusting her helmet strap and was gloveless on arrival at the crease outside the allotted 75 seconds time limit.
She was ordered by umpires to stand aside while Heat spinner Sarah Glenn had a free bowl at the stumps. She was on target and Dottin departed without facing a ball.

The drama preceded a tense final over when the Renegades required nine runs from Lucy Hamilton's bowling.
Annie Wikman smacked the first ball for four, and five singles followed, including Wikman's winning run from an inside edge.
Tess Flintoff top-scored in the chase with 30 from 32 balls and Georgia Wareham made a valuable 29 from 26 against a Heat bowling attack led by Glenn's 2-15 and Sienna Ginger's 2-13.

The Renegades (nine points) replaced the Heat (eight points) in fourth spot and, if they beat the Melbourne Stars on Saturday, will feature in the finals.
Renegades captain Sophie Molineux was instrumental in Wednesday night's win at Melbourne's Junction Oval, taking 4-20.
Earlier, Molineux produced her superb bowling spell to stymie the Heat batters.
The Australian captain claimed a wicket in her first over, bowling dangerous opener Grace Harris for a golden duck, and another in her second.
Molineux returned to bowl the 13th over and took two more wickets in three balls, while Orla Prendergast top-scored with 23 from 15 balls, and Lucy Hamilton (21 from 15) and Sienna Ginger (21 from 21) played handy knocks.
Brisbane must now hope the Renegades are defeated by the Stars on Saturday to have any hope of reaching the play-offs.
The Heat will know their fate by the time they host the Perth Scorchers on Sunday.
Sydney Thunder, on six points, remain the only other chance to reach the finals. They meet the finals-bound Adelaide Strikers on Thursday and the Sydney Sixers on Saturday.
The Thunder must win both games but, even then, they're relying on the Renegades and Heat to both lose their respective last matches.
