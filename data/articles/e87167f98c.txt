Indian 15yo Sooryavanshi hits 200 from 52 balls in List A cricket record
In short:
Indian 15-year-old Vaibhav Sooryavanshi scored 200 from 52 balls to set a new List A record.
Sooryavanshi also set new records for the fastest century and fastest 150.
Sooryavanshi had previously broken the record as the youngest player to represent India in an ODI.
