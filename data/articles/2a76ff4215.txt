A lot has happened in the last 24 hours in the community working to get Grand Theft Auto 6 running on PC. And now, Rockstar has dealt a major blow to their efforts.
After infamous leaker CyberLeek made good on their promise and released a full Xbox build of GTA 6, the build immediately spread online amongst modders and recompilers. Their goal? Get the game playable on PC as soon as possible.
The largest hub of all that activity has been the Project Leonida Discord server, which had its own repository and a team of volunteer modders working - with help from Claude AI - to recompile GTA 6's leaked Xbox build to run on PC. Rockstar has allegedly taken down the server, causing the project's leaders to dismantle the entire operation.
Project Leonida's lead NotRexed posted on X to announce they're ending development, after fans noticed the Discord server has been removed, as has Project Leonida's GitHub repository.
"I quit guys. I'm sorry," NotRexed wrote. "I really want to make cool stuff but every single time shit happens that I can't control so I'm stepping away. Thank you everybody, especially in the discord that allowed me to have this opportunity."
The modder, who claims to be a "15 year-old kid", later wrote in a follow-up X post that Rockstar is behind the server's takedown, though that's not the only reason for the project's end.
"Last post from me. I wanna clear things up," they wrote. "Rockstar taking my server down was only part of the reason. Like I've said before, I'm a 15 year-old kid. Managing school and sports is hard enough without having middle aged men yell and complain to you on Discord. Goodbye."
Before the Project Leonida server disappeared, footage emerged showing GTA 6 running on PC. The game is able to reach the initial setup screen, but quickly goes into a black screen afterwards. As unimpressive as the result was, it was achieved within hours of the full Xbox build's leak.
Incidentally, while Project Leonida was the biggest and most prominent of the various GTA 6 recompilation projects, the leaking of the full Xbox build has given rise to various other groups. PortWatch VI is a website dedicated to tracking their progress, and though the data is almost entirely self-reported, a few other teams appear to have made notable progress themselves, so this may not be the end of the story.
Rockstar has yet to comment publicly on the Xbox build's leak, and the company has not announced GTA 6 for PC. However, a report suggested GTA 6 arrives on PC in the first quarter of 2027, earlier than everyone expected.
