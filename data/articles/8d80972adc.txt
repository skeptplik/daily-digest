Story of Seasons developer Marvelous is offering voluntary resignations to reduce its workforce by 65 positions.
As Automaton reports, the developer plans to implement an "early retirement" program as it restructures its Consumer Business Division, which focuses on console and PC games instead of mobile and live-service titles.
"In the consumer game market, the business environment is changing, with development projects becoming larger in scale and development cycles growing longer," the developer said (via VGC).
The company will accept voluntary resignations from December 14, 2026, to January 8, 2027. This applies to both full-time and contract employees. Departing staff are expected to leave by the end of the fiscal year in March 2027.
A "special lump-sum retirement payout" will be given to those who voluntarily resign. They will also receive support in finding new employment elsewhere.
Marvelous stated it aims to "transition to a more flexible business and development structure by appropriately combining in-house development with collaboration with external partners, while also optimising our workforce to align with future business operations."
It concluded: "The consumer business remains one of our core businesses. Going forward, we will focus on titles that leverage our strengths and optimise development structures according to the characteristics of each title, while working on the planning and development of new titles."
Over the summer, reports indicated that Tencent was considering exiting some of its minority investments in Japanese studios, including Marvelous.
Tencent acquired a 20% stake in Marvelous in 2020 for an estimated ¥7 billion ($65 million). Marvelous intended to use the investment to expand existing properties and launch new IP.
In response, Tencent told GamesIndustry.biz: "Video games are core to Tencent's business. We remain fully committed to working with our investees and maintaining our strong presence in the Japanese game market over the long term."
