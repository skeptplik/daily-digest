Cairo – The son of Hamas chief Khalil al-Hayya survived an assassination attempt by the Israeli military on Friday, senior Hamas official Basem Naim said.
He said that Izzeldeen al-Hayya and his family had survived a dawn airstrike and Hamas sources said several people, including al-Hayya’s daughter-in-law, had been wounded but there had been no deaths.
The Israeli military had no immediate comment.
