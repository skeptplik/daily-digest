Denmark accuses Russia of assassination plot, expels diplomat
Issued on:
Share
Denmark has accused Moscow of being behind a failed assassination attempt targeting a Ukrainian-American rapper in Copenhagen, further escalating tensions between Europe and Russia. In response, Danish authorities have ordered the expulsion of a Russian diplomat and announced tighter restrictions on visas for Russian tourists. Yuka Royer reports.
