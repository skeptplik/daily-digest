Geelong Cats' surge into AFLW finals contention no accident
In short:
Geelong was the highest-scoring regular-season AFLW team of all time and only finished second behind North Melbourne on percentage.
Co-captain Nina Morrison says Geelong's rise to premiership contenders "doesn't feel like it's come out of the blue".
What's next?
Geelong takes on Melbourne in Saturday's qualifying final at Princes Park.
