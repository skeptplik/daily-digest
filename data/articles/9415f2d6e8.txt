3D printing is a booming hobby at the moment thanks to the prices of quality 3D printers dropping constantly. However, once you've dived into the world of 3D printing, making sure you maintain your printer is a priority.
Regular maintenance improves the quality of your prints, and if you also want to tidy up the finished look of your creations, it's worth building a small toolkit comprised of a few useful tools. Although some manufacturers may throw in a few cheap tools with a new 3D printer, they don't cover everything, or may be of poor quality, so it's still worth investing a few bucks into a dedicated 3D printer toolkit.
