New Socceroo Eli Adams faces nearly a year on the sidelines through a horror knee injury.
Adams made his first appearance for Australia off the bench in last week's 3-0 friendly win over Qatar, but was injured at training and had to leave camp before the Socceroos' 2-0 loss to Jordan.
His Japanese club Gamba Osaka confirmed today that Adams had ruptured the ACL in his left knee, along with injuring his MCL and suffering a lateral meniscus tear.
The 24-year-old will return to Australia to start his rehabilitation.
The Queenslander will have a knee reconstruction and faces at least several months — if not up to a year — on the sidelines.
It is a shocking blow for Adams, who earned a move to Japan off the back of stellar form at the Newcastle Jets.
Between 2024 and 2026, Adams scored 20 goals in 52 A-League appearances for Newcastle.
He has since notched two goals in 15 appearances for Gamba Osaka.
