If the street style proceedings of the past year have taught us anything, it’s that looking chic needn’t come at the expense of comfortable footwear. Case in point: Salomon. The French outdoor sportswear brand has become a fixture in fashion circles, with its once-niche trail-running sneakers now worn alongside designer handbags, oversized tailoring, and some of the season’s most sought-after outerwear.
Naturally, Salomon was among the first footwear destinations we perused for Black Friday. And while its sale might not be generating quite as much noise as those of its contemporaries, we uncovered some surprisingly good finds, including sneakers equipped with waterproof Gore-Tex technology.
Salomon’s Black Friday Sale at a Glance
- What’s on sale: Select technical sneakers, trail-running shoes, and waterproof Gore-Tex models.
- What to prioritize: Street style-approved silhouettes and winter-ready footwear.
- International savings: Salomon’s U.K. website offers additional discounted styles at up to 50 percent off.
- Where to shop: Salomon’s official website.
The virality of Salomon’s sneakers, particularly its signature XT-6 silhouette, speaks to a broader shift in how we approach footwear. Technical details once reserved for outdoor pursuits have become fashion statements in themselves, and there’s something refreshing about a sneaker trend that prioritizes functionality.
For those who have been contemplating a pair, we’d argue that the Gore-Tex models are especially smart purchases ahead of winter. After all, there’s little point in investing in a stylish sneaker if a rainy forecast immediately renders it unsuitable.
It’s also worth noting that our search extended to Salomon’s U.K. website, where we found a wider assortment of discounted styles, some marked down by as much as 50 percent — a little bonus for our international readers.
Salomon’s Black Friday Sneaker Deals Worth Shopping
Below, we’ve narrowed down the sale to the sneakers we’d happily incorporate into our own wardrobes, long after Black Friday has come and gone.
Salomon X Ultra 5 Gore-Tex
Don’t let the sporty silhouette fool you: There’s plenty of styling potential in this hiking shoe beyond the confines of a trail. Its durable Matryx upper, waterproof membrane, and grippy outsole make it an especially worthwhile purchase ahead of winter.
Salomon S/Lab Alpinway
Designed with mountain climbing in mind, the S/Lab Alpinway is arguably one of the more adventurous silhouettes in Salomon’s repertoire. Its dual-density cushioning offers comfortable support without compromising the precision needed for rocky terrain.
Salomon Speedcross Gore-Tex
A shoe designed to tackle muddy terrain might not sound like an obvious fashion purchase, but such is the beauty of gorpcore. Salomon’s Speedcross 6 boasts five-millimeter lugs for traction on uneven ground and a surprisingly lightweight construction.
