GTA 6 players are convinced that Rockstar Games painted a fake tunnel in Vice City just to trick them into ramming into it in a Looney Tunes-style crash.
Picture it: One day, Lucia's driving through Vice City without a care in the world, when suddenly – wham! – she splats right into the wall. That's a story that's become all too common in the early days of GTA 6 thanks to a very silly (and very convincing) mural located in the game.
If you've ever seen a Wile E. Coyote cartoon, you probably know where this is going. The idea is that the painting on the wall looks so lifelike that anyone not hyper-focused on the details thinks they can drive straight through it like any other tunnel. While the trick typically works out for the Road Runner, Jason, Lucia, and the rest of the internet haven't been so lucky.

Let's be honest: Rockstar probably did this on purpose. These crashes and Looney Tunes comparisons are way, way too common for it to have been something the developers didn't consider. Maybe the team didn't expect that many people to fall for the gag, or maybe they expected more players to notice it before running face-first into the wall, but it's hilarious, either way.
If there's anything we've learned from the early days of GTA 6, it's that Rockstar spent its lengthy development time well. Leonida is packed with things to do, people to meet, and secrets to discover, and even though so much has already been found after just one weekend, it's clear players won't be done anytime soon. The fact that everything looks so real it's tricking everyone online is just a bonus.
One example of an Easter Egg that someone (somehow) solved in just three days arrived this morning. Instead of a blink-and-you'll-miss-it moment or a convincing mural, this one teleports players to a retro version of Vice City.
