Brad Pitt, Angelina Jolie settle dispute over French estate Château Miraval
Allegations of abuse emerged in court filings during contentious legal battle
Brad Pitt and Angelina Jolie have reached a settlement in their contentious dispute over the French winery and estate Château Miraval, where they were wed.
"We have settled out of court in the best interests of our family and will not be commenting further," Pitt and Jolie's representatives told CBC News in a joint statement on Wednesday afternoon.
"Any comments in media stories attributed to 'sources' are in fact not coming from either of us, or anyone close to us," they added.
Terms of the settlement were not disclosed.
The Hollywood couple, formerly dubbed Brangelina, had leased the 1,200-acre vineyard and estate — located in Correns, in the southern French region of Provence — back in 2008, eventually purchasing it.

It was the site of the pair's wedding in 2014. At the time of their nuptials, the duo had been romantic partners for about a decade.
However, just two years later, Jolie filed for divorce, citing irreconcilable differences.
Their split — which involved a bitter custody dispute and allegations of abuse levelled at Pitt — dragged out for eight years, with the A-list Hollywood actors ultimately reaching a divorce settlement in late December 2024.
Duelling lawsuits over estate
In fall 2021, Jolie sold her half of Château Miraval to a wine subsidiary of Russian businessman Yuri Shefler's SPI Group, the international consortium that sells Stoli vodka and other alcohol brands worldwide.
Pitt sued her and Shefler over the sale in 2022, contending that he and Jolie had previously agreed to seek each other's consent before selling their stakes.

Jolie countersued and — in a subsequent court filing tied to the winery dispute — made public for the first time a raft of details about the in-flight confrontation involving the pair and their children in 2016 that prompted her, days later, to file for divorce.
Jolie alleged in the filing that she and her children had experienced physical and verbal abuse from Pitt while on that private flight, en route from Château Miraval to their home in Los Angeles. The incident was investigated at the time, and no charges were filed against Pitt.
Wednesday's settlement ends the Miraval legal battle between Pitt and Jolie, but Pitt's case against Shefler remains.
