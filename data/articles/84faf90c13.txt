The American West may play host to the lion’s share of iconic national parks—Yosemite, Yellowstone, Grand Canyon—but Acadia National Park is the crown jewel of the East Coast. Despite being one of the most compact national parks in the country, it’s also one of the most frequented, with around four million visitors a year who journey here to experience its historic carriage roads and the rugged Atlantic coast.
If you’re searching for a place to stay in proximity to the region’s dense forests and craggy coastlines, start by looking at the best Airbnbs near Acadia National Park. Offering more flexibility than a hotel—plus the possibility for true off-the-grid stays—the best homes are surrounded by nature. Winter travelers will want to bookmark amenities like fire pits and freestanding soaking tubs that make for a particularly cozy trip.
