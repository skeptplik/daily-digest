Amazon Early Black Friday Tech Deals

This is the lowest price on Amazon; however, it has dropped to this price before. Note that Walmart and Best Buy are also offering the XM6 at this sale price.

The AirTag seems to be on sale all the time, but no matter—it’s currently the lowest price on Amazon and cheaper than buying from Apple. It’s one of the best Bluetooth trackers. If you can swing it, go for the four-pack.
Alongside the AirTag, the AirPods Max 2 and AirPods Pro 3 are also on sale. These are well-regarded among WIRED testers, but we recommend waiting until after Thanksgiving to see if they drop further—don’t worry, they are often on sale
Phone accessories are aplenty on Amazon, but few look as good as Moft’s. You can pick up this ingenious wallet-kickstand hybrid that attaches to an iPhone via MagSafe, or peruse the rest of Moft's Black Friday deals.
You can expect Amazon’s own devices to go on sale during Black Friday. While Blink’s security cameras didn’t make our shortlist, WIRED reviewer Simon Hill still thinks they’re worth considering, especially if you’re on a budget. A downer is that they only work with Alexa, but if that’s you, it’s NBD. Like so many companies, Amazon has increased prices on its products. WIRED’s audio editor won’t stop praising Soundcore products, but he’s not wrong: This nascent brand (soon to be rebranded as Anker, its parent company) makes terrific audio products without the premium price. WIRED reviewer Louryn Strampe included this Bluetooth boombox in a roundup of her favorite festival gear. It’s the lowest price ever on Amazon currently, but it’s not the cheapest—keep an eye out at other retailers during Black Friday.
This EcoFlow portable battery is WIRED reviewer Simon Hill’s pick as the best power bank for laptops. It has a large 27,650-mAh capacity, which is plenty for charging smaller devices too. It’s great for travel since it’s allowed on a plane, and it has a retractable USB-C cable, so you can leave extras at home.
At $359, the second-gen Bose QuietComfort over-ear headphones seem overpriced considering the competition. But when it’s on sale at $199 to $219 (depending on the color), they suddenly become a great buy. As expected from Bose, the noise cancellation and sound quality are excellent. They are also comfortable, making them great travel companions. Note that the first-gen version is also on sale for $169 (it’s frequently at this price), but it’s outdated, and we think you should spend $30 to $50 more on the latest version instead.
