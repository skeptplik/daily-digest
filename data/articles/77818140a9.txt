David Warner lashes ball-tampering investigation, sources say all bowlers spoken to
In short:
David Warner has posted on social media that bowlers Mitchell Starc, Nathan Lyon and Josh Hazlewood were not interviewed in the Cape Town ball-tampering investigation.
A source close to the bowling group has told the ABC the "comments are just not true", while Pat Cummins already said he was part of the 2018 inquiry.
Warner said the investigation was "shoddy" and "stinks of a cover-up".
