A Japanese opposition lawmaker has suggested the possibility of submitting a censure motion against education minister Yoshihiro Seki over a fundraising party that he held in June 2024.
The possibility of submitting such a motion to the Senate, the upper chamber of parliament, “cannot be ruled out,” Yoshitaka Saito, the Constitutional Democratic Party of Japan’s (CDP) Diet affairs chief, told reporters Tuesday. All CDP lawmakers are members of the Upper House.
Seki, a lawmaker of the ruling Liberal Democratic Party in the House of Representatives, the lower chamber, has been under fire for holding the fundraising party at a building for lawmakers in violation of the Lower House’s internal rules and collecting ¥90,000 ($565) as a fee from each participant.
