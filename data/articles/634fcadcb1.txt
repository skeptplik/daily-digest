Bahrain says an Iranian attack has struck a civilian industrial facility, starting a fire, in the latest escalation of the war in the Middle East.
Bahrain’s Defence Force said its air defences intercepted several Iranian aerial attacks late on Saturday.
In a post on X, Bahrain’s Ministry of Interior said the attack was part of “Iranian aggression”.
It said preliminary reports pointed to material damage but no injuries, and that civil defence teams were working to bring the fire under control.
The Interior Ministry did not name the facility or say where it was. Earlier, air raid sirens sounded in Bahrain, and the ministry urged residents to move to the nearest safe place without saying what the threat was.
In a statement after the attack, Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) said it had carried out a combined missile and drone operation targeting United States military positions at the al-Riffa and Awali bases in Bahrain.
The IRGC claimed it destroyed two US Patriot air defence radars at the bases.
The attack was “in response to continued aggression and violations of the airspace of the Islamic Republic of Iran”, the statement said.
“After two US MQ-1 and MQ-9 reconnaissance and combat drones violated Iranian airspace, US military positions at the aforementioned bases were targeted in an operation by the IRGC Aerospace Force,” it added.
Earlier, Iran’s semi-official Fars news agency reported that the IRGC had attacked two military bases used by US forces at Sheikh Isa and Juffair.
The IRGC said that operation, as well as being in response to continued violations of Iranian airspace, came following a US attack on a radar installation on Iran’s Hengam Island.
Iran’s state news agency IRNA reported that an interceptor missile launched from the US Fifth Fleet base in Bahrain had also crashed on Sitra Island and started a large fire. It cited an outlet called Bahrain News, which blamed a recurring malfunction in the Patriot air defence system.
Bahrain hosts the US Navy’s Fifth Fleet. It has been attacked repeatedly since the US-Israel war on Iran began in late February, including three drone attacks in recent days after a lull of several weeks.
Other Gulf states have also come under repeated fire from Tehran since the US-Israel war began.
Reactions to latest Iranian strikes
In a statement on X, Bahrain’s Foreign Ministry called on the United Nations Security Council to take “firm measures to compel Iran” to implement Resolution 2817.
The resolution, adopted on March 11, condemns Iranian attacks against Gulf states and Jordan, and demands an immediate halt to hostilities and maritime interference.
The head of the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC) expressed his “full solidarity” with Bahrain following Iran’s attack on the industrial facility.
Jassim Mohamed AlBudaiwi, GCC secretary-general, said Bahrain’s security, territorial integrity, facilities, citizens and residents are an integral part of the security of the GCC states, and that the council stands united in confronting threats to their security and stability.
The GCC backs “all measures” Bahrain takes to deal with the attack, he added.
