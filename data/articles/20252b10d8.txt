When we need that perfect everyday black bootie or trendy ballet flat that doesn’t cost an entire paycheck, Tony Bianco always comes through. The Australian brand has made high-quality shoes since 1972, blending classic style with fashion-forward design (and quality leather and suede). With nine boutiques across Australia, the brand is growing its U.S. presence. The latest city? Sunny Los Angeles.
Tony Bianco began its U.S. retail expansion with a pop-up in New York City’s SoHo neighborhood this March. Thanks to the location, a hot spot for travelers, it’s so successful that the brand decided to make it a permanent store. Building on the momentum, a new L.A. pop-up opened in late October on trendy Melrose Ave.
“Following the incredible response to our New York boutique, Los Angeles felt like the natural next step for us,” Anthony Bianco, director of Tony Bianco, said in a statement. “Seeing our SoHo pop-up evolve into a permanent location has been an exciting milestone, and we’re looking forward to continuing that journey on the West Coast.”
Inside the six-month residency (for now?) is a curation of the brand’s seasonal arrivals and most-loved styles. The L.A. crowd has already made a beeline for the store, snapping up sandal wedges and turquoise flats (and even cookies, iced coffee and matcha for opening week).
“We’ve built such a strong community in the U.S., and we’re excited to create another space where our customers can experience Tony Bianco in person,” Bianco continued. “Los Angeles has always had a strong connection to fashion and personal style, and we’re excited to become part of that community.”
