Even after clearing Grand Theft Auto 6’s long story, I keep finding myself drawn back to Leonida. That’s thanks in large part to Jason and Lucia, a pair of charismatic protagonists that I genuinely enjoy spending time with. While you can treat them as independent characters and take them out on solo adventures, GTA 6 shines brightest when you travel around Leonida as a pair. In fact, you’re bound to miss a whole lot if you aren’t letting Jason and Lucia enjoy a date from time to time.
While playing as one character in GTA 6, you have the option to invite your partner to hang out with you. (This feature unlocks partway through Chapter 2 of the story.) If you’re playing as Jason, for instance, you could text Lucia and ask if she wants to hang out, prompting her to drive to your location. You can coordinate crimes this way too; if you find a bank you want to rob, you can ping Jason and tell him to meet Lucia at the spot to pull off the job together. There are a lot of benefits to working together, as heists will become easier and you’ll be able to ask your partner to run off and fence any goods you grab.
But keeping Jason and Lucia together has some less obvious benefits, too. When the two explore together, they’ll occasionally break out into natural conversations. One time while driving through Lummox County listening to a country radio station, Lucia talked about how she thought the genre was a joke growing up. A little odd couple exchange ensued, where Jason talked about how much he loves country music. Later, upon finding a stray bicycle, Lucia told Jason about the time she tried to ride a bike as a kid and crashed into an ice cream truck.
There are even entire running gags that you’ll only hear while playing together. One recurring bit involves Lucia constantly answering spam robocalls and gradually losing her cool over them. That culminates in her giving the AI voice a firm thrashing, while Jason chuckles from the sidelines. Those bits of contextual chatter help make Jason and Lucia feel like an authentic couple rather than voiceless pawns to be moved around.
More importantly, crucial story details can come out of those moments. One time while exploring a swamp in search of Skunk Ape effigies (weird game!), Jason and Lucia broke out into a conversation about their family dynamics. It started when Jason mentioned Lucia’s mother doesn’t like him very much. After a bit of unconvincing denial, Lucia admitted that there’s truth to Jason’s anxiety: Lucia lied to her mother, saying that Jason essentially walked out on her and their child. That revelation triggered a conversation where Lucia recalled a moment where Jason once told her he didn’t want children, which is why she initially didn’t tell Jason about their son. It’s an important moment that gives depth to Jason and Lucia’s history and fills in some questions about why Tommy was kept secret for so long.
So, if you’ve been exploring Leonida solo for most of your GTA 6 playthrough, make sure to invite your partner to hang out regularly. You’ll miss some of the best human moments in the game if you do, and you’ll understand each character so much more by listening to their chatter. And, of course, you’ll also be able to send Jason off with a bag full of cocaine if you don’t want to trek back to Vice City to sell it.
